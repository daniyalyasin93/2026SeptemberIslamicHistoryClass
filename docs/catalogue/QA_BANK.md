# QA_BANK — answers prepared before they are asked

Pre-written, safe, Sunni answers to hostile and sensitive questions. Grows all term.
**Never improvise a position on the مشاجرات from the floor.**

**Venue policy that shapes every answer:** there are **no live questions**. Slips go into a box at
the door and the speaker stays 15 minutes afterwards for one-to-one. So every answer below is
delivered **quietly, to one person, off the record** — not to a room. That is a large advantage and
the speaker should use it: the register is conversational, not defensive.

**The shape of every answer on this page is the same:**

> *the page is open · here is what it says · here is what the author himself says about it.*

Never defend a Companion by disputing a report that is in the book. Open the book instead.

## 0 · The three lines to have word-perfect

These are said before anything else, and they defuse most of what follows.

**۱ · The guardrail** *(also delivered from the podium at 0:02, so this is a reminder, not a new claim)*

> تاریخِ اسلام = تاریخِ مسلمین. یہ مسلمانوں کی تاریخ ہے، دین کا ماخذ نہیں۔
> ہم وہی بیان کرتے ہیں جو کتاب بیان کرتی ہے — اور صحابہ ؓ کے درمیان فیصلہ نہیں کرتے۔

**۲ · The deferral**

> اس پر اصول کے ساتھ بات نشست ۳ میں ہوگی۔ فقہاء کی رائے یہ ہے کہ بلا ضرورت مشاجراتِ صحابہ ؓ کا

**۳ · The honest limit** — the most disarming of the three, and it is simply true:

> میں عالم نہیں ہوں۔ میں ایک کتاب پڑھ رہا ہوں اور آپ کو بتا رہا ہوں کہ اُس میں کیا لکھا ہے۔
> صفحہ کھلا ہوا ہے؛ جو اِس میں ہے وہی میں کہوں گا، اور جو نہیں ہے وہ نہیں کہوں گا۔

### 1.8 ⛔ The ambush: someone reads out ابن کثیر's polemic

**ج۵ ص۳۵۵.** Refuting the claim of a نص for علی ؓ, ابن کثیر writes that whoever ascribes collective
فجور to the Companions ؓ «فقد خَلَعَ رِبْقَةَ الإسلامِ … وكانَ إراقَةُ دَمِه أحَلَّ من إراقَةِ المُدامِ».

**Never quote it, never paraphrase it, never put it on a slide, never read it from the floor.**
The speaker's only reason to know the page exists is so he is not ambushed by someone else reading
it. If that happens, the answer is short and **does not defend it**:

> یہ ابن کثیر رحمہ اللہ ایک عقیدے کے دعوے کا رد کر رہے ہیں — کوئی واقعہ بیان نہیں کر رہے۔
> ہم واقعات بیان کر رہے ہیں، اور واقعات سامنے والے صفحے پر ہیں۔

Then move on. Do not elaborate.

## 2 · Session 1 — the method and the framing

### 2.1 «آپ نے انبیاء ؑ کی تاریخیں کہاں سے لیں؟»

**Answer the honest way, because the slide already says it.** Those dates are
`[CONVENTIONAL-ESTIMATE]` — approximations from general scholarship, shown **only to convey
distance**, and our sources do not fix them. That is why they are drawn **hollow** and marked
*approx.*, and why آدم ؑ and نوح ؑ carry **no number at all**.

> ترتیب وحی سے معلوم ہے۔ تاریخیں معلوم نہیں — اور جو معلوم نہ ہو، اُسے ہم معلوم نہیں کہتے۔

This is the strongest single moment in the evening for the speaker's credibility. Use it.

### 2.2 «آپ سنی نقطۂ نظر پیش کر رہے ہیں»

> جی ہاں — اور میں یہ شروع میں کہہ چکا ہوں۔ یہ کورس اہلِ سنت کی معتبر کتابوں پر مبنی ہے،
> اور کتابوں کے نام میں نے آپ کو بتا دیے ہیں۔ میں کوئی نیا دعویٰ نہیں کر رہا۔

Naming the framing openly is the defence. Never pretend to a neutrality the course does not claim.

### 2.3 «سہیل بن عمرو ؓ والا واقعہ کتنا مستند ہے؟»

**Answer it straight — the honest answer is more impressive than a confident one.** Both the teeth
incident and the Mecca speech rest on **مغازی reporting**, not on a ṣaḥīḥ chain. البدایہ's version
runs ابن اسحاق ← محمد بن عمرو بن عطاء and **ابن کثیر himself grades it «مرسلٌ، بل معضلٌ»**; the
Mecca episode there is ابن ہشام ← «أبو عبيدة وغيره من أهل العلم»; الکامل gives it with no chain.

> یہ واقعہ سیرت و مغازی کی روایت ہے، حدیثِ صحیح نہیں — اور خود ابن کثیر رحمہ اللہ نے اِس کی سند
> پر کلام کیا ہے۔ میں یہ آپ کو بتا کر سنا رہا ہوں، چھپا کر نہیں۔

`docs/research/suhayl-ibn-amr.md` carries both wordings and both pages.

### 2.4 «آپ کہتے ہیں کورس *تاریخِ امت* پر ہے، مگر یہ حوالہ عربی کتاب کا ہے»

True, and say so plainly. The Urdu volumes we hold cover only مقدمہ pp.۳۲–۷۸ and pp.۴۵۶–۴۶۵ —
**this episode is not in the extracted pages.** The Arabic works used (البدایہ، سیر، الکامل،
الإعلان) are the four that *تاریخِ امت*'s own مقدمہ names and relies on, so citing them is staying
inside the course's frame, not stepping outside it.

## 5 · Session 2 — اليمامة, and the numbers

### 5.1 "How many bearers of the Qurʾān were killed at al-Yamāma?" — THE LIKELIEST HOSTILE SLIP OF THE EVENING

`docs/research/zayd-ibn-al-khattab-and-the-dead-of-yamama.md` §12 calls it exactly that, and the
finding must not be softened: **the approved sources give no number of reciters at all.**

| | |
|---|---|
| **What the room expects** | Seventy. Or five hundred. Or "four hundred and fifty reciters" |
| **What the books actually say** | Three approved works give the **reason** and none gives a **figure**. البدایہ ج۷ ص۷۹: «وذلك بعد ما استَحَرّ القتلُ في القُرّاء يوم اليمامة كما ثبت به الحديث في صحيح البخاري» (ed. fn.: **صحيح البخاري ٤٩٨٦**) · سیر (الراشدون) ص۶۳: «لما استحر القتل بقراء القرآن يوم اليمامة» · الکامل ج۲ ص۲۱۹: «لِمَا رَأَى مِنْ كَثْرَةِ مَنْ قُتِلَ مِنَ الصَّحَابَةِ؛ لِئَلَّا يَذْهَبَ الْقُرْآنُ» |
| **Where 450 comes from, and what it counts** | البدایہ ج۷ ص۵۵ — and read the sentence to the end: «أربعمئة وخمسون **من حَملة القرآن ومن الصَّحابة وغيرهم**». It is a total for **three categories together**. Anyone who says "450 reciters were killed" has misread the page |
| **The answer** | *The books say the killing ran hot among the reciters, and that this is why the Qurʾān was collected. They do not say how many reciters died. Ibn Kathīr gives a total of four hundred and fifty for the well-known Muslim dead, and he counts bearers of the Qurʾān, Companions and others together in that one figure. So the honest thing to say is that we do not know how many reciters fell — only that enough of them fell for Abū Bakr ؓ to act.* |
| **Do not say** | Seventy. Five hundred. "Four hundred and fifty reciters." Any figure at all |

**The pages:** البدایہ ج۷ ص۷۹ · https://shamela.ws/book/30097/3225 — البدایہ ج۷ ص۵۵ ·
https://shamela.ws/book/30097/3201 — سیر (الراشدون) ص۶۳ · https://shamela.ws/book/10906/1211 —
الکامل ج۲ ص۲۱۹ · https://shamela.ws/book/21712/910.

Searched and **not found**: `+"قراء القرآن" +اليمامة`, `+"من القراء" +اليمامة`,
`+"سبعين" +القرآن +اليمامة`, `+"سبعمائة" +اليمامة`.

> **This is a moment of strength, not weakness.** "We do not know how many" is a stronger sentence
> than any number, and it is true. It is also a «کیسے پتا چلا؟» remark spoken exactly where a
> listener would ask it (`CLAUDE.md` §1.7).

### 5.2 "Then how many Muslims died at al-Yamāma altogether?"

| | |
|---|---|
| **The books give** | «ستمئة، وقيل: خمسمئة» — البدایہ ج۷ ص۳۵ · «نحو من ستّ مائة» — سیر ج۱ ص۲۹۸ · 360 Muhājirūn and Anṣār **of the people of المدینہ** + 300 Muhājirūn **from outside المدینہ** — الکامل ج۲ ص۲۱۸ · «أربع مائة وخمسون» (أبو معشر) — سیر الراشدون ص۴۹ · «أربعمئة وخمسون» (Ibn Kathīr's own summary) — البدایہ ج۷ ص۵۵ · «ثمانية وخمسون رجلًا» of المهاجرون والأنصار (خليفة بن خياط) — سیر الراشدون ص۶۱ |
| **The answer** | *The books give figures from under a hundred to over a thousand, depending on who is being counted. Ibn Kathīr's own summary is four hundred and fifty. These are figures that were transmitted, not figures that were audited — and the books print two of them side by side rather than choosing. What is not disputed is that it was the worst day the Muslims had had:* «وَلَمْ يَلْقَ الْمُسْلِمُونَ حَرْبًا مِثْلَهَا قَطُّ» (الکامل ج۲ ص۲۱۶) |
| **Do not say** | Never attribute الکامل's 360 to the Anṣār — that was an error in a first draft and it is wrong. The 360 is **Muhājirūn and Anṣār together, of the people of المدینہ**. And never explain خليفة's 58 by saying it counts only the two migrations: that is very probably why it is small, but **the books do not say so** |

### 5.3 "Who killed Musaylima?"

**Four accounts in the safe list, and the sources themselves decline to choose.** وحشي ؓ throws the
javelin and an Anṣārī strikes with the sword; ابن کثیر's own view is that وحشي ؓ struck first and
أبو دجانة ؓ finished him (البدایہ ج۷ ص۳۴); الواقدي names **عبد الله بن زيد بن عاصم ؓ**; سيف names
**عدي بن سهل** (البدایہ ج۴ ص۱۸۵). And البدایہ ج۷ ص۵۱ has وحشي ؓ refuse the claim himself:

> فَربُّك أعلمُ أيُّنا قَتَله
> — البدایہ والنہایہ ج۷ ص۵۱ · https://shamela.ws/book/30097/3197
> *"Your Lord knows better which of us killed him."*

**The answer:** *The books name four men, and the man best placed to know would not claim it. Waḥshī ؓ
said: your Lord knows better which of us killed him. That is the answer the sources themselves
prefer, and I will not improve on it.* **Never state a single killer.**

### 6.1 ⚠ "Why did Khālid ؓ kill Mālik b. Nuwayra ؓ at al-Buṭāḥ — and then marry his widow?"

**⚠ SPEAKER'S DISCRETION. This is مشاجرات-adjacent and the guardrail governs it
(`CLAUDE.md` §1.6).** Full working: `docs/research/ridda-campaign-the-conduct-of-the-wars.md` §5.7.

**Step 1 — say what is not in dispute, in one breath.** مالك ؓ was at البُطاح, undecided, after
dealing with سجاح and breaking with her; the chiefs of تميم had come in and paid and he had not; a
patrol brought him in with a party of his people; **the patrol itself disagreed about them** —
**أبو قتادة الأنصاري ؓ testified they had given the adhān and prayed, and others said they had not**;
they were killed, ضِرار بن الأزور killed مالك, and خالد ؓ married his widow. (البدایہ ج۷ ص۳۰; الکامل
ج۲ ص۲۱۲–۲۱۳; سیر الراشدون ص۴۵.)

**Step 2 — say that the books disagree about how it happened, and show that they do.** Four accounts:

| Source | How it is told |
|---|---|
| **البدایہ ج۷ ص۳۰** | A bitterly cold night; خالد ؓ's crier called «أدفئوا أسراكم»; «فَظنَّ القوم أنّه أراد القتلَ، فقتلوهم». خالد ؓ came out at the wailing and said «إذا أراد الله أمرًا أصابه» |
| **البدایہ ج۷ ص۳۰–۳۱** («ويقال») | خالد ؓ reproached him over سجاح and the zakāt — «ألم تعلمْ أنها قرينةُ الصلاة؟»; مالك: «إن **صاحبَكم** كان يزعمُ ذلك»; خالد: «أهو صاحبُنا وليس بصاحبك؟ يا ضرار اضربْ عُنُقه» |
| **الکامل ج۲ ص۲۱۳** | The same «أدفئوا أسراكم», **with the linguistic explanation on the page**: «وَهِيَ فِي لُغَةِ كِنَانَةَ الْقَتْلُ، فَظَنَّ الْقَوْمُ أَنَّهُ أَرَادَ الْقَتْلَ، وَلَمْ يُرِدْ إِلَّا الدِّفْءَ» |
| **سیر (الراشدون) ص۴۴–۴۵** | Two more, including الزهري's: the patrol bound them **after** they laid down their arms; أبو قتادة ؓ said they had been given quarter, «وَخَالَفَ أَبَا قَتَادَةَ جَمَاعَةُ السَّرِيَّةِ، فَأَخْبَرُوا خَالِدًا أَنَّهُ لَمْ يَكُنْ لَهُمْ أَمَانٌ» |

**Step 3 — the reckoning at المدينة, verbatim. This is the usable part and it is where the answer
ends.**

> قَتَلْتَ امْرَأً مُسْلِمًا، ثُمَّ نَزَوْتَ عَلَى امْرَأَتِهِ، وَاللَّهِ لَأَرْجُمَنَّكَ بِأَحْجَارِكَ!
> — الکامل فی التاریخ ج۲ ص۲۱۳ · https://shamela.ws/book/21712/904
> *ʿUmar ؓ, in the mosque, tearing the arrows from Khālid's ؓ turban: "You killed a Muslim man, then
> you leapt upon his wife. By Allah, I will stone you with your own stones!"*

> هِيهِ يَا عُمَرُ! **تَأَوَّلَ فَأَخْطَأَ**، فَارْفَعْ لِسَانَكَ عَنْ خَالِدٍ، **فَإِنِّي لَا أَشِيمُ سَيْفًا سَلَّهُ اللَّهُ عَلَى الْكَافِرِينَ.**
> — الکامل فی التاریخ ج۲ ص۲۱۳ · https://shamela.ws/book/21712/904
> *Abū Bakr's ؓ answer: "Enough, ʿUmar! **He interpreted and was mistaken.** Lift your tongue off
> Khālid — **for I do not sheathe a sword Allah has drawn against the disbelievers.**"*

> ليس على خالد ما تقول، هبه تأول فأخطأ.
> — سیر أعلام النبلاء (سير الخلفاء الراشدين) ص۴۶ · https://shamela.ws/book/10906/1194
> *"Khālid does not bear what you say. Grant that he interpreted and was mistaken."*

**Step 4 — the safest single sentence available on the whole matter, and it is ابن کثیر's own**
(البدایہ ج۷ ص۳۲):

> واستمرَّ أبو بكر بخالدٍ على الإمرة، **وإن كان قد اجتهد في قتلِ مالك بن نويرة وأخطأَ في قتله**
> *"Abū Bakr kept Khālid in command — **even though he had exercised his judgement in killing Mālik
> b. Nuwayra and was mistaken in killing him**."*

On that same page ابن کثیر compares it to the Prophet's ﷺ own handling of خالد ؓ at بني جَذيمة, where
the Prophet ﷺ paid the blood-money, said «**اللهم إني أبرأ إليك مما صنع خالد**», **and did not remove
him either.** That comparison is the whole answer: **a mistake was made, it was paid for, and the
command was kept — and the precedent for that handling is prophetic.**

**Step 5 — close on consolation, not on blame** (الکامل ج۲ ص۲۱۴ · https://shamela.ws/book/21712/905):

> فَقَالَ عُمَرُ: **لَوْ كُنْتُ أَقُولُ الشِّعْرَ لَرَثَيْتُ أَخِي زَيْدًا.** فَقَالَ مُتَمِّمٌ: وَلَا سَوَاءَ يَا أَمِيرَ الْمُؤْمِنِينَ، **لَوْ كَانَ أَخِي صُرِعَ مَصْرَعَ أَخِيكَ لَمَا بَكَيْتُهُ.** فَقَالَ عُمَرُ: **مَا عَزَّانِي أَحَدٌ بِأَحْسَنَ مِمَّا عَزَّيْتَنِي بِهِ.**
> *ʿUmar ؓ said to Mālik's ؓ brother Mutammim: "Were I a man who made verse, I would have elegised
> my brother Zayd." Mutammim said: "They are not alike, Commander of the Believers — had my brother
> fallen where your brother fell, I would not have wept for him." ʿUmar said: "**No one has ever
> consoled me better than you have consoled me.**"*

**And then line ۲ of §0** — *this is handled with اصول in session 3; I do not settle it standing here.*

**⛔ Do not say:**
- Do not choose one of the four accounts and tell it as what happened.
- Do not offer a defence of خالد ؓ that the books do not make, and do not offer a charge against him
  that the books do not make. **أبو بكر ؓ's «تأول فأخطأ» and ابن کثیر's «وإن كان قد اجتهد في قتلِ مالك بن نويرة وأخطأَ في قتله» are the only
  verdicts on the page. Neither is the speaker's.**
- Do not moralise about the marriage. State it in the same clause as the rest and move on.
- Do not use الکامل ج۲ ص۲۱۴'s note that two of خالد ؓ's nephews fell «في هذه الوقعة» — the antecedent
  of "this engagement" is not unambiguous on the page.

### 6.2 ⛔ "I have heard that Mālik's ؓ head was used as a stone under a cooking pot"

**It is on the page** — البدایہ ج۷ ص۳۱, and سیر ص۴۴: that مالك ؓ's head was used as one of three
stones for a pot خالد ؓ ate from that night, «ليرهب بذلك الأعرابَ».

**The recommendation is against using it, and it is not to be volunteered.** If a slip asks:

> *Yes, Ibn Kathīr prints it, and so does al-Dhahabī. I did not tell it because it adds nothing you
> need and it is the kind of detail that takes over an evening. The page is there; the ruling on the
> whole affair is Abū Bakr's ؓ, and I have given it to you.*

Then stop. **Do not elaborate, do not describe it, do not put it on a slide.**

### 6.3 "Khālid ؓ burned men alive. How was that lawful?"

**Do not smooth this. It is in the books, and عمر ؓ's objection is in the books beside it.**

**What the page says (الکامل ج۲ ص۲۰۶–۲۰۷):** خالد ؓ would accept no submission from أسد، غطفان، طيّئ،
سُلَيم، عامر until they handed over **«الَّذِينَ حَرَّقُوا وَمَثَّلُوا وَعَدَوْا عَلَى الْإِسْلَامِ فِي حَالِ
رِدَّتِهِمْ»** — the men who had themselves burned and mutilated Muslims during the rising. They were
handed over and were executed by fire, stoning and being thrown from heights — **and he wrote to
أبو بكر ؓ telling him what he had done.** البدایہ ج۷ ص۲۷ gives the stated purpose: «كلّ هذا
**ليعتبرَ بهم من يسمعُ بخبرهم** من مُرْتدَّةِ العرب».

**And the objection, on the page, from عمر ؓ** — a separate burning report about بنو سُلَيم:

> كَانَ فِي بَنِي سُلَيْمٍ رِدَّةٌ، فَبَعَثَ أَبُو بَكْرٍ إِلَيْهِم خَالِدَ بنَ الوَلِيْدِ، فَجَمَعَ رِجَالاً مِنْهُم فِي الحَظَائِرِ، ثُمَّ أَحْرَقَهُم. فَقَالَ عُمَرُ لِأَبِي بَكْرٍ: **أَتَدَعُ رَجُلاً يُعَذِّبُ بِعَذَابِ اللهِ؟** قَالَ: **وَاللهِ لاَ أَشِيْمُ سَيْفاً سَلَّهُ اللهُ عَلَى عَدُوِّهِ.**
> — سیر أعلام النبلاء ج۱ ص۳۷۲ · https://shamela.ws/book/10906/1798
> *"There was apostasy among Banū Sulaym, and Abū Bakr sent Khālid b. al-Walīd to them. He gathered
> men of them into enclosures and burned them. ʿUmar said to Abū Bakr: **Will you leave a man who
> punishes with the punishment of Allah?** He said: **By Allah, I will not sheathe a sword Allah has
> drawn against His enemy.**"*
> (الذہبی's own note: the isnād is هشام بن عروة عن أبيه, and the editor marks it **mursal**.)

**The answer, in order — and the order is the answer:** *first*, the reprisals answered killings and
mutilations that the same page names; *second*, they were carried out and reported openly, not
concealed; *third*, **ʿUmar ؓ objected to them in exactly the words you are objecting in** — "will you
leave a man who punishes with the punishment of Allah?" — *fourth*, Abū Bakr ؓ answered him and kept
Khālid ؓ. **The dispute is inside the book. I am not adding one.**

**Do not say:** do not defend the burnings; do not excuse them; do not adjudicate between the two
Companions; do not describe the methods beyond the one clause the page gives.

### 6.4 ⚠ "Abū Bakr's ؓ own letter orders them to be burned with fire"

**It is on the page and it must be neither quoted nor denied.** البدایہ ج۷ ص۲۴ carries, in the
circular letter sent to the tribes, the clause «وأن يحرقهم بالنار وأن يقتلهم كلَّ قتلة، وأن يسبيَ
النساءَ والذَّراري». Handling was settled in `arabian-tribes-and-the-ridda-setup.md` §3.6 and
re-affirmed in `ridda-campaign-the-conduct-of-the-wars.md` §3.3.

**The answer:** *Yes, that clause is on that page — I am not going to pretend otherwise. And the
operative test in the very same letter, the one the armies actually worked by, is the adhān:*

> وقد أمرتُ رسولي أن يقرأ كتابه في كلِّ مَجْمعٍ لكم، **والداعية الأذان، فإذا إذَّنَ المسلمون فأذنوا فكُفُّوا عنهم، وإن لم يُؤَذِّنوا فسلوهم ما عليهم، فإن أبوا عاجِلوهم**
> — البدایہ والنہایہ ج۷ ص۲۴ · https://shamela.ws/book/30097/3170
> *"I have ordered my messenger to read his letter in every assembly of yours. **The summons is the
> adhān: when the Muslims call it and they call it too, hold off from them; and if they do not call
> it, ask them what they owe; and if they refuse, move on them at once.**"*

*That is the rule of engagement of the whole war, in the same document. The fuller ruling on the
letter's other clauses is a matter for the فقهاء, not for me.* Then defer.

**Do not:** quote the burning clause; paraphrase it onto a slide; deny it is there.

### 6.5 "The zakāt-withholders said the kalima. Why were they fought?"

**The most predictable slip of the whole session, and the answer is entirely on the page.**

**(a) The threshold أبو بكر ؓ set** (الکامل ج۲ ص۱۹۹, in the delegation scene):

> فَقَالَ أَبُو بَكْرٍ: وَاللَّهِ لَوْ مَنَعُونِي عِقَالًا لَجَاهَدْتُهُمْ عَلَيْهِ
> *"By Allah, if they withheld from me a hobbling-cord, I would fight them for it."*

**(b) What was actually taken as the pledge in the field** — خالد ؓ's own formula from بنو عامر after
بُزاخة, and it is three items with nothing else in it:

> عَلَيْكُمْ عَهْدُ اللَّهِ وَمِيثَاقُهُ **لَتُؤْمِنُنَّ بِاللَّهِ وَرَسُولِهِ، وَلَتُقِيمُنَّ الصَّلَاةَ، وَلَتُؤْتُنَّ الزَّكَاةَ**، وَتُبَايِعُونَ عَلَى ذَلِكَ أَبْنَاءَكُمْ وَنِسَاءَكُمْ. فَيَقُولُونَ: نَعَمْ.
> — الکامل فی التاریخ ج۲ ص۲۰۶ · https://shamela.ws/book/21712/897
> *"Upon you is Allah's covenant and His pact **that you will believe in Allah and His Messenger, and
> establish the prayer, and pay the zakāt** — and that you will take this pledge from your sons and
> your women. And they would say: yes."*

**(c) The standing patrol order** (الکامل ج۲ ص۲۱۳): give the adhān at every halt; if they answer it,
hold off; then ask about the zakāt; if they acknowledge it, accept from them.

**The answer:** *Three items were asked for, and nothing else — belief, the prayer, the zakāt. Nothing
about the caliph's person, nothing about tribute. The test at every halt was the adhān. This was not
a war about obedience to a ruler; it was about whether the zakāt was part of the dīn, and Abū Bakr ؓ
said it was. That is the whole of it, and it is on the page.*

**And the counterweight the same page supplies, which should be said in the same breath:** at بُزاخة
**no captives were taken** — «**وَلَمْ يُؤْخَذْ مِنْهُمْ سَبْيٌ**؛ لِأَنَّهُمْ كَانُوا قَدْ أَحْرَزُوا حَرِيمَهُمْ،
فَلَمَّا انْهَزَمُوا أَقَرُّوا بِالْإِسْلَامِ خَشْيَةً عَلَى عِيَالَاتِهِمْ، **فَآمَنَهُمْ**» (الکامل ج۲ ص۲۰۶;
ابن خلدون ج۲ ص۴۹۷ agrees).

### 6.6 "Abū Bakr ؓ himself had al-Fujāʾa burned"

**Also on the page, also not to be denied.** الکامل ج۲ ص۲۰۷: «فَلَمَّا قَدِمَ أَمَرَ أَبُو بَكْرٍ أَنْ
تُوقَدَ لَهُ نَارٌ فِي مُصَلَّى الْمَدِينَةِ، ثُمَّ رُمِيَ بِهِ فِيهِ مَقْمُوطًا». البدایہ ج۷ ص۲۷–۲۸ says
**البقيع** rather than the مصلى — **two different places; record both, pick neither.**

**The answer:** *Yes. He had asked the caliph for weapons, was given weapons and a command, and used
them to raid Muslims. The books record the execution and they record that it was by fire. They also
disagree about where it happened. The ruling on the manner of it is a matter for the فقهاء; the event
is what I can give you.* Then stop.

### 6.7 "Al-Ashʿath b. Qays ؓ apostatised — how is he then a Companion?"

**Narrate the whole arc and let it end where the books end it.** الکامل ج۲ ص۲۳۲ ·
https://shamela.ws/book/21712/923; سیر ج۲ ص۳۹–۴۰; ابن خلدون ج۲ ص۴۹۴ in outline.

He came out of النُّجَير with nine men and negotiated a safe-conduct for them and their households in
exchange for opening the gate — «**وَنَسِيَ الْأَشْعَثُ أَنْ يَكْتُبَ نَفْسَهُ**» — and when the document was
read, every man in it was spared and he was not in it. المهاجر ؓ said: «**الْحَمْدُ لِلَّهِ الَّذِي خَطَّأَ
فَاكَ يَا أَشْعَثُ**». The women of his own tribe named him **«عُرْفَ النَّارِ»**, *the name for a betrayer
among them*. At المدينة أبو بكر ؓ told him plainly «فَإِنِّي أَقْتُلُكَ» and answered his plea — «إِنَّمَا
وَجَبَ الصُّلْحُ بَعْدَ خَتْمِ الصَّحِيفَةِ عَلَى مَنْ فِيهَا، وَإِنَّمَا كُنْتَ قَبْلَ ذَلِكَ مُرَاوِضًا» — **and
then pardoned him anyway**, restored his wife فَرْوة بنت أبي قحافة, and he stayed at المدينة.

**The answer:** *He rose, he was taken, he was told to his face that his blood was lawful, and the
caliph pardoned him. That is what the books record. سیر also records his own words afterwards —*
«وإني ارتددتُ» *— he said it about himself. The category the ummah settled on for such men is not
mine to revise from a stage.*

**⚠ الکامل ج۲ ص۲۳۲ carries a second, harsher version («وَقِيلَ») in which he deliberately betrayed his
companions — «وَغَدَرَ بِأَصْحَابِهِ» — and the captives of his own tribe cursed him.** Both versions are
on the same page. **Record both, adjudicate neither.** ⚠ الکامل and ابن خلدون write «أم فروة»; سیر
writes «فروة». **Do not pick.**

### 6.8 "What became of the women and children taken captive?" — the ending almost nobody tells

**A rare question with an entirely positive, entirely sourced answer.** الکامل ج۲ ص۲۳۲–۲۳۳: when
عمر ؓ became caliph he said «**إِنَّهُ لَقَبِيحٌ بِالْعَرَبِ أَنْ يَمْلِكَ بَعْضُهُمْ بَعْضًا**» — *it is an ugly
thing for Arabs to own one another* — consulted on ransoming the Arab captives of the ردة, and set the
ransom at **six or seven camels a head, lighter for حنيفة and كندة because so many of their men had
been killed**; and the women were sought out «فَتَتَبَّعَ النِّسَاءُ بِكُلِّ مَكَانٍ فَقَدُوهُنَّ».

**The answer:** *The war's captives were bought back by the state, on the caliph's own initiative, and
at a lower rate for the tribes that had lost most. That is the end of the story, and it is in the
books.*

## 7 · Session 2 — the households, and the difference between a kinship and a guess

### 7.1 ⚠ "Was not al-Barāʾ b. Mālik ؓ the son of Umm Sulaym ؓ?" — NOT ESTABLISHED

**This is the one kinship in the whole strand with no page behind it, and it is the one a retelling
reaches for first.**

| | |
|---|---|

### 7.2 ⛔ "Tell us the story of al-Rubayyiʿ bint al-Naḍr ؓ — the Book of Allah is retaliation"

**It must not be told, and the reason is worth giving.** Searched البدایہ (30097), سیر (10906) and
الکامل (21712) for «الربيع بنت النضر»، «كتاب الله القصاص»، «لتكسرن ثنية»: **zero hits in the safe
list** except the أحد passage at البدایہ ج۴ ص۲۰۳ and ج۱۷ ص۴۹۷, which mention her only as the sister
who identified her brother's body.

**The answer:** *That report is in Ṣaḥīḥ al-Bukhārī. It is not in any of the four books this course
narrates from, and I have not read it on a page myself — so I will not tell it to you as though I
had. If you want it, take it from the Bukhari page, not from me.*

> ⚠ **Why this one matters more than the others.** It would make a superb bridge — uncle and nephew
> both named in ḥadīths about answered oaths — and **that is exactly the kind of thing that gets
> fabricated.** It stays out until someone reads the Bukhari page. Do not attribute it to البدایہ or
> to سیر.

### 7.3 "Al-Barāʾ ؓ killed thousands — «ألف ألف»"

**Searched 10906, 30097 and 21712 for «البراء» + «ألف ألف»: zero results. Nothing larger than a
hundred exists in the safe list.**

What the sources actually give: **ninety-nine** in single combat, in his own words (سیر ج۱ ص۱۹۸ — but
**the editor grades that chain «إسناده ضعيف لضعف أبي سهل»**); **ninety-odd**, his own words, same
page, a different chain, **«إسناده صحيح»**; **a hundred**, «وَقَدِ اشْتُهِرَ» (سیر ج۱ ص۱۹۶); **a
hundred in single combat at تُسْتَر alone** (البدایہ ج۷ ص۱۹۷ · الکامل ج۲ ص۳۶۸); **ten or more** inside
the garden (البدایہ ج۶ ص۳۹۶).

**The answer:** *The largest figure in these books is a hundred, and even that one al-Dhahabī marks
as "it became well known" rather than as established. The soundest chain gives ninety-odd, in his own
words. Anything larger than that is not in these books.*

### 7.4 "His duʿāʾ ended: and join me with Your Prophet ﷺ"

**Both approved books stop earlier, and the addition must not be made.**

> قال المسلمون للبراء بن مالك - وكان مجابَ الدعوة -: يا براء، أقسم على ربك ليهزمنّهم لنا. فقال: اللهم اهزمهم لنا، واستشهدني.
> — البدایہ والنہایہ ج۷ ص۱۹۷ · https://shamela.ws/book/30097/3343
> *"The Muslims said to al-Barāʾ b. Mālik — and his supplication was answered — Barāʾ, swear upon
> your Lord that He will defeat them for us. He said: O Allah, defeat them for us, **and take me as a
> martyr**."*

> قَالَ الْمُسْلِمُونَ: يَا بَرَاءُ أَقْسِمْ عَلَى رَبِّكَ لَيَهْزِمَنَّهُمْ لَنَا. قَالَ: اللَّهُمَّ اهْزِمْهُمْ لَنَا وَاسْتَشْهِدْنِي، وَكَانَ مُجَابَ الدَّعْوَةِ، فَهَزَمُوهُمْ.
> — الکامل فی التاریخ ج۲ ص۳۶۸ · https://shamela.ws/book/21712/1059

سیر ج۱ ص۱۹۷ stops even earlier — «لَمَّا مَنَحْتَنَا أَكْتَافَهُم، وَذَكَرَ الحَدِيْثَ».

**The answer:** *Two of our books give the whole request and both end at "and take me as a martyr".
The third breaks off before it. The extra clause you have heard is not on any of those pages, so I
did not say it.* **«واستشهدني» is where the sources stop. Nothing may be added after it.**

### 7.5 "Abū Ḥudhayfa ؓ killed his own father at Badr"

**No approved source says it, and this is the version most often retold.** What exists is one line at
سیر ج۱ ص۱۶۵, from **أبو الزناد without a chain**: «أَنَّ أَبَا حُذَيْفَةَ بنَ عُتْبَةَ **دَعَا** يَوْمَ بَدْرٍ
أَبَاهُ إِلَى البرَازِ» — that he **called** his father to single combat. The books then say the father
was met by **عبيدة بن الحارث ؓ** and finished by **حمزة ؓ and علي ؓ** (البدایہ ج۴ ص۶۰), or killed by
**حمزة ؓ** (سیر ج۱ ص۱۷۲). Phrase searches of all three narrative works for «فقتل أباه» and for
«أبو حذيفة» with «قتل أباه» returned **nothing**.

**The sourced sentence, and the only one to use:** *his father and his brother were killed at Badr,
and he was on the other side.*

### 7.6 "Tell the full story of Abū ʿAqīl ؓ — the severed arm, the fourteen wounds"

**The long famous version is not in any approved source.** Searched `+"أبو عقيل" +اليمامة` and
`+جحجبا` across 30097, 10906 and 21712: the only two hits are two short notices, one sentence each.

**What the books give:** the arrow, pulling it out, binding up, the sword, and many wounds found
afterwards. **Not** the night before the battle, **not** the dead arm, **not** the severed limb, **not**
ابن عمر ؓ finding him with fourteen mortal wounds, **not** "who won?".

**The answer:** *The version you have heard is longer than the version in these books. What Ibn Kathīr
and al-Dhahabī give is the arrow, that he pulled it out and bound himself up, that he fought with the
sword, and that many wounds were found on him afterwards. That is the story I have. It is enough.*

### 7.7 "The one cup of water passed between three men — what were their names?"

**The incident is on the page. The names are not.** البدایہ ج۷ ص۹۴ ·
https://shamela.ws/book/30097/3240 gives it from الواقدي and others, and **the pronoun «أنهم» refers
back to the four hundred who had pledged on death with عكرمة ؓ — that is all the page supplies.**
What the page does **not** contain: the names عكرمة ؓ / عياش بن أبي ربيعة ؓ / الحارث بن هشام ؓ attached
to the three cups; the narrator **حذيفة العدوي** searching for his cousin; the detail that he returned
to the first man and found him dead. Searched: `"حذيفة العدوي"` (every hit is a different man),
`"ولم يشربها أحد"` (this page only), `+"شربة" +"اليرموك"`, `+"عكرمة" +"عياش" +"شربة"` — nothing.

**The safe form, and it is the only form:** *Ibn Kathīr records, from al-Wāqidī and others, that when
the men who had pledged on death were struck down they asked for water, and passed the one cup from
hand to hand until every one of them had died and none had drunk. **The books we are using do not
name the three.***

### 7.8 "The ḥadīth «لا تسبوا الأموات فتؤذوا الأحياء», given before ʿIkrima ؓ arrived"

**Exact-phrase search across 30097, 10906 and 21712: zero results. Do not tell it.**

**What the sources give in its place does the same work and is on a page:** the welcome itself — the
Prophet ﷺ springing up and the cloak (البدایہ; see `the-men-who-had-fought-against-it.md` §3.3).

⚠ **And do not replace one invention with another.** Do **not** say "nothing was said to him about his
father" — that is an argument from silence, and it is the door back into the story. **The page records
the welcome, and nothing about what was or was not said.**

### 7.9 "I have heard that Waḥshī ؓ was flogged again and again for wine"

**It exists — البدایہ ج۴ ص۱۸۶, from ابن هشام: «فبلغني أن وحشيًّا لم يزل يُحَدّ في الخمر حتى خُلع من
الديوان» — introduced with «فبلغني», i.e. a بلاغ with no chain at all.** It is recorded in the notes
only so the speaker is not ambushed by it. **It is CUT.**

**The answer:** *Ibn Hishām mentions it as something that reached him, with no chain at all. Ibn Kathīr
records it without endorsing it. We do not build anything on a report of that grade about a
Companion ؓ.* Then stop.

## 8 · The فقہی questions — hand them back, warmly and completely

The register here is the same every time: **the ruling is for the فقهاء; the history is mine.**

### 8.1 ⚠ "What does the ḥadīth of رضاعة الكبير in Sālim's ؓ household mean?"

**⚠ SPEAKER'S DISCRETION — and the recommendation is to leave the whole matter out of the session.**
It is a live مسئلہ among the fuqahāʾ, the evening does not need it, and the room does not expect it.
Working: `docs/research/abu-hudhayfa-and-salim-mawla-abi-hudhayfa.md` §6.

**If a written slip asks, the answer has exactly four clauses and then it stops:**

1. **The report is in صحیح مسلم** — the سیر editor's own footnote on the page gives it as
   **مسلم (١٤٥٣)(٢٨)، «كتاب الرضاع: باب رضاعة الكبير»**, connected through أحمد, with النسائي،
   ابن ماجه، أبو داود، عبد الرزاق and مالك by several routes (سیر ج۱ ص۱۶۷).
2. **The Prophet's ﷺ wives themselves held it a dispensation specific to سالم ؓ**, as أم سلمة ؓ
   reports:

> إِنَّمَا هِيَ رُخْصَةٌ لِسَالِمٍ خَاصَّةً.
> — سیر أعلام النبلاء ج۱ ص۱۶۷ · https://shamela.ws/book/10906/1593
> *"It is only a dispensation for Sālim in particular."*

3. **الذہبی رحمہ اللہ records the majority of the scholars taking it that way:**

> وَخُصَّا بِذَاكَ الحُكْمِ عِنْدَ جُمْهُوْرِ العُلَمَاءِ.
> — سیر أعلام النبلاء ج۱ ص۱۶۵ · https://shamela.ws/book/10906/1591
> *"The two of them were singled out by that ruling, in the view of the majority of the scholars."*

4. **And then: the ruling itself is a matter for the فقهاء and not for this lecture.**

**⛔ Do not adjudicate. Do not name schools. Do not go further. Do not put any of this on a slide.**

**What may safely be told from the platform is the un-contested half:** that the verse
﴾ادْعُوهُمْ لِآبَائِهِمْ﴿ [الأحزاب: ٥] ended adoption as a legal fact, that سالم ؓ therefore could no
longer be «ابن أبي حذيفة» — **and that the household kept him anyway**, by marriage and by every other
means available to it (البدایہ ج۷ ص۵۰ · https://shamela.ws/book/30097/3196). That is the عبرت, and it
needs none of the فقہ.

### 8.2 "Why did ʿUmar ؓ refuse to divide the land of al-Sawād? Which verse did he argue from?"

**A slide may say he refused to divide the land and may give الکامل's reasons. A slide may NOT quote a
verse in his mouth on this question — because no page fetched carries one.**

**And البدایہ says in so many words that it declined to treat the question at all:**

> وقد تكلم ابن جرير هاهنا فيما رواه عن سيف على ما يتعلّق بأرض السواد وخراجها، **وموضع تحرير ذلك كتاب "الأحكام".**
> — البدایہ والنہایہ ج۷ ص۱۷۷ · https://shamela.ws/book/30097/3323
> *"Ibn Jarīr spoke here … about what pertains to the land of al-Sawād and its land tax; but the place
> for settling that is the book of al-Aḥkām."*

ابن کثیر makes the same referral for the شروط العمرية on the Christians of الشام (ج۷ ص۱۶۲). **البدایہ
deliberately routes the fiqh of conquered land out of the history and into a legal work we do not
hold.**

**The answer:** *He refused to divide it, and Ibn al-Athīr gives his practical and political reasons.
The argument from a verse is in the fiqh literature, not on the history page — and Ibn Kathīr says
plainly that the place to settle that question is a book of aḥkām, not his history. So that is where I
leave it.*

### 8.3 ⛔ "In the famine, the man who came to the Prophet's ﷺ grave and saw a dream — that report?"

**Do not go near it, in either direction.** Both البدایہ ج۷ ص۲۰۴ and الکامل ج۲ ص۳۷۵ carry the story of
the man of مزينة who called out «يا محمداه» and saw the Prophet ﷺ in a dream. **البدایہ's own printed
footnotes on that page condemn it twice** — once for the invocation itself, and once because the
identification with بلال بن الحارث ؓ is «من رواية سيف وهو متروك». The parallel **مالك الدار** report at
ج۷ ص۲۰۵, though ابن کثیر calls the chain صحيح, carries a printed footnote that **مالك الدار is
مجهول** and the story therefore weak.

**The answer:** *That report is doctrinally contested and its chains are contested too — the printed
edition's own footnotes speak against it on that same page. It is not a matter I am equipped to settle
from a stage, and this course does not narrate it. The famine material I did give you is the oath, the
relief convoys and the prayer for rain, and all of it is on the page.*

**The safe عام الرمادة material, for the same slip:** the oath (no سمن, nothing fat, until the people
were relieved — سیر جراشدون ص۸۶; البدایہ ج۷ ص۲۰۲–۲۰۶); أبو عبيدة ؓ's four thousand mounts of food
(الکامل ج۲ ص۳۷۵); and the استسقاء through العباس ؓ, **which البدایہ ج۷ ص۲۰۵ attributes to البخاري by
number on the page** (البخاري ۳۷۱۰, per the printed edition's own footnote) ·
https://shamela.ws/book/30097/3351.

## 9 · Sessions 2–3 — عمر ؓ's caliphate: the questions with an edge

### 9.1 "Why did ʿUmar ؓ dismiss Khālid ؓ? Was there enmity between them?"

**Handling rule (`iraq-syria-and-yarmuk-12-15ah.md` §5): both men are Companions; the sources record
عمر ؓ's reason in his own words, in writing, twice, and it is not a charge of misconduct.**

**First — separate the two removals. They are four years apart and they get run together.**

| | When | Scope | Reason the sources give |
|---|---|---|---|
| **First** | On عمر ؓ's accession, **13 AH** | Removed from **command of Syria**; أبو عبيدة ؓ made amīr; خالد ؓ stayed and fought on under him | ابن إسحاق: «لكلام بلغه عنه، ولما كان من أمر مالك بن نُويرة» (البدایہ ج۷ ص۱۰۳) · ابن عون: so that people would know God gives the victory (سیر ج۱ ص۳۷۸) |
| **Second** | **17 AH** | Removed from **قنسرين** and from any command after it | A gift of ten thousand to الأشعث ؓ out of the campaign's spoil; عمر ؓ had it audited (البدایہ ج۷ ص۱۹۰–۱۹۱; الکامل ج۲ ص۳۵۹–۳۶۰) |

**Second — put عمر ؓ's own written reason on the record, because it answers the question directly:**

> إِنِّي لَمْ أَعْزِلْ خَالِدًا عَنْ سُخْطَةٍ وَلَا خِيَانَةٍ، **وَلَكِنَّ النَّاسَ فَخَّمُوهُ وَفُتِنُوا بِهِ، فَخِفْتُ أَنْ يُوكَلُوا إِلَيْهِ، فَأَحْبَبْتُ أَنْ يَعْلَمُوا أَنَّ اللَّهَ هُوَ الصَّانِعُ، وَأَنْ لَا يَكُونُوا بِعَرَضِ فِتْنَةٍ.**
> — الکامل فی التاریخ ج۲ ص۳۶۰ · https://shamela.ws/book/21712/1051
> *"I did not remove Khālid out of displeasure, nor for any dishonesty — **but the people magnified him
> and were entranced by him, and I feared they would be left to rely on him. So I wanted them to know
> that God is the Doer, and that they should not stand exposed to a trial.**"*

> لأَنْزِعَنَّ خَالِداً، حَتَّى يُعْلَمَ أَنَّ اللهَ إِنَّمَا يَنْصُرُ دِيْنَهُ.
> — سیر أعلام النبلاء ج۱ ص۳۷۸ · https://shamela.ws/book/10906/1804
> *"I shall certainly remove Khālid, so that it may be known that it is God who gives His religion the
> victory."*

**Third — خالد ؓ's answer, in one sentence:** «**سمعًا وطاعة لأمير المؤمنين**» (البدایہ ج۷ ص۱۰۳ ·
https://shamela.ws/book/30097/3249), and in الکامل ج۲ ص۳۶۰: «**نَسْمَعُ وَنُطِيعُ لِوُلَاتِنَا وَنُفَخِّمُ
وَنَخْدِمُ مَوَالِيَنَا**».

**Fourth — the two sentences that close the subject.** عمر ؓ, later, when the reports from قنسرين
reached المدینہ:

> يرحمُ الله أبا بكر، **كان أعلمَ بالرجالِ مني**، واللّهِ إنِّي **لم أعزلْهُ عن ريبةٍ** ولكن خشيتُ أن يُوكلَ الناسُ إليه.
> — البدایہ والنہایہ ج۷ ص۱۵۳ · https://shamela.ws/book/30097/3299
> *"May God have mercy on Abū Bakr — **he knew men better than I do**. By God, **I did not remove him
> for any suspicion**, but I feared that people would come to rely on him."*

And after the audit, سیر ج۱ ص۳۸۰: «**يَا خَالِدُ! وَاللهِ إِنَّكَ لَكَرِيْمٌ عَلَيَّ، وَإِنَّكَ لَحَبِيْبٌ
إِلَيَّ**، وَلَنْ تُعَاتِبَنِي بَعْدَ اليَوْمِ عَلَى شَيْءٍ.»

**And the handover itself is the material that makes the whole affair safe to tell in this room** —
each man quoting a ḥadīth in the other's praise (البدایہ ج۷ ص۲۳۶ · https://shamela.ws/book/30097/3382,
sourced there to مسند أحمد ٤/٩٠, graded **حسن** by the printed edition's editor): خالد ؓ — «**أمينُ هذه
الأمة أبو عبيدة بن الجراح**»; أبو عبيدة ؓ — «**خالدٌ سيفٌ من سيوف الله، نِعْمَ فَتى العشيرة**».

⚠ **Note the disagreement and do not resolve it:** البدایہ ج۷ ص۱۰۳ gives the مالك بن نويرة affair as a
cause; سیر جراشدون ص۹۸ gives it too; البدایہ ج۷ ص۱۹۰ gives the الأشعث ؓ payment. **Two different causes
inside the safe list, for two different removals. State the dismissal, quote «سمعًا وطاعةً», and stop.**

### 9.2 ⛔ "I have heard Khālid ؓ broke ʿUmar's ؓ leg when they were boys, and the enmity began there"

**This is the single likeliest thing a hostile question will be built from, and it has a one-line
answer.** The report is at البدایہ ج۷ ص۲۳۸ — «وكان ذلك سببَ العداوة بينهما» — transmitted through
**مجالد بن سعيد**, whom **Ibn Kathīr's own editor marks ضعيف on that very page**.

**The answer:** *The book carries it, and the printed edition's own footnote marks its narrator weak.
Against it stands the caliph's written reason, which is on the record twice, and his own words that he
did not remove him for any suspicion. I will take the written reason over a weak report.*

**Do not use the story.** Do not repeat it from the platform even to refute it, unless asked.

### 9.3 "Did anyone object to the dismissal, to ʿUmar's ؓ face?" — the answer is yes, and it is safe

**A Companion objected publicly, to عمر ؓ's face, at الجابية, and the report has a sound chain**
(البدایہ ج۷ ص۲۳۸; the page names أحمد ٣/٤٧٥ and البخاري, التاريخ الأوسط ١/٥٧; سیر ج۱ ص۳۷۹ has it
shorter).

**Tell it in this order and no other:** عمر ؓ's own stated ground **first** — «أمرتُه أن يحبسَ هذا المال
على ضعَفَةِ المهاجرين، فأعطاه ذا البأس، وذا الشرف واللسان» — then the objection of
**أبو عمرو بن حفص بن المغيرة ؓ**: «**ما اعتذرت يا عمر، لقد نزعتَ عاملًا استعمله رسول الله ﷺ، ووضعتَ
لواء رفعه رسول الله ﷺ، وأغمدت سيفًا سلَّه الله**» — then عمر ؓ's reply: «**إنك قريبُ القرابة، حديثُ
السن، مغضبٌ في ابن عمك**».

⚠ **The page carries a further clause of the objection — «ولقد قطعتَ الرحمَ وحسدتَ ابنَ العم» — which is
NOT for delivery.** Stop where the sources' usable part stops.

**Why this is worth having ready:** it shows a caliph being argued with in the open, by name, and the
argument preserved by the historians rather than tidied away. **It must be told with عمر ؓ's reason
first.**

### 9.4 "Did Abū Luʾluʾa act alone, or was it a conspiracy?"

**The sources do not establish a conspiracy; they record an allegation and one piece of circumstantial
testimony. Say no more than that, and only if asked.**

البدایہ ج۷ ص۲۸۴ reports only that «**قد قيل** إنهما مالأا أبا لؤلؤة على قتل عمر … **فالله أعلم**» — that
it *had been said* that الهرمزان and جفينة conspired with him — and ابن کثیر closes with *and Allah
knows best*. سیر ص۱۴۱ carries عبد الرحمن بن أبي بكر ؓ's report of having seen the three together with
the dagger.

**The answer:** *The books record that it was said, and one man's testimony that he had seen them
together. Ibn Kathīr writes "and Allah knows best" and leaves it. So do I.*

⚠ **X.1 caution, and it matters because the sentence is famous:** the conditional in the burial
request — that if أم المؤمنين عائشة ؓ should change her mind he was to be carried to the common
graveyard of the Muslims — **is not on any of the 100+ cached pages of 30097, 10906 or 21712.**
سیر ص۹۱ and الکامل ج۲ ص۴۲۸ carry the **request**, and سیر ص۹۱ carries عائشة ؓ's **answer**; neither
carries the conditional. **Do not put it on a slide or in a script.** `(to verify)` — it would have to
be sourced directly from a printed صحیح البخاری and logged separately.

### 9.5 ⛔ "What happened to Abū Luʾluʾa afterwards? His shrine?"

**Entirely outside the safe list, and not looked for.**

**The answer, and it is short:** *The four books this course uses record that he killed himself in the
mosque, and nothing further. Everything attached to it after that is a later dispute, and I am not
going to conduct it.* Then move on. **Do not elaborate.**

### 9.6 ⚠ "What happened in the shūrā? Was ʿAlī ؓ wronged?"

**Session 3, under اصول — line ۲ of §0, and nothing more from the floor.**

`docs/research/nahawand-the-shura-and-the-death-of-umar.md` §7.7 records that الکامل ج۲ ص۴۴۱–۴۴۵ carries
a long strand of partisan speech around the شورى, and **none of it belongs in session 2**. Nor do
عمر ؓ's assessments of the six (الکامل ج۲ ص۴۴۱) — that is the doorway to the مشاجرات.

**What may safely be said, and it is enough:** the affair was left to six men **«ممن تُوفي رسولُ الله ﷺ
وهو عنهم راضٍ»**; عمر ؓ refused to name one of them — «لا أتحمّل أمرهم حيًا وميتًا»; and
**سعيد بن زيد ؓ was deliberately left out because he was عمر ؓ's own cousin** and he feared the
relationship would be indulged (البدایہ ج۷ ص۲۸۰). Then defer.

**And if pressed, ابن کثیر supplies the guardrail himself, which is far better than the speaker
supplying it** (البدایہ ج۷ ص۲۸۳ · https://shamela.ws/book/30097/3429): after summarising the reports
that علی ؓ accused عبد الرحمن ؓ of a stitch-up, he writes —

> إلى غير ذلك من الأخبار المخالفة لما ثبت في الصحاح فهي مردودة على قائليها وناقليها، والله أعلم.
> *"…and other than that of the reports contradicting what is established in the authentic
> collections — they are rejected, back upon those who said them and those who transmitted them; and
> God knows best."*

**The point that makes for the room, and the speaker should make no other:** *a report existing in a
history book is not the same as a report being established. The historian himself sorts — and here you
can watch him reject.* **Do not read the remainder of that passage aloud; it names groups, and this
room does not need it.**

## 11 · The famous sayings of عمر ؓ that are not where people think they are

### 11.1 "If an animal perished on the bank of the Euphrates — where is that saying?"

**It is real, it is in الکامل — and two things must be said with it, both of them on the page.**

> قِيلَ: خَطَبَ عُمَرُ النَّاسَ فَقَالَ: وَالَّذِي بَعَثَ مُحَمَّدًا - صَلَّى اللَّهُ عَلَيْهِ وَسَلَّمَ - بِالْحَقِّ لَوْ أَنَّ جَمَلًا هَلَكَ ضَيَاعًا بِشَطِّ الْفُرَاتِ لَخَشِيتُ أَنْ يَسْأَلَنِي اللَّهُ عَنْهُ
> — الکامل فی التاریخ ج۲ ص۴۳۳ · https://shamela.ws/book/21712/1123
> *"ʿUmar addressed the people and said: By Him who sent Muḥammad ﷺ with the truth — if a **camel**
> perished, lost, on the bank of the Euphrates, I would fear that God would question me about it."*

**First:** ابن الأثير introduces it with **«قِيلَ»** — *it is said* — **with no chain and no named
authority.** Introduce it as *"the books relate"*, never as *"he said"*. **Second: the animal is a
camel (جَمَل), not a mule, and not "an animal" in general**, and the verb is «هَلَكَ ضَيَاعًا».

**Searched and not found:** «بغلة» in this saying; any wording putting the phrase in the first person
as a direct address to a governor. `+"بغلة"`, `+"عثرت"`, `+"سائلي عن"`, `+"لسألني الله"` across the
whole cache — no hit. **Do not use those forms.**

**The stronger substitute:** the basket of fish, سیر (راشدون) ص۸۲ — it makes the same point, about an
animal and about answerability, **in عمر ؓ's own recorded words with a named chain.**

### 11.2 "Read us ʿUmar's ؓ letter on judgement to Abū Mūsā ؓ"

فريضة»، «آس بين الناس» across the whole cache. **Zero hits.**

### 11.3 "Since when did you enslave people, when their mothers bore them free?"

Searched for «استعبدتم» / «اسْتَعْبَدْتُم» across the entire cache of all six Shamela books held.

### 11.4 "Khālid ؓ fought a hundred battles"

**The number is not in the source.** البدایہ ج۷ ص۲۳۷ and سیر ج۱ ص۳۸۲ both read «**كذا وكذا زحفًا**» —

على فراشي».

## 12 · «کیسے پتا چلا؟» — the method questions, and how to turn them

These are not hostile; they are the best questions the room can ask, and each has a page behind it.

### 12.1 ⭐ "Why do you not use Ibn Khaldūn?" — the finest answer in the bank

**Because on his own pages he gives three different men as the killer of الحُطَم.** ابن خلدون
ج۲ ص۵۰۴ and ج۲ ص۵۰۶: **قيس بن عاصم**; **جابر بن بحير**; and «**قَتَلَهُ زَيْدٌ وَسُمَيْفَعُ**».

**And the approved narrative books do not have that problem here:** البدایہ ج۷ ص۳۹ has خالد ؓ's man cut
the leg and **قيس بن عاصم** finish him — and then قيس sees the severed leg and says «**واسوأتاه، لو
أعلمُ ما به لم أحرِّكْهُ**» (https://shamela.ws/book/30097/3185). الکامل ج۲ ص۲۲۴ names the man who cut
the leg, **عفيف بن المنذر التيمي**, and confirms قيس killed him.

**The answer:** *Ibn Khaldūn رحمہ اللہ is a great man and I use him — for how to read an event, never
for a fact. Here is why: on two of his own pages he gives three different men as the killer of one man.
That is not a scandal; it is what happens when a historian is summarising rather than sifting. So I
take my names, dates, numbers and wordings from the books that sift, and I take my explanations from
him — and I say his name when I do.*

**That is `DECISIONS.md` #29 in one anecdote, and it is worth having word-perfect.**

⚠ **Two more limits on him, for the same slip:** the printed edition we hold is corrupt at the level of
the word in places (ج۲ ص۴۹۵ prints «بالمعروب» for «بالمعروف» and «وطنماذ», which is not a word) — **so
he cannot be the authority for a wording.** And the famous image «والمسلمون كالغنم في الليلة الممطرة»
at ج۲ ص۴۸۹ **is not his**: البدایہ ج۵ ص۳۹۷ carries it from **ابن إسحاق** ·
https://shamela.ws/book/30097/2581. **Never credit that image to ابن خلدون.**

### 12.2 "Why do you not accept al-Ṭabarī?"

**Do not say "we don't accept al-Ṭabarī".** Say what the course's own مقدمہ says.

**The answer:** *al-Ṭabarī collects with chains and does not sift — his own preface says so. Presence
in al-Ṭabarī is not authenticity (this is the muqaddima's own point, p.۷۱). So this course uses him
as corroboration and never alone, and never for anything touching the disputes among the Companions ؓ.
Where a report reaches Ibn Kathīr or Ibn al-Athīr through al-Ṭabarī or through Sayf b. ʿUmar, the
printed editions say so in their own footnotes, and I say so too.*

**The concrete instances the room may catch, so the speaker is not surprised:**
- **غَمْرة، النُّقْرة, ظَفَر** as engagements of خالد ؓ's march: **a full-text grep of every cached page
  of البدایہ, الکامل and ابن خلدون returns nothing for any of the three.** They are طبری / سيف material.
  **They are not on our map, deliberately.**
- **The commanders' standing commission**: البدایہ and الکامل both say each amīr got one and
  **neither prints its text**. Only ابن خلدون prints it (ج۲ ص۴۹۵) — **so it may not be quoted.**
- **The البدایہ ج۷ ص۸ second inaugural خطبہ**, transmitted by **سيف بن عمر** — see §1.11.
- **The 120,000 Byzantine dead at الواقوصة** (البدایہ ج۷ ص۹۶) is attributed by ابن کثیر to **ابن جرير**
  — a طبری number at one remove. **It does not go on a slide.**

### 12.3 "Why do the numbers not agree with each other?"

**The best question of the evening. The answer is one sentence and then two examples.**

**The sentence:** *These figures were transmitted, not audited. Nobody counted; the numbers travelled
with the story. And the books preserve their disagreements instead of smoothing them — which is a
discipline, not a defect.*

**The examples, both on one page each:**
- ابن کثیر prints, in a single sentence, «فكان جملة من قُتلوا في الحديقة وفي المعركة قريبًا من عشرة آلاف مقاتل، وقيل:
  أحد وعشرون ألفًا، وقتل من المسلمين ستمئة، وقيل: خمسمئة، **فالله أعلم**» (البدایہ ج۷ ص۳۵ · https://shamela.ws/book/30097/3181).
  **He gives the figure and then declines to certify it.** A speaker who does the same is doing exactly
  what the source does.
- At القادسية **ابن کثیر prints four different sizes for the Muslim army within eight pages**: 30,000, "and it is said
  36,000" (ج۷ ص۱۳۰), «perhaps no more than 7,000 or 8,000» from أبو وائل who was there (ج۷ ص۱۳۵),
  «between 7,000 and 8,000» from ابن إسحاق (ج۷ ص۱۳۹), «6,000 or 7,000» from قيس بن أبي حازم who was
  also there (ج۷ ص۱۴۲). **What is not in doubt is that the Muslims were the smaller army.**

⚠ **Two figures that must never be spoken as facts:** the 100,000 at البويب, which البدایہ introduces
with **«فيُقال»**; and the 100,000 at جلولاء, which is offered as a **folk etymology of the place-name**
(جلَّلوا وجه الأرض بالقتلى → جلولاء) — a literary device, not a count.

### 12.4 ⭐ "But your books were written centuries after the events"

**Answer it with a historian correcting himself in public.** سیر ج۱ ص۵۵۵–۵۵۶: on سلمان ؓ's age
الذہبی رحمہ اللہ writes — *in this order on the page, and do not reverse it* — that he had stated in
his own *Tārīkh al-Kabīr* that سلمان ؓ lived two hundred and fifty years, **«وأنا الساعةَ لا أرتضي ذلك
ولا أصحّحُه»** — *and at this moment I do not accept that and do not hold it sound* — and he reckons him
instead a man in his eighties.

**The answer:** *Yes, and that is exactly why they are usable. These men tell you where they got it,
they print the disagreement, they grade the chain, and al-Dhahabī رحمہ اللہ here retracts his own
earlier book on a page you can open. Seven hundred years ago. That is what a discipline looks like.*

### 12.5 "ʿAbd Allāh b. Ḥadhf — was that one man or two?"

**A real ambiguity, not a slip, and the honest answer is the whole answer.** The same name stands in
two different places:

- **Inside جواثى, among the besieged**, as the man who composed four lines of verse — «وقد قال رجل منهم
  يقال له عبد الله بن حذف، **أحد بني بكر بن كلاب**، وقد اشتد عليه الجوع» (البدایہ ج۷ ص۳۸ ·
  https://shamela.ws/book/30097/3184).
- **In العلاء ؓ's camp at هَجَر**, as the volunteer scout — البدایہ ج۷ ص۳۹
  (https://shamela.ws/book/30097/3185) and الکامل ج۲ ص۲۲۳ (https://shamela.ws/book/21712/914), the
  latter adding that **his mother was of عِجْل**.

If it is one man the sequence is coherent — besieged and starving in جواثى, the province relieved, then
with the army at هَجَر — **and hunger appears in both scenes**, which is suggestive. But **no page joins
them, and البدایہ gives a كلابي paternal line while الکامل gives a عِجْلي maternal one** — which is not
a contradiction, and is not a confirmation either.

**The answer:** *The books name him twice, in two different places, and they never tell us whether it is
one person. I told you both scenes as they stand. I am not going to join them for you, because the
books do not.*

⚠ **Do not assert from the platform that they are the same man.**

⚠ **And the related one, from the same night:** *why were they drinking?* **No page says.** Neither book
gives an occasion — no feast, no festival. البدایہ ج۷ ص۳۹ has only «فوجدهم سُكارى لا يعقلون من الشراب».
What the pages **do** supply is the setting: a **month** of trench stalemate (الکامل ج۲ ص۲۲۳). **State
the setting; do not supply a reason the books withhold.**
