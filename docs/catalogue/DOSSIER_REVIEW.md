===== OVERALL =====
Read as a whole, this does read as an organised and unusually self-aware course. The fixed 45-minute shape holds in all ten evenings, the timings add up, the map is a real teaching instrument rather than decoration, and the safety thinking is structural rather than improvised: the fiqh guardrail is installed before the disputed material, the کربلا block is bounded and read from the page, ۱۹۲۴ and ۱۹۴۷ have announced boundaries, the will_not_be_said lists are specific enough to be checked against a recording, and the plans repeatedly refuse numbers, refuse verdicts and refuse modern analogies. Session 4's handling of «عام الجماعۃ» — refusing to print a year's name as a man's title — is the kind of discipline that should reassure a manager. What a manager should not be reassured by is the sourcing. Six of the ten evenings are written against volumes nobody has read, and in several places the drafting has quietly filled that vacuum: a scene composed at a railway platform in session 7, an Arabic saying set into the plan in session 4, an Urdu quotation drafted for امام علی بن المدینیؒ in session 5, a quoted phrase attributed to a book not in hand in session 2, a named forgery in session 1 that session 3 treats as unknown, a command arrangement asserted in session 6, and a count of مصحف copies built into a map artifact in session 3. Each is small; together they are the exact failure the course exists to avoid, and every one of them reaches the page under the speaker's name.
