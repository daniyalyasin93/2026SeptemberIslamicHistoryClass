# PEOPLE — the master تعارف register

Thirty profiles across ten sessions, four lines each, in the classical تراجم format. **Reused
verbatim across artifacts** so no two ever disagree.

Format: `نام و نسب` (with the لقب **the tradition gave** — never invented) · `سنین` ·
what they did in one sentence · **`ایک واقعہ`** — one authentic incident, cited.

**No invented لقب. No emblems. Honorifics always.** A field stays empty until it is read off a page.
