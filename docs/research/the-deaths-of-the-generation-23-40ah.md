# The generation thins — who died, year by year, 23–40 AH

**Question this note answers:** البدایہ والنہایہ files an obituary register under most years. Going
through those registers for 23–40 AH, who among the Companions and the notables died, in what year,
and what does the book actually say about each one? The thread this note exists to supply for
session 3 is that the generation which had seen the Prophet ﷺ was visibly thinning across exactly
the years in which the fitna arrived — so the note gives the names, the years, the one-line notice
for each, and the notices rich enough to be told as a scene. It also records, explicitly, every
place where the book leaves the year of a death undecided, and every place where ابن کثیر رحمہ اللہ
refuses a report, grades it weak, or says the truth is not known.

## 0. The sentences this note licenses

Each of these may now go on a slide, with the printed page given.

1. **البدایہ does not file an obituary register under every year of this window.** In 23–40 AH it
   files one under **23 AH** (ج۷ ص۲۷۴ ff.), **30 AH** (a فصل, ص۲۹۴), **32 AH** (ص۳۰۱), a **fourth,
   undated register for the whole of عثمان ؓ's rule** (ص۳۸۸), **37 AH** (ص۵۲۱), **38 AH** (ص۵۳۲) and
   **39 AH** (ص۵۳۷). For **24–29, 31, 33–36 and 40 AH there is no obituary chapter at all**
   `[SOURCED]` — `sources/shamela/30097/_toc.tsv`, rows 3420–3684, cross-checked against the pages.
2. **ابن کثیر states that the 23 AH register and the 37 AH register are both taken from الذهبي's
   تاريخ الإسلام** `[SOURCED]` — ج۷ ص۲۷۴ («وقال شيخنا أبو عبد الله الذهبي في "تاريخه" … في سنة ثلاث
   وعشرين»); ج۷ ص۵۲۱ footnote (١) («هذا الفصل نقله المصنف من تاريخ الإسلام للذهبي»). The undated
   register at ص۳۸۸ says so in its own chapter title.
3. **There is a whole class of Companions whose year of death the tradition simply does not fix, and
   the book says so in a chapter heading**: «فصل في ذكر من توفي في زمان دولة عثمان ممن لا يعرف وقت
   وفاته على التعيين» `[SOURCED]` — ج۷ ص۳۸۸.
4. **أبي بن كعب ؓ is filed under 30 AH on الواقدي's authority, and the printed edition's own footnote
   records four other years for him — 19, 20, 22 and 32** `[SOURCED]` — ج۷ ص۲۹۴ and its footnote (٧).
5. **العباس ؓ, the Prophet's ﷺ uncle, died in 32 AH at eighty-eight; عثمان ؓ prayed over him and he

   رمضان, without settling either** `[SOURCED]` — ج۷ ص۳۰۳.

   `[SOURCED]` — ج۷ ص۳۰۲.

   stipend on his deathbed; the book gives the dialogue with عثمان ؓ in full** `[SOURCED]` — ج۷ ص۳۰۵.
8. **Who prayed the funeral prayer over ابن مسعود ؓ is not known: the book gives الزبير ؓ (by his own

**What this means for the evening.** The register is not a census. It is what الذهبي رحمہ اللہ had
in front of him, copied by ابن کثیر رحمہ اللہ — ص۵۲۱ footnote (١) states it plainly of the 37 AH
list: «هذا الفصل نقله المصنف من تاريخ الإسلام للذهبي». A Companion missing from a given year's
register is not a Companion who did not die that year.

## 2. The last register of عمر ؓ's caliphate — 23 AH

### 2.1 قتادة بن النعمان ؓ — the eye that was put back

The 23 AH register opens with him (ج۷ ص۲۷۴):

> ثم ذكر وفاة من مات فيها. فمنهم قَتَادة بن النُّعْمان الأنْصاري الأوْسي الظفري أخو أبي سعيد الخُدري لأمه، وقتادة أكبر منه، شهد بدرًا وأصيبتْ عينُه في يوم أُحد حتى وقعت على خدّه فردَّها رسولُ الله ﷺ فصارت أحسنَ عينيه، وكان من الرُّماة المذكورين، وكان على مُقَدّمة عمر حين قدم إلى الشام. توفي في هذه السنة على المشهور عن خمس وستين سنة، ونزل عمر في قبره، وقيل إنه توفي في التي قبلها.
> — البدایہ والنہایہ ج۷ ص۲۷۴ · https://shamela.ws/book/30097/3420
> *English:* "Then he mentioned the deaths of those who died in it. Among them: قتادة بن النعمان الأنصاري الأوسي الظفري, the maternal brother of أبو سعيد الخدري — and قتادة was the elder of the two. He was present at بدر, and his eye was struck at أحد until it fell upon his cheek, and the Messenger of Allah ﷺ put it back, and it became the better of his two eyes. He was among the noted archers, and he was over عمر's vanguard when he came to the Shām. He died in this year, by the better-known account, at sixty-five, and عمر went down into his grave — and it is said he died in the year before it."

Three things a room can hold: the eye; **عمر ؓ himself climbing down into the grave**; and the fact
that even for a man this prominent the book carries two years and settles on «على المشهور».
`[SOURCED]`

### 2.2 The roll that follows — «ذكر من توفي في خلافة عمر بن الخطاب ﵁»

ابن کثیر then says that الذهبي wrote at length on عمر ؓ himself and moves straight into the register
(ج۷ ص۲۷۴):

> ثم قال: ذكر من توفي في خلافة عمر بن الخطاب ﵁:
> — البدایہ والنہایہ ج۷ ص۲۷۴ · https://shamela.ws/book/30097/3420
> *English:* "Then he said: an account of those who died during the caliphate of عمر بن الخطاب ﵁."

The names, in the book's own order, each with the one line the book gives — ج۷ ص۲۷۴–۲۷۹,

### 2.3 أم المؤمنين سودة بنت زمعة ؓ — the day she gave away

The register ends with two women (ج۷ ص۲۷۹):

> سَوْدَة بنت زَمْعَة (٣) القرشية العامرية أم المؤمنين، أول من دخل بها رسول الله ﷺ بعد خديجة ﵂، وكانت صَوَّامةً قَوَّامة
> — البدایہ والنہایہ ج۷ ص۲۷۹ · https://shamela.ws/book/30097/3425
> *English:* "سودة بنت زمعة القرشية العامرية, Mother of the Believers, the first the Messenger of Allah ﷺ took after خديجة ﵂; and she was much given to fasting and to standing in prayer."

The notice then gives her request to keep her place by assigning her day to عائشة ؓ, the verse of
النساء 128 revealed — عائشة ؓ says — concerning her, and closes: «توفيت في خلافة عمر بن الخطاب».
`[SOURCED]` — ص۲۷۹

### 2.4 هند بنت عتبة — a death the book will not date

The very last line of the register (ج۷ ص۲۷۹):

> هند بنت عتبةُ (٤) يقال: ماتت في خلافة عمر وقيل توفيت قبل ذلك كما تقدم فالله أعلم (٥).
> — البدایہ والنہایہ ج۷ ص۲۷۹ · https://shamela.ws/book/30097/3425
> *English:* "هند بنت عتبة: it is said she died in the caliphate of عمر, and it is said she died before that, as has preceded — and Allah knows best."

`[SOURCED]`. The (٥) is a footnote marker; the editor's note there is about where a manuscript part
ends, not about the date.

## 3. 24 AH — the first year of عثمان ؓ, and the deaths inside it

البدایہ files **no obituary chapter** for 24 AH. Two sets of deaths are nonetheless recorded inside
the year's narrative.

### 3.1 The killings by عبيد الله بن عمر — the first case brought to عثمان ؓ

(ج۷ ص۲۸۴. The chapter heading on this page is printed «خلافة أمير المؤمنين عثمان بن عفان ﵁ ثي
استهلت سنة أربع وعشرين» — **«ثي» is a Shamela typo**; do not reproduce the heading from the text
file without a page image.)

> وأما أول حكومة حكم فيها فقضية عبيد الله بن عمر، وذلك أنه غدا على ابنة أبي لؤلؤة قاتل عمر فقتلها، وضرب (رجلًا) نصرانيًا يقال له جفينة بالسيف فقتله، وضرب الهرمزان الذي كان صاحب تُسْتر فقتله، وكان قد قيل إنهما مالأا أبا لؤلؤة على قتل عمر، فالله أعلم.
> — البدایہ والنہایہ ج۷ ص۲۸۴ · https://shamela.ws/book/30097/3430
> *English:* "As for the first case he judged, it was the affair of عبيد الله بن عمر: he went out in the morning against the daughter of أبو لؤلؤة, the killer of عمر, and killed her; and he struck a Christian man called جفينة with the sword and killed him; and he struck الهرمزان, who had been the lord of تستر, and killed him — and it had been said that those two had conspired with أبو لؤلؤة over the killing of عمر, and Allah knows best."

The book records that عمر ؓ had ordered عبيد الله imprisoned so that the next caliph could judge him;
that **علي ؓ said «ما من العدل تركه» and ordered his execution**; that others objected; that عمرو بن
العاص counselled otherwise; and that **عثمان ؓ paid the blood-money for the dead out of his own
property and released him** `[SOURCED]` — ص۲۸۴–۲۸۵. The book then gives, in verse, زياد بن لبيد
البياضي's public reproach of عبيد الله («أصَبْتَ دمًا واللهِ في غيرِ حِلّهِ») and عثمان ؓ's rebuke of
زياد for it — ص۲۸۵.

> **Guardrail.** This is a disagreement between Companions and the book does not resolve it. Narrate
> exactly the four moves — the killings, علي ؓ's view, the objection, عثمان ؓ's payment and release —
> attribute each to the man who made it, and stop. **Do not draw the inference.** See §Not for the hall.

### 3.2 سراقة بن مالك بن جعشم ؓ — 24 AH

The one dated death notice of the year (ج۷ ص۲۸۷). It is filed under the words «وفيها توفي:» and it
is the last page of 24 AH before 25 AH opens:

> سُراقة بن مالك بن جُعْشُم المُدْلِجي (٢). ويكنى بأبي سفيان، وكان ينزل قديدًاً، وهو الذي اتَّبع رسول الله ﷺ وأبا بكر وعامر بن فهيرة وعبد الله بن أُرَيْقط الدّيلي حين خرجوا من غار ثور قاصدين المدينة
> — البدایہ والنہایہ ج۷ ص۲۸۷ · https://shamela.ws/book/30097/3433
> *English:* "سراقة بن مالك بن جعشم المدلجي, whose kunya was أبو سفيان, and he used to camp at قديد. He is the one who followed the Messenger of Allah ﷺ and أبو بكر and عامر بن فهيرة and عبد الله بن أريقط الديلي when they came out of the cave of ثور making for المدینہ."

The notice continues with the hundred camels offered for each of the two, the horse's legs sinking,
the writ of safe-conduct أبو بكر ؓ wrote for him by the Prophet ﷺ's leave, his Islam after الطائف,
and his question about the عمرة answered «بل لأبد الأبد». `[SOURCED]` — ص۲۸۷

**This is the strand's cleanest single card**: the man who chased the هجرة for a bounty is buried a
Muslim in the first year of عثمان ؓ's caliphate. The generation that saw the beginning is going.

## 4. 30 AH — the فصل, and أبي بن كعب ؓ

### 4.1 أبي بن كعب ؓ, and five years for one death

The 30 AH فصل opens with a single line (ج۷ ص۲۹۴):

> وممن ذكر شيخنا أبو عبد الله الذهبي (٥) أنه توفي في هذه السنة - أعني سنة ثلاثين:
> أُبيّ بن كعب (٦) فيما صححه الواقدي (٧).
> — البدایہ والنہایہ ج۷ ص۲۹۴ · https://shamela.ws/book/30097/3440
> *English:* "And among those whom our shaykh أبو عبد الله الذهبي mentioned as having died in this year — that is, the year thirty: أبي بن كعب, according to what الواقدي held to be sound."

**The printed edition's own footnote (٧) on that line reads** (this is the modern editor's note, not
ابن کثیر's, and it prints the years in Latin-style Arabic numerals as shown):

> (٧) قيل إنه مات سنة ١٩، وقيل سنة ٢٠، وقيل سنة ٢٢، وقيل سنة ٣٢.
> — البدایہ والنہایہ ج۷ ص۲۹۴, footnote (٧) · https://shamela.ws/book/30097/3440
> *English:* "(7) It is said he died in the year 19, and it is said the year 20, and it is said the year 22, and it is said the year 32."

`[SOURCED]`. **Five candidate years for سيد القراء.** This is the single best illustration in the
whole strand that the death register is a record of what was reported, not a civil register — and
it is exactly the distinction the session-1 guardrail installed (`CLAUDE.md` §1.6). **Because the
four other years are the editor's note and not ابن کثیر's text, say so on the slide if the slide
carries them.**

### 4.3 The same year — why أبو ذر ؓ went to الربذة

The 30 AH narrative carries the notice that explains the 32 AH death (ج۷ ص۲۹۴):

> وفي هذه السنة وقع بين معاوية وأبي ذرٍّ بالشام، وذلك أن أبا ذرٍّ أنكر على معاوية بعض الأمور، وكان ينكر على من يقتني مالًا من الأغنياء. ويمنع أن يدَّخر فوقَ القُوت، ويوجب أن يُتصدّقَ بالفضل
> — البدایہ والنہایہ ج۷ ص۲۹۴ · https://shamela.ws/book/30097/3440
> *English:* "And in this year there occurred between معاوية and أبو ذر in the Shām what occurred: أبو ذر objected to معاوية over certain matters. He used to object to the wealthy who amassed property, and to forbid storing up beyond one's sustenance, and to hold that the surplus must be given in charity."

The book then gives: معاوية forbidding him to publish it and being disobeyed; the complaint to
عثمان ؓ; the summons to المدینہ; عثمان ؓ's reproach and his instruction that أبو ذر ؓ reside at
الربذة — **and then a second report that أبو ذر ؓ himself asked for it**, on the strength of a saying
of the Prophet ﷺ, «إذا بلغ البناء سلعا فاخرج منها»; and عثمان ؓ's instruction that he keep visiting
المدینہ so as not to revert to a bedouin after his هجرة. It closes «فلم يزل مقيمًا بها حتى مات على
ما سنذكره ﵁». `[SOURCED]` — ص۲۹۴

> **Guardrail.** Two accounts of *why* he was at الربذة stand side by side on one page and the book
> merges neither. Give both, in that order, and let the room hold both.

## 5. 32 AH — the heaviest year in the window

Four of the largest names in the community die inside one register: **العباس ؓ · عبد الله بن مسعود ؓ ·
عبد الرحمن بن عوف ؓ · أبو ذر ؓ**. ج۷ ص۳۰۱–۳۰۸, indices 3447–3454. If the evening carries one slide
on the thinning of the generation, it is this year.

### 5.1 العباس بن عبد المطلب ؓ — عم رسول الله ﷺ

The register opens with him (ج۷ ص۳۰۱):

> العباس بن عبد المطلب (٥) بن هاشم بن عبد مناف القرشي الهاشمي أبو الفضل المكي عم رسول الله ﷺ، ووالد الخلفاء العباسيين، وكان أسنَّ من رسول الله ﷺ بسنتين أو ثلاث، أسر يوم بدر فافتدى نفسه بمالٍ، وافتدى ابني أخويه عقيل بن أبي طالب ونوفل بن الحارث.
> — البدایہ والنہایہ ج۷ ص۳۰۱ · https://shamela.ws/book/30097/3447
> *English:* "العباس بن عبد المطلب بن هاشم بن عبد مناف القرشي الهاشمي, أبو الفضل المكي, the paternal uncle of the Messenger of Allah ﷺ and the father of the Abbasid caliphs. He was older than the Messenger of Allah ﷺ by two years or three. He was taken captive on the day of بدر and ransomed himself with property, and ransomed the two sons of his brothers, عقيل بن أبي طالب and نوفل بن الحارث."

**The scene of بدر — the sleepless night.** The sentence runs across the page break from ص۳۰۱ to
ص۳۰۲, so it must be cited to both:

> وقد ذكرنا أنه لما أُسر وشُدَّ في الوثاق وأمسى الناس، أرقَ رسول الله ﷺ فقيل يا رسول الله مالك؟ فقال "إني أسمع أنين العباس في وثاقه فلا
> — البدایہ والنہایہ ج۷ ص۳۰۱ · https://shamela.ws/book/30097/3447
> *English:* "We have mentioned that when he was taken captive and bound tight and the people came to evening, the Messenger of Allah ﷺ could not sleep, and it was said: O Messenger of Allah, what ails you? He said: 'I hear the groaning of العباس in his bonds, and I cannot —'"

and the sentence completes on the next page:

> أنام" (١) فقام رجل من المسلمين فحلَّ من وثاق العباس حتى سكن أنينه فنام رسول الله ﷺ
> — البدایہ والنہایہ ج۷ ص۳۰۲ · https://shamela.ws/book/30097/3448
> *English:* "'— sleep.' So a man of the Muslims rose and loosened العباس from his bonds until his groaning stopped, and the Messenger of Allah ﷺ slept."

**The rain-prayer.** The single most quotable item in the notice (ج۷ ص۳۰۲):

> وثبت في صحيح البخاري (١١) عن أنس: أن عمر خرج يستسقي وخرج بالعباس معه يستسقي به، وقال: اللهم إنّا كنّا إذا قحطنا توسّلنا إليك بنبينا فتسقينا، وإنا نتوسل إليك بعمِّ نبينا، قال: فيسقون.
> — البدایہ والنہایہ ج۷ ص۳۰۲ · https://shamela.ws/book/30097/3448
> *English:* "And it is established in صحيح البخاري, from أنس, that عمر went out to pray for rain and took العباس out with him to seek rain through him, and said: O Allah, when we were struck by drought we used to seek a means to You through our Prophet and we were given rain; and we now seek a means to You through the uncle of our Prophet. He said: and they would be given rain."

**The two caliphs dismounting** (ج۷ ص۳۰۳):

> ويقال: إن عمر بن الخطاب وعثمان بن عفان كانا إذا مرا بالعباس وهما راكبان ترجّلا إكرامًا له.
> — البدایہ والنہایہ ج۷ ص۳۰۳ · https://shamela.ws/book/30097/3449
> *English:* "And it is said that عمر بن الخطاب and عثمان بن عفان, when they passed العباس while riding, would dismount out of honour for him."

**The death notice itself, with its disagreements intact** (ج۷ ص۳۰۳):

> قال الواقدي وغير واحد: توفي العباس في يوم الجمعة لاثنتي (١) عشرة ليلة خلت من رجب، وقيل من رمضان سنة ثنتين وثلاثين، عن ثمان وثمانين سنة، وصلى عليه عثمان بن عفان، ودفن بالبقيع وقيل توفي سنة ثلاث وثلاثين، وقيل سنة أربع وثلاثين، وفضائله ومناقبه كثيرة جدًا.
> — البدایہ والنہایہ ج۷ ص۳۰۳ · https://shamela.ws/book/30097/3449
> *English:* "الواقدي and more than one said: العباس died on Friday, twelve nights having passed of رجب — and it is said of رمضان — in the year thirty-two, at eighty-eight years of age. عثمان بن عفان prayed over him and he was buried at البقيع. And it is said he died in the year thirty-three, and it is said the year thirty-four. And his merits and virtues are very many."

**Three years, two months, one age.** `[SOURCED]`. Other items the notice carries: the Prophet ﷺ's
«هذا بقيةُ آبائي» (ص۳۰۲); «أجودُ قريش كَفًّا وأوصلُها» from مسند أحمد (ص۳۰۲); «أما شعرت أن عمَّ
الرجل صنو أبيه» from the Ṣaḥīḥayn (ص۳۰۲); ten sons named; and «وأعتق سبعين مملوكًا من غلمانه»
(ص۳۰۲).

### 5.2 عبد الله بن مسعود ؓ — the last conversation

The notice is long. Its useful parts:

**How he came to Islam** — the shepherd boy, the ewe on which no male had mounted, the milk, and
«إنك غلام معلَّم» (ص۳۰۳). **The first man to recite the Qurʾān aloud at مكة** — سورة الرحمن at the
Kaʿba, and they rose and beat him (ص۳۰۳, on the authority of محمد بن إسحاق). **He killed أبو جهل at
بدر after the two sons of عفراء had brought him down** (ص۳۰۴). **The Prophet ﷺ asked him to recite,
and wept** at النساء 41 (ص۳۰۴).

**حذيفة ؓ on him** (ج۷ ص۳۰۴):

> وقال حذيفة: ما رأيت أحدًا أشبه برسول الله ﷺ في هَدْيهِ ودَلِّه وسَمْتهِ من ابن مسعود.
> — البدایہ والنہایہ ج۷ ص۳۰۴ · https://shamela.ws/book/30097/3450
> *English:* "And حذيفة said: I have not seen anyone more like the Messenger of Allah ﷺ in his manner, his bearing and his demeanour than ابن مسعود."

**عمر ؓ on him** (ج۷ ص۳۰۴) — the line about his short stature:

> وقال عمر بن الخطاب ﵁ -وقد نظر إلى قصره وكان يوازي بقامته الجلوس- فجعل يتبعه ثمَّ قال: هو كُنَيف (٧) مليء علمًا.
> — البدایہ والنہایہ ج۷ ص۳۰۴ · https://shamela.ws/book/30097/3450
> *English:* "And عمر بن الخطاب ﵁ — having looked at his shortness, for standing he was level with a seated man — followed him with his eyes and then said: he is a small vessel filled with knowledge."

**He was at أبو ذر ؓ's death before his own** (ج۷ ص۳۰۴):

> وكان قدم من العراق حاجًا فمر بالرَّبذة فشهد وفاة أبي ذر ودفنه، ثم قدم إلى المدينة فمرض بها فجاءه عثمان بن عفان عائدًا
> — البدایہ والنہایہ ج۷ ص۳۰۴ · https://shamela.ws/book/30097/3450
> *English:* "He had come from العراق as a pilgrim and passed by الربذة, and was present at the death of أبو ذر and at his burial; then he came to المدینہ and fell ill there, and عثمان بن عفان came to visit him."

**The deathbed dialogue** (ج۷ ص۳۰۵) — the scene of the strand:

> أنه قال له: ما تشتكي؟ قال ذنوبي، قال فما تشتهي؟ قال رحمة ربي، قال ألا آمر لك بطبيب؟ فقال: الطبيب أمرضني، قال: ألا آمر لك بعطائك؟ -وكان قد تركه سنتين- فقال: لا حاجة لي فيه. فقال: يكون لبناتك من بعدك. فقال: أتخشى على بناتي الفقر؟ إني أمرتُ بناتي أن يقرأْنَ كلَّ ليلةٍ سورةَ الواقعة
> — البدایہ والنہایہ ج۷ ص۳۰۵ · https://shamela.ws/book/30097/3451
> *English:* "That he said to him: of what do you complain? He said: of my sins. He said: what do you desire? He said: the mercy of my Lord. He said: shall I not order a physician for you? He said: the physician made me ill. He said: shall I not order your stipend paid to you? — and he had left it two years — He said: I have no need of it. He said: it will be for your daughters after you. He said: do you fear poverty for my daughters? I have ordered my daughters to recite سورة الواقعة every night."

`[SOURCED]`. **Note for the hall:** the two men in this scene are عثمان ؓ visiting the sick and
ابن مسعود ؓ declining money. Told exactly as it stands it is a scene of two men at their best, and
that is how it should be told.

**Who prayed over him is not known** (ج۷ ص۳۰۵):

> وأوصى عبد الله بن مسعود إلى الزّبير بن العوّام، فيقال إنه هو الذي صلى عليه ليلًا، ثمّ عاتب عثمانُ الزبير على ذلك، وقيل بل صلَّى عليه عثمان، وقيل عمَّار، فالله أعلم. ودفن بالبقيع عن بضع وستين سنة.
> — البدایہ والنہایہ ج۷ ص۳۰۵ · https://shamela.ws/book/30097/3451
> *English:* "عبد الله بن مسعود made الزبير بن العوام the executor of his will, and it is said that it was he who prayed over him by night, and that عثمان then reproached الزبير for it; and it is said rather that عثمان prayed over him; and it is said عمار — and Allah knows best. He was buried at البقيع at a little over sixty."

`[SOURCED]`. **Three accounts, one فالله أعلم.** This is the model for how the evening handles a
disputed report: give the three, name the book, stop.

## 6. 32 AH — عبد الرحمن بن عوف ؓ, and two reports ابن کثیر refuses

### 6.1 The notice

ج۷ ص۳۰۵–۳۰۷. The opening establishes his standing in one breath — «أحد العشرة المشهود لهم بالجنة،
وأحد الثمانية السابقين إلى الإسلام، وأحد الستة أصحاب الشورى، ثمَّ أحد الثلاثة الذين انتهت إليهم
منهم» — and then adds, plainly, «ثمّ كان هو الذي اجتهد في تقديم عثمان ﵁» `[SOURCED]` — ص۳۰۵.

His giving, on الزهري's authority (ص۳۰۵): half his property in the Prophet ﷺ's time — four thousand;
then forty thousand; then forty thousand dinars; then five hundred horses in the path of Allah; then
five hundred mounts. `[SOURCED]`

### 6.2 The bequest to the men of بدر

ج۷ ص۳۰۶ — the strongest single scene in the notice:

> ولما حضرته الوفاة أوصى لكل رجل ممن بقي من أهل بدر بأربعمئة دينار -وكانوا مئة- فأخذوها حتى عثمان وعلي، وقال علي: اذهب يا بن عوف فقد أدركت صفوها، وسبقت زيفها، وأوصى لكلّ امرأةٍ من أمُّهات المؤمنين بمبلغٍ كثيرٍ حتَّى كانت عائشةُ تقولُ: سقاهُ اللهُ من السَّلْسَبيل.
> — البدایہ والنہایہ ج۷ ص۳۰۶ · https://shamela.ws/book/30097/3452
> *English:* "And when death came to him he bequeathed to every man remaining of the people of بدر four hundred dinars — and they were a hundred — and they took it, even عثمان and علي. And علي said: go, son of عوف, for you have attained its pure part and outrun its dross. And he bequeathed to every one of the Mothers of the Believers a large sum, so that عائشة used to say: may Allah give him to drink of السلسبيل."

`[SOURCED]`. **Read the number slowly in the hall: a hundred men of بدر were still alive in 32 AH.**
That one figure carries the whole strand: it says both how many were left and how few.

### 6.3 The estate, and his burial

ج۷ ص۳۰۷:

> ثمّ تركَ بعد ذلك كلِّه مالًا جزيلًا، من ذلك ذهبٌ قُطِّعَ بالفُؤوس حتى مجلت (١) أيدي الرجال، وتركَ ألفَ بعيرٍ ومئةَ فرسٍ، وثلاثةَ آلافِ شاةٍ تَرْعى بالبقيع.
> — البدایہ والنہایہ ج۷ ص۳۰۷ · https://shamela.ws/book/30097/3453
> *English:* "Then after all of that he left abundant wealth: among it gold that was cut up with axes until the men's hands blistered; and he left a thousand camels and a hundred horses and three thousand sheep grazing at البقيع."

### 6.4 Two things ابن کثیر will not let stand — the method on display

**(a) The «حَبْوًا» report.** The notice gives, twice, the report that عائشة ؓ heard the Prophet ﷺ say

> — البدایہ والنہایہ ج۷ ص۳۰۶ · https://shamela.ws/book/30097/3452

**(b) The brotherhood pairing.** ج۷ ص۳۰۶:

> وأما قوله (٧) في سياق عبد بن حميد: إنه آخى بينه وبين عثمان بن عفان، فغلط (٨) محض مخالف لما في صحيح البخاري (٩) من أن الذي آخى بينه وبينه إنما هو سعد بن الربيع الأنصاري ﵄.
> — البدایہ والنہایہ ج۷ ص۳۰۶ · https://shamela.ws/book/30097/3452
> *English:* "As for his words in عبد بن حميد's version — that he paired him in brotherhood with عثمان بن عفان — that is a pure error, contrary to what is in صحيح البخاري: that the one he paired him with was سعد بن الربيع الأنصاري ﵄."

## 7. 32 AH — أبو ذر الغفاري ؓ at الربذة

### 7.1 The notice, and a grading in the middle of it

ج۷ ص۳۰۷. The opening: «واسمه جُنْدُبُ بنُ جُنَادَة على المشهور، أسلم قديمًا بمكة فكان رابع أربعة أو
خامس خمسة» — and that he was the first to greet the Prophet ﷺ with the greeting of Islam.
`[SOURCED]`

> وجاء في فضله أحاديث كثيرة من أشهرها: ما رواه الأعمشُ، عن أبي اليقظانِ عثمان بن عُمَيْر، عن أبي حَرْب بن أبي الأسود، عن عبد الله بن عمرو أن رسول الله ﷺ قال "ما أظلَّتِ الخَضْراء، ولا أقلَّت الغَبْراء أصدقَ لهجةً من أبي ذر" (٧) وفيه ضعف.
> — البدایہ والنہایہ ج۷ ص۳۰۷ · https://shamela.ws/book/30097/3453
> *English:* "Many reports have come concerning his merit, among the most famous of them what الأعمش related, from أبو اليقظان عثمان بن عمير, from أبو حرب بن أبي الأسود, from عبد الله بن عمرو, that the Messenger of Allah ﷺ said: 'The green sky has not shaded, nor the dusty earth borne, one more truthful of tongue than أبو ذر' — and there is weakness in it."

`[SOURCED]`. **«وفيه ضعف» is ابن کثیر's own remark on the page**, attached to one of the best-loved
sayings about a Companion. If the evening uses the saying, it uses it with that clause attached.

> *English:* "Then when the Messenger of Allah ﷺ died, and أبو بكر died, he went out to the Shām and was there until what occurred between him and معاوية occurred, and عثمان summoned him to المدینہ; then he settled at الربذة and remained there until he died in ذو الحجة of this year, with no one present but his wife and his children. ## 9. 36 AH — طلحة ؓ and الزبير ؓ

البدایہ files **no obituary chapter** for 36 AH. It gives both men full notices inside the year's
narrative, ج۷ ص۴۲۸–۴۳۳, indices 3574–3579.

### 9.1 طلحة بن عبيد الله ؓ — طلحة الخير, طلحة الفياض

The notice names him with the ألقاب the tradition already gave: «ويعرف بطَلْحة الخَيْر، وطلحة
الفَيّاض لكرمه ولكثرة جوده» — ص۴۲۸. It adds a third and fourth on a report from his own son (ص۴۲۹):
«سمّاني رسول الله ﷺ يومَ أحدٍ طلحةَ الخير، ويوم العسرة طلحة الفياض. ويوم حنين طلحة الجود» — the
editor's footnote (٣) marks that chain weak. `[SOURCED]`

**أحد.** ج۷ ص۴۲۸:

> وكانت له يوم أحد اليدُ البيضاءُ وشَلت يده يومئذ لأنه وقى بها رسول الله ﷺ واستمرت كذلك إلى أن مات. وكان الصديق إذا حدث عن يوم أحد يقول: ذاك يوم [كان] كله لطلحة
> — البدایہ والنہایہ ج۷ ص۴۲۸ · https://shamela.ws/book/30097/3574
> *English:* "His was the white hand on the day of أحد, and his hand was withered that day because he shielded the Messenger of Allah ﷺ with it, and it remained so until he died. And الصديق, whenever he spoke of the day of أحد, would say: that was a day that belonged entirely to طلحة."

The same page gives the Prophet ﷺ stooping over طلحة ؓ's back to climb the rock, and «أوْجَبَ طلحة».
`[SOURCED]`

**The arrow, and ابن کثیر's refusal.** ج۷ ص۴۲۸:

> فجاءه سهمٌ غَرْبٌ فوقع في ركبته وقيل في رقبته، والأول أشهر
> — البدایہ والنہایہ ج۷ ص۴۲۸ · https://shamela.ws/book/30097/3574
> *English:* "A stray arrow came to him and fell in his knee — and it is said in his neck, and the first is the better known."

> ويقال إن الذي رماه بهذا السهم مروان بن الحكم، وقال لأبان بن عثمان: قد كفيتك رجالاً (٨) من قتلة عثمان، وقد قيل إن الذي رماه غيره، وهذا عندي أقرب، وإن كان الأول مشهوراً والله أعلم.
> — البدایہ والنہایہ ج۷ ص۴۲۸ · https://shamela.ws/book/30097/3574
> *English:* "And it is said that the one who shot him with this arrow was مروان بن الحكم, and that he said to أبان بن عثمان: I have spared you men from among the killers of عثمان. And it has been said that the one who shot him was someone else — and this to me is the nearer, even if the first is the more widely reported, and Allah knows best."

`[SOURCED]`. **This is the guardrail sentence of the whole strand.** The most famous version of who
killed a Companion of the ten is the version ابن کثیر himself declines. A speaker who has this line
never has to adjudicate from the platform: the book already declined, and he is only reading it.

**علي ؓ over the body.** ج۷ ص۴۲۸:

> وأنَّ علياً لما دار بين القتلى رآه فجعل (٥) يمسحُ عن وجهه التُّرابَ وقال: رحمة الله عليك أبا محمد، يعز عليَّ أن أراك مُجَدَّلاً (٦) تحت نجوم السماء، ثم قال: إلى الله أشكو عُجَري وبُجَري (٧)، والله لوددتُ أني كنتُ متُّ قبل هذا اليوم بعشرين سنة.
> — البدایہ والنہایہ ج۷ ص۴۲۸ · https://shamela.ws/book/30097/3574
> *English:* "And that علي, when he went round among the slain, saw him and began to wipe the dust from his face and said: the mercy of Allah be upon you, أبو محمد. It is hard for me to see you laid out under the stars of the sky. Then he said: to Allah I complain of my griefs and my sorrows. By Allah, I would have wished to die twenty years before this day."

`[SOURCED]`. Note that the same words — «لوددتُ أني مت قبل هذا اليوم بعشرين سنة» — are said by عمرو
بن العاص over the dispute about عمار ؓ at ص۵۲۴. Two different men, two different days; do not merge
them.

**The date, the age, the reburial.** ج۷ ص۴۲۹:

> وكان يوم الخميس لعشر خلون من جمادى الآخر سنة ست وثلاثين، ودفن طلحة إلى جانب الكلأ (١) وكان عمره ستين سنة، وقيل: بضعاً وستين سنة
> — البدایہ والنہایہ ج۷ ص۴۲۹ · https://shamela.ws/book/30097/3575
> *English:* "And it was on Thursday, ten nights having passed of جمادى الآخر, in the year thirty-six. طلحة was buried beside الكلأ, and his age was sixty years — and it is said sixty-odd."

The same page carries the report of the dream in which he asked to be moved because the water had
reached him, and the house bought at البصرة for ten thousand dirhams to move him into — «فإذا قد
اخضّر من جسده ما يلي الماء، وإذا هو كهيئته يوم أصيب» `[SOURCED]` — ص۴۲۹. The editor's footnote
marks the chain weak (علي بن زيد بن جدعان); say so if it is told.

### 9.2 الزبير بن العوام ؓ — حواريّ رسول الله ﷺ

ج۷ ص۴۳۰–۴۳۳. Nephew of the Prophet ﷺ through صفية بنت عبد المطلب; accepted Islam at fifteen; both
هجرات; present at every مشهد. `[SOURCED]` — ص۴۳۰

**The لقب, from the day of الأحزاب** (ج۷ ص۴۳۰):

> فقال رسول الله ﷺ: "إنَّ لكل نبيٍّ حوارياً وحواريَّ الزبير" (٧) ثبت ذلك من رواية زِزًّ عن على (٨).
> — البدایہ والنہایہ ج۷ ص۴۳۰ · https://shamela.ws/book/30097/3576
> *English:* "The Messenger of Allah ﷺ said: 'Every prophet has a disciple, and my disciple is الزبير.' That is established from the narration of زر from علي."

(**«زِزًّ» is the Shamela rendering of the transmitter's name زِرّ**; the editor's footnote (٨)
attaches the takhrīj. Quote this only from a page image.)

**How he left the field, and how he was killed** (ج۷ ص۴۳۱):

> فلما كان يوم الجمل ذكَّره علي بما ذكّره به [كما تقدم] فرجع عن القتال وكرَّ راجعاً إلى المدينة
> — البدایہ والنہایہ ج۷ ص۴۳۱ · https://shamela.ws/book/30097/3577
> *English:* "And when the day of الجمل came, علي reminded him of what he reminded him of, as has preceded, and he turned back from the fighting and set off returning to المدینہ."

The book then gives **two accounts of the killing itself and says which is the better known**: that
عمرو بن جرموز and others fell on him together; or that عمرو asked to speak with him, the prayer-time
came, الزبير ؓ went forward to lead the two of them in prayer, and was struck; or that he was found
asleep at وادي السباع — «وهذا القول هو الأشهر». `[SOURCED]` — ص۴۳۱

**علي ؓ and the killer** (ج۷ ص۴۳۲):

> ولما قتله عمرو (بن جرموز) احتزَّ رأسَه وذهب به إلى عليٍّ ورأى أن ذلك يحصل له به حظوة عنده، فاستأذنَ فقال علىّ: لا تأذنوا له وبشِّروه بالنار، وفي رواية أن علياً قال: سمعت رسول الله ﷺ يقول: " بشر قاتلَ ابن صفية بالنار" (٧)
> — البدایہ والنہایہ ج۷ ص۴۳۲ · https://shamela.ws/book/30097/3578
> *English:* "And when عمرو بن جرموز had killed him he cut off his head and went with it to علي, thinking that he would gain favour with him by it, and asked leave to enter. علي said: do not admit him; and give him tidings of the Fire. ### 10.4 عمار بن ياسر ؓ

ج۷ ص۵۲۳–۵۲۴. The notice opens with his early Islam and the torture of his household — «وكان ممن
يُعذَّبُ في الله هو وأبوه وأمه سُميَّة» — and that he is said to be the first to make a mosque in his
house. `[SOURCED]`

It then gives the hadith by which he is best known, and attributes its placement to the earlier
chapter (ج۷ ص۵۲۳):

> وقد شهد بدرًا وما بعدها وقد قدمنا كيفية مقتله يوم صفين وأن رسول الله ﷺ قال: "تقتلك الفئة الباغية" (٥)
> — البدایہ والنہایہ ج۷ ص۵۲۳ · https://shamela.ws/book/30097/3669
> *English:* "He was present at بدر and what came after it; and we have already given the manner of his killing on the day of صفين, and that the Messenger of Allah ﷺ said: 'The transgressing party will kill you.'"

> **Guardrail — read this before the deck.** This hadith is the hinge of every argument about صفين.
> The strand's rule is: **narrate that the book files it in عمار ؓ's obituary, and go no further.**
> Do not name a party, do not gloss «الفئة الباغية», and do not answer it from the floor — defer to
> the session where the مشاجرات are handled with اصول (`CLAUDE.md` §1.6). البدایہ's own fuller
> treatment sits at «مقتل عمار بن ياسر ﵁», index 3601, ج۷ ص۴۵۵ ff., **which this pass did not read.**

The page also carries «مرحبًا بالطيب المطيب»; «لقد ملئ عمار إيمانًا من قدمه إلى مُشاشِه»; عائشة ؓ's
word about him; and a report in which the Prophet ﷺ tells خالد بن الوليد ؓ not to hurt عمار ؓ — on
which **the editor's footnote (٤) says the chain is defective and disconnected** («وهو إسناد معلول
… فهو منقطع») — ص۵۲۴. `[SOURCED]`

**The killing, and the dispute over it** (ج۷ ص۵۲۴):

> قتل بصفين عن إحدى (٥) وقيل ثلاث وقيل أربع وتسعين سنة، طعنه أبو الغادية فسقط ثم أكب عليه رجل فاحتز رأسه، ثم اختصما إلى معاوية أيهما قتله فقال لهما عمرو بن العاص: اندرا فواللّه إنكما لتختصمان في النار، فسمعها منه معاوية فلامه على تسميعه إياهما ذلك، فقال له عمرو: واللّه إنك لتعلم ذلك، ولوددت أني مت قبل هذا اليوم بعشرين سنة.
> — البدایہ والنہایہ ج۷ ص۵۲۴ · https://shamela.ws/book/30097/3670
> *English:* "He was killed at صفين at ninety-one — and it is said ninety-three, and it is said ninety-four. أبو الغادية speared him and he fell; then a man bent over him and cut off his head. Then the two of them disputed before معاوية as to which of them had killed him, and عمرو بن العاص said to them: withdraw — by Allah, the two of you are disputing over a place in the Fire. معاوية heard it from him and blamed him for letting them hear it; and عمرو said to him: by Allah, you know that; and I would have wished to die twenty years before this day."

`[SOURCED]`. **Three ages, two claimants, and a judgement spoken by عمرو بن العاص and attributed to
him by name.** Narrate it exactly so. Do not paraphrase it into a verdict of our own.

**علي ؓ prayed over him without washing him** (ج۷ ص۵۲۴):

> أن عليًّا صلَّى عليه ولم يُغَسّله وصلى (معه) على هاشم بن عتبة، فكان عمار مما يلي عليًا، وهاشم إلى نحو القبلة. قالوا: وقبره هنالك.

`[SOURCED]`. The chain is given as الواقدي's, from ابن سعد — recorded as **the book's own attribution**.

### 10.5 The number of the dead at صفين

The register closes with the figures, and البدایہ chooses between neither set (ج۷ ص۵۲۴, running onto
ص۵۲۵):

> وقد قتل في هذه السنة في أيام صفين خلقٌ كثيرٌ وجمٌّ غفير، فقيل: قتل من أهل الشام خمسةٌ وأربعون ألفًا، ومن أهل العراق خمسة وعشرون ألفًا. وقيل: قتل من أهل العراق أربعون ألفًا - من مئة وعشرين

> *English:* "And there were killed in this year, in the days of صفين, a very great number. It is said: there were killed of the people of the Shām forty-five thousand, and of the people of العراق twenty-five thousand. And it is said: there were killed of the people of العراق forty thousand — out of a hundred and twenty"

and completes (ج۷ ص۵۲۵):

> ألفًا - وقتل من أهل الشام عشرون ألفًا من ستين ألفًا، وبالجملة فقد كان فيهم أعيانٌ ومشاهيرُ يطولُ استقصاؤهم وفيما ذكرناه كفايةٌ، واللّه تعالى أعلم.

`[SOURCED, disputed]`. **Two incompatible totals, printed side by side.** If a figure goes on a

## What could NOT be established

1. **The deaths of سلمان الفارسي ؓ, حذيفة بن اليمان ؓ and أبو الدرداء ؓ are not in any obituary
   register in this window.** البدایہ files **no «من توفي» chapter at all for 33, 34, 35 or 36 AH**
   (`_toc.tsv`, indices 3454–3583, checked page by page), which is exactly where the conventional
   dates for سلمان ؓ and حذيفة ؓ fall. Searching every cached page from 3426 to 3666 for their names
   turns up **سلمان الفارسي ؓ only as a living man** — among those who withdrew toward جيلان and
   جرجان after بلنجر, ج۷ ص۳۰۰ (index 3446), and as the transmitter of a hadith at ج۷ ص۵۰۷ — and
   **حذيفة ؓ only as a living commander and transmitter**, e.g. on the Ṭabaristān expedition at
   ج۷ ص۲۹۲ (index 3438) and in the Armenian treaty notice at ج۷ ص۲۸۶ (index 3432). **This note
   therefore carries no death notice for either.** That both died in 36 AH is `[STANDARD]`
   *(to verify)* — **it must not go on a slide until a page is found.** The place to look next is
   **سیر أعلام النبلاء 10906**, which was not opened in this pass.
2. **Where the 37 AH obituary of عمار ؓ points back to «مقتل عمار بن ياسر ﵁» (index 3601, ج۷ ص۴۵۵
   ff.), that chapter was not read.** Everything this note says about عمار ؓ comes from the obituary
   notice at ص۵۲۳–۵۲۴ only.
3. **الکامل and سیر were not consulted at all.** Every «and it is said» recorded above is البدایہ's
   own spread. A second book may narrow a spread — or widen it — and until one is read, no row of
   §13 may be presented as the full range of opinion, only as *what البدایہ carries*.
4. **No total count of surviving Companions at any point in this window can be given.** The only hard
   number the pages yield is عبد الرحمن بن عوف ؓ's bequest in 32 AH — «وكانوا مئة», about a hundred
   men of بدر still alive (ج۷ ص۳۰۶) — and that is a count of the men of بدر, not of Companions.
   Anything beyond it would be `[CONVENTIONAL-ESTIMATE]` and is not offered here.
5. **The صفين casualty figures cannot be reconciled.** Two sets, ج۷ ص۵۲۴–۵۲۵, and البدایہ chooses
   neither. Do not average them, do not pick the smaller, do not pick the larger.
6. **Nothing in this pass was verified against a page image.** Every Arabic line below and above was
   machine-checked byte-for-byte against the cached Shamela text, which fixes transcription but not
   Shamela's own typos. **Two are flagged in place (§3.1, §10.1) and a third at §9.2; there may be
   others.** Anything that reaches a slide must be eyeballed against the printed page first
   (`CLAUDE.md` §1.1).

## Not for the hall

Real material, on the pages, that must not be narrated from the platform — with the reason.

1. **The عبيد الله بن عمر affair (ج۷ ص۲۸۴–۲۸۵).** The killings of الهرمزان, جفينة and the daughter of
   أبو لؤلؤة; علي ؓ's «ما من العدل تركه»; عثمان ؓ's payment of the دية and release; and زياد بن لبيد's
   verse reproach. Every element is sourced — **and the room will hear it as a verdict on عثمان ؓ's
   first act as caliph.** The strand's need is a death register, not this case. If it must be
   mentioned at all, mention only that the first case brought to عثمان ؓ was inherited from the
   killing of عمر ؓ, and move on. **Never adjudicate it.** (`CLAUDE.md` §1.6.)
2. **«تقتلك الفئة الباغية» (ج۷ ص۵۲۳).** The hadith goes on the page of this note because it is in the
   obituary. **It does not go on a slide** and it is not glossed from the platform. Session 3's
   fiqh treatment of the مشاجرات is where it belongs, with اصول; a written slip at the door is the
   route, not a remark in the narrative.
3. **The report naming مروان بن الحكم as طلحة ؓ's killer (ج۷ ص۴۲۸).** Tell it only in the form
   البدایہ gives it: a widely-reported version which ابن کثیر himself says is not, in his view, the
   nearer one. Never as what happened.
4. **ابن کثیر's remark that a figure in صحيح البخاري «فيه نظر» (ج۷ ص۴۳۳).** It concerns الزبير ؓ's
   estate arithmetic, it is genuinely on the page, and it is entirely beside the point of the
   evening — and a room that hears "there is something to look at in البخاري" will hear nothing else
   for ten minutes. **Leave it out.**
5. **عروة بن حزام (ج۷ ص۳۹۰) and أبو زبيد الطائي (ج۷ ص۳۹۳).** Both are in the undated register; the
   first is a love story ending in death, with verses; the second ends in عثمان ؓ's personal remark
   about the man. Neither carries the evening anywhere, and the register has twenty better names.
   Recorded so nobody researches them twice.
6. **The طلحة ؓ reburial dream (ج۷ ص۴۲۹) and the سعد ؓ imprecation story (ج۷ ص۴۳۰).** Both are on the
   page; the printed edition's footnotes mark both chains weak (علي بن زيد بن جدعان in each). If
   either is told, the grading is told with it — and on a 45-minute evening neither is worth the
   time it costs to tell honestly.
7. **The two claimants to having killed عمار ؓ (ج۷ ص۵۲۴).** The scene is permitted — عمرو بن العاص's
   words are attributed to him by name and the book prints them. What is **not** permitted is any
   extension of them: the speaker does not say who the «فئة باغية» was, does not draw the inference
   about معاوية, and does not allow the floor to. It is told and then left.
8. **Death-toll figures used rhetorically.** Seventy thousand Muslims dead in one year is a real
   number on a real page and it will do the work of the evening by itself — which is precisely why
   it must be given with both variants and the words «وقيل», not thrown at the room as a fact.

### E-DG1 · The book keeps a register of the dead

**Tier:** CORE · **When:** 23–40 AH `[SOURCED]` · **Map:** No map. The timeline strip only, with the years that carry a register marked.

**What happened:** Every so often, at the end of a year, the historian stops narrating and writes a list. He names the men and women who died that year, gives each a line or two, and moves on. In the years we are looking at tonight, that list is the quietest thing in the book and the heaviest. It is where you see the generation that had seen the Prophet ﷺ going, one name at a time, in the same years the trouble arrives. And the book is honest about the list. Some years it has one. Some years it has none at all.

> ثم قال: ذكر من توفي في خلافة عمر بن الخطاب ﵁:
> — البدایہ والنہایہ ج۷ ص۲۷۴ · https://shamela.ws/book/30097/3420
> *English:* "Then he said: an account of those who died during the caliphate of عمر بن الخطاب ﵁."

**عبرت:** A community that counts its dead by name is a community that knows what it is losing.

**Tier:** GOOD · **When:** 24–29, 31, 33–36, 40 AH `[SOURCED]` · **Map:** No map. The timeline strip with the empty years marked.

**What happened:** For most of the years in this stretch the book keeps its list of the dead. For some of them it keeps none — not a short one, none at all. Nothing for twenty-four through twenty-nine, nothing for thirty-one, nothing for thirty-three to thirty-six, nothing for forty. Some of the greatest of them died inside those gaps. We should be honest about this from the platform: what we are reading is what reached the historian, laid out year by year, and where nothing reached him he has written nothing.

**The statement:**
none in the sources we use

**عبرت:** A gap in a record is a fact about the record, and it should be said out loud rather than filled in.

### E-DG49 · Read the list slowly

**Tier:** CORE · **When:** 23–40 AH `[SOURCED]` · **Map:** The closing map for this strand: المدینہ · الربذة · الكوفة · البصرة · صفين · مصر, each with a name and a year.

**What happened:** Put the years side by side and say the names in order. Twenty-three: قتادة ؓ, and عمر ؓ himself. Twenty-four: سراقة ؓ. Thirty: أبي بن كعب ؓ. Thirty-two: العباس ؓ, ابن مسعود ؓ, عبد الرحمن بن عوف ؓ, أبو ذر ؓ. Thirty-six: طلحة ؓ and الزبير ؓ, on one Thursday. Thirty-seven: خباب ؓ, his son, خزيمة ؓ, عمار ؓ. Thirty-eight: سهل بن حنيف ؓ, صهيب ؓ. In seventeen years the room that had actually seen the Prophet ﷺ emptied out — and the arguing was loudest in exactly the years the room was emptying.

**The statement:**
> وبالجملة فقد كان فيهم أعيانٌ ومشاهيرُ يطولُ استقصاؤهم وفيما ذكرناه كفايةٌ، واللّه تعالى أعلم.
> — البدایہ والنہایہ ج۷ ص۵۲۵ · https://shamela.ws/book/30097/3671
> *English:* "In sum, among them were notables and well-known men whom it would be long to enumerate, and what we have mentioned is sufficient — and Allah, exalted be He, knows best."

**عبرت:** The quarrels of those years were being carried on by fewer and fewer of the people who had been there at the beginning.

**Hands-up?** Which of the Companions you have heard of tonight do you think were still alive in the year forty?
