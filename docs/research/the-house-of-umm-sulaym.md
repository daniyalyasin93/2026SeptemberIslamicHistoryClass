# The house of ⁨أم سليم ؓ⁩ — one Anṣārī household from ⁨أحد⁩ to ⁨تُسْتَر⁩

**Question this note answers:** When ⁨البراء بن مالك ؓ⁩ asks to be thrown over the wall at
⁨حديقة الموت⁩ in ⁨۱۲ھ⁩, whose brother is he? Can the room be made to *already know* — because they met his
brother, his uncle and his brother's household earlier in the same course? This note establishes
the household exactly, page by page, and then the incidents that attach to each member, so that the
connection can be made from the platform **in one sentence, with a citation behind it**.

**Sources fetched:**
`10906` ⁨سیر أعلام النبلاء⁩ — indices 544–548, 894, 1044, 1621–1628, 2007–2014, 2284–2300, 3003–3014 ·
`30097` البدایہ والنہایہ — 1680, 1691–1698, 1744–1746, 2077–2079, 3105, 3179–3181, 3329, 3338–3345,
3348, 3383, 3435–3437, 4880, 8221 ·
`21712` الکامل فی التاریخ — 909, 1043, 1055–1060, 1065.
Full table in §Z.

**Everything below was fetched and read from those cached pages. Nothing here is from memory.**

   brothers, sons of ⁨مالك بن النضر⁩; ⁨أنس بن النضر ؓ⁩ is their father's brother, and ⁨أنس ؓ⁩ was named
   after him. `[SOURCED]` ⁨سیر ج۱ ص۱۹۵⁩ · ⁨سیر ج۳ ص۳۹۵⁩ · ⁨البدایہ ج۴ ص۲۰۳، ص۲۰۷⁩.

   stepfather — is ⁨أبو طلحة الأنصاري ؓ⁩ (⁨زيد بن سهل⁩).** `[SOURCED]` ⁨سیر ج۲ ص۳۰۴⁩ · ⁨سیر ج۳ ص۳۹۶⁩

   ⁨بئر معونة⁩, is their brother.** `[SOURCED]` ⁨سیر ج۲ ص۳۱۶⁩ · ⁨سیر ج۲ ص۳۰۷⁩ · ⁨البدایہ ج۴ ص۲۵۵⁩.

   `[SOURCED]` ⁨سیر ج۲ ص۲۷⁩ · ⁨سیر ج۱ ص۱۹۵⁩ · ⁨سیر ج۲ ص۳۰۴⁩ · ⁨سیر ج۲ ص۳۱۶⁩ · ⁨سیر ج۳ ص۳۹۶⁩.
6. **⁨أنس بن النضر ؓ⁩ was found at ⁨أحد⁩ with eighty-odd wounds and was recognised only by his fingertips,

## 1. The household, as a diagram in words

Read it aloud in this order; it is the order in which the sources give it.

**The parent generation — ⁨بنو النجار⁩, of the ⁨خزرج⁩ of ⁨المدینہ⁩.**

- **⁨مِلْحان بن خالد⁩** ؓ — father of three: **⁨أم سليم ؓ⁩**, **⁨أم حرام ؓ⁩**, **⁨حرام بن ملحان ؓ⁩**.
- **⁨النضر بن ضمضم⁩** — father of **⁨مالك بن النضر⁩** (⁨أنس ؓ⁩'s father), of **⁨أنس بن النضر ؓ⁩**, and of
  **الرُّبيِّع بنت النضر ؓ**.

**The marriage that begins it.**

- **⁨أم سليم بنت ملحان ؓ⁩** married **⁨مالك بن النضر⁩**. ⁨سیر⁩ names **one** son of hers by him:
  **أنس ؓ** (ج۲ ص۳۰۴ «أُمُّ خَادِمِ النَّبِيِّ ﷺ؛ أَنَسِ بنِ مَالِكٍ»; ج۳ ص۳۹۶ «وَأُمِّهِ أُمِّ
  ⁨سُلَيْمٍ⁩»). **⁨البراء ؓ⁩** is on the pages read only as **⁨مالك بن النضر⁩'s son and ⁨أنس ؓ⁩'s brother**
  (⁨سیر ج۱ ص۱۹۵⁩). ⚠ **No page read here says ⁨أم سليم ؓ⁩ was his mother** — that step is ours, and it
  must not be made from the platform. See §X.14.
- ⁨مالك⁩ died. **⁨أبو طلحة زيد بن سهل ؓ⁩** then married her, and to him she bore **⁨أبو عمير⁩** and
  **عبد الله بن أبي طلحة ؓ**. سیر ج۲ ص۳۰۴:

  > <div dir="rtl">⁨فَمَاتَ زَوْجُهَا مَالِكُ بنُ النَّضْرِ، ثُمَّ تَزَوَّجَهَا أَبُو طَلْحَةَ زَيْدُ بنُ سَهْلٍ الأَنْصَارِيُّ، فَوَلَدَتْ لَهُ أَبَا عُمَيْرٍ، وَعَبْدَ اللهِ⁩.</div>
  > — ⁨سیر أعلام النبلاء ج۲ ص۳۰۴⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/10906/2284
  > *"Her husband Mālik b. al-Naḍr died; then Abū Ṭalḥa Zayd b. Sahl al-Anṣārī married her, and she
  > bore him Abū ʿUmayr and ʿAbd Allāh."*

  So **⁨أبو طلحة ؓ⁩ is ⁨أنس ؓ⁩'s stepfather**, and ⁨سیر⁩ calls ⁨أنس ؓ⁩ his **⁨ربيب⁩** (stepson) explicitly:
  «رَوَى عَنْهُ: رَبِيْبُهُ؛ أَنَسُ بنُ مَالِكٍ» (ج۲ ص۲۷).

**So, exactly:**

**The clan.** All of them are **⁨بنو النجار⁩**, of the **⁨خزرج⁩**. The two nasab strings, verbatim:

> <div dir="rtl">⁨البَرَاءُ بنُ مَالِكِ بنِ النَّضْرِ بنِ ضَمْضَمٍ الأَنْصَارِيُّ ابْنِ زَيْدِ بنِ حَرَامِ بنِ جُنْدَبِ بنِ عَامِرِ بنِ غَنْمِ بنِ عَدِيِّ بنِ النَّجَّارِ الأَنْصَارِيُّ، النَّجَّارِيُّ، المَدَنِيُّ⁩.</div>
> — ⁨سیر أعلام النبلاء ج۱ ص۱۹۵⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/10906/1621

> <div dir="rtl">⁨بِنْتُ مِلْحَانَ بنِ خَالِدِ بنِ زَيْدِ بنِ حَرَامِ بنِ جُنْدُبِ بنِ عَامِرِ بنِ غَنْمِ بنِ عَدِيِّ بنِ النَّجَّارِ الأَنْصَارِيَّةُ، الخَزْرَجِيَّةُ⁩.</div>
> — ⁨سیر أعلام النبلاء ج۲ ص۳۰۴⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/10906/2284

The two lines converge at **⁨زيد بن حرام بن جندب بن عامر بن غنم بن عدي بن النجار⁩** — i.e. **⁨أم سليم ؓ⁩
married a man of her own sub-clan**. ⁨أبو طلحة ؓ⁩'s line is given differently and should not be merged
with theirs:

> <div dir="rtl">⁨زَيْدُ بنُ سَهْلِ بنِ الأَسْوَدِ بنِ حَرَامِ بنِ عَمْرِو بنِ زَيْدِ مَنَاةَ بنِ عَدِيِّ بنِ عَمْرِو بنِ مَالِكِ بنِ النَّجَّارِ الخَزْرَجِيُّ، النَّجَّارِيُّ⁩.</div>
> — ⁨سیر أعلام النبلاء ج۲ ص۲۷⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/10906/2007

**Safe podium formula:** *"All of them are of ⁨بنو النجار⁩, the Khazrajī clan that Dhahabī ⁨رحمہ اللہ⁩
describes as the Prophet's ﷺ maternal kin."* — ⁨سیر ج۲ ص۲۷⁩ says of ⁨أبو طلحة ؓ⁩:
«⁨صَاحِبُ رَسُوْلِ اللهِ ﷺ⁩ **⁨وَمِنْ بَنِي أَخْوَالِهِ⁩**». Do **not** claim on the platform that
⁨أبو طلحة ؓ⁩ and ⁨أم سليم ؓ⁩ were of the *same* sub-branch; the two nasabs printed above do not say so.

## 2. أم سليم ؓ

### 2.1 Her name — the sources disagree, and the disagreement is printed in one line

⁨سیر⁩ heads the entry with five possibilities and does not choose:

> <div dir="rtl">⁨أُمُّ سُلَيْمٍ الغُمَيْصَاءُ بِنْتُ مِلْحَانَ الأَنْصَارِيَّةُ⁩ … ⁨وَيُقَالُ⁩: ⁨الرُّمَيْصَاءُ⁩. ⁨وَيُقَالُ⁩: ⁨سَهْلَةُ⁩. ⁨وَيُقَالُ⁩: ⁨أُنَيْفَةُ⁩. ⁨وَيُقَالُ⁩: ⁨رُمَيْثَةُ⁩.</div>
> — ⁨سیر أعلام النبلاء ج۲ ص۳۰۴⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/10906/2284
> *"Umm Sulaym al-Ghumayṣāʾ bint Milḥān al-Anṣāriyya. It is also said: al-Rumayṣāʾ. It is also said:
> Sahla. It is also said: Unayfa. It is also said: Rumaytha."*

**Do not pick one.** ⁨الذہبی رحمہ اللہ⁩ did not. The Paradise ḥadīth on ⁨ج۲ ص۳۰۹⁩ uses **⁨الغُمَيْصَاء⁩**;
the parallel from ⁨جابر ؓ⁩ in the editor's footnote to the same page uses **⁨الرُّمَيْصَاء⁩**. Both are in
the ṣaḥīḥ material; that is precisely why the name is unsettled. On a slide, write
**«⁨أم سليم بنت ملحان ؓ⁩»** and, if the given name is wanted, print the list.

### 2.2 She believed first, and her husband left

> <div dir="rtl">⁨أَنَّهَا آمَنَتْ بِرَسُوْلِ اللهِ⁩ -⁨صَلَّى اللَّهُ عَلَيْهِ وَسَلَّمَ⁩-. ⁨قَالَتْ⁩: ⁨فَجَاءَ أَبُو أَنَسٍ وَكَانَ غَائِباً، فَقَالَ⁩: ⁨أَصَبَوْتِ؟ فَقَالَتْ⁩: ⁨مَا صَبَوْتُ، وَلَكِنِّي آمَنْتُ⁩. ⁨وَجَعَلَتْ تُلَقِّنُ أَنَساً⁩: ⁨قُلْ⁩: ⁨لاَ إِلَهَ إِلاَّ اللهُ، قُلْ⁩: ⁨أَشْهَدُ أَنَّ مُحَمَّداً رَسُوْلَ اللهِ، فَفَعَلَ، فَيَقُوْلُ لَهَا أَبُوْهُ⁩: ⁨لاَ تُفْسِدِي عَلَيَّ ابْنِي⁩. ⁨فَتَقُوْلُ⁩: ⁨إِنِّي لاَ أُفْسِدُه⁩</div>
> — ⁨سیر أعلام النبلاء ج۲ ص۳۰۵⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/10906/2285
> *"She believed in the Messenger of Allah ﷺ. She said: Anas's father came — he had been away — and
> said: have you gone over? She said: I have not gone over; I have believed. And she began teaching
> Anas: say, there is no god but Allah; say, I bear witness that Muḥammad is the Messenger of Allah.
> And he did. His father would say to her: do not spoil my son for me. She would say: I am not
> spoiling him."*

The same page then reports that when ⁨مالك⁩ went out an enemy of his met him and killed him
(«⁨فَخَرَجَ مَالِكٌ، فَلَقِيَهُ عَدُوٌّ لَهُ، فَقَتَلَهُ⁩»), after which she said she would not wean
⁨أنس⁩ until he left the breast himself and would not marry until ⁨أنس⁩ told her to.

⚠ **Two different accounts of how ⁨أنس ؓ⁩'s father died sit in the same work.** ⁨ج۲ ص۳۰⁩ has:

> <div dir="rtl">⁨قَالَ مَالِكٌ⁩ - ⁨وَالِدُ أَنَسٍ⁩ - ⁨لامْرَأَتِهِ⁩: ⁨أَرَى هَذَا الرَّجُلَ يُحَرِّمُ الخَمْرَ⁩. ⁨فَانْطَلَقَ حَتَّى أَتَى الشَّامَ، فَهَلَكَ هُنَاكَ⁩.</div>
> — ⁨سیر أعلام النبلاء ج۲ ص۳۰⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/10906/2010
> *"Mālik, Anas's father, said to his wife: I see this man forbidding wine. And he set off until he
> reached Syria, and perished there."*

Say **"the reports differ — one says he was killed by an enemy, one says he left for Syria and died
there"** and stop. Do not choose.

### 2.3 The dower that was Islam — three wordings, all in ⁨سیر⁩

**(a) ⁨ج۲ ص۳۰۵⁩**, ⁨خالد بن مخلد⁩ ← ⁨محمد بن موسى⁩ ← ⁨عبد الله بن عبد الله بن أبي طلحة⁩ ← ⁨أنس ؓ⁩:

> <div dir="rtl">⁨خَطَبَ أَبُو طَلْحَةَ أُمَّ سُلَيْمٍ، فَقَالَتْ⁩: ⁨إِنِّي قَدْ آمَنْتُ، فَإِنْ تَابَعْتَنِي تَزَوَّجْتُكَ⁩. ⁨قَالَ⁩: ⁨فَأَنَا عَلَى مِثْلِ مَا أَنْتِ عَلَيْهِ⁩. ⁨فَتَزَوَّجَتْهُ أُمُّ سُلَيْمٍ، وَكَانَ صَدَاقَهَا الإِسْلاَمُ⁩.</div>
> — ⁨سیر أعلام النبلاء ج۲ ص۳۰۵⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/10906/2285
> *"Abū Ṭalḥa proposed to Umm Sulaym. She said: I have believed; if you follow me I will marry you.
> He said: then I am upon the like of what you are upon. So Umm Sulaym married him, and her dower
> was Islam."*

**(b) ⁨ج۲ ص۳۰۶⁩**, ⁨سليمان بن المغيرة⁩ ← ⁨ثابت⁩ ← ⁨أنس ؓ⁩ — this is the one with the argument in it:

> <div dir="rtl">⁨إِنَّهُ لاَ يَنْبَغِي أَنْ أَتَزَوَّجَ مُشْرِكاً، أَمَا تَعْلَمُ يَا أَبَا طَلْحَةَ أَنَّ آلِهَتَكُمْ يَنْحَتُهَا عَبْدُ آلِ فُلاَنٍ، وَأَنَّكُمْ لَو أَشْعَلْتُم فِيْهَا نَاراً لاحْتَرَقَتْ⁩. ⁨قَالَ⁩: ⁨فَانْصَرَفَ وَفِي قَلْبِهِ ذَلِكَ، ثُمَّ أَتَاهَا، وَقَالَ⁩: ⁨الَّذِي عَرَضْتِ عَلَيَّ قَدْ قَبِلْتُ⁩. ⁨قَالَ⁩: ⁨فَمَا كَانَ لَهَا مَهْرٌ إِلاَّ الإِسْلاَمُ⁩.</div>
> — ⁨سیر أعلام النبلاء ج۲ ص۳۰۶⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/10906/2286
> *"It is not fitting that I should marry a mushrik. Do you not know, Abū Ṭalḥa, that your gods are
> carved by the slave of the family of so-and-so, and that if you set a fire in them they would
> burn? He went away with that in his heart; then he came to her and said: what you offered me, I
> have accepted. And she had no dower but Islam."*

**(c) ⁨ج۲ ص۲۹⁩**, ⁨جعفر بن سليمان⁩ ← ⁨ثابت⁩ ← ⁨أنس ؓ⁩ — the wording most worth printing, because ⁨ثابت⁩'s own
comment closes it:

> <div dir="rtl">⁨أَمَا إِنِّي فِيْكَ لَرَاغِبَةٌ، وَمَا مِثْلُكَ يُرَدُّ، وَلَكِنَّكَ كَافِرٌ، فَإِنْ تُسْلِمْ فَذَلِكَ مَهْرِي، لاَ أَسْأَلُكَ غَيْرَهُ⁩. ⁨فَأَسْلَمَ، وَتَزَوَّجَهَا⁩. ⁨قَالَ ثَابِتٌ⁩: ⁨فَمَا سَمِعْنَا بِمَهْرٍ كَانَ قَطُّ أَكْرَمَ مِنْ مَهْرِ أُمِّ سُلَيْمٍ⁩: ⁨الإِسْلاَمِ⁩.</div>
> — ⁨سیر أعلام النبلاء ج۲ ص۲۹⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/10906/2009
> *"I am indeed willing in your case, and a man like you is not refused; but you are a disbeliever.
> If you accept Islam, that is my dower — I ask you for nothing else. So he accepted Islam and
> married her. Thābit said: we never heard of a dower nobler than the dower of Umm Sulaym: Islam."*

**Isnād note, and it is good news for once.** The ⁨سیر⁩ editor's footnote to ⁨ج۲ ص۳۰۵⁩ records ⁨النسائي⁩
٦/١١٤, «⁨باب التزويج على الإسلام⁩», and calls that chain **⁨صحيح⁩**; the footnote to ⁨ج۲ ص۳۰۶⁩ calls
⁨سليمان بن المغيرة⁩'s chain **⁨صحيح⁩**; the footnote to ⁨ج۲ ص۲۹⁩ likewise **⁨إسناده صحيح⁩**. This is not
⁨مغازی⁩ material — it is ṣaḥīḥ-grade and can be told without hedging.

**Who transmits it:** in every wording the narrator is **⁨أنس بن مالك ؓ⁩** — the son of the marriage,
reporting his own mother's condition to his own stepfather. Say that; it is the strongest thing about
the report.

### 2.4 The dagger — at ⁨أحد⁩ and at ⁨حنين⁩

Two separate reports, two separate days. Do not merge them.

**At ⁨أحد⁩**, ⁨ابن سيرين⁩, one line only:

> <div dir="rtl">⁨كَانَتْ أُمُّ سُلَيْمٍ مَعَ النَّبِيِّ⁩ -⁨صَلَّى اللَّهُ عَلَيْهِ وَسَلَّمَ⁩- ⁨يَوْمَ أُحُدٍ، وَمَعَهَا خِنْجَر⁩</div>
> — ⁨سیر أعلام النبلاء ج۲ ص۳۰۴⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/10906/2284
> *"Umm Sulaym was with the Prophet ﷺ on the day of Uḥud, and she had a dagger with her."*

**At ⁨حنين⁩**, ⁨حماد بن سلمة⁩ ← ⁨ثابت⁩ ← ⁨أنس ؓ⁩ (the editor: «⁨إسناده صحيح⁩»):

> <div dir="rtl">⁨أَنَّ أُمَّ سُلَيْمٍ اتَّخَذَتْ خِنْجَراً يَوْمَ حُنَيْنٍ، فَقَالَ أَبُو طَلْحَةَ⁩: ⁨يَا رَسُوْلَ اللهِ⁩! ⁨هَذِهِ أُمُّ سُلَيْمٍ مَعَهَا خِنْجَرٌ⁩. ⁨فَقَالَتْ⁩: ⁨يَا رَسُوْلَ اللهِ⁩! ⁨إِنْ دَنَا مِنِّي مُشْرِكٌ بَقَرْتُ بِهِ بَطْنَهُ⁩.</div>
> — ⁨سیر أعلام النبلاء ج۲ ص۳۰۴⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/10906/2284
> *"Umm Sulaym took up a dagger on the day of Ḥunayn. Abū Ṭalḥa said: Messenger of Allah, this is
> Umm Sulaym — she has a dagger. She said: Messenger of Allah, if a mushrik comes near me I will open
> his belly with it."*

**⁨البدایہ ج۴ ص۵۸۹⁩ carries the fuller version**, from ⁨أحمد⁩ ← ⁨عفان⁩ ← ⁨حماد بن سلمة⁩ ← ⁨إسحاق بن عبد الله⁩
⁨بن أبي طلحة⁩ ← ⁨أنس ؓ⁩, and the page names ⁨مسلم ١٨٠٩⁩ for the dagger portion:

> <div dir="rtl">⁨قال⁩: ⁨ولقي أبو طلحة أمَّ سليم ومعها خَنجرٌ، فقال أبو طلحة⁩: ⁨ما هذا؟ فقالت⁩: ⁨إن دنا مني بعض المشركين أن أبعج به بطنه⁩. ⁨فقال أبو طلحة⁩: ⁨أما تسمع ما تقول أمُّ سليمٍ؟ فضحك رسول الله ﷺ، فقالت⁩: ⁨يا رسول الله، اقتلْ من بعدنا من الطُّلقاء؛ انهزموا بك⁩. ⁨فقال⁩: "⁨إن الله قد كفى وأحسن يا أمَّ سليمٍ⁩".</div>
> — ⁨البدایہ والنہایہ ج۴ ص۵۸۹⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/30097/2078
> *"Abū Ṭalḥa met Umm Sulaym and she had a dagger. Abū Ṭalḥa said: what is this? She said: if any of
> the mushrikūn comes near me, that I may split his belly with it. Abū Ṭalḥa said: do you not hear
> what Umm Sulaym is saying? And the Messenger of Allah ﷺ laughed. She said: Messenger of Allah, kill
> those of the Ṭulaqāʾ who are behind us — they fled with you. He said: 'Allah has sufficed and done
> well, Umm Sulaym.'"*

⚠ **Delivery caution.** The second half — her request about the **⁨الطلقاء⁩** — is on the page and is
in ⁨مسلم⁩, but it is a Companion asking for other Companions to be killed, and the answer is a
refusal. If it is told at all, it must be told **whole**, with the Prophet's ﷺ reply as the point:
*she asked; he ﷺ declined, and said Allah had sufficed.* Never tell the first half alone. Safest
option for a single evening: use the ⁨سیر⁩ wording (dagger and the reply about the mushrik) and stop
there.

### 2.5 ⁨أم سليم ؓ⁩ and ⁨عائشہ ؓ⁩ carrying water at ⁨أحد⁩

From ⁨أنس ؓ⁩, in the same passage where ⁨أبو طلحة ؓ⁩ shields the Prophet ﷺ (⁨البخاري⁩, per the editor's
footnote at ⁨ج۲ ص۳۱⁩):

> <div dir="rtl">⁨فَلَقَدْ رَأَيْتُ عَائِشَةَ وَأُمَّ سُلَيْمٍ وَإِنَّهُمَا لَمُشَمِّرَاتٍ، أَرَى خَدَمَ سُوْقِهِمَا تَنْقُزَانِ، القِرَبُ عَلَى مُتُوْنِهِمَا، وَتُفْرِغَاَنِهَا فِي أَفْوَاهِ القَوْمِ، وَتَرْجِعَانِ، فَتَمْلآنِهَا⁩.</div>
> — ⁨سیر أعلام النبلاء ج۲ ص۳۱⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/10906/2011
> *"I saw ʿĀʾisha and Umm Sulaym with their garments tucked up, and I could see the anklets on their
> legs; they were hurrying along with waterskins on their backs, emptying them into the mouths of the
> men, then going back and filling them again."*

### 2.6 The house the Prophet ﷺ entered

> <div dir="rtl">⁨لَمْ يَكُنْ رَسُوْلُ اللهِ⁩ -⁨صَلَّى اللَّهُ عَلَيْهِ وَسَلَّمَ⁩- ⁨يَدْخُلُ بَيْتاً غَيْرَ بَيْتِ أُمِّ سُلَيْمٍ، فَقِيْلَ لَهُ، فَقَالَ⁩: (⁨إِنِّي أَرْحَمُهَا، قُتِلَ⁩</div>
> — ⁨سیر أعلام النبلاء ج۲ ص۳۰۶⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/10906/2286

The sentence runs over the page break; ⁨سیر⁩ completes it at the head of ⁨ص۳۰۷⁩:

> <div dir="rtl">⁨أَخُوْهَا مَعِي⁩</div>
> — ⁨سیر أعلام النبلاء ج۲ ص۳۰۷⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/10906/2287
> *English:* (the two pages together) "The Messenger of Allah ﷺ would not enter any house but the
> house of Umm Sulaym. It was said to him about this, and he said: 'I have compassion for her — her
> brother was killed with me.'"

⁨الذہبی رحمہ اللہ⁩ then supplies the identification in his own voice on ⁨ج۲ ص۳۰۷⁩:
«⁨قُلْتُ⁩: ⁨أَخُوْهَا هُوَ حَرَامُ بنُ مِلْحَانَ الشَّهِيْدُ⁩». The editor's footnote to the ḥadīth cites
**⁨البخاري ٦⁩/⁨٣٧ ومسلم⁩ (٢٤٥٥)**. This single sentence is the hinge of the whole strand: *the reason the
Prophet ﷺ came to that house at all was a death in it.*

### 2.7 The Paradise ḥadīth

> <div dir="rtl">⁨قَالَ النَّبِيُّ⁩ -⁨صَلَّى اللَّهُ عَلَيْهِ وَسَلَّمَ⁩-: (⁨دَخَلْتُ الجَنَّةَ، فَسَمِعْتُ خَشْفَةً بَيْنَ يَدَيَّ، فَإِذَا أَنَا بِالغُمَيْصَاءِ بِنْتِ مِلْحَان⁩</div>
> — ⁨سیر أعلام النبلاء ج۲ ص۳۰۹⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/10906/2289
> *"The Prophet ﷺ said: 'I entered Paradise, and I heard a soft footfall before me, and there was
> al-Ghumayṣāʾ bint Milḥān.'"*

Editor's footnote: «⁨إسناده صحيح⁩»، **⁨مسلم⁩ (٢٤٥٦)**; and the parallel from ⁨جابر ؓ⁩,
**⁨البخاري ٧⁩/⁨٣٤ ومسلم⁩ (٢٤٥٧)**, which reads «⁨فإذا أنا بالرميصاء امرأة أبي طلحة⁩».

### 2.8 What ⁨سیر⁩ does **not** give for her

**No death date. No death year. No place of death.** The entry ends at ⁨ج۲ ص۳۱۱⁩ with a ḥadīth count
(«⁨رَوَتْ⁩: ⁨أَرْبَعَةَ عَشَرَ حَدِيْثاً⁩») and nothing more. Do not put a year on a slide for her.

## 3. أبو طلحة الأنصاري ؓ — زيد بن سهل

### 3.1 Who he was

> <div dir="rtl">⁨صَاحِبُ رَسُوْلِ اللهِ⁩ -⁨صَلَّى اللَّهُ عَلَيْهِ وَسَلَّمَ⁩- ⁨وَمِنْ بَنِي أَخْوَالِهِ، وَأَحَدُ أَعْيَانِ البَدْرِيِّيْنَ، وَأَحَدُ النُّقَبَاءِ الاثْنَيْ عَشَرَ لَيْلَةَ العَقَبَة⁩</div>
> — ⁨سیر أعلام النبلاء ج۲ ص۲۷⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/10906/2007
> *"A Companion of the Messenger of Allah ﷺ and of the clan of his maternal kin; one of the leading
> men of Badr; and one of the twelve naqībs on the night of ʿAqaba."*

Cross-reference: `docs/research/medina-ansar-structure.md` already holds the twelve ⁨نقباء⁩. **He is
already on that list** — this note adds that the ⁨نقيب⁩ of ʿAqaba is the same man who married
⁨أم سليم ؓ⁩ for the dower of Islam. ⁨أحمد بن البرقي⁩, quoted at ⁨ج۲ ص۲۹⁩: «⁨أَبُو طَلْحَةَ بَدْرِيٌّ،⁩
⁨نَقِيْبٌ، صَلَّى عَلَيْهِ عُثْمَانُ⁩». His physical description at ⁨ج۲ ص۳۴⁩:
«⁨وَكَانَ جَلْداً، صَيِّتاً، آدَمَ، مَرْبُوْعاً، لاَ يُغَيِّرُ شَيْبَهُ⁩» — *strong, loud-voiced,
brown-skinned, of middling height; he did not dye his grey.*

### 3.2 The voice

> <div dir="rtl">⁨وَهُوَ الَّذِي قَالَ فِيْهِ رَسُوْلُ اللهِ⁩ -⁨صَلَّى اللَّهُ عَلَيْهِ وَسَلَّمَ⁩-: (⁨صَوْتُ أَبِي طَلْحَةَ فِي الجَيْشِ خَيْرٌ مِنْ فِئَة⁩</div>
> — ⁨سیر أعلام النبلاء ج۲ ص۲۸⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/10906/2008
> *"He is the one of whom the Messenger of Allah ﷺ said: 'The voice of Abū Ṭalḥa in the army is
> better than a company of men.'"*

⚠ Three wordings sit within seven pages, and their chains differ. ⁨ج۲ ص۳۲⁩ has
«⁨لَصَوْتُ أَبِي طَلْحَةَ⁩ **⁨أَشَدُّ عَلَى المُشْرِكِيْنَ⁩** ⁨مِنْ فِئَةٍ⁩» (⁨حماد⁩ ← ⁨ثابت⁩ ← ⁨أنس⁩), and
«⁨لَصَوْتُ أَبِي طَلْحَةَ فِي الجَيْشِ⁩ **⁨خَيْرٌ مِنْ أَلْفِ رَجُلٍ⁩**» — of which the editor's footnote to
⁨ج۲ ص۲۸⁩ says the chain is «⁨حسن في الشواهد⁩», and of the ⁨ص۳۲⁩ version that ⁨علي بن زيد بن جدعان⁩ is ⁨ضعيف⁩.
**Print the first wording, captioned ⁨سیر ج۲ ص۲۸⁩, and no other.**

### 3.3 ⁨أحد⁩ — the shield and the arrows

> <div dir="rtl">⁨لَمَّا كَانَ يَوْمُ أُحُدٍ، انْهَزَمَ نَاسٌ عَنْ رَسُوْلِ اللهِ، وَأَبُو طَلْحَةَ بَيْنَ يَدَيْهِ مُجَوِّباً عَلَيْهِ بِحَجْفَةٍ⁩. ⁨وَكَانَ رَامِياً شَدِيْدَ النَّزْعِ، كَسَرَ يَوْمَئِذٍ قَوْسَيْنِ أَوْ ثَلاَثَةً⁩... ⁨فَيَقُوْلُ أَبُو طَلْحَةَ⁩: ⁨يَا نَبِيَّ اللهِ، بِأَبِي أَنْتَ، لاَ تُشْرِفْ، لاَ يُصِيْبُكَ سَهْمٌ، نَحْرِي دُوْنَ نَحْرِكَ⁩.</div>
> — ⁨سیر أعلام النبلاء ج۲ ص۳۱⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/10906/2011
> *"On the day of Uḥud people fled from the Messenger of Allah, and Abū Ṭalḥa was in front of him,
> shielding him with a buckler. He was an archer of a strong draw; he broke two or three bows that
> day... And Abū Ṭalḥa would say: Prophet of Allah — may my father be your ransom — do not look out,
> lest an arrow strike you. My throat before your throat."*

Editor's footnote: **⁨البخاري ٧⁩/⁨٢٨٧، غزوة أحد⁩**. And ⁨ج۲ ص۳۲⁩: «⁨نَفْسِي لِنَفْسِكَ الفِدَاءُ،⁩
⁨وَوَجْهِي لِوَجْهِكَ الوِقَاءُ⁩» — *my life a ransom for your life, my face a shield for your face.*

⚠ **One textual snag worth knowing.** ⁨ج۲ ص۳۰⁩ prints his own words as
«⁨لَقَدْ سَقَطَ السَّيْفُ مِنِّي⁩ **⁨يَوْمَ بَدْرٍ⁩**⁨، لِمَا غَشِيَنَا مِنَ النُّعَاسِ⁩», but the editor's
footnote on the same page gives ⁨البخاري ٨⁩/١٧١ with **«⁨يوم أحد⁩»**, and ⁨ج۲ ص۳۱⁩ ends
«⁨فَلَقَدْ وَقَعَ السَّيْفُ مِنْ يَدِ أَبِي طَلْحَةَ مَرَّتَيْنِ أَوْ ثَلاَثاً مِنَ النُّعَاسِ⁩» inside an
⁨أحد⁩ narration. **Do not attach the drowsiness to ⁨بدر⁩ on a slide.** If used at all, attach it to ⁨أحد⁩
and cite ⁨البخاري⁩ through the ⁨سیر⁩ editor's footnote, or leave it out.

### 3.4 ⁨حنين⁩ — twenty men

> <div dir="rtl">⁨أَنَّ رَسُوْلَ اللهِ⁩ -⁨صَلَّى اللَّهُ عَلَيْهِ وَسَلَّمَ⁩- ⁨قَالَ يَوْمَ حُنَيْنٍ⁩: (⁨مَنْ قَتَلَ قَتِيْلاً فَلَهُ سَلَبُهُ⁩) . ⁨فَقَتَلَ أَبُو طَلْحَةَ يَوْمَئِذٍ عِشْرِيْنَ رَجُلاً، وَأَخَذَ أَسْلاَبَهُم⁩</div>
> — ⁨سیر أعلام النبلاء ج۲ ص۳۲⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/10906/2012
> *"The Messenger of Allah ﷺ said on the day of Ḥunayn: 'Whoever kills a man, the spoils of that man
> are his.' And Abū Ṭalḥa killed twenty men that day and took their spoils."*

Editor: «⁨إسناده صحيح⁩»، **⁨أبو داود⁩ (٢٧١٨)**. ⁨البدایہ ج۴ ص۵۸۹⁩ carries the same from ⁨أحمد⁩.

### 3.5 بيرحاء — «أحبُّ أموالي إليّ»

> <div dir="rtl">⁨كَانَ أَبُو طَلْحَةَ أَكْثَرَ أَنْصَارِيٍّ بِالمَدِيْنَةِ مَالاً مِنْ نَخْلٍ، فَقَالَ⁩: ⁨يَا رَسُوْلَ اللهِ، إِنَّ أَحَبَّ أَمْوَالِي إِلَيَّ بَيْرُحَاءُ، وَإِنَّهَا صَدَقَةٌ للهِ، أَرْجُوْ بِرَّهَا وَذُخْرَهَا، فَضَعْهَا يَا رَسُوْلَ اللهِ حَيْثُ أَرَاكَ اللهُ⁩. ⁨فَقَالَ⁩: (⁨بَخٍ⁩! ⁨ذَلِكَ مَالٌ رَابِحٌ، وَإِنِّي أَرَى أَنْ تَجْعَلَهَا فِي الأَقْرَبِيْنَ⁩).</div>
> — ⁨سیر أعلام النبلاء ج۲ ص۳۳⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/10906/2013
> *"Abū Ṭalḥa had more property in date-palms than any of the Anṣār at Medina. He said: Messenger of
> Allah, the dearest of my property to me is Bayruḥāʾ, and it is a ṣadaqa for Allah — I hope for its
> goodness and its treasure with Him. Place it, Messenger of Allah, where Allah shows you. He said:
> 'Bakhkh! That is profitable property. I think you should put it among your near kin.'"*

Editor's footnote: **⁨مالك ٢⁩/⁨٩٩٥، البخاري ٣⁩/٢٥٧ (⁨كتاب الزكاة، باب الزكاة على الأقارب⁩)⁨، ومسلم⁩ (٩٩٨)**,
and the ⁨البخاري⁩ chapter «⁨لن تنالوا البر حتى تنفقوا مما تحبون⁩». The connection to
⁨آل عمران ۹۲⁩ is the editor's chapter-heading, not ⁨سیر⁩'s own sentence — attribute it as such.

### 3.6 The end — and ⁨سیر⁩ disagreeing with itself

**ج۲ ص۲۸:**

> <div dir="rtl">⁨قِيْلَ⁩: ⁨إِنَّهُ غَزَا بَحْرَ الرُّوْمِ، فَتُوُفِّيَ فِي السَّفِيْنَةِ⁩. ⁨وَالأَشْهَرُ⁩: ⁨أَنَّهُ مَاتَ بِالمَدِيْنَةِ، وَصَلَّى عَلَيْهِ عُثْمَانُ فِي سَنَةِ أَرْبَعٍ وَثَلاَثِيْنَ⁩.</div>
> — ⁨سیر أعلام النبلاء ج۲ ص۲۸⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/10906/2008
> *"It is said that he raided the sea of the Byzantines and died on the ship. The better-known view
> is that he died at Medina, and ʿUthmān prayed over him, in the year 34."*

**⁨ج۲ ص۳۴⁩** then gives the sea account in full, with a chain the editor calls **⁨صحيح⁩**:

> <div dir="rtl">⁨أَنَّ أَبَا طَلْحَةَ قَرَأَ⁩: {⁨انْفِرُوا خِفَافاً وَثِقَالاً⁩} [⁨التَّوْبَةُ⁩: ٤٢] ⁨، فَقَالَ⁩: ⁨اسْتَنْفَرَنَا اللهُ وَأَمَرَنَا، شُيُوْخَنَا وَشَبَابَنَا، جَهِّزُوْنِي⁩. ⁨فَقَالَ بَنُوْهُ⁩: ⁨يَرْحَمُكَ اللهُ⁩! ⁨إِنَّكَ قَدْ غَزَوْتَ عَلَى عَهْدِ رَسُوْلِ اللهِ⁩ -⁨صَلَّى اللَّهُ عَلَيْهِ وَسَلَّمَ⁩- ⁨وَأَبِي بَكْرٍ، وَعُمَرَ، وَنَحْنُ نَغْزُو عَنْكَ الآنَ⁩. ⁨قَالَ⁩: ⁨فَغَزَا البَحْرَ، فَمَاتَ، فَلَمْ يَجِدُوا لَهُ جَزِيْرَةً يَدْفِنُوْنَهُ فِيْهَا إِلاَّ بَعْدَ سَبْعَةِ أَيَّامٍ، فَلَمْ يَتَغَيَّر⁩</div>
> — ⁨سیر أعلام النبلاء ج۲ ص۳۴⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/10906/2014
> *"Abū Ṭalḥa read: {Go forth, light and heavy}. He said: Allah has called us out and commanded us —
> our old and our young. Equip me. His sons said: may Allah have mercy on you; you fought in the time
> of the Messenger of Allah ﷺ and of Abū Bakr and of ʿUmar — we will fight in your place now. He
> said: and he went to sea, and died; and they found no island to bury him on for seven days, and he
> did not change."*

⚠ **Two cautions.** (i) The printed ⁨سیر⁩ cites the verse as **[⁨التوبة⁩: ٤٢]**; the verse
«⁨انفروا خفافًا وثقالًا⁩» is **⁨التوبۃ ۴۱⁩**. Verify against a muṣḥaf before printing, and print ۴۱.
(ii) **Give no single death year.** ⁨ج۲ ص۳۴⁩ lists **⁨۳۴ھ⁩**, then **⁨خليفة⁩ alone: ⁨۳۲ھ⁩**, then
**«⁨وَقِيْلَ⁩: ⁨مَاتَ سَنَةَ إِحْدَى وَخَمْسِيْنَ⁩»** (⁨۵۱ھ⁩). Say: *"the reports give 32, 34, and 51 — and
they differ on whether he died at Medina or at sea."*

## 4. أنس بن مالك ؓ

### 4.1 The header ⁨الذہبی رحمہ اللہ⁩ gives him

> <div dir="rtl">⁨الإِمَامُ، المُفْتِي، المُقْرِئُ، المُحَدِّثُ، رَاوِيَةُ الإِسْلاَمِ، أَبُو حَمْزَةَ الأَنْصَارِيُّ، الخَزْرَجِيُّ، النَّجَّارِيُّ، المَدَنِيُّ، خَادِمُ رَسُوْلِ اللهِ⁩ -⁨صَلَّى اللَّهُ عَلَيْهِ وَسَلَّمَ⁩ - ⁨وَقَرَابَتُهُ مِنَ النِّسَاءِ، وَتِلْمِيذُهُ، وَتَبَعُهُ، وَآخِرُ أَصْحَابِهِ مَوْتا⁩</div>
> — ⁨سیر أعلام النبلاء ج۳ ص۳۹۶⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/10906/3004
> *"The imām, the muftī, the reciter, the ḥadīth-master, the transmitter of Islam; Abū Ḥamza
> al-Anṣārī al-Khazrajī al-Najjārī al-Madanī; the servant of the Messenger of Allah ﷺ and his kin
> through the women; his pupil, his follower, and the last of his Companions to die."*

These descriptors are **the tradition's own**, from ⁨الذہبی⁩'s pen. Use them; invent nothing.

### 4.2 The handing over

> <div dir="rtl">⁨جَاءتْ بِي أُمُّ سُلَيْمٍ إِلَى رَسُوْلِ اللهِ⁩ -⁨صَلَّى اللَّهُ عَلَيْهِ وَسَلَّمَ⁩ - ⁨قَدْ أَزَّرَتْنِي بِنِصْفِ خِمَارِهَا، وَرَدَّتْنِي بِبَعْضِهِ، فَقَالَتْ⁩: ⁨يَا رَسُوْلَ اللهِ⁩! ⁨هَذَا أُنَيْسٌ ابْنِي، أَتَيْتُكَ بِهِ يَخْدُمُكَ، فَادْعُ اللهَ لَهُ⁩. ⁨فَقَالَ⁩: (⁨اللَّهُمَّ أَكْثِرْ مَالَهُ وَوَلَدَه⁩</div>
> — ⁨سیر أعلام النبلاء ج۳ ص۳۹۸⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/10906/3006
> *"Umm Sulaym brought me to the Messenger of Allah ﷺ, having made me a lower garment of half her
> head-covering and a cloak of the rest of it, and said: Messenger of Allah, this is Unays, my son.
> I have brought him to you to serve you — so pray to Allah for him. He said: 'O Allah, increase his
> wealth and his children.'"*

Editor: **⁨مسلم⁩ (٢٤٨١)**. The parallel on ⁨ج۳ ص۳۹۸⁩ from ⁨علي بن زيد⁩ (of whom ⁨الذہبی⁩ says «⁨وَفِيْهِ⁩
⁨لِينٌ⁩») has her say she had nothing else to give as a gift:

> <div dir="rtl">⁨يَا رَسُوْلَ اللهِ⁩! ⁨لَمْ يَبْقَ رَجُلٌ وَلاَ امْرَأَةٌ مِنَ الأَنْصَارِ إِلاَّ وَقَدْ أَتْحَفَكَ بِتُحْفَةٍ، وَإِنِّي لاَ أَقْدِرُ عَلَى مَا أُتْحِفُكَ بِهِ إِلاَّ ابْنِي هَذَا، فَخُذْهُ، فَلْيَخْدُمْكَ مَا بَدَا لَكَ⁩. ⁨قَالَ⁩: ⁨فَخَدَمْتُهُ عَشْرَ سِنِيْنَ، فَمَا ضَرَبَنِي، وَلاَ سَبَّنِي، وَلاَ عَبَسَ فِي وَجْهِي⁩.</div>
> — ⁨سیر أعلام النبلاء ج۳ ص۳۹۸⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/10906/3006
> *"Messenger of Allah, there is not a man or a woman of the Anṣār left who has not given you a gift,
> and I can manage no gift for you but this son of mine — take him, and let him serve you as long as
> you wish. He said: so I served him ten years, and he never struck me, never abused me, and never
> frowned in my face."*

⚠ The ⁨سیر⁩ editor notes that **this wording is not ⁨الترمذي⁩'s** but ⁨أبو يعلى⁩'s. **Do not caption it
«⁨رواه الترمذي⁩».** Caption it **⁨سیر ج۳ ص۳۹۸⁩** and say ⁨الذہبی⁩ himself flags «⁨لِين⁩» in the chain.

### 4.3 The better-attested version of the same sentence

⁨سیر⁩ carries the ṣaḥīḥ wording **elsewhere in the same work**, in the ⁨السیرہ⁩ volumes:

> <div dir="rtl">⁨وقال أنس⁩: ⁨خدمته صلى الله عليه وسلم عشر سنين، فوالله ما قال لي⁩: ⁨أف قط، ولا قال لشيء فعلته⁩: ⁨لم فعلت كذا، ولا لشيء لم أفعله⁩: ⁨ألا فعلت كذا؟⁩</div>
> — ⁨سیر أعلام النبلاء، جلد السیرہ ۲ ص۳۸۶⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/10906/1044
> *"Anas said: I served him ﷺ ten years, and by Allah he never said 'uff' to me once; he never said
> of anything I did, why did you do that; nor of anything I did not do, why did you not do that."*

**This is the wording to put on a slide.** It sits in ⁨سیر⁩'s chapter «⁨ذكر صفة النبي ﷺ⁩» among ṣaḥīḥ
material. (Citation shape: **the ⁨السیرہ⁩ material is in TWO volumes, not one**, and their page numbers
collide. The Shamela headers label them «⁨جسيرة⁩ 1» and «⁨جسيرة⁩ 2»: index 1044 is **⁨جسيرة⁩ 2 ⁨ص۳۸۶⁩**,
index 544 is **⁨جسيرة⁩ 1 ⁨ص۴۰۷⁩**. So cite this one as *⁨سیر أعلام النبلاء، جلد السیرہ ۲، ص۳۸۶⁩* — never
as «⁨جلد السیرہ ص۳۸۶⁩» bare, and never as «⁨ج۲ ص۳۸۶⁩», which is a different page of a different volume.)

### 4.6 He was at ⁨تُسْتَر⁩ — where his brother died

> <div dir="rtl">⁨وَقَدْ شَهِدَ أَنَسٌ فَتْحَ تُسْتَرَ، فَقَدِمَ عَلَى عُمَرَ بِصَاحِبِهَا الهُرْمُزَانَ، فَأَسْلَمَ، وَحَسُنَ إِسْلاَمُهُ⁩.</div>
> — ⁨سیر أعلام النبلاء ج۳ ص۴۰۲⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/10906/3010
> *"Anas was present at the conquest of Tustar, and he came to ʿUmar with its lord al-Hurmuzān; and
> he accepted Islam, and his Islam was good."*

⁨البدایہ ج۷ ص۱۹۸⁩ has his own account of that morning, quoted from ⁨البخاري⁩ on the page:

> <div dir="rtl">⁨شهدت فتح تُسْتَر، وذلك عند صلاة الفجر، فاشتغل الناس بالفتح فما صلوا الصبح إلا بعد طلوع الشمس فما أحب أن لي بتلك الصلاة حمرَ النعم⁩.</div>
> — ⁨البدایہ والنہایہ ج۷ ص۱۹۸⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/30097/3344
> *"I was present at the conquest of Tustar, and it was at the time of the dawn prayer; the men were
> taken up with the conquest and did not pray Ṣubḥ until after sunrise — and I would not wish red
> camels in exchange for that prayer."*

The same page says **how** they got in, immediately before quoting ⁨أنس ؓ⁩:

> <div dir="rtl">⁨فانتدب⁩ [⁨لذلك⁩] ⁨رجال من الشجعان والأبطال، وجاؤوا فدخلوا مع الماء⁩ - ⁨كالبطّ⁩ - ⁨إلى البلد، وذلك في الليل⁩… ⁨وجاءوا إلى البوابين فأناموهم وفتحوا الأبواب، وكبَّر المسلمون فدخلوا البلد، وذلك في وقت الفجر⁩…</div>
> — ⁨البدایہ والنہایہ ج۷ ص۱۹۸⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/30097/3344
> *"Men of the bold and the champions volunteered, and came and entered the town with the water —
> like ducks — and that was at night… and they came to the gatekeepers and put them to sleep and
> opened the gates, and the Muslims called the takbīr and entered the town, and that was at the time
> of Fajr."*

**This is the sentence that welds the strand to the campaign narrative: the boy who served the
Prophet ﷺ is standing at the wall where his elder brother is killed.**

## 5. ⁨البراء بن مالك ؓ⁩ — the man who goes over the wall

### 5.1 The heading ⁨سیر⁩ gives him

> <div dir="rtl">⁨البَطَلُ الكَرَّارُ، صَاحِبُ رَسُوْلِ اللهِ⁩ -⁨صَلَّى اللَّهُ عَلَيْهِ وَسَلَّمَ⁩- ⁨وَأَخُوْ خَادِمِ النَّبِيِّ⁩ -⁨صَلَّى اللَّهُ عَلَيْهِ وَسَلَّمَ⁩- ⁨أَنَسِ بنِ مَالِكٍ⁩. ⁨شَهِدَ أُحُداً، وَبَايَعَ تَحْتَ الشَّجَرَة⁩</div>
> — ⁨سیر أعلام النبلاء ج۱ ص۱۹۵⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/10906/1621
> *"The warrior who charges again and again; a Companion of the Messenger of Allah ﷺ, and the brother
> of the Prophet's ﷺ servant Anas b. Mālik. He was present at Uḥud, and he gave the pledge beneath
> the tree."*

**«⁨البطل الكرار⁩» is ⁨الذہبی⁩'s own descriptor.** It may be used because the tradition supplies it —
but caption it to ⁨سیر ج۱ ص۱۹۵⁩, and never expand it into a slogan of our own.

Note what this heading does: **⁨الذہبی⁩ defines him by his brother.** That is the licence for the whole
strand. He was at ⁨أحد⁩, and at ⁨بيعة الرضوان⁩.

### 5.2 ⁨عمر ؓ⁩'s instruction

> <div dir="rtl">⁨قِيْلَ⁩: ⁨كَتَبَ عُمَرُ بنُ الخَطَّابِ إِلَى أُمَرَاءِ الجَيْشِ⁩: ⁨لاَ تَسْتَعْمِلُوا البَرَاءَ عَلَى جَيْشٍ، فَإِنَّهُ مَهْلَكَةٌ مِنَ المَهَالِكِ يَقْدَمُ بِهِم⁩.</div>
> — ⁨سیر أعلام النبلاء ج۱ ص۱۹۶⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/10906/1622
> *"It is said that ʿUmar b. al-Khaṭṭāb wrote to the commanders of the armies: do not put al-Barāʾ in
> command of an army, for he is a destruction among destructions — he presses forward with them."*

⚠ **The page opens with «⁨قِيْلَ⁩».** It is a report without a chain in ⁨سیر⁩; the editor traces it to
⁨المستدرك ٣⁩/٢٩١ and ⁨ابن سعد ٧⁩/١/١٠. Say **"it is reported that ʿUmar ؓ wrote..."** — not "ʿUmar ؓ
wrote". The point is not that ⁨عمر ؓ⁩ doubted him; the sentence is a compliment shaped as a warning:
*a man like this must not have other men's lives attached to his own courage.*

### 5.3 حديقة الموت

**⁨سیر ج۱ ص۱۹۶⁩** — and note that ⁨سیر⁩ itself introduces this with «⁨وَبَلَغَنَا⁩», a ⁨بلاغ⁩:

> <div dir="rtl">⁨وَبَلَغَنَا أَنَّ البَرَاءَ يَوْمَ حَرْبِ مُسَيْلِمَةَ الكَذَّابِ أَمَرَ أَصْحَابَهُ أَنْ يَحْتَمِلُوْهُ عَلَى تُرْسٍ، عَلَى أَسِنَّةِ رِمَاحِهِم، وَيُلْقُوْهُ فِي الحَدِيْقَةِ⁩. ⁨فَاقْتَحَمَ إِلَيْهِم، وَشَدَّ عَلَيْهِم، وَقَاتَلَ حَتَّى افْتَتَحَ بَابَ الحَدِيْقَةِ⁩. ⁨فَجُرِحَ يَوْمَئِذٍ بِضْعَةً وَثَمَانِيْنَ جُرْحاً، وَلِذَلِكَ أَقَامَ خَالِدُ بنُ الوَلِيْدِ عَلَيْهِ شَهْراً يُدَاوِي جِرَاحَهُ⁩.</div>
> — ⁨سیر أعلام النبلاء ج۱ ص۱۹۶⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/10906/1622
> *"It has reached us that al-Barāʾ, on the day of the war against Musaylima the liar, ordered his
> companions to carry him on a shield, upon the points of their spears, and to throw him into the
> garden. He plunged in upon them, charged them, and fought until he opened the gate of the garden.
> He was wounded that day with eighty-odd wounds, and for that reason Khālid b. al-Walīd stayed a
> month over him treating his wounds."*

**⁨الکامل ج۲ ص۲۱۸⁩** carries his own words (already logged in
`docs/research/ridda-campaign-the-conduct-of-the-wars.md`, §on ⁨حديقة الموت⁩ — build on that note, do
not re-fetch):

> <div dir="rtl">⁨قَالَ الْبَرَاءُ⁩: ⁨يَا مَعْشَرَ الْمُسْلِمِينَ، أَلْقُونِي عَلَيْهِمْ فِي الْحَدِيقَةِ⁩. ⁨فَقَالُوا⁩: ⁨لَا نَفْعَلُ، فَقَالَ⁩: ⁨وَاللَّهِ لَتَطْرَحُنَّنِي عَلَيْهِمْ بِهَا⁩! ⁨فَاحْتُمِلَ حَتَّى أَشْرَفَ عَلَى الْجِدَارِ فَاقْتَحَمَهَا عَلَيْهِمْ، وَقَاتَلَ عَلَى الْبَابِ وَفَتَحَهُ لِلْمُسْلِمِينَ⁩.</div>
> — ⁨الکامل فی التاریخ ج۲ ص۲۱۸⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/21712/909
> *"Al-Barāʾ said: Company of Muslims — throw me over onto them into the garden. They said: we will
> not. He said: by Allah, you will throw me onto them in it! So he was lifted until he topped the
> wall and plunged in upon them; and he fought at the gate and opened it for the Muslims."*

**⁨البدایہ ج۷ ص۳۴⁩** has the same, and **⁨البدایہ ج۶ ص۳۹۶⁩** repeats it in a different context — where
⁨ابن کثیر⁩ calls him **«⁨أخو أنس بن مالك⁩ — ⁨وكان الأكبر⁩»** (*the brother of Anas b. Mālik, and he was the
elder*) and adds a count: «⁨وقاتلَ المشركين حتى قتل عشرة أو أكثر⁩».

⚠ **Three textual snags on this incident.**
1. ⁨سیر⁩'s version is a **⁨بلاغ⁩** («⁨وبلغنا⁩»). ⁨الکامل⁩ gives its version with **no chain**. The incident
   is universally reported and is in the safe list three times over, but it is **not ṣaḥīḥ-graded
   material** in the way the ⁨أحد⁩ ḥadīth is. Say so if asked.
2. **⁨الکامل ج۲ ص۲۱۸⁩ calls him «⁨أَخُو أَسَدِ بْنِ مَالِكٍ⁩».** That is almost certainly a copyist's
   corruption of **⁨أَنَس⁩**; ⁨الکامل⁩ itself writes «⁨وَهُوَ أَخُو أَنَسِ بْنِ مَالِكٍ⁩» at ⁨ج۲ ص۳۶۸⁩, and
   ⁨سیر⁩ and ⁨البدایہ⁩ both say ⁨أنس⁩. **Do not quote the ⁨ج۲ ص۲۱۸⁩ kinship phrase.**
3. The same ⁨الکامل⁩ page carries an earthy physical detail about a shaking that took him before
   battle. It is on the page; it is **not** for reading aloud in this room. Logged, not used.

### 5.4 The ḥadīth about his oath

> <div dir="rtl">⁨عَنْ أَنَسٍ مَرْفُوْعاً، قَالَ⁩: (⁨كَمْ مِنْ ضَعِيْفٍ مُتَضَعِّفٍ ذِي طِمْرَيْنِ، لَوْ أَقْسَمَ عَلَى اللهِ لأَبَرَّهُ، مِنْهُم⁩: ⁨البَرَاءُ بنُ مَالِكٍ⁩).</div>
> — ⁨سیر أعلام النبلاء ج۱ ص۱۹۷⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/10906/1623
> *"From Anas, raised to the Prophet ﷺ: 'How many a weak one, counted weak, in two worn garments —
> were he to swear an oath upon Allah, He would fulfil it for him. Among them is al-Barāʾ b.
> Mālik.'"*

**Hadith number, as printed on that page by the editor:** **⁨الترمذي⁩ (٣٨٥٣)⁨، المناقب⁩: ⁨باب مناقب البراء⁩
⁨بن مالك⁩**, in the wording «⁨كم من أشعث أغبر ذي طمرين لا يؤبه له لو أقسم على الله لأبره⁩. ⁨منهم البراء بن⁩
⁨مالك⁩», graded by ⁨الترمذي⁩ «⁨حسن صحيح⁩» and by the editor «⁨وهو كما قال⁩». Also ⁨الحاكم ٣⁩/٢٩٢, ⁨صححه ووافقه⁩
⁨الذهبي⁩. **Do not quote a number the page does not give.**

The same page gives him using it:

> <div dir="rtl">⁨فَقَالُوا لَهُ⁩: ⁨يَا بَرَاءُ⁩! ⁨إِنَّ رَسُوْلَ اللهِ⁩ -⁨صَلَّى اللَّهُ عَلَيْهِ وَسَلَّمَ⁩- ⁨قَالَ⁩: ⁨إِنَّكَ لَوْ أَقْسَمْتَ عَلَى اللهِ لأَبَرَّكَ، فَأَقْسِمْ عَلَى رَبِّكَ⁩. ⁨قَالَ⁩: ⁨أُقْسِمُ عَلَيْكَ يَا رَبّ لَمَّا مَنَحْتَنَا أَكْتَافَهُم، وَذَكَرَ الحَدِيْث⁩</div>
> — ⁨سیر أعلام النبلاء ج۱ ص۱۹۷⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/10906/1623
> *"They said to him: Barāʾ! The Messenger of Allah ﷺ said that if you swore an oath upon Allah He
> would fulfil it for you — so swear upon your Lord. He said: I adjure You, my Lord, that You give us
> their backs. And he mentioned the rest of the ḥadīth."*

⚠ Note «**⁨وَذَكَرَ الحَدِيْثَ⁩**» — ⁨سیر⁩ abbreviates and does **not** give the rest. Whatever the famous
retelling adds after this point is not on this page.

### 5.5 ⁨تُسْتَر⁩ — the oath and the death

Two approved sources, near-identical, and **both** carry the second half of the request:

> <div dir="rtl">⁨قال المسلمون للبراء بن مالك⁩ - ⁨وكان مجابَ الدعوة⁩ -: ⁨يا براء، أقسم على ربك ليهزمنّهم لنا⁩. ⁨فقال⁩: ⁨اللهم اهزمهم لنا، واستشهدني⁩.</div>
> — ⁨البدایہ والنہایہ ج۷ ص۱۹۷⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/30097/3343
> *"The Muslims said to al-Barāʾ b. Mālik — and his supplication was answered — Barāʾ, swear upon
> your Lord that He will defeat them for us. He said: O Allah, defeat them for us, and take me as a
> martyr."*

> <div dir="rtl">⁨قَالَ الْمُسْلِمُونَ⁩: ⁨يَا بَرَاءُ أَقْسِمْ عَلَى رَبِّكَ لَيَهْزِمَنَّهُمْ لَنَا⁩. ⁨قَالَ⁩: ⁨اللَّهُمَّ اهْزِمْهُمْ لَنَا وَاسْتَشْهِدْنِي، وَكَانَ مُجَابَ الدَّعْوَةِ، فَهَزَمُوهُمْ⁩.</div>
> — ⁨الکامل فی التاریخ ج۲ ص۳۶۸⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/21712/1059
> *"The Muslims said: Barāʾ, swear upon your Lord that He will defeat them for us. He said: O Allah,
> defeat them for us, and take me as a martyr. And his supplication was answered — and they defeated
> them."*

**⁨سیر⁩'s account of the death itself** (⁨معمر⁩ ← ⁨أيوب⁩ ← ⁨ابن سيرين⁩; the editor: «⁨رجاله ثقات، لكنه منقطع⁩.
⁨ابن سيرين لم يسمع من البراء⁩»): ⁨أبو موسى الأشعري ؓ⁩ told him a tunnel had been found into the middle of
the city; ⁨البراء ؓ⁩ asked ⁨مجزأة بن ثور⁩ to name him a bold, hardy man from his people, and ⁨مجزأة⁩ said
«⁨فَإِنِّي أَنَا ذَلِكَ الرَّجُلُ⁩» — *I am that man*. ⁨مجزأة⁩ went in first and was crushed with a rock
as he came out; ⁨البراء ؓ⁩ came out after and fought inside the city, and was killed —
«وَقُتِلَ رَضِيَ اللهُ عَنْهُ وَفَتَحَ اللهُ عَلَيْهِم» (سیر ج۱ ص۱۹۶–۱۹۷).

> <div dir="rtl">⁨اسْتُشْهِدَ يَوْمَ فَتْحِ تُسْتَرَ، سَنَةَ عِشْرِيْنَ⁩.</div>
> — ⁨سیر أعلام النبلاء ج۱ ص۱۹۸⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/10906/1624
> *"He was martyred on the day of the conquest of Tustar, in the year 20."*

⚠ **Date conflict, and it must be shown, not hidden.** ⁨سیر⁩ says **⁨۲۰ھ⁩**. But **⁨البدایہ⁩ files the
second (forcible) conquest of ⁨تُسْتَر⁩ under ⁨سنة سبع عشرة⁩ — ⁨۱۷ھ⁩** (chapter «⁨ذكر فتح تستر ثانية عنوة⁩»,
**⁨ج۷ ص۱۹۶⁩–۱۹۸**, sitting between «⁨ثم دخلت سنة سبع عشرة⁩» at **⁨ج۷ ص۱۸۳⁩** and «⁨ثم دخلت سنة ثماني عشرة⁩»
at **⁨ج۷ ص۲۰۲⁩**), and **⁨الکامل⁩ likewise files it under ⁨سنة سبع عشرة⁩** (⁨ج۲ ص۳۶۷،⁩ Shamela index 1058,
between «⁨ثم دخلت سنة سبع عشرة⁩» at index 1043 and «⁨ثم دخلت سنة ثمان عشرة⁩» at index 1065). ⁨البدایہ⁩ takes that placement from
⁨ابن جرير عن سيف⁩, which is exactly the chain the course handles with care.
**Podium formula:** *"Dhahabī ⁨رحمہ اللہ⁩ puts his death at Tustar in 20 AH; Ibn Kathīr and Ibn al-Athīr
narrate Tustar under the year 17. Take the event, not the year."* `[SOURCED]` both ways.

**His grave.** The ⁨سیر⁩ editor's footnote at ⁨ج۱ ص۱۹۶⁩ says of ⁨تُسْتَر⁩: «⁨هي أعظم مدينة بخوزستان⁩. **⁨فيها⁩
⁨قبر البراء بن مالك⁩.**» That is the **editor's** footnote, not ⁨الذہبی⁩'s text. Cite it as such or not at
all.

### 5.6 The singing, and the count he gives himself

> <div dir="rtl">⁨أَنَّهُ دَخَلَ عَلَى أَخِيْهِ البَرَاءِ وَهُوَ يَتَغَنَّى، فَقَالَ⁩: ⁨تَتَغَنَّى؟ قَالَ⁩: ⁨أَتَخْشَى عَلَيَّ أَنْ أَمُوْتَ عَلَى فِرَاشِي وَقَدْ قَتَلْتُ تِسْعَةً وَتِسْعِيْنَ نَفَساً مِنَ المُشْرِكِيْنَ مُبَارَزَةً، سِوَى مَا شَارَكْتُ فِيْهِ المُسْلِمِيْنَ⁩.</div>
> — ⁨سیر أعلام النبلاء ج۱ ص۱۹۸⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/10906/1624
> *"He came in upon his brother al-Barāʾ, who was singing, and said: are you singing? He said: do you
> fear for me that I shall die in my bed, when I have killed ninety-nine of the mushrikūn in single
> combat, apart from what I shared in with the Muslims?"*

The visitor is **⁨أنس ؓ⁩** — the brother.

⚠ **The ninety-nine wording is the weaker of the two, and the note above must not be read without
this.** The editor's footnote to it on ⁨ج۱ ص۱۹۸⁩ opens **«⁨إسناده ضعيف لضعف أبي سهل⁩»**, then adds that
⁨الحاكم ٣⁩/٢٩١ carries it by another route (⁨عبد الله بن عوف⁩ ← ⁨ثمامة بن أنس⁩ ← ⁨أنس⁩) **«⁨وصححه، ووافقه⁩
⁨الذهبي⁩»**, and that ⁨الحافظ⁩ in ⁨الإصابة ١⁩/٢٣٦ reports it from ⁨البغوي⁩ «⁨بإسناد صحيح⁩». So: the number
ninety-nine reaches us ⁨صحيح⁩ only through those other routes, **not** through the chain ⁨سیر⁩ prints.

A second chain on the same page (⁨حماد بن سلمة⁩ ← ⁨ثابت⁩ ← ⁨أنس⁩; editor: «⁨إسناده صحيح⁩» — this is the
**ninety-odd** wording, and it is the safe one to quote):

> <div dir="rtl">⁨دَخَلْتُ عَلَى البَرَاءِ وَهُوَ يَتَغَنَّى، وَيُرَنِّمُ قَوْسَهُ، فَقُلْتُ⁩: ⁨إِلَى مَتَى هَذَا؟ قَالَ⁩: ⁨أَترَانِي أَمُوْتُ عَلَى فِرَاشِي؟ وَاللهِ لَقَدْ قَتَلْتُ بِضْعاً وَتِسْعِيْنَ⁩.</div>
> — ⁨سیر أعلام النبلاء ج۱ ص۱۹۸⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/10906/1624
> *"I came in upon al-Barāʾ, and he was singing and humming over his bow. I said: how long will this
> go on? He said: do you see me dying in my bed? By Allah, I have killed ninety-odd."*

A third wording on the same page, unattributed: «⁨يَا أَخِي⁩! ⁨تَتَغَنَّى بِالشِّعْرِ وَقَدْ أَبْدَلَكَ⁩
⁨اللهُ بِهِ القُرْآنَ؟⁩» — *my brother, do you sing poetry when Allah has given you the Qurʾān in its
place?* **This is a brother's rebuke to a brother**, and both halves of the exchange are worth having.
The numbers are treated in §Y.

## 6. ⁨أنس بن النضر ؓ⁩ — the uncle, and the most quotable passage in the strand

### 6.1 The full ṣaḥīḥ text, with the hadith number the page itself prints

> <div dir="rtl">⁨قال أنسٌ⁩: ⁨عَمِّي⁩ -⁨قال هاشمٌ⁩: ⁨أنس بن النّضير⁩- ⁨سمّيت به، ولم يشهد مع رسول اللَّه ﷺ يوم بدرٍ⁩. ⁨قال⁩: ⁨فشقّ عليه، وقال⁩: ⁨أول مشهدٍ شهده رسول اللَّه ﷺ غبت عنه⁩! ⁨لئن أراني اللَّه مشهدًا فيما بعد مع رسول اللَّه ﷺ ليرين اللَّه ما أصنع⁩. ⁨قال⁩: ⁨فهاب أن يقول غيرها، فشهد مع رسول اللَّه ﷺ يوم أحدٍ⁩. ⁨قال⁩: ⁨فاستقبل سعد بن معاذٍ، فقال له أنسٌ⁩: ⁨يا أبا عمرو أين؟ واهًا لريح الجنة، أجده دون أحدٍ⁩. ⁨قال⁩: ⁨فقاتلهم حتى قتل، فوجد في جسده بضعٌ وثمانون من ضربةٍ وطعنةٍ ورميةٍ⁩. ⁨قال⁩: ⁨فقالت أخته عمتي الرّبيّع بنت النّضر⁩: ⁨فما عرفت أخي إلا ببنانه⁩. ⁨ونزلت هذه الآية⁩: ⁨﴿مِنَ الْمُؤْمِنِينَ رِجَالٌ صَدَقُوا مَا عَاهَدُوا اللَّهَ عَلَيْهِ فَمِنْهُمْ مَنْ قَضَى نَحْبَهُ وَمِنْهُمْ مَنْ يَنْتَظِرُ وَمَا بَدَّلُوا تَبْدِيلًا﴾⁩. ⁨قال⁩: ⁨فكانوا يرون أنها نزلت فيه وفي أصحابه⁩</div>
> — ⁨البدایہ والنہایہ ج۴ ص۲۰۳⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/30097/1692
> *"Anas said: my uncle — Anas b. al-Naḍr, after whom I was named — was not present with the
> Messenger of Allah ﷺ at Badr. It weighed on him, and he said: the first field the Messenger of
> Allah ﷺ took part in, and I was absent from it! If Allah shows me a field hereafter with the
> Messenger of Allah ﷺ, Allah will surely see what I do. He was afraid to say anything more than
> that. So he was present with the Messenger of Allah ﷺ at Uḥud. He met Saʿd b. Muʿādh and said to
> him: Abū ʿAmr, where to? Ah, the scent of Paradise — I find it this side of Uḥud. And he fought
> them until he was killed; and there were found on his body eighty-odd — of sword-cut, spear-thrust
> and arrow-strike. His sister, my paternal aunt al-Rubayyiʿ bint al-Naḍr, said: I recognised my
> brother only by his fingertips. And this verse came down: {Among the believers are men true to
> what they pledged to Allah: some of them have fulfilled their vow, and some are still waiting; and
> they have not changed in the least}. They used to hold that it came down about him and his
> companions."*

**The page gives the attributions itself:** «⁨رواه أحمد في مسنده ٣⁩/١٩٤» and
**«⁨ورواه مسلم⁩ (١٩٠٣)»**, «⁨ورواه الترمذيّ⁩ (٣٢٠٠) ⁨والنسائيّ⁩ (٨٢٩١)», with ⁨الترمذي⁩: «⁨حسنٌ صحيحٌ⁩».
And ⁨البدایہ ج۱۷ ص۴۹۷⁩, retelling the same, prints **«⁨رواه البخاري⁩ (٢٨٠٥) ⁨ومسلم⁩ (١٩٠٣)»**.

**These four numbers — ⁨أحمد ٣⁩/⁨١٩٤، البخاري ٢٨٠٥، مسلم ١٩٠٣، الترمذي ٣٢٠٠⁩ — are printed on pages I
read. They may be used. Nothing else may.**

**The verse, isolated for a slide:**

> ﴿مِنَ الْمُؤْمِنِينَ رِجَالٌ صَدَقُوا مَا عَاهَدُوا اللَّهَ عَلَيْهِ﴾ [الأحزاب: ٢٣]
> — as quoted at ⁨البدایہ والنہایہ ج۴ ص۲۰۳⁩ and ⁨سیر، جلد السیرہ ۱ ص۴۰۷⁩
> *"Among the believers are men true to what they pledged to Allah."*

⚠ **The two safe-list works quote two different fragments of the same verse.** ⁨البدایہ ج۴ ص۲۰۲⁩ (from
⁨أحمد⁩) quotes **⁨﴿فَمِنْهُمْ مَنْ قَضَى نَحْبَهُ وَمِنْهُمْ مَنْ يَنْتَظِرُ﴾⁩**; ⁨البدایہ ج۴ ص۲۰۳⁩ (from
⁨مسلم⁩) quotes the whole verse; ⁨سیر ص۴۰۷⁩ quotes **⁨﴿مِنَ الْمُؤْمِنِينَ رِجَالٌ صَدَقُوا مَا عَاهَدُوا⁩
⁨اللَّهَ عَلَيْهِ﴾⁩**. All three are ⁨الأحزاب ۲۳⁩. Print the verse whole and there is nothing to get wrong.

### 6.2 The ⁨سیر⁩ wording — for a second slide, or if ⁨البدایہ⁩ is not wanted

> <div dir="rtl">⁨فقال⁩: ⁨اللهم إني أبرأ إليك مما جاء به هؤلاء⁩ - ⁨يعني المشركين⁩ - ⁨وأعتذر إليك مما صنع هؤلاء⁩ - ⁨يعني المسلمين⁩ - ⁨من الهزيمة⁩. ⁨فمشى بسيفه فلقيه سعد بن معاذ فقال⁩: ⁨أي سعد إني لأجد ريح الجنة دون أحد، واهًا لريح الجنة⁩! ⁨فقال سعد⁩: ⁨يا رسول الله⁩! ⁨فما استطعت أن أصنع كما صنع⁩. ⁨قال أنس بن مالك⁩: ⁨فوجدناه بين القتلى، به بضع وثمانون جراحة من ضربة بسيف وطعنة برمح ورمية بسهم، فما عرفناه، حتى عرفته أخته ببنانه⁩.</div>
> — ⁨سیر أعلام النبلاء، جلد السیرہ ۱ ص۴۰۷⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/10906/544
> *"He said: O Allah, I declare myself free before You of what these — meaning the mushrikūn — have
> brought, and I excuse myself before You for what these — meaning the Muslims — have done, of
> flight. Then he walked forward with his sword and met Saʿd b. Muʿādh, and said: Saʿd! I find the
> scent of Paradise this side of Uḥud. Ah, the scent of Paradise! And Saʿd said: Messenger of Allah,
> I could not do what he did. Anas b. Mālik said: we found him among the slain with eighty-odd
> wounds — sword-cut, spear-thrust, arrow-strike — and we did not recognise him; his sister
> recognised him by his fingertips."*

⁨سیر⁩ closes: «⁨متفق عليه⁩, ⁨لكن مسلم من حديث ثابت البناني، عن أنس⁩» — *agreed upon, though Muslim's is by
way of Thābit al-Bunānī from Anas.* Note that **⁨سیر⁩ here says only «⁨أخته⁩», without naming her.** The
name **⁨الرُّبيِّع بنت النضر⁩** comes from ⁨البدایہ ج۴ ص۲۰۳⁩.

### 6.3 «What will you do with life after him?»

> <div dir="rtl">⁨انتهى أنس بن النّضر عمّ أنس بن مالكٍ إلى عمر بن الخطَّاب وطلحة بن عبيد اللَّه، في رجالٍ من المهاجرين والأنصار، وقد ألقوا بأيديهم، فقال⁩: ⁨فما يجلسكم؟ قالوا⁩: ⁨قتل رسول اللَّه ﷺ⁩. ⁨قال⁩: ⁨فما تصنعون بالحياة بعدّه؟⁩! ⁨قوموا فموتوا على ما مات عليه رسول اللَّه ﷺ، ثم استقبل القوم فقاتل حتى قتل، وبه سمّي أنس بن مالكٍ⁩.</div>
> — ⁨البدایہ والنہایہ ج۴ ص۲۰۷⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/30097/1696
> *"Anas b. al-Naḍr, the paternal uncle of Anas b. Mālik, came upon ʿUmar b. al-Khaṭṭāb and Ṭalḥa b.
> ʿUbayd Allāh among men of the Muhājirūn and the Anṣār who had let their hands fall, and said: what
> keeps you sitting? They said: the Messenger of Allah ﷺ has been killed. He said: and what will you
> do with life after him? Rise, and die upon what the Messenger of Allah ﷺ died upon. Then he faced
> the enemy and fought until he was killed — and Anas b. Mālik was named after him."*

⚠ **This one is ⁨ابن إسحاق⁩ ← ⁨القاسم بن عبد الرحمن⁩**, i.e. ⁨مغازی⁩ material with a broken link, **not**
the ṣaḥīḥ report of §6.1. Two consequences: (i) if a single quotation is used, use §6.1; (ii) if this
one is used, say plainly that it comes through ⁨ابن إسحاق⁩. It is nonetheless where the *naming*
sentence sits — «⁨وبه سمّي أنس بن مالكٍ⁩» — which is the sentence that welds the uncle to the nephew.

⚠ A **third**, longer version at **⁨البدایہ ج۴ ص۱۹۱⁩** has him say «⁨يا قوم، إن كان محمدٌ قد قتل، فإن⁩
⁨ربّ محمدٍ لم يقتل⁩» — but **⁨ابن کثیر⁩ closes that entire report with «⁨وهذا غريبٌ جدًا، وفي بعضه⁩
⁨نكارةٌ، واللَّه أعلم⁩»** (*this is very strange, and part of it has objectionable material*).
**Do not use the ⁨ج۴ ص۱۹۱⁩ wording.** It is the version most often retold in Urdu and it is the version
⁨ابن کثیر⁩ himself flags.

## 7. أم حرام بنت ملحان ؓ

> <div dir="rtl">⁨أُخْتُ أُمِّ سُلَيْمٍ، وَخَالَةُ أَنَسِ بنِ مَالِكٍ، وَزَوْجَةُ عُبَادَةَ بنِ الصَّامِتِ⁩... ⁨كَانَتْ مِنْ عِلْيَةِ النِّسَاءِ⁩.</div>
> — ⁨سیر أعلام النبلاء ج۲ ص۳۱۶⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/10906/2296
> *"The sister of Umm Sulaym, the maternal aunt of Anas b. Mālik, and the wife of ʿUbāda b.
> al-Ṣāmit... She was among the highest of women."*

### 7.1 The sea

> <div dir="rtl">⁨أَنَّ رَسُوْلَ اللهِ⁩ -⁨صَلَّى اللَّهُ عَلَيْهِ وَسَلَّمَ⁩- ⁨قَالَ فِي بَيْتِهَا يَوْماً، فَاسْتَيْقَظَ وَهُوَ يَضْحَكُ⁩. ⁨فَقُلْتُ⁩: ⁨يَا رَسُوْلَ اللهِ⁩! ⁨مَا أَضْحَكَكَ؟ قَالَ⁩: (⁨عُرِضَ عَلَيَّ نَاسٌ مِنْ أُمَّتِي يَرْكَبُوْنَ ظَهْرَ هَذَا البَحْرِ كَالمُلُوْكِ⁩</div>
> — ⁨سیر أعلام النبلاء ج۲ ص۳۱۶⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/10906/2296

The ḥadīth runs over the page break; ⁨سیر⁩ completes it at the head of ⁨ص۳۱۷⁩:

> <div dir="rtl">⁨عَلَى الأَسِرَّةِ⁩) . ⁨قُلْتُ⁩: ⁨يَا رَسُوْلَ اللهِ⁩! ⁨ادْعُ اللهَ أَنْ يَجْعَلَنِي مِنْهُم⁩. ⁨قَالَ⁩: (⁨أَنْتِ مِنَ الأَوَّلِيْنَ⁩</div>
> — ⁨سیر أعلام النبلاء ج۲ ص۳۱۷⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/10906/2297
> *English:* (the two pages together) "The Messenger of Allah ﷺ took his midday rest in her house
> one day, and woke laughing. I said: Messenger of Allah, what has made you laugh? He said: 'People
> of my umma were shown to me riding the back of this sea like kings upon thrones.' I said:
> Messenger of Allah, pray to Allah to make me one of them. He said: 'You are among the first.'"

Editor's footnote, **on ⁨ص۳۱۷⁩ (index 2297), not on ⁨ص۳۱۶⁩**: **⁨البخاري ١٢⁩/٣٤٥ (⁨التعبير⁩: ⁨باب رؤيا⁩
⁨النهار⁩)⁨، ومسلم⁩ (١٩١٢)**, plus ⁨أبو⁩

ḥadīth and its footnote prints **⁨البخاري⁩ (٢٧٩٩) ⁨ومسلم⁩ (١٩١٢)(١٦١)**.

### 7.2 The death

> <div dir="rtl">⁨فَتَزَوَّجَهَا عُبَادَةُ بنُ الصَّامِتِ، فَغَزَا بِهَا فِي البَحْرِ، فَحَمَلَهَا مَعَهُ، فَلَمَّا رَجَعُوا قُرِّبَتْ لَهَا بَغْلَةٌ لِتَرْكَبَهَا، فَصَرَعَتْهَا، فَدُقَّتْ عُنُقُهَا، فَمَاتَتْ⁩ - ⁨رَضِيَ اللهُ عَنْهَا⁩ -. ⁨قُلْتُ⁩: ⁨يُقَالُ هَذِهِ غَزْوَةُ قُبْرُسَ فِي خِلاَفَةِ عُثْمَان⁩</div>
> — ⁨سیر أعلام النبلاء ج۲ ص۳۱۷⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/10906/2297
> *"ʿUbāda b. al-Ṣāmit married her and campaigned with her at sea, carrying her with him. When they
> came back, a mule was brought up for her to ride; it threw her, her neck was broken, and she died.
> I say: it is said this was the expedition to Cyprus, in the caliphate of ʿUthmān."*

**⁨البدایہ ج۷ ص۲۹۱⁩** gives the same and adds where she was buried:

> <div dir="rtl">⁨فلما أرادوا الخروج منها قُدِّمتْ لأم حرام بغلةٌ لتركبها فسقطت عنها فاندقت عنقها، فماتت هناك، فقبرها هنالك يُعظِّمونه ويستسقون به ويقولون قبر المرأة الصالحة⁩.</div>
> — ⁨البدایہ والنہایہ ج۷ ص۲۹۱⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/30097/3437
> *"When they wished to leave it, a mule was brought up for Umm Ḥarām to ride; she fell from it and
> her neck was broken, and she died there. And her grave is there — they venerate it and seek rain by
> it, and say: the grave of the righteous woman."*

⚠ **Delivery caution.** The last clause is ⁨ابن کثیر⁩ *reporting local practice*, not endorsing it, and
a Tanzeem-e-Islami room will hear it as a doctrinal statement if it is read out bare. **Stop the
quotation at «⁨فماتت هناك⁩».** (⁨الذہبی⁩ has his own version of the same aside at ⁨سیر ج۲ ص۳۱۷⁩:
«⁨وَبَلَغَنِي أَنَّ قَبْرَهَا تَزُوْرُهُ الفِرَنْجُ⁩» — also a ⁨بلاغ⁩, also better left off the slide.)

### 7.3 The year of the expedition — the sources give three

**⁨البدایہ ج۷ ص۲۹۰⁩** sets them out in one paragraph:

> <div dir="rtl">⁨قال ابن جرير⁩: ⁨قال بعضهم⁩: ⁨وفي هذه السنة غزا معاوية قبرص⁩. ⁨وقال الواقدي⁩: ⁨كان ذلك في سنة ثمان وعشرين⁩. ⁨وقال أبو معشر⁩: ⁨غزاها معاوية سنة ثلاث وثلاثين، فالله أعلم⁩.</div>
> — ⁨البدایہ والنہایہ ج۷ ص۲۹۰⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/30097/3436
> *"Ibn Jarīr said: some said Muʿāwiya raided Cyprus in this year [i.e. 27]. Al-Wāqidī said it was in
> the year 28. Abū Maʿshar said Muʿāwiya raided it in the year 33. And Allah knows best."*

`[SOURCED]` **27, 28 or 33 — three reports, one page.** ⁨ابن کثیر⁩'s own chapter heading files it under
⁨۲۸ھ⁩. **On a slide: «⁨۲۸ھ⁩ (reports differ: ۲۷، ۲۸، ۳۳)» and nothing tighter.**

One further fact from ⁨ج۷ ص۲۹۱⁩, and it is a useful one for the caliphate narrative:
⁨عمر ؓ⁩ had refused permission for the sea («⁨فأبى أن يُمكِّنه من حمل المسلمين على هذا الخلق العظيم⁩
⁨الذي لو اضطرب لهلكوا عن آخرهم⁩»); ⁨عثمان ؓ⁩ gave it. So the ḥadīth she heard in her own house was
fulfilled a caliphate later than the one that had said no.

## 8. حرام بن ملحان ؓ — بئر معونة

> <div dir="rtl">⁨أن النبيّ ﷺ بعث خاله حرامًا⁩ - ⁨أخًا لأمِّ سليم⁩ - ⁨في سبعين راكبًا⁩... ⁨فقال⁩: ⁨أتؤمنوني حتى أُبَلِّغ رسالة رسول اللَّه ﷺ؟ فجعل يحدّثهم، وأومؤوا إلى رجلٍ فأتاه من خلفه فطعنه⁩... ⁨فقال⁩: ⁨اللَّه أكبر، فزت وربّ الكعبة⁩.</div>
> — ⁨البدایہ والنہایہ ج۴ ص۲۵۵⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/30097/1744
> *"The Prophet ﷺ sent his maternal uncle Ḥarām — a brother of Umm Sulaym — among seventy riders...
> He said: will you grant me safety until I deliver the message of the Messenger of Allah ﷺ? And he
> began speaking to them; and they signalled to a man, who came at him from behind and speared him...
> And he said: Allāhu akbar — I have won, by the Lord of the Kaʿba."*

The page names **⁨البخاري رقم⁩ (٤٠٩١)** for that report and **⁨البخاري رقم⁩ (٤٠٩٢)** for the next, which
is the one that carries the gesture:

> <div dir="rtl">⁨لما طعن حرام بن ملحان⁩ - ⁨وكان خاله⁩ - ⁨يوم بئر معونة قال بالدَّم هكذا؛ فنضحه على وجهه ورأسه، ثم قال⁩: ⁨فزت وربّ الكعبة⁩.</div>
> — ⁨البدایہ والنہایہ ج۴ ص۲۵۵⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/30097/1744
> *"When Ḥarām b. Milḥān — and he was his maternal uncle — was speared on the day of Biʾr Maʿūna, he
> did thus with the blood, sprinkling it over his face and his head, and then said: I have won, by
> the Lord of the Kaʿba."*

**"His maternal uncle" is ⁨أنس ؓ⁩'s** — ⁨أنس ؓ⁩ is the narrator throughout. ⁨سیر ج۲ ص۳۰۷⁩ confirms the
identification in ⁨الذہبی⁩'s own voice, and ties it back to §2.6: this is the death that made the
Prophet ﷺ enter that house.

⚠ **Do not describe ⁨حرام ؓ⁩ as lame.** The Bukhari text on ⁨ج۴ ص۲۵۵⁩ reads «⁨فانطلق حرامٌ، أخو أمّ⁩
⁨سليمٍ، وهو رجلٌ أعرج، ورجلٌ من بني فلانٍ⁩» and then «⁨فقتلوا كلُّهم غير الأعرج، وكان في رأس جبلٍ⁩» — the
lame man **survived**, so he cannot be ⁨حرام ؓ⁩, who was killed. The phrasing is ambiguous and the
identification is not made on the page. Leave the detail out.

⚠ **Numbers.** ⁨البخاري⁩ on this page: **«⁨سبعين راكبًا⁩»**. ⁨ابن إسحاق⁩ on the very next page, ⁨ج۴ ص۲۵۶⁩:
**«⁨في أربعين رجلًا من أصحابه⁩»**. Both in the safe list, four inches apart. See §Y.

## 9. The children of the house

> <div dir="rtl">⁨وَأَخٌ لِي أَصْغَرُ مِنِّي، يُكْنَى أَبَا عُمَيْرٍ، فَزَارَنَا يَوْماً، فَقَالَ⁩: (⁨مَا لِي أَرَى أَبَا عُمَيْرٍ خَاثِرَ النَّفْسِ؟⁩). ⁨قَالَتْ⁩: ⁨مَاتَتْ صَعْوَةٌ لَهُ كَانَ يَلْعَبُ بِهَا⁩. ⁨فَجَعَلَ النَّبِيُّ يَمْسَحُ رَأْسَهُ، وَيَقُوْلُ⁩: (⁨يَا أَبَا عُمَيْرٍ، مَا فَعَلَ النُّغَيْرُ؟⁩).</div>

> — ⁨سیر أعلام النبلاء ج۲ ص۳۱۰⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/10906/2290

**⚠ Where the famous retelling differs from the page — this matters.**
- The popular Urdu retelling frequently names the child who died as **⁨أبو عمير⁩**. **⁨سیر⁩ does not name
  him.** ⁨ج۲ ص۳۱۰⁩ says only «⁨ابْنٌ لأُمِّ سُلَيْمٍ⁩». **Say "a son of hers" and stop.**
- The popular retelling often puts the exchange **at the door, before supper**. The page has the
  order: she prepares the body → tells the household to say nothing → he eats → the night → and only
  then, «⁨فَلَمَّا كَانَ مِنْ آخِرِ اللَّيْلِ⁩», the question about the loan.
- The popular retelling often gives the Prophet's ﷺ words as a prediction of a son. The page has
  **«⁨بَارَكَ اللهُ لَكُمَا فِي لَيْلَتِكُمَا⁩»** — a blessing, not a prediction. The son is then born,
  and named ⁨عبد الله⁩ by the Prophet ﷺ (⁨ج۲ ص۳۱۰⁩–۳۱۱).

### 9.3 ⁨عبد الله بن أبي طلحة ؓ⁩, and the seven

⁨أنس ؓ⁩'s own reports run through (⁨سیر ج۳ ص۳۹۶⁩; ⁨ج۲ ص۳۰۵⁩ has him narrating «⁨عَنْ جَدَّتِهِ أُمِّ⁩

## X. What could NOT be established

**This section is the most useful part of the note. These are the things the speaker must not say.**

1. **The tooth-for-a-tooth story of ⁨الرُّبيِّع بنت النضر ؓ⁩, and «⁨إِنَّ مِنْ عِبَادِ اللهِ مَنْ لَوْ⁩
   ⁨أَقْسَمَ عَلَى اللهِ لَأَبَرَّهُ⁩».** Searched ⁨البدایہ⁩ (30097), ⁨سیر⁩ (10906) and ⁨الکامل⁩ (21712) for
   «⁨الربيع بنت النضر⁩»، «⁨كتاب الله القصاص⁩»، «⁨لتكسرن ثنية⁩». **Zero hits in the safe list** except the
   ⁨أحد⁩ passage at ⁨البدایہ ج۴ ص۲۰۳⁩ and ⁨ج۱۷ ص۴۹۷⁩, which mention her only as the sister who identified
   the body. The ⁨قصاص⁩ incident is in ⁨صحيح البخاري⁩, but **we have not read it on a page**, and it is
   not in any of the four works this course narrates from. **Do not tell it. Do not attribute it to
   ⁨البدایہ⁩ or ⁨سیر⁩.** (It would make a superb bridge — uncle and nephew both named in ḥadīths about
   answered oaths — and that bridge is exactly the kind of thing that gets fabricated. It stays out
   until someone reads the Bukhari page.)
2. **«⁨ألف ألف⁩» — any claim that ⁨البراء ؓ⁩ killed a thousand, or a thousand thousand.** Searched
   10906, 30097, 21712 for «⁨البراء⁩» + «⁨ألف ألف⁩»: **zero results.** What the sources give is: ninety-
   nine (his own words), ninety-odd (his own words), a hundred (⁨اشتهر⁩, in ⁨سیر⁩), a hundred in single
   combat at ⁨تُسْتَر⁩ alone (⁨البدایہ ج۷ ص۱۹۷⁩ · ⁨الکامل ج۲ ص۳۶۸⁩), and ten or more inside the garden
   (⁨البدایہ ج۶ ص۳۹۶⁩). **Nothing larger exists in the safe list.**
3. **The words «and join me with Your Prophet ﷺ» in ⁨البراء ؓ⁩'s oath.** Not on any page read. ⁨سیر⁩
   ⁨ج۱ ص۱۹۷⁩ stops at «⁨لَمَّا مَنَحْتَنَا أَكْتَافَهُم، وَذَكَرَ الحَدِيْثَ⁩». ⁨البدایہ ج۷ ص۱۹۷⁩ and
   ⁨الکامل ج۲ ص۳۶۸⁩ both give «**⁨وَاسْتَشْهِدْنِي⁩**» — *and take me as a martyr* — and nothing further.
   **Use «⁨واستشهدني⁩». Do not add.**
4. **Any death date, year or place for ⁨أم سليم ؓ⁩.** ⁨سیر⁩'s entry (⁨ج۲ ص۳۰۴⁩–۳۱۱) ends without one, and
   ⁨الکامل⁩ returned **zero hits** for «⁨أم سليم⁩». `[CONVENTIONAL-ESTIMATE]` territory at best;
   **leave it blank.**
5. **The name of the child of ⁨أم سليم ؓ⁩ who died.** ⁨سیر ج۲ ص۳۱۰⁩ says «⁨ابْنٌ لأُمِّ سُلَيْمٍ⁩». The

   ⁨تُسْتَر⁩ under ⁨سنة سبع عشرة⁩ (⁨۱۷ھ⁩). Both are in the safe list. **Show both or neither.**
7. **A single year for ⁨أبو طلحة ؓ⁩'s death, or a settled answer to where he died.** ⁨سیر ج۲ ص۲۸⁩
   prefers Medina, ⁨۳۴ھ⁩; ⁨سیر ج۲ ص۳۴⁩ carries the sea-death with a chain its own editor calls ⁨صحيح⁩, and
   lists ۳۴، ۳۲ (⁨خليفة⁩ alone) and ۵۱.
8. **A single year for ⁨غزوة قبرص⁩, and so for ⁨أم حرام ؓ⁩'s death.** ⁨البدایہ ج۷ ص۲۹۰⁩ prints three: 27,
   28, 33.
9. **Whether the household is of the same sub-branch of ⁨بنو النجار⁩ as ⁨أبو طلحة ؓ⁩.** The two nasab
   strings printed at ⁨سیر ج۱ ص۱۹۵⁩ / ⁨ج۲ ص۳۰۴⁩ (⁨عدي بن النجار⁩) and ⁨سیر ج۲ ص۲۷⁩ (⁨عدي بن عمرو بن مالك بن⁩
   ⁨النجار⁩) do **not** meet. Say «⁨بنو النجار⁩» and stop.
10. **The ⁨ج۴ ص۱۹۱⁩ wording of ⁨أنس بن النضر ؓ⁩'s speech** («⁨إن كان محمدٌ قد قتل، فإن ربّ محمدٍ لم⁩
    ⁨يقتل⁩») — this exists on the page but **⁨ابن کثیر⁩ grades the whole report «⁨غريبٌ جدًا، وفي بعضه⁩
    ⁨نكارةٌ⁩»**. It is the version most often retold. **Do not use it.**
11. **That ⁨حرام بن ملحان ؓ⁩ was lame.** See §8. The page's «⁨الأعرج⁩» survived; ⁨حرام ؓ⁩ did not.
12. **Anything from ⁨تاریخ الطبری⁩.** A search for «⁨الربيع بنت النضر⁩» returned a ⁨طبری⁩ hit (9783/6380).
    It was **not fetched and is not used** — ⁨طبری⁩ is corroboration only and was not needed here, since
    ⁨البدایہ⁩ carries the same material with ⁨مسلم⁩'s number printed on the page.
13. **Whether the ⁨البراء ؓ⁩ of ⁨حديقة الموت⁩ ever met the ⁨أم سليم ؓ⁩ narrative in one source.** He does
    not. The household exists in the sources only as **kinship headers** («⁨أخو خادم النبي ﷺ⁩»,
    «⁨أم خادم النبي ﷺ⁩», «⁨عمّ أنس بن مالك⁩», «⁨خالة أنس بن مالك⁩», «⁨خاله حرامًا أخًا لأم سليم⁩»). Those
    headers are what license the strand; **there is no single passage narrating the family as a
    family**, and the lecture must not imply that there is. The joining-up is ours; the kinships are
    theirs, each with a page.
14. **That ⁨أم سليم ؓ⁩ was ⁨البراء ؓ⁩'s mother.** ⚠ **This is the one kinship in the whole strand with no
    page behind it,** and it is the one a retelling reaches for first. What the pages actually give:
    ⁨البراء ؓ⁩ is **⁨مالك بن النضر⁩'s** son (⁨سیر ج۱ ص۱۹۵⁩, the nasab) and **⁨أنس ؓ⁩'s brother** (same page,
    «أَخُوْ خَادِمِ النَّبِيِّ ﷺ أَنَسِ بنِ مَالِكٍ»; البدایہ ج۶ ص۳۹۶ «أخو أنس بن مالك — وكان
    ⁨الأكبر⁩»; ⁨البدایہ ج۷ ص۱۹۷⁩; ⁨الکامل ج۲ ص۳۶۸⁩). ⁨أم سليم ؓ⁩ is **⁨مالك بن النضر⁩'s wife** and
    **⁨أنس ؓ⁩'s mother** (⁨سیر ج۲ ص۳۰۴⁩ · ⁨ج۳ ص۳۹۶⁩). Every page read names her sons as ⁨أنس ؓ⁩, ⁨أبو عمير⁩ and
    ⁨عبد الله ؓ⁩ — **⁨البراء ؓ⁩ is never among them, and his mother is never named.** A search of 10906,
    30097 and 21712 for «⁨البراء⁩» together with «⁨أم سليم⁩» returns **one** hit, an editor's footnote
    about a different man (⁨البراء بن زيد⁩, ⁨سیر ج۲ ص۳۰۸⁩). She may well be his mother; **our pages do not
    say so.** `[CONVENTIONAL-ESTIMATE]` at best. **Say "⁨أنس ؓ⁩'s elder brother", never "⁨أم سليم ؓ⁩'s
    son"** — and see §X.13: the joining-up of this household is ours, and this particular join has no
    page under it at all.

are rounded, and the same book will print two of them a few pages apart. Where a number is used from
the platform, it should be spoken as *"the reports say…"*, and the disagreements below are worth
showing on a slide once, in this course, because they teach the method better than any lecture on

### E-HS1 · The dower that was Islam
**Tier:** CORE · **When:** before the Hijra, at Medina `[STANDARD]` (the sources fix no year) · **Map:** Medina — ⁨بنو النجار⁩ quarter
**What happened:** Abū Ṭalḥa came to ask for Umm Sulaym in marriage. He was still a pagan; she had already believed. She told him she would not refuse a man like him, but that he was a disbeliever. Then she named her price. If he accepted Islam, that would be her dower, and she would ask for nothing else. He accepted Islam, and he married her.
**Beats:**
1. Who he is — Abū Ṭalḥa ؓ of the Anṣār; his own name is Zayd b. Sahl. ⁨الذہبی⁩: a man of Badr, and one of the twelve naqībs of the night of al-ʿAqaba.
2. He comes to ask for her in marriage — Still a pagan; she had already believed.
3. What she answered — She would not refuse a man like him, but he was a disbeliever.
4. Her price — If he accepted Islam, that would be her dower, and she would ask for nothing else.
5. What happened — He accepted Islam, and he married her.
**Quote after beat:** 4
**The statement:**
> <div dir="rtl">⁨أَمَا إِنِّي فِيْكَ لَرَاغِبَةٌ، وَمَا مِثْلُكَ يُرَدُّ، وَلَكِنَّكَ كَافِرٌ، فَإِنْ تُسْلِمْ فَذَلِكَ مَهْرِي، لاَ أَسْأَلُكَ غَيْرَهُ⁩.</div>
> — ⁨سیر أعلام النبلاء ج۲ ص۲۹⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/10906/2009
> *English:* "I am indeed willing in your case, and a man like you is not refused; but you are a disbeliever. If you accept Islam, that is my dower — I ask you for nothing else."
**⁨عبرت⁩:** She set the terms of her own household before she entered it.
**Hands-up?** no — but ⁨ثابت⁩'s closing line is worth adding aloud: *"We never heard of a nobler dower than Umm Sulaym's dower: Islam."*
