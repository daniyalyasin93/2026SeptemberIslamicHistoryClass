# ⁨عثمان ؓ⁩ — the ⁨مصحف⁩, and the state he ran

**Question this note answers:** What problem was reported to ⁨عثمان ؓ⁩ about the reading of the Qurʾān,
who brought it to him, who sat on the committee that copied the ⁨مصحف⁩, what was written, where the
copies were sent, and what was done with the copies that disagreed — and, just as important, where our
four books *disagree with each other* about that. Then the rest of the administration: his enlargement
of ⁨المسجد الحرام⁩ and of ⁨المسجد النبوي⁩, the third call to prayer on ⁨الجمعة⁩, the four rakʿahs at ⁨مِنى⁩ and
every explanation the books record for it, the ⁨حِمى⁩, the ⁨عطاء⁩ and the treasuries, the ring of the
Prophet ﷺ lost in ⁨بئر أريس⁩, the "first to do X" notices of his caliphate, and how much of ⁨عمر ؓ⁩'s
machinery he kept unchanged and where he changed it.

## 0. The sentences this note licenses

1. **A Companion, ⁨حذيفة بن اليمان ؓ⁩, came to ⁨عثمان ؓ⁩ off a campaign and told him the Muslims were
   differing over the reading of the Qurʾān, and asked him to reach the umma before it split over its
   Book the way the Jews and the Christians split over theirs** `[SOURCED]` — ⁨البدایہ ج۷ ص۳۸۴⁩;
   ⁨سیر ص۱۵۷⁩; ⁨الکامل ج۲ ص۴۸۲⁩. **All three books give the same sentence in almost the same words.**
2. **⁨البدایہ⁩ names the two reading-traditions that collided: Syrians reading after ⁨المقداد بن الأسود ؓ⁩ and
   ⁨أبو الدرداء ؓ⁩, Iraqis reading after ⁨عبد الله بن مسعود ؓ⁩ and ⁨أبو موسى ؓ⁩ — and says the ignorant among them
   went as far as declaring the other wrong, or declaring him an unbeliever** `[SOURCED]` — ⁨البدایہ ج۷ ص۳۸۴⁩.
3. **The base text was the ⁨صحف⁩ collected under ⁨أبو بكر ؓ⁩, held by ⁨عمر ؓ⁩, then by ⁨أم المؤمنين حفصة ؓ⁩;
   ⁨عثمان ؓ⁩ sent for them, had them copied, and returned them to her** `[SOURCED]` — ⁨الکامل ج۲ ص۴۸۲⁩;
   ⁨سیر ص۱۵۷⁩. (⁨البدایہ ج۷ ص۳۸۴⁩ has the same chain of custody but does not mention the return.)
4. ⚠ **The books do not agree on what the four men on the committee each did, and the two versions must
   not be merged.** **⁨سیر ص۱۵۷⁩ and ⁨الکامل ج۲ ص۴۸۲⁩ name all four — ⁨زيد بن ثابت ؓ⁩, ⁨سعيد بن العاص⁩, ⁨عبد الله⁩
   ⁨بن الزبير ؓ⁩, ⁨عبد الرحمن بن الحارث بن هشام⁩ — as copyists together.** **⁨البدایہ ج۷ ص۳۸۴⁩ gives them
   different jobs: ⁨زيد ؓ⁩ writes, ⁨سعيد بن العاص⁩ dictates to him, and the other two are present.**
   `[SOURCED, disputed]` See §9.
5. **The instruction when they differed was one sentence: write it in the tongue of ⁨قريش⁩, for it came
   down in their tongue** `[SOURCED]` — ⁨سیر ص۱۵۷⁩; ⁨الکامل ج۲ ص۴۸۲⁩; ⁨البدایہ ج۷ ص۳۸۴⁩ (⁨البدایہ⁩ has «⁨بلغةِ⁩
   ⁨قريشٍ⁩», the other two «⁨بلسان قريش⁩»).
6. **⁨البدایہ⁩ names seven destinations: ⁨الشام⁩ · ⁨مصر⁩ · ⁨البصرة⁩ · ⁨الكوفة⁩ · ⁨مكة⁩ · ⁨اليمن⁩ · and one kept at
   ⁨المدينة⁩** `[SOURCED]` — ⁨البدایہ ج۷ ص۳۸۴⁩. **⁨سیر ص۱۵۷⁩ and ⁨الکامل ج۲ ص۴۸۲⁩ give no list and no number** —
   ⁨سیر⁩ says one to every ⁨جند⁩ of the Muslims, ⁨الکامل⁩ says one to every ⁨أفق⁩. **A slide must not say "seven"
   as though all three books said it.** See §10.
7. **⁨ابن کثیر⁩ states in his own voice that these copies are called ⁨المصاحف الأئمة⁩, that not one of them is
   in ⁨عثمان ؓ⁩'s own hand, that they are in ⁨زيد بن ثابت ؓ⁩'s hand, and that they are called "Uthmanic" the way
   a coin is called Heraclian — after the reign, not the hand** `[SOURCED]` — ⁨البدایہ ج۷ ص۳۸۴⁩.
8. **The disagreeing copies were burned, and the order to burn them is in the sources, not inferred**
   `[SOURCED]` — ⁨سیر ص۱۵۷⁩ («⁨وأمرهم أن يحرقوا كل مصحف يخالف المصحف الذي أرسل إليهم به⁩»); ⁨الکامل ج۲ ص۴۸۲⁩
   («وحرق ما سوى ذلك»); البدایہ ج۷ ص۳۸۵ («فحرقه، لئلا يقع بسببه اختلاف»).
9. **⁨علی ؓ⁩ is quoted twice on the burning, and the second quotation is the one that matters: he forbade
   people to go to excess against ⁨عثمان ؓ⁩ over it, said he did not burn them except before an assembly of
   the Companions, and said that had he himself held that office he would have done the same**
   `[SOURCED]` — ⁨البدایہ ج۷ ص۳۸۵⁩ (both reports); ⁨سیر ص۱۵۸⁩ (the short form).
10. **⁨ابن مسعود ؓ⁩ was displeased when his own ⁨مصحف⁩ was taken and burned, and the books say how it ended:
    ⁨عثمان ؓ⁩ wrote to him calling him to what the Companions had agreed on, and he came round — «⁨فأناب⁩
    ⁨وأجاب إلى المتابعة وترك المخالفة⁩»** `[SOURCED]` — ⁨البدایہ ج۷ ص۳۸۵⁩. **⁨الکامل ج۲ ص۴۸۲⁩ has him rebuking
    his own partisans in Kufa when they refused the new ⁨مصحف⁩** — «⁨فاربعوا على ظلعكم⁩». See §12.
11. **⁨ابن الأثیر⁩ states in his own voice that everyone recognised the merit of the act except what came
    from the people of Kufa** `[SOURCED]` — ⁨الکامل ج۲ ص۴۸۲⁩.
12. **⁨عثمان ؓ⁩ enlarged ⁨المسجد الحرام⁩ in 26 AH: he bought the extension, some owners refused, their property
    was pulled down and the price put in ⁨بيت المال⁩; they shouted at him, he jailed them, then released
    them — and his answer to them is quoted: «⁨ما جرأكم علي إلا حلمي، وقد فعل هذا بكم عمر فلم تصيحوا عليه⁩»**
    `[SOURCED]` — ⁨سیر ص۱۶۹⁩. **⁨البدایہ⁩ and ⁨الکامل⁩ do not carry this at the pages read.**
13. **He rebuilt and enlarged ⁨المسجد النبوي⁩, in ⁨قَصَّة⁩ (lime) and carved stone, with inlaid stone columns
    and a teak roof, 160 cubits long by 150 wide, six doors — «⁨على ما كانت⁩ (⁨عليه⁩) ⁨في زمان عمر بن الخطاب⁩»
    — begun in ⁨ربيع الأول⁩** `[SOURCED]` — ⁨البدایہ ج۷ ص۲۹۲⁩; ⁨سیر ص۱۷۶⁩ (the bare notice). **Both books file it
    under 29 AH, not 30.** See §5.
14. **He added the third call on ⁨الجمعة⁩, given on ⁨الزوراء⁩** `[SOURCED]` — ⁨البدایہ ج۷ ص۲۹۴⁩; ⁨الکامل ج۲ ص۴۸۵⁩.
    The ⁨البدایہ⁩ editor's footnote identifies ⁨الزوراء⁩ as a place by the market of ⁨المدينة⁩ near the mosque.
15. **He prayed four rakʿahs at ⁨مِنى⁩, and Companions objected to it openly — ⁨البدایہ⁩ names ⁨علی ؓ⁩,
    ⁨عبد الرحمن بن عوف ؓ⁩ and ⁨عبد الله بن مسعود ؓ⁩** `[SOURCED]` — ⁨البدایہ ج۷ ص۲۹۲⁩; ⁨سیر ص۱۷۷⁩.
16. **The books record four different explanations for the four rakʿahs, and ⁨ابن کثیر⁩ rejects one of them
    by name and questions a second** `[SOURCED]` — ⁨البدایہ ج۷ ص۳۸۵⁩–۳۸۶; ⁨سیر ص۱۷۷⁩, ⁨ص۱۹۰⁩. See §7 — this is
    the single most useful passage in the note for declining to adjudicate from the platform.
17. **⁨ابن مسعود ؓ⁩ objected to it and then prayed four himself, and gave his reason: «⁨إني أكره الخلاف⁩» — and
    in one narration «⁨الخلاف شر⁩»** `[SOURCED]` — ⁨البدایہ ج۷ ص۳۸۵⁩, ⁨ص۳۸۶⁩.
18. **⁨ابن کثیر⁩ uses that fact as an argument: if ⁨ابن مسعود ؓ⁩ followed ⁨عثمان ؓ⁩ in this secondary matter, how
    much more in the matter of the Qurʾān itself** `[SOURCED]` — ⁨البدایہ ج۷ ص۳۸۶⁩. This is ⁨ابن کثیر⁩'s own
    reasoning and must be attributed to him.
19. **The ring of the Prophet ﷺ fell from ⁨عثمان ؓ⁩'s hand into ⁨بئر أريس⁩, two miles from ⁨المدينة⁩; it was
    never recovered; he had worn it six years; he made a replacement, and when he was killed that one too
    disappeared and nobody knew who took it** `[SOURCED]` — ⁨البدایہ ج۷ ص۲۹۳⁩; ⁨الکامل ج۲ ص۴۸۳⁩.
20. ⚠ **The two books tell the loss differently and it must not be merged.** **⁨البدایہ ج۷ ص۲۹۳⁩: the well
    was one of the least-watered wells; much money was spent and no trace of it was found "to this
    hour".** **⁨الکامل ج۲ ص۴۸۳⁩: they had dug a well as drinking water for the Muslims, he sat at the head
    of it toying with the ring, it fell, they drained the water and could not find it, he set a great
    reward, and he grieved intensely.** `[SOURCED, disputed]` See §13.
21. **⁨الکامل⁩ gives the ring's inscription as three lines — ⁨محمد⁩ / ⁨رسول⁩ / ⁨الله⁩; ⁨البدایہ⁩ gives the
    replacement's inscription as «⁨محمدٌ رسولُ الله⁩»** `[SOURCED]` — ⁨الکامل ج۲ ص۴۸۳⁩; ⁨البدایہ ج۷ ص۲۹۳⁩.
22. **On the ⁨حِمى⁩ the books preserve two different answers from ⁨عثمان ؓ⁩, and they are not the same answer**
    `[SOURCED, disputed]` — ⁨سیر ص۱۸۴⁩: he protected it not for his own camels or sheep but for the camels
    of the ⁨صدقة⁩. ⁨سیر ص۱۹۰⁩ (⁨سیر⁩'s own attribution: ⁨سیف⁩): he protected nothing but what had been protected
    before him, and added that he had been the Arabs' largest owner of camels and sheep when he took
    office and now had two camels, for his Ḥajj. See §14.
23. **On the ⁨عطاء⁩, ⁨الحسن البصري رحمہ اللہ⁩ is quoted describing the payouts of ⁨عثمان ؓ⁩'s reign in the
    present tense: stipends called out, rations called out, then clarified butter and honey — «⁨الأعطيات⁩
    ⁨جارية، والأرزاق دارَّةٌ⁩»** `[SOURCED]` — ⁨البدایہ ج۷ ص۳۷۹⁩.
24. **A newborn in ⁨المدينة⁩ was entered on the roll at fifty dirhams and a garment, to be raised to a
    hundred when the child completed a year** `[SOURCED]` — ⁨البدایہ ج۷ ص۳۸۰⁩.
25. **When the eastern conquests came in, the ⁨خراج⁩ grew so large that treasuries were set up and the
    stipends made to flow** `[SOURCED]` — ⁨سیر ص۱۷۹⁩.
26. **The sea was opened under ⁨عثمان ؓ⁩, and it had been closed under ⁨عمر ؓ⁩ — ⁨سیر⁩ preserves ⁨عمر ؓ⁩'s
    refusal, ⁨عمرو بن العاص ؓ⁩'s written description of the sea, and ⁨عمر ؓ⁩'s answer: «⁨والله لا أحمل فيه⁩
    ⁨مسلما أبدًا⁩»** `[SOURCED]` — ⁨سیر ص۱۷۴⁩. **This is the cleanest single instance of a policy that changed
    between the two caliphates.** See §16.3.
27. **⁨عثمان ؓ⁩ required his governors to attend the Ḥajj season every year, and wrote to the provinces that
    anyone with a grievance against a governor should come to the season — «⁨فإني آخذُ له حقَّه من عامله⁩»**
    `[SOURCED]` — ⁨البدایہ ج۷ ص۳۸۶⁩.
28. **He permitted senior Companions to travel and settle where they wished; ⁨عمر ؓ⁩ had restrained them,
    and ⁨ابن کثیر⁩ gives ⁨عمر ؓ⁩'s stated reason in ⁨عمر ؓ⁩'s own words** `[SOURCED]` — ⁨البدایہ ج۷ ص۳۸۶⁩.
    ⚠ **⁨ابن کثیر⁩ then draws a consequence from that change. That consequence is his reading and must be
    attributed to him by name, never narrated as what happened.** See §16.4 and §23.
29. **Syria was gathered into one command for ⁨معاوية ؓ⁩ two years into ⁨عثمان ؓ⁩'s caliphate, and ⁨الکامل⁩ gives
    the whole chain of deaths and appointments that produced it — most of them ⁨عمر ؓ⁩'s own appointments**
    `[SOURCED]` — ⁨الکامل ج۲ ص۴۸۸⁩.
30. **A land-exchange was opened in Iraq: those with a share in the conquered land could sell it against
    property in ⁨الحجاز⁩, ⁨اليمن⁩ and elsewhere — ⁨الکامل⁩ records that the people were delighted and that the
    transfers went through by mutual consent** `[SOURCED]` — ⁨الکامل ج۲ ص۴۸۰⁩.
31. **The «first» notices of the caliphate that our pages actually carry are five: the first tent pitched

34. **On the ⁨مصحف⁩ complaint specifically, his answer is one sentence and it is safe to quote: the Qurʾān

36. **His own relation to the ⁨مصحف⁩ is recorded in his own words: he hated a day to pass without looking

## 1. The state he took over — what the books say about the first years

Three separate authorities are preserved on how long the reign was quiet, and they do not give the same
number. **⁨الحسن البصري رحمہ اللہ⁩, in ⁨سیر⁩, gives twelve years** (⁨سیر ص۱۵۸⁩):

> وقال أبو هلال: سمعت الحسن يقول: عمل عثمان اثنتي عشرة سنة، ما ينكرون من إمارته شيئا.
> — ⁨سیر أعلام النبلاء جراشدون ص۱۵۸⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/10906/1300
> *English:* "Abū Hilāl said: I heard al-Ḥasan say — ʿUthmān governed twelve years, and they found nothing in his rule to object to."

**⁨الزهري رحمہ اللہ⁩ gives six, and adds a comparison the room will find striking** (⁨سیر ص۱۸۵⁩):

> وعن الزهري قال: ولي عثمان، فعمل ست سنين لا ينقم عليه الناس شيئا، وإنه لأحب إليهم من عمر؛ لأن عمر كان شديدا عليهم، فلما وليهم عثمان لان لهم ووصلهم، ثم إنه توانى في أمرهم، واستعمل
> — ⁨سیر أعلام النبلاء جراشدون ص۱۸۵⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/10906/1327
> *English:* "From al-Zuhrī: ʿUthmān took office and governed six years with the people holding nothing against him, and he was dearer to them than ʿUmar — because ʿUmar had been severe with them, and when ʿUthmān governed them he was gentle with them and joined them with gifts; then he grew slack in their affair, and employed…"

⚠ The sentence is cut at the page break: the printed page ends at «⁨واستعمل⁩». **Do not complete it.** What
al-Zuhrī went on to say about the appointments stands on the next printed page, which was not fetched in
this pass; the gap is recorded in §22.

**⁨قتادة رحمہ اللہ⁩ and ⁨خليفة بن خياط⁩ fix the length of the caliphate itself** (⁨سیر ص۱۶۱⁩):

> قال قتادة: ولي عثمان اثنتي عشرة سنة، غير اثني عشر يوما وكذا قال خليفة بن خياط، وغيره.
> — ⁨سیر أعلام النبلاء جراشدون ص۱۶۱⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/10906/1303
> *English:* "Qatādah said: ʿUthmān governed twelve years less twelve days; and so said Khalīfah b. Khayyāṭ and others."

The three numbers are not a contradiction — ⁨الحسن⁩ describes the whole reign, ⁨الزهري⁩ a first phase inside
it, ⁨قتادة⁩ the arithmetic of the term — but they must be given as three statements from three named men,
never fused into one sentence.

## 2. The first khutbah, and the ⁨منبر⁩ as an instrument

**⁨البدایہ⁩ preserves his first address after the ⁨بيعة⁩, on ⁨الواقدي⁩'s authority** (⁨البدایہ ج۷ ص۳۸۲⁩). It is
four clauses long, and it is the best opening quotation in the strand, because it is a man saying in
public that he has not done this before:

> أن عثمانَ لمّا بويع خرجَ إلى الناس فخطبهم، فحمدَ اللهَ وأثنى عليه، ثم قال: أيها الناس أولُ كلِّ مركبٍ صعبٌ، وإنَّ بعد اليوم أيامًا، وإن أعشْ تأتِكُم الخطبة على وجهها، وما كنا خطباء وسيُعلمُنا الله.
> — ⁨البدایہ والنہایہ ج۷ ص۳۸۲⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/30097/3528
> *English:* "When ʿUthmān received the pledge he went out to the people and addressed them. He praised God and extolled Him, then said: O people, the first mounting of any beast is hard. After today there are days to come, and if I live the address will reach you as it should. We were never orators — and God will teach us."

**⁨البدایہ⁩ also carries what it calls the last address he delivered to an assembly**, on ⁨سیف بن عمر⁩'s
authority — that is ⁨البدایہ⁩'s own attribution (⁨البدایہ ج۷ ص۳۸۲⁩). Its closing instruction is the line worth
a slide:

> واحذروا من اللّه الغِيَرَ، والزموا جماعتكم لا تصيروا أحزابًا
> — ⁨البدایہ والنہایہ ج۷ ص۳۸۲⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/30097/3528
> *English:* "Beware of God's reversals, and hold fast to your community together; do not become parties."

He then recited ⁨آل عمران ١٠٣⁩ — the printed text gives «⁨وَاذْكُرُوا نِعْمَتَ اللَّهِ عَلَيْكُمْ إِذْ كُنْتُمْ أَعْدَاءً فَأَلَّفَ بَيْنَ قُلُوبِكُمْ فَأَصْبَحْتُمْ بِنِعْمَتِهِ إِخْوَانًا⁩» (⁨البدایہ ج۷ ص۳۸۲⁩).

**A third notice shows the ⁨منبر⁩ used as an administrative instrument.** ⁨الإمام أحمد رحمہ اللہ⁩, whose isnād
the ⁨البدایہ⁩ editor grades ṣaḥīḥ in his footnote, has ⁨موسى بن طلحة⁩ say (⁨البدایہ ج۷ ص۳۸۳⁩):

> سمعت عثمان بن عفان وهو على المنبر والمؤذن يقيم الصلاة وهو يستخبر الناس يسألهم عن أخبارهم، وأسعارهم (٢).
> — ⁨البدایہ والنہایہ ج۷ ص۳۸۳⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/30097/3529
> *English:* "I heard ʿUthmān b. ʿAffān while he was upon the pulpit and the muʾadhdhin was giving the iqāmah, and he was seeking news of the people, asking them about their tidings and their prices."

The «(٢)» is the printed edition's footnote marker: it records that the printed copy ⁨ط⁩ had «⁨وأسفارهم⁩» and
that the editor restored «⁨وأسعارهم⁩» from ⁨المسند⁩. **Quote the word "prices" only after looking at a page
image.**

## 3. The first judgement put before him — ⁨عبيد الله بن عمر⁩ and ⁨الهرمزان⁩

This belongs to the administration, because it is the first thing put before him after the ⁨بيعة⁩. Narrate
it exactly as ⁨سیر⁩ narrates it, and stop.

⁨سیر⁩ sets out the background in ⁨الذهبي⁩'s own voice — the paragraph opens «⁨قلت⁩:», the author speaking, not a
transmitter (⁨سیر ص۱۶۷⁩). After ⁨عمر ؓ⁩ was struck, ⁨عبيد الله بن عمر⁩ killed ⁨الهرمزان⁩ — king of ⁨تُستر⁩, whose
Islam ⁨سیر⁩ has already recorded — and also ⁨جفينة⁩ and the daughter of ⁨أبو لؤلؤة⁩; ⁨سعد ؓ⁩ took the sword out of
his hand and imprisoned him. ⁨سعيد بن المسيب رحمہ اللہ⁩ is then quoted on the reason: ⁨أبو لؤلؤة⁩, ⁨جفينة⁩ and
⁨الهرمزان⁩ had been seen sitting together with a two-headed dagger between them, and ⁨عبد الرحمن بن أبي بكر ؓ⁩
described that dagger after ⁨عمر ؓ⁩ was struck, and it matched.

The decision itself (⁨سیر ص۱۶۷⁩):

> فلما استخلف عثمان قال له علي: أقد عبيد الله من الهرمزان، فقال عثمان: ما له ولي غيري، وإني قد عفوت ولكن أديه.
> — ⁨سیر أعلام النبلاء جراشدون ص۱۶۷⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/10906/1309
> *English:* "When ʿUthmān was made caliph, ʿAlī said to him: exact retaliation from ʿUbayd Allāh for al-Hurmuzān. ʿUthmān said: he has no next of kin but me, and I have pardoned — but I shall pay his blood-money."

⁨سیر ص۱۶۶⁩ gives the same scene inside the chamber, with ⁨عثمان ؓ⁩ asking a group of the ⁨مهاجرون⁩ for counsel,
⁨علی ؓ⁩ answering «⁨أرى أن تقتله⁩», another saying «⁨قتل أبوه بالأمس ويقتل هو اليوم؟⁩!», and ⁨عمرو بن العاص ؓ⁩
arguing that God had spared him the event. **Two Companions differed on a point of law and the caliph
decided. Say that, and do not say who was right.**

## 4. ⁨المسجد الحرام⁩, 26 AH — the expropriation, and the sentence about ⁨عمر ؓ⁩

Only ⁨سیر⁩ carries this at the pages read (⁨سیر ص۱۶۹⁩), and it is among the best administrative anecdotes in
the window, because it contains its own precedent:

> فيها: زاد عثمان في المسجد الحرام ووسعه، واشترى الزيادة من قوم، وأبى آخرون، فهدم عليهم ووضع الأثمان في بيت المال، فصاحوا بعثمان فأمر بهم إلى الحبس، وقال: ما جرأكم علي إلا حلمي، وقد فعل هذا بكم عمر فلم تصيحوا عليه، ثم كلموه فيهم فأطلقهم.
> — ⁨سیر أعلام النبلاء جراشدون ص۱۶۹⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/10906/1311
> *English:* "In it: ʿUthmān added to the Sacred Mosque and widened it. He bought the addition from some people, and others refused; so it was pulled down upon them and the prices were placed in the treasury. They cried out at ʿUthmān, and he ordered them to prison, and said: nothing made you bold against me but my forbearance — ʿUmar did this very thing to you and you did not cry out at him. Then people spoke to him on their behalf, and he released them."

Three administrative facts sit inside that one paragraph: **compulsory purchase with the price lodged in
⁨بيت المال⁩ · imprisonment for public disorder · release on intercession** — and the caliph's own statement
that the policy was not new.

## 5. ⁨المسجد النبوي⁩ — the measurements, and the six doors kept as ⁨عمر ؓ⁩ left them

**⁨البدایہ⁩ gives the full building specification** (⁨البدایہ ج۷ ص۲۹۲⁩):

> وفيها وسَّع عثمانُ بن عفان مسجدَ النبي ﷺ، وبناه بالقَصَّة (١) -وهي الكلس- كان يؤتى به من بطن نخل (٢)، والحجارة المنقوشة، وجعل عمده حجارةً مرصعةً، وسقفه بالساج، وجعل طوله ستين ومئة ذراع، وعرضه خمسين ومئة ذراع، وجعل أبوابه ستة، على ما كانت (عليه) في زمان عمر بن الخطاب، ابتدأ بناءه في ربيع الأول منها.
> — ⁨البدایہ والنہایہ ج۷ ص۲۹۲⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/30097/3438
> *English:* "In it ʿUthmān b. ʿAffān widened the mosque of the Prophet ﷺ and built it with qaṣṣah — that is, lime — which used to be brought from Baṭn Nakhl, and with carved stone; he made its columns of inlaid stone and its roof of teak; he made its length one hundred and sixty cubits and its breadth one hundred and fifty; and he made its doors six, as they had been in the time of ʿUmar b. al-Khaṭṭāb. He began its building in Rabīʿ al-Awwal of that year."

«(١)» and «(٢)» are the printed edition's footnote markers — (١) glosses ⁨القَصَّة⁩ as gypsum from ⁨اللسان⁩,
(٢) locates ⁨بطن نخل⁩ as a village near ⁨المدينة⁩ on the Basra road, from ⁨معجم البلدان⁩. «(⁨عليه⁩)» in round
brackets is the editor's supplement.

**The clause «⁨على ما كانت⁩ (⁨عليه⁩) ⁨في زمان عمر بن الخطاب⁩» is the point of this card for our purposes.** The
building is new; the number of doors is ⁨عمر ؓ⁩'s. That is continuity you can show in one line.

**⁨سیر⁩ has only the bare notice** (⁨سیر ص۱۷۶⁩):

> وفيهاك زاد عثمان في مسجد رسول الله صلى الله عليه وسلم فوسعه وبناه بالحجارة
> — ⁨سیر أعلام النبلاء جراشدون ص۱۷۶⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/10906/1318
> *English:* "And in it ʿUthmān added to the mosque of the Messenger of God ﷺ, widened it, and built it with stone."

⚠ **«⁨وفيهاك⁩» is a typo in the Shamela text for «⁨وفيها⁩».** Do not reproduce it, and do not silently correct
it on an artifact — quote this notice from a page image, or quote ⁨البدایہ⁩ instead.

**On the year.** ⁨البدایہ ج۷ ص۲۹۲⁩ carries the year-heading «[⁨سنة ثلاثين من الهجرة النبوية⁩]» *in the middle
of the page*, **after** the mosque notice and after the ⁨مِنى⁩ notice — so in ⁨البدایہ⁩ both belong to the
preceding year, 29 AH. ⁨سیر⁩ independently files both under ⁨سنة تسع وعشرين⁩ (⁨سیر ص۱۷۶⁩–۱۷۷, immediately before
its heading «⁨سنة ثلاثين⁩:»). **The two books agree: 29 AH** `[SOURCED]`. A slide saying 30 AH would be
following neither.

## 6. The third call on ⁨الجمعة⁩, given on ⁨الزوراء⁩

Both books record it in one line, and neither explains it (⁨البدایہ ج۷ ص۲۹۴⁩):

> وفي هذه السنة زاد عثمان النداء الثالث يوم الجمعة على الزَّوْراء (٤).
> — ⁨البدایہ والنہایہ ج۷ ص۲۹۴⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/30097/3440
> *English:* "And in this year ʿUthmān added the third call on the day of Jumuʿah, upon al-Zawrāʾ."

«(٤)» is the printed edition's footnote marker; that footnote identifies ⁨الزوراء⁩ from ⁨معجم البلدان⁩ as
«⁨موضع عند سوق المدينة قرب المسجد⁩» — a spot by the market of ⁨المدينة⁩, near the mosque — and notes the report
is in ⁨تاریخ الطبری⁩. **That is ⁨البدایہ⁩'s own cross-reference, not a citation by us.**

⁨الکامل⁩ gives it in the same words under the same year (⁨الکامل ج۲ ص۴۸۵⁩):

> فِي هَذِهِ السَّنَةِ زَادَ عُثْمَانُ النِّدَاءَ الثَّالِثَ يَوْمَ الْجُمُعَةِ عَلَى الزَّوْرَاءِ.
> — ⁨الکامل فی التاریخ ج۲ ص۴۸۵⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/21712/1175
> *English:* "In this year ʿUthmān added the third call on the day of Jumuʿah, upon al-Zawrāʾ."

⚠ **What the pages do not say:** neither book says *why* it was added, neither says the reason was that the
city had grown, and neither calls it an «⁨أذان⁩» — both say «⁨النداء الثالث⁩». A slide must not supply a
reason. See §22.

## 7. The four rakʿahs at ⁨مِنى⁩ — the page where ⁨ابن کثیر⁩ refuses a report

This is the most valuable single passage in the strand, because it shows a Sunni authority of the first
rank laying out four explanations for a disputed act, rejecting one outright, doubting a second, and
still pronouncing no verdict on the Companion.

### 7.1 What was done, and who objected

⁨البدایہ ج۷ ص۲۹۲⁩, under 29 AH:

> وفيها حج بالناس عثمان بن عفان، وضرب له بمنى فسطاطًا، فكان أول فسطاط ضربه عثمان بمنى، وأتمَّ الصلاة عامه هذا، فأنكر ذلك عليه غيرُ واحد من الصحابة، كعلي وعبد الرحمن بن عوف وعبد الله بن مسعود، حتى قال ابن مسعود ليتَ حظّي من أربع ركعات ركعتان متقبلتان
> — ⁨البدایہ والنہایہ ج۷ ص۲۹۲⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/30097/3438
> *English:* "In it ʿUthmān b. ʿAffān led the people in Ḥajj, and a tent was pitched for him at Minā — and that was the first tent ʿUthmān pitched at Minā — and he completed the prayer in full that year of his. More than one of the Companions objected to it — such as ʿAlī, ʿAbd al-Raḥmān b. ʿAwf and ʿAbd Allāh b. Masʿūd — until Ibn Masʿūd said: would that my share of four rakʿahs were two, accepted."

⁨البدایہ ج۷ ص۳۸۵⁩ notes that the ⁨ابن مسعود ؓ⁩ saying is «⁨في الصحيح⁩», and the editor's footnote gives ⁨البخاري⁩
١٠٨٤ and ⁨مسلم ٦٩٥⁩.

### 7.2 The exchange with ⁨عبد الرحمن بن عوف ؓ⁩ — two versions, and they differ

**⁨البدایہ ج۷ ص۲۹۲⁩ gives it at length, on ⁨ابن جرير⁩'s authority — ⁨البدایہ⁩'s own attribution.** ⁨عثمان ؓ⁩ is
asked whether he has taken a wife at ⁨مكة⁩; he answers that he has a wife at ⁨المدينة⁩ too, and is told he
lodges where his family is; he says he has property at ⁨الطائف⁩ he means to look to after the pilgrims
disperse, and is told ⁨الطائف⁩ is three days off; he says a party from ⁨اليمن⁩ have been saying the prayer at
home is two rakʿahs and may cite him if they see him praying two. ⁨عبد الرحمن ؓ⁩ answers with the precedent,
and the exchange closes:

> فقال له: قد كان رسول الله ﷺ ينزل عليه الوحي (والناس يومئذ الإسلام فيهم قليل)، وكان يصلّي ها هنا ركعتين، وكان أبو بكر يصلّي ها هنا ركعتين، وكذلك عمر بن الخطاب، وصليتَ أنتَ ركعتين صدرًا من إمارتك، قال فسكت عثمان ثم قال: إنما هو رأي رأيته.
> — ⁨البدایہ والنہایہ ج۷ ص۲۹۲⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/30097/3438
> *English:* "He said to him: the revelation used to come down upon the Messenger of God ﷺ — and Islam among the people was then but little — and he used to pray here two rakʿahs; and Abū Bakr used to pray here two rakʿahs; and likewise ʿUmar b. al-Khaṭṭāb; and you yourself prayed two for the first part of your rule. ʿUthmān was silent, then said: it is only a view I have taken."

**⁨سیر ص۱۷۷⁩ gives a shorter and differently ordered version, in which ⁨علی ؓ⁩ speaks first and ⁨عبد الرحمن ؓ⁩
second, and in which ⁨عبد الرحمن ؓ⁩ rejects the excuse outright:**

> وحج عثمان بالناس وضرب له بمنى فسطاط، وأتم الصلاة بها وبعرفة، فعابوا عليه ذلك فجاءه علي، فقال: والله ما حدث أمر ولا قدم عهد، ولقد عهدت نبيك صلى الله عليه وسلم يصلي ركعتين، ثم أبا بكر، ثم عمر، ثم أنت صدرا من ولايتك فقال: رأي رأيته.
> — ⁨سیر أعلام النبلاء جراشدون ص۱۷۷⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/10906/1319
> *English:* "ʿUthmān led the people in Ḥajj, and a tent was pitched for him at Minā, and he completed the prayer there and at ʿArafah, and they faulted him for it. ʿAlī came to him and said: by God, no new matter has arisen and no covenant has grown old; I knew your Prophet ﷺ praying two rakʿahs, then Abū Bakr, then ʿUmar, then yourself for the first part of your governance. He said: a view I have taken."

> وكلمه عبد الرحمن بن عوف، فقال: إني أخبرت عن جفاة الناس قد قالوا: إن الصلاة للمقيم ركعتان وقالوا: هذا عثمان يصلي ركعتين فصليت أربعا لهذا، وإني قد اتخذت بمكة زوجة. فقال عبد الرحمن: ليس هذا بعذر. قال: هذا رأي رأيته.
> — ⁨سیر أعلام النبلاء جراشدون ص۱۷۷⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/10906/1319
> *English:* "ʿAbd al-Raḥmān b. ʿAwf spoke to him, and he said: I was told of the coarse among the people who have been saying that the prayer for a resident is two rakʿahs, and saying, this is ʿUthmān praying two — so I prayed four for that reason; and I have taken a wife at Mecca. ʿAbd al-Raḥmān said: this is no excuse. He said: this is a view I have taken."

⚠ **The two accounts differ in who says what.** In ⁨البدایہ⁩ the points about the wife, ⁨الطائف⁩ and the
Yemenis are ⁨عثمان ؓ⁩'s answers *to ⁨عبد الرحمن ؓ⁩*, drawn out one by one; in ⁨سیر⁩ they are a single speech, and
the Prophet–Abū Bakr–ʿUmar precedent is put by *⁨علی ؓ⁩*. **Do not merge them.** What both books give
word-for-word is the last thing ⁨عثمان ؓ⁩ says: **«⁨رأي رأيته⁩» — "a view I have taken."** That is the line for
the slide.

### 7.3 The four explanations, and the one ⁨ابن کثیر⁩ refuses

⁨البدایہ ج۷ ص۳۸۶⁩ collects them:

> وقد حكى الزُّهري (١) وغيره: أن عثمان إنما (أتم) خشية على الأعراب أن يعتقدوا أن فرض الصلاة ركعتان، وقيل: بل قد تأهل بمكة.
> — ⁨البدایہ والنہایہ ج۷ ص۳۸۶⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/30097/3532
> *English:* "Al-Zuhrī and others have related that ʿUthmān only completed the prayer out of fear for the bedouin, lest they believe that the obligation of the prayer is two rakʿahs; and it is said: rather, he had taken a wife at Mecca."

«(⁨أتم⁩)» in round brackets is the editor's supplement. Then a third explanation — a ḥadīth reported through
⁨أبو يعلى⁩ — and **⁨ابن کثیر⁩'s own verdict on it, in his own voice**:

> أنَّ عثمانَ صلَّى بهم بمنى أربع ركعات، ثم أقبل عليهم فقال: إني سمعتُ رسولَ الله ﷺ يقول: "إذا تزوَّجَ الرجلُ ببلد فهو من أهله" وإني أتمت لأني تزوجتُ بها منذ قدمتها. وهذا الحديث لا يصح، وقد تزوَّجَ رسول الله ﷺ في عمرة القضاء بميمونة بنت الحارث ولم يتمّ الصلاة
> — ⁨البدایہ والنہایہ ج۷ ص۳۸۶⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/30097/3532
> *English:* "…that ʿUthmān prayed four rakʿahs with them at Minā, then turned to them and said: I heard the Messenger of God ﷺ say, 'when a man marries in a town he is of its people' — and I completed the prayer because I married there when I came to it. **But this ḥadīth is not sound**; and the Messenger of God ﷺ married Maymūnah bint al-Ḥārith during the ʿUmrah of Fulfilment and did not complete the prayer."

And a fourth explanation, which **⁨ابن کثیر⁩ also questions**:

> وقد قيل إن عثمان تأول أنه أمير المؤمنين حيث كان، وهكذا تأولت عائشة فأتمت، وفي هذا التأويل نظر، فإن رسول الله ﷺ هو رسول الله حيث كان، ومع هذا ما أتمَّ الصلاةَ في الأسفار.
> — ⁨البدایہ والنہایہ ج۷ ص۳۸۶⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/30097/3532
> *English:* "And it has been said that ʿUthmān construed that he was Commander of the Faithful wherever he was — and ʿĀʾishah construed likewise and completed the prayer. **In this construal there is something to consider**, for the Messenger of God ﷺ was the Messenger of God wherever he was, and with that he did not complete the prayer on journeys."

**This is exactly the material the guardrail exists for.** ⁨ابن کثیر⁩ sifts the reports, rejects one and
doubts another, and pronounces no verdict on ⁨عثمان ؓ⁩ at all. The speaker may do the same from the platform,
and say openly that this is what the book does.

### 7.4 What ⁨ابن مسعود ؓ⁩ then did, and how ⁨ابن کثیر⁩ uses it

⁨البدایہ ج۷ ص۳۸۵⁩, on ⁨أبو إسحاق⁩'s authority:

> أن عبد الله بن مسعود دخل مسجد منى فقال كم صلَّى أميرُ المؤمنين الظهرَ؟ قالوا: أربعاً، فصلَّى ابنُ مسعود أربعاً، فقالوا: ألم تُحدِّثنا أن رسول الله ﷺ وأبا بكر وعمر صلّوا ركعتين؟ فقال: نعم! وأنا أحدثكموه الآن، ولكني أكره الاختلاف.
> — ⁨البدایہ والنہایہ ج۷ ص۳۸۵⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/30097/3531
> *English:* "ʿAbd Allāh b. Masʿūd entered the mosque of Minā and said: how many did the Commander of the Faithful pray for Ẓuhr? They said: four. So Ibn Masʿūd prayed four. They said: did you not narrate to us that the Messenger of God ﷺ and Abū Bakr and ʿUmar prayed two? He said: yes — and I narrate it to you now; but I dislike disagreement."

⁨البدایہ ج۷ ص۳۸۶⁩ gives the variant wording, then draws an inference — **and the inference is ⁨ابن کثیر⁩'s, not
ours**:

> فقيل له: عَتبتَ على عثمان وصليتَ أربعاً؟ فقال: إني أكره الخلاف -وفي رواية: الخلاف شر- فإذا كان هذا متابعة من ابن مسعود إلى عثمان في هذا الفرع فكيف بمتابعته إياه في أصل القرآن؟
> — ⁨البدایہ والنہایہ ج۷ ص۳۸۶⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/30097/3532
> *English:* "It was said to him: you reproached ʿUthmān and then prayed four? He said: I dislike disagreement — and in one narration: disagreement is evil. So if this is Ibn Masʿūd following ʿUthmān in this secondary matter, what then of his following him in the very foundation of the Qurʾān?"

## 8. The ⁨توحيد المصاحف⁩ — the alarm, and the man who raised it

### 8.1 What ⁨ابن کثیر⁩ says the act was, before he narrates it

⁨ابن کثیر⁩ files the whole thing under **⁨مناقبه⁩** — his merits — and opens with the theological description
of the act before a single event (⁨البدایہ ج۷ ص۳۸۴⁩):

> ومن مناقبه الكبار وحسناته العظيمة أنَّه جمعَ الناسَ على قراءة واحدةٍ، وكتب المصحفَ على العرضة الأخيرة، التي درسَها جبريل على رسول الله ﷺ في آخر سنيِّ حياته
> — ⁨البدایہ والنہایہ ج۷ ص۳۸۴⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/30097/3530
> *English:* "And among his great merits and mighty good deeds is that he gathered the people upon a single reading, and wrote the muṣḥaf according to the final review which Jibrīl went over with the Messenger of God ﷺ in the last of the years of his life."

### 8.2 The problem, in ⁨البدایہ⁩'s words — the two reading-traditions that collided

⁨البدایہ ج۷ ص۳۸۴⁩ is the only one of the three that names the traditions on both sides:

> وكان سبب ذلك أن حُذَيْفَة بن اليَمان كان في بعض الغزوات، وقد اجتمع فيها خلقٌ من أهل الشام، ممَّنْ يقرأُ على قراءةِ المِقْدادِ بن الأسْود، وأبي الدَّرْداء، وجماعة من أهل العراق، ممَّنْ يَقْرأُ على قراءة عبد الله بن مسعود، وأبي موسى، وجعل من لا يعلم بَسَوغان القراءة على سبعة أحرف، يفضل قراءته على قراءة غيره، وربما خَطَّأ الآخر أو كَفَّره، فأدى ذلك إلى اختلاف شديد، وانتشار في الكلام السيء بين الناس
> — ⁨البدایہ والنہایہ ج۷ ص۳۸۴⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/30097/3530
> *English:* "The cause of that was that Ḥudhayfah b. al-Yamān was on one of the campaigns, in which a great many of the people of Syria had gathered — those who recited after the reading of al-Miqdād b. al-Aswad and Abū al-Dardāʾ — and a company of the people of Iraq who recited after the reading of ʿAbd Allāh b. Masʿūd and Abū Mūsā. And those who did not know of the permissibility of reciting upon seven aḥruf began to prefer their own reading over the reading of another, and sometimes to declare the other wrong, or to declare him an unbeliever. That led to severe disagreement and to the spread of evil speech among the people."

### 8.3 What ⁨حذيفة ؓ⁩ said — the sentence all three books carry

⁨البدایہ ج۷ ص۳۸۴⁩ continues:

> فركبَ حُذَيْفَة إلى عثمان فقال: يا أمير المؤمنين أدْرِكْ هذه الأمةَ قبلَ أن تختلف في كتابها كاختلافِ اليهود والنصارى في كتبهم.
> — ⁨البدایہ والنہایہ ج۷ ص۳۸۴⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/30097/3530
> *English:* "Ḥudhayfah rode to ʿUthmān and said: O Commander of the Faithful, reach this umma before it differs over its Book as the Jews and the Christians differed over their books."

⁨سیر ص۱۵۷⁩ has it in ⁨أنس ؓ⁩'s narration, with the campaign located and with one extra word — **⁨عثمان ؓ⁩'s
alarm**:

> وقال أنس: إن حذيفة قدم على عثمان، وكان يغزو مع أهل العراق قبل أرمينية، فاجتمع في ذلك الغزو أهل الشام وأهل العراق، فتنازعوا في القرآن حتى سمع حذيفة من اختلافهم ما يكره، فركب حتى أتى عثمان فقال: يا أمير المؤمنين أدرك هذه الأمة قبل أن يختلفوا في القرآن اختلاف اليهود والنصارى في الكتب. ففزع لذلك عثمان
> — ⁨سیر أعلام النبلاء جراشدون ص۱۵۷⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/10906/1299
> *English:* "Anas said: Ḥudhayfah came to ʿUthmān — he used to campaign with the people of Iraq towards Armenia — and in that campaign the people of Syria and the people of Iraq had come together and disputed over the Qurʾān, until Ḥudhayfah heard of their disagreement what he disliked. So he rode until he came to ʿUthmān and said: O Commander of the Faithful, reach this umma before they differ over the Qurʾān as the Jews and the Christians differed over the Books. **ʿUthmān was alarmed at that**…"

⚠ **The two books place the campaign differently.** ⁨سیر⁩ says he was campaigning with the Iraqis «⁨قبل⁩
⁨أرمينية⁩»; ⁨البدایہ⁩ says only «⁨في بعض الغزوات⁩». Say "on campaign" and leave the geography alone unless ⁨سیر⁩ is
being cited.

### 8.4 What ⁨ابن کثیر⁩ says ⁨عثمان ؓ⁩ did *before* he acted

This matters, because it is the answer to the objection that he acted alone (⁨البدایہ ج۷ ص۳۸۴⁩):

> فعند ذلك جمعَ عثمانُ الصحابةَ وشاورهم في ذلك، ورأى أن يكتب المصحف على حرف واحد، وأن يجمعَ الناسَ في سائر الأقاليم على القراءة به، دون ما سواه، لما رأى في ذلك من مصلحةِ كفِّ المنازعةِ، ودفعِ الاختلافِ
> — ⁨البدایہ والنہایہ ج۷ ص۳۸۴⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/30097/3530
> *English:* "At that ʿUthmān gathered the Companions and took counsel with them in the matter, and held the view that the muṣḥaf should be written upon a single ḥarf and that the people in all the regions should be gathered upon reciting by it and nothing else — for the benefit he saw in it of stopping the contention and driving away the disagreement."

**A report on the same page is graded by the printed edition's editor and must be labelled.** ⁨البدایہ ج۷⁩
⁨ص۳۸۴⁩ carries, on ⁨الواقدي⁩'s authority, ⁨أبو هريرة ؓ⁩ coming in to ⁨عثمان ؓ⁩ after the copying and saying
«⁨أصَبْتَ ووُفِّقْتَ⁩» — you have got it right and been granted success — and citing a ḥadīth about "the
suspended leaves"; ⁨عثمان ؓ⁩ was pleased and ordered ten thousand for him. **Footnote (٣) of the printed
edition says of that ḥadīth: «⁨وهو ضعيف جداً⁩» — very weak.** `[SOURCED — and graded weak by the edition's
editor]`. It is recorded here so nobody puts it on a slide; see §23.

## 9. The committee — where the three books disagree, and it must not be merged

**⁨سیر ص۱۵۷⁩ names four men doing one job together:**

> فأمر زيد بن ثابت، وسعيد بن العاص وعبد الله بن الزبير، وعبد الرحمن بن الحارث بن هشام، أن ينسخوها في المصاحف وقال: إذا اختلفتم أنتم وزيد في عربية فاكتبوها بلسان قريش، فإن القرآن إنما نزل بلسانهم.
> — ⁨سیر أعلام النبلاء جراشدون ص۱۵۷⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/10906/1299
> *English:* "He ordered Zayd b. Thābit, Saʿīd b. al-ʿĀṣ, ʿAbd Allāh b. al-Zubayr and ʿAbd al-Raḥmān b. al-Ḥārith b. Hishām to copy them into the muṣḥafs, and said: if you and Zayd differ over a point of Arabic, write it in the tongue of Quraysh, for the Qurʾān came down only in their tongue."

**⁨الکامل ج۲ ص۴۸۲⁩ gives the same four, in the same one job, and adds the whole ⁨أبو بكر ؓ⁩ backstory first:**

> فَأَرْسَلَ عُثْمَانُ إِلَيْهَا [مَنْ] أَخَذَهَا مِنْهَا، وَأَمَرَ زَيْدَ بْنَ ثَابِتٍ وَعَبْدَ اللَّهِ بْنَ الزُّبَيْرِ وَسَعِيدَ بْنَ الْعَاصِ وَعَبْدَ الرَّحْمَنِ بْنَ الْحَارِثِ بْنِ هِشَامٍ فَنَسَخُوهَا فِي الْمَصَاحِفِ، وَقَالَ عُثْمَانُ: إِذَا اخْتَلَفْتُمْ فَاكْتُبُوهَا بِلِسَانِ قُرَيْشٍ، فَإِنَّمَا نَزَلَ بِلِسَانِهِمْ، فَفَعَلُوا.
> — ⁨الکامل فی التاریخ ج۲ ص۴۸۲⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/21712/1172
> *English:* "So ʿUthmān sent to her someone who took them from her, and ordered Zayd b. Thābit, ʿAbd Allāh b. al-Zubayr, Saʿīd b. al-ʿĀṣ and ʿAbd al-Raḥmān b. al-Ḥārith b. Hishām, and they copied them into the muṣḥafs; and ʿUthmān said: if you differ, write it in the tongue of Quraysh, for it came down only in their tongue. And they did so."

The «[⁨مَنْ⁩]» in square brackets is the modern editor's supplement to the ⁨الکامل⁩ text.

**⁨البدایہ ج۷ ص۳۸۴⁩ gives the four men different jobs:**

> وأمر زيد بن ثابت الأنصاري أن يكتبَ وأن يمليَ عليه سعيدُ بن العاص الأموي، بحضرة عبد الله بن الزبير الأسدي وعبد الرحمن بن الحارث بن هشام المخزومي، وأمرهم إذا اختلفوا في شيء أن يكتبوه بلغةِ قريشٍ
> — ⁨البدایہ والنہایہ ج۷ ص۳۸۴⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/30097/3530
> *English:* "He ordered Zayd b. Thābit al-Anṣārī to write, and Saʿīd b. al-ʿĀṣ al-Umawī to dictate to him, in the presence of ʿAbd Allāh b. al-Zubayr al-Asadī and ʿAbd al-Raḥmān b. al-Ḥārith b. Hishām al-Makhzūmī; and he ordered them, when they differed over anything, to write it in the speech of Quraysh."

⚠ **This is a genuine disagreement inside the safe list, and it is itself a finding.** The four names are
the same in all three books; **what each man did is not.** A slide may say "four men, one of them
⁨زيد بن ثابت ؓ⁩" and quote the instruction about ⁨قريش⁩, which all three carry. It must not say "⁨زيد⁩ wrote and
⁨سعيد⁩ dictated" as though that were agreed, and it must not say "the four wrote together" as though that
were agreed either.

**Note also the clan tags.** ⁨البدایہ⁩ is the only one that attaches them — ⁨الأنصاري⁩, ⁨الأموي⁩, ⁨الأسدي⁩,
⁨المخزومي⁩ — and that is worth keeping, because it shows a committee drawn from four different houses.

**The chain of custody of the ⁨صحف⁩ is in ⁨الکامل⁩ at length** (⁨الکامل ج۲ ص۴۸۲⁩): ⁨عمر ؓ⁩'s proposal to ⁨أبو بكر ؓ⁩
after ⁨اليمامة⁩, ⁨زيد ؓ⁩ collecting «⁨مِنَ الرِّقَاعِ وَالْعُسُبِ وَصُدُورِ الرِّجَالِ⁩» — from parchments, palm
stalks and the breasts of men — the ⁨صحف⁩ with ⁨أبو بكر ؓ⁩, then ⁨عمر ؓ⁩, then ⁨حفصة ؓ⁩. And their return:

> فَلَمَّا نَسَخُوا الصُّحُفَ رَدَّهَا عُثْمَانُ إِلَى حَفْصَةَ، وَأَرْسَلَ إِلَى كُلِّ أُفُقٍ بِمُصْحَفٍ، وَحَرَقَ مَا سِوَى ذَلِكَ، وَأَمَرَ أَنْ يَعْتَمِدُوا عَلَيْهَا وَيَدَعُوا مَا سِوَى ذَلِكَ.
> — ⁨الکامل فی التاریخ ج۲ ص۴۸۲⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/21712/1172
> *English:* "When they had copied the leaves, ʿUthmān returned them to Ḥafṣah, and sent a muṣḥaf to every horizon, and burned what was besides that, and ordered that they rely upon these and leave what was besides them."

## 10. Where the copies went — and the one sentence about whose handwriting it is

**⁨البدایہ⁩ is the only book of the three that lists the destinations** (⁨البدایہ ج۷ ص۳۸۴⁩):

> فكتبَ لأهل الشام مصحفاً، ولأهل مصر آخر، وبعث إلى البصرة مصحفاً وإلى الكوفة بآخر، وأرسل إلى مكة مصحفاً وإلى اليمن مثله، وأقر بالمدينة مصحفاً.
> — ⁨البدایہ والنہایہ ج۷ ص۳۸۴⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/30097/3530
> *English:* "He wrote for the people of Syria a muṣḥaf, and for the people of Egypt another; he sent to Basra a muṣḥaf and to Kufa another; he sent to Mecca a muṣḥaf and to Yemen the like of it; and he kept one at Medina."

That is **seven**: ⁨الشام⁩ · ⁨مصر⁩ · ⁨البصرة⁩ · ⁨الكوفة⁩ · ⁨مكة⁩ · ⁨اليمن⁩ · ⁨المدينة⁩. ⁨سیر ص۱۵۷⁩ says only «⁨وأرسل إلى كل⁩
⁨جند من أجناد المسلمين بمصحف⁩» — to every army-district of the Muslims; ⁨الکامل ج۲ ص۴۸۲⁩ says «⁨وَأَرْسَلَ⁩
⁨إِلَى كُلِّ أُفُقٍ بِمُصْحَفٍ⁩» — to every horizon. **Neither gives a list or a number.** A map slide showing
seven arrows must carry ⁨البدایہ⁩'s page, not "the sources".

**And then the sentence that answers the question the room will actually ask** (⁨البدایہ ج۷ ص۳۸۴⁩):

> ويقال لهذه (١) المصاحف الأئمة، وليست كلها بخطّ عثمان، بل ولا واحد منها، وإنما هي بخطّ زيد بن ثابت، وإنما يقال لها المصاحف العثمانية نسبةً إلى أمره وزمانه، وإمارته، كما يقال دينار هرقلي، أي ضُرب في زمانه ودولته.
> — ⁨البدایہ والنہایہ ج۷ ص۳۸۴⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/30097/3530
> *English:* "These muṣḥafs are called the Imāms; and they are not all in ʿUthmān's hand — no, not one of them — but rather in the hand of Zayd b. Thābit. They are called the ʿUthmānic muṣḥafs only by attribution to his command, his time and his rule, as one says a Heraclian dīnār, meaning it was struck in his time and his state."

«(١)» is the printed edition's footnote marker, recording that manuscript ⁨أ⁩ read «⁨ويقال لها⁩». **The
⁨دينار هرقلي⁩ comparison is ⁨ابن کثیر⁩'s own, and it is the single most usable line in the strand** — it
settles, from inside the tradition and on a printed page, what "the muṣḥaf of ʿUthmān" means.

## 11. What was done with the other copies — and ⁨علی ؓ⁩'s two statements

**⁨البدایہ ج۷ ص۳۸۵⁩ opens the section with the act and the reason in one sentence:**

> ثم عمد إلى بقية المصاحف التي بأيدي الناس مما يخالف ما كتبه فحرقه، لئلا يقع بسببه اختلاف.
> — ⁨البدایہ والنہایہ ج۷ ص۳۸۵⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/30097/3531
> *English:* "Then he turned to the rest of the muṣḥafs in the people's hands, of what differed from what he had written, and burned it — so that disagreement should not arise on account of it."

**⁨سیر ص۱۵۷⁩ gives the order as an order, and ends with a flat sentence about what followed:**

> وأمرهم أن يحرقوا كل مصحف يخالف المصحف الذي أرسل إليهم به، فذلك زمان حرقت فيه المصاحف بالنار.
> — ⁨سیر أعلام النبلاء جراشدون ص۱۵۷⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/10906/1299
> *English:* "And he ordered them to burn every muṣḥaf that differed from the muṣḥaf he had sent to them — and that was the time in which the muṣḥafs were burned with fire."

**⁨علی ؓ⁩ is quoted twice, and the second quotation is the one that matters.** ⁨البدایہ ج۷ ص۳۸۵⁩ gives the
short form first, from ⁨سُوَيْد بن غَفَلة⁩ through ⁨أبو بكر بن أبي داود⁩'s ⁨كتاب المصاحف⁩:

> قال لي عليٌّ حين حرق عثمان المصاحف: لو لم يصنعْه هو لصنعته.
> — ⁨البدایہ والنہایہ ج۷ ص۳۸۵⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/30097/3531
> *English:* "ʿAlī said to me when ʿUthmān burned the muṣḥafs: had he not done it, I would have done it."

⁨سیر ص۱۵۸⁩ carries the same line in the same words. **⁨البدایہ⁩ then gives the long form, from ⁨البيهقي⁩, and
this is the one for the slide:**

> قال عليٌّ: أيها الناس! إياكم والغُلُوَّ في عثمان تقولون حرّق المصاحف، والله ما حرّقها إلا عن ملأ من أصحاب محمد ﷺ، ولو وليت مثل ما ولي لفعلتُ (مثل) الذي فعل.
> — ⁨البدایہ والنہایہ ج۷ ص۳۸۵⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/30097/3531
> *English:* "ʿAlī said: O people, beware of going to excess concerning ʿUthmān, saying 'he burned the muṣḥafs'. By God, he did not burn them except before an assembly of the Companions of Muḥammad ﷺ; and had I held the office he held, I would have done the like of what he did."

«(⁨مثل⁩)» in round brackets is the editor's supplement.

**And ⁨ابن الأثیر⁩'s own verdict on the act, in his own voice** (⁨الکامل ج۲ ص۴۸۲⁩):

> فَكُلُّ النَّاسِ عَرَفَ فَضْلَ هَذَا الْفِعْلِ، إِلَّا مَا كَانَ مِنْ أَهْلِ الْكُوفَةِ
> — ⁨الکامل فی التاریخ ج۲ ص۴۸۲⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/21712/1172
> *English:* "And all the people recognised the merit of this act, except what came from the people of Kufa."

## 12. ⁨ابن مسعود ؓ⁩ — the objection, and how the books end it

**⁨البدایہ⁩ states the objection plainly, with its three parts** (⁨البدایہ ج۷ ص۳۸۵⁩): that his own ⁨مصحف⁩ was
taken from him and burned; that he spoke of his own precedence in Islam over ⁨زيد بن ثابت ؓ⁩, who wrote the
copies; and that he told his companions to keep their own copies back, reciting ⁨آل عمران ١٦١⁩. And then the
end of it:

> فكتب إليه عثمان ﵁ يدعوه إلى اتباع الصحابة فيما أجمعوا عليه من المصلحة في ذلك، وجمع الكلمة، وعدم الاختلاف، فأناب وأجاب إلى المتابعة وترك المخالفة ﵃ أجمعين.
> — ⁨البدایہ والنہایہ ج۷ ص۳۸۵⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/30097/3531
> *English:* "So ʿUthmān ؓ wrote to him calling him to follow the Companions in the benefit they had agreed upon in that, and the gathering of the word, and the absence of disagreement — and he turned back and answered to compliance and left off opposition, may God be pleased with them all."

**⁨الکامل⁩ shows the same thing from the Kufan end, and it is stronger, because there ⁨ابن مسعود ؓ⁩ rebukes his
own partisans** (⁨الکامل ج۲ ص۴۸۲⁩): when the muṣḥaf reached Kufa the Companions there rejoiced at it, but
those attached to ⁨عبد الله ؓ⁩ refused it and faulted the people —

> فَقَامَ فِيهِمُ ابْنُ مَسْعُودٍ وَقَالَ: وَلَا كُلُّ ذَلِكَ، فَإِنَّكُمْ وَاللَّهِ قَدْ سُبِقْتُمْ سَبْقًا بَيِّنًا، فَارْبِعُوا عَلَى ظَلْعِكُمْ.
> — ⁨الکامل فی التاریخ ج۲ ص۴۸۲⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/21712/1172
> *English:* "So Ibn Masʿūd stood among them and said: not all of that! For by God you have been outstripped, plainly outstripped — so go gently, according to your own lameness."

**The two books do not tell the same scene** — ⁨البدایہ⁩'s ending is a letter from ⁨المدينة⁩, ⁨الکامل⁩'s is a
speech in Kufa. **Both end the same way: he closed the matter himself.** Give whichever one is used, and
say which book it is from.

## 13. The ring of the Prophet ﷺ in ⁨بئر أريس⁩

**⁨البدایہ ج۷ ص۲۹۳⁩, under 30 AH:**

> وفي هذه السنة سقطَ خاتمُ النبيّ ﷺ من يد عثمان في بئر أَرِيس (٥)، وهي على ميلين من المدينة، وهي من أقل الآبار ماءً، فلم يدرك خبره بعد بذل مال جزيل، والاجتهاد في طلبه حتى الساعة، فاستخلف عثمان بعده خاتمًا من فضةٍ، ونقشَ عليه "محمدٌ رسولُ الله ﷺ"، فلما قُتل عثمان ذهب الخاتم فلم يُدْرَ (٦) من أخذه.
> — ⁨البدایہ والنہایہ ج۷ ص۲۹۳⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/30097/3439
> *English:* "And in this year the ring of the Prophet ﷺ fell from ʿUthmān's hand into the well of Arīs — and it is two miles from Medina, and it is among the wells of least water — and no news of it was reached, after the giving of abundant money and striving in seeking it, down to this hour. So ʿUthmān put in its place a ring of silver, and had inscribed upon it 'Muḥammad, the Messenger of God ﷺ'. And when ʿUthmān was killed the ring went, and it was not known who took it."

«(٥)» and «(٦)» are the printed edition's footnote markers; footnote (٥) reports from ⁨ياقوت⁩ that the well
was named after ⁨أريس⁩, a Jewish man of ⁨المدينة⁩, and (٦) records a variant reading in manuscript ⁨أ⁩.

**⁨الکامل ج۲ ص۴۸۳⁩ tells the loss itself quite differently, and the difference is the whole scene:**

> فَحَفَرُوا بِئْرًا بِالْمَدِينَةِ شِرْبًا لِلْمُسْلِمِينَ، فَقَعَدَ عَلَى رَأْسِ الْبِئْرِ فَجَعَلَ يَعْبَثُ بِالْخَاتَمِ فَسَقَطَ مِنْ يَدِهِ فِي الْبِئْرِ، فَطَلَبُوهُ فِيهَا وَنَزَحُوا مَا فِيهَا مِنَ الْمَاءِ فَلَمْ يَقْدِرُوا عَلَيْهِ، فَجَعَلَ فِيهِ مَالًا عَظِيمًا لِمَنْ جَاءَ بِهِ، وَاغْتَمَّ لِذَلِكَ غَمًّا شَدِيدًا. فَلَمَّا يَئِسَ مِنْهُ صَنَعَ خَاتَمًا آخَرَ عَلَى مِثَالِهِ وَنَقْشِهِ فَبَقِيَ فِي إِصْبَعِهِ حَتَّى هَلَكَ، فَلَمَّا قُتِلَ ذَهَبَ الْخَاتَمُ فَلَمْ يُدْرَ مَنْ أَخَذَهُ.
> — ⁨الکامل فی التاریخ ج۲ ص۴۸۳⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/21712/1173
> *English:* "They had dug a well at Medina as drinking water for the Muslims, and he sat at the head of the well and began toying with the ring, and it fell from his hand into the well. They sought it in it and drained out the water that was in it and could not get at it. So he set a great sum of money for whoever brought it, and he grieved over it with intense grief. When he despaired of it he made another ring after its pattern and its inscription, and it remained on his finger until he perished. And when he was killed the ring went, and it was not known who took it."

⚠ **The two accounts are not compatible and must not be blended.** ⁨البدایہ⁩: a well two miles out, with
little water, never fathomed. ⁨الکامل⁩: a well newly dug in ⁨المدينة⁩ for drinking water, which they drained.
**Give one, name the book, and say plainly that the other tells it differently.**

**On the ring itself, ⁨الکامل⁩ is the fuller** (⁨الکامل ج۲ ص۴۸۳⁩): the Prophet ﷺ had it made when he wished to
write to the non-Arabs and was told they accept no letter unless sealed; iron was made and ⁨جبرائیل⁩ forbade
it; brass was made and ⁨جبرائیل⁩ said throw it away; silver was made and ⁨جبرائیل⁩ told him to keep it. Its
inscription was three lines — **⁨محمد⁩ a line, ⁨رسول⁩ a line, ⁨الله⁩ a line**. He wore it until he died, then
⁨أبو بكر ؓ⁩ until he died, then ⁨عمر ؓ⁩ until he died —

> ثُمَّ تَخَتَّمَ بِهِ عُثْمَانُ سِتَّ سِنِينَ.
> — ⁨الکامل فی التاریخ ج۲ ص۴۸۳⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/21712/1173
> *English:* "Then ʿUthmān wore it for six years."

⁨البدایہ ج۷ ص۲۹۳⁩ gives the same six years, attributing the long ḥadīth to ⁨ابن جرير⁩ and noting that part of
it has already been given «⁨في الصحيح⁩». **The ⁨البدایہ⁩ editor's footnote (٨) disputes the gold-ring detail in
⁨ابن جرير⁩'s version and cites ⁨البخاري ٥٨٧٠⁩ for the description of the Prophet ﷺ's ring — that is the
edition's editor, not ⁨ابن کثیر⁩.**

## 14. The ⁨حِمى⁩ — and the two different answers the books preserve

The ⁨حِمى⁩ — pasture closed off to general grazing — is one of the five things put to him, and **the books do
not preserve one answer to it; they preserve two, and they are different arguments.**

**⁨سیر ص۱۸۴⁩, in the exchange conducted through ⁨علی ؓ⁩:**

> وأما الحمى فوالله ما حميته لإبلي ولا لغنمي، وإنما حميته لإبل الصدقة.
> — ⁨سیر أعلام النبلاء جراشدون ص۱۸۴⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/10906/1326
> *English:* "As for the protected pasture — by God, I did not protect it for my camels nor for my sheep; I protected it only for the camels of the ṣadaqah."

**⁨سیر ص۱۹۰⁩, in the public assembly, on ⁨سیف⁩'s authority — ⁨سیر⁩'s own attribution:**

> قالوا: وحميت الحمى، وإني والله ما حميت إلا ما حمي قبلي، وإني قد وليت وإني لأكثر العرب بعيرا وشاء، فمالي اليوم غير بعيرين لحجتي، أكذاك؟ قالوا: نعم.
> — ⁨سیر أعلام النبلاء جراشدون ص۱۹۰⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/10906/1332
> *English:* "They said: and you protected the pasture. But by God I have protected nothing except what was protected before me; and I took office being the greatest of the Arabs in camels and sheep, and today I have nothing but two camels, for my Ḥajj. Is it not so? They said: yes."

⚠ **These are two different defences.** The first is about *purpose* — the pasture is for the ṣadaqah
herds, not for him. The second is about *precedent and personal loss* — nothing new was closed, and he is
poorer than when he started. **A slide may carry either, sourced, and must not fuse them into a single
speech.** Note that in the second, the crowd itself confirms the claim: «⁨أكذاك؟ قالوا⁩: ⁨نعم⁩» — "is it not
so? They said: yes" — which is the most striking detail in the passage and appears after four separate
points in that assembly.

## 15. The ⁨عطاء⁩, the ⁨أرزاق⁩, and the treasuries

**⁨البدایہ ج۷ ص۳۷۹⁩ carries the best single description of what the state felt like to live in.** It is
⁨الحسن البصري رحمہ اللہ⁩, quoted by ⁨البخاري⁩ in his ⁨التاريخ⁩, and he begins by saying he was there:

> سمعت الحسن يقول: أدركت عثمان على ما نقموا عليه. قلَّما يأتي على الناس يومٌ إلا وهم يقتسمون فيه خيرًا، يقال لهم: يا معشر المسلمين اغدوا على أعطياتكم، فيأخذونها وافرة، ثم يقال لهم: اغدوا على أرزاقكم فيأخذونها وافرة، ثم يقال لهم: اغدوا على السمن والعسل، الأعطيات جارية، والأرزاق دارَّةٌ، والعدو منفيٌّ (٨)، وذات البين حسن، والخيرُ كثيرٌ
> — ⁨البدایہ والنہایہ ج۷ ص۳۷۹⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/30097/3525
> *English:* "I heard al-Ḥasan say: I lived through ʿUthmān's time, for all that they held against him. Scarcely a day came upon the people but that they were dividing some good in it. It would be said to them: O company of Muslims, come in the morning for your stipends — and they would take them in full. Then it would be said to them: come in the morning for your rations — and they would take them in full. Then it would be said to them: come in the morning for the clarified butter and the honey. The stipends were flowing, the rations pouring, the enemy driven off, relations between people good, and the good abundant."

«(٨)» is the printed edition's footnote marker, recording that the printed copy ⁨ط⁩ had «⁨متقي⁩» and that the
editor restored «⁨منفيٌّ⁩» from manuscript ⁨أ⁩ and its sources. **Do not put that particular word on a slide
without a page image.**

**A single household's entitlement is recorded, and it is the most concrete number in the strand.** ⁨محمد⁩
⁨بن هلال⁩'s grandmother used to visit ⁨عثمان ؓ⁩; he missed her one day and was told she had given birth that
night (⁨البدایہ ج۷ ص۳۸۰⁩):

> قالت: فأرسل إليّ بخمسين درهمًا وشُقَيْقَة سنبلانية (٥)، وقال: هذا عطاء ابنك وكسوته، فإذا مرت به سنة رفعناه إلى مئة.
> — ⁨البدایہ والنہایہ ج۷ ص۳۸۰⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/30097/3526
> *English:* "She said: he sent me fifty dirhams and a full-length shuqayqah, and said: this is your son's stipend and his clothing; and when a year has passed over him we shall raise it to a hundred."

«(٥)» is the printed edition's footnote marker, glossing ⁨الشُّقة⁩ and ⁨السنبلانية⁩ from ⁨اللسان⁩. **A child on
the roll at birth, at a stated rate, rising on a stated schedule: that is a register, not a gift.**

**Where the money came from, and what was done with it** (⁨سیر ص۱۷۹⁩):

> ولما فتح ابن عامر هذه البلاد الواسعة كثر الخراج على عثمان وأتاه المال من كل وجه حتى اتخذ له الخزائن وأدر الأرزاق، وكان يأمر للرجل بمائة ألف بدرة في كل بدرة أربعة آلاف وافية.
> — ⁨سیر أعلام النبلاء جراشدون ص۱۷۹⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/10906/1321
> *English:* "When Ibn ʿĀmir conquered these wide lands the kharāj upon ʿUthmān became great and wealth came to him from every direction, until he set up treasuries for it and made the rations flow. And he used to order for a man a hundred thousand — a badrah, in each badrah four thousand of full weight."

⚠ **The last clause is difficult in the Shamela text** and the English above is deliberately literal. The
sense of «⁨بمائة ألف بدرة⁩» is not secure — the next sentence in the same page, from ⁨أبو يوسف القاضي⁩, uses
the unit cleanly and should be preferred if a number is needed at all:

> وقال أبو يوسف القاضي: أخرجوا من خزائن كسرى مائتي ألف بدرة في كل بدرة أربعة آلاف.
> — ⁨سیر أعلام النبلاء جراشدون ص۱۷۹⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/10906/1321
> *English:* "Abū Yūsuf the qāḍī said: they brought out from the treasuries of Kisrā two hundred thousand badrahs, in each badrah four thousand."

**Two spending decisions of his own are preserved, and both are tellable.** In the first, ⁨قَطَن بن عوف⁩
⁨الهلالي⁩, ⁨ابن عامر⁩'s governor of ⁨كرمان⁩, promised a thousand dirhams to every man who would swim a flooded
wadi, and paid out four million; ⁨ابن عامر⁩ refused to allow it against the accounts, and the matter was
referred (⁨البدایہ ج۷ ص۳۸۳⁩):

> فكتب بذلك إلى عثمان بن عفان، فكتب عثمان: أن احْسِبْها له، فإنه إنَّما أعانَ المسلمين في سبيل اللّه. فمن ذلك اليوم سُمِّيتِ الجوائزَ لإجازةِ الوادي
> — ⁨البدایہ والنہایہ ج۷ ص۳۸۳⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/30097/3529
> *English:* "He wrote about it to ʿUthmān b. ʿAffān, and ʿUthmān wrote: reckon it to his account, for he only helped the Muslims in the way of God. And from that day the word jawāʾiz — bounties — was so called, from the crossing of the wadi."

In the second, ⁨البدایہ⁩ on ⁨ابن جرير⁩'s authority — ⁨البدایہ⁩'s own attribution — ⁨طلحة ؓ⁩ met him going out to the
mosque and told him the fifty thousand owed was now ready (⁨البدایہ ج۷ ص۳۸۳⁩):

> فقال له عثمان: إنا قد وهبناكها لمروءتك.
> — ⁨البدایہ والنہایہ ج۷ ص۳۸۳⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/30097/3529
> *English:* "ʿUthmān said to him: we have made a gift of it to you, for your nobility."

**And one where he was on the losing side of the bargain.** He had bought land from a man who then delayed
taking the money and told him «⁨إنك غَبْنتني⁩» — you got the better of me — and that everyone he met blamed
him. ⁨عثمان ؓ⁩'s answer, from the ⁨مسند⁩ of ⁨أحمد⁩, whose isnād the ⁨البدایہ⁩ editor grades ḥasan (⁨البدایہ ج۷ ص۳۸۳⁩):

> قال: فاختر بين أرضكَ ومالكَ، ثم قال: قال رسول اللّه ﷺ: "أدخل اللّه الجنةَ رجلًا كان سهلًا مشتريًا وبائعًا، وقاضيًا مقتضيًا".
> — ⁨البدایہ والنہایہ ج۷ ص۳۸۳⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/30097/3529
> *English:* "He said: then choose between your land and your money. Then he said: the Messenger of God ﷺ said, 'God admitted to Paradise a man who was easy when buying and selling, and when settling a debt and calling one in.'"

## 16. What he inherited from ⁨عمر ؓ⁩, and the four places it changed

### 16.1 Syria — a consolidation that was mostly ⁨عمر ؓ⁩'s own doing

⁨الکامل⁩ explains, against the charge that Syria was handed to one man as a favour, that the command
assembled itself out of deaths (⁨الکامل ج۲ ص۴۸۸⁩): ⁨أبو عبيدة ؓ⁩ left ⁨عياض بن غنم⁩ over his work; ⁨عياض⁩ died and
**⁨عمر ؓ⁩** put ⁨سعيد بن حِذْيَم الجمحي⁩ in his place; ⁨سعيد⁩ died and **⁨عمر ؓ⁩** appointed ⁨عمير بن سعد الأنصاري⁩;
⁨يزيد بن أبي سفيان ؓ⁩ died and **⁨عمر ؓ⁩** put his brother ⁨معاوية ؓ⁩ in his place, so that ⁨الأردن⁩ and ⁨دمشق⁩ came
together for ⁨معاوية ؓ⁩ in ⁨عمر ؓ⁩'s own time. Then:

> وَمَرِضَ عُمَيْرُ بْنُ سَعْدٍ فَاسْتَعْفَى عُثْمَانَ وَاسْتَأْذَنَهُ فِي الرُّجُوعِ إِلَى أَهْلِهِ، فَأَذِنَ لَهُ، وَضَمَّ عُثْمَانُ حِمْصَ وَقِنَّسْرِينَ إِلَى مُعَاوِيَةَ، وَمَاتَ عَبْدُ الرَّحْمَنِ بْنُ عَلْقَمَةَ، وَكَانَ عَلَى فِلَسْطِينَ، فَضَمَّ عُثْمَانُ عَمَلَهُ إِلَى مُعَاوِيَةَ فَاجْتَمَعَ الشَّامُ لِمُعَاوِيَةَ لِسَنَتَيْنِ مِنْ إِمَارَةِ عُثْمَانَ، فَهَذَا كَانَ سَبَبُ اجْتِمَاعِ الشَّامِ لَهُ.
> — ⁨الکامل فی التاریخ ج۲ ص۴۸۸⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/21712/1178
> *English:* "ʿUmayr b. Saʿd fell ill and asked ʿUthmān to excuse him, and sought his leave to return to his family, and he granted it; and ʿUthmān joined Ḥimṣ and Qinnasrīn to Muʿāwiya. And ʿAbd al-Raḥmān b. ʿAlqamah died — he was over Palestine — and ʿUthmān joined his district to Muʿāwiya; so Syria came together for Muʿāwiya two years into ʿUthmān's amirate. That was the cause of Syria's coming together for him."

`[SOURCED]` — and note that ⁨ابن الأثیر⁩ sets this out precisely as an explanation, «⁨فَهَذَا كَانَ⁩
⁨سَبَبُ⁩…». **Four of the five appointments in the chain are ⁨عمر ؓ⁩'s.**

### 16.2 The governorships that did change — ⁨الذهبي⁩'s own list

⁨سیر ص۱۸۶⁩ opens with «⁨قلت⁩:» — ⁨الذهبي⁩ in his own voice — and gives five replacements as things held against
him:

> قلت: ومما نقموا عليه أنه عزل عمير بن سعد عن حمص، وكان صالحا زاهدا، وجمع الشام لمعاوية، ونزع عمرو بن العاص عن مصر، وأمر ابن أبي سرح عليها، ونزع أبا موسى الأشعري عن البصرة، وأمر عليها عبد الله بن عامر، نزع المغيرة بن شعبة عن الكوفة وأمر عليها سعيد بن العاص.
> — ⁨سیر أعلام النبلاء جراشدون ص۱۸۶⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/10906/1328
> *English:* "I say: and among what they held against him is that he removed ʿUmayr b. Saʿd from Ḥimṣ — and he was upright and ascetic — and gathered Syria for Muʿāwiya; and took ʿAmr b. al-ʿĀṣ off Egypt and set Ibn Abī Sarḥ over it; and took Abū Mūsā al-Ashʿarī off Basra and set ʿAbd Allāh b. ʿĀmir over it; and took al-Mughīrah b. Shuʿbah off Kufa and set Saʿīd b. al-ʿĀṣ over it."

⚠ **⁨الذهبي⁩ is reporting what was held against him, not endorsing it.** The clause «⁨ومما نقموا عليه⁩» is the
whole frame of the sentence and must survive any use of it. **Never quote the list without that clause.**
The removal of ⁨عمرو بن العاص ؓ⁩ from ⁨مصر⁩ is given its administrative reason elsewhere in ⁨سیر⁩: each of the two
men wrote to ⁨عثمان ؓ⁩ complaining of the other, and he separated the offices — «⁨انصرف وولي عبد الله الخراج⁩
⁨والجند⁩» (⁨سیر ص۱۷۳⁩) — after which ⁨عمرو ؓ⁩ came back angry, and ⁨سیر⁩ records the two dry exchanges between them
about the cotton of his cloak and the milking of the she-camels (⁨سیر ص۱۷۳⁩).

### 16.3 The sea — the clearest reversal of an ⁨عمر ؓ⁩ policy in the window

⁨سیر ص۱۷۴⁩, on ⁨سیف⁩'s authority — ⁨سیر⁩'s own attribution — gives the whole thing: ⁨معاوية ؓ⁩ pressed ⁨عمر ؓ⁩ for
leave to fight at sea; ⁨عمر ؓ⁩ answered that a village of ⁨حمص⁩ whose dogs and cocks he could hear was dearer
to him than everything on the sea; and then he wrote to ⁨عمرو بن العاص ؓ⁩ to describe the sea and the man who
rides it. The answer is one of the finest paragraphs in ⁨سیر⁩:

> فكتب إليه: إني رأيت خلقا كبيرا يركبه خلق صغير، إن ركد حرق القلوب، وإن تحرك أراع العقول، يزداد فيه اليقين قلة، والشك كثرة، وهم فيه كدود على عود، إن مال غرق، وإن نجا برق.
> — ⁨سیر أعلام النبلاء جراشدون ص۱۷۴⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/10906/1316
> *English:* "He wrote to him: I have seen a great creature ridden by a small creature. If it is still it burns the hearts; if it moves it terrifies the minds. Certainty in it grows less and doubt grows more. They upon it are like worms on a stick: if it tips, they drown; if it escapes, they are dazed."

> فلما قرأ عمر الكتاب كتب إلى معاوية: والله لا أحمل فيه مسلما أبدًا.
> — ⁨سیر أعلام النبلاء جراشدون ص۱۷۴⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/10906/1316
> *English:* "When ʿUmar read the letter he wrote to Muʿāwiya: by God, I will never carry a Muslim upon it."

**Under ⁨عثمان ؓ⁩, ⁨قبرص⁩ was taken from the sea.** ⁨سیر ص۱۷۰⁩, under 27 AH: ⁨معاوية ؓ⁩ raided ⁨قبرص⁩, «⁨فركب البحر⁩
⁨بالجيوش⁩»; ⁨عبادة بن الصامت ؓ⁩ was with him, and his wife ⁨أم حرام بنت ملحان ؓ⁩ was thrown from her mule and died
a martyr there, as the Prophet ﷺ had given her tidings. **That one pair of pages — ⁨عمر ؓ⁩'s refusal and the
first naval expedition — is the cleanest "what changed" card in the strand** `[SOURCED]`.

### 16.4 The Companions were allowed to travel — and ⁨ابن کثیر⁩'s reading of what followed

⁨البدایہ ج۷ ص۳۸۶⁩ gives first the administrative mechanism, then the change:

> ومما كان يعتمده عثمان بن عفان أنه كان يُلْزمُ عُمَّاله بحضور الموسم كلَّ عام، ويكتب إلى الرعايا: منْ كانتْ له عند أحدٍ منهم مَظْلمةٌ فليوافِ إلى الموسم فإني آخذُ له حقَّه من عامله
> — ⁨البدایہ والنہایہ ج۷ ص۳۸۶⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/30097/3532
> *English:* "And among what ʿUthmān b. ʿAffān made a practice of was that he obliged his governors to attend the pilgrimage season every year, and he wrote to the subject peoples: whoever has a grievance against any one of them, let him come to the season, for I shall take his right for him from his governor."

**That is an annual, public, empire-wide grievance procedure, and it is one line on a printed page.** It is
the strongest administrative card in the note `[SOURCED]`.

Immediately after it, the change and its consequence:

> وكان عثمانُ قد سمحَ لكثير من (كبار) الصحابة في المسير حيث شاؤوا من البلاد، وكان عمر يحجر عليهم في ذلك، حتى ولا في الغزو، ويقول: إني أخاف أن تروا الدنيا وأن يراكم (٤) أبناؤها
> — ⁨البدایہ والنہایہ ج۷ ص۳۸۶⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/30097/3532
> *English:* "ʿUthmān had permitted many of the senior Companions to travel where they wished among the lands; ʿUmar used to restrain them in that, not even for a campaign, and would say: I fear that you should see the world, and that the world's sons should see you."

«(⁨كبار⁩)» is the editor's supplement; «(٤)» is the printed edition's footnote marker recording a variant in
manuscript ⁨أ⁩.

⚠ **What ⁨ابن کثیر⁩ writes next is his own reading of cause and effect** — that when they went out in ⁨عثمان⁩
ؓ's time people gathered round them, each acquired a following, and each group came to want its own man in
the general command after ⁨عثمان ؓ⁩ — **and it must be attributed to ⁨ابن کثیر⁩ by name, as how he read the
event, never narrated as what happened.** See §23. The paragraph ends with «⁨فإنا لله وإنا إليه راجعون⁩».

## 17. Land, ⁨صلح⁩ money, and a policy that let Iraqis sell their share

**⁨الکامل ج۲ ص۴۸۰⁩ records a land-exchange that is barely known and is entirely administrative.** ⁨سعيد بن⁩
⁨العاص⁩ had written that Kufa's old families were being swamped by later arrivals; ⁨عثمان ؓ⁩ put the matter to
the people of ⁨المدينة⁩ and then said:

> فَقَالَ عُثْمَانُ: يَا أَهْلَ الْمَدِينَةِ اسْتَعِدُّوا وَاسْتَمْسِكُوا فَقَدْ دَبَّتْ إِلَيْكُمُ الْفِتَنُ، وَإِنِّي وَاللَّهِ لَأَتَخَلَّصَنَّ لَكُمُ الَّذِي لَكُمْ حَتَّى أَنْقُلَهُ إِلَيْكُمْ إِنْ رَأَيْتُمْ، حَتَّى يَأْتِيَ مَنْ شَهِدَ مَعَ أَهْلِ الْعِرَاقِ سَهْمَهُ، فَيُقِيمَ مَعَهُ فِي بِلَادِهِ.
> — ⁨الکامل فی التاریخ ج۲ ص۴۸۰⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/21712/1170
> *English:* "ʿUthmān said: O people of Medina, prepare yourselves and hold fast, for the trials have crept toward you. And by God I shall extract for you what is yours, until I transfer it to you if you see fit — so that whoever was present with the people of Iraq may have his share come to him, and stay with him in his own country."

They asked how a share of land could be moved to them, and:

> فَقَالَ: يَبِيعُهَا مَنْ شَاءَ بِمَا كَانَ لَهُ بِالْحِجَازِ وَالْيَمَنِ وَغَيْرِهِمَا مِنَ الْبِلَادِ. فَفَرِحُوا وَفَتَحَ اللَّهُ لَهُمْ أَمْرًا لَمْ يَكُنْ فِي حِسَابِهِمْ، وَفَعَلُوا ذَلِكَ وَاشْتَرَاهُ رِجَالٌ مِنْ كُلِّ قَبِيلَةٍ، وَجَازَ لَهُمْ عَنْ تَرَاضٍ مِنْهُمْ وَمِنَ النَّاسِ وَإِقْرَارٍ بِالْحُقُوقِ.
> — ⁨الکامل فی التاریخ ج۲ ص۴۸۰⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/21712/1170
> *English:* "He said: let whoever wishes sell it against what he holds in the Ḥijāz and the Yemen and other lands. So they rejoiced, and God opened for them a matter that had not been in their reckoning; and they did that, and men of every tribe bought it, and it passed for them by mutual consent from themselves and from the people, and with acknowledgement of the rights involved."

`[SOURCED]` — ⁨الکامل ج۲ ص۴۸۰⁩. **This is a real, dated, transferable-title policy, and no other book in the
safe list carries it at the pages read.**

**A second decision shows the same office working the other way.** ⁨ابن أبي سرح⁩ had been given the fifth of
the fifth of ⁨إفريقية⁩'s spoil by ⁨عثمان ؓ⁩'s own grant; a delegation came and objected. ⁨سیر ص۱۷۲⁩:

> فقال: أنا نفلته، وذلك إليكم الآن، فإن رضيتم فقد جاز، وإن سخطتم فهو رد، قالوا: إنا نسخطه قال: فهو رد وكتب إلى عبد الله برد ذلك واستصلاحهم.
> — ⁨سیر أعلام النبلاء جراشدون ص۱۷۲⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/10906/1314
> *English:* "He said: I awarded it to him, and that is now up to you — if you are content, it has passed; and if you are displeased, it is returned. They said: we are displeased. He said: then it is returned — and he wrote to ʿAbd Allāh to return it and to conciliate them."

`[SOURCED]` — ⁨سیر ص۱۷۲⁩. **The grant was revoked because the army objected.** ⁨سیر ص۱۹۱⁩ has him make the same
point himself in the public assembly, adding that ⁨أبو بكر ؓ⁩ and ⁨عمر ؓ⁩ had made awards of the same kind.

**And the ⁨صلح⁩ figures the pages happen to fix**, useful because they are rare and specific: ⁨أرجان⁩ at
2,200,000 and ⁨دارابجرد⁩ at 1,080,000, agreed by ⁨عثمان بن أبي العاص ؓ⁩ and ⁨أبو موسى ؓ⁩ in 27 AH `[SOURCED]` —
⁨سیر ص۱۷۰⁩; ⁨مرو⁩ at 2,200,000 `[SOURCED]` — ⁨سیر ص۱۷۸⁩ and ⁨الکامل ج۲ ص۴۹۵⁩; ⁨بلخ⁩ at 400,000 (⁨الکامل⁩: "and it is
said 700,000") `[SOURCED, disputed]` — ⁨البدایہ ج۷ ص۳۰۱⁩; ⁨سیر ص۱۷۸⁩; ⁨الکامل ج۲ ص۴۹۶⁩; ⁨نيسابور⁩ at 1,000,000
`[SOURCED]` — ⁨الکامل ج۲ ص۴۹۴⁩; ⁨جرجان⁩ at "sometimes 100,000, sometimes 200,000, sometimes 300,000" — and
⁨الکامل⁩ says the people of ⁨جرجان⁩ themselves varied it and called each figure «⁨هذا صلح صلحنا⁩»
`[SOURCED, disputed]` — ⁨الکامل ج۲ ص۴۸۱⁩; ⁨البدایہ ج۷ ص۲۹۳⁩.

## 18. ⁨أبو ذر ؓ⁩ and ⁨الربذة⁩ — and where ⁨ابن الأثیر⁩ refuses the transmission

This is an administrative act — a Companion is required to live outside the capital — and it is also the
place where our own safe list most explicitly declines to narrate a scandal. **Both halves must be carried
together.**

### 18.1 ⁨البدایہ⁩'s account is mild, and gives ⁨أبو ذر ؓ⁩'s own request

⁨البدایہ ج۷ ص۲۹۴⁩ narrates that ⁨أبو ذر ؓ⁩ objected in ⁨الشام⁩ to the holding of wealth and construed
⁨التوبة ٣٤⁩ upon it; ⁨معاوية ؓ⁩ forbade him to spread that and he did not stop; ⁨معاوية ؓ⁩ complained to ⁨عثمان ؓ⁩,
who wrote for him to come to ⁨المدينة⁩, blamed him for some of what had come from him, asked him to retract
and he did not; then:

> فأمره بالمقام بالرَّبَذَةِ -وهي شرقي المدينة- ويقال إنه سأل عثمان أن يقيم (بها) وقال: إن رسول الله ﷺ قال (لي): "إذا بلغ البناء سلعا (٢) فاخرج منها" (٣) وقد بلغ البناء سلعًا، فأذن له عثمان بالمقام بالرَّبَذَة، وأمره أن يتعاهد المدينة في بعض الأحيان، حتى لا يرتدّ أعرابيًا بعد هِجْرتهِ
> — ⁨البدایہ والنہایہ ج۷ ص۲۹۴⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/30097/3440
> *English:* "He ordered him to stay at al-Rabadhah — it is east of Medina. And it is said that he asked ʿUthmān to stay there, and said: the Messenger of God ﷺ said to me, 'when the building reaches Salʿ, go out from it' — and the building had reached Salʿ. So ʿUthmān gave him leave to stay at al-Rabadhah, and ordered him to visit Medina from time to time, so that he should not revert to being a bedouin after his hijrah."

«(٢)» and «(٣)» are the printed edition's footnote markers; (٢) locates ⁨سَلْع⁩ near ⁨المدينة⁩ from ⁨معجم⁩
⁨البلدان⁩, and (٣) names ⁨البيهقي⁩'s ⁨دلائل النبوة⁩ for the ḥadīth — that is **⁨البدایہ⁩'s own referencing**.

### 18.2 ⁨ابن الأثیر⁩ refuses the lurid version, in his own voice

This is the passage to keep (⁨الکامل ج۲ ص۴۸۳⁩):

> وَقَدْ ذُكِرَ فِي سَبَبِ ذَلِكَ أُمُورٌ كَثِيرَةٌ، مِنْ سَبِّ مُعَاوِيَةَ إِيَّاهُ وَتَهْدِيدِهِ بِالْقَتْلِ، وَحَمْلِهِ إِلَى الْمَدِينَةِ مِنَ الشَّامِ بِغَيْرِ وِطَاءٍ، وَنَفْيِهِ مِنَ الْمَدِينَةِ عَلَى الْوَجْهِ الشَّنِيعِ، لَا يَصِحُّ النَّقْلُ بِهِ، وَلَوْ صَحَّ لَكَانَ يَنْبَغِي أَنْ يُعْتَذَرَ عَنْ عُثْمَانَ، فَإِنَّ لِلْإِمَامِ أَنْ يُؤَدِّبَ رَعِيَّتَهُ
> — ⁨الکامل فی التاریخ ج۲ ص۴۸۳⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/21712/1173
> *English:* "Many things have been mentioned as the cause of that — Muʿāwiya's reviling of him and threatening him with death, and his being carried to Medina from Syria without a saddle-pad, and his banishment from Medina in the disgraceful manner. **The transmission of it is not sound**; and had it been sound, it would still have been fitting to excuse ʿUthmān, for it belongs to the imām to discipline his subjects."

`[SOURCED]` — ⁨الکامل ج۲ ص۴۸۳⁩. ⚠ **This sentence is worth more to the speaker than any narrative in the
section.** It lets him say, on the authority of ⁨ابن الأثیر⁩, that the ugly version does not stand.

### 18.3 The letter, and the things that are safe to tell

**⁨عثمان ؓ⁩'s letter to ⁨معاوية ؓ⁩ about him** (⁨الکامل ج۲ ص۴۸۴⁩):

> فَكَتَبَ إِلَيْهِ عُثْمَانُ: إِنَّ الْفِتْنَةَ قَدْ أَخْرَجَتْ خَطْمَهَا وَعَيْنَيْهَا وَلَمْ يَبْقَ إِلَّا أَنْ تَثِبَ، فَلَا تَنْكَأِ الْقَرْحَ، وَجَهِّزْ أَبَا ذَرٍّ إِلَيَّ، وَابْعَثْ مَعَهُ دَلِيلًا وَكَفْكِفِ النَّاسَ وَنَفْسَكَ مَا اسْتَطَعْتَ.
> — ⁨الکامل فی التاریخ ج۲ ص۴۸۴⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/21712/1174
> *English:* "ʿUthmān wrote to him: the trial has put out its muzzle and its eyes, and nothing remains but that it should spring. So do not tear open the sore; equip Abū Dharr to come to me, send a guide with him, and restrain the people and yourself as far as you can."

**And ⁨أبو ذر ؓ⁩'s own verdict on ⁨عثمان ؓ⁩, afterwards.** A man in a gathering hinted at the ⁨الربذة⁩ business,
supposing ⁨أبو ذر ؓ⁩ held it against him (⁨سیر ص۱۵۴⁩):

> فقال أبو ذر: لا تقل في عثمان إلا خيرا
> — ⁨سیر أعلام النبلاء جراشدون ص۱۵۴⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/10906/1296
> *English:* "Abū Dharr said: say nothing of ʿUthmān but good."

`[SOURCED]` — ⁨سیر ص۱۵۴⁩. **That, with §18.2, is the pair the speaker needs if the question comes on a slip.**

Two further details from ⁨الکامل ج۲ ص۴۸۵⁩, both safe and both human: when his family came out to ⁨الربذة⁩ with
a heavy leather bag and someone said "look at this man who renounces the world — what has he got?", his
wife answered that it was neither dīnārs nor dirhams but small copper coins bought out of his stipend for
the household's needs; and when the prayer was called at ⁨الربذة⁩ he refused to lead it, telling the man in
charge of the ṣadaqah to go forward, on the ḥadīth «⁨اسْمَعْ وَأَطِعْ وَإِنْ كَانَ عَلَيْكَ عَبْدٌ⁩
⁨مُجَدَّعٌ⁩» — hear and obey though a mutilated slave be set over you — the man being a slave of the ṣadaqah
named ⁨مُجاشع⁩. **⁨الکامل ج۲ ص۵۰۵⁩ and ⁨سیر⁩ both put his death in 32 AH, ⁨الکامل⁩ adding «⁨وَقِيلَ⁩: ⁨كَانَ مَوْتُهُ⁩
⁨سَنَةَ إِحْدَى وَثَلَاثِينَ⁩»** `[SOURCED, disputed]`.

## 19. ⁨الوليد بن عقبة ؓ⁩ and the governorship of Kufa — and where ⁨الکامل⁩ corrects the report

This is in scope because it is the administration, and it is handled here **only** so that it is never
re-researched. **Most of it is listed in §23 as not for the hall.**

**What the books say first is the part nobody quotes** (⁨الکامل ج۲ ص۴۷۷⁩): ⁨الوليد⁩ was appointed to Kufa in
the second year of the caliphate, and —

> وَأَنَّهُ كَانَ مَحْبُوبًا إِلَى النَّاسِ، فَبَقِيَ كَذَلِكَ خَمْسَ سِنِينَ وَلَيْسَ لِدَارِهِ بَابٌ
> — ⁨الکامل فی التاریخ ج۲ ص۴۷۷⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/21712/1167
> *English:* "…and that he was beloved to the people, and remained so for five years, and his house had no door."

**A real administrative precedent comes out of his governorship.** A gang broke into the house of
⁨ابن الحيسمان الخزاعي⁩ and killed him; ⁨أبو شريح الخزاعي ؓ⁩ and his son witnessed against them; ⁨الوليد⁩ wrote to
⁨عثمان ؓ⁩, who wrote back ordering their execution, and they were killed at the palace gate — and ⁨ابن الأثیر⁩
gives the legal consequence (⁨الکامل ج۲ ص۴۷۷⁩):

> وَلِهَذَا السَّبَبِ أَخَذَ فِي الْقَسَامَةِ بِقَوْلِ وَلِيِّ الْمَقْتُولِ عَنْ مَلَإٍ مِنَ النَّاسِ لِيَفْطِمَ النَّاسَ عَنِ الْقَتْلِ.
> — ⁨الکامل فی التاریخ ج۲ ص۴۷۷⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/21712/1167
> *English:* "And for this reason he took, in the qasāmah, the word of the slain man's next of kin before an assembly of the people — in order to wean the people away from killing."

`[SOURCED]` — ⁨الکامل ج۲ ص۴۷۷⁩. **A caliph adjusting a rule of evidence to deter murder is a real piece of
statecraft and can be told without any of the rest of the story.**

**The deposition itself.** Witnesses testified before ⁨عثمان ؓ⁩ that ⁨الوليد⁩ had drunk wine; **the ḥadd was
carried out, by ⁨عثمان ؓ⁩'s order, in his presence** — ⁨البدایہ ج۷ ص۲۹۳⁩ says ⁨سعيد بن العاص⁩ administered it and
that it is said ⁨علی ؓ⁩ removed his cloak; and he was removed and ⁨سعيد بن العاص⁩ appointed. **⁨الکامل ج۲ ص۴۷۸⁩
corrects the report in its own voice:**

> هَكَذَا فِي هَذِهِ الرِّوَايَةِ، وَالصَّحِيحُ أَنَّ الَّذِي جَلَدَهُ عَبْدُ اللَّهِ بْنُ جَعْفَرِ بْنِ أَبِي طَالِبٍ لِأَنَّ عَلِيًّا أَمَرَ ابْنَهُ الْحَسَنَ أَنْ يَجْلِدَهُ، فَقَالَ الْحَسَنُ: وَلِّ حَارَّهَا مَنْ تَوَلَّى قَارَّهَا! فَأَمَرَ عَبْدَ اللَّهِ بْنَ جَعْفَرٍ فَجَلَدَهُ أَرْبَعِينَ.
> — ⁨الکامل فی التاریخ ج۲ ص۴۷۸⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/21712/1168
> *English:* "Thus it is in this narration; but the sound position is that the one who flogged him was ʿAbd Allāh b. Jaʿfar b. Abī Ṭālib — because ʿAlī ordered his son al-Ḥasan to flog him, and al-Ḥasan said: let him who took its cool take its heat! So he ordered ʿAbd Allāh b. Jaʿfar, and he flogged him forty."

And a point of fiqh comes straight out of it, in ⁨علی ؓ⁩'s words (⁨الکامل ج۲ ص۴۷۸⁩):

> فَقَالَ عَلِيٌّ: أَمْسِكْ، جَلَدَ رَسُولُ اللَّهِ - صَلَّى اللَّهُ عَلَيْهِ وَسَلَّمَ -، وَأَبُو بَكْرٍ أَرْبَعِينَ وَجَلَدَ عُثْمَانُ ثَمَانِينَ وَكُلٌّ سُنَّةٌ وَهَذَا أَحَبُّ إِلَيَّ.
> — ⁨الکامل فی التاریخ ج۲ ص۴۷۸⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/21712/1168
> *English:* "ʿAlī said: hold. The Messenger of God ﷺ flogged, and Abū Bakr, forty; and ʿUthmān flogged eighty; **and each is sunnah**, and this is the dearer to me."

`[SOURCED]` — ⁨الکامل ج۲ ص۴۷۸⁩. ⚠ **«⁨وَكُلٌّ سُنَّةٌ⁩» — "and each is sunnah" — is the single most useful
sentence in this section**, because it is a Companion saying, about a difference between two caliphs, that
both are within the sunnah and that he merely prefers one. That is the guardrail stated from inside the
event.

**And the incoming governor's own first words to Kufa** (⁨الکامل ج۲ ص۴۷۹⁩):

> أَلَا إِنَّ الْفِتْنَةَ قَدْ أَطْلَعَتْ خَطْمَهَا وَعَيْنَيْهَا، وَوَاللَّهِ لَأَضْرِبَنَّ وَجْهَهَا حَتَّى أَقْمَعَهَا أَوْ تُعْيِينِي، وَإِنِّي لَرَائِدٌ نَفْسِيَ الْيَوْمَ.
> — ⁨الکامل فی التاریخ ج۲ ص۴۷۹⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/21712/1169
> *English:* "Truly the trial has shown its muzzle and its eyes; and by God I shall strike its face until I subdue it, or it defeats me. And today I am a scout on my own behalf."

## 20. The five things put to him, and the answers he gave

⁨سیر ص۱۸۴⁩ preserves the whole exchange in one paragraph, and it is the most efficient page in the strand:
the Egyptians camped at ⁨الجحفة⁩, ⁨عثمان ؓ⁩ went up the ⁨منبر⁩, asked three times which of them would go and ask
the people what they objected to and what they wanted, and nobody answered; then ⁨علی ؓ⁩ stood and said "I
will", and ⁨عثمان ؓ⁩ said «⁨أنت أقربهم رحما⁩».

The five, in the book's own order:

> قالوا: نقمنا أنه محا كتاب الله -يعني كونه جمع الأمة على مصحف- وحمى الحمى، واستعمل أقرباءه، وأعطى مروان مائة الفن وتناول أصحاب رسول الله صلى الله عليه وسلم
> — ⁨سیر أعلام النبلاء جراشدون ص۱۸۴⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/10906/1326
> *English:* "They said: we hold against him that he erased the Book of God — meaning his gathering the umma upon one muṣḥaf — and protected the pasture, and employed his relatives, and gave Marwān a hundred thousand, and spoke sharply against the Companions of the Messenger of God ﷺ."

⚠ **The gloss «-⁨يعني كونه جمع الأمة على مصحف⁩-» is ⁨الذهبي⁩'s own explanatory insertion**, telling the reader
what the accusation actually referred to. Reproduce it whenever the accusation is quoted; without it the
sentence is unusable.

**His answer on the ⁨مصحف⁩, which is the one in scope here and is entirely safe to put on a slide:**

> أما القرآن فمن عند الله، إنما نهيتكم عن الاختلاف فاقرؤوا على أي حرف شئتم
> — ⁨سیر أعلام النبلاء جراشدون ص۱۸۴⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/10906/1326
> *English:* "As for the Qurʾān — it is from God. I only forbade you to differ; so recite upon whichever ḥarf you wish."

**And the end of that meeting** (⁨سیر ص۱۸۴⁩):

> فرضي الناس واصطلحوا ودخلوا المدينة.
> — ⁨سیر أعلام النبلاء جراشدون ص۱۸۴⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/10906/1326
> *English:* "So the people were satisfied and made peace, and entered Medina."

**A second, fuller assembly is recorded at ⁨سیر ص۱۹۰⁩–۱۹۱**, on ⁨سیف⁩'s authority — ⁨سیر⁩'s own attribution. There
⁨عثمان ؓ⁩ answers point by point before the Companions, and after each answer asks «⁨أكذاك؟⁩» and is answered
«⁨نعم⁩». The four points answered there are: the completing of the prayer in travel; the ⁨حِمى⁩ (§14); the
recall of ⁨الحكم⁩, to which he answers that the Prophet ﷺ exiled him and the Prophet ﷺ brought him back; the
appointing of young men, to which he answers that he appointed none but a man mature and approved, and
cites the appointment of ⁨أسامة ؓ⁩; and the award to ⁨ابن أبي سرح⁩ (§17). **The «⁨أكذاك؟ قالوا⁩: ⁨نعم⁩» refrain is
the striking thing and belongs to ⁨سیر⁩ alone at the pages read.**

**Of the five, two are outside what this note handles** — the employment of relatives and the sharp words
to Companions — and they are listed in §23.

## 21. The "first to do X" notices this window actually carries

Five, and no more. **Anything else offered as a "first of ⁨عثمان ؓ⁩" is not on these pages.**

1. **The first tent pitched at ⁨مِنى⁩** — «⁨فكان أول فسطاط ضربه عثمان بمنى⁩» `[SOURCED]` — ⁨البدایہ ج۷ ص۲۹۲⁩.
   ⁨سیر ص۱۷۷⁩ and ⁨الکامل⁩'s parallel record the tent without the word "first".
2. **The third call on ⁨الجمعة⁩, on ⁨الزوراء⁩** `[SOURCED]` — ⁨البدایہ ج۷ ص۲۹۴⁩; ⁨الکامل ج۲ ص۴۸۵⁩. Neither book
   uses the word "first"; both say «⁨زاد⁩» — he added. **Say "added", not "invented".**
3. **The first disagreement between the people of Kufa and the people of Syria**, when ⁨سلمان بن ربيعة⁩ and
   ⁨حبيب بن مسلمة⁩ each claimed the command at ⁨الباب⁩ `[SOURCED]` — ⁨البدایہ ج۷ ص۳۰۰⁩ («⁨فكان أولَ اختلافٍ وقعَ⁩
   بين أهل الكوفة وأهل الشام»); الکامل ج۲ ص۵۰۵ («فَكَانَ ذَلِكَ أَوَّلَ اخْتِلَافٍ وَقَعَ بَيْنَ أَهْلِ
   ⁨الْكُوفَةِ وَالشَّامِ⁩»). Both books quote the same verses of ⁨أوس بن مَغْراء⁩ on it. ⚠ **⁨الکامل⁩ explains the
   cause differently from ⁨البدایہ⁩** — ⁨البدایہ⁩ says the two men disputed the command; ⁨الکامل⁩ says ⁨حبيب⁩ wanted
   to command the man holding ⁨الباب⁩ as an incoming army commander would. `[SOURCED, disputed]`
4. **The word ⁨جوائز⁩ is dated to ⁨قَطَن بن عوف⁩'s crossing of the wadi** `[SOURCED]` — ⁨البدایہ ج۷ ص۳۸۳⁩ (§15).
5. **The first campaign into ⁨طبرستان⁩ — and this one is disputed inside the safe list.** ⁨البدایہ ج۷ ص۲۹۲⁩, on
   ⁨الواقدي⁩, ⁨أبو معشر⁩ and ⁨المدائني⁩, says ⁨سعيد بن العاص⁩ opened it and «⁨هو أول من غزاها⁩», then immediately
   records that **⁨سیف⁩ claimed the people had already made terms with ⁨سويد بن مُقَرِّن⁩ under ⁨عمر ؓ⁩ on payment
   of money, so that it was not to be raided** — and ⁨ابن کثیر⁩ leaves it open with «⁨فالله أعلم⁩».
   ⁨الکامل ج۲ ص۴۸۰⁩ states the "first" flatly — «⁨فَإِنَّهَا لَمْ يَغْزُهَا أَحَدٌ إِلَى هَذِهِ السَّنَةِ⁩» —
   and also refers back to the disagreement. `[SOURCED, disputed]`

**Two more "firsts" sit on these pages but are not ⁨عثمان ؓ⁩'s** and are recorded so they are not mistaken for
his: ⁨قتيبة بن مسلم⁩ was the first to shift the ⁨خراسان⁩ road to ⁨قومس⁩ (⁨الکامل ج۲ ص۴۸۱⁩), and ⁨الواقدي⁩ is quoted
saying ⁨سنان بن أبي سنان ؓ⁩ was the first to give the pledge under the tree (⁨سیر ص۱۸۱⁩).

## What could NOT be established

- **Why the third call on ⁨الجمعة⁩ was added.** Neither ⁨البدایہ ج۷ ص۲۹۴⁩ nor ⁨الکامل ج۲ ص۴۸۵⁩ gives a reason —
  no growth of the city, no distance from the market, nothing. The familiar explanation is **outside these
  pages** `[STANDARD]` *(to verify)*. A slide must not supply it.
- **Who first called the copies «⁨المصاحف الأئمة⁩», and how many were made in total.** ⁨البدایہ ج۷ ص۳۸۴⁩ names
  seven destinations; ⁨سیر⁩ and ⁨الکامل⁩ give none. Whether seven is the total, or ⁨البدایہ⁩'s enumeration of the
  principal ones, is not settled on these pages.
- **What the burned copies contained.** All three books say copies that differed were burned; **none of the
  three gives a single example of a difference.** Any statement about what was in ⁨ابن مسعود ؓ⁩'s ⁨مصحف⁩ is
  outside this note.
- **The rest of ⁨الزهري⁩'s sentence at ⁨سیر ص۱۸۵⁩.** The printed page breaks at «⁨واستعمل⁩». The continuation is
  on ⁨سیر ص۱۸۶⁩, which was fetched — and ⁨ص۱۸۶⁩ opens with ⁨الذهبي⁩'s own «⁨قلت⁩:», not with ⁨الزهري⁩'s continuation,
  so **the sentence is not completed anywhere in the pages read.** Do not complete it.
- **The exact year of the enlargement of ⁨المسجد الحرام⁩ beyond ⁨سیر⁩'s filing under 26 AH.** ⁨البدایہ⁩ and
  ⁨الکامل⁩ do not carry the notice at the pages read, so there is nothing to corroborate it with.
- **The ⁨حِمى⁩'s location and extent.** Both answers preserved (§14) are about purpose and precedent; **no
  page read names where it was or how large.**
- **Whether the ⁨عطاء⁩ was increased across the board.** ⁨البدایہ ج۷ ص۳۷۹⁩–۳۸۰ shows generous and regular
  payment and one household's rate; ⁨سیر ص۱۷۹⁩ shows treasuries and «⁨أدر الأرزاق⁩». **None of the pages read
  states a general increase over ⁨عمر ؓ⁩'s scale, and no comparative figure exists in them.** A slide saying

- **The full text of ⁨عثمان ؓ⁩'s letter about the ⁨مصحف⁩ to the provinces.** Only the instruction to the

- **The size of the ⁨المسجد النبوي⁩ enlargement relative to ⁨عمر ؓ⁩'s mosque.** ⁨البدایہ ج۷ ص۲۹۲⁩ gives the new
  dimensions but no earlier figure to compare against, and none of the pages read supplies one.
- **Whether ⁨عثمان ؓ⁩ prayed four at ⁨مِنى⁩ in more than one year.** ⁨البدایہ ج۷ ص۲۹۲⁩ ties it to «⁨عامه هذا⁩»;
  ⁨سیر ص۱۷۷⁩ adds that he completed at ⁨عرفة⁩ too. No page read says how many years it continued.

## Not for the hall

Everything below is on the pages read and is real. **None of it is to be narrated from the platform**, and
the reason is given for each so that nobody re-derives it and puts it on a slide by accident.

1. **⁨البدایہ ج۷ ص۲۹۷⁩ — the two named men who "showed the fault of ʿUthmān" during ⁨غزوة الصواري⁩**, on
   ⁨الواقدي⁩'s authority through ⁨الزهري⁩, including the claim that his blood was lawful. **This is an
   accusation against a Companion, narrated with names. It is not told, not paraphrased, and not
   alluded to.** If a slip asks, the answer is §18.2's principle: the books record, and we do not
   adjudicate between Companions.
2. **⁨البدایہ ج۷ ص۲۹۳⁩ and ⁨الکامل ج۲ ص۴۷۷⁩–۴۷۸ — the drinking charge against ⁨الوليد بن عقبة ؓ⁩.** The ḥadd was
   carried out and the books record it; the course does not narrate a ḥadd upon a Companion from the
   platform. **What may be used from those pages is only §19's first paragraph (five years, no door),
   the ⁨قسامة⁩ precedent, and ⁨علی ؓ⁩'s «⁨وَكُلٌّ سُنَّةٌ⁩».**
3. **The lurid version of ⁨أبو ذر ؓ⁩'s removal — the reviling, the death-threat, the ride without a
   saddle-pad.** ⁨الکامل ج۲ ص۴۸۳⁩ says «⁨لَا يَصِحُّ النَّقْلُ بِهِ⁩». **Recorded, never narrated;** if pressed,
   quote ⁨ابن الأثیر⁩'s refusal (§18.2), and stop there.
4. **⁨الکامل ج۲ ص۴۸۳⁩–۴۸۴ and ⁨سیر ص۱۸۷⁩ — ⁨ابن السوداء⁩.** Both books carry a figure who moves between the
   provinces stirring the discontent; ⁨سیر⁩'s account is on ⁨سیف⁩'s authority. **It is not narrated**: it names
   an agent for the fitna, and the room would take it as a settled explanation of what followed. Recorded
   so it is never re-researched.
5. **⁨سیر ص۱۸۸⁩ — the exchange between ⁨عمار بن ياسر ؓ⁩ and ⁨سعد بن أبي وقاص ؓ⁩**, in which one declares he has
   withdrawn from ⁨عثمان ؓ⁩ as he pulls off his turban, and the other answers as he answers. **Two Companions in
   direct conflict, with words that cannot be softened. Never from the platform.**
6. **⁨سیر ص۱۹۶⁩ — «⁨نعثل⁩».** The insult thrown at ⁨عثمان ؓ⁩ from the floor of his own mosque, with the
   lexicographical gloss ⁨الذهبي⁩ gives it. **Never spoken aloud in the hall**, in any language.
7. **⁨سیر ص۱۹۴⁩–۱۹۵ — the letter found with the courier at ⁨البويب⁩**, ordering punishment of the delegation,
   and ⁨عثمان ؓ⁩'s denial of it under oath: «⁨مَا كتبت ولا علمت، وقد يكتب الكتاب على لسان الرجل وينقش الخاتم⁩
   ⁨على الخاتم⁩». **This belongs to the siege strand, not to this one**, and it is left to the agent who owns
   that material; it is flagged here only because the seal and the ring both appear in this note and must
   not be confused.
8. **⁨البدایہ ج۷ ص۳۸۶⁩ — ⁨ابن کثیر⁩'s causal reading** that permitting the senior Companions to travel produced
   followings, and followings produced candidates, and candidates hastened his death. **If it is used at
   all it must be attributed to ⁨ابن کثیر⁩ by name as his reading of the event** (§16.4), and it is simpler
   not to use it. The facts before it — that ⁨عمر ؓ⁩ restrained them and ⁨عثمان ؓ⁩ did not — are safe on their
   own.
9. **⁨البدایہ ج۷ ص۳۸۴⁩ — ⁨أبو هريرة ؓ⁩'s «⁨أصَبْتَ ووُفِّقْتَ⁩» report.** A splendid line, and **the printed
   edition's own footnote grades the ḥadīth in it «⁨ضعيف جداً⁩»** (§8.4). **Not for a slide.** If the sentiment
   is wanted, ⁨علی ؓ⁩'s «⁨لو لم يصنعْه هو لصنعته⁩» (§11) carries it and is far better attested.
10. **⁨سیر ص۱۸۶⁩ — ⁨عثمان ؓ⁩'s reported words about ⁨بني أمية⁩ and the keys of Paradise.** Recorded; **not
    narrated**, for obvious reasons, and note that the narration as printed does not attribute the sentence
    to the Prophet ﷺ.

## EVENT CARDS

### E-MS1 · The first morning on the pulpit
**Tier:** CORE · **When:** 24 AH `[SOURCED]` · **Map:** ⁨المدينة⁩ lights. No movement.

**What happened:** When the pledge had been given, the new caliph came out to the people and climbed the
pulpit of the Prophet ﷺ. He praised God, and then said something no one expected from a man who had just
been given the command of the Muslims. He said that the first mounting of any beast is hard; that there
were days ahead; that if he lived, the address would come to them as it should. And he ended by saying
plainly that he had never been an orator, and that God would teach him.

**The statement:**
> أيها الناس أولُ كلِّ مركبٍ صعبٌ، وإنَّ بعد اليوم أيامًا، وإن أعشْ تأتِكُم الخطبة على وجهها، وما كنا خطباء وسيُعلمُنا الله.
> — ⁨البدایہ والنہایہ ج۷ ص۳۸۲⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/30097/3528
> *English:* "O people, the first mounting of any beast is hard. After today there are days to come, and if I live the address will reach you as it should. We were never orators — and God will teach us."

**⁨عبرت⁩:** A man who admits at the start that he is not yet equal to the office has told the truth about
every office.

**Hands-up?** no

### E-MS2 · The first case laid before the new caliph
**Tier:** GOOD · **When:** 24 AH `[SOURCED]` · **Map:** No map.

**What happened:** Before the new caliph had done anything else, a prisoner was waiting. The son of ⁨عمر ؓ⁩
had killed three people in ⁨المدينة⁩ in the days after his father was struck, and ⁨سعد ؓ⁩ had taken the sword
out of his hand and held him. ⁨عثمان ؓ⁩ called a group of the ⁨مهاجرون⁩ and asked them what he should do. ⁨علی ؓ⁩
told him he should put the man to death; another said his father had been killed only yesterday, was the
son to be killed today; ⁨عمرو بن العاص ؓ⁩ said God had spared him the deed. The caliph decided that as the
only next of kin of the slain man he would pardon, and pay the blood-money himself.

**The statement:**
> فلما استخلف عثمان قال له علي: أقد عبيد الله من الهرمزان، فقال عثمان: ما له ولي غيري، وإني قد عفوت ولكن أديه.
> — ⁨سیر أعلام النبلاء جراشدون ص۱۶۷⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/10906/1309
> *English:* "When ʿUthmān was made caliph, ʿAlī said to him: exact retaliation from ʿUbayd Allāh for al-Hurmuzān. ʿUthmān said: he has no next of kin but me, and I have pardoned — but I shall pay his blood-money."

**⁨عبرت⁩:** Men of the highest rank read the same law and reached different conclusions, and the office had
to choose one of them; that is what an office is for.

**Hands-up?** no

---

### E-MS3 · How long the quiet lasted — three men, three answers
**Tier:** CORE · **When:** 24–35 AH `[SOURCED]` · **Map:** The Line: the twelve years marked as one block.

**What happened:** Three separate authorities in our books are asked, in effect, the same question: how
long was this reign untroubled. ⁨الحسن البصري⁩, who lived through it, says twelve years, and that they found
nothing in his rule to object to. ⁨الزهري⁩ says six years with nothing held against him, and adds that he
was dearer to the people than ⁨عمر ؓ⁩ had been, because ⁨عمر ؓ⁩ was hard on them and this man was gentle. ⁨قتادة⁩
and ⁨خليفة بن خياط⁩, counting the term itself, make it twelve years less twelve days. The three are not
contradicting each other; they are answering three different questions, and that is worth saying out loud.

**The statement:**
> وقال أبو هلال: سمعت الحسن يقول: عمل عثمان اثنتي عشرة سنة، ما ينكرون من إمارته شيئا.
> — ⁨سیر أعلام النبلاء جراشدون ص۱۵۸⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/10906/1300
> *English:* "Abū Hilāl said: I heard al-Ḥasan say — ʿUthmān governed twelve years, and they found nothing in his rule to object to."

**⁨عبرت⁩:** The years that are remembered as trouble were a small part of the years that were lived.

**Hands-up?** Twelve years is longer than the whole caliphate of ⁨أبو بكر ؓ⁩ and ⁨عمر ؓ⁩ put together — hands up
who would have guessed that.

### E-MS4 · The caliph asleep in the half-built mosque
**Tier:** GOOD · **When:** during the rebuilding of ⁨المسجد النبوي⁩ `[SOURCED]` · **Map:** ⁨المدينة⁩.

**What happened:** A boy went into the mosque at midday with a bird he meant to let loose, while the
building work was going on. He found an old man asleep on the ground with a brick, or part of a brick,
under his head, and stood looking at him because he had never seen a face like it. The man woke and asked
who he was, and then called a servant, who came back with a robe and a thousand dirhams. The boy was
dressed in the robe with the money wrapped in it and sent home. He told his father he did not know who it
was, only that it was a man sleeping in the mosque; and his father told him it was the Commander of the
Faithful.

**The statement:**
> فإذا شيخ جميل حسن الوجه نائم، تحت رأسه لبنة أو بعض لَبنة، فقمت أنظر إليه أتعجب من جماله
> — ⁨البدایہ والنہایہ ج۷ ص۳۸۰⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/30097/3526
> *English:* "And there was an old man, handsome, of fine face, asleep, with a brick or part of a brick under his head; so I stood looking at him, marvelling at his beauty."

**⁨عبرت⁩:** Rank was not something he carried about with him; a child could stand over him and not know what
he was looking at.

**Hands-up?** no

### E-MS5 · What was cut into his seal
**Tier:** GOOD · **When:** his caliphate `[SOURCED]` · **Map:** No map.

**What happened:** Rulers of that age sealed letters with a signet, and the words cut into the stone were
a kind of public statement. Two of our books record what was on his. It was not a name and not a title. It
was a sentence of the Qurʾān about God, who created and then shaped what He created. One other report
gives it as a declaration of belief in God the Mighty. Either way, what the chancery of the Muslim state
stamped on its letters for twelve years was a line about the Creator.

**The statement:**
> كان نقش خاتم عثمان آمنت بالذي خلق فسوى.
> — ⁨البدایہ والنہایہ ج۷ ص۳۷۹⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/30097/3525
> *English:* "The inscription of ʿUthmān's ring was: I have believed in the One who created and then shaped."

**⁨عبرت⁩:** A state says what it believes in the smallest places first.

**Hands-up?** no

### E-MS6 · Buying the ground around the Kaʿbah
**Tier:** CORE · **When:** 26 AH `[SOURCED]` · **Map:** ⁨مكة⁩ lights.

**What happened:** The Sacred Mosque had become too small for the numbers coming, so he widened it. He
bought the ground from the owners. Some sold and some refused; the houses of those who refused were pulled
down anyway and the price of each was put into the public treasury for them. They shouted at him in
public, and he had them put in prison. What he said to them is the part worth keeping: nothing had made
them bold against him but his own mildness, and ⁨عمر ؓ⁩ had done exactly this to them and they had not
shouted at him. Then people interceded, and he let them out.

**The statement:**
> وقال: ما جرأكم علي إلا حلمي، وقد فعل هذا بكم عمر فلم تصيحوا عليه، ثم كلموه فيهم فأطلقهم.
> — ⁨سیر أعلام النبلاء جراشدون ص۱۶۹⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/10906/1311
> *English:* "And he said: nothing made you bold against me but my forbearance — ʿUmar did this very thing to you and you did not cry out at him. Then people spoke to him on their behalf, and he released them."

**⁨عبرت⁩:** The same act done by a hard man and a mild man is not received the same way, and the mild man
pays for it.

**Hands-up?** no

### E-MS7 · The sea that ⁨عمر ؓ⁩ would not let a Muslim cross
**Tier:** CORE · **When:** the reign of ⁨عمر ؓ⁩, and the reversal under ⁨عثمان ؓ⁩ `[SOURCED]` · **Map:** The
Mediterranean coast of ⁨الشام⁩, and the sea beyond it.

**What happened:** ⁨معاوية ؓ⁩ had pressed ⁨عمر ؓ⁩ for years for permission to fight at sea, because the Romans
were close. ⁨عمر ؓ⁩ answered that a single village of ⁨حمص⁩ whose dogs and cocks he could hear was dearer to
him than everything in that sea. Then he wrote to ⁨عمرو بن العاص ؓ⁩ and asked him to describe the sea and the
man who rides it. The answer he got back is one of the strangest letters in our books, and when ⁨عمر ؓ⁩ had
read it he wrote one line back to ⁨معاوية ؓ⁩. That line held until he died. Under the next caliph, the ban
was lifted and the Muslims put to sea.

**The statement:**
> فكتب إليه: إني رأيت خلقا كبيرا يركبه خلق صغير، إن ركد حرق القلوب، وإن تحرك أراع العقول، يزداد فيه اليقين قلة، والشك كثرة، وهم فيه كدود على عود، إن مال غرق، وإن نجا برق.
> — ⁨سیر أعلام النبلاء جراشدون ص۱۷۴⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/10906/1316
> *English:* "He wrote to him: I have seen a great creature ridden by a small creature. If it is still it burns the hearts; if it moves it terrifies the minds. Certainty in it grows less and doubt grows more. They upon it are like worms on a stick: if it tips, they drown; if it escapes, they are dazed."

**⁨عبرت⁩:** One caliph's caution and the next caliph's boldness were both judgements, not principles, and the
books record both without deciding between them.

**Hands-up?** Hands up if you think a state that has just conquered two empires would be afraid of a boat.

### E-MS8 · Cyprus, and the woman who was promised the sea
**Tier:** GOOD · **When:** 27 AH `[SOURCED]` · **Map:** ⁨قبرص⁩ lights, first island beyond the coast.

**What happened:** With the ban lifted, ⁨معاوية ؓ⁩ raided Cyprus, and he went by ship with armies. ⁨عبادة بن⁩
⁨الصامت ؓ⁩ went with him, and so did his wife ⁨أم حرام بنت ملحان ؓ⁩, the aunt of ⁨أنس ؓ⁩, whom the Prophet ﷺ used
to visit and to whom he had given the tidings that she would be among those who rode that sea. On the
island she was thrown from her mule and died. Our book says the people of the place still point to the
grave and call it the grave of the righteous woman.

**The statement:**
> فصرعت عن بغلتها فماتت شهيدة -رحمها الله، وكان النبي صلى الله عليه وسلم يغشاها ويقيل عندها، وبشرها بالشهادة، فقبرها بقبرس يقولون: هذا قبر المرأة الصالحة.

### E-MS14 · A historian weighs four explanations and throws one away
**Tier:** CORE · **When:** the eighth century of the Hijrah, looking back at 29 AH `[SOURCED]` ·
**Map:** No map.

**What happened:** Centuries later, ⁨ابن کثیر⁩ sat down to write this same incident and found four different
explanations in circulation for why the caliph prayed four. He set them all out. The first was fear that
the bedouin would conclude the obligation itself was two rakʿahs. The second was that he had married at
⁨مكة⁩. The third came with a ḥadīth attached, and of that ḥadīth he wrote four words: it is not sound. The
fourth was that he judged himself Commander of the Faithful wherever he was — and of that he said there is
something in it to consider, because the Messenger of God ﷺ was the Messenger of God wherever he was, and
he did not pray four on a journey. Having done all that, he passed no judgement on the man at all.

**The statement:**
> وهذا الحديث لا يصح، وقد تزوَّجَ رسول الله ﷺ في عمرة القضاء بميمونة بنت الحارث ولم يتمّ الصلاة
> — ⁨البدایہ والنہایہ ج۷ ص۳۸۶⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/30097/3532
> *English:* "But this ḥadīth is not sound; and the Messenger of God ﷺ married Maymūnah bint al-Ḥārith during the ʿUmrah of Fulfilment and did not complete the prayer."

**⁨عبرت⁩:** Weighing the reports and declining to condemn the man are not two different attitudes; in this
tradition they are the same one.

### E-MS15 · The soldier who rode to ⁨المدينة⁩
**Tier:** CORE · **When:** 25–30 AH, on campaign toward ⁨أرمينية⁩ `[SOURCED]` · **Map:** The eastern front,

### E-MS18 · The leaves in the keeping of ⁨حفصة ؓ⁩

### E-MS21 · Not one of them was in his handwriting
**Tier:** CORE · **When:** 30 AH `[SOURCED]` · **Map:** No map.

### E-MS43 · The heavy bag at ⁨الربذة⁩
**Tier:** GOOD · **When:** after 30 AH `[SOURCED]` · **Map:** ⁨الربذة⁩, east of ⁨المدينة⁩.

**What happened:** When his family came out to join him at ⁨الربذة⁩, they came with a leather bag heavy

### E-MS44 · The Companion who would not step forward
**Tier:** GOOD · **When:** after 30 AH `[SOURCED]` · **Map:** ⁨الربذة⁩.

**What happened:** The prayer was called at ⁨الربذة⁩, and the man in charge of the ṣadaqah there — a slave,

### E-MS45 · A historian refuses to repeat a story
**Tier:** CORE · **When:** the seventh century of the Hijrah, looking back at 30 AH `[SOURCED]` ·
**Map:** No map.

**What happened:** There were ugly versions of how ⁨أبو ذر ؓ⁩ came to leave ⁨الشام⁩, and they were in
circulation long before ⁨ابن الأثیر⁩ wrote. He lists them: that ⁨معاوية ؓ⁩ reviled him, that he threatened him
with death, that he was carried from ⁨الشام⁩ to ⁨المدينة⁩ on a bare mount without a saddle-pad, that he was
banished from ⁨المدينة⁩ in a disgraceful manner. Then, having listed them, he writes six words that a
speaker can stand on. And he adds a second thought: that even if it had been sound, it would have needed
to be excused, because disciplining subjects belongs to the office.

**The statement:**
> وَنَفْيِهِ مِنَ الْمَدِينَةِ عَلَى الْوَجْهِ الشَّنِيعِ، لَا يَصِحُّ النَّقْلُ بِهِ، وَلَوْ صَحَّ لَكَانَ يَنْبَغِي أَنْ يُعْتَذَرَ عَنْ عُثْمَانَ، فَإِنَّ لِلْإِمَامِ أَنْ يُؤَدِّبَ رَعِيَّتَهُ
> — ⁨الکامل فی التاریخ ج۲ ص۴۸۳⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/21712/1173
> *English:* "…and his banishment from Medina in the disgraceful manner — the transmission of it is not sound; and had it been sound, it would still have been fitting to excuse ʿUthmān, for it belongs to the imām to discipline his subjects."

**⁨عبرت⁩:** A historian who tells you which reports he will not carry has told you something about all the
ones he does.

### E-MS46 · "And each is sunnah"
**Tier:** CORE · **When:** around 30 AH `[SOURCED]` · **Map:** ⁨المدينة⁩.

**What happened:** A punishment had to be carried out in ⁨المدينة⁩, and there was a question about the number
of lashes, because two caliphs had used two different numbers. ⁨علی ؓ⁩ settled it on the spot, in one breath.
He said the Messenger of God ﷺ and ⁨أبو بكر ؓ⁩ gave forty, and that ⁨عثمان ؓ⁩ gave eighty. And then he said the
thing that makes this card worth the evening: each of them is sunnah — and this one is dearer to me. He did
not say the other was wrong. He said it was sunnah, and that he preferred the other.

**The statement:**
> جَلَدَ رَسُولُ اللَّهِ - صَلَّى اللَّهُ عَلَيْهِ وَسَلَّمَ -، وَأَبُو بَكْرٍ أَرْبَعِينَ وَجَلَدَ عُثْمَانُ ثَمَانِينَ وَكُلٌّ سُنَّةٌ وَهَذَا أَحَبُّ إِلَيَّ.
> — ⁨الکامل فی التاریخ ج۲ ص۴۷۸⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/21712/1168
> *English:* "The Messenger of God ﷺ flogged, and Abū Bakr, forty; and ʿUthmān flogged eighty; and each is sunnah, and this is the dearer to me."

**⁨عبرت⁩:** He had the words for a difference between two great men that did not require either of them to
be in the wrong, and those words are still available.

**Hands-up?** no

### E-MS47 · Changing a rule of evidence to stop the killing
**Tier:** GOOD · **When:** around 30 AH `[SOURCED]` · **Map:** ⁨الكوفة⁩.

**What happened:** A gang of young men in ⁨الكوفة⁩ broke into a house at night, and when the householder came
out at them with a sword and shouted, they killed him. A Companion and his son had seen it and testified.
The governor wrote to ⁨المدينة⁩; the caliph wrote back ordering the killers put to death, and they were
executed at the palace gate. And out of that case a rule was adjusted: in the procedure of ⁨قسامة⁩ he took
the word of the slain man's next of kin, before an assembly of the people — and our book states the purpose
of the change in five words.

**The statement:**
> وَلِهَذَا السَّبَبِ أَخَذَ فِي الْقَسَامَةِ بِقَوْلِ وَلِيِّ الْمَقْتُولِ عَنْ مَلَإٍ مِنَ النَّاسِ لِيَفْطِمَ النَّاسَ عَنِ الْقَتْلِ.
> — ⁨الکامل فی التاریخ ج۲ ص۴۷۷⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/21712/1167
> *English:* "And for this reason he took, in the qasāmah, the word of the slain man's next of kin before an assembly of the people — in order to wean the people away from killing."

**⁨عبرت⁩:** Law is not only what is right in the case in front of you; it is also what the next man will
believe he can get away with.

**Hands-up?** no

### E-MS48 · A governor whose house had no door

### E-MS49 · Five things laid before him

### E-MS52 · The pasture, and who it was for
**Tier:** GOOD · **When:** 35 AH `[SOURCED]` · **Map:** No map.

**What happened:** One of the five charges was the ⁨حِمى⁩ — the pasture closed off from general grazing.
Closing pasture was an old practice and there was nothing new in the fact of it; the charge was that he had
closed it for himself. His answer to that, in the exchange carried through ⁨علی ؓ⁩, is one sentence with an
oath in the middle of it, and it is about purpose: not for my camels, not for my sheep, but for the camels
of the ṣadaqah — the animals held by the state on behalf of the poor.

**The statement:**
> وأما الحمى فوالله ما حميته لإبلي ولا لغنمي، وإنما حميته لإبل الصدقة.
> — ⁨سیر أعلام النبلاء جراشدون ص۱۸۴⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/10906/1326
> *English:* "As for the protected pasture — by God, I did not protect it for my camels nor for my sheep; I protected it only for the camels of the ṣadaqah."

**⁨عبرت⁩:** The same act done for oneself and done for the treasury looks identical from the road, and that is
why it was believed.

### E-MS53 · Two camels for the Ḥajj

**What happened:** At the assembly in ⁨المدينة⁩ he answered the same charge about the pasture a second time,

### E-MS55 · The first quarrel between ⁨الكوفة⁩ and ⁨الشام⁩

### E-MS56 · "Hold fast together; do not become parties"
**Tier:** CORE · **When:** late in the caliphate `[SOURCED]` · **Map:** ⁨المدينة⁩, the mosque — and the
closing map of the session.

**What happened:** Our book records what it calls the last address he delivered to an assembly. He told them
that God gave them this world to seek the next with, not to lean upon; that this one passes and that one
remains; that they should prefer what stays to what goes. Then he told them to fear God, because fear of Him
is a shield. And he ended with an instruction and a verse, and the instruction is the one that the years
after him make hard to hear.

**The statement:**
> واحذروا من اللّه الغِيَرَ، والزموا جماعتكم لا تصيروا أحزابًا

> *English:* "Beware of God's reversals, and hold fast to your community together; do not become parties."

**⁨عبرت⁩:** The last thing he asked of them in public was the one thing they did not give him.

**Hands-up?** no
