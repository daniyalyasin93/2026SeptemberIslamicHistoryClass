# The مشاجرات — the اصول for narrating a fitna from the platform

**Question this note answers:** Session 3 carries the fuller treatment of the guardrail, and it has to
carry it with pages, not with assurances. What is تاریخ as against حدیث and فقہ, such that a report in a
history book is neither a ruling nor a creed? Where, inside the fitna chapters themselves, do
**ابن كثير رحمہ اللہ** and **الذهبي رحمہ اللہ** refuse a report, call it a forgery, grade a chain, or say
plainly that the thing is not known? What does **السخاوي رحمہ اللہ** say in الإعلان بالتوبيخ about the
uses of history, about liars, and about the brake a historian must keep his foot on? And out of all of
that, what are the **safe forms of words** — sentences a speaker can say aloud in a Tanzeem-e-Islami hall
that narrate the fitna, attribute every judgement to the authority who made it, and adjudicate nothing?

## 0. The sentences this note licenses

Each of these may now go on a slide or into a script, with the page given.

1. **العيني رحمہ اللہ, quoted by السخاوي, calls تاریخ a branch of the science of ḥadīth:
   «عِلْمُ التَّارِيخُ فَنٌّ مِنْ فُنُونِ الْحَدِيثِ النَّبَوِيِّ»** `[SOURCED]` — الإعلان ص١٧٢. This is the
   cleanest single sentence for the حدیث/تاریخ block, and it comes from inside our own safe list.
2. **السخاوي's own brake on the discipline: history must be written and transmitted, but
   «مَعَ الِاحْتِرَازِ عَنِ الْمُجَازَفَةِ وَالرَّجْمِ بِالْغَيْبِ» — guarding against reckless assertion and
   guessing at the unseen** `[SOURCED]` — الإعلان ص١٥٩. **This is the guardrail sentence of the evening.**
3. **The same chapter states the rule of selection: the good in the forbears' traces is followed, and what
   sound minds recoil from among their reports is not listened to** `[SOURCED]` — الإعلان ص١٧٣.
4. **الحافظ ابن حجر رحمہ اللہ, quoted by السخاوي, demolishes a story told about الشافعي رحمہ اللہ with
   nothing but dates, and says so in as many words: anyone with the least knowledge of تاریخ knows it is a
   lie** `[SOURCED]` — الإعلان ص١٠٢–١٠٣. Note also that المعافى, who transmitted it, had already written
   «اللهُ أَعْلَمُ بِصِحَّتِهَا» beside it — الإعلان ص١٠٢.
5. **One of the reasons السخاوي gives for dating by years is precisely so that the ummah knows when the
   period of the خلفاء راشدون ended, and when the بدع first appeared** `[SOURCED]` — الإعلان ص١٦٨. This is
   the source-sentence that makes session 3's endpoint (40ھ) a subject of knowledge rather than opinion.
6. **ابن كثير رحمہ اللہ declares forged, on his own authority, the report that the Prophet ﷺ commanded
   fighting «الناكثين والقاسطين والمارقين» with علي ؓ: «هذا السياق الظاهر أنه موضوع»** `[SOURCED]` —
   البدایہ ج۷ ص۵۱۶. **This is the single most valuable line in the note** — the classification of the three
   parties as "the treaty-breakers, the deviators and the renegades" is the standard partisan frame for
   the whole fitna, and our own source rejects the report it rests on.
7. **ابن كثير refuses the report that بنو ناجية apostatised, on the grounds that it is the Shīʿa's own
   claim and that no Arab tribe is known to have apostatised after the ردة of الصدیق ؓ — and the refusal
   is الهيثم بن عدي's, quoted by ابن كثير** `[SOURCED]` — البدایہ ج۷ ص۵۲۰.
8. **ابن كثير refuses a report about تفضیل transmitted through معمر, saying «وهذا الكلام فيه تخبيط كثير»
   — there is a great deal of confusion in it** `[SOURCED]` — البدایہ ج۷ ص۶۰۹.
9. **ابن كثير grades as he goes inside the fitna chapters: أبو موسى الأشعري ؓ's saying about the killing
   of عثمان ؓ is given, and then «وهذا منقطع» — the chain is broken** `[SOURCED]` — البدایہ ج۷ ص۳۴۸.
10. **ابن كثير states that علي ؓ's declaration from the منبر of الكوفة — that the best of this ummah after

    البدایہ ج۷ ص۶۱۱.
11. **علي ؓ's own account of how he came to the caliphate is recorded by الذهبي in his own words, and it

## 1. تاریخ is a branch of the science of ḥadīth — السخاوي's strongest statement of it

The earlier note (§4.1) set out the three-discipline contrast from the Urdu مقدمہ, where it could only be
paraphrased. Here is the same idea in Arabic, on a page we hold. السخاوي is quoting his own teacher
**البدر العيني رحمہ اللہ**:

> عِلْمُ التَّارِيخُ فَنٌّ مِنْ فُنُونِ الْحَدِيثِ النَّبَوِيِّ، وَزَيْنٌ تَقَرُّ بِهِ الْعُيُونُ، حَيْثُ سُلِكَ فِيهِ المَنْهَجُ الْقَوِيمُ الْمُسْتَوِي، بَلْ وَقْعُهُ مِنَ الدِّينِ عَظِيمٌ، وَنَفْعُهُ يَتَعَيَّنُ (٤) فِي الشَّرْعِ لِشُهْرَتِهِ، غَنَيٌّ عَنْ مَزِيدِ البَيَانِ وَالتَّفْهِيمِ.
> — الإعلان بالتوبيخ ص١٧٢ · https://shamela.ws/book/34/171
> *English:* "The science of history is one of the branches of the science of Prophetic ḥadīth, and an ornament at which eyes are gladdened — **where the straight and level method has been kept to in it.** Indeed its bearing on the religion is great, and its usefulness in the Sharīʿa is settled by its very fame, needing no further explaining."

⚠ The `(٤)` inside the quotation is the printed edition's footnote marker. Remove it by eye from a page
image before the sentence is set on a slide.

**Read the conditional clause, because the conditional clause is the guardrail.** It is a branch of ḥadīth
science **حيث سُلك فيه المنهج القويم** — where the straight method has been kept to in it. Not otherwise.
That single clause is the whole reason this course reads البدایہ and سیر to the room rather than handing
the room a history.

The same passage, one page on, states the rule of selection that follows from it:

> حَيْثُ تُتَبَّعُ الأُمُورُ الْحَسَنَةُ مِنْ آثَارِهِمْ، وَلَا يَسْمَعُ مِنْهُمْ فِيمَا تَنْفِرُ عَنْهُ الْعُقُوُل المُسْتَحْسِنَةُ مِنْ أَخْبَارِهِمْ.
> — الإعلان بالتوبيخ ص١٧٣ · https://shamela.ws/book/34/172
> *English:* "…in that the good matters among their traces are followed, and there is not listened to, of their reports, that from which sound minds recoil."

⚠ `الْعُقُوُل` is printed with two ḍammas in the Shamela text — an evident typo for `الْعُقُولُ`. **Do not
silently correct it, and do not quote this line without reading it off a page image first.**

The immediately preceding clause of the same sentence names what the chapter is describing — the reports
of the **الخلفاء والملوك والأمراء**, and «مَا أَبْقَى الدَّهْرِ مِنْ فَضَائِلِهِمْ أَوْ رَذَائِلِهِمْ»
(الإعلان ص١٧٣). So the rule is stated **about rulers**, by an author inside our safe list, in the very
passage that concedes that histories carry their faults as well as their merits. That is the licence and
the limit together.

## 2. The discipline's own brake — «المجازفة والرجم بالغيب»

This is the single most useful sentence in all 77 pages of الإعلان read for this note, and it is the one
the speaker should have in his mouth when a slip comes out of the box asking him to settle something.

> فَإِذًا لَا غَنَاءَ عَنِ التَّارِيخِ، فَيَنْبَغِي أَنْ يُعْتَنَى بِشَأْنِهِ، وَيُكْتَبَ وَيُنْقَلَ مَعَ الِاحْتِرَازِ عَنِ الْمُجَازَفَةِ وَالرَّجْمِ بِالْغَيْبِ، بَلْ عَلَى حَسَبِ مَا تَقَدَّمَ.
> — الإعلان بالتوبيخ ص١٥٩ · https://shamela.ws/book/34/158
> *English:* "So then, there is no dispensing with history. It ought to be attended to, and written, and transmitted — **with a guarding against reckless assertion and against stoning at the unseen** — rather, according to what has gone before."

**«الرجم بالغيب»** is the idiom for firing blindly at what one cannot see. السخاوي puts it in the same
breath as **المجازفة**, over-claiming. Between them they name exactly the failure that turns a fitna
narrative into a partisan one: filling the gap between two reports with a motive.

The context matters, because this is not an academic warning tucked into a preface. The whole of ص١٥٩ is
an argument that kings, viziers and commanders need history; the brake is applied at the very end of that
argument, as the price of the licence.

## 3. Chronology destroys a story about الشافعي رحمہ اللہ — السخاوي's second worked forgery

The earlier note's §6 has the forged Khaybar document. On the next leaves السخاوي gives a **second** case,
and it is better for a hall, because what is destroyed is a piece of gossip about a famous imam rather
than a legal instrument. **المعافى بن زكريا النهرواني** had transmitted, through
**معمر بن شبيب بن شيبة**, that المأمون tested الشافعي رحمہ اللہ by making him drink نبيذ. Three things
happen on the page, in order, and the order is the lesson.

**First, the transmitter himself flagged it.** السخاوي reports المعافى's own remark on the spot:

> وَقَالَ المُعَافَى عَقِبهَا: اللهُ أَعْلَمُ بِصِحَّتِهَا!
> — الإعلان بالتوبيخ ص١٠٢ · https://shamela.ws/book/34/101
> *English:* "And المعافى said immediately after it: **Allah knows best whether it is sound!**"

**Second, السخاوي's own teacher settles it with dates.** «شَيْخُنَا» here is
**الحافظ ابن حجر رحمہ اللہ**, in لسان الميزان:

> "لَا يَخْفَى عَلَى مَنْ لَهُ أَدْنَى مَعْرِفَةٍ بِالتَّارِيخِ أَنَّهَا كَذِبٌ؛ وَذَلِكَ أَنَّ الشَّافِعِيَّ دَخَلَ مِصْرَ عَلَى رَأْسِ الْمِائَتَيْنِ، وَالْمَأْمُونُ إِذْ ذَاكَ بِخُرَاسَانَ، ثُمَّ
> — الإعلان بالتوبيخ ص١٠٢ · https://shamela.ws/book/34/101
> *English:* "It is not hidden from anyone who has the least acquaintance with chronology **that it is a lie** — and that is because الشافعي entered Egypt at the head of the year 200, while المأمون at that time was in خراسان, then…"

The sentence completes on the facing page:

> مَاتَ الشَّافِعِيُّ بِمِصْرَ سَنَةَ دَخَلَ الْمَأْمُونُ مِنْ خُرَاسَانَ إِلَى الْعِرَاقِ، وَهِيَ سَنَةُ أَرْبَعٍ وَمِائَتَيْنِ، فَمَا الْتَقَيَا قَطُّ وَالْمَأْمُونُ خَلِيفَةٌ!
> — الإعلان بالتوبيخ ص١٠٣ · https://shamela.ws/book/34/102
> *English:* "الشافعي died in Egypt in the very year that المأمون came from خراسان to العراق — and that is the year 204. **So the two of them never met at all while المأمون was caliph!**"

**Third, and only third, he adds a character argument:**

> وَكَيْفَ يُعْتَقَدُ أَنَّ الشَّافِعِيَّ يَفْعَلُ هَذَا، وَهُوَ الْقَائِلُ: لَوْ أَنَّ الْمَاءَ الْبَارِدَ يُفْسِدُ مُرُوءَتِي مَا شَرِبْتُ المَاءَ إِلَّا حَارًّا!
> — الإعلان بالتوبيخ ص١٠٣ · https://shamela.ws/book/34/102
> *English:* "And how is it to be believed that الشافعي would do this, when he is the one who said: **'If cold water would spoil my مروءة, I would drink water only hot'**?"

> **Why this belongs in session 3 and not session 1.** It is a worked demonstration that the way to defend
> a reputation is **an almanac, not an argument** — and the room is about to spend forty minutes on a
> period in which reputations are attacked with reports. The order on the page is the teaching point: the
> dates settle it, and the character note is an ornament placed afterwards.

⚠ The edition's footnote on the chain says of the transmitter: «مَعْمر له روايات مُنكرة» — الإعلان ص١٠٢,
footnote (٨). That is the **editor's** note, not السخاوي's; cite it as such if it is used at all.

## 4. Why the years matter for THIS session in particular

السخاوي lists what dating by years is actually *for*, and two items on his list are the spine of
session 3:

> وَكَذَا مَعْرِفَةُ الْقُرُونِ الْفَاضِلَةِ الْمُشَارِ إِلَيْهَا بِقَوْلِهِ -صلى الله عليه وسلم-: "خَيْرُ النَّاسِ قَرْنِي، ثُمَّ الَّذِينَ يَلُونَهُمْ، ثُمَّ الَّذِينَ يَلُونَهُمْ" (٣) لِيَتَمَيَّزَ الْمُقْتَدَى بِهِ مِنْ غَيْرِهِ
> — الإعلان بالتوبيخ ص١٦٨ · https://shamela.ws/book/34/167
> *English:* "And likewise, knowing the excellent generations, indicated by his saying ﷺ: **'The best of people is my generation, then those who follow them, then those who follow them'** — so that the one to be followed is distinguished from another."

And immediately after it:

> وَمَعْرِفَةُ انْقِضَاءِ الزَّمَنِ الْمُحَدَّدِ لِلْخُلَفَاءِ الرَّاشِدِينَ الَّذِينَ أُمِرْنَا بِاقْتِفَاءِ سُنَّتِهِمْ.
> وَبَيَانُ الْوَقْتِ الَّذِي ظَهَرَتْ فِيهِ الْبِدَعُ وَالْحَوَادِثُ.
> — الإعلان بالتوبيخ ص١٦٨ · https://shamela.ws/book/34/167
> *English:* "And knowing **the expiry of the period fixed for the Rightly-Guided Caliphs**, whose سنت we were commanded to follow. And making plain **the time at which the innovations and the novelties appeared.**"

The edition's takhrīj for the ḥadīth of the generations: **البخاري ٣٦٥١، مسلم ٢٥٣٣**, from ابن مسعود ؓ —
الإعلان ص١٦٨, footnote (٣). ⚠ Those are the **editor's** numbers, reproduced as the page prints them;
verify them against a page image before printing them, per `CLAUDE.md` §1.1.

> **The lecture point.** The room's instinct is that dating is bookkeeping. السخاوي's answer is that the
> ummah has to know **when a period ended**, because a command to follow a سنت is useless without knowing
> whose. Session 3 is the session in which that period ends. The date is not a decoration on the slide;
> it is the subject of the slide.

## 5. The historian's honesty, and what histories are made of

Three passages from the فوائد chapter that the earlier note did not cover and that a speaker can use.

**(a) A scholar who wanted to be in a history even among the liars.** السخاوي reports of
**أبو علي الحسن بن أحمد بن البناء القرشي الحنبلي رحمہ اللہ**:

> "لَيْتَ الْخَطِيبَ البَغْدَادِيَّ ذَكَرَنِي فِي "تَارِيخِهِ" وَلَو فِي الْكَذَّابِينَ" (٣).
> — الإعلان بالتوبيخ ص١١٣ · https://shamela.ws/book/34/112
> *English:* "Would that الخطيب البغدادي had mentioned me in his History — **even among the liars.**"

The `(٣)` is the edition's footnote marker. السخاوي pairs it on the same page with a remark made about
himself — someone who supposed he only wrote entries for the dead said «لَيْتَنِي أَمُوتُ فِي حَيَاةِ
السَّخَاوِيِّ حَتَّى يُتَرْجِمَنِي» (الإعلان ص١١٣). Both are light, both are true, and both make the same
point: the historian's book is the only door to being remembered at all — **which is exactly why lies get
pushed in through it.**

**(b) Histories are a mixture, and their own compilers say so.** السخاوي quotes
سبط ابن الجوزي رحمہ اللہ giving his reason for writing مرآة الزمان. The sentence begins at the foot of one
page and completes on the next:

> قَالَ: "وَلَمَّا كَانَ الغَالِبُ عَلَى التَّوَارِيخِ جَمْعَ الْغَثِّ وَالسَّمِينِ، وَالْوَاهِي
> — الإعلان بالتوبيخ ص١٣٤ · https://shamela.ws/book/34/133

> وَالْمَتِينِ، وَالتِّكرَارَ الْخَالِيَ عَنِ الفَوَائِدِ وَالْفَرَائِدِ الَّتِي يَعْجِزُ عَن جَمْعِهَا أَلْفُ رَائِدٍ، اسْتَخَرْتُ اللهَ ...
> — الإعلان بالتوبيخ ص١٣٥ · https://shamela.ws/book/34/134
> *English:* (the two halves together) "And since **what predominates in the histories is the gathering of the lean with the fat, and the flimsy with the firm**, and repetition empty of benefits and of those rarities which a thousand searchers are unable to collect — I sought God's guidance…"

> **This is the sentence to say aloud before narrating anything contested.** It is not our claim that the
> histories carry weak material beside sound. It is the claim of a man who wrote one, offered as his reason
> for writing another.

**(c) The two uses, from ابن الجوزي رحمہ اللہ's مقدمة to المنتظم:**

> "وَللسِّيَرِ وَالتَّوَارِيخِ فَوَائِدُ كَثِيرَةٌ، أَهَمُّهَا فَائِدَتَانِ:
> إِحْدَاهُمَا: أَنَّهُ إِنْ ذُكِرَتْ سِيرَةُ حَازِمٍ، وَوُصِفَتْ عَاقِبَةُ حَالِهِ، أَفَادَتْ حُسْنَ التَّدْبِيرِ، وَاسْتِعْمَالَ الْحَزْمِ، أَوْ [إِنْ ذُكِرَتْ] (٦) سِيرَةُ مُفَرِّطٍ، وَوُصِفَتْ عَاقِبَتُهُ، أَفَادَتْ الْخَوْفَ مِنَ التَّفْرِيطِ
> — الإعلان بالتوبيخ ص١٢٥ · https://shamela.ws/book/34/124
> *English:* "Biographies and histories have many benefits, the most important of them two. The first: that **if the life of a resolute man is related and the outcome of his case described, it yields good management and the exercise of resolve;** or **if the life of a negligent man is related and his outcome described, it yields the fear of negligence.**"

The `[إِنْ ذُكِرَتْ]` in square brackets is **the editor's supplement from المنتظم**, and `(٦)` is his
footnote marker. Both are reproduced exactly as printed.

## 6. ⭐ ابن كثير refuses the standard partisan frame of the whole fitna

This is the most valuable finding in the note, and it needs to be understood before it is used.

There is a widely-repeated report in which **أبو أيوب الأنصاري ؓ**, returning from صفين, is asked how he
can draw his sword on people of «لا إله إلا الله», and answers that the Messenger of Allah ﷺ commanded
them to fight three groups alongside علي ؓ — **الناكثين** (whom he identifies as أهل الجمل),
**القاسطين** (whom he identifies as those he has just come back from), and **المارقين**. The report then
runs on into a string of sayings addressed to عمار ؓ. **البدایہ carries the whole thing** — with its chain,
which it takes from **الخطيب البغدادي**'s تاريخ بغداد — and then, in one line at the foot of the page,
throws it out:

> هذا السياق الظاهر أنه موضوع وآفته من جهة المعلى بن عبد الرحمن فإنه متروك الحديث، والله أعلم (٧).
> — البدایہ والنہایہ ج۷ ص۵۱۶ · https://shamela.ws/book/30097/3662
> *English:* "**This wording — it is apparent that it is fabricated**, and its defect comes from the direction of المعلى بن عبد الرحمن, for he is abandoned in ḥadīth. And Allah knows best."

The `(٧)` is the edition's footnote marker. The edition's own note at that point goes further than
ابن كثير does — «بل هو كذاب يسرق الحديث» — and refers the reader to ابن الجوزي's الموضوعات. **That
harder verdict is the editor's, not ابن كثير's.** Cite ابن كثير's «الظاهر أنه موضوع» and attribute the
editor's sentence to the editor if it is used at all.

> **Why this is worth a slide of its own.** The three-way labelling of the parties in the fitna — the
> treaty-breakers, the deviators, the renegades — is the frame in which the whole period is usually
> presented to a room that has read anything about it. It settles, in advance, who was right at الجمل and
> who was right at صفين. **Our own primary narrative source carries the report and then says it appears to
> be fabricated.** That is not us declining to adjudicate; that is ابن كثير declining, in print, on the
> page, with the transmitter named. It is exactly the material the guardrail was built to stand on.

⚠ **Handling rule for the hall.** Do not narrate the report's content and then knock it down — a room
remembers the story, not the verdict. If the frame comes up in a written slip, the answer is one sentence:
*"ابن كثير رحمہ اللہ records that report and says it appears to be fabricated, and names the man in the
chain."* Give the page. Say nothing about who was right.

## 7. ابن كثير and الهيثم بن عدي refuse a second report — and the refusal names its source

In the same stretch of chapters, البدایہ reports that after النهروان a man named **الخِرِّيت بن راشد
الناجي** broke away with a body of بنو ناجية, that علي ؓ sent **معقل بن قيس الرماحي** against them, and
that five hundred households were taken. Then it gives a **rival version** of the same event and refuses it:

> قال الهيثم: وهذا قول الشيعة ولم يسمع بحيٍّ من العرب ارتدوا [عن الإسلام] بعد الردة التى كانت في أيام الصديق.
> — البدایہ والنہایہ ج۷ ص۵۲۰ · https://shamela.ws/book/30097/3666
> *English:* "الهيثم said: **and this is the Shīʿa's own statement** — and no Arab tribe has ever been heard of as having apostatised [from Islam] after the ردة that was in the days of الصدیق ؓ."

The `[عن الإسلام]` in square brackets is the editor's supplement. The rejected version — that بنو ناجية
had apostatised, so that the captives were lawfully taken — البدایہ attributes through الهيثم to
**سفيان الثوري and إسرائيل, from عمّار الدُّهني, from أبو الطفيل** (ج۷ ص۵۲۰).

⚠ **Two source cautions on this page, both of which matter.**
1. **الهيثم بن عدي is not one of our books and is not a source we cite.** He is named by البدایہ as its
   own informant, and ابن كثير even praises his book on the خوارج — «وهو من أحسن ما صنف في ذلك»
   (ج۷ ص۵۱۶). **The edition's footnote at that exact spot reads «لكنه كذاب متروك!»** — "but he is a liar,
   abandoned!" (ج۷ ص۵۱۶, footnote (٩)). That is the **editor** contradicting **ابن كثير** in a footnote, on
   the page, about the reliability of the source of several paragraphs that follow.
2. **Therefore nothing in §7 is usable as a fact.** What *is* usable is the shape: a history that names
   its informant, prints his verdict, and lets a later editor print his own against it. That is what
   "تاریخ records rather than rules" looks like in practice, and it can be shown to a room in one
   screenshot without adjudicating anything.

## 8. ابن كثير refuses a third report — this one about تفضیل

In the closing pages of the biography of علي ؓ, under a heading ابن كثير gives as
**«غريبة من الغرائب وآبدة من الأوابد»** — a strange thing among strange things — he reports, from
ابن أبي خيثمة through عبد الرزاق, a conversation in which **معمر** claims that the moderates of الكوفة
put علي ؓ ahead of أبو بكر ؓ and عمر ؓ, and names **سفيان الثوري** among them; and in which عبد الرزاق then
takes the claim to **وكيع بن الجراح**, who is appalled by it and laughs it off. ابن كثير prints the whole
exchange and then gives his own verdict:

> وهذا الكلام فيه تخبيط كثير ولعله اشتبه على معمر فإن المشهور عن بعض الكوفيين تقديم عليّ على عثمان، فأمّا على الشيخين فلا، ولا يخفى فضل الشيخين على سائر الصحابة إلَّا على غبيّ، فكيف يخفى على هؤلاء الأئمة؟
> — البدایہ والنہایہ ج۷ ص۶۰۹ · https://shamela.ws/book/30097/3755
> *English:* "**And in this speech there is a great deal of confusion**, and perhaps the matter became unclear to معمر — for what is well known of some of the Kūfans is the putting of علي ahead of عثمان; as for ahead of the two Shaykhs, no. And the merit of the two Shaykhs over the rest of the Companions is hidden from none but a dull man — so how should it be hidden from these imams?"

He then, on the facing page, endorses a saying of others against the lesser claim as well:

> كأيوب والدارقطني [وغير واحد]- من قدَّم عليًا على عثمان فقد أزرى بالمهاجرين والأنصار. وهذا الكلام حق وصدق وصحيح ومليح.
> — البدایہ والنہایہ ج۷ ص۶۱۰ · https://shamela.ws/book/30097/3756
> *English:* "…like أيوب and الدارقطني [and more than one other] — whoever puts علي ahead of عثمان has disparaged the Muhājirūn and the Anṣār. **And this speech is truth, and honesty, and sound, and fine.**"

⚠ **This second passage is `[SOURCED]` but it is NOT platform material.** It is a ترتیب/تفضیل statement
about two Companions, and CLAUDE.md §1.6 forbids the speaker from adjudicating between them from the
floor. It is recorded here for three reasons: so that nobody re-researches it; so that the speaker is not
ambushed by it from the question box; and because **the first passage** — ابن كثير saying in plain words
that a report he has just printed is confused — is exactly the demonstration the evening needs, and is
perfectly safe. **Use §8's first quotation, not its second.**

## 9. ابن كثير grades chains inside the killing of عثمان ؓ

The clearest single instance. ابن كثير gives a saying of **أبو موسى الأشعري ؓ** with its chain and then, in
three words, tells the reader what the chain is worth:

> لو كان قتل عثمان هُدًى لاحتلبت به الأُمَّةُ لبنًا، ولكنّه كان ضلالًا فاحتلبت به الأُمَّةُ دمًا. وهذا منقطع.
> — البدایہ والنہایہ ج۷ ص۳۴۸ · https://shamela.ws/book/30097/3494
> *English:* "Had the killing of عثمان been guidance, the ummah would have milked milk by it; but it was misguidance, and so the ummah milked blood by it. **And this one is broken** [i.e. its chain is discontinuous]."

**Note carefully what is and is not graded.** The identical image — milk against blood — is also
transmitted from **حذيفة بن اليمان ؓ**, on the facing page, in a longer form, **without** any such
remark; and ابن كثير adds that البخاري mentioned it in his صحیح:

> اللهمَّ إن كان قتل عثمان بن عفان خيرًا، فليس لي فيه نصيب؛ وإن كان قتله شرًا فأنا منه بريء، واللّه لئن كان قتله خيرًا ليحلبنه لبنًا، وإن كان قتله شرًا ليمتصن (١) به دمًا. وقد ذكره البخاري في "صحيحه" (٢).
> — البدایہ والنہایہ ج۷ ص۳۴۸ · https://shamela.ws/book/30097/3494
> *English:* "O Allah, if the killing of عثمان بن عفان was good, then I have no share in it; and if his killing was evil, then I am quit of it. By Allah — if his killing was good, it will surely be milked as milk, and if his killing was evil, blood will surely be sucked by it. And البخاري mentioned it in his Ṣaḥīḥ."

⚠ **And the edition's footnote (٢) at that point contradicts ابن كثير:**
«هكذا قال، وما أظنه صوابًا، فإننا لم نقف عليه في صحيح البخاري» — *"thus he said, and I do not think it
correct, for we did not come upon it in صحیح البخاری."* — البدایہ ج۷ ص۳۴۸, footnote (٢).
**So the البخاري attribution is contested on the page itself, by the editor.** It must therefore **not** be
put on a slide as "in البخاري". Say instead: *"ابن كثير says البخاري mentioned it; the editor of the
printed edition says he could not find it there."* That sentence is true, complete, and costs nothing.
Marked `[SOURCED, disputed]`.

A parallel instance from the same page: after reporting a claim that no one who took part in the killing
died except violently, ابن كثير writes «رواه ابن جرير» and then gives his own reasoning for why it *should*
be so — the first ground being the answered supplication of **سعد بن أبي وقاص ؓ** (ج۷ ص۳۴۳,
https://shamela.ws/book/30097/3489). ⚠ **That is البدایہ citing ابن جرير; it is not a citation of الطبری
by us**, and the claim itself is a claim about outcomes we do not need and should not carry.

## 10. Where the books frankly do not know — five worked disagreements

This section is the evidence for the single most useful thing the speaker can say all evening: *our books
do not pretend to know what they do not know.* All five are on pages we hold, and all five are about
matters a listener would assume were settled.

**(a) How long the siege lasted, and on what day it ended.**

> كانت مدة حصار (٥) عثمان ﵁ في داره أربعين يومًا على المشهور، وقيل كانت بضعةً وأربعين يومًا. وقال الشعبي: كانت ثنتين وعشرين ليلةً. ثم كان قتله ﵁ في يوم الجمعة بلا خلاف.
> — البدایہ والنہایہ ج۷ ص۳۴۳ · https://shamela.ws/book/30097/3489
> *English:* "The period of the siege of عثمان ؓ in his house was **forty days, according to what is best known**; and it is said it was **some forty days**; and الشعبي said it was **twenty-two nights**. Then his killing ؓ was **on a Friday, with no disagreement.**"

Note the craft of it: three figures for the length, and then **بلا خلاف** for the day. The author marks
exactly where certainty begins.

The same page continues into the hour, and shows ابن كثير choosing between two without hiding the other —
سيف بن عمر's مشايخ and مصعب بن الزبير say the last hour of the Friday, «وقال آخرون ضحوة (نهارها)، وهذا
أشبه» — *others said mid-morning, and this is the more likely* (ج۷ ص۳۴۳). ⚠ سيف بن عمر is named there as
**البدایہ's own informant**, not as a source of ours.

**(b) Which year.** ص۳۴۴ gives 35ھ «على الصحيح المشهور», records 36ھ from مصعب بن الزبير «وطائفة», and
comments on the second:

> وقيل سنة ست وثلاثين، قال مصعب بن الزبير (٢) وطائفة: وهو غريب.
> — البدایہ والنہایہ ج۷ ص۳۴۴ · https://shamela.ws/book/30097/3490
> *English:* "And it is said: the year 36 — مصعب بن الزبير and a group said so — **and that is strange.**"

The same page then computes the length of the caliphate from the first date: **twelve years less twelve
days**, because the bayʿa was at the beginning of المحرم 24ھ (ج۷ ص۳۴۴).

**(c) How old he was.** The spread on one page is **63 · 75 · 82 · 82-and-some-months · 84 · 88 · 90**, and
ابن كثير attaches a comment to the two outliers:

> وأغرب منه ما رواه (سيف) بن عمر عن مشايخه، وهم محمد وطلحة وأبو عثمان وأبو حارثة أنهم قالوا: قتل عثمان ﵁ عن ثلاث وستين سنة (٨).
> — البدایہ والنہایہ ج۷ ص۳۴۴ · https://shamela.ws/book/30097/3490
> *English:* "And stranger still than that is what سيف بن عمر related from his teachers — محمد and طلحة and أبو عثمان and أبو حارثة — that they said: **عثمان ؓ was killed at sixty-three years of age.**"

(The preceding "strange" report, which this outdoes, is هشام بن الكلبي's seventy-five: «وهذا غريب جدًا» —
ج۷ ص۳۴۴.) ⚠ Again: سيف بن عمر and هشام بن الكلبي are **البدایہ's attributions**, reproduced as such.

**(d) Who prayed over him.** One sentence, six names, no adjudication:

> وصلى عليه جُبَيْر بن مطعم، وقيل الزبير بن العوام، وقيل حكيم بن حزام، وقيل مروان بن الحكم، وقيل المسور بن مَخْرَمة
> — البدایہ والنہایہ ج۷ ص۳۴۵ · https://shamela.ws/book/30097/3491
> *English:* "And **جبير بن مطعم ؓ** prayed over him — and it is said **الزبير بن العوام ؓ**, and it is said **حكيم بن حزام ؓ**, and it is said **مروان بن الحكم**, and it is said **المسور بن مخرمة ؓ**."

On the same page a separate report has قتادة saying «صلى الزبير على عثمان ودفنه وكان أوصى إليه», which
agrees with هشام بن عروة's report on ج۷ ص۳۳۴ that عثمان ؓ made his bequest to الزبير ؓ. **That is a
convergence, not a proof, and the note records it as a convergence.** The place of burial, by contrast, is
given as settled: «وأما موضع قبره فلا خلاف أنه (دفن) بحشّ كوكب -شرقي البقيع» (ج۷ ص۳۴۴).

**(e) Where علي ؓ is buried.** The most striking of the five, because ابن كثير lets the possibility stand
that the grave is simply lost. He gives: at دار الخلافة · facing the جامع · in a room of the houses of
آل جعدة بن هبيرة · بظاهر البهوفة · بالكناسة · in the open country · in the wall of the جامع of الكوفة
(ج۷ ص۶۱۱); then **شريك القاضي and أبو نعيم**'s report that الحسن ؓ moved him to البقيع at المدینہ beside
فاطمة ؓ; then عيسى بن دأب's:

> فلما مروا به ببلاد طيِّئ أضلوا ذلك البعير فأخذته طيِّء تحسب فيه مالًا، فلما وجدوا بالصندوق (٩) ميتًا دفنوه في بلادهم فلا يعرف قبره إلى الآن، والمشهور أن قبره إلى الآن بالكوفة
> — البدایہ والنہایہ ج۷ ص۶۱۱ · https://shamela.ws/book/30097/3757
> *English:* "…and when they passed with him through the country of طيِّئ, they lost that camel, and طيِّئ took it supposing there was money in it; and when they found a dead man in the chest, they buried him in their own country — **so his grave is not known to this day.** And what is best known is that his grave is at الكوفة to this day."

> **The sentence this licenses, and it is a good one:** *"Our books tell us the day of the week with no
> disagreement, and cannot agree on the year, the age, or who led the prayer. That is not a weakness in
> them. That is what an honest record looks like."*
