## 0.1 How to read the transmission flags in this note

Every item below carries one of these, taken from the page itself or its printed footnote. **This
matters more here than in a narrative note**, because a quotation set in 44pt on a screen is a
claim about words, and words are the first thing a chain loses.

| Flag | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **GRADED SOUND** | The book or its editor grades it: «⁨إسناد صحيح⁩», «⁨حسن⁩», «⁨على شرط الشيخين⁩», or it is in ⁨الصحيحين⁩ / ⁨السنن⁩ with a number printed on the page. |
| **PLAIN** | Printed without grading, chain ordinary, no known defect flagged. |
| **VIA ⁨سيف⁩ / ⁨الطبری⁩** | Reaches ⁨ابن كثير⁩ or ⁨ابن الأثير⁩ through **⁨سيف بن عمر⁩** or through ⁨ابن جرير⁩. It is inside our frame because it is on an approved page — but ⁨ابن كثير⁩'s editor footnotes it to ⁨تاريخ الطبري⁩, and the course's own ⁨مقدمہ⁩ says presence in ⁨الطبری⁩ is not authenticity. **Say «reported» and do not press the wording.** |
| **FLAGGED WEAK** | The page or its editor names a defect (⁨الواقدي متروك⁩ · ⁨سيف ضعيف⁩ · ⁨مرسل⁩ · a named weak narrator). **Recorded so it is never re-researched. Do not put it on a slide.** |
| **DO NOT USE** | On the page, but unsuitable for this room — contested theologically, or touching the ⁨مشاجرات⁩. Recorded with its page so nobody has to look for it again. |

## 0.2 A typographic caveat that applies to every quotation below

The Shamela text is a **typed edition**, not OCR, so it is far cleaner than the Urdu scans — but it
is not error-free, and the pages read in this pass carry visible slips: ⁨البدایہ ج۷ ص۱۸۶⁩ prints
«⁨صحيح البخارب⁩» for ⁨البخاري⁩; ⁨ج۷ ص۲۳۹⁩ prints «⁨قاك⁩» for «⁨قال⁩» and «⁨حتى دم يبق⁩» for «⁨حتى لم يبق⁩»;
⁨ج۷ ص۲۶۴⁩ prints «⁨وفيل لعمر⁩» for «⁨وقيل لعمر⁩»; ⁨ج۷ ص۱۸⁩ prints «⁨كانو⁩» and «⁨وفي رواِية⁩».
**Wherever a sentence below is going to be set in 44pt on a screen, it must first be read off a
printed page image.** Items where the Shamela line itself looked damaged are flagged in place.

**Every Arabic blockquote in this note has been machine-checked, character by character, against the
cached Shamela page it cites**, and the whole note was re-checked adversarially on 2026-09-06. The
policy is that the note quotes what the edition prints, not what it ought to print, so the page's own
typographical faults are reproduced. **The 2026-09-06 re-check found three places where that policy
had been broken, and all three are now fixed:** §1.6 read «⁨رواه الجماعة⁩…» where the page prints
«**⁨وقد روى⁩** ⁨الجماعة⁩…»; §2.5(d) read «⁨الرجال⁩ **⁨الذين⁩** ⁨روى عنهم⁩» where the page prints «⁨الرجال⁩
**⁨الدين⁩** ⁨روى عنهم⁩»; and §5.1 carried a spliced sentence with no ellipsis (see §5.1). Anything still
quoted below is what the cached page carries. So the following really are on the page and are
**not** transcription errors here —

- ⁨البدایہ ج۷ ص۱۸⁩ prints «⁨كانو يؤدّونه⁩» (for ⁨كانوا⁩) and «⁨وفي رواِية⁩» (for ⁨روايةٍ⁩) · ⁨ج۷ ص۱۸⁩ also prints
  «⁨الحمدُ الله الذي هَدى فكَفَى⁩» (for ⁨الحمدُ لله⁩) · ⁨ج۷ ص۱۰۰⁩ prints «⁨فالحمد الله الذي ردَّ كيدَكم⁩»
  (same fault) · ⁨ج۷ ص۱۸۶⁩ prints «⁨صحيح البخارب⁩» · ⁨ج۷ ص۲۶۴⁩ prints «⁨وفيل لعمر⁩: ⁨إنك قضاء⁩» where the
  sense requires «⁨وقيل لعمر⁩: ⁨إنك فظٌّ غليظ⁩» · ⁨سیر⁩ (⁨راشدون⁩) ⁨ص۸۵⁩ prints «⁨ما شيتهما⁩» as two words.
- **Where a sound reading exists in a second approved book, this note quotes the second book and
  says so** — e.g. the «⁨فظ غليظ⁩» exchange is taken from ⁨سیر ص۸۰⁩, not from the damaged ⁨البدایہ⁩ line.
- **Two conventions inside the blockquotes:** « … » marks material deliberately omitted from the
  middle of a passage, and a printed page-break is marked the same way — the words on either
  side are verbatim, but the edition's footnote apparatus stands between them on the page (this
  occurs once,
  in ⁨أبو عبيدة ؓ⁩'s letter, which runs from ⁨سیر ج۱ ص۱۸⁩ onto ⁨ص۱۹⁩).

**None of this replaces the page-image check.** The machine check proves the note matches the
Shamela edition; it cannot prove the Shamela edition matches the printed book. **Anything going on a
slide still gets eyeballed against a page image first.**

# 1. أبو بكر الصديق ؓ

## 1.1 The inaugural address, in full — ⁨البدایہ ج۷ ص۶⁩

**Setting.** The morning after ⁨السقيفة⁩. ⁨عمر ؓ⁩ has just stood up and told the people to give the
general bayʿah in the mosque. ⁨أبو بكر ؓ⁩ then rises for the first time as ⁨خليفة رسول الله ﷺ⁩.

> أما بعدُ، أيُّها الناس فإني قد وُلّيتُ عليكم ولستُ بخيركم فإنْ أحسنتُ فأعينوني وإن أسأتُ فقوِّموني.
> الصدقُ أمانةٌ والكذبُ خيانةٌ، والضعيف فيكم قوي عندي حتى أُرْجعَ عليه حقَّه إن شاء الله، والقويُّ فيكم
> ضعيفٌ حتى آخذَ الحقَّ منه إن شاء الله، لا يدعُ قومٌ الجهادَ في سبيل الله إلا خذلهم اللهُ بالذلّ، ولا
> تشيعُ الفاحشةُ في قومٍ إلا عمَّهم اللهُ بالبلاء، أطيعوني ما أطعت اللهَ ورسولَه، فإذا عصيْتُ الله ورسولَه
> فلا طاعة لي عليكم، قوموا إلى صلاتكم يرحمكم الله
> — ⁨البدایہ والنہایہ ج۷ ص۶⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/30097/3152
> *English:* "Now then, people — I have been placed over you and I am not the best of you. If I do
> well, help me; if I do badly, straighten me. Truthfulness is a trust and lying is a betrayal. The
> weak among you is strong in my sight until I restore his right to him, God willing; and the strong
> among you is weak in my sight until I take the right from him, God willing. No people abandon
> struggle in God's path but God forsakes them to humiliation; and indecency does not spread among
> a people but God covers them with affliction. Obey me so long as I obey God and His Messenger; if
> I disobey God and His Messenger, then you owe me no obedience. Rise to your prayer, God have mercy
> on you."

**⁨ابن كثير⁩'s own grading, printed immediately after it:** «**⁨وهذا إسنادٌ صحيحٌ⁩**» (same page). The
chain is ⁨محمد بن إسحاق⁩ ← ⁨الزهري⁩ ← ⁨أنس بن مالك ؓ⁩.

**Reading aloud:** about 45–50 seconds at a dignified pace. **Too long for one slide.** Split it:
the first sentence («⁨فإني قد وُلّيتُ عليكم⁩ … ⁨فقوِّموني⁩») is one slide, ~7 seconds; «⁨الصدقُ أمانةٌ والكذبُ⁩
⁨خيانةٌ⁩» plus the weak/strong pair is a second slide, ~15 seconds; «⁨أطيعوني ما أطعتُ اللهَ⁩ … ⁨فلا طاعة لي⁩
⁨عليكم⁩» is the third and is the one the room will remember, ~10 seconds.

**The ⁨الکامل⁩ wording of the same address**, for comparison — do not merge the two:

> أَيُّهَا النَّاسُ، قَدْ وُلِّيتَ عَلَيْكُمْ وَلَسْتُ بِخَيْرِكُمْ، فَإِنْ أَحْسَنْتُ فَأَعِينُونِي،
> وَإِنْ أَسَأْتُ فَقَوِّمُونِي، الصِّدْقُ أَمَانَةٌ، وَالْكَذِبُ خِيَانَةٌ، وَالضَّعِيفُ فِيكُمْ قَوِيٌّ
> عِنْدِي حَتَّى آخُذَ لَهُ حَقَّهُ، وَالْقَوِيُّ ضَعِيفٌ عِنْدِي حَتَّى آخُذَ مِنْهُ الْحَقَّ، إِنْ شَاءَ
> اللَّهُ تَعَالَى، لَا يَدَعُ أَحَدٌ مِنْكُمُ الْجِهَادَ، فَإِنَّهُ لَا يَدَعُهُ قَوْمٌ إِلَّا ضَرَبَهُمُ
> اللَّهُ بِالذُّلِّ، أَطِيعُونِي مَا أَطَعْتُ اللَّهَ وَرَسُولَهُ، فَإِذَا عَصْيَتُ اللَّهَ وَرَسُولَهُ
> فَلَا طَاعَةَ لِي عَلَيْكُمْ، قُومُوا إِلَى صَلَاتِكُمْ - رَحِمَكُمُ اللَّه
> — ⁨الکامل فی التاریخ ج۲ ص۱۹۲⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/21712/883
> *English:* as above; the sense is identical, the phrasing differs slightly ("until I take his right
> **for** him" rather than "restore it **to** him"), the verb in the jihād clause differs
> (⁨الکامل⁩ «**⁨ضربهم⁩** ⁨الله بالذل⁩» — *strikes* them with humiliation; ⁨البدایہ⁩ «**⁨خذلهم⁩** ⁨الله بالذل⁩» —
> *forsakes* them to it), and the ⁨الکامل⁩ text drops the clause about indecency.

⚠ ⁨الکامل⁩'s page prints «⁨فَإِذَا عَصْيَتُ⁩» — a typo for «⁨عَصَيْتُ⁩». Use ⁨البدایہ⁩'s spelling.

## 1.2 «I am only a man like you» — the second address, ⁨البدایہ ج۷ ص۸⁩

**Setting.** The day after the Prophet's ﷺ death. The crier has just gone out ordering every man of
⁨أسامة ؓ⁩'s army back to the camp at ⁨الجُرْف⁩. ⁨أبو بكر ؓ⁩ then stands in the people.

> أيها الناسُ إنما أنا مثلكم وإني [لا أدري] (٤) لعلكم تُكلّفوني ما كان رسولُ الله ﷺ يُطيق، إنَّ الله اصطفى
> محمدًا على العالمين، وعَصَمهُ من الآفاتِ، وإنما أنا متَّبع ولست بمُبتدعٍ، فإن استقمتُ فبايعوني، وإن
> زُغْتُ (٥) فقوِّموني
> — ⁨البدایہ والنہایہ ج۷ ص۸⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/30097/3154
> *English:* "People, I am only a man like you, and I do not know — perhaps you will burden me with
> what the Messenger of God ﷺ was able to bear. God chose Muḥammad above all the worlds and shielded
> him from every flaw. I am a follower, not an innovator. If I stand straight, then follow me; if I
> swerve, straighten me."

**Reading aloud:** ~20 seconds. Fits one slide if the bracketed «⁨لا أدري⁩» is kept (the editor
supplies it from ⁨الطبری⁩ and marks it) — say so, or drop the bracket and quote from «⁨إنَّ الله اصطفى⁩».

⚠ The chain is **⁨سيف بن عمر التميمي⁩**, and ⁨ابن كثير⁩'s editor footnotes it to ⁨تاريخ الطبري ٣⁩/٢٢٣.
It is on the ⁨البدایہ⁩ page, so it is inside our frame — but it is not of the strength of §1.1.

The same page continues into a long homiletic passage («⁨أين الجبّارونَ⁩ … ⁨أين الملوكُ الذين أثاروا الأرضَ⁩
⁨وعمروها؟ قد بعدُوا ونُسِيَ ذكرُهم⁩» — ⁨ج۷ ص۹⁩). **Recorded here so it is never re-researched; not
recommended for delivery** — it is two full printed pages and it is ⁨سيف⁩'s transmission.

## 1.3 The refusal to recall ⁨أسامة ؓ⁩'s army — three wordings, three pages

The single most quoted decision of the first weeks exists in our sources in three shapes. **Use one.
Do not blend them.**

**(a) ⁨البدایہ ج۷ ص۹⁩** — ⁨ابن كثير⁩'s own narrative voice reporting ⁨أبو بكر ؓ⁩:

> والله لا أحلُّ عقدةً عقدها رسولُ الله ﷺ، ولو أنَّ الطير تَخْطفُنا، والسباع من حول المدينة، ولو أنَّ
> الكلابَ جرت بأرجلِ أمهات المؤمنين لأجَهّزن جيشَ أسامة
> — ⁨البدایہ والنہایہ ج۷ ص۹⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/30097/3155
> *English:* "By God, I will not untie a knot the Messenger of God ﷺ tied — not though the birds
> snatch us away and the wild beasts are around Medina; not though the dogs drag at the feet of the
> Mothers of the Believers. I will send out Usāma's army."

**(b) ⁨البدایہ ج۷ ص۱۰⁩**, through ⁨هشام بن عروة⁩ ← ⁨عروة⁩, the shortest and cleanest:

> والذي نفس أبي بكر بيده لو ظننت أن السباعَ تَخْطفني لأنفذتُ بعثَ أسامةَ كما أمرَ به رسولُ الله ﷺ؛ ولو لم
> يبقَ في القرى غيري لأنفذته
> — ⁨البدایہ والنہایہ ج۷ ص۱۰⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/30097/3156
> *English:* "By Him in whose hand is Abū Bakr's soul — if I thought the wild beasts would snatch me
> away, I would still send out Usāma's expedition as the Messenger of God ﷺ ordered; and if no one
> were left in the settlements but me, I would still send it."

**(c) ⁨الکامل ج۲ ص۱۹۵⁩**, with ⁨عمر ؓ⁩ standing there as the messenger:

> لَوْ خَطَفَتْنِي الْكِلَابُ وَالذِّئَابُ لَأَنْفَذْتُهُ كَمَا أَمَرَ بِهِ رَسُولُ اللَّهِ - صَلَّى
> اللَّهُ عَلَيْهِ وَسَلَّمَ - وَلَا أَرُدُّ قَضَاءً قَضَى بِهِ رَسُولُ اللَّهِ - صَلَّى اللَّهُ عَلَيْهِ
> وَسَلَّمَ - وَلَوْ لَمْ يَبْقَ فِي الْقُرَى غَيْرِي لَأَنْفَذْتُه
> — ⁨الکامل فی التاریخ ج۲ ص۱۹۵⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/21712/886
> *English:* "If the dogs and the wolves snatched me away I would still send it out as the Messenger
> of God ﷺ ordered, and I do not reverse a decision the Messenger of God ﷺ decided; and if no one
> were left in the settlements but me, I would still send it."

**Reading aloud:** (b) is ~12 seconds and is the best slide of the three. (a) is ~15 seconds and is
the most vivid but its two "not though" clauses are hard to hold in one breath.

**And the corroboration on the same theme — ⁨أبو هريرة ؓ⁩'s oath, ⁨البدایہ ج۷ ص۱۱⁩:**

> والله الذي لا إله إلَّا هو، لولا أن أبا بكر استُخلف ما عُبد الله
> — ⁨البدایہ والنہایہ ج۷ ص۱۱⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/30097/3157
> *English:* "By God besides whom there is no god — had Abū Bakr not been made successor, God would
> not have been worshipped."

He said it three times and was asked to stop; the reason he then gave is the ⁨أسامة ؓ⁩ decision.
⚠ The chain runs through **⁨عبّاد بن كَثير⁩**, and ⁨ابن كثير⁩ discusses him on the page: if it is the
⁨البرمكي⁩ he is «⁨مقارب الحديث⁩»; if the ⁨البصري الثقفي⁩, «⁨متروك الحديث⁩». **Say "reported" and give Ibn
Kathīr's own hesitation** — that hesitation is itself good method content.

## 1.4 The walk beside ⁨أسامة ؓ⁩'s horse — ⁨البدایہ ج۷ ص۱۱⁩ and ⁨الکامل ج۲ ص۱۹۵⁩

**Setting.** ⁨أبو بكر ؓ⁩ has gone out to ⁨الجُرْف⁩ on foot to inspect the army. ⁨أسامة ؓ⁩, aged about
eighteen, is riding; the caliph is walking beside him; ⁨عبد الرحمن بن عوف ؓ⁩ is leading the caliph's
mount.

> فقال أسامةُ: يا خليفةَ رسولِ الله: إما أن تركبَ وإما أن أنزلَ، فقال: والله لستَ بنازلٍ ولستُ براكب
> — ⁨البدایہ والنہایہ ج۷ ص۱۱⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/30097/3157
> *English:* Usāma said: "Successor of the Messenger of God — either you ride, or I dismount." He
> said: "By God, you will not dismount, and I will not ride."

The ⁨الکامل⁩ version adds the reason, and it is the better slide:

> فَقَالَ: وَاللَّهِ لَا نَزَلْتَ وَلَا أَرْكَبُ، وَمَا عَلَيَّ أَنْ أُغَبِّرَ قَدَمَيَّ سَاعَةً فِي
> سَبِيلِ اللَّه
> — ⁨الکامل فی التاریخ ج۲ ص۱۹۵⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/21712/886
> *English:* "By God, you shall not dismount and I shall not ride. What harm is it to me that my two
> feet gather dust for an hour in the path of God?"

**Reading aloud:** ~8 seconds. One slide, easily.

On the same page, the coda that makes the whole scene land: ⁨أبو بكر ؓ⁩ asked ⁨أسامة ؓ⁩ to release
⁨عمر ؓ⁩ from the expedition so he could keep him in Medina — *the caliph asking the eighteen-year-old
commander's permission* — «⁨فلهذا كان عمرُ لا يلقاه بعد ذلك إلا قال⁩: **⁨السلام عليكَ أيها الأميرُ⁩**»
(⁨البدایہ ج۷ ص۱۱⁩): "and this is why ʿUmar, ever afterwards, never met him without saying: *Peace be
upon you, commander.*"

## 1.5 The ten rules of war, in full — ⁨الکامل ج۲ ص۱۹۶⁩

**Setting.** Immediately after the exchange in §1.4. ⁨أبو بكر ؓ⁩ turns to the army before it marches
and gives it its standing orders. **This is the item the strand asked for, and this is the only
approved source in this pass that carries the wording.**

> ثُمَّ وَصَّاهُمْ فَقَالَ: لَا تَخُونُوا، وَلَا تَغْدِرُوا، وَلَا تَغُلُّوا، وَلَا تُمَثِّلُوا، وَلَا
> تَقْتُلُوا طِفْلًا وَلَا شَيْخًا كَبِيرًا وَلَا امْرَأَةً، وَلَا تَعْقِرُوا نَخْلًا وَتُحْرِقُوهُ، وَلَا
> تَقْطَعُوا شَجَرَةً مُثْمِرَةً، وَلَا تَذْبَحُوا شَاةً وَلَا بَقَرَةً وَلَا بَعِيرًا إِلَّا
> لِمَأْكَلَةٍ، وَسَوْفَ تَمُرُّونَ بِأَقْوَامٍ قَدْ فَرَّغُوا أَنْفُسَهُمْ فِي الصَّوَامِعِ، فَدَعُوهُمْ
> وَمَا فَرَّغُوا أَنْفُسَهُمْ لَه … انْدَفِعُوا بِاسْمِ اللَّه
> — ⁨الکامل فی التاریخ ج۲ ص۱۹۶⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/21712/887
> *English:* "Do not betray. Do not act treacherously. Do not steal from the spoils. Do not mutilate.
> Do not kill a child, nor an aged man, nor a woman. Do not cut down a date-palm and burn it. Do not
> fell a fruit-bearing tree. Do not slaughter a sheep or a cow or a camel except for food. You will
> pass by people who have withdrawn into cells and given themselves wholly to what they are in —
> leave them and what they have given themselves to … Set out, in the name of God."

⚠ **The ellipsis is real and it is deliberate.** Between «⁨وَمَا فَرَّغُوا أَنْفُسَهُمْ لَهُ⁩» and
«⁨انْدَفِعُوا بِاسْمِ اللَّهِ⁩» the page carries one further clause, about a second group of men
identified by a shaved crown, and it is an order to strike them. It is on the page at ⁨ج۲ ص۱۹۶⁩ and it
is recorded here so that nobody has to go looking for it again. **Recommended handling: excerpt as
above, mark the ellipsis on the slide, and if the omission is challenged, say plainly that the
sentence is on the page, that it concerns a specific fighting group and not the monks, and defer.**
Do not read it aloud and do not deny it.

**Reading aloud:** the excerpt as given is ~30 seconds. That is a full slide on its own and it earns
the time. If it must be shortened, the first line alone — «⁨لَا تَخُونُوا، وَلَا تَغْدِرُوا، وَلَا تَغُلُّوا،⁩
⁨وَلَا تُمَثِّلُوا، وَلَا تَقْتُلُوا طِفْلًا وَلَا شَيْخًا كَبِيرًا وَلَا امْرَأَةً⁩» — is ~10 seconds and carries
the point.

## 1.6 The zakāt refusers, and ⁨عمر ؓ⁩'s admission — ⁨البدایہ ج۷ ص۱۸⁩

**Setting.** Delegations are coming into Medina accepting prayer and refusing the zakāt. ⁨عمر ؓ⁩
objects to fighting them and cites the ḥadīth of «⁨أمرت أن أقاتل الناس⁩». ⁨أبو بكر ؓ⁩ answers.

> فقال أبو بكر: والله لو مَنَعوني عَناقًا، وفي رواِية: عِقالًا كانو يؤدّونه إلى رسول الله ﷺ لأقاتِلَنَّهم
> على منعها، إنَّ الزكاةَ حقُّ المال، واللهِ لأقاتِلَنَّ منْ فرَّقَ بينَ الصلاةِ والزكاةِ، قال عمر: فما هو
> إلا أن رأيتُ اللهَ قد شرحَ صدرَ أبي بكر للقتال، فعرفتُ أنه الحق
> — ⁨البدایہ والنہایہ ج۷ ص۱۸⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/30097/3164
> *English:* Abū Bakr said: "By God, if they withheld from me a young goat — in another wording, a
> hobbling-rope — which they used to render to the Messenger of God ﷺ, I would fight them over
> withholding it. The zakāt is a due upon wealth. By God, I will fight whoever separates prayer from
> zakāt." ʿUmar said: "It was nothing but that I saw God had opened Abū Bakr's breast to the fight —
> **and I knew it was the truth.**"

**Takhrīj, copied from ⁨ابن كثير⁩'s own footnote on that page** (do not retype from memory):
البخاري ٦٩٢٤ و ٦٩٢٥ في استتابة المرتدين · مسلم ٢٠ في الإيمان · أبو داود ١٥٥٦ في الزكاة ·
⁨الترمذي ٢٦٠٧ في الإيمان⁩ · ⁨النسائي ٥⁩/⁨١٤ في الزكاة⁩ · ⁨مسند أحمد ٢⁩/٥٢٨. ⁨ابن كثير⁩'s text says
«**وقد روى** الجماعةُ في كتبهم سوى ابن ماجه».
⚠ The Bukhārī reference is printed on the page as «⁨رقم⁩ (⁨٦٩٢٤ وه ٦٩٢⁩)» — the second number is
damaged in the Shamela text. **۶۹۲۴ is safe; do not state the second number from this page.**

**Reading aloud:** ~22 seconds for the pair. **The pair is the point** — the slide must carry
⁨عمر ؓ⁩'s last four words, not only ⁨أبو بكر ؓ⁩'s oath. This is the single strongest "disagreement inside
the Companions, resolved without rupture" item in the whole session. **Say "it is in the Six except
⁨ابن ماجه⁩", not "it is in the Six"** — that is what the page itself says, and the takhrīj above lists
five collections, not six.

The parallel through ⁨القاسم بن محمد⁩, one clause, ⁨البدایہ ج۷ ص۱۹⁩ — useful as a cue-card line:
**«⁨لو مَنَعوني عِقالًا لجاهَدْتُهم⁩»** (https://shamela.ws/book/30097/3165).

## 1.7 The ⁨ردة⁩ address — ⁨البدایہ ج۷ ص۱۸⁩–۱۹

**Setting.** The delegations have gone home. ⁨أبو بكر ؓ⁩ stands in the people to explain what is about
to be done. Transmitted by ⁨ابن عساكر⁩ through ⁨صالح بن كيسان⁩.

> الحمدُ الله الذي هَدى فكَفَى، وأعْطى فأغْنَى، إن اللهَ بعثَ محمدًا ﷺ والعلم شريد، والإسلام غريبٌ طريدٌ،
> قد رثَّ حبلُه، وخَلقَ عهدُه، وضلَّ أهلُه منه …
> — ⁨البدایہ والنہایہ ج۷ ص۱۸⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/30097/3164
> *English:* "Praise be to God, who guided and so sufficed, and gave and so enriched. God sent
> Muḥammad ﷺ when knowledge was a fugitive and Islam a stranger driven out; its rope was frayed, its
> covenant worn thin, and its people had strayed from it …"

and the operative sentence, on the following page:

> والله لا أدَعُ أن أقاتلَ على أمر الله حتى يُنْجزَ اللهُ وعدَه، ويوفي لنا عهدَه
> — ⁨البدایہ والنہایہ ج۷ ص۱۹⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/30097/3165
> *English:* "By God, I will not give up fighting for God's command until God fulfils His promise and
> keeps His covenant with us."

**Reading aloud:** the opening rhymed pair «⁨هَدى فكَفَى، وأعْطى فأغْنَى⁩» is four words and is a
beautiful slide on its own (~4 seconds). The full opening is ~18 seconds. The operative sentence is
~8 seconds.

## 1.8 The address before the Syrian expedition — ⁨البدایہ ج۷ ص۸۳⁩

**Setting.** ⁨۱۳ھ⁩. The armies are assembled in Medina for ⁨الشام⁩. This is ⁨أبو بكر ؓ⁩'s send-off.

> ألا [إنّ] (٥) لكلِّ أمرٍ جوامع، فمنْ بلغَها فهي حسبُه، ومن عملَ للّه كفاهُ اللهُ، عليكم بالجدِّ والقصد
> فإنَّ القصدَ أبلغُ، ألا إنه لا دينَ لأحدٍ لا إيمانَ له، ولا إيمانَ لمنْ لا حسبه (٦) له، ولا عملَ لمن لا
> نيَّة له
> — ⁨البدایہ والنہایہ ج۷ ص۸۳⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/30097/3229
> *English:* "Every matter has its summary points; whoever reaches them, they suffice him. Whoever
> works for God, God suffices him. Take to earnestness and to moderation, for moderation carries
> further. There is no religion for one who has no faith; no faith for one who has no reckoning with
> himself; and no deed for one who has no intention."

⚠ The Shamela line prints «⁨ولا إيمانَ لمنْ لا⁩ **⁨حسبه⁩** ⁨له⁩» with the editor noting a variant
«⁨ولا أجر لمن لا حسبة له⁩». The reading «⁨حِسْبة⁩» is the one that makes sense. **Check the page image.**
⚠ Via ⁨سيف⁩/⁨الطبری⁩; the editor footnotes ⁨تاريخ الطبري ٣⁩/٣٩٠.

**Reading aloud:** ~20 seconds. The final tricolon alone («⁨لا دينَ⁩ … ⁨ولا عملَ لمن لا نيَّةَ له⁩») is
~8 seconds and is the slide.

## 1.9 Equality in the stipend — ⁨الکامل ج۲ ص۲۶۵⁩

**Setting.** ⁨أبو بكر ؓ⁩ divided the ʿaṭāʾ equally between the earliest Muslims and the latest, between
free and slave, male and female. He was asked to grade it by precedence. **This is the exact policy
⁨عمر ؓ⁩ later reversed, which is why the pair is worth teaching.**

> فَقَالَ: إِنَّمَا أَسْلَمُوا لِلَّهِ، وَوَجَبَ أَجْرُهُمْ عَلَيْهِ يُوَفِّيهِمْ ذَلِكَ فِي الْآخِرَةِ،
> وَإِنَّمَا هَذِهِ الدُّنْيَا بَلَاغ
> — ⁨الکامل فی التاریخ ج۲ ص۲۶۵⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/21712/956
> *English:* "They accepted Islam for God, and their reward falls due upon Him; He will pay it to
> them in full in the next world. This present world is only a provision to get by on."

**Reading aloud:** ~10 seconds. Excellent slide, and it needs no comment from the speaker.

## 1.10 «I will still milk your sheep» — ⁨الکامل ج۲ ص۲۶۶⁩

**Setting.** ⁨أبو بكر ؓ⁩ used to milk the flocks of his neighbourhood in ⁨السُّنْح⁩. When the bayʿah was
given, a girl of the quarter said: *now he will not milk our sheep for us any more.*

> فَسَمِعَهَا فَقَالَ: بَلَى لَعَمْرِي لَأَحْلِبَنَّهَا لَكُمْ، وَإِنِّي لَأَرْجُو أَنْ لَا يُغَيِّرَ بِي
> مَا دَخَلْتُ فِيه
> — ⁨الکامل فی التاریخ ج۲ ص۲۶۶⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/21712/957
> *English:* He heard her and said: "On the contrary — by my life, I shall certainly milk them for
> you. And I hope that what I have entered into will not change me."

**Reading aloud:** ~9 seconds. One of the two or three warmest items in the note.

Six months later he moved into Medina and left trade, and the reason is on the same page:

> مَا تَصْلُحُ أُمُورُ النَّاسِ مَعَ التِّجَارَةِ، وَمَا يَصْلُحُ إِلَّا التَّفَرُّغُ لَهُمْ وَالنَّظَرُ
> فِي شَأْنِهِم
> — ⁨الکامل فی التاریخ ج۲ ص۲۶۶⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/21712/957
> *English:* "People's affairs cannot be run properly alongside trade. Nothing serves but to be free
> for them and to look into their business."

## 1.11 The dying instructions — ⁨الکامل ج۲ ص۲۶۲⁩, ۲۶۳, ۲۶۵

**(a) On his shroud** — ⁨ج۲ ص۲۶۲⁩: he directed that he be wrapped in the two garments he had, with a
third bought to go with them, and said:

> الْحَيُّ أَحْوَجُ إِلَى الْجَدِيدِ مِنَ الْمَيِّتِ، إِنَّمَا هُوَ لِلْمُهْلَةِ وَالصَّدِيد
> — ⁨الکامل فی التاریخ ج۲ ص۲۶۲⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/21712/953
> *English:* "The living have more need of what is new than the dead. This is only for the rot and
> the discharge."

**(b) His last words** — ⁨ج۲ ص۲۶۳⁩: «⁨وَكَانَ آخِرُ مَا تَكَلَّمَ بِهِ⁩:»

> تَوَفَّنِي مُسْلِمًا وَأَلْحِقْنِي بِالصَّالِحِين
> — ⁨الکامل فی التاریخ ج۲ ص۲۶۳⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/21712/954
> *English:* "Take me in Islam, and join me to the righteous."

**(c) The audit of his own caliphate**, to ⁨أم المؤمنين عائشة ؓ⁩ — ⁨ج۲ ص۲۶۵⁩. **This is the strongest
single item in the ⁨أبو بكر ؓ⁩ section and it ends with ⁨عمر ؓ⁩ in tears:**

> أَمَا إِنَّا مُنْذُ وَلِينَا أَمْرَ الْمُسْلِمِينَ لَمْ نَأْكُلْ لَهُمْ دِينَارًا وَلَا دِرْهَمًا،
> وَلَكِنَّا قَدْ أَكَلْنَا مِنْ جَرِيشِ طَعَامِهِمْ، وَلَبِسْنَا مَنْ خَشِنِ ثِيَابِهِمْ، وَلَيْسَ
> عِنْدَنَا مِنْ فَيْءِ الْمُسْلِمِينَ إِلَّا هَذَا الْعَبْدُ وَهَذَا الْبَعِيرُ وَهَذِهِ الْقَطِيفَةُ،
> فَإِذَا مُتُّ فَابْعَثِي بِالْجَمِيعِ إِلَى عُمَر
> — ⁨الکامل فی التاریخ ج۲ ص۲۶۵⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/21712/956
> *English:* "Since we took charge of the Muslims' affairs we have not eaten a dinar or a dirham of
> theirs. But we have eaten of their coarse food and worn of their rough clothing, and there is
> nothing of the Muslims' revenue with us except this slave, this camel and this blanket. When I die,
> send the lot of it to ʿUmar."

and, on the same page, ⁨عمر ؓ⁩'s reaction when the three items arrived:

> فَلَمَّا رَآهُ بَكَى حَتَّى سَالَتْ دُمُوعُهُ إِلَى الْأَرْضِ وَجَعَلَ يَقُولُ: رَحِمَ اللَّهُ أَبَا
> بَكْرٍ! لَقَدْ أَتْعَبَ مَنْ بَعْدَه
> — ⁨الکامل فی التاریخ ج۲ ص۲۶۵⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/21712/956
> *English:* When he saw it he wept until his tears ran to the ground, and kept saying: "God have
> mercy on Abū Bakr! He has worn out whoever comes after him."

**Reading aloud:** (c) is ~25 seconds for the will and ~10 for ⁨عمر ؓ⁩'s response. **Deliver them as
one beat.** The last five words are the entire hinge of the evening from ⁨أبو بكر ؓ⁩ to ⁨عمر ؓ⁩.

⁨عبد الرحمن بن عوف ؓ⁩ objected that a slave, a water-camel and a threadbare blanket worth five dirhams
should be returned to the family; ⁨عمر ؓ⁩ refused, on the same page: «⁨لَا وَالَّذِي بَعَثَ مُحَمَّدًا ﷺ، لَا⁩
⁨يَكُونُ هَذَا فِي وِلَايَتِي، وَلَا خَرَجَ أَبُو بَكْرٍ مِنْهُ وَأَتَقَلَّدُهُ أَنَا⁩» — *"No, by Him who sent Muḥammad ﷺ.
This will not happen under my authority: Abū Bakr got clear of it and I take it on my own neck?"*

## 1.12 The succession to ⁨عمر ؓ⁩ — the consultation, the objection, the covenant

**(a) The soundings** — ⁨الکامل ج۲ ص۲۶۶⁩–۲۶۷. He asked ⁨عبد الرحمن بن عوف ؓ⁩ about ⁨عمر ؓ⁩, who answered
that he was better than ⁨أبو بكر ؓ⁩'s own opinion of him, «⁨إِلَّا أَنَّهُ فِيهِ غِلْظَةٌ⁩» — "except that there
is a harshness in him." ⁨أبو بكر ؓ⁩'s answer is the psychological observation of the whole session:

> ذَلِكَ لِأَنَّهُ يَرَانِي رَقِيقًا، وَلَوْ أَفْضَى الْأَمْرُ إِلَيْهِ لَتَرَكَ كَثِيرًا مِمَّا هُوَ
> عَلَيْهِ، وَقَدْ رَمَقْتُهُ فَكُنْتُ إِذَا غَضِبْتُ عَلَى رَجُلٍ أَرَانِي الرِّضَاءَ عَنْهُ، وَإِذَا
> لِنْتُ لَهُ أَرَانِي الشِّدَّةَ عَلَيْه
> — ⁨الکامل فی التاریخ ج۲ ص۲۶۶⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/21712/957
> *English:* "That is because he sees me as soft. If the office passed to him he would drop much of
> what he does now. I have watched him closely: when I was angry with a man he would show me
> contentment with him, and when I was gentle with a man he would show me severity toward him."

⁨عثمان ؓ⁩, asked the same question, answered — same page-run, ⁨ج۲ ص۲۶۷⁩: «⁨سَرِيرَتُهُ خَيْرٌ مِنْ عَلَانِيَتِهِ،⁩
⁨وَلَيْسَ فِينَا مِثْلُهُ⁩» ("his inward is better than his outward, and there is none among us like him").

**(b) ⁨طلحة ؓ⁩'s objection, and the answer** — ⁨الکامل ج۲ ص۲۶۷⁩. **This is a recorded dialogue of the
first rank and it is entirely safe: a Companion objects, the caliph answers, nobody is impugned.**

> فَقَالَ أَبُو بَكْرٍ: أَجْلِسُونِي، فَأَجْلَسُوهُ، فَقَالَ: أَبِاللَّهِ تُخَوِّفُنِي! إِذَا لَقِيتُ
> رَبِّي فَسَأَلَنِي قُلْتُ: اسْتَخْلَفْتُ عَلَى أَهْلِكَ خَيْرَ أَهْلِك
> — ⁨الکامل فی التاریخ ج۲ ص۲۶۷⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/21712/958
> *English:* Abū Bakr said: "Sit me up." They sat him up, and he said: "Is it with God that you
> frighten me? When I meet my Lord and He asks me, I shall say: *I appointed over Your people the
> best of Your people.*"

⁨طلحة ؓ⁩'s words, immediately before, on the same page: «⁨اسْتَخْلَفْتَ عَلَى النَّاسِ عُمَرَ وَقَدْ رَأَيْتَ مَا⁩
يَلْقَى النَّاسُ مِنْهُ وَأَنْتَ مَعَهُ، وَكَيْفَ بِهِ إِذَا خَلَا بِهِمْ وَأَنْتَ لَاقٍ رَبَّكَ فَسَائِلُكَ عَنْ رَعِيَّتِكَ!»
— *"You have appointed ʿUmar over the people, and you have seen what people get from him while you
are there beside him. What of him when he is alone with them — and you are going to meet your Lord,
and He will ask you about your flock?"*

**Reading aloud:** the pair is ~20 seconds and needs both halves.

**(c) The covenant, and ⁨عثمان ؓ⁩ writing through the faint** — ⁨الکامل ج۲ ص۲۶۷⁩. ⁨أبو بكر ؓ⁩ dictated
«⁨بِسْمِ اللَّهِ الرَّحْمَنِ الرَّحِيمِ، هَذَا مَا عَهِدَ أَبُو بَكْرِ بْنُ أَبِي قُحَافَةَ إِلَى الْمُسْلِمِينَ، أَمَّا بَعْدُ⁩ …» and then
lost consciousness. ⁨عثمان ؓ⁩ completed the sentence himself: «⁨فَإِنِّي قَدِ اسْتَخْلَفْتُ عَلَيْكُمْ عُمَرَ بْنَ⁩
⁨الْخَطَّابِ، وَلَمْ آلُكُمْ خَيْرًا⁩». When ⁨أبو بكر ؓ⁩ came round and had it read back to him he said takbīr
and said:

> أَرَاكَ خِفْتَ أَنْ يَخْتَلِفَ النَّاسُ إِنْ مُتُّ فِي غَشْيَتِي. قَالَ: نَعَمْ. قَالَ: جَزَاكَ اللَّهُ
> خَيْرًا عَنِ الْإِسْلَامِ وَأَهْلِه
> — ⁨الکامل فی التاریخ ج۲ ص۲۶۷⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/21712/958
> *English:* "I see you feared the people would split if I died in my faint." He said: "Yes." He said:
> "God reward you well on behalf of Islam and its people."

**(d) The address to the people** — same page: «⁨أَتَرْضُونَ بِمَنِ اسْتَخْلَفْتُ عَلَيْكُمْ؟ فَإِنِّي مَا⁩
⁨اسْتَخْلَفْتُ عَلَيْكُمْ ذَا قَرَابَةٍ⁩ … ⁨فَإِنِّي وَاللَّهِ مَا أَلَوْتُ مِنْ جُهْدِ الرَّأْيِ⁩» — *"Are you content with
the one I have appointed over you? I have not appointed a relative of mine over you … By God, I have
spared no effort of judgement."* Note the clause **«⁨ما استخلفتُ عليكم ذا قرابة⁩»**; it is short, it is
on the page, and it is the honest, non-polemical way to make the point about kinship.

**(e) The private charge to ⁨عمر ؓ⁩** — ⁨الکامل ج۲ ص۲۶۷⁩–۲۶۸, the longest continuous piece of ⁨أبو بكر ؓ⁩'s
speech we hold. The usable core:

> يَا عُمَرُ، إِنَّ لِلَّهِ حَقًّا بِاللَّيْلِ لَا يَقْبَلُهُ فِي النَّهَارِ، وَحَقًّا فِي النَّهَارِ لَا
> يَقْبَلُهُ بِاللَّيْلِ، وَإِنَّهُ لَا يَقْبَلُ نَافِلَةً حَتَّى تُؤَدَّى الْفَرِيضَة
> — ⁨الکامل فی التاریخ ج۲ ص۲۶۷⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/21712/958
> *English:* "ʿUmar — God has a due by night that He does not accept by day, and a due by day that He
> does not accept by night; and He accepts no voluntary act until the obligation has been discharged."

and its closing line, ⁨ج۲ ص۲۶۸⁩:

> فَإِنْ حَفِظْتَ وَصِيَّتِي فَلَا يَكُونَنَّ غَائِبٌ أَحَبَّ إِلَيْكَ مِنْ حَاضِرٍ مِنَ الْمَوْتِ،
> وَلَسْتَ بِمُعْجِزِه
> — ⁨الکامل فی التاریخ ج۲ ص۲۶۸⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/21712/959
> *English:* "If you keep my charge, then let nothing absent be dearer to you than death, which is
> present — and you will not escape it."

**Reading aloud:** the two extracts are ~14 and ~9 seconds. The full charge is over two minutes and
should not be read aloud.

## 1.13 Two more, short and safe

**«I was never eager for the command»** — ⁨البدایہ ج۷ ص۷⁩, from ⁨موسى بن عقبة⁩'s *Maghāzī*:

> واللهِ ما كنتُ حريصًا على الإمارةِ يومًا ولا ليلةً، ولا سألتُها اللهَ في سِرٍّ ولا علانية
> — ⁨البدایہ والنہایہ ج۷ ص۷⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/30097/3153
> *English:* "By God, I was never eager for the command a day or a night, and I never asked God for it
> in secret or openly."

**The blind woman** — ⁨الکامل ج۲ ص۲۶۵⁩. ⁨عمر ؓ⁩ used to look after a blind woman in Medina by night, and
kept finding that someone had got there before him and done everything she needed. He lay in wait,
and it was ⁨أبو بكر ؓ⁩ — still doing it while he was caliph.

> فَقَالَ لَهُ: أَنْتَ هُوَ لَعَمْرِي
> — ⁨الکامل فی التاریخ ج۲ ص۲۶۵⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/21712/956
> *English:* He said to him: "It is you — by my life!"

**Reading aloud:** four words. It is the best short slide in the note.

## 1.14 The charge to ⁨يزيد بن أبي سفيان ؓ⁩ — ⁨الکامل ج۲ ص۲۴۹⁩–۲۵۰ · **PLAIN**

**Setting.** ⁨۱۳ھ⁩. ⁨أبو بكر ؓ⁩ has put ⁨يزيد بن أبي سفيان ؓ⁩ over the largest of the Syrian columns —
⁨سهيل بن عمرو ؓ⁩ and the men of Mecca are in it — and he walks the column out of Medina on foot.
This is the item the strand asked for and it is only in ⁨الکامل⁩. **⁨ابن الأثير⁩ signs it off himself
on the same page: «⁨وَهَذِهِ مِنْ أَحْسَنِ الْوَصَايَا وَأَكْثَرِهَا نَفْعًا لِوُلَاةِ الْأَمْرِ⁩»** — *"this is
among the best charges ever given, and the most useful to those who hold office."* A source
flagging its own item as first-rank is exactly what §Y of this note is for.

**The opening — the whole theory of appointment in one sentence:**

> إِنِّي قَدْ وَلَّيْتُكَ لِأَبْلُوَكَ وَأُجَرِّبَكَ وَأُخَرِّجَكَ، فَإِنْ أَحْسَنْتَ رَدَدْتُكَ إِلَى
> عَمَلِكَ وَزِدْتُكَ، وَإِنْ أَسَأْتَ عَزَلْتُك
> — ⁨الکامل فی التاریخ ج۲ ص۲۴۹⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/21712/940
> *English:* "I have put you in office to test you, to try you and to bring you out. If you do well
> I will return you to your post and add to it; and if you do badly I will dismiss you."

**The clause that belongs with the tribal-map slides:**

> فَإِيَّاكَ وَعُبِّيَّةَ الْجَاهِلِيَّةِ، فَإِنَّ اللَّهَ يُبْغِضُهَا وَيُبْغِضُ أَهْلَهَا
> — ⁨الکامل فی التاریخ ج۲ ص۲۴۹⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/21712/940
> *English:* "Beware the arrogance of the Jāhiliyya — God hates it, and hates those who carry it."

**Three short rules of command, from the same page-run, any one of which is a slide on its own:**

> وَإِذَا وَعَظْتَهُمْ فَأَوْجِزْ؛ فَإِنَّ كَثِيرَ الْكَلَامِ يُنْسِي بَعْضُهُ بَعْضًا، وَأَصْلِحْ
> نَفْسَكَ يَصْلُحْ لَكَ النَّاس
> — ⁨الکامل فی التاریخ ج۲ ص۲۴۹⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/21712/940
> *English:* "When you exhort them, be brief — much speech makes one part of it forget another. Put
> yourself right, and the people will come right for you."

> وَلَا تَجَسَّسْ عَلَيْهِمْ فَتَفْضَحَهُمْ، وَلَا تَكْشِفِ النَّاسَ عَنْ أَسْرَارِهِمْ، وَاكْتَفِ
> بِعَلَانِيَتِهِم
> — ⁨الکامل فی التاریخ ج۲ ص۲۵۰⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/21712/941
> *English:* "Do not spy on them and so expose them. Do not strip people of their secrets. Their
> outward conduct is enough for you."

> وَلَا تَجْبُنْ فَيَجْبُنَ النَّاسُ، وَاجْتَنِبِ الْغُلُولَ فَإِنَّهُ يُقَرِّبُ الْفَقْرَ وَيَدْفَعُ
> النَّصْر
> — ⁨الکامل فی التاریخ ج۲ ص۲۵۰⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/21712/941
> *English:* "Do not be a coward, or the people will be cowards. Keep away from stealing from the
> spoils — it brings poverty near and pushes victory away."

And the charge closes on the monks, exactly as the ⁨أسامة ؓ⁩ charge did (§1.5) — **but here, at
⁨ج۲ ص۲۵۰⁩, there is no accompanying order to strike anyone**, so this wording is the safer of the two
to quote:

> وَسَتَجِدُونَ أَقْوَامًا حَبَسُوا أَنْفُسَهُمْ فِي الصَّوَامِعِ فَدَعْهُمْ وَمَا حَبَسُوا أَنْفُسَهُمْ
> لَه
> — ⁨الکامل فی التاریخ ج۲ ص۲۵۰⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/21712/941
> *English:* "You will find people who have shut themselves into cells — leave them, and leave them
> to what they have shut themselves in for."

**Reading aloud.** The full charge is about two and a half minutes and must not be read whole. The
opening sentence is ~9 seconds; «⁨إياك وعبية الجاهلية⁩» ~6; «⁨وأصلح نفسك يصلح لك الناس⁩» ~4 and is the
best four seconds in the section.

## 1.15 Two letters that are dialogue, not oratory — ⁨الکامل ج۲ ص۲۴۹⁩ and ⁨ص۲۵۱⁩ · **PLAIN**

**(a) ⁨عمرو بن العاص ؓ⁩ answers his posting order.** ⁨أبو بكر ؓ⁩ wrote offering him the Syrian command
and expressly leaving him free to keep the post he already held: «⁨إِلَّا أَنْ يَكُونَ الَّذِي أَنْتَ فِيهِ⁩
⁨أَحَبَّ إِلَيْكَ⁩». ⁨عمرو ؓ⁩ wrote back:

> إِنِّي سَهْمٌ مِنْ سِهَامِ الْإِسْلَامِ، وَأَنْتَ بَعْدَ اللَّهِ الرَّامِي بِهَا وَالْجَامِعُ لَهَا،
> فَانْظُرْ أَشَدَّهَا وَأَخْشَاهَا وَأَفْضَلَهَا فَارْمِ بِه
> — ⁨الکامل فی التاریخ ج۲ ص۲۴۹⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/21712/940
> *English:* "I am one arrow of the arrows of Islam, and you — after God — are the one who shoots
> them and the one who gathers them. So look for the hardest of them, and the most feared, and the
> best, and shoot it."

**Reading aloud:** ~11 seconds. It is the best short exchange in the ⁨أبو بكر ؓ⁩ section, and it is
a Companion volunteering to be spent.

**(b) The commanders at ⁨اليرموك⁩ write asking what to do, outnumbered.** ⁨عمرو ؓ⁩'s own answer to
them was *concentrate*. Then ⁨أبو بكر ؓ⁩'s answer arrived, saying the same thing and adding the
sentence:

> إِنَّ مِثْلَكُمْ لَا يُؤْتَى مِنْ قِلَّةٍ، وَإِنَّمَا يُؤْتَى الْعَشْرَةُ آلَافٍ مِنَ الذُّنُوبِ،
> فَاحْتَرِسُوا مِنْهَا
> — ⁨الکامل فی التاریخ ج۲ ص۲۵۱⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/21712/942
> *English:* "Men like you are not overcome by being few. Ten thousand are overcome by their sins —
> so guard yourselves against them."

**Reading aloud:** ~10 seconds. One slide. It is the caliph refusing to discuss numbers at all.

# 2. عمر الفاروق ؓ

The ⁨عمر ؓ⁩ material is far larger than the ⁨أبو بكر ؓ⁩ material, and it divides cleanly into five
things a slide can be about: **the office**, **the money**, **the governors**, **the famine**, and
**the dying**. Kept in that order below.

## 2.1 The first address as caliph — ⁨الکامل ج۲ ص۲۶۸⁩ · **PLAIN**

**Setting.** ⁨أبو بكر ؓ⁩ has just been buried. ⁨عمر ؓ⁩ goes up and speaks. **This is the only wording of
⁨عمر ؓ⁩'s inaugural on the pages we hold — ⁨البدایہ⁩ carries none on any ⁨البدایہ⁩ page in the cache.**
(That is a statement about the cache, not about the book; see the note at the head of §X.)

⚠ **⁨الکامل⁩ prints the same sentence twice, with different pronouns.** At ⁨ج۲ ص۲۶۸⁩ it is the accession
speech, «⁨لَأَحْمِلَنَّ⁩**⁨كُمْ⁩** ⁨عَلَى الطَّرِيق⁩» — *I will carry* **you** *onto the road*. At
⁨ج۲ ص۴۳۲⁩ (https://shamela.ws/book/21712/1122), inside a «⁨ذكر بعض سيرته⁩» miscellany and **not** framed
as an address, it reads «⁨قَالَ عُمَرُ⁩: … ⁨فَأَمَّا أَنَا فَوَرَبِّ الْكَعْبَةِ لَأَحْمِلَنَّ⁩**⁨هُمْ⁩**
⁨عَلَى الطَّرِيقِ⁩» — *I will carry* **them**. **Quote the ⁨ص۲۶۸⁩ page if the point is the inaugural**,
and do not mix the two pronouns.

> إِنَّمَا مَثَلُ الْعَرَبِ مَثَلُ جَمَلٍ أَنِفٍ اتَّبَعَ قَائِدَهُ، فَلْيَنْظُرْ قَائِدُهُ حَيْثُ
> يَقُودُهُ، وَأَمَّا أَنَا فَوَرَبِّ الْكَعْبَةِ لَأَحْمِلَنَّكُمْ عَلَى الطَّرِيق
> — ⁨الکامل فی التاریخ ج۲ ص۲۶۸⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/21712/959
> *English:* "The Arabs are like a camel with a ring in its nose that follows its driver — so let
> its driver look where he is driving it. As for me: by the Lord of the Kaʿba, I will carry you onto
> the road."

**Reading aloud:** ~14 seconds; one slide. **Note what it is not.** It is not humility-language like
⁨أبو بكر ؓ⁩'s. It is a driver's sentence, and putting the two inaugurals side by side is the single
cleanest way to show the room that these were two different men doing the same job. That contrast
is the point; the speaker does not have to say anything more.

## 2.2 The name ⁨أمير المؤمنين⁩ — ⁨البدایہ ج۷ ص۲۶۸⁩ · **VIA ⁨ابن جرير⁩**

**Setting.** «⁨خليفة خليفة رسول الله⁩» is what people had begun calling him, and the title was already
one link too long.

> لما ولي عمر قالوا: يا خليفةَ خليفةِ رسولِ الله. فقال عمر: هذا أمرٌ يطولُ، بل أنتم المؤمنون وأنا أميركم.
> فسمي أمير المؤمنين
> — ⁨البدایہ والنہایہ ج۷ ص۲۶۸⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/30097/3414
> *English:* When ʿUmar took office they said: "Successor of the Successor of the Messenger of God."
> ʿUmar said: **"This is a thing that will go on getting longer. Rather — you are the believers, and
> I am your commander."** So he was called *Commander of the Believers*.

⚠ ⁨ابن كثير⁩ gives it from **⁨ابن جرير⁩**, and the chain runs through a woman of Kufa said to have
been a hundred and thirty years old. **Report it as "it is related"; do not date it.** Who greeted
him with the title first is itself disputed on the same page: «⁨وأوَّلُ من حَيَّاهُ بها المغيرةُ بن شعبة،⁩
**⁨وقيل غيره، فالله أعلم⁩**» (⁨ج۷ ص۲۶۸⁩), while ⁨الزهري⁩ says flatly it was ⁨المغيرة ؓ⁩
(⁨سیر⁩ (⁨راشدون⁩) ⁨ص۷۹⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/10906/1224). **Give both; do not adjudicate.**

**Reading aloud:** ~11 seconds. Excellent slide, and it is the origin of a phrase the room hears
every week.

## 2.3 On the office itself — four sayings, four pages

**(a) He would rather have his neck struck than hold it** — ⁨سیر⁩ (⁨راشدون⁩) ⁨ص۷۹⁩–۸۰ · **PLAIN**
(القاسم بن محمد):

> ليعلم من ولي هذا الأمر من بعدي أن سيريده عنه القريب والبعيد، أني لأقاتل الناس عن نفسي قتالا، ولو علمت أن
> أحدا أقوى عليه مني لكنت أن أقدم فتضرب عنقي أحب إلي من أن أليه
> — ⁨سیر أعلام النبلاء⁩ (⁨سير الخلفاء الراشدين⁩) ⁨ص۷۹⁩–۸۰ · https://shamela.ws/book/10906/1224 ⁨و⁩ 1225
> ⚠ **This one straddles the page break.** «… ⁨أني لأقاتل الناس عن نفسي قتالا⁩» ends ⁨ص۷۹⁩ (index 1224);
> «⁨ولو علمت أن أحدا أقوى عليه مني⁩ …» opens ⁨ص۸۰⁩ (index 1225). Both indices are cached.
> *English:* "Let whoever holds this office after me know that near and far alike will try to turn
> it away from him. I fight people off myself, truly — and had I known of any man stronger for it
> than I am, I would sooner be brought forward and have my neck struck than hold it."

**(b) When he was told people call him harsh** — ⁨سیر⁩ (⁨راشدون⁩) ⁨ص۸۰⁩ · **PLAIN** (⁨ابن عباس ؓ⁩):

> قال: يزعمون أنك فظ غليظ. قال: الحمد لله الذي ملأ قلبي لهم رحما وملأ قلوبهم لي رعبا
> — ⁨سیر أعلام النبلاء⁩ (⁨سير الخلفاء الراشدين⁩) ⁨ص۸۰⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/10906/1225
> *English:* It was said to him: "They claim you are harsh and rough." He said: **"Praise be to God,
> who filled my heart with mercy toward them and filled their hearts with awe of me."**

⚠ **Use the ⁨سیر⁩ wording.** ⁨البدایہ⁩ carries the same saying at ⁨ج۷ ص۲۶۴⁩, but that Shamela line is
damaged — it prints «⁨وفيل لعمر⁩: ⁨إنك قضاء⁩» where the sense requires «⁨وقيل لعمر⁩: ⁨إنك فظٌّ غليظ⁩». One
of the clearest cases in this note of §0.2 doing real work.

**(c) Talking to himself behind a garden wall** — ⁨سیر⁩ (⁨راشدون⁩) ⁨ص۸۳⁩ · **PLAIN** (⁨أنس ؓ⁩); the same
scene is at ⁨البدایہ ج۷ ص۲۶۵⁩:

> عمر بن الخطاب أمير المؤمنين بخ، والله لتتقين الله بني الخطاب أو ليعذبنك
> — ⁨سیر أعلام النبلاء⁩ (⁨سير الخلفاء الراشدين⁩) ⁨ص۸۳⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/10906/1228
> · ⁨البدایہ والنہایہ ج۷ ص۲۶۵⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/30097/3411
> *English:* "ʿUmar ibn al-Khaṭṭāb, Commander of the Believers. Well done! By God, you will fear God,
> little son of al-Khaṭṭāb — or He will punish you."

⁨أنس ؓ⁩ was on the other side of the wall and heard it. **This is the strongest single item in the
whole note on "what he said when praised", because the praise is his own and he answers it himself.**

**(d) The waterskin** — ⁨سیر⁩ (⁨راشدون⁩) ⁨ص۸۳⁩ · **PLAIN**:

> إن نفسي أعجبتني فأردت أن أذلها
> — ⁨سیر أعلام النبلاء⁩ (⁨سير الخلفاء الراشدين⁩) ⁨ص۸۳⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/10906/1228
> *English:* (asked why he was carrying a waterskin on his own shoulder) "My soul pleased itself with
> me, so I wanted to humble it."

**Reading aloud:** (b) ~9 s · (c) ~10 s · (d) ~5 s. (c) and (d) together make one slide and one
beat, about twenty seconds, and they are the best twenty seconds in section 2.

## 2.4 On the money — the self-limit, and the son who made a profit

**(a) What the caliph may take** — ⁨سیر⁩ (⁨راشدون⁩) ⁨ص۸۰⁩ · **PLAIN** (⁨الأحنف بن قيس⁩); also ⁨البدایہ ج۷ ص۲۶۴⁩:

> لا يحل لعمر من مال الله إلا حلتين: حلة للشتاء وحلة للصيف، وما حج به واعتمر، وقوت أهلي كرجل من قريش ليس
> بأغناهم، ثم أنا رجل من المسلمين
> — ⁨سیر أعلام النبلاء⁩ (⁨سير الخلفاء الراشدين⁩) ⁨ص۸۰⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/10906/1225
> *English:* "Nothing of God's wealth is lawful to ʿUmar except two sets of clothes — one for winter
> and one for summer — and what he performs Ḥajj and ʿUmra with, and my family's food at the level
> of a man of Quraysh who is not the richest of them. After that I am one of the Muslims."

⚠ **The wording above is ⁨سیر⁩'s. ⁨البدایہ ج۷ ص۲۶۴⁩ (https://shamela.ws/book/30097/3410) carries the
same saying but not the same words**: «⁨لا يحلُّ⁩ **⁨لي⁩** ⁨من مال الله إلا⁩ **⁨حلَّتان⁩** ⁨حلَّة للشتاء وحلَّة⁩
⁨للصيف، وقوت أهلي كرجل من قريش ليس بأغناهم، ثم أنا رجل من المسلمين⁩» — first person «⁨لي⁩» not «⁨لعمر⁩»,
«⁨حلَّتان⁩» not «⁨حلتين⁩», and **no «⁨وما حج به واعتمر⁩» clause at all.** Quote ⁨سیر⁩, and do not present the
⁨البدایہ⁩ page as carrying the ḥajj clause.

**Reading aloud:** ~16 seconds. It is a budget, read aloud, by the man it constrains. **Two of the
three sources carry the saying; only ⁨سیر⁩ carries this wording.**

**(b) ⁨عبد الله بن عمر ؓ⁩'s forty thousand** — ⁨سیر⁩ (⁨راشدون⁩) ⁨ص۸۳⁩–۸۴ · **PLAIN**. His son bought goods
out of the spoils at ⁨جلولاء⁩ for forty thousand and came to Medina. ⁨عمر ؓ⁩ asked him first whether,
if he were shown the Fire and told *ransom yourself*, he would give it — and then explained the real
objection:

> كأني شاهد الناس حين تبايعوا فقالوا: عبد الله بن عمر صاحب رسول الله صلى الله عليه وسلم وابن أمير المؤمنين
> وأحب الناس إليه، وأنت كذلك فكان أن يرخصوا عليك أحب إليهم من أن يغلوا عليك، وإني قاسم مسئول وأنا معطيك
> أكثر ما ربح تاجر من قريش، لك ربح الدرهم درهم
> — ⁨سیر أعلام النبلاء⁩ (⁨سير الخلفاء الراشدين⁩) ⁨ص۸۳⁩–۸۴ · https://shamela.ws/book/10906/1228 ⁨و⁩ 1229
> *English:* "It is as if I were standing among the people when they traded with you and said: *this
> is ʿAbdullāh son of ʿUmar, Companion of the Messenger of God ﷺ, son of the Commander of the
> Believers, the dearest of people to him* — and so you are. It was dearer to them to go cheap on you
> than to go dear on you. **And I am a divider, and I will be asked.** So I will give you more than
> any Qurashī merchant ever made: **for every dirham, one dirham of profit.**"

He then had the traders buy the goods for four hundred thousand, handed his son eighty thousand,
and sent the rest to ⁨سعد بن أبي وقاص ؓ⁩ to be divided (⁨ص۸۴⁩).

**Reading aloud:** ~26 seconds for the whole; **~7 seconds for the two bolded clauses alone**, which
is the slide. This is the strongest governance item in the note and it needs no comment.

**(c) The son-in-law** — ⁨سیر⁩ (⁨راشدون⁩) ⁨ص۸۴⁩ · **PLAIN** (⁨ابن سيرين⁩). A relative by marriage came
asking for something out of ⁨بيت المال⁩:

> فانتهره عمر، وقال: أردت أن ألقى الله ملكا خائنا!؟ فلما كان بعد ذلك أعطاه من صلب ماله عشرة آلاف درهم
> — ⁨سیر أعلام النبلاء⁩ (⁨سير الخلفاء الراشدين⁩) ⁨ص۸۴⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/10906/1229
> *English:* ʿUmar rebuffed him and said: **"You wanted me to meet God a treacherous king?"** Then
> afterwards he gave him ten thousand dirhams **out of his own money**.

**The second half is what makes it a slide** — the refusal is not meanness; the money comes out of
his own pocket. ~13 seconds.

**(d) The treasure of Kisrā laid out in the mosque** — ⁨سیر⁩ (⁨راشدون⁩) ⁨ص۸۵⁩ · **PLAIN**. It was brought
in and ⁨عبد الله بن الأرقم⁩ asked whether to store it in the treasury until it could be divided.

> لا والله لا آويها إلى سقف حتى أمضيها
> — ⁨سیر أعلام النبلاء⁩ (⁨سير الخلفاء الراشدين⁩) ⁨ص۸۵⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/10906/1230
> *English:* "No, by God — I will not put it under a roof until I have sent it on its way."

They put it in the middle of the mosque and guarded it overnight. In the morning he uncovered it,
saw the red gold and the white silver almost glittering, **and wept**. ⁨أُبَيّ ؓ⁩ said: this is a day
for gratitude, not tears.

> ويحك إن هذا لم يعطه قوم إلا ألقيت بينهم العداوة والبغضاء
> — ⁨سیر أعلام النبلاء⁩ (⁨سير الخلفاء الراشدين⁩) ⁨ص۸۵⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/10906/1230
> *English:* "Woe to you — no people were ever given this without enmity and hatred being thrown
> down between them."

**Reading aloud:** the pair is ~16 seconds and must be delivered together. **This is the strongest
⁨عبرت⁩ item in the note**, and it is a sentence about the future said by a man looking at a heap of
treasure on the floor of a mosque.

**(e) The basket of fresh fish** — ⁨سیر⁩ (⁨راشدون⁩) ⁨ص۸۲⁩ · **PLAIN**. He mentioned that a craving for
fresh fish had crossed his heart; his servant ⁨يرفأ⁩ rode four days out and back and brought a basket.
⁨عمر ؓ⁩ went to look at the mount, found sweat under its ear that had not been washed off, and said:

> عذبت بهيمة في شهوة عمر، لا والله لا يذوق عمر مكتلك
> — ⁨سیر أعلام النبلاء⁩ (⁨سير الخلفاء الراشدين⁩) ⁨ص۸۲⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/10906/1227
> *English:* **"An animal was made to suffer for ʿUmar's appetite. No, by God — ʿUmar will not taste
> your basket."**

~9 seconds, one slide. See §X.1: this — an animal, and answerability — is the **named-chain** item
that should be preferred to the famous "a lost animal on the banks of the Euphrates" line. That line
**is** in an approved source (⁨الکامل ج۲ ص۴۳۳⁩), but ⁨ابن الأثير⁩ gives it anonymously, with «⁨قيل⁩» and no
chain; this one does not need that caveat.

**(f) Two more short ones on the same page-run** — ⁨سیر⁩ (⁨راشدون⁩) ⁨ص۸۱⁩–۸۲:
«**⁨كَفَى بِالْمَرْءِ سَرَفًا أَنْ يَأْكُلَ كُلَّ مَا اشْتَهَى⁩**» (*"it is extravagance enough in a man that he
eats everything he has an appetite for"*, ⁨ص۸۲⁩) · and, urged to eat better food so as to be stronger
for the work: «**⁨إِنِّي تَرَكْتُ صَاحِبَيَّ عَلَى جَادَّةٍ، فَإِنْ تَرَكْتُ جَادَّتَهُمَا لَمْ أُدْرِكْهُمَا فِي⁩
⁨الْمَنْزِلِ⁩**» (*"I left my two companions on a road. If I leave their road I shall not catch them at
the lodging."*, ⁨ص۸۱⁩; also ⁨البدایہ ج۷ ص۲۶۵⁩). Both ~7 seconds.

## 2.5 With his governors — the written contract, and four exchanges

**(a) The four conditions in every commission** — ⁨سیر⁩ (⁨راشدون⁩) ⁨ص۸۰⁩ · **PLAIN** (⁨خزيمة بن ثابت ؓ⁩);
⁨البدایہ ج۷ ص۲۶۴⁩ carries the same four and adds that he had a group of the ⁨مهاجرون⁩ witness it:

> كان عمر إذا استعمل عاملًا كتب له عهدًا وأشهد عليه رهطًا من المهاجرين واشترط عليه أن لا يركب برذونًا، ولا
> يأكل نقيًا، ولا يلبس رقيقًا، ولا يغلق بابهَ دون ذوي الحاجات. فإن فعل شيئًا من ذلك حَلَّتْ عليه العقوبة
> — ⁨البدایہ والنہایہ ج۷ ص۲۶۴⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/30097/3410
> · ⁨سیر أعلام النبلاء⁩ (⁨سير الخلفاء الراشدين⁩) ⁨ص۸۰⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/10906/1225
> *English:* When ʿUmar appointed a governor he wrote him a commission and had a group of the
> Muhājirūn witness it, stipulating: **he shall not ride a fine horse; he shall not eat sifted
> flour; he shall not wear fine cloth; and he shall not shut his door against those in need** — and
> if he does any of these, the penalty falls due on him.

**Reading aloud:** ~20 seconds; the four clauses alone are ~10 and are the slide. **Two sources, the
same four clauses.** It is the most concrete single artifact of ʿUmarī government we hold.

**(b) The charge to the man he set over the protected pasture** — ⁨سیر⁩ (⁨راشدون⁩) ⁨ص۸۵⁩ ·
**GRADED SOUND — the page itself says «⁨أخرجه البخاري⁩»**:

> يا هني اضمم جناحك عن المسلمين واتق دعوة المظلوم فإنها مستجابة، وأدخل رب الصريمة والغنيمة، وإياي ونعم ابن
> عوف ونعم ابن عفان فإنهما إن تهلك ما شيتهما يرجعان إلى زرع ونخل، وإن رب الصريمة والغنيمة إن تهلك ماشيتهما
> يأتيني ببنيه فيقول: يا أمير المؤمنين! أفتاركهم أنا لا أبا لك
> — ⁨سیر أعلام النبلاء⁩ (⁨سير الخلفاء الراشدين⁩) ⁨ص۸۵⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/10906/1230
> *English:* "⁨هُنَيّ⁩ — draw in your wing from the Muslims, and beware the prayer of the wronged, for
> it is answered. Let in the man with the few camels and the few sheep. And keep away from me the
> herds of Ibn ʿAwf and the herds of Ibn ʿAffān: if their livestock perish they fall back on crops
> and palms; but if the man with the few camels and the few sheep loses his livestock he comes to me
> with his sons and says *Commander of the Believers!* — am I then to abandon them, confound you?"

**Reading aloud:** ~40 seconds whole — too long. The slide is the first line plus the last clause,
~14 seconds. **This is the best-attested governance item in the note**, and it names two of the
richest Companions as the people the rule is aimed at.

**(c) The first letter he wrote as caliph — to ⁨أبو عبيدة ؓ⁩, on taking over ⁨خالد ؓ⁩'s army** —
⁨البدایہ ج۷ ص۱۰۳⁩ · **VIA ⁨ابن جرير⁩** (⁨عن صالح بن كيسان⁩):

> وأوصيك بتقوى الله الذي يَبْقى ويَفْنى ما سواه … وقد استعملتك على جُنْد خالد بن الوليد فقم بأمرهم الذي
> يحق عليك، لا تقدم المسلمين إلى هلكة رجاء غنيمة، ولا تنزلهم منزلًا قبل أن تَسْتريده (٥) لهم وتعلم كيف
> مأتاه … وقد أبلاك الله بي وأبلاني بك، فَغُضَّ بصرَكَ عن الدنيا، وألْهِ قلبَكَ عنها
> — ⁨البدایہ والنہایہ ج۷ ص۱۰۳⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/30097/3249
> *English:* "I charge you with fear of God, who remains while everything else passes … I have put
> you over Khālid ibn al-Walīd's army, so undertake their affair as is your duty. **Do not send the
> Muslims forward into destruction in the hope of spoils.** Do not settle them in a camp before you
> have scouted it for them and know how it is approached … God has tested you with me and tested me
> with you: **so lower your gaze from this world, and turn your heart away from it.**"

**Reading aloud:** the two bolded clauses are ~11 seconds and are the slide.

**(d) ⁨خالد ؓ⁩'s answer to being dismissed** — ⁨البدایہ ج۷ ص۱۰۳⁩ · **PLAIN**. ⁨أبو عبيدة ؓ⁩ carried out the
order: the turban came off his head and his property was divided in half with the treasury — Ibn
Kathīr's text says the division went as far as **one of his two sandals**:

> فقاسمه أبو عبيدة حتى أخذ [إحدى] (٢) نعليه وترك له الأخرى (٣)، وخالد يقول سمعًا وطاعة لأمير المؤمنين
> — ⁨البدایہ والنہایہ ج۷ ص۱۰۳⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/30097/3249
> *English:* Abū ʿUbayda divided with him until he took one of his two sandals and left him the
> other — **and Khālid kept saying: "To hear and to obey, for the Commander of the Believers."**

**Reading aloud:** ~11 seconds. **The safest and most useful item in the whole conquest narrative**:
the greatest field commander of the age, dismissed at the height of his success, in one sentence. It
settles the question the room will be asking without the speaker having to take anybody's side.

⚠ **Why he was dismissed is disputed inside our own sources and must not be settled on a slide.**
⁨البدایہ ج۷ ص۱۰۳⁩ and ⁨الکامل ج۲ ص۲۶۸⁩ both give the ⁨مالك بن نويرة⁩ affair and ⁨عمر ؓ⁩'s long-standing
displeasure. ⁨سیر ج۱ ص۳۸۰⁩ gives a different reason — a gift of ten thousand to ⁨الأشعث ؓ⁩ — and its
editor writes of that route: «**⁨لا يصح لضعف سيف، وجهالة الرجال الدين روى عنهم⁩**» (the page prints
«⁨الدين⁩» for «⁨الذين⁩» — §0.2). And ⁨سیر ج۱ ص۳۸۳⁩
has ⁨عمر ؓ⁩ tell ⁨علي ؓ⁩ he dismissed him «⁨لِبَذْلِهِ الْمَالَ لِأَهْلِ الشَّرَفِ وَذَوِي اللِّسَانِ⁩» — but the
editor there notes **⁨الواقدي متروك⁩**. **State the fact of the dismissal, quote ⁨خالد ؓ⁩'s answer, and
stop.**

**(e) The rhymed report on ⁨مُكران⁩, and ⁨عمر ؓ⁩'s one-line reply** — ⁨البدایہ ج۷ ص۲۶۱⁩ ·
**VIA ⁨سيف⁩ / ⁨ابن جرير⁩**. ⁨صُحار العَبْدي⁩ came in with the fifth from the Sind frontier and described
the country in rhyme:

> أرضٌ سهلُها جبلٌ، وماؤُها وَشَلُ (٤)، وتَمْرُها (٥) دَقَلُ (٦)، وعدوُّها بطلٌ، وخيرُها قليلٌ، وشرُّها
> طويلٌ، والكثيرُ بها قليلٌ، والقليلُ بها ضائعٌ، وما وراءها شرٌّ منها. فقال عمر: أسجَّاعٌ أنتَ أم مُخْبِر
> — ⁨البدایہ والنہایہ ج۷ ص۲۶۱⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/30097/3407
> *English:* "A land whose plain is mountain, whose water is a trickle, whose dates are the poorest
> kind, whose enemy is a champion; its good is little and its evil is long; much there is little,
> and little there is wasted; and what lies beyond it is worse than it." ʿUmar said: **"Are you
> making rhymes at me, or reporting?"**

He then wrote to ⁨الحكم بن عمرو ؓ⁩ **not to campaign beyond ⁨مُكران⁩** and to stop at the river (same
page). **Reading aloud:** the report is ~16 seconds, the reply ~3. Deliver as a pair: the laugh is
real, and then the decision it produced is the point — *a frontier was closed on the strength of one
plain report.*

## 2.6 عام الرمادة — ۱۸ھ

**Setting.** A famine so severe that the wind carried dust like ash, which is where the year gets
its name; ⁨الکامل ج۲ ص۳۷۴⁩ says wild animals came in to human settlements, and a man would slaughter
a sheep and find the meat inedible.

**(a) The oath, and the sentence that explains it** — ⁨الکامل ج۲ ص۳۷۴⁩–۳۷۵ · **PLAIN**. He swore to
taste no clarified butter, milk or meat until the people recovered. A servant bought him a skin of
butter and one of milk in the market for forty dirhams:

> فَقَالَ عُمَرُ: أَغْلَيْتَ بِهِمَا فَتَصَدَّقْ بِهِمَا فَإِنِّي أَكْرَهُ أَنْ آكُلَ إِسْرَافًا. وَقَالَ:
> كَيْفَ يَعْنِينِي شَأْنُ الرَّعِيَّةِ إِذَا لَمْ يُصِبْنِي مَا أَصَابَهُم
> — ⁨الکامل فی التاریخ ج۲ ص۳۷۵⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/21712/1066
> *English:* ʿUmar said: "You paid dear for these two — so give them away in charity; I dislike to
> eat extravagantly." And he said: **"How should the people's condition concern me, if what has
> struck them has not struck me?"**

**This is the answer to the strand's question, "what did he say in ⁨عام الرمادة⁩".** ~12 seconds for
the pair; the bolded sentence alone is ~6 and is the slide.

**(b) What the year did to him** — ⁨سیر⁩ (⁨راشدون⁩) ⁨ص۸۶⁩ · **PLAIN** (⁨أنس ؓ⁩): his stomach rumbled from
eating nothing but oil, having denied himself clarified butter; he prodded his own stomach with a
finger and said «**⁨إِنَّهُ لَيْسَ عِنْدَنَا غَيْرُهُ حَتَّى يَحْيَا النَّاسُ⁩**» — *"we have nothing else
until the people come back to life."* And ⁨أسلم⁩, at ⁨ص۸۷⁩: «**⁨لَوْ لَمْ يَرْفَعِ اللَّهُ الْمَحْلَ عَامَ⁩
⁨الرَّمَادَةِ لَظَنَنَّا أَنَّ عُمَرَ يَمُوتُ⁩**» — *"had God not lifted the drought in the year of ashes, we
would have thought ʿUmar would die."*
(https://shamela.ws/book/10906/1231 · https://shamela.ws/book/10906/1232)

**(c) The relief operation, in the numbers the sources give** — ⁨سیر⁩ (⁨راشدون⁩) ⁨ص۸۶⁩ · **VIA ⁨الواقدي⁩**:
he had men count those who ate at his table one night — **seven thousand**; the sick and the
dependants counted **forty thousand**, and days later the total reached **sixty thousand**.
⁨أبو عبيدة ؓ⁩ was the first governor to reach him, with **four thousand camel-loads of food**
(⁨الکامل ج۲ ص۳۷۵⁩), and ⁨عمرو بن العاص ؓ⁩ opened the Red Sea route so that grain reached Medina at the
Egyptian price (same page). **Read §Y before using any of these figures.**

**(d) The ⁨استسقاء⁩ through ⁨العباس ؓ⁩** — ⁨الکامل ج۲ ص۳۷۵⁩–۳۷۶ · **PLAIN**; ⁨سیر⁩ (⁨راشدون⁩) ⁨ص۱۲۰⁩ has the
short form «**⁨اللَّهُمَّ إِنَّا نَسْتَسْقِيكَ بِعَمِّ نَبِيِّكَ⁩**» (⁨ابن إسحاق⁩). The longer duʿāʾ:

> اللَّهُمَّ عَجَزَتْ عَنَّا أَنْصَارُنَا وَعَجَزَ عَنَّا حَوْلُنَا وَقُوَّتُنَا وَعَجَزَتْ عَنَّا
> أَنْفُسُنَا، وَلَا حَوْلَ وَلَا قُوَّةَ إِلَّا بِكَ، اللَّهُمَّ فَاسْقِنَا وَأَحْيِ الْعِبَادَ
> وَالْبِلَاد
> — ⁨الکامل فی التاریخ ج۲ ص۳۷۵⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/21712/1066
> *English:* "O God — our helpers have failed us; our strength and our power have failed us; our own
> selves have failed us, and there is no power and no strength except by You. O God, give us rain,
> and bring the servants and the lands to life."

⚠ **On the same page-run ⁨الکامل⁩ carries a second report — a man of ⁨مُزَينة⁩ who called out at the
Prophet's ﷺ name and was answered in a dream (⁨ج۲ ص۳۷۵⁩) — and ⁨سیر⁩ (⁨راشدون⁩) ⁨ص۸۶⁩ carries a report of a
man coming to the grave. Both touch a live theological dispute. Recorded here so nobody re-researches
them; DO NOT USE either in this room. If raised: say the reports are on the page, that the course
narrates and does not adjudicate, and defer.** Logged for `QA_BANK.md`.

## 2.7 The nameless dead at ⁨نهاوند⁩ — ⁨البدایہ ج۷ ص۲۳۳⁩ · **VIA ⁨سيف⁩ / ⁨ابن جرير⁩**

**Setting.** ⁨۲۱ھ⁩. The dispatch has come in from ⁨نهاوند⁩. ⁨النعمان بن مقرن ؓ⁩, the commander, is dead.
⁨عمر ؓ⁩ wept, and asked ⁨السائب بن الأقرع⁩ who else had been killed. ⁨السائب⁩ named the well-known and the
notable — and then said: *and others of the ordinary people, whom the Commander of the Believers does
not know.*

> فجعل يبكي ويقول: وما ضرهم أن لا يعرفهم أمير المؤمنين؟ لكنَّ الله يعرفهم وقد أكرمهم الله بالشهادة، وما
> يصنعون بمعرفة عمر
> — ⁨البدایہ والنہایہ ج۷ ص۲۳۳⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/30097/3379
> *English:* And he kept weeping and saying: **"And what harm did it do them that the Commander of
> the Believers does not know them? But God knows them, and God has honoured them with martyrdom —
> and what would they want with ʿUmar knowing them?"**

**Reading aloud:** ~15 seconds. **The best single sentence of the conquest era in this note**, and
the safest: no tribe, no faction, nothing to adjudicate, and it turns the whole subject of the
conquests away from generals and onto the dead nobody listed.

## 2.8 The address at ⁨الجابية⁩ — ⁨البدایہ ج۷ ص۱۵۸⁩ · **core GRADED SOUND**

**Setting.** ⁨عمر ؓ⁩ has ridden north; the army commanders meet him at ⁨الجابية⁩ before the handover of
⁨بيت المقدس⁩. ⁨ابن كثير⁩ calls the address «⁨طويلة بليغة⁩» and abridges it himself; the piece he keeps is
footnoted on the page to **⁨مسند أحمد ۱⁩/⁨۱۸ و ۲۶⁩ · ⁨الترمذي ۲۱۶۵⁩ · ⁨ابن ماجه ۲۳۶۳⁩** — so the core is in
the ḥadīth collections, not only in ⁨سيف⁩.

> أيها الناسُ أصْلحُوا سَرائرَكُم تَصْلُحْ علانيتكُمْ، واعملُوا لآخرتِكُم تُكْفَوا أمرَ دنياكم، واعلموا أن
> رجلًا ليس بينه وبين آدم أبٌ حيٌّ، ولا بينه وبين الله هوادة … فمن أراد بُحْبُوحَةَ (٢) الجنة فليلزمِ
> الجماعة، فإن الشيطانَ مع الواحدِ وهو مع الاثنين أبعد
> — ⁨البدایہ والنہایہ ج۷ ص۱۵۸⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/30097/3304
> *English:* "People — put your inward selves right and your outward will come right. Work for your
> next world and you will be spared the trouble of this one. **And know that no man has a living
> father between himself and Adam, and that between himself and God there is no favouritism** …
> Whoever wants the middle of the Garden, let him hold to the community — for Satan is with the one,
> and further away from the two."

**Reading aloud:** ~25 seconds whole; **the bolded sentence alone is ~9 seconds and is the slide.**
It is the exact answer to the tribal reading of the ⁨ردة⁩ set out in
`arabian-tribes-and-the-ridda-setup.md` — spoken by the caliph, in front of the commanders, at the
moment the state became an empire.

**Two scenes on the same page-run, each worth one line in the script.** At ⁨ج۷ ص۱۵۷ أبو عبيدة ؓ⁩ and
⁨عمر ؓ⁩ both dismount when they meet; ⁨أبو عبيدة ؓ⁩ reaches for ⁨عمر ؓ⁩'s hand to kiss it and ⁨عمر ؓ⁩ bends
toward ⁨أبو عبيدة ؓ⁩'s foot, and each stops the other. At ⁨ج۷ ص۱۵۸⁩ the commanders ride out to meet him
in brocade tunics and ⁨عمر ؓ⁩ goes at them **to throw pebbles**, until they explain they have armour
underneath and need it. Both **VIA ⁨سيف⁩**.

## 2.9 ⁨سَرْغ⁩ — the plague on the road — ⁨البدایہ ج۷ ص۱۸۶⁩ · **GRADED SOUND**

**Setting.** ⁨عمر ؓ⁩ is riding to ⁨الشام⁩. At ⁨سَرْغ⁩ the commanders — ⁨أبو عبيدة ؓ⁩, ⁨يزيد بن أبي سفيان ؓ⁩
and ⁨خالد ؓ⁩ — meet him with the news that plague has broken out in Syria. He consults; the Companions
split; he orders the return. ⁨أبو عبيدة ؓ⁩ objects.

> فقال أبو عبيدة: أفرارًا من قدر الله؟ قال: نعم! نفرُّ من قدر الله إلى قدر الله، أرأيت لو هبطت واديًا ذا
> عدوتين إحداهما مخصبة والأخرى مجدبة، فإن رعيت الخِصبة رعيتها بقدر الله، وإن أنت رعيت الجدبة رعيتها بقدر
> الله؟ ثم قال: لو غيرك يقولها يا أبا عبيدة
> — ⁨البدایہ والنہایہ ج۷ ص۱۸۶⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/30097/3332
> *English:* Abū ʿUbayda said: **"Fleeing from the decree of God?"** He said: **"Yes — we flee from
> the decree of God to the decree of God.** Suppose you came down into a valley with two banks, one
> fertile and one barren: if you grazed the fertile one you grazed it by God's decree, and if you
> grazed the barren one you grazed it by God's decree." Then he said: **"If it were anyone but you
> saying it, Abū ʿUbayda."**

⁨عبد الرحمن بن عوف ؓ⁩ then arrived from an errand and supplied the ḥadīth — «⁨إذا سمعتم به بأرضِ قومٍ⁩
⁨فلا تَقْدَموا عليه، وإذا وقعَ بأرضٍ وأنتم فيها فلا تخرجوا فرارًا منه⁩» — and ⁨عمر ؓ⁩ praised God and
took the people back. The page footnotes it **⁨صحيح البخاري ۵۷۲۹ في الطب⁩**, and prints a second route
from ⁨مسند أحمد⁩ whose isnād the editor calls **⁨صحيح⁩**.

⚠ This page prints «**⁨صحيح البخارب⁩**» for ⁨البخاري⁩ — §0.2 again.

**Reading aloud:** ~30 seconds whole. The slide is the first exchange plus the last line, ~14
seconds. **The last line is what makes it usable in this room**: a caliph overruled his own greatest
commander, and then told him in front of everyone that he would have taken it from no one else.

## 2.10 The calendar — ⁨البدایہ ج۷ ص۱۸۱⁩–۱۸۲ · **VIA ⁨الواقدي⁩ / ⁨ابن جرير⁩**

**Setting.** ⁨۱۶ھ⁩. A promissory note was brought to ⁨عمر ؓ⁩: a debt falling due «⁨في شعبان⁩».

> فقال: أي شعبان؟ أمن هذه السنة أم التي قبلها، أم التي بعدها؟ ثم جمع الناس فقال: ضعوا للناس شيئًا يعرفون
> فيه حلول ديونهم
> — ⁨البدایہ والنہایہ ج۷ ص۱۸۱⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/30097/3327
> *English:* He said: **"Which Shaʿbān? This year's, or last year's, or next year's?"** Then he
> gathered the people and said: **"Set the people something by which they can know when their debts
> fall due."**

The options canvassed, on the next page: date it like the Persians from each king's accession
(rejected); like the Romans from Alexander (rejected, and too long); from the Prophet's ﷺ birth;
from his sending. **And then:**

> وأشار علي بن أبي طالب وآخرون أن يؤرخ من هجرته من مكة إلى المدينة لظهوره لكلّ أحد فإنه أظهر من المولد
> والمبعث. فاستحسن ذلك عمر والصحابة
> — ⁨البدایہ والنہایہ ج۷ ص۱۸۲⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/30097/3328
> *English:* ʿAlī ibn Abī Ṭālib and others advised dating **from his emigration from Mecca to
> Medina, because it is plain to everyone** — plainer than the birth or the sending. ʿUmar and the
> Companions thought that best.

They then dated from the **Muḥarram** of that year, «⁨لأنَّه أضبطُ لئلا تختلفَ الشهورُ⁩» (same page);
⁨ابن كثير⁩ notes ⁨مالك رحمہ اللہ⁩, as ⁨السهيلي⁩ reports him, put the year's start at ⁨ربيع الأول⁩ instead,
and that the ⁨جمهور⁩ are on ⁨المحرم⁩. ⁨سیر⁩ (⁨راشدون⁩) ⁨ص۱۱۹⁩ gives it in one line: **«⁨أوَّلُ مَنْ كتبَ⁩
التاريخَ عمرُ بنُ الخطاب لسنتينِ ونصفٍ من خلافته، فكتبَ لستَّ عشرةَ من الهجرةِ بمشورةِ عليٍّ»**
(https://shamela.ws/book/10906/1264).

**Reading aloud:** the two ⁨عمر ؓ⁩ sentences are ~13 seconds together and are the slide. **This is the
best "how a civilisation gets its instruments" item in the note** — the Hijrī calendar begins with an
undated IOU, and the reason for choosing the ⁨هجرة⁩ is *because it is plain to everyone*, i.e. it is
public, checkable and shared. That is also a method point for «⁨کیسے پتا چلا؟⁩».

## 2.11 The wounding and the dying — ⁨۲۳ھ⁩

**(a) The duʿāʾ that was answered** — ⁨سیر⁩ (⁨راشدون⁩) ⁨ص۸۹⁩ · **GRADED SOUND, the page says «⁨أخرجه⁩
⁨البخاري⁩»**; ⁨البدایہ ج۷ ص۲۶۸⁩ cites **⁨صحيح البخاري ۱۷۹۱⁩** for it:

> اللهم ارزقني شهادة في سبيلك، واجعل موتي في بلد رسولك
> — ⁨سیر أعلام النبلاء⁩ (⁨سير الخلفاء الراشدين⁩) ⁨ص۸۹⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/10906/1234
> · ⁨البدایہ والنہایہ ج۷ ص۲۶۸⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/30097/3414
> *English:* "O God, grant me martyrdom in Your path, and make my death in the city of Your
> Messenger."

⁨ابن كثير⁩'s comment on the same ⁨البدایہ⁩ page is the whole point of quoting it: God joined the two
things for him — martyrdom, and in Medina — «**⁨وهذا عزيزٌ جدًا⁩**». ~8 seconds. One slide.

**(b) The duʿāʾ at ⁨الأبطح⁩, weeks before** — ⁨سیر⁩ (⁨راشدون⁩) ⁨ص۸۸⁩–۸۹ · **PLAIN** (⁨سعيد بن المسيب⁩). After
the Ḥajj of ⁨۲۳ھ⁩ he stopped at ⁨الأبطح⁩, heaped up a mound of pebbles, lay back and raised his hands:

> اللهم كبرت سني وضعفت قوتي وانتشرت رعيتي فاقبضني إليك غير مضيع ولا مفرط
> — ⁨سیر أعلام النبلاء⁩ (⁨سير الخلفاء الراشدين⁩) ⁨ص۸۹⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/10906/1234
> *English:* "O God — my years have grown many, my strength has weakened, and my flock has spread
> wide. Take me to You having neither squandered nor fallen short."

«**⁨فما انسلخَ ذو الحجةِ حتى طُعِنَ فماتَ⁩**» — *Dhū l-Ḥijja had not run out before he was stabbed and
died.* ~13 seconds. **A slide, and a whole session's arc in one sentence: the empire had outgrown
the man administering it, and he knew it.**

**(c) The killing itself, and the first thing he said** — ⁨البدایہ ج۷ ص۲۶۸⁩ · **PLAIN**; the same at
⁨سیر⁩ (⁨راشدون⁩) ⁨ص۹۱⁩. Struck at ⁨فجر⁩ on the Wednesday four nights from the end of ⁨ذو الحجة⁩ by
**⁨أبو لؤلؤة فيروز⁩**, a slave of ⁨المغيرة بن شعبة ؓ⁩. Asked who had done it:

> الحمد لله الذي لم يجعل منيتي بيد رجل يدعي الإسلام
> — ⁨سیر أعلام النبلاء⁩ (⁨سير الخلفاء الراشدين⁩) ⁨ص۹۱⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/10906/1236
> *English:* "Praise be to God, who did not put my death at the hands of a man who claims Islam."

⁨البدایہ⁩ carries the same words in a fuller form on the same page:

> الحمد لله الذي لم يجعل منيَّتي على يَدَيْ رجل يَدَّعي الإيمانَ ولم يسجد لله سجدة.
> — ⁨البدایہ والنہایہ ج۷ ص۲۶۸⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/30097/3414
> *English:* "Praise be to God, who did not put my death at the hands of a man who claims faith
> and has never made a single prostration to God."

⚠ **The wordings differ between the two books («⁨الإسلام⁩» / «⁨الإيمان⁩ … ⁨ولم يسجد لله سجدة⁩»). Pick one
book and quote it; do not blend.** ~8 seconds.

And, when they woke him for the prayer as he lay bleeding — ⁨البدایہ ج۷ ص۲۶۸⁩ and ⁨سیر⁩ (⁨راشدون⁩) ⁨ص۹۲⁩:
«**⁨نعم، ولا حظَّ في الإسلامِ لمن تركها⁩**» — *"Yes — and there is no share in Islam for one who
abandons it."* «⁨فصلَّى وجُرحُه يَثْعَبُ دمًا⁩» (⁨سیر ص۹۲⁩). ~7 seconds; among the strongest four seconds
of Arabic available for this session.

**(d) The audit of his own life** — ⁨سیر⁩ (⁨راشدون⁩) ⁨ص۹۰⁩ · **PLAIN**; and ⁨الکامل ج۲ ص۴۴۰⁩ has the same
verdict inside a longer speech. People had begun praising him — *you were, and you were.*

> أما والله وددت أني خرجت منها كفافا لا علي ولا لي وأن صحبة رسول الله صلى الله عليه وسلم سلمت لي
> — ⁨سیر أعلام النبلاء⁩ (⁨سير الخلفاء الراشدين⁩) ⁨ص۹۰⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/10906/1235
> *English:* "By God, I would love to come out of it break-even — nothing against me and nothing for
> me — and that my companionship of the Messenger of God ﷺ came through safe."

The ⁨الکامل⁩ wording of the same thought, ⁨ج۲ ص۴۴۰⁩, is longer and harder:

> بِحَسْبِ آلِ عُمَرَ أَنْ يُحَاسَبَ مِنْهُمْ رَجُلٌ وَاحِدٌ، وَيُسْأَلَ عَنْ أَمْرِ أُمَّةِ مُحَمَّدٍ،
> أَمَا لَقَدْ جَهَدْتُ نَفْسِي وَحَرَمْتُ أَهْلِي، وَإِنْ نَجَوْتُ كِفَافًا لَا وِزْرَ وَلَا أَجْرَ
> إِنِّي لَسَعِيد
> — ⁨الکامل فی التاریخ ج۲ ص۴۴۰⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/21712/1130
> *English:* "It is enough for the family of ʿUmar that one man of them be called to account and
> asked about the affair of Muḥammad's community. I have worn myself out and deprived my own
> household — **and if I get out of it break-even, no sin and no reward, I am a fortunate man.**"

**Reading aloud:** the ⁨سیر⁩ line ~11 seconds, the ⁨الکامل⁩ passage ~18. **Either is a slide; the pair is
too much.** The ⁨سیر⁩ line is the cleaner one for the room.

**(e) Refusing to be prayed back to life** — ⁨سیر⁩ (⁨راشدون⁩) ⁨ص۹۳⁩ · **PLAIN** (⁨ابن عباس ؓ⁩). ⁨كعب⁩ swore
that if the Commander of the Believers would only make duʿāʾ, God would raise him up for this
community. It was put to him. He answered:

> لا والله لا أدعو الله ولكن شقي عمر إن لم يغفر الله له
> — ⁨سیر أعلام النبلاء⁩ (⁨سير الخلفاء الراشدين⁩) ⁨ص۹۳⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/10906/1238
> *English:* "No, by God, I will not ask God — but ʿUmar is a ruined man if God does not forgive
> him."

~9 seconds. On the same page, ⁨صهيب ؓ⁩ came in wailing *my friend! my brother! ʿUmar!* and was
stopped: «**⁨مَهْلًا يَا صُهَيْبُ، أَوَمَا بَلَغَكَ أَنَّ الْمُعَوَّلَ عَلَيْهِ يُعَذَّبُ بِبَعْضِ بُكَاءِ أَهْلِهِ عَلَيْهِ⁩**»
— he corrected a point of law while dying of a stab wound.

**(f) Why he would not appoint his own son** — ⁨سیر⁩ (⁨راشدون⁩) ⁨ص۹۳⁩ · **PLAIN**. ⁨عبد الله بن عمر ؓ⁩
suggested he name someone. He had himself sat up, and the answer ends:

> فما أنا قائل لله إذا سألني عمن أمرت عليهم … فلا والذي نفسي بيده لأرددنها إلى الذي دفعها إلي أول مرة،
> ولوددت أن عليها من هو خير مني
> — ⁨سیر أعلام النبلاء⁩ (⁨سير الخلفاء الراشدين⁩) ⁨ص۹۳⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/10906/1238
> *English:* "What am I to say to God when He asks me about the man I set over them? … No — by Him
> in whose hand is my soul, I shall hand it back to the One who gave it to me in the first place.
> **And I would love it if someone better than me were over it.**"

⁨الکامل ج۲ ص۴۴۰⁩ has the same refusal from a different angle, when a man proposed ⁨عبد الله ؓ⁩ by name:
«**قَاتَلَكَ اللَّهُ! وَاللَّهِ مَا أَرَدْتَ اللَّهَ بِهَذَا … كَيْفَ أَسْتَخْلِفُ رَجُلًا عَجَزَ عَنْ طَلَاقِ امْرَأَتِهِ؟**»
(https://shamela.ws/book/21712/1130). ~12 seconds. **A slide, and it is the cleanest available answer
to "was there a dynasty?" — the caliph rules his own son out, by name, on the record.**

**(g) The debt, and the permission he had to ask for** — ⁨سیر⁩ (⁨راشدون⁩) ⁨ص۹۱⁩ · **PLAIN**. His debts came
to **eighty-six thousand or thereabouts**; he directed that they be paid from his own family's
property first, then from ⁨بني عدي⁩, then from ⁨قريش⁩. Then he sent his son to ⁨أم المؤمنين عائشة ؓ⁩:

> اذهب إلى أم المؤمنين عائشة فقل: يستأذن عمر أن يدفن مع صاحبيه … فقالت: كنت أريده -تعني المكان- لنفسي
> ولأؤثرنه اليوم على نفسي
> — ⁨سیر أعلام النبلاء⁩ (⁨سير الخلفاء الراشدين⁩) ⁨ص۹۱⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/10906/1236
> *English:* "Go to the Mother of the Believers, ʿĀʾisha, and say: **ʿUmar asks permission to be
> buried with his two companions.**" … She said: "I wanted it for myself — **but today I shall
> certainly prefer him to myself.**"

And when the body was carried, ⁨ص۹۲⁩: ⁨عبد الله ؓ⁩ gave the greeting and said «**⁨عمرُ يستأذنُ⁩**» — *ʿUmar
asks permission* — before he was brought in. **~18 seconds for the pair; it is the closing slide of
the session and it needs no commentary at all.**

⚠ **⁨الکامل ج۲ ص۴۴۱⁩ carries ⁨عمر ؓ⁩'s own remarks on the six of the ⁨شورى⁩, including his assessments of
⁨عثمان ؓ⁩ and ⁨علي ؓ⁩. DO NOT USE.** It is the doorway to the ⁨مشاجرات⁩ and belongs to session 3, under

# 3. The commanders — ⁨أبو عبيدة ؓ⁩ · ⁨خالد ؓ⁩ · ⁨معاذ ؓ⁩

## 3.1 ⁨أبو عبيدة بن الجراح ؓ⁩ — the letter that refused to leave his army

**Setting.** ⁨طاعون عمواس⁩. ⁨عمر ؓ⁩ wrote him a letter that did not mention the plague at all: *a
need has arisen and I must speak to you face to face; the moment you read this, do not put it down
until you come to me.*

⚠ **The year.** This note dates ⁨عمواس⁩ to **⁨۱۸ھ⁩** `[CONVENTIONAL-ESTIMATE]`, and the ⁨سیر⁩ pages carry no
year at all. **⁨البدایہ⁩ prints its ⁨عمواس⁩ material (⁨ج۷ ص۱۸۷⁩–۱۸۹) inside the chapter «⁨ثم دخلت سنة سبع⁩
⁨عشرة⁩» — ⁨۱۷ھ⁩.** Say "the year of the plague of ʿAmwās" rather than a number, or give both. Do not put
a bare ⁨۱۸ھ⁩ on a slide sourced to these pages.

**The ⁨سیر⁩ route is the strong one and should be the one quoted** — ⁨سیر ج۱ ص۱۸⁩–۱۹ ·
**GRADED SOUND**: the editor's footnote records ⁨الحاكم⁩'s «⁨رواته كلهم ثقات⁩» on this chain
(⁨شعبة عن قيس بن مسلم عن طارق بن شهاب⁩) and ⁨الذہبی⁩'s own verdict in *⁨المختصر⁩*: «⁨هو على شرط البخاري⁩
ومسلم».

> عَرَفْتُ حَاجَةَ أَمِيْرِ المُؤْمِنِيْنَ، إِنَّهُ يُرِيْدُ أَنْ يَسْتَبْقِيَ مَنْ لَيْسَ بِبَاقٍ.
> فَكَتَبَ: إِنِّي قَدْ عَرَفْتُ حَاجَتَكَ، فَحَلِّلْنِي مِنْ عَزِيْمَتك، فَإِنِّي فِي جُنْد … مِنْ
> أَجْنَادِ المُسْلِمِيْنَ، لاَ أَرْغَبُ بِنَفْسِي عَنْهُم
> — ⁨سیر أعلام النبلاء ج۱ ص۱۸⁩–۱۹ · https://shamela.ws/book/10906/1444 ⁨و⁩ 1445
> *English:* "I know what the Commander of the Believers wants: he wants to keep alive one who is not
> going to remain." Then he wrote back: "I know what you need of me — so release me from your
> insistence. **I am in one of the armies of the Muslims, and I will not hold myself above them.**"
> («⁨لا أرغب بنفسي عنهم⁩» is the idiom *to think oneself too good for* — not "desire".)

And ⁨عمر ؓ⁩'s reaction, same page:

> فَلَمَّا قَرَأَ عُمَرُ الكِتَابَ، بَكَى، فَقِيْلَ لَهُ: مَاتَ أَبُو عُبَيْدَةَ؟ قَالَ: لاَ، وَكَأَنْ قَد
> — ⁨سیر أعلام النبلاء ج۱ ص۱۹⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/10906/1445
> *English:* When ʿUmar read the letter he wept. It was said to him: "Has Abū ʿUbayda died?" He said:
> **"No — but as good as."**

**⁨البدایہ ج۷ ص۱۸۸⁩ carries the same exchange at more length** (⁨ابن إسحاق عن⁩ ... ⁨عن طارق بن شهاب⁩,
**VIA ⁨الطبری⁩**), with ⁨عمر ؓ⁩'s first letter in full — «⁨فعزمتُ عليك إذا نظرتَ في كتابي هذا أن لا تضعَه⁩
⁨من يدِك حتى تُقْبِلَ إليَّ⁩» — and ⁨أبو عبيدة ؓ⁩'s opening «**⁨يغفرُ اللهُ لأميرِ المؤمنين⁩**», and the
second letter that followed: **move the men to higher ground**. ⁨أبو عبيدة ؓ⁩ obeyed that one at once,
and was struck as he put his foot in the stirrup (https://shamela.ws/book/30097/3334).

**Reading aloud:** the refusal ~13 seconds; «⁨لا، وكأن قد⁩» is four words and about two seconds, and it
is the best two seconds in section 3. **Deliver the pair.** The point is that both men were right and
both obeyed: he would not leave his men, and he did move them.

## 3.2 ⁨أبو عبيدة ؓ⁩ — the two sayings of his own that the pages carry

**(a) The one that belongs in this room** — ⁨سیر ج۱ ص۱۸⁩ · **PLAIN** (⁨ثابت البناني⁩):

> يَا أَيُّهَا النَّاسُ! إِنِّي امْرُؤٌ مِنْ قُرَيْشٍ، وَمَا مِنْكُم مِنْ أَحْمَرَ وَلاَ أَسْوَدَ
> يَفْضُلُنِي بِتَقْوَى، إِلاَّ وَدِدْتُ أَنِّي فِي مِسْلاَخِه
> — ⁨سیر أعلام النبلاء ج۱ ص۱۸⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/10906/1444
> *English:* "People — I am a man of Quraysh, and there is not one of you, red or black, who
> surpasses me in fear of God but I wish I were in his skin."

**~13 seconds. This is the single best line in the note for the tribal-map slides**, because it is
said by a Qurashī commander-in-chief about men under his own command, and it dismantles the whole
logic of ancestry without arguing against it.

**(b) The one he said riding through the camp** — ⁨سیر ج۱ ص۱۸⁩ · **FLAGGED ⁨مرسل⁩** (the editor's note
records Ibn Ḥajar: «⁨سنده مرسل⁩»):

> أَلاَ رُبَّ مُبَيِّضٍ لِثِيَابِهِ، مُدَنِّسٍ لِدِيْنِهِ! أَلاَ رُبَّ مُكْرِمٍ لِنَفْسِهِ وَهُوَ لَهَا
> مُهِيْنٌ! بَادِرُوا السَّيِّئَاتِ القَدِيْمَاتِ بِالحَسَنَاتِ الحَدِيْثَات
> — ⁨سیر أعلام النبلاء ج۱ ص۱۸⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/10906/1444
> *English:* "How many a man whitens his clothes and blackens his religion! How many a man honours
> himself and is in truth degrading himself! Outrun your old bad deeds with new good ones."

**Beautiful, and mursal.** Use it only if the speaker is willing to say «⁨یہ روایت مرسل ہے⁩» — or keep
it in the DRILL and off the screen.

## 3.3 ⁨عمر ؓ⁩ in ⁨أبو عبيدة ؓ⁩'s house — ⁨سیر ج۱ ص۱۷⁩ · **FLAGGED WEAK, but see below**

**Setting.** ⁨عمر ؓ⁩ has come to ⁨الشام⁩ and asks to see where his commander-in-chief lives. ⁨أبو عبيدة ؓ⁩
tries to put him off: *what do you want at my place? You only want to squeeze your eyes over me.*

> فَدَخَلَ، فَلَمْ يَرَ شَيْئاً، قَالَ: أَيْنَ مَتَاعُكَ؟ لاَ أَرَى إِلاَّ لِبْداً وَصَحْفَةً (٢)
> وَشَنّاً، وَأَنْتَ أَمِيْر … فَبَكَى عُمَر … قَالَ عُمَرُ: غَيَّرَتْنَا الدُّنْيَا كُلَّنَا، غَيْرَكَ
> يَا أَبَا عُبَيْدَة
> — ⁨سیر أعلام النبلاء ج۱ ص۱۷⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/10906/1443
> *English:* He went in and saw nothing. He said: "Where are your possessions? I see only a felt mat,
> a bowl and a waterskin — and you are a commander." … ʿUmar wept … and said: **"The world has changed
> all of us, except you, Abū ʿUbayda."**

⚠ **The editor's footnote grades this chain ⁨ضعيف⁩** (⁨عبد الله بن عمر العمري⁩: «⁨ضعيف عابد⁩»), while
noting the ⁨ذہبی⁩ says ⁨أبو داود⁩ carries it by another route. **So: do not put the sentence on a slide
as a quotation.** What *can* be used is ⁨الذہبی⁩'s own comment on the page, because that is his
judgement and not a report: «**⁨وَهَذَا⁩ -⁨وَاللهِ⁩- ⁨هُوَ الزُّهْدُ الخَالِصُ، لاَ زُهْدُ مَنْ كَانَ فَقِيْراً⁩
⁨مُعْدِماً⁩**» — *"and this, by God, is pure asceticism — not the asceticism of a man who is poor and
has nothing."* That distinction, from ⁨الذہبی رحمہ اللہ⁩, is worth a slide on its own.

## 3.4 ⁨خالد بن الوليد ؓ⁩ on his deathbed — two wordings, and the famous one is the weaker

**(a) The stronger wording** — ⁨سیر ج۱ ص۳۸۱⁩ · **GRADED SOUND** (⁨عاصم بن بهدلة عن أبي وائل⁩; the
editor traces it to ⁨ابن المبارك في⁩ *⁨الجهاد⁩* through ⁨حماد بن زيد عن عبد الله بن المختار⁩, and records
Ibn Ḥajar: «**⁨وإسناده حسن⁩**»):

> لَقَدْ طَلَبْتُ القَتْلَ مَظَانَّهُ، فَلَمْ يُقَدَّرْ لِي إِلاَّ أَنْ أَمُوْتَ عَلَى فِرَاشِي، وَمَا
> مِنْ عَمَلِي شَيْءٌ أَرْجَى عِنْدِي بَعْدَ التَّوْحِيْدِ مِنْ لَيْلَةٍ بِتُّهَا وَأَنَا مُتَتَرِّسٌ،
> وَالسَّمَاءُ تُهِلُّنِي، نَنْتَظِرُ الصُّبْحَ حَتَّى نُغِيْرَ عَلَى الكُفَّارِ. ثُمَّ قَالَ: إِذَا
> مِتُّ، فَانْظُرُوا إِلَى سِلاَحِي وَفَرَسِي، فَاجْعلُوْهُ عُدَّةً فِي سَبِيْلِ الله
> — ⁨سیر أعلام النبلاء ج۱ ص۳۸۱⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/10906/1807
> *English:* "I sought death in the places where it is most likely to be found, and it was not decreed
> for me except to die in my bed. **And nothing of my deeds do I hope in more, after tawḥīd, than one
> night I spent behind my shield with the sky raining on me, waiting for the dawn so that we could
> ride down on the unbelievers.**" Then he said: "When I die, look to my weapon and my horse, and make
> them equipment in the path of God."

**~28 seconds; the bolded middle is ~15 and is the slide.** *Not* the battles — one wet night waiting.

**(b) The famous wording** — ⁨البدایہ ج۷ ص۲۳۷⁩ · **FLAGGED WEAK (⁨الواقدي⁩)**; ⁨سیر ج۱ ص۳۸۲⁩ has it from
أبي الزناد:

> لقد حضرتُ كذا وكذا زحفًا، وما في جسدي شبرٌ إلا وفيه ضربةُ سيفٍ، أو طعنةٌ برمحٍ، أو رميةٌ بسهمٍ، وها أنا
> أموت على فراشي حتفَ أنفي كما يموت البعيرُ، فلا نامت (٢) أعينُ الجبناء
> — ⁨البدایہ والنہایہ ج۷ ص۲۳۷⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/30097/3383
> *English:* "I was present at so-many-and-so-many engagements, and there is not a hand's breadth on
> my body without a sword-cut or a spear-thrust or an arrow-wound in it — and here I am dying in my
> bed of natural causes, as a camel dies. **So may the eyes of cowards never sleep.**"

⁨سیر⁩ carries the same sentence from ⁨أبو الزناد⁩, with the beast named differently:

> لَقِيْتُ كَذَا وَكَذَا زَحْفاً، وَمَا فِي جَسَدِي شِبْرٌ إِلاَّ وَفِيْهِ ضَرْبَةٌ بِسَيْفٍ، أَوْ رَمْيَةٌ بِسَهْمٍ،
> وَهَا أَنَا أَمُوْتُ عَلَى فِرَاشِي حَتْفَ أَنْفِي كَمَا يَمُوْتُ العِيْرُ، فَلاَ نَامَتْ أَعْيُنُ الجُبَنَاء
> — ⁨سیر أعلام النبلاء ج۱ ص۳۸۲⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/10906/1808
> *English:* "I met so-many-and-so-many engagements, and there is not a hand's breadth on my body
> without a sword-cut or an arrow-wound in it — and here I am dying in my bed of natural causes, as
> the donkey dies. So may the eyes of cowards never sleep." (Where ⁨البدایہ⁩ has the camel,
> ⁨سیر⁩ has the donkey.)

⚠ **Two things the speaker must know.** First, ⁨البدایہ⁩'s route is **⁨الواقدي عن عبد الرحمن بن أبي⁩
⁨الزناد عن أبيه⁩**, and ⁨سیر⁩'s ⁨ج۱ ص۳۸۲⁩ route is ⁨أبو الزناد⁩ — neither is graded on the page, and ⁨الواقدي⁩
is ⁨متروك⁩ by the standard the same editor applies elsewhere. Second, **the number is not in the
text.** The page says «⁨كذا وكذا زحفًا⁩» — *so-many-and-so-many* — not "a hundred battles". **Anyone
who says "a hundred battles" from this page has invented it.** If the wording is used at all, say
«so many engagements», and prefer (a).

**(c) What he left** — ⁨سیر ج۱ ص۳۸۳⁩ · **PLAIN** (⁨جويرية عن نافع⁩):

> لَمَّا مَاتَ خَالِدٌ، لَمْ يَدَعْ إِلاَّ فَرَسَهُ وَسِلاَحَهُ وَغُلاَمَهُ. فَقَالَ عُمَرُ: رَحِمَ اللهُ
> أَبَا سُلَيْمَانَ، كَانَ عَلَى مَا ظَنَنَّاهُ بِه
> — ⁨سیر أعلام النبلاء ج۱ ص۳۸۳⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/10906/1809
> *English:* When Khālid died he left nothing but his horse, his weapon and his servant. ʿUmar said:
> **"God have mercy on ⁨أبو سليمان⁩ — he was as we thought him to be."**

**~11 seconds, and it is the item that closes the dismissal story** (§2.5d) without anyone having to
argue about who was right. On the same page ⁨عمر ؓ⁩'s other verdict: «**⁨كَانَ وَاللهِ سَدَّادًا لِنَحْرِ⁩
⁨العَدُوِّ، مَيْمُوْنَ النَّقِيْبَةِ⁩**» — *"he was, by God, a stopper thrust into the enemy's throat, and
blessed in his undertakings"* — though that sits inside a **⁨الواقدي⁩** report the editor flags.

**(d) On mourning him** — ⁨سیر ج۱ ص۳۸۳⁩ · **GRADED SOUND-ish**: the women of ⁨بني المغيرة⁩ gathered in
his house weeping and ⁨عمر ؓ⁩ said «**⁨مَا عَلَيْهِنَّ أَنْ يُرِقْنَ مِنْ دُمُوْعِهِنَّ مَا لَمْ يَكُنْ نَقْعًا أَوْ⁩
⁨لَقْلَقَةً⁩**» — *"there is no harm in their pouring out their tears, so long as there is no dust on
the head and no shrieking."* The editor traces it to ⁨الحاكم⁩ and to ⁨البخاري⁩'s *⁨التاريخ⁩* via ⁨الأعمش عن⁩
⁨أبي وائل⁩. ~9 seconds; a lawful, humane, quotable ruling given at a graveside.

## 3.5 ⁨معاذ بن جبل ؓ⁩ — the plague sermon, and the hand

**Setting.** ⁨طاعون عمواس⁩ (for the year, see the warning at §3.1). ⁨أبو عبيدة ؓ⁩ stood up in the camp when the plague took hold, said his piece, was
struck and died; ⁨معاذ ؓ⁩ succeeded him and stood up and said the same thing about his own family; his
son ⁨عبد الرحمن⁩ was struck and died; then he prayed it for himself and was struck in the palm of his
hand. ⁨البدایہ ج۷ ص۱۸۸⁩–۱۸۹ · **VIA ⁨الطبری⁩** (⁨ابن إسحاق عن أبان بن صالح عن شهر بن حوشب⁩).

> أيها الناس، إن هذا الوجعَ رحمةُ ربِّكم (٦) ودعوةُ نبيّكم وموتُ الصالحين قبلكم (وإنَّ أبا عبيدة يسأل الله
> أن يقسم لأبي عبيدة حظَّه
> — ⁨البدایہ والنہایہ ج۷ ص۱۸۸⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/30097/3334
> *English:* "People — this affliction is **your Lord's mercy, and your Prophet's supplication, and
> the death of the righteous who went before you**. And Abū ʿUbayda asks God to allot Abū ʿUbayda his
> share of it."

And ⁨معاذ ؓ⁩, looking at his struck hand and turning it over — ⁨البدایہ ج۷ ص۱۸۹⁩:

> ما أحبُّ أنَّ لي بما (فيك) شيئًا من الدنيا
> — ⁨البدایہ والنہایہ ج۷ ص۱۸۹⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/30097/3335
> *English:* **"I would not want anything of this world in exchange for what is in you."**

**Reading aloud:** the sermon ~15 seconds, the hand ~5. **The hand is the slide.** ⚠ Report the whole
sequence as «⁨روایت میں آتا ہے⁩» — it is a ⁨سيف⁩/⁨الطبری⁩ page-run and the wording must not be pressed.

## 3.6 ⁨عمرو بن العاص ؓ⁩ breaks the plague — ⁨البدایہ ج۷ ص۱۸۹⁩ · **VIA ⁨الطبری⁩**

He took command after ⁨معاذ ؓ⁩ and told the camp the plague catches like fire and to scatter into the
hills. **A Companion, ⁨أبو واثلة الهذلي⁩, called him a liar to his face** and said he had kept the
Prophet's ﷺ company while «⁨أنت شرٌّ من حماري هذا⁩». ⁨عمرو ؓ⁩'s answer is the reason to record the scene:

> والله ما أردّ عليك ما تقول، وأيم الله لا نقيم عليه. قال: ثم خرج وخرج الناسُ فتفرقوا ودفعه الله عنهم …
> فبلغ ذلك عمر بن الخطاب من رأي عمرو بن العاص فوالله ما كرهه
> — ⁨البدایہ والنہایہ ج۷ ص۱۸۹⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/30097/3335
> *English:* "By God, I will not answer you back for what you say — **but by God, we are not staying
> here.**" Then he went out and the people went out and scattered, and God lifted it from them. …
> And word of ʿAmr's judgement reached ʿUmar ibn al-Khaṭṭāb — **and by God, he did not dislike it.**

**~17 seconds.** A commander insulted in public, refusing to answer, and acting anyway; and the
caliph, told about it, letting it stand.

# 4. Envoys and outsiders — the words of men looking in from outside

These are the most *tellable* items in the note, and the least dangerous, because none of them
touches an internal dispute. **All three are second-hand or weakly-chained. Introduce every one of
them with "it is related" and none with "he said".**

## 4.1 ⁨ربعي بن عامر ؓ⁩ before ⁨رستم⁩ — ⁨البدایہ ج۷ ص۱۳۴⁩ · **VIA ⁨الطبری⁩**

**Setting.** ۱۴–⁨۱۵ھ⁩, before ⁨القادسية⁩. ⁨سعد بن أبي وقاص ؓ⁩ has sent a second envoy. ⁨رستم⁩'s audience hall
is laid with gilded cushions and silk rugs; the jewels are out; he is on a throne of gold. ⁨ربعي ؓ⁩
rides in on a short horse in coarse clothes, walks the animal onto the edge of the carpet, ties it to
one of the cushions and comes forward in helmet and mail, leaning on his spear so that it tears the
rugs. He is told to put down his weapon and refuses: *I did not come to you — you called me.*

> الله ابتعثنا لنخرج من شاء من عبادة العباد إلى عبادة الله، ومن ضيق الدنيا إلى سعتها، ومن جَور الأديان إلى
> عدل الإسلام، فأرسلنا بدينه إلى خلقه لندعوهم إليه، فمن قَبِلَ ذلك (قبلنا) منه ورجعنا عنه، ومن أبى قاتلناه
> أبدًا حتى نفضي إلى موعود (٥) الله. قالوا: وما موعود الله؟ قال: الجنةُ لمن ماتَ على قتال منْ أبى، والظفرُ
> لمن بقي
> — ⁨البدایہ والنہایہ ج۷ ص۱۳۴⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/30097/3280
> *English:* **"God sent us to bring out whoever wishes, from the worship of servants to the worship
> of God; from the narrowness of this world to its breadth; and from the injustice of the religions
> to the justice of Islam.**" He sent us with His religion to His creation to call them to it.
> Whoever accepts it, we accept that from him and turn back from him; and whoever refuses, we fight
> him until we come to what God has promised." They said: "And what has God promised?" He said:
> **"The Garden, for whoever dies fighting the one who refuses; and victory, for whoever remains."**

And, asked whether he was their chief — same page:

> لا: ولكن المسلمون كالجسد الواحد يجير أدناهم على أعلاهم
> — ⁨البدایہ والنہایہ ج۷ ص۱۳۴⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/30097/3280
> *English:* **"No — but the Muslims are like one body: the lowest of them can give protection that
> binds the highest of them."**

⚠ **VIA ⁨سيف⁩ / ⁨الطبری⁩** — the page's own footnote gives ⁨تاريخ الطبري ۳⁩/۵۱۸–۵۱۹. **This is the most
famous sentence of the entire period and it is not soundly chained.** It is on an approved page, so
it is inside the course frame; it must be introduced as *"the way the books tell it"*, and it must
never be given a chain it does not have.

**Reading aloud:** the first passage ~26 seconds — a full slide, and it earns it; the tricolon alone
(«⁨من عبادةِ العباد⁩ … ⁨إلى عدلِ الإسلام⁩») is ~10 seconds. «⁨كالجسد الواحد⁩» is ~7 and is the better slide
for this room, because it is about the ummah rather than about war.

On the **preceding** page, ⁨البدایہ ج۷ ص۱۳۴⁩ also has ⁨المغيرة بن شعبة ؓ⁩'s earlier answer to the same
court, in three clauses, of which the middle one is the same idea: «**⁨وإخراجُ العبادِ من عبادةِ⁩
⁨العبادِ إلى عبادةِ اللهِ⁩**» … «**⁨والناسُ بنو آدمَ، فهم إخوةٌ لأبٍ وأمٍّ⁩**» — and ⁨رستم⁩'s repeated
«⁨وحسنٌ أيضًا⁩» after each. That last clause — *people are the children of Adam, brothers by one
father and one mother* — is another anti-tribal line, said to a foreign court.

## 4.2 The Ghassānī scout's report — ⁨البدایہ ج۷ ص۹۹⁩ · **PLAIN, but with an unnamed link**

**Setting.** The Muslims are camped in the Jordan country. The commander of Damascus sends for two
Christian Arabs in the market and asks one of them to go and look at these people. He comes back:

> جئتكَ من عند رجال (٣) دقاق يركبون خيولا عتاقًا، أما الليل فرهبان، وأما النهار ففرسان، يريشون النبل
> ويبرونها، ويثقفون القنا، لو حدَّثت جليسك حديثًا ما فهمه عنك لما علا من أصواتهم بالقرآن والذكر. قال
> فالتفت إلى أصحابه وقال: أتاكم منهم ما لا طاقة لكم به
> — ⁨البدایہ والنہایہ ج۷ ص۹۹⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/30097/3245
> *English:* "I have come to you from lean men riding thoroughbred horses. **By night they are monks
> and by day they are horsemen.** They feather their arrows and trim them and straighten their
> spears — **and if you spoke to the man sitting beside you he would not catch what you said, for the
> noise of their voices with the Qurʾān and the remembrance of God.**" He turned to his companions
> and said: **"There has come upon you from them what you have no strength against."**

⚠ The chain is «⁨الوليد بن مسلم⁩: **⁨أخبرني من سمع⁩** ⁨يحيى بن يحيى الغساني⁩…» — there is an **unnamed
man** in it. Report it as an account, not as testimony. **It is still the best picture of the army in
the whole note, and it is an outsider's picture, which is why it works.**

**Reading aloud:** ~26 seconds whole; «⁨أمَّا الليلُ فرُهبانٌ، وأمَّا النهارُ ففُرسانٌ⁩» is four words
and about three seconds and is one of the two or three best short slides available.

## 4.3 Heraclius asks his own men why they keep losing — ⁨البدایہ ج۷ ص۹۹⁩ · **PLAIN (maqṭūʿ)**

**Setting.** After ⁨اليرموك⁩, at Antioch, with the beaten army coming in. He asks: are these people not
human beings like you? Are you not more numerous? Then why do you run? An old man of their great men
answered:

> من أجل أنهم يقومون الليل ويصومون النهار، ويوفون بالعهد، ويأمرون بالمعروف، وينهون عن المنكر، ويتناصفون
> بينهم، ومن أجل أنا نشرب الخمر، ونزني، ونركب الحرام، وننقض العهد، ونغْصب ونظلم … فقال: أنت صدقتني
> — ⁨البدایہ والنہایہ ج۷ ص۹۹⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/30097/3245
> *English:* "Because they stand at night and fast by day; they keep their covenant; they command
> what is right and forbid what is wrong; and they deal justly among themselves — **and because we
> drink wine, and commit adultery, and ride into the forbidden, and break covenants, and seize and
> oppress** …" He said: **"You have told me the truth."**

⚠ Chain: «⁨أبو معاوية عن عمرو عن أبي إسحاق قال⁩» — a maqṭūʿ statement of ⁨أبي إسحاق السبيعي⁩, with no
grading on the page.

**Reading aloud:** ~24 seconds. **This is the most usable "why did they win" slide in the note**,
because the explanation is put in an enemy's mouth and is entirely moral. It is also the honest
alternative to any triumphal military account, and it costs the speaker nothing to say that the
report reaches us through one man in Kufa two generations later.

## 4.4 ⁨المثنى بن حارثة ؓ⁩ answers a Persian king's insult — ⁨البدایہ ج۷ ص۱۰۰⁩ · **VIA ⁨الطبری⁩**

⁨شهريار⁩ wrote to him that the army sent against him was made of the dregs of Persia, "keepers of
chickens and pigs", and that he would not fight him with better. ⁨المثنى ؓ⁩ wrote back:

> إنما أنتَ أحدُ رجلين إما باغٍ فذلك (٥) شرٌّ لك وخيرٌ لنا، وإما كاذبٌ فأعظمُ الكاذبين عقوبةً وفضيحةً عند
> الله في الناس الملوك … فالحمد الله الذي ردَّ كيدَكم إلى رعاة الدجاجِ والخنازير
> — ⁨البدایہ والنہایہ ج۷ ص۱۰۰⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/30097/3246
> *English:* "You are one of two men: either a man overreaching — and that is worse for you and
> better for us — or a liar; **and of all liars, the ones God punishes and exposes most before the
> people are kings** … So praise be to God, who has reduced your scheming to keepers of chickens and
> pigs."

⁨ابن كثير⁩ adds that the Persians were dismayed by the letter and blamed ⁨شهريار⁩ for having written
his own. **~20 seconds.** GOOD rather than CORE: it is sharp, but it is a taunt, and the register of
this course is dignified.

## 5.1 ⁨سلمان ؓ⁩, dying — ⁨سیر ج۱ ص۵۵۲⁩ · **GRADED SOUND** (the editor: «⁨حديث صحيح⁩»)

**Setting.** ⁨سعد بن أبي وقاص ؓ⁩ and ⁨ابن مسعود ؓ⁩ came in to him at ⁨المدائن⁩ as he was dying, and found
him weeping.

> قَالَ: عَهْدٌ عَهِدَهُ إِلَيْنَا رَسُوْلُ اللهِ - صَلَّى اللَّهُ عَلَيْهِ وَسَلَّمَ - لَمْ نَحْفَظْهُ.
> قَالَ: (لِيَكُنْ بَلاَغُ أَحَدِكُم مِنَ الدُّنْيَا كَزَادِ الرَّاكِبِ) . وَأَمَّا أَنْتَ يَا سَعْدُ،
> فَاتَّقِ اللهَ فِي حُكْمِكَ إِذَا حَكَمْتَ، وَفِي قَسْمِكَ إِذَا قَسَمْتَ، وَعِنْدَ هَمِّكَ إِذَا
> هَمَمْت
> — ⁨سیر أعلام النبلاء ج۱ ص۵۵۲⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/10906/1978
> *English:* He said: "A charge the Messenger of God ﷺ laid on us which we have not kept. He said:
> **'Let what any one of you takes from this world be like the provisions of a rider.'** And as for
> you, Saʿd — **fear God in your judgement when you judge, and in your dividing when you divide, and
> in your intention when you set your mind on something.**"

Thābit's addition on the next page, ⁨ص۵۵۳⁩: **«⁨ما تركَ إلَّا بضعةً وعشرينَ درهمًا⁩»** — *he left only
twenty-odd dirhams* — from a man who had governed ⁨المدائن⁩. (The other report on that page values the
estate at «⁨بضعة وعشرون درهما،⁩ **⁨أو بضعة وثلاثون درهما⁩**» — twenty-odd *or* thirty-odd.) In the longer
version at ⁨ص۵۵۶⁩ (which ⁨أبو حاتم⁩ prefers as mursal) ⁨سعد ؓ⁩ asks *what makes you weep after eighty
years?* — and the answer, **quoted whole because the two halves must not be run together**, is:
«**وَاللهِ مَا يُبْكِيْنِي وَاحِدَةٌ مِنْ ثِنْتَيْنِ، مَا أَبْكِي حُبّاً بِالدُّنْيَا، وَلاَ
⁨كَرَاهِيَةً لِلِقَاءِ اللهِ⁩**» (⁨سیر ج۱ ص۵۵۶⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/10906/1982) — *"By God, it is
not one of two things that makes me weep: I do not weep out of love for this world, nor out of
dislike of meeting God."*

**Reading aloud:** the charge to ⁨سعد ؓ⁩ is ~11 seconds and is the slide — three offices of a governor
in three clauses, said by a dying man to the conqueror of ⁨العراق⁩.

**One more, warm and short** — ⁨سیر ج۱ ص۵۵۱⁩ · **PLAIN**: a guest wished there were thyme with the
bread and salt, so ⁨سلمان ؓ⁩ pawned his water-jug to buy some; when the guest then said *praise God who
has made us content with what He provides*, ⁨سلمان ؓ⁩ said: «**⁨لَوْ قَنِعْتَ لَمْ تَكُنْ مِطْهَرَتِي⁩
⁨مَرْهُوْنَةً⁩**» — *"if you had been content, my water-jug would not be in pawn."*
(https://shamela.ws/book/10906/1977) ~7 seconds.

## 5.2 ⁨أبو ذر ؓ⁩ — what the fetched pages actually carry

**Honest finding: the ⁨أبو ذر ؓ⁩ pages fetched in this pass (⁨سیر ج۲ ص۴۶⁩–۵۱) carry no saying of his own
that can be fixed inside ۱۱–⁨۲۳ھ⁩.** What they do carry, and carry well, is the ḥadīth qudsī he is

⁨أبو إدريس الخولاني⁩ used to go down on his knees before narrating it:

> يَا عِبَادِي، إِنِّي حَرَّمتُ الظُّلْمَ عَلَى نَفْسِي، وَجَعَلْتُهُ بَيْنَكُم مُحَرَّماً، فَلاَ
> تَظَالَمُوا … يَا عِبَادِي، إِنَّمَا هِيَ أَعْمَالُكُم أَحْفَظُهَا عَلَيْكُم، فَمَنْ وَجَدَ خَيْراً،
> فَلْيَحْمَدِ اللهَ، وَمَنْ وَجَدَ غَيْرَ ذَلِكَ، فَلاَ يَلُوْمَنَّ إِلاَّ نَفْسَه

⚠ **The Shamela page prints the phrase as «⁨أَحْفَظُهَا عَلَيْكُم⁩» in one place. Read it off a printed
⁨مسلم⁩ before it goes on a slide** — this is the one item in the note where the words are God's, and
§0.2 applies with full force.

**Reading aloud:** the two bolded clauses ~14 seconds. **Tier CORE for the ethics of the conquest
slides**, but attribute it correctly: it is a ḥadīth qudsī narrated by ⁨أبو ذر ؓ⁩, not a saying of his.

## 6.1 «يا سارية الجبل» — البدایہ ج۷ ص۲۵۹–۲۶۰

**Setting.** ⁨سارية بن زُنَيْم ؓ⁩ is besieged at ⁨فَسَا⁩ and ⁨دَارَابجَرْد⁩. ⁨عمر ؓ⁩, on the minbar in Medina
in the middle of the khuṭba, calls out.

> فبينما عمرُ يخطب (فجعل ينادي): يا ساري الجبل (يا ساري الجبل ثلاثًا) ثم قدم رسولُ الجيش فسأله عمر: فقال:
> يا أميرَ المؤمنين هُزمنا، فبينما نحن كذلك إذ سمعنا مناديًا: يا سارية الجبلَ، ثلاثًا -فأسندنا ظهورنا
> بالجبل، فهزمهم الله … وهذا إسناد جيد حسن
> — ⁨البدایہ والنہایہ ج۷ ص۲۶۰⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/30097/3406
> *English:* While ʿUmar was giving the sermon he began to call out: **"Sāriya — the mountain! Sāriya
> — the mountain!"** three times. Then the army's messenger arrived and ʿUmar questioned him. He
> said: "Commander of the Believers, we were being routed — and while we were in that state we heard
> a caller: **'Sāriya, the mountain!'** three times. So we put our backs to the mountain, and God
> routed them." … **"And this is a good, sound chain."**

**And what ⁨عمر ؓ⁩ himself said about it** — same page, in the ⁨الواقدي⁩ route:

> فقيل لعمر بن الخطاب: ما ذلك الكلام؟ فقال: والله ما ألقيت له إلَّا بشيء ألْقِيَ على لساني
> — ⁨البدایہ والنہایہ ج۷ ص۲۶۰⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/30097/3406
> *English:* ʿUmar was asked: "What was that speech?" He said: **"By God, I gave no thought to it at
> all — it was something cast onto my tongue."**

⚠ **How to handle this.** ⁨ابن كثير⁩ gives four routes: the ⁨سيف⁩ route (⁨ج۷ ص۲۵۹⁩, **VIA ⁨الطبری⁩**), the
⁨ابن وهب⁩ ← ⁨نافع⁩ ← ⁨ابن عمر⁩ route which he grades **⁨جيد حسن⁩**, the ⁨الواقدي⁩ routes, and a ⁨مالك⁩ ← ⁨نافع⁩
route about which he says «**⁨وفي صحته من حديث مالك نظر⁩**». He then concludes: «**⁨فهذه طرقٌ يشدُّ⁩
⁨بعضُها بعضًا⁩**». **Narrate it as ⁨ابن كثير⁩ narrates it — with his grading spoken aloud — and stop
there.** Do not build a doctrine of ⁨كرامات⁩ on it and do not deny it; both are outside what the page
supports. ⁨عمر ؓ⁩'s own «⁨ما ألقيتُ له إلَّا بشيءٍ أُلقيَ على لساني⁩» is the sentence that keeps the item
modest, and it should be on the slide with the story.

**Reading aloud:** the scene ~24 seconds; ⁨عمر ؓ⁩'s own explanation ~7. **GOOD, not CORE** — the room
will love it, and it must not become the thing they remember about ⁨عمر ؓ⁩.

## 6.2 The messenger who did not know he was eating with the caliph — ⁨البدایہ ج۷ ص۲۵۹⁩, ۲۶۲ · **VIA ⁨سيف⁩**

⁨سارية ؓ⁩'s messenger came in with the fifth and a casket of jewels the army had asked to give ⁨عمر ؓ⁩
personally. He found a man standing with a stick feeding people, who told him to sit and eat; then
followed him home to bread, oil and salt; and only after the meal said who he was. ⁨عمر ؓ⁩ **refused
the casket outright and ordered it sent back to be divided among the fighters** (⁨ج۷ ص۲۵۹⁩, and again
in the parallel report at ⁨ج۷ ص۲۶۲⁩: «⁨فأبى أن يأخذَه وأقسمَ على ذلك، وأمرَه بأن يردَّه فيُقْسَمَ بينَ⁩
الغانمينَ»).

**The statement:** none in the sources we use — the scene carries itself and has no quotable line;
what is quotable is only ⁨أم كلثوم ؓ⁩'s exchange with her husband about her clothing, which is
domestic and better left out. **Tier GOOD, and tell it as narrative, not as a quotation.**

### E-ST1 · ⁨أبو بكر ؓ⁩ takes the office and tells the room to correct him
**Tier:** CORE · **When:** ⁨۱۱ھ⁩, the morning after ⁨السقيفة⁩ `[SOURCED]` · **Map:** n/a
**What happened:** The general pledge had just been given in the mosque, the morning after
⁨السقيفة⁩. ⁨أبو بكر ؓ⁩ stood up to speak for the first time as the man in charge. He did not open with
a claim. He opened by saying he was not the best of them, and by asking to be corrected if he went
wrong. Then he set out how the weak and the strong would be treated in his court. ⁨ابن كثير⁩, who
copies the whole address, adds four words of his own after it: *this chain is sound.*
**The statement:**
> فإني قد وُلّيتُ عليكم ولستُ بخيركم فإنْ أحسنتُ فأعينوني وإن أسأتُ فقوِّموني … أطيعوني ما أطعت اللهَ
> ورسولَه، فإذا عصيْتُ الله ورسولَه فلا طاعة لي عليكم
> — ⁨البدایہ والنہایہ ج۷ ص۶⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/30097/3152
> *English:* "I have been placed over you and I am not the best of you. If I do well, help me; if I do badly, straighten me … Obey me so long as I obey God and His Messenger; if I disobey God and His Messenger, then you owe me no obedience."
**⁨عبرت⁩:** Authority that asks to be corrected has already conceded that it is not the highest thing in the room.
**Hands-up?** Ask before telling it: *when a new leader speaks for the first time, what does he usually say about himself?*

### E-ST2 · ⁨أبو بكر ؓ⁩ will not untie a knot the Prophet ﷺ tied
**Tier:** CORE · **When:** ⁨۱۱ھ⁩, the first days `[SOURCED]` · **Map:** the arrow north to ⁨الجُرْف⁩ and out toward ⁨الشام⁩, drawn while every other frontier is on fire
**What happened:** The Prophet ﷺ had appointed ⁨أسامة ؓ⁩ over an expedition and it had not yet
marched. Then he died, and tribes across Arabia broke away. Senior Companions asked ⁨أبو بكر ؓ⁩ to
hold the army back and use it to defend Medina. He refused. He said he would not undo something the
Messenger of God ﷺ had settled — not if wild animals came to the edges of the city, and not if he
were the last man left in the settlements.
**The statement:**
> والذي نفس أبي بكر بيده لو ظننت أن السباعَ تَخْطفني لأنفذتُ بعثَ أسامةَ كما أمرَ به رسولُ الله ﷺ؛ ولو لم
> يبقَ في القرى غيري لأنفذته
> — ⁨البدایہ والنہایہ ج۷ ص۱۰⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/30097/3156
> *English:* "By Him in whose hand is Abū Bakr's soul — if I thought the wild beasts would snatch me away, I would still send out Usāma's expedition as the Messenger of God ﷺ ordered; and if no one were left in the settlements but me, I would still send it."
**⁨عبرت⁩:** A commitment that is only kept while it is safe was never a commitment.
**Hands-up?** *Your house is surrounded and you have one unit left. Do you send it away, or keep it?*

### E-ST3 · The caliph walks and the eighteen-year-old rides
**Tier:** CORE · **When:** ⁨۱۱ھ⁩, at ⁨الجُرْف⁩ `[SOURCED]` · **Map:** ⁨الجُرْف⁩, just outside Medina
**What happened:** ⁨أبو بكر ؓ⁩ went out on foot to see the army off. ⁨أسامة ؓ⁩, who was about
eighteen, was mounted. The caliph walked beside his horse. ⁨أسامة ؓ⁩ said: either you ride, or I get
down. ⁨أبو بكر ؓ⁩ refused both. Then, before he turned back, he asked ⁨أسامة ؓ⁩ — the young commander —
for permission to keep ⁨عمر ؓ⁩ back in Medina. ⁨عمر ؓ⁩ never met him afterwards without greeting him
as *commander*.
**The statement:**
> وَاللَّهِ لَا نَزَلْتَ وَلَا أَرْكَبُ، وَمَا عَلَيَّ أَنْ أُغَبِّرَ قَدَمَيَّ سَاعَةً فِي سَبِيلِ اللَّه
> — ⁨الکامل فی التاریخ ج۲ ص۱۹۵⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/21712/886
> *English:* "By God, you shall not dismount and I shall not ride. What harm is it to me that my two feet gather dust for an hour in the path of God?"
**⁨عبرت⁩:** Rank inside an office and rank before God are two different measurements, and confusing them is where rulers go wrong.
**Hands-up?** *How old was the commander of the first army the first caliph sent out?*

### E-ST4 · The ten rules given to an army before it marched
**Tier:** CORE · **When:** ⁨۱۱ھ⁩, before ⁨جيش أسامة⁩ marched `[SOURCED]` · **Map:** n/a
**What happened:** With the army drawn up and about to leave, ⁨أبو بكر ؓ⁩ gave it its standing
orders. They are not tactics. They are a list of things the army is forbidden to do — to people, to
animals, and to trees — and they were given before a single one of the great conquests had begun.
The last of them concerns men who have withdrawn into cells and given their lives to worship: leave
them alone.
**The statement:**
> لَا تَخُونُوا، وَلَا تَغْدِرُوا، وَلَا تَغُلُّوا، وَلَا تُمَثِّلُوا، وَلَا تَقْتُلُوا طِفْلًا وَلَا
> شَيْخًا كَبِيرًا وَلَا امْرَأَةً، وَلَا تَعْقِرُوا نَخْلًا وَتُحْرِقُوهُ، وَلَا تَقْطَعُوا شَجَرَةً
> مُثْمِرَةً، وَلَا تَذْبَحُوا شَاةً وَلَا بَقَرَةً وَلَا بَعِيرًا إِلَّا لِمَأْكَلَةٍ، وَسَوْفَ
> تَمُرُّونَ بِأَقْوَامٍ قَدْ فَرَّغُوا أَنْفُسَهُمْ فِي الصَّوَامِعِ، فَدَعُوهُمْ وَمَا فَرَّغُوا
> أَنْفُسَهُمْ لَه … انْدَفِعُوا بِاسْمِ اللَّه
> — ⁨الکامل فی التاریخ ج۲ ص۱۹۶⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/21712/887
> *English:* "Do not betray. Do not act treacherously. Do not steal from the spoils. Do not mutilate. Do not kill a child, nor an aged man, nor a woman. Do not cut down a date-palm and burn it. Do not fell a fruit-bearing tree. Do not slaughter a sheep or a cow or a camel except for food. You will pass by people who have withdrawn into cells and given themselves wholly to what they are in — leave them and what they have given themselves to … Set out, in the name of God."
**⁨عبرت⁩:** The limits on an army are set before it marches, or they are not set at all.
**Hands-up?** no — but the ellipsis on the slide is deliberate and §1.5 tells the speaker what to say if it is challenged.

### E-ST5 · The zakāt refusers, and ⁨عمر ؓ⁩ saying out loud that he had been wrong
**Tier:** CORE · **When:** ⁨۱۱ھ⁩ `[SOURCED]` · **Map:** the delegations arriving into Medina from every direction
**What happened:** Delegations came into Medina accepting the prayer and refusing the zakāt. ⁨عمر ؓ⁩
argued against fighting them, and he had a ḥadīth to argue with. ⁨أبو بكر ؓ⁩ answered him with a
single distinction: the zakāt is a due upon wealth, and it cannot be separated from the prayer. The
report is in the Six except ⁨ابن ماجه⁩ — ⁨ابن كثير⁩ says so on the page. And the last thing in it is not
⁨أبو بكر ؓ⁩'s oath. It is ⁨عمر ؓ⁩ saying, on the record, that he came to see his companion was right.
**The statement:**
> واللهِ لأقاتِلَنَّ منْ فرَّقَ بينَ الصلاةِ والزكاةِ، قال عمر: فما هو إلا أن رأيتُ اللهَ قد شرحَ صدرَ أبي
> بكر للقتال، فعرفتُ أنه الحق
> — ⁨البدایہ والنہایہ ج۷ ص۱۸⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/30097/3164
> *English:* "By God, I will fight whoever separates prayer from zakāt." ʿUmar said: "It was nothing but that I saw God had opened Abū Bakr's breast to the fight — and I knew it was the truth."
**⁨عبرت⁩:** Two men can disagree at the top of a state, settle it by argument, and leave the record of the disagreement standing.
**Hands-up?** *Who was right — the one who wanted to fight, or the one who did not?* Then read the last line and let it answer itself.

### E-ST6 · The audit ⁨أبو بكر ؓ⁩ left behind, and ⁨عمر ؓ⁩ weeping over a blanket
**Tier:** CORE · **When:** ⁨جمادى الآخرة ۱۳ھ⁩ `[SOURCED]` · **Map:** n/a
**What happened:** Dying, ⁨أبو بكر ؓ⁩ told ⁨عائشة ؓ⁩ what he had taken from the Muslims in two years
as caliph. Not a dinar, not a dirham. He had eaten their coarse food and worn their rough cloth.
Three items were in the house that belonged to the treasury: a servant, a camel and a blanket. He
told her to send all three to ⁨عمر ؓ⁩ the moment he died. When they arrived, ⁨عمر ؓ⁩ wept until his
tears ran onto the ground.
**The statement:**
> فَلَمَّا رَآهُ بَكَى حَتَّى سَالَتْ دُمُوعُهُ إِلَى الْأَرْضِ وَجَعَلَ يَقُولُ: رَحِمَ اللَّهُ أَبَا
> بَكْرٍ! لَقَدْ أَتْعَبَ مَنْ بَعْدَه
> — ⁨الکامل فی التاریخ ج۲ ص۲۶۵⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/21712/956
> *English:* When he saw it he wept until his tears ran to the ground, and kept saying: "God have mercy on Abū Bakr! He has worn out whoever comes after him."
**⁨عبرت⁩:** The standard a man sets by his own conduct is the heaviest thing he can leave to his successor.
