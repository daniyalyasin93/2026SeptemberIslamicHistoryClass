# TIMELINE PEGS — the three nested timelines

**Question this note answers.** Session 1 zooms out three times: (1) Pakistan, 1947 → today;
(2) the whole Ummah, ۱ھ → now; (3) the prophets, back to آدم ؑ. Each timeline shades the previous
one as a sliver at its end, so the audience feels the change of scale. **What are the dated pegs
for each, and how certain is each one?**

Written for L01 «ایک نظر میں — پورا نقشہ»; the Ummah table also feeds every later session and
should be promoted into `docs/catalogue/TIMELINE.md`.

## 0. How to read the certainty labels

| Label | Meaning | What the slide must do |
|---|---|---|
| **`[SOURCED]`** | Traceable to a page actually fetched and read. The printed page is given here. | May be stated flatly. Cite if the slide has room. |
| **`[STANDARD]`** | A universally agreed conventional date, **not currently page-cited in this repo**. | Carries `(to verify)` in this note. **Check against a named source before print.** |
| **`[CONVENTIONAL-ESTIMATE]`** | An approximation from general (non-Islamic) scholarship that our Islamic sources **do not fix**. | **Must be labelled as an estimate on the slide itself.** Never presented as coming from our sources. |

**The rule that governs Timeline 3 in particular:** never present a `[CONVENTIONAL-ESTIMATE]`
as if it came from البدایہ والنہایہ or سیر. The order of the prophets is known from revelation;
the years are not.

### Page-citation convention in this note

Printed pages are given **exactly as the Shamela tool reported them**, in Latin digits —
`ج1 ص157`, `ج15 ص312`. That is deliberate: retyping into Urdu-Indic digits is where transcription
errors get introduced. Artifacts that need the Urdu-Indic form (`ج۱ ص۱۵۷`) convert at build time.

### Hijri ↔ Gregorian conversions in this note

Computed with the **tabular (arithmetic) Islamic calendar**, epoch 1 Muḥarram 1 AH = 16 July 622 CE
(Julian), verified against known anchors. **The year mapping is reliable. Day-level conversions can
drift 1–3 days from the observed calendar the sources actually used** — so where a day is given it
is marked, and where the tabular result disagrees with the well-known conventional day, both are
recorded rather than reconciled.

Two independent cross-checks landed exactly, which is why the method is trusted here:

- 14 August 1947 → **27 Ramaḍān 1366** (the widely-known correspondence) ✔
- 3 March 1924 → **27 Rajab 1342** (the widely-known correspondence) ✔
- and 19 July 711 → **24 Ramaḍān 92**, which agrees with الذہبی's own «فِي رَمَضَانَ، سَنَةَ اثْنَتَيْنِ وَتِسْعِيْنَ» ✔

### Where the source is silent, say so

Rows 17–24 are all `[STANDARD]`. That is not a weakness to hide — **البدایہ والنہایہ ends in the
760s ھ because ابن کثیر رحمہ اللہ died in ۷۷۴ھ.** Everything after that needs a different book,
and `docs/catalogue/SOURCES.md` records that sessions 7–9 have no acquired source yet. On the slide
this is an asset: it is the same point the مقدمہ makes about how we know the past. On a printed
handout, do not put a citation next to rows 17–24 until one exists.

### Register warnings on the Crusade and Mongol rows

ابن کثیر's own wording on rows 12 and 15 carries imprecations against the Frankish and Mongol
armies — «خذلهم الله تعالى», «لعنهم الله», «أهل جهنم». That is his text, not ours. **Decide
consciously before it reaches a slide**: either quote the neutral clause only, or quote the whole
line and attribute it plainly as ابن کثیر's wording. Do not silently edit an author's words and
still present the result as a quotation.

Row 12 also carries a casualty figure — «فقتلوا في وسطه أزيد من سبعين (٦) ألف قتيل» (more than 70,000), البدایہ
**ج13 ص260**. The edition's own footnote records that one manuscript (ط) reads **ستين** (60,000),
and that الکامل agrees with 70,000. If the number goes on a slide, it goes with "as ابن کثیر
reports it", not as a settled figure.

# TIMELINE 3 — the prophets

## THE CRITICAL RULE

**Our Islamic sources do not fix dates for آدم، نوح، إبراهيم، موسى، داود ؑ.** They fix the *order*,
they sometimes fix *intervals*, and they record disagreement even about lifespans. The audience is
being shown approximate centuries **purely to feel the distance from our own time**, and the slide
must say so in as many words.

### 3A. What البدایہ والنہایہ actually says — this is `[SOURCED]` and it is the heart of the slide

ابن کثیر opens his book with بدء الخلق and the قصص الأنبياء, and what he supplies is **intervals and
lifespans, with the disagreements left standing** — not a chronology. Six findings:

**(i) Between آدم ؑ and نوح ؑ: ten قرون — and ابن کثیر himself says the word is ambiguous.**
البدایہ **ج1 ص157**. He cites the hadith of أبو أمامة ؓ from صحيح ابن حبان, and a report of
ابن عباس ؓ, then reasons in both directions: if قرن means a hundred years, then **a thousand years**;
if قرن means a generation, and the generations before نوح ؑ lived very long, then **thousands of
years**. He closes with واللَّه أعلم. *He does not choose.*

> <div dir="rtl">وكان بينهما عشرة قرون، كما قال الحافظ أبو حاتم بن حبان في صحيحه (٢): حدَّثنا محمد بن عمر بن يوسف، حدَّثنا محمد بن عبد الملك بن زنجويه، حدَّثنا أبو توبة، حدَّثنا معاوية بن سلام، عن أخيه زيد بن سلام، سمعتُ أبا سلام، سمعتُ أبا أمامة: أنَّ رجلًا قال: يا رسول اللَّه! أنبيُّ كانَ آدمُ؟ قال: "نعم، مُكلَّم". قال: فكم كان بينه وبين نوح؟ قال: "عشرة قرون". قلت: وهذا على شرط مسلم، ولم يخرِّجوه.</div>

> <div dir="rtl">فإن كان المراد بالقرن مئة سنة كما هو المتبادر عند كثير من الناس، فبينهما ألف سنة لا محالة، لكن لا ينفي أن يكون أكثر باعتبار ما قيد به ابن عبَّاس بالإسلام، إذ قد يكون بينهما قرون أُخر متأخرة لم يكونوا على الإسلام، لكن حديث أبي أمامة يدلُّ على الحصر في عشرة قرون، وزادَنا ابنُ عبَّاس أنهم كلهم كانوا على الإسلام. وهذا يردُّ قولَ من زعم من أهل التواريخ وغيرهم من أهلِ الكتاب: أنَّ قابيلَ وبنيه عَبدُوا النَّار، واللَّه أعلم. وإن كانَ المرادُ بالقَرْن الجيلَ من الناس، كما في قوله تعالى: ﴿وَكَمْ أَهْلَكْنَا مِنَ الْقُرُونِ مِنْ بَعْدِ نُوحٍ﴾ [الإسراء: ١٧] وقوله: ﴿ثُمَّ أَنْشَأْنَا مِنْ بَعْدِهِمْ قَرْنًا آخَرِينَ﴾ [المؤمون: ٣١] وقال تعالى: ﴿وَقُرُونًا بَيْنَ ذَلِكَ كَثِيرًا﴾ [الفرقان: ٣٨] وقال ﴿وَكَمْ أَهْلَكْنَا قَبْلَهُمْ مِنْ قَرْنٍ﴾ [مريم: ٧٤] وكقوله ﵇: "خيرُ القرونِ قرني" (٤) الحديث. فقد كان الجيل قبلَ نوح يُعمَّرون الدهور الطويلة، فعلى هذا يكون بين آدم ونوح ألوف من السنين، واللَّه أعلم.</div>

> *English (rendering, not on the slide as a quotation):* "And between them were ten qarn … If what
> is meant by qarn is a hundred years, as most people take it, then between them is a thousand years
> without doubt … And if what is meant is a generation … then between Ādam and Nūḥ are thousands of
> years, and Allah knows best."

⚠ **Honesty note the edition itself forces on us.** ابن کثیر attributes the ابن عباس report to
صحيح البخاري. The muḥaqqiq's footnote says he traced it to الطبري, ابن سعد and الحاكم and adds:
«**ولم أجده في صحيح البخاري، وأظنه سبق قلم**» — "I did not find it in Ṣaḥīḥ al-Bukhārī, and I think
it a slip of the pen." **Do not cite this to البخاري.** Cite it as ابن کثیر reports it, البدایہ
**ج1 ص157**, and note the editor's correction. This is exactly the kind of thing the «کیسے پتا چلا؟»
slot exists for.

**(ii) نوح ؑ born 126 years after آدم ؑ died** — البدایہ **ج1 ص157**, on the authority of ابن جرير:

> <div dir="rtl">وكان مولدُه بعد وفاة آدم بمئة سنة وست وعشرين سنة فيما ذكره ابن جرير (١) وغيره. وعلى تاريخ أهل الكتاب المتقدم يكون بين مولد نوح وموت آدم مئة وست وأربعون سنة.</div>

Note that ابن کثیر gives **two figures side by side** — 126 by ابن جرير, 146 by the chronology of
أهل الكتاب — and does not choose. That is the method, and it is worth saying aloud.

**(iii) آدم ؑ's lifespan is disputed** — البدایہ **ج1 ص153**. A marfūʿ report from ابن عباس ؓ and
أبو هريرة ؓ gives **1000 years**; the Torah gives 930; ابن کثیر rejects the Torah figure where it
conflicts. Again: a *lifespan*, never a *date*.

**(iv) The intervals between the revealed Books** — البدایہ **ج2 ص265**. This is the only
chain of intervals our sources supply that spans موسى → داود → عيسى ؑ:

> <div dir="rtl">أُنزلت التوراة على موسى في ست ليال خلون من شهر رمضان. ونزل الزبور على داود في اثنتي عشرة ليلة خلت من شهر رمضان. وكذلك بعد التوراة بأربع مئة سنة واثنتين وثمانين سنة. وأنزل الإنجيل على عيسى ابن مريم في ثمانية عشرة ليلة خلت من رمضان. بعد الزبور بألف عام وخمسين عامًا. وأنزل الفرقان على محمد ﷺ في أربع وعشرين من شهر رمضان.</div>

> *English (rendering):* "The Tawrāh was sent down upon Mūsā on the sixth night of Ramaḍān. The
> Zabūr was sent down upon Dāwūd on the twelfth night of Ramaḍān — and that was **482 years after
> the Tawrāh**. The Injīl was sent down upon ʿĪsā ibn Maryam on the eighteenth night of Ramaḍān —
> **1050 years after the Zabūr**. And the Furqān was sent down upon Muḥammad ﷺ on the twenty-fourth
> of Ramaḍān."

⚠ **This has a weak chain and must be labelled as such if used.** The isnād as البدایہ gives it is
«قال (٢) أبو زرعة الدمشقي: حدّثنا عبد اللّه بن صالح، حدّثني معاوية بن صالح عمّن حدّثه قال:» — the final
authority is **unnamed**. It is a report ابن کثیر passes on, not a hadith. Presented honestly it is
still valuable: *even the numbers our own tradition preserves come to us as intervals with a soft
chain, not as dates.*

**(v) عيسى ؑ** — البدایہ **ج2 ص265**, on the authority of ابن جرير: revelation came to him at
**thirty**, and he was raised at **thirty-three**.

> <div dir="rtl">وذى ابن جرير في "تأريخه" (٤) أنه أنزل عليه وهو ابن ثلاثين سنةً، ومكث حتى رفع إلى السماء وهو ابن ثلاث وثلاثين سنة، كما سيأتي بيانه إن شاء اللّه تعالى.</div>

*(The «وذى» is the edition's own typo for «وذكر». Quote it as printed or paraphrase; do not silently
correct and still call it a quotation.)*

**(vi) إبراهيم ؑ's age at death is disputed** — البدایہ **ج1 ص256**: «ثم مرض إبراهيم ﵇ وماتَ عن مئة وخمس وسبعين، وقيل: وتسعينَ سنة» (175, or 195), and «وقد وردَ ما يدلُّ أنه عاشَ مئتي سنة، كما قاله ابن الكلبي» (200), and
a hadith in صحيح ابن حبان that he was circumcised at 120 and lived eighty years after. **Four figures,
no verdict.** A lifespan, never a date.

and never a year attached to either. البدایہ ج2 ص265 gives what we say: sent at thirty, raised at
thirty-three.

⚠ **Note what happens when 3A and 3B are laid side by side.** البدایہ's chain gives موسى → داود as
**482 years** and داود → عيسى as **1050 years**. The conventional estimates give roughly 300 and
roughly 1000. **The second interval agrees closely; the first does not.** Record the disagreement,
do not resolve it, and do not draw the timeline in a way that quietly picks a side — the strip for
Timeline 3 should be visibly *unscaled*, with soft edges, not a ruler.

### 3C. آدم ؑ and نوح ؑ go OFF-SCALE

**No number is invented for them. None exists.** On the slide the line does not begin — it runs off
the left edge, fading, with a marker. This is the single most important design decision on the slide
and it is a truthful one: the sliver-within-a-sliver device is what makes the evening land, and it
lands harder when the far end is honestly open.

Draft marker text:

> English — "Ādam ؑ · Nūḥ ؑ — off the scale. No defensible date exists."
>
> <div dir="rtl">اردو — «آدم ؑ · نوح ؑ — پیمانے سے باہر۔ کوئی قابلِ اعتماد تاریخ موجود نہیں۔»</div>

And the one line that turns the gap into content rather than an absence:

> English — "Our sources give an interval, not a date: ten *qarn* between Ādam ؑ and Nūḥ ؑ. Ibn
> Kathīr رحمہ اللہ writes that if a *qarn* is a hundred years that is a thousand; if it is a
> generation, it is thousands. He does not choose."
>
> <div dir="rtl">اردو — «ہمارے مآخذ سَن نہیں دیتے، فاصلہ دیتے ہیں: آدم ؑ اور نوح ؑ کے درمیان دس قرن۔ ابنِ کثیر رحمہ اللہ خود لکھتے ہیں کہ اگر قرن سے سو سال مراد ہوں تو ہزار سال بنتے ہیں، اور اگر نسل مراد ہو تو ہزاروں سال۔ وہ فیصلہ نہیں کرتے۔»</div>

### 3D. The caption line the slide must carry — final drafts

This is the epistemic guard. It is not a footnote; it is set large enough to be read from the back
of the room, and the speaker says it out loud.

**Full form (for the slide):**

> **The order is from revelation. The dates are not.**
> Our sources fix no year for any prophet. The centuries below are modern estimates, put here only
> so the distance can be felt — not asserted as history.

> <div dir="rtl"><b>ترتیب وحی سے معلوم ہے — سَنین نہیں۔</b><br>
> انبیاء علیہم السلام کے لیے ہمارے مآخذ کوئی سَن مقرر نہیں کرتے۔ نیچے جو صدیاں دی گئی ہیں وہ جدید محققین کے تخمینے ہیں — صرف فاصلے کا اندازہ کرانے کے لیے، دعوے کے طور پر نہیں۔</div>

**Short form (for the timeline strip itself, and for the workbook):**

> **Order: from revelation. Dates: modern estimates, not from our sources.**

> <div dir="rtl"><b>ترتیب: وحی سے۔ تاریخیں: جدید تخمینہ — ہمارے مآخذ سے نہیں۔</b></div>

**Typography, per `CLAUDE.md` §1.3:** the Urdu block is `dir="rtl"`, in `Noto Nastaliq Urdu`, in its

### ۱ھ — why the calendar starts at the Hijra · البدایہ **ج3 ص478**

> <div dir="rtl">فمالَ عمرُ ﵁ إلى التاريخ بالهجرة لظهورهِ واشتهاره. واتفقوا معه على ذلك.</div>

> <div dir="rtl">عن سهل بن سعد قال: ما عدُّوا من مَبْعَثِ النبيِّ ﷺ ولا من وفاته، ما عدُّوا إلا من مَقْدَمِه المدينة.</div>

The second is a hadith ابن کثیر quotes from **صحيح البخاري** (the edition's footnote gives

البخاري** — unlike the ابن عباس report at ج1 ص157. It is the best single caption for the ۱ھ peg:
the Ummah did not date itself from the Prophet's ﷺ birth, nor from his passing, but from the day the

### ۴۱ھ — خلافت becomes ملک · البدایہ **ج8 ص5**

> <div dir="rtl">قد تقدَّم في الحديث أنَّ الخلافة بعده ﵇ ثلاثون سنة، ثم تكون مُلكًا، وقد انقضت الثلاثون سنة بخلافة الحسن بن علي، فأيام معاوية أول المُلْك، فهو أولُ ملوك الإسلام وخيارُهم.</div>

> *English:* "It has already been given in the hadith that the caliphate after him ﷺ is thirty
> years, then it becomes kingship. The thirty years came to their end with the caliphate of
> al-Ḥasan ibn ʿAlī. So the days of Muʿāwiya are the beginning of the kingship — and he is the first
> of the kings of Islam **and the best of them**."

**This is the single most important `[SOURCED]` line in the whole note for session 4.** خلافت →
ملوکیت is the evening that sits closest to Tanzeem-e-Islami's own reason for existing
(`CLAUDE.md` §1.5), and here ابن کثیر states the transition **and** the قدر of سیدنا معاویہ ؓ in one
sentence. The speaker narrates this and stops. The room may want him to draw the inference; he does
not. It also fixes the ۱۱–۴۰ھ span of Timeline 2 row 3 from the source rather than by assumption.

> <div dir="rtl">فَبَادَرَ طَارِقٌ، وَعَدَّى فِي جُنْدِهِ، وَهَزَمَ الفِرَنْجَ، وَافْتَتَحَ قُرْطُبَةَ، وَقَتَلَ صَاحِبَهَا لُذْرِيْقَ، وَكَتَبَ بِالنَّصْرِ إِلَى مَوْلاَهُ، فَحَسَدَهُ عَلَى الانْفِرَادِ بِهَذَا الفَتْحِ العَظِيْمِ، وَتَوَعَّدَهُ، وَأَمَرَهُ أَنْ لاَ يَتَجَاوَزَ مَكَانَهُ، وَأَسْرَعَ مُوْسَى بِجُيُوْشِهِ، فَتَلَقَّاهُ طَارِقٌ، وَقَالَ: إِنَّمَا أَنَا مَوْلاَكَ، وَهَذَا الفَتْحُ لَكَ.</div>

*(«١٩٦ -» is الذہبی's entry number in the سیر, not part of the sentence.)* البدایہ **ج9 ص248**
