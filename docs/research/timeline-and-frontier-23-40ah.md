# Timeline, frontier and gazetteer — 23–41 AH

**Question this note answers.** Every date, every place and every frontier line that session 3
(«⁨پہلا امتحان⁩», 23–40 AH) will put on a slide, in one file, each carrying its printed page and its
certainty label — so that no two artifacts can disagree and nobody re-derives a date. It answers
three questions and nothing else:

1. **The dates.** Every dated event in the window, year by year from 23 to 41 AH, in both ⁨ھ⁩ and
   ⁨عیسوی⁩. **Where the safe list disagrees with itself about a year — and in this window it does so
   constantly — both readings are given with the book each comes from.**
2. **The frontier, three times over** — at 23 AH (where session 2 closes), at 35 AH (the killing of
   ⁨عثمان ؓ⁩) and at 40–41 AH (the handover) — region by region, so the opening and the closing maps of
   the evening can be drawn and the change between them is visible.
3. **The gazetteer** — every place the session-3 cards name, with what the sources themselves say
   about where it is, and a modern identification labelled `[CONVENTIONAL-ESTIMATE]` where they say
   nothing.

Its model is `timeline-pegs.md`, which does the same job for the whole 1400 years.

## 0. How to read this note

### 0.1 The certainty labels

| Label | Meaning | What the slide must do |
|---|---|---|
| **`[SOURCED]`** | A page was fetched and read, and it is given. | May be stated flatly. |
| **`[SOURCED, disputed]`** | The page was read **and it carries more than one answer.** | **Must show the spread, never one number.** |
| **`[STANDARD]`** | Conventional and universally agreed, but **not page-cited in this repo**. Carries `(to verify)`. | Check before print. |
| **`[CONVENTIONAL-ESTIMATE]`** | General scholarship; our sources do not fix it. | **Must be labelled as an estimate on the slide itself.** |

### 0.2 The ⁨عیسوی⁩ equivalents are all `[CONVENTIONAL-ESTIMATE]`

Our books give ⁨ھ⁩ years. They give ⁨عیسوی⁩ years nowhere. **Every CE date in this note is computed, not
sourced**, by the tabular (arithmetic) Islamic calendar, epoch 1 Muḥarram 1 AH = 16 July 622 CE, and
rendered in the **Julian** calendar, which is the calendar actually in use in the seventh century and
the one the conventional dates for these events are quoted in.

- **Year-level conversions are reliable.** A ⁨ھ⁩ year straddles two CE years and both are given.
- **Day-level conversions are not.** The tabular calendar can drift 1–3 days from the observed
  calendar the sources used. Where a day is converted it is marked `[CONVENTIONAL-ESTIMATE]` and the
  drift is stated. Two checks: 18 ⁨ذو الحجة⁩ 35 computes to **17 June 656**, which is exactly the
  conventional date for the killing of ⁨عثمان ؓ⁩; 17 ⁨رمضان⁩ 40 computes to **24 January 661**, four days
  from the conventional 28 January 661. **That spread is why no day-level CE date goes on a slide.**

> **Rule for the deck.** The timeline strip carries **⁨ھ⁩ years only**. A CE year appears once, in the
> orientation line — "this is the 650s of the Christian era" — and nowhere else. Latin digits
> throughout (`CLAUDE.md` §1.3).

### 0.3 The page-citation convention here

Printed pages are given exactly as the harvested note gives them — `⁨ج۷ ص۴۳۳⁩`, `⁨سیر جراشدون ص۸۸⁩` —
in Arabic-Indic digits, taken from the cached file's own `PRINTED PAGE:` header, never the Shamela
index. Where a quotation is reproduced it carries the direct `shamela.ws` URL beneath it.

> **This contrast is itself content.** The same book that admits three answers to one question gives

## 3. The frontier, three times over

Three maps, three moments. **The whole point of drawing them is the comparison**, so they must be the

## 5. What could NOT be established

- **A CE date for anything, from our sources.** Not one page gives one. Every ⁨عیسوی⁩ date in this note
  is computed, and the day-level ones drift by up to four days against the conventional figures.
- **A description of the ground at ⁨صفين⁩.** Neither book gives more than *by the ⁨الفرات⁩, east of the
  land of ⁨الشام⁩*, *level and wide*, and *one watering place*. **Any detailed battle map would be
  invention.**
- **How ⁨دومة الجندل⁩ and ⁨أذرح⁩ relate to each other.** ⁨البدایہ⁩ names them together, as one meeting
  place, and gives the distance to ⁨الكوفة⁩ and ⁨الشام⁩. It does not explain the pairing, and on a modern
  map the two candidate sites are far apart. **Record the book's phrase; do not choose a pin.**
- **The year of ⁨النهروان⁩ as a single number.** See §2.
- **A single number for the withdrawal at ⁨حروراء⁩, for the return, or for the last stand.** Four
  figures circulate for the withdrawal alone and the books say so.
- **The date within 40 AH of the ⁨مهادنة⁩**, its place, its negotiators or its text. One sentence in
  ⁨الکامل⁩ is all that survives in the safe list.
- **What the expedition to ⁨الأندلس⁩ under ⁨عثمان ؓ⁩ actually did.** Both books use the word ⁨فتح⁩ and
  neither describes a landing, a battle, a town, a garrison or a governor.
- **The strength of the Muslim army at ⁨سبيطلة⁩**, and **the casualties at ⁨ذات الصواري⁩**. Numbers exist
  for neither in a form that can be printed.
- **Which of the accounts of ⁨يزدجرد⁩'s death is correct.** ⁨ابن الأثير⁩ refuses to choose.
- **The name of the Syrian claimant at the ⁨حج⁩ of 39.**
- **The ⁨خراسان⁩ governor's name in 38.** Two spellings, plus «⁨وقيل ابن أبزى⁩».
- **Whether ⁨طبرستان⁩ had genuinely never been raided before 30 AH.** Both books report ⁨سيف⁩'s contrary
  account and leave it open; ⁨البدایہ⁩ closes with «⁨فالله أعلم⁩».
