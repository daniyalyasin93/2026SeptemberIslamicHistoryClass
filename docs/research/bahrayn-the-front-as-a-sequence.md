# Bahrayn — the front as a sequence of map events

**Question this note answers:** The Bahrayn front of the ridda is being redrawn as animated maps — one
theatre map and two close-ups — and every arrow, icon and caption has to come off a page. So:
**(A)** is the Prophet's ﷺ embassy to al-Mundhir b. Sāwā on a page of the approved books, and in what
year does the page put it; **(B)** what is every discrete event of the front, in the order the pages
give it — who, does what, from where, to where; **(C)** for each one, what must *not* be claimed;
**(D)** what do the pages say each place is.

**Raised by:** Daniyal's review of the evening-6 deck, slides 10–24, on 2026-10-08. §1 answers each of
his questions in one line, with its page.

**Written:** 2026-10-08, against commit `db9300c`.

**Read first, and do not repeat:** `ridda-campaign-the-conduct-of-the-wars.md` §7.1–§7.4 and §12.3
(the narrative), and the cards `RCT/E-RC71, RC72, RC24, RC25, RC26, RC73, RC74, RC27, RC75, RC28` and
`ATA/E-TB24` in `L02_baarah_saal/CONTENT.md`. **This note is the sequence and the geometry.** Where it
corrects a card or that note, §0 says so and gives the line.

**Sources read — the pages themselves, not the earlier note.** The Bahrayn chapter whole in both

> ⚠ **Four standing cautions.** (1) **The sequence the deck tells is one telling — Sayf b. ʿUmar's**, as
> ⁨الکامل⁩ and ⁨البدایہ⁩ carry it; the editor's footnotes at ⁨البدایہ ج۷ ص۳۷⁩ and ⁨البدایہ ج۷ ص۴۰⁩ point to ⁨الطبری⁩
> throughout. The same pages we hold carry **four other tellings** that disagree with it on things a map
> draws — where the night battle was, when Dārīn was, what Juwāthā was. They are laid out in §5 and are
> not resolved. (2) **The Arabic below was lifted byte-for-byte out of the cache files**, not retyped; it
> is still Shamela's text, and anything that reaches a slide must be eyeballed against a page image
> (CLAUDE.md §1.1, §1.4). Three words in the cached ⁨الکامل⁩ are doubtful and are flagged where they occur.
> (3) **No coordinate in this note.** The pages place every one of these sites relatively. §4 gives what
> the page says each place is, and labels every modern identification `[CONVENTIONAL-ESTIMATE]`.
> (4) The water at al-Dahnāʾ and the crossing to Dārīn are **⁨كرامہ⁩ narratives** — Ibn Kathīr ⁨رحمہ اللہ⁩ uses
> that word for them himself (⁨البدایہ ج۵ ص۵۰۷⁩; ⁨البدایہ ج۷ ص۴۰⁩). Narrate them as what the book narrates;
> build no doctrinal point on them; and draw what the page describes, which is the wading of a shallow
> strait (§3.9).

## 0. What this note changes — the short list for the builder

Line numbers are `L02_baarah_saal/CONTENT.md` **as committed at `db9300c`** — the working copy was being
edited while this note was written — and each is given with its card and beat so it can be found again.
Quotation keys in `code` (`V1`, `H2` …) are the labels on the Arabic blocks in §2–§4.

1. **The man they made king is captured on the night of the trench battle, not at Dārīn.** ⁨الکامل⁩
   narrates the pursuit, his capture and his Islam, then "and when morning came al-ʿAlāʾ divided the
   spoils", and only *then* the flight to Dārīn (`V1`, then `W1`; both on ⁨الکامل ج۲ ص۲۲۴⁩, in that order).
   `RC27` beat 11 (line 2770) tells it at Dārīn, and the warning on `RC71` (line 3625) says it "belongs
   at" Dārīn. Move it to the Hajar close-up. The award of al-Ḥuṭam's cloak to Thumāma ؓ is the same
   morning (`V2`).
2. **"He ordered that the enemy be come at from behind his own back" is a misreading.** `RC27` beat 6
   (line 2765) translates the cached verb of `W3` as *he ordered*. The sense of the passage is the
   opposite — the roads are sealed, so he *no longer fears* being come at from behind — and ⁨الطبری⁩
   (corroboration only, ⁨ج۳ ص۳۱۰⁩) has exactly that: he was now certain nothing would come at him
   from the rear. **No arrow "from behind" on any map.**
3. **ʿAbd al-Qays did not "never break".** `RC24` beat 8 (line 2532) says "one tribe … never broke at
   all". ⁨الکامل⁩ says the slogan was *theirs* — "it had reached him that **they** said" — and that after
   al-Jārūd ؓ spoke *they accepted Islam*, and held (`M2`, `G1`). Ibn Isḥāq, in ⁨البدایہ⁩, says the same of
   "those of his people who had accepted Islam" (`J4`). **Say: he brought his own tribe back, and they
   held.** That is also the one-line background Daniyal asked for on `ATA/E-TB24`.
4. **"A few days' ride" is on no page — and neither is any position for ʿAbd al-Qays.** `ATA/E-TB24`
   beat 1 (line 783) and `RC24` beat 8 (line 2532) set them "a few days' ride" from Juwāthā, and the
   scene draws al-Jārūd ؓ to the south of it. No page gives a distance or a direction. What the pages
   do give: Juwāthā is ʿAbd al-Qays's own place — "the mosque of ʿAbd al-Qays at Juwāthā" (`H8`), and
   the editors' glosses (`P4`, `P8`); in ⁨البدایہ⁩'s running text the people al-Jārūd ؓ stands up among
   are the same "them" as the besieged (`G6`); and at the trenches his side is the side of al-Ḥuṭam
   away from Hajar (`T1`). **Whether he was inside the ring the pages do not settle** (§3.12). Put his
   marker **beside Juwāthā**, on his tribe's ground, and move it when the page moves it.
5. **al-Ḥuṭam "came down at" al-Qaṭīf and Hajar; the page does not say he "took" them.** The verb in
   `H2` is *nazala*. `RC72` (lines 3654, 3661), `RC74` (line 3799) and the map key in
   `S06_hadramawt_bahrayn/build.py:96` (also at `db9300c`) — `("al-Qaṭīf, Hajar", "taken")` — all say
   *took*. And al-ʿAlāʾ ؓ
   later camps **at Hajar** itself (`T1`). The map label `l_0308`, "al-Qaṭīf, then Hajar", also supplies
   an order the page does not give: it says *and*.
6. **Nobody goes to al-Khaṭṭ.** The verb in `H2` is *istaghwaw* — they led it astray, won it over. On
   the map it is a colour change on a stretch of coast, never an arrow. The dashed arrow Daniyal asked
   about on slide 14 is scene object `r_0309`, the detachment to Dārīn, left standing a step too long.
7. **The uncle comes before the report.** In ⁨الکامل⁩ the order is: the noise → the volunteer → seized →
   the uncle → fed → mounted and let through → *then* "he entered the Muslims' camp and told them the
   people were drunk" → the assault (`N1`–`N6`, consecutive on ⁨الکامل ج۲ ص۲۲۳⁩). `RC73` beat 1 (line 3727)
   opens "back up a few minutes", so the deck knowingly tells it after `RC26`'s words slide. **Daniyal
   is right: it happened before.**
8. **The month and the trenches are ⁨الکامل⁩'s alone.** ⁨البدایہ⁩ has no trench and no month: the two
   armies camp side by side, and it is that night (`T4`, `N7`). Cite ⁨الکامل⁩ for both, never "both books".
9. **The two books do not describe the water the same way.** `RC27` (line 2865) says they do. ⁨الکامل⁩:
   water that **covers** the camels' pads (`X6`). ⁨البدایہ⁩: water that does **not** cover them, and does
   not reach the horses' knees (`X3`). Three more depths are on other pages (§3.9).
10. **"A day and a night" is the ships' passage, not the army's.** Both books say so (`X3`, `X6`), and
    ⁨البدایہ⁩ says the army crossed, fought and came back "all of that in one day" (`X4`). The map label
    `l_0331`, "straight across the water — a day and a night", reads as the crossing time.
11. **Abū Hurayra ؓ is not said to have "found the pool still there".** `RC25` beat 8 (line 2568). ⁨سیر⁩
    has the man who went back find his gear "and not the water" (`D14`); the Sahm b. Minjāb telling in
    ⁨البدایہ⁩ has "as though water had never touched it" (`D13`); ⁨الکامل⁩'s cached wording goes the other
    way (`D15`). **State neither.** What they share is the guide's sentence — he had never seen water in
    that place before that day — and the water-skin left as a marker.
12. **The "Abnāʾ" on this road are a clan of Tamīm.** `RC25` (lines 2558, 2563) lists "the Abnāʾ" among
    those who joined. The room has met *the* Abnāʾ as the Persians of Ṣanʿāʾ, on the Yemen evening.
    ⁨الکامل⁩ glosses these ones on the page: *these are clans of Tamīm* (`S5`). **Say "clans of Tamīm";
    do not say "the Abnāʾ".**
13. **It was Qays b. ʿĀṣim who had divided the ṣadaqa out — he had not "held it back".** `RC25`
    (line 2558). ⁨الکامل⁩: he divided it among al-Muqāʿis and al-Buṭūn, repented, and when al-ʿAlāʾ ؓ drew
    near "brought out the ṣadaqa and met him with it, then went out with him" (`S7`, `S5`).
14. **The trench is not said to lie "between Hajar and al-Qaṭīf".** `RC26` (line 2661), `RC74`
    (line 3796), and `ridda-campaign-the-conduct-of-the-wars.md:1696`. The page gives two *sides* —
    al-ʿAlāʾ ؓ "on the side next to Hajar", al-Jārūd ؓ "on the side next to him" — and no line between two
    towns (`T1`). Draw the sides; label nothing "between".
15. **`RC73`'s second quotation rests on a doubtful word.** Line 3754; it is `N3` below. ⁨الطبری⁩
    (corroboration only, ⁨ج۳ ص۳۰۸⁩) reads the verb as *uqtalu* — *why am I to be killed, with the
    troops of ʿIjl all round me?* — which is what the scene needs. Read it off a page image before it is
    spoken.
16. **A fourth form of the king's name**, on a safe-list page the cards do not list: the ⁨لقب⁩ given as the
    *son* — "al-Gharūr b. al-Mundhir b. al-Nuʿmān b. al-Mundhir" (`J4`, ⁨البدایہ ج۵ ص۱۹⁩). It strengthens
    the standing rule: name no king.
17. **al-Jārūd ؓ now has an ending on a page.** `ATA/E-TB24` beat 2 (line 784) says our pages give him no
    dates. Still no dates — but ⁨الکامل ج۲ ص۳۶۲⁩ has him killed at a place called Ṭāwūs, in Persia, on the
    sea expedition al-ʿAlāʾ ؓ sent out in ʿUmar's ؓ time (`P13`, `J5`), and ⁨البدایہ ج۵ ص۱۹⁩ carries Ibn
    Hishām's note that he had been a Christian (`J2`).
18. **New material that is on no card: the words of the duʿāʾ.** §3.6. They are on ⁨البدایہ ج۶ ص۲۳۱⁩ and
    ⁨البدایہ ج۶ ص۳۸۴⁩, through a chain that is *not* Sayf's.

`A1`

> وَفِيهَا بَعَثَ رَسُولُ اللَّهِ - صَلَّى اللَّهُ عَلَيْهِ وَسَلَّمَ - الرُّسُلَ إِلَى كِسْرَى وَقَيْصَرَ وَالنَّجَاشِيِّ وَغَيْرِهِمْ

### 2.2 What happened when he arrived — and it is not "Bahrayn became Muslim"

`A3`

> وَأَمَّا الْمُنْذِرُ بْنُ سَاوَى وَالِي الْبَحْرَيْنِ، فَلَمَّا أَتَاهُ الْعَلَاءُ بْنُ الْحَضْرَمِيِّ يَدْعُوهُ وَمَنْ مَعَهُ بِالْبَحْرَيْنِ إِلَى الْإِسْلَامِ أَوِ الْجِزْيَةِ، وَكَانَتْ وِلَايَةُ الْبَحْرَيْنِ لِلْفُرْسِ، فَأَسْلَمَ الْمُنْذِرُ بْنُ سَاوَى، وَأَسْلَمَ جَمِيعُ الْعَرَبِ بِالْبَحْرَيْنِ

`A4`

### 2.4 How the slide may be labelled

- **Title — `[SOURCED]`:** *"Before the conquest of Mecca: al-ʿAlāʾ b. al-Ḥaḍramī ؓ is sent to al-Mundhir
  b. Sāwā"* (⁨البدایہ ج۵ ص۱۹⁩–۲۰; ⁨البدایہ ج۴ ص۵۱۷⁩).
- **Year line, if one is wanted — `[SOURCED]`, as two years:** *"6 AH — or, it is said, 8 AH"*
  (⁨الکامل ج۲ ص۹۱⁩). **Not** *"8 AH"* alone: the only page that dates this embassy gives 8 as the *qīla*,
  the "it is said".
- **The word "letter" — `[STANDARD]` *(to verify)*.** Every page above says he *sent al-ʿAlāʾ*. The
  embassy sits inside the two chapters on the letters to the kings, so a letter is the natural reading
  and a letter icon is defensible — but **no page we hold quotes a letter to al-Mundhir, or says in so
  many words that al-ʿAlāʾ ؓ carried one.** Caption the arrow *"sends al-ʿAlāʾ ؓ"*; if the face says
  *letter*, the notes carry the label.
- **Who he was, from the page:** "the governor of Bahrayn" — and the governorship was the Persians'
  (⁨الکامل⁩); "its king" (⁨البدایہ⁩); "al-ʿAbdī", "the brother of Banū ʿAbd al-Qays" (Ibn Isḥāq). No ؓ: no
  page we hold places him in the Prophet's ﷺ presence — `RC71`'s ruling stands.

⚠ **Do not merge this with the letter to Kisrā.** A page of the same chapter has a letter pass *through*
Bahrayn, and names nobody:

same chapter on Banū Tamīm, ⁨الکامل ج۲ ص۲۰۹⁩); (4) **al-Rabāb**. No place is given for (2)–(4).

⚠ **The last word of `S4`.** The cache vocalises it *ʿuddatihi*, "his gear"; the cards read it as a
number, "a force the size of his own", and ⁨الطبری⁩ (corroboration only, ⁨ج۳ ص۳۰۶⁩) supports that
sense — *the like of his army*. **Either way the page gives no figure.** Sixteen is the only number on
this road.

**Not on the page:** any number after the sixteen; where the clans of Tamīm met him; the road from
Medina to al-Yamāma; that Thumāma ؓ or Qays "answered the letter" — the page says they *caught him up*.

### 3.5 al-Dahnāʾ: the night — event 15

`D1`

> فَسَلَكَ بِهِمُ الدَّهْنَاءَ، حَتَّى إِذَا كَانُوا فِي بُحْبُوحَتِهَا نَزَلَ، وَأَمَرَ النَّاسَ بِالنُّزُولِ
> — ⁨الکامل فی التاریخ ج۲ ص۲۲۲⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/21712/913
> *English:* “So he took them through al-Dahnāʾ, until, when they were in the very middle of it, he halted, and ordered the people to halt …”

`D2`

> فِي اللَّيْلِ، فَنَفَرَتْ إِبِلُهُمْ بِأَحْمَالِهَا، فَمَا بَقِيَ عِنْدَهُمْ بَعِيرٌ وَلَا زَادٌ وَلَا مَاءٌ
> — ⁨الکامل فی التاریخ ج۲ ص۲۲۳⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/21712/914
> *English:* “… in the night; and their camels bolted with their loads, and there was not left with them a camel, nor provision, nor water.”

`D9`

> أنه نزلَ منزلًا فلم يستقر الناسُ على الأرض حتى نَفَرتِ الإبلُ بما عليها من زادِ الجيش وخيامهم وشرابهم، وبقوا على الأرض ليس معهم شيءٌ سوى ثيابهم - وذلك ليلًا - ولم يقدروا منها على بعيرٍ واحدٍ
> — ⁨البدایہ والنہایہ ج۷ ص۳۹⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/30097/3185
> *English:* “… that he halted at a halting-place, and the people had not yet settled on the ground when the camels bolted with what was on them — the army's provisions, their tents and their drink — and they were left on the ground with nothing but their clothes — and that was at night — and they could not recover a single camel of them.”

`D10`

> فركبَ الناسَ من الهمِّ والغم ما لا يُحدُّ ولا يُوصفُ، وجعلَ بعضُهم يوصي إلى بعض، فنادى منادي العلاء، فاجتمعَ الناسُ إليه
> — ⁨البدایہ والنہایہ ج۷ ص۳۹⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/30097/3185
> *English:* “And there came over the people such anxiety and grief as can be neither bounded nor described, and they began making their bequests to one another. Then al-ʿAlāʾ's crier called out, and the people gathered to him.”

`D3`

> مَا هَذَا الَّذِي غَلَبَ عَلَيْكُمْ مِنَ الْغَمِّ؟ فَقَالُوا: كَيْفَ نُلَامُ وَنَحْنُ إِنْ بَلَغْنَا غَدًا لَمْ تُحْمَ الشَّمْسُ حَتَّى نَهْلِكَ
> — ⁨الکامل فی التاریخ ج۲ ص۲۲۳⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/21712/914
> *English:* “What is this grief that has overcome you? They said: How are we to be blamed, when, if we reach tomorrow, the sun will not have grown hot before we perish?”

**The name al-Dahnāʾ is ⁨الکامل⁩'s** (and Abū Hurayra's ؓ, in ⁨سیر⁩ — `D14`). ⁨البدایہ⁩ says only "a
halting-place".

**Not on the page:** where in al-Dahnāʾ, beyond "its very middle"; an hour, beyond "at night"; how many
camels; how many men.

### 3.6 al-Dahnāʾ: the three questions, the prayer, the duʿāʾ, the water — events 16–17

**(a) The three questions — ⁨البدایہ⁩; and ⁨الکامل⁩ has them as three statements.**

`D5`

> أيها الناسُ ألستم المسلمين؟ ألستُم في سبيل الله؟ ألستُم أنصارَ الله؟ قالوا: بلى، قال: فأبشروا فواللهِ لا يخذل اللهُ منْ كانَ في مثل حالكم
> — ⁨البدایہ والنہایہ ج۷ ص۳۹⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/30097/3185
> *English:* “O people — are you not the Muslims? Are you not in the path of Allah? Are you not the helpers of Allah? They said: Yes indeed. He said: Then rejoice — for by Allah, Allah does not forsake those who are in a state like yours.”

`D4`

> لَنْ تُرَاعُوا، أَنْتُمُ الْمُسْلِمُونَ، وَفِي سَبِيلِ اللَّهِ، وَأَنْصَارُ اللَّهِ، فَأَبْشِرُوا، فَوَاللَّهِ لَنْ تُخْذَلُوا
> — ⁨الکامل فی التاریخ ج۲ ص۲۲۳⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/21712/914
> *English:* “You shall not be alarmed. You are the Muslims, and in the path of Allah, and the helpers of Allah. So rejoice — for by Allah, you will not be forsaken.”

**(b) The manner of the prayer — ⁨البدایہ⁩, in order.**

`D6`

> ونُودي بصلاةِ الصبحِ، حين طلعَ الفجرُ فصلَّى بالناس، فلما قضى الصلاةَ جثا على رُكبتيه وجثا الناسُ، ونصبَ في الدعاء ورفع يديه وفعلَ الناسُ مثله حتى طلعتِ الشمسُ
> — ⁨البدایہ والنہایہ ج۷ ص۳۹⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/30097/3185
> *English:* “And the call was given for the dawn prayer when the dawn broke, and he led the people in prayer. And when he had finished the prayer he went down on his knees, and the people went down on theirs; and he set himself to supplication and raised his hands, and the people did as he did, until the sun rose.”

`D7`

> وجعلَ الناسُ ينظرون إلى سراب الشمس يلمعُ مرةً بعد أخرى، وهو يجتهدُ في الدعاء فلما بلغَ الثالثةَ إذا قد خلق الله إلى جانبهم غديرًا عظيمًا من الماءَ القَراح، فمشى ومشى الناسُ إليه فشربوا واغتسلوا
> — ⁨البدایہ والنہایہ ج۷ ص۳۹⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/30097/3185
> *English:* “And the people began looking at the shimmer of the sun's mirage, flashing time after time, while he strove in supplication; and when it reached the third, there, Allah had created beside them a great pool of pure water. So he walked, and the people walked, to it, and they drank and washed.”

`D8`

> فما تعالى النهارُ حتى أقبلتِ الإبل من كلّ فجٍّ بما عليها، لم يفقدِ الناسُ من أمتعتهم سِلْكًا (٣)، فَسَقوا الإبلَ عَللًا بعد نَهل
> — ⁨البدایہ والنہایہ ج۷ ص۳۹⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/30097/3185
> *English:* “And the day had not grown high before the camels came in from every pass with what was on them; the people had not lost a thread of their baggage. And they watered the camels, a second draught after the first.”

`D17`

> فكان هذا مما عاينَ الناس من آيات الله بهذه السرية
> — ⁨البدایہ والنہایہ ج۷ ص۳۹⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/30097/3185 (Ibn Kathīr's own sentence)
> *English:* “And this was among what the people saw with their own eyes of the signs of Allah on this expedition.”

⁨الکامل⁩ gives the same scene in two lines:

`D18`

> فَلَمَّا صَلَّوُا الصُّبْحَ دَعَا الْعَلَاءُ وَدَعَوْا مَعَهُ، فَلَمَعَ لَهُمُ الْمَاءُ، فَمَشَوْا إِلَيْهِ وَشَرِبُوا وَاغْتَسَلُوا. فَمَا تَعَالَى النَّهَارُ حَتَّى أَقْبَلَتِ الْإِبِلُ تُجْمَعُ مِنْ كُلِّ وَجْهٍ، فَأَنَاخَتْ إِلَيْهِمْ فَسَقَوْهَا
> — ⁨الکامل فی التاریخ ج۲ ص۲۲۳⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/21712/914
> *English:* “And when they had prayed the dawn prayer, al-ʿAlāʾ supplicated and they supplicated with him; and the water gleamed before them, so they walked to it and drank and washed. And the day had not grown high before the camels came, gathering from every direction, and knelt down by them, and they watered them.”

**So the manner, for the slide: the dawn prayer in congregation → he goes down on his knees → the
whole army on its knees → hands raised → supplication until the sun is up → the gleam, three times →
the pool.** ⚠ "The third" is the third gleam, not a third supplication: ⁨الطبری⁩ (corroboration only,
⁨ج۳ ص۳۰۷⁩) spells it out — a scout is sent at each gleam and twice reports mirage.

**(c) The words of the duʿāʾ — not in that telling. They are on two other pages of ⁨البدایہ⁩, through
another chain** (Sahm b. Minjāb, by way of Ibn Abī al-Dunyā and al-Bayhaqī), **where the prayer is two
rakʿas and the water is rain.**

`D12`

> وقال في الدعاء: يا عليمُ، يا حليمُ، ويا عليُّ، يا عظيمُ، إنا عبيدُك وفي سبيلك نقاتلُ عدوَّك، اسقنا غَيثًا نشربُ منه ونتوضَّأ، فإذا تركناه فلا تجعل لأحدٍ فيه نصيبًا غيرَنا. وقال في البحر: اجعل لنا سبيلًا إلى عدوِّك
> — ⁨البدایہ والنہایہ ج۶ ص۲۳۱⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/30097/2940 (the telling of Sahm b. Minjāb)
> *English:* “And he said in the supplication: O All-Knowing, O Forbearing, O Most High, O Tremendous — we are Your servants, and in Your path we fight Your enemy; give us rain from which we may drink and make wuḍūʾ, and when we leave it, give no one a share in it but us. And he said at the sea: Make for us a way to Your enemy.”

`D11`

> فنزلنا منزلًا فطلبَ الماء فلم يجده، فقام وصلَّى ركعتين، وقال: اللَّهُمَّ إنَّا عبيدُك وفي سبيلك، نقاتلُ عدوَّك، اللَّهُمَّ اسقنا غيثًا نتوضَأ به ونشربُ، ولا يكونُ لأحد فيه نصيبٌ غيرنا
> — ⁨البدایہ والنہایہ ج۶ ص۳۸۴⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/30097/3093 (the same telling, as Ibn Kathīr's teacher quotes it)
> *English:* “… and we halted at a halting-place, and he sought water and did not find it. So he stood and prayed two rakʿas, and said: O Allah, we are Your servants, and in Your path, fighting Your enemy. O Allah, give us rain to make wuḍūʾ with and to drink, and let no one have a share in it but us.”

`D13`

> وملأتُ إداوتي وتركتها مكانَها حتى أنظر هل استجيب له أم لا، فسرنا قليلًا ثم قلت لأصحابي: نسيتُ إداوتي فرجعت إلى ذلك المكان فكأنَّه لم يصبه ماء قط
> — ⁨البدایہ والنہایہ ج۶ ص۳۸۴⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/30097/3093 (the same telling)
> *English:* “And I filled my water-skin and left it in its place, to see whether he had been answered or not. We travelled a little, then I said to my companions: I have forgotten my water-skin. So I went back to that place — and it was as though water had never touched it.”

**How this may go on a slide.** The three questions (`D5`) are one statement slide, cited to
⁨البدایہ ج۷ ص۳۹⁩. The manner of the prayer (`D6`) is the next. **The words of the duʿāʾ (`D12`) may be
shown — but under their own citation, ⁨البدایہ ج۶ ص۲۳۱⁩, and with the words "in another telling".** They
may not be set inside the dawn scene as though the page that describes the kneeling also gave the
words. It does not.

**For the picture behind the questions:** what the page licenses is night, open sand, and men with
nothing left but their clothes (`D9`). The camels are *gone* in that scene; they come back after
sunrise, "from every pass" (`D8`). So camels belong behind the second slide, not the first.

**(d) Three more tellings of the water, all on pages we hold — none of them to be merged in.**

`D14`

> رَأَيْتُ مِنَ العَلاَءِ ثَلاَثَةَ أَشْيَاءَ، لاَ أَزَالُ أُحِبُّهُ أَبَداً: قَطَعَ البَحْرَ عَلَى فَرَسِهِ يَوْمَ دَارِيْنَ (٣) ، وَقَدِمَ يُرِيْدُ البَحْرَيْنِ، فَدَعَا اللهَ بِالدَّهْنَاءِ، فَنَبَعَ لَهُم مَاءً، فَارْتَوَوْا، وَنَسِيَ رَجُلٌ مِنْهُم بَعْضَ مَتَاعِهِ، فَرَدَّ، فَلَقِيَهُ، وَلَمْ يَجِدِ المَاءَ
> — ⁨سیر أعلام النبلاء ج۱ ص۲۶۵⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/10906/1691 (Abū Hurayra ؓ; the third thing, his burial, is on the next page)
> *English:* “I saw three things from al-ʿAlāʾ for which I shall never cease to love him: he crossed the sea on his horse on the day of Dārīn; and he came, making for Bahrayn, and called on Allah at al-Dahnāʾ, and water welled up for them and they drank their fill; and a man of them forgot some of his gear, and went back, and found it — and did not find the water.”

`D19`

> ثم جهَّزَ عمرُ بن الخطاب جَيشًا واستعملَ عليهم العلاء بن الحضرمي، قال أنس: وكنتُ في غزاتِه فأتينا مغازينا، فوجدنا القومَ قد نذروا بنا فعَفوا آثارَ الماء، والحرُّ شديد، فجَهَدنَا العطشُ ودوائنَا، وذلك يوم الجمعة، فلما مالتِ الشمسُ لغروبها صلَّى بنا ركعتين، ثم مدَّ يدَه إلى السماء، وما نرى في السماء شيئًا. قال: فوالله ما حط يدَه حتى بعثَ الله ريحًا وأنشأَ سَحابًا، وأفرغت حتى ملأت الغُدُرَ والشعاب، فشربنا وسقينا ركابنا واستقينا
> — ⁨البدایہ والنہایہ ج۶ ص۲۳۰⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/30097/2939 (Anas ؓ; al-Bayhaqī calls the chain broken)
> *English:* “Then ʿUmar b. al-Khaṭṭāb equipped an army and put al-ʿAlāʾ b. al-Ḥaḍramī over them. Anas said: I was on that campaign of his. We came to our ground and found that the people had had warning of us and had effaced the traces of the water; the heat was severe, and thirst wore down us and our animals — and that was a Friday. When the sun leaned toward its setting he prayed two rakʿas with us, then stretched his hand to the sky — and we could see nothing in the sky. He said: By Allah, he had not lowered his hand before Allah sent a wind and raised a cloud, and it poured until it filled the pools and the ravines; and we drank, and watered our mounts, and drew water.”

`D16`

> منها أنّه سارَ بجيشه على وَجْهِ البَحْرِ ما يصلُ إلى رُكَبِ خُيولهم، وقيل: إنه ما بَلّ أسافل نعالِ خُيولهم. وأمرهم كلَّهم فجعلوا يقولون: يا حليم يا عظيم
> — ⁨البدایہ والنہایہ ج۵ ص۵۰۷⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/30097/2691 (Ibn Kathīr's summary of the reports)
> *English:* “Among them: that he went with his army over the face of the sea, it not reaching the knees of their horses — and it is said: it did not wet the bottoms of their horses' hooves — and he ordered them all, and they began saying: O Forbearing, O Tremendous.”

**(e) Abū Hurayra ؓ and the guide — event 17.** ⁨الکامل⁩'s telling is on `RC25`. Its cached text has the
guide find the pool:

`D15`

> قَالَ: فَرَجَعْتُ بِهِ إِلَى ذَلِكَ الْمَكَانِ، فَلَمْ نَجِدْ إِلَّا غَدِيرَ الْمَاءِ، فَقُلْتُ لَهُ: وَاللَّهِ لَوْلَا الْغَدِيرُ لَأَخْبَرْتُكَ أَنَّ هَذَا هُوَ الْمَكَانُ، وَمَا رَأَيْتُ بِهَذَا الْمَكَانِ مَاءً قَبْلَ الْيَوْمِ
> — ⁨الکامل فی التاریخ ج۲ ص۲۲۳⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/21712/914
> *English:* “He said: So I went back with him to that place, and we found nothing but the pool of water. I said to him: By Allah, were it not for the pool I would tell you that this is the place — and I never saw water in this place before today.”

⁨سیر⁩ (`D14`) and the Sahm telling (`D13`) have the water **gone**; ⁨الطبری⁩ (corroboration only,
⁨ج۳ ص۳۰۷⁩) has *no pool there, and no trace of water*. **Tell what all of them share** — he went
back; the man who knew that country said he had never seen water in that place before that day; the
water-skin was where it had been left — **and do not say whether the pool was still there.**

**Not on the page:** the words of the duʿāʾ in the dawn telling; tayammum (⁨الطبری⁩ only); that the
water of the dawn telling was rain — it is a pool; the number of camels; any name for the spot.

### 3.7 Hajar: two sides, and a month of trenches — events 18–21

`T1`

> ثُمَّ سَارُوا فَنَزَلُوا بِهَجَرَ، وَأَرْسَلَ الْعَلَاءُ إِلَى الْجَارُودِ يَأْمُرُهُ أَنْ يَنْزِلَ بِعَبْدِ الْقَيْسِ عَلَى الْحُطَمِ مِمَّا يَلِيهِ، وَسَارَ هُوَ فِيمَنْ مَعَهُ حَتَّى نَزَلَ عَلَيْهِ مِمَّا يَلِي هَجَرَ
> — ⁨الکامل فی التاریخ ج۲ ص۲۲۳⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/21712/914
> *English:* “Then they marched on and camped at Hajar. And al-ʿAlāʾ sent to al-Jārūd, ordering him to come down with ʿAbd al-Qays upon al-Ḥuṭam from the side next to him; and he himself marched with those who were with him until he came down upon him from the side next to Hajar.”

`T2`

> فَاجْتَمَعَ الْمُشْرِكُونَ كُلُّهُمْ إِلَى الْحُطَمِ إِلَّا أَهْلَ دَارِينَ، وَاجْتَمَعَ الْمُسْلِمُونَ إِلَى الْعَلَاءِ
> — ⁨الکامل فی التاریخ ج۲ ص۲۲۳⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/21712/914
> *English:* “And all the mushrikūn gathered to al-Ḥuṭam — except the people of Dārīn — and the Muslims gathered to al-ʿAlāʾ.”

`T3`

> وَخَنْدَقَ الْمُسْلِمُونَ عَلَى أَنْفُسِهِمْ وَالْمُشْرِكُونَ، وَكَانُوا يَتَرَاوَحُونَ الْقِتَالَ وَيَرْجِعُونَ إِلَى خَنْدَقِهِمْ، فَكَانُوا كَذَلِكَ شَهْرًا
> — ⁨الکامل فی التاریخ ج۲ ص۲۲۳⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/21712/914
> *English:* “And the Muslims dug a trench about themselves, and so did the mushrikūn; and they would take turns at the fighting and go back to their trench — and they were like that for a month.”

⁨البدایہ⁩ has none of this — no order to al-Jārūd ؓ, no trench, no month:

`T4`

> ثم لما اقترب من جيوش المرتدة - وقد حشدوا وجمعوا خلقًا عظيمًا - نزل ونزلوا، وباتوا مُتجاورين في المنازل
> — ⁨البدایہ والنہایہ ج۷ ص۳۹⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/30097/3185
> *English:* “Then when he drew near the armies of the apostates — who had mustered and gathered a great multitude — he halted and they halted, and they passed the night camped side by side.”

**What the close-up may draw:** al-ʿAlāʾ's ؓ camp at Hajar; a messenger to al-Jārūd ؓ; two Muslim forces
moving on al-Ḥuṭam from opposite sides; al-Ḥuṭam's host swelling as "all the mushrikūn" come in; Dārīn
left out of it, and labelled; then two trench lines and a month.

**Not on the page:** a *siege* at this stage — the word is used of Juwāthā, never of the trenches;
al-Ḥuṭam inside Hajar — Hajar is on al-ʿAlāʾ's ؓ side; where the trenches lay, their length, their
shape; a second Muslim camp or a second trench for al-Jārūd ؓ — the next clause has the Muslims
"gather to al-ʿAlāʾ", and the trench is one; that al-Jārūd ؓ did come down — the page gives the order
and then the gathering, not his march; the ring at Juwāthā opening — it follows from "all the
mushrikūn gathered to al-Ḥuṭam", and is drawn as an inference, uncaptioned.

### 3.11 "Rabīʿa" on these pages

**It is the name over both tribes, not a third tribe and not another word for Bakr.** ⁨الکامل⁩ splits
"the people of Bahrayn" into two — *Bakr*, which persisted in its ridda, and *ʿAbd al-Qays*, whom
al-Jārūd ؓ gathered (`M2`) — and then says "Rabīʿa in Bahrayn agreed upon the ridda, **except al-Jārūd
and those who followed him**" (`R1`). The exception is a man of ʿAbd al-Qays; so ʿAbd al-Qays is inside
the word. al-Ḥuṭam, who does the fighting, "went out among **Bakr b. Wāʾil**" (`H1`).

- **On the map:** label the revolt **Bakr b. Wāʾil**, and the part that came back **ʿAbd al-Qays, with
  al-Jārūd ؓ**. If the word *Rabīʿa* appears on a face it sits over both, as the parent name.
- **That both descend from Rabīʿa** is `[STANDARD]` *(to verify)*: no safe-list page we hold says it in
  so many words. ⁨ابن خلدون⁩ does (⁨خلدون ج۲ ص۵۰۴⁩), and he is framing only. The usage above is `[SOURCED]`.
- **The two sentences of ⁨الکامل⁩ do not quite agree on how much held** — the tribe (`M2`, `G1`), or
  al-Jārūd ؓ "and those who followed him" (`R1`). They are two reports laid end to end. Draw ʿAbd
  al-Qays as a marker with al-Jārūd's ؓ name on it, not as a shaded territory: no page bounds one.

⚠ **Dārīn: the page's distance and the usual identification do not fit, and a page we hold says so.**
"A day and a night for ships" is the one measurement on this whole front, and both books give it
(`X3`, `X6`). The port usually pointed to stands a short channel off al-Qaṭīf `[CONVENTIONAL-ESTIMATE]`.
The geographer the
editors quote noticed the difficulty himself — `P6` — and wondered whether the description was really
that of Uwāl. **The pages do not settle it, and neither does this note.** So the close-up Dārīn is
also a schematic: a shore, a strait, an offshore port; the page's distance as the caption; **no modern
island named on the face.** In the scene file Dārīn and al-Qaṭīf sit almost on top of one another;
on the theatre map draw Dārīn clear of the coast and say "not to scale" in the notes.

⚠ **Who the Zuṭṭ and the Sabābija were is on no page we hold.** ⁨الکامل⁩ names them once more, at
al-Baṣra a generation later (⁨الکامل ج۲ ص۵۷۷⁩), and glosses them neither time. `RC72`'s rule stands:
name them as the page does, and stop.

**What the deck has done, stated once so nobody has to rediscover it:** it tells telling 1, and takes

**Why this matters on a map:** the name the books use for the fighting on this front is Juwāthā's.
al-Dhahabī heads it "the affair of Juwāthā" (⁨سیر جراشدون ص۶۱⁩); Ibn Kathīr does the same when he dates

the fort al-ʿAlāʾ ؓ *attacks*; and the editors' gloss — "took it by force in the year 12" (`P4`) — is
the second. **Do not say "the battle of Juwāthā" over the trench map, and do not draw al-ʿAlāʾ ؓ
storming Juwāthā.** The trench map is captioned by Hajar, which is the only place-name `T1` gives it.

# X. WHAT COULD **NOT** BE ESTABLISHED

1. **A bare "8 AH" for the embassy.** The one page that dates it gives year 6, with 8 as "it is said"
   (⁨الکامل ج۲ ص۹۱⁩); the other says "before the conquest of Mecca" (⁨البدایہ ج۵ ص۱۹⁩).
2. **A letter to al-Mundhir** — its text, or a sentence saying al-ʿAlāʾ ؓ carried one. Every page says
   *sent*.
3. **Where al-ʿAlāʾ ؓ was when al-Mundhir died.** Ibn Isḥāq: at his side, as amīr (`A10`). al-Miswar:
   the Prophet ﷺ had replaced him with Abān b. Saʿīd (⁨سیر ج۱ ص۲۶۴⁩). Ibn Saʿd and Sayf: Abū Bakr ؓ
   sent him out from Medina (`S1`, `S2`). **No page narrates him leaving Bahrayn.** Do not draw it.
4. **The month of al-Mundhir's death.** "Shortly after" is all the safe-list pages give.
5. **Where al-Jārūd ؓ gathered ʿAbd al-Qays; the bounds of their country; any distance** from them to
   Juwāthā, or from Juwāthā to Hajar.
6. **Whether a tribe held, or a few.** The tribe (⁨الکامل⁩, ⁨البدایہ⁩); "al-Jārūd and those who followed
   him" (⁨الکامل⁩); "a small group" (⁨سیر⁩).
7. **Who led the ring at Juwāthā, how long it stood, and how it opened.**
8. **Whether Juwāthā was the Muslims' refuge or the enemy's fort** — §5.
9. **Where the man they made king sat.** And the words *Lakhmid* and *al-Ḥīra*, for a safe-list page.
10. **Who the Zuṭṭ and the Sabābija were; how far al-Khaṭṭ ran.**
11. **The purpose, size and commander of the detachment to Dārīn.** (⁨الطبری⁩ and ⁨ابن خلدون⁩ give a
    purpose — to put ʿAbd al-Qays between two forces. Neither book may carry it.)
12. **Any number for the army after the sixteen**, and where the clans of Tamīm met it.
13. **The words of the duʿāʾ in the dawn telling.** And whether the water was a pool found at sunrise
    (Sayf) or rain after two rakʿas (Sahm, Anas ؓ); and whether it was still there afterwards.
14. **Where the trenches lay**, their length, their shape; whether al-Jārūd ؓ had a camp of his own;
    where he was from the trench month onward.
15. **Why the enemy camp was drinking.** No page, in any telling, gives an occasion.
16. **Three readings in the cached ⁨الکامل⁩** that need a page image: the verb of `N3`; the verb of the
    bracketed clause in `W3`; the vocalisation of the last word of `S4`. And the sense of `G5`.
17. **Which road each of the loyal men of Bakr b. Wāʾil held**, and the names of the roads.
18. **The port the fugitives sailed from; the point where the army entered the water; which island
    Dārīn is; how deep the water was** — six descriptions, §3.9.
19. **Whether Dārīn fell under Abū Bakr ؓ or after him** — telling 1 says under him; tellings 3 and 5
    put it after.
20. **The year of the fighting** — 11 or 12.
21. **Honorifics.** No page read in this pass states the Companionship of ʿAbdullāh b. Ḥadhf, Abjar
    b. Bujayr, ʿAfīf b. al-Mundhir, Qays b. ʿĀṣim, ʿUtayba b. al-Nahhās, al-Muthannā b. Ḥāritha, or
    al-Mundhir b. Sāwā. The cards' rulings stand unchanged. *(to verify)*
22. **What became of the captured king** after his Islam; and **any consequence** for the men who
    killed Thumāma ؓ.
23. **One man or two — ʿAbdullāh b. Ḥadhf.** Unchanged: the besieged poet and the scout carry one
    name, and no safe-list page joins them. ⁨الطبری⁩ uses the name three times without distinguishing,
    which is corroboration and not proof.
