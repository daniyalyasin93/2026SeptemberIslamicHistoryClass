# The killing of علي ؓ — رمضان 40 AH

**Question this note answers:** What the books report was foretold about this death, and how they
grade those reports; who the three men were, where they met and what they agreed; who عبد الرحمن بن
ملجم was before that night and what is said about his motive; the night and the morning at the جامع
of الكوفة; the exact wording of what علي ؓ said when he was struck and in the day or two he lived;
his instruction about his own killer; his وصية; the burial, the day, his age, the disagreement over
both, and the length of his caliphate; why the place of the grave is reported as concealed; who was
struck elsewhere that same night; and what became of ابن ملجم.

## 0. The sentences this note licenses

Each of these may now go on a slide, with the page given. Read this section instead of the note.

1. **علي ؓ was struck at the door of the جامع of الكوفة as he came out calling the people to the فجر
   prayer, on the night of Friday, 17 رمضان 40 AH** `[SOURCED]` — البدایہ ج۷ ص۵۴۵, ص۵۴۹; الکامل ج۲
   ص۷۳۶, ص۷۳۹; سیر ص۲۸۴.
2. **The date is disputed inside the safe list, and both books say so themselves.** البدایہ: killed
   Friday at سحر, 17 رمضان — «وقيل إنه قتل في ربيع الأول والأول هو الأصح الأشهر» `[SOURCED]` — ج۷
   ص۵۴۹. الکامل: 17 رمضان — «وقيل: لإحدى عشرة، وقيل: لثلاث عشرة بقيت منه، وقيل: في شهر ربيع الآخر
   سنة أربعين. والأول أصح» `[SOURCED]` — ج۲ ص۷۳۶–۷۳۷. ⚠ **The two books do not name the same
   alternative month: البدایہ says ربيع الأول, الکامل says ربيع الآخر.** See §12.1.
3. **البدایہ also transmits a second reckoning that has him die on the night of Sunday, with eleven
   nights of رمضان remaining, and reports that الواقدي called that one «الثبت عندنا»** `[SOURCED]` —
   ج۷ ص۵۵۰. This sits beside ابن كثير's own «الأصح الأشهر» on the page before. See §12.1.
4. **Three men of the خوارج agreed together to kill علي ؓ, معاوية ؓ and عمرو بن العاص ؓ on one night,
   17 رمضان — عبد الرحمن بن ملجم, البرك بن عبد الله التميمي, and عمرو بن بكر التميمي** `[SOURCED]` —
   البدایہ ج۷ ص۵۴۴; الکامل ج۲ ص۷۳۸; سیر ص۲۸۴.
5. **Their stated reason was النهروان: they were avenging the خوارج dead, whom they called «أئمة
   الضلال» their targets** `[SOURCED]` — البدایہ ج۷ ص۵۴۴; الکامل ج۲ ص۷۳۸.
6. **سیر places the pact at مكة; البدایہ and الکامل do not name the place** `[SOURCED]` — سیر ص۲۸۴
   («فاجتمعوا بمكة»). ابن ملجم himself later tells الحسن ؓ he gave God a covenant **عند الحطيم** —
   البدایہ ج۷ ص۵۵۰; الکامل ج۲ ص۷۴۲.
7. **ابن ملجم was, before this, a man of مصر who had fought at فتح مصر, had read the Qur'an with معاذ
   بن جبل ؓ, was counted among العباد, and had had his house moved next to the mosque on عمر ؓ's own
   written instruction so that he could teach the Qur'an and فقه — and he had fought at صفين on علي
   ؓ's side** `[SOURCED]` — سیر ص۲۸۷, on الذهبي's citation of ابن يونس's تاريخ مصر.
8. **البدایہ describes him physically: brown-skinned, handsome, بلح of eye, hair to the lobes of his
   ears, and the mark of prostration on his forehead** `[SOURCED]` — ج۷ ص۵۴۴.
9. **His name is not given the same way by the three books.** البدایہ ج۷ ص۵۴۳: «عبد الرحمن بن عمرو
   المعروف بابن ملجم الحميري ثم الكندي حليف بني حنيفة … المصري». الکامل ج۲ ص۷۳۸ and سیر ص۲۸۴, ص۲۸۷:
   «عبد الرحمن بن ملجم المرادي» `[SOURCED]`. See §4.1.
10. **All three books report قطام بنت الشجنة, of تيم الرباب, whose father and brother علي ؓ had killed
    at النهروان, and the marriage-price she named.** البدایہ ج۷ ص۵۴۴: three thousand dirhams, a
    servant, a singing-girl, and the killing of علي ؓ. سیر ص۲۸۵ gives a shorter price: **three
    thousand dirhams and the killing of علي ؓ, with no servant and no singing-girl** `[SOURCED]`. See
    §4.3.
11. **The accomplices were وردان, of قطام's own people, and a man of أشجع.** The أشجعi's name differs:
    البدایہ ج۷ ص۵۴۴ «شبيب بن نجدة الأشجعي الحروري»; الکامل ج۲ ص۷۳۸ and سیر ص۲۸۵ «شبيب بن بجرة
    الأشجعي», and الکامل vowels it — «بَجَرَةَ: بِفَتْحِ الْبَاءِ وَالْجِيمِ» `[SOURCED]` — ج۲ ص۷۴۲.
12. **شبيب at first refused, and said why: he knew علي ؓ's precedence in Islam and his kinship to the
    Prophet ﷺ, and «ما أجدني أنشرح صدرًا لقتله». He was talked round with النهروان** `[SOURCED]` —
    البدایہ ج۷ ص۵۴۴; الکامل ج۲ ص۷۳۹.
13. **Both blows are reported: شبيب struck first and his sword fell on the door-frame; ابن ملجم's
    sword landed on his قرن, and the blood ran down into his beard** `[SOURCED]` — البدایہ ج۷ ص۵۴۵;
    الکامل ج۲ ص۷۳۹.
14. **The killer's cry as he struck is reported in two wordings — البدایہ «لا حكم إلا لله ليس لك يا
    علي ولا لأصحابك», الکامل «الْحُكْمُ لِلَّهِ لَا لَكَ يَا عَلِيُّ وَلَا لِأَصْحَابِكَ» — and البدایہ adds that he
    recited البقرة ٢٠٧** `[SOURCED]` — البدایہ ج۷ ص۵۴۵; الکامل ج۲ ص۷۳۹.
15. **The فجر prayer was completed: علي ؓ put جعدة بن هبيرة forward, and الکامل adds that جعدة was his
    sister أم هانئ's son** `[SOURCED]` — البدایہ ج۷ ص۵۴۵; الکامل ج۲ ص۷۴۰.
16. **ابن ملجم was brought in bound, and the exchange is given nearly identically in both books: «أي
    عدو الله ألم أحسن إليك؟ … فما حملك على هذا؟ قال: شحذته أربعين صباحًا وسألت الله أن يقتل به شر
    خلقه»** `[SOURCED]` — البدایہ ج۷ ص۵۴۵; الکامل ج۲ ص۷۴۰.
17. **His instruction about his killer is one man only, and no mutilation.** الکامل ج۲ ص۷۴۰:
    «النَّفْسُ بِالنَّفْسِ … أَلَا لَا يُقْتَلَنَّ إِلَّا قَاتِلِي … وَلَا تُمَثِّلَنَّ بِالرَّجُلِ», with the hadith
    «إِيَّاكُمْ وَالْمُثْلَةَ وَلَوْ بِالْكَلْبِ الْعَقُورِ». البدایہ ج۷ ص۵۴۵ gives the same in shorter form: «إن
    مت فأقتلوه وإن عشت فأنا أعلم كيف أصنع به» `[SOURCED]`. ⚠ **البدایہ ج۷ ص۵۴۶ also carries a
    contrary report, from أحمد, that he said «اقتلوه ثم حرقوه» — and the printed edition's footnote
    grades that chain ضعيف.** See §8.3.
18. **He refused to appoint a successor, exactly as عمر ؓ had: جندب بن عبد الله asked whether they
    should give the بيعة to الحسن ؓ, and he said «لا آمركم ولا أنهاكم، أنتم أبصر»** `[SOURCED]` —
    البدایہ ج۷ ص۵۴۵; الکامل ج۲ ص۷۴۰.
19. **In the foretelling reports he had already refused to name a successor in the same words the
    room will recognise from عمر ؓ: «لا، ولكن أترككم كما ترككم رسول الله ﷺ»** `[SOURCED]` — البدایہ ج۷
    ص۵۴۰, ص۵۴۱.
20. **The last words are «لا إله إلا الله», repeated, and both books say he spoke nothing else until
    he died** `[SOURCED]` — البدایہ ج۷ ص۵۴۶; الکامل ج۲ ص۷۴۱. **البدایہ adds «وقد قيل» that the last
    thing he spoke was الزلزلة ٧–٨** `[SOURCED]` — ج۷ ص۵۴۵.
21. **The written وصية is printed in full in البدایہ ج۷ ص۵۴۵–۵۴۶ — the شهادة, then a chain of «الله
    الله في …» charges: the orphans, the neighbours, the Qur'an, the prayer, the House of your Lord,
    رمضان, جهاد, زكاة, the ذمة of your Prophet, the Companions of your Prophet, the poor, and those
    your right hands own. ⚠ The printed edition's footnote says «وإسناد هذه الوصية ضعيف معضل»**
   `[SOURCED, editor's grading]` — ج۷ ص۵۴۶ n.4.
22. **الکامل gives a different and shorter deathbed charge, spoken to الحسن ؓ and الحسين ؓ and then to
    محمد ابن الحنفية — and the two books must not be merged**: «لَا تَبْغِيَا الدُّنْيَا وَإِنْ بَغَتْكُمَا …
    وَكُونَا لِلظَّالِمِ خَصِيمًا، وَلِلْمَظْلُومِ نَاصِرًا» `[SOURCED]` — ج۲ ص۷۴۱. See §9.2.
23. **He was washed by الحسن ؓ, الحسين ؓ and عبد الله بن جعفر ؓ, and الحسن ؓ prayed over him** —
    but **the number of تكبيرات differs: البدایہ says nine (twice), الکامل says seven** `[SOURCED]` —
    البدایہ ج۷ ص۵۴۶, ص۵۴۸; الکامل ج۲ ص۷۴۱. الکامل adds he was shrouded in three garments with no
    قميص. See §12.2.
24. **The grave was concealed deliberately, and the books state the reason: fear that the خوارج would
    dig the body up** `[SOURCED]` — البدایہ ج۷ ص۵۴۸ («خوفًا عليه من الخوارج أن ينبشوا عن جثته»), ص۵۴۹
    (ابن الكلبي: «وعمَّوا قبره خيفة عليه من الخوارج وغيرهم»).
25. **Where the grave is, is disputed inside the safe list itself, and the two books contradict each
    other flatly.** البدایہ ج۷ ص۵۴۸: the well-known position is دار الإمارة at الكوفة, and the النجف
    shrine «فلا دليل على ذلك ولا أصل له». الکامل ج۲ ص۷۴۶, **in the editor's round brackets**: «وَالْأَصَحُّ
    أَنَّ قَبَرَهُ هُوَ الْمَوْضِعُ الَّذِي يُزَارُ وَيُتَبَرَّكُ بِهِ» `[SOURCED]`. **Because the safe list
    disagrees with itself here, the platform does not settle it** — see §13 and §Not for the hall.
26. **His age is disputed: 63 (البدایہ, from أبو جعفر الباقر, and «صححه الواقدي وابن جرير»; الکامل,
    «والأول أصح») · 58 (a report from جعفر الصادق, البدایہ; also الکامل) · 65 (both) · 68 (البدایہ
    only) · 59 (الکامل only)** `[SOURCED]` — البدایہ ج۷ ص۵۴۹–۵۵۰; الکامل ج۲ ص۷۴۶.
27. **The caliphate lasted four years and nine months — البدایہ states it that way, الکامل states the
    same length as «خمس سنين إلا ثلاثة أشهر»** `[SOURCED]` — البدایہ ج۷ ص۵۵۰; الکامل ج۲ ص۷۴۶.
28. **ابن ملجم was executed by الحسن ؓ after علي ؓ died, having first offered a bargain — let me go
    and kill معاوية and I will come back and put my hand in yours — which الحسن ؓ refused: «كلا والله
    حتى تعاين النار». The people then wrapped the body in matting and burned it** `[SOURCED]` —
    البدایہ ج۷ ص۵۵۰; الکامل ج۲ ص۷۴۲. البدایہ adds a «وقد قيل» version with mutilation before death,
    and closes it «والله أعلم» — ج۷ ص۵۵۰. See §8.4.
29. **The same night, البرك بن عبد الله struck معاوية ؓ in دمشق as he came out for the فجر prayer; the
    blow fell in his hip. He survived; the physician's remedy is reported to have ended his having
    children** `[SOURCED]` — البدایہ ج۷ ص۵۴۸; الکامل ج۲ ص۷۴۲–۷۴۳. الکامل names the physician
    «السَّاعِدِيِّ»; البدایہ says only «الطبيب». **البدایہ says he was killed at once; الکامل reports a
    second account in which his hand and foot were cut off and he lived on until زياد killed him at
    البصرة** — ج۲ ص۷۴۳. See §12.3.
30. **Out of that one night came the مقصورة and the night-guard in the mosque: both books say معاوية ؓ
    was the first to do it, and why** `[SOURCED]` — البدایہ ج۷ ص۵۴۸; الکامل ج۲ ص۷۴۳.
31. **عمرو بن العاص ؓ did not come out that night because of a pain in his stomach; his deputy, who
    led the prayer in his place, was killed instead, and the words «أردتني وأراد الله خارجة» became a
    proverb** `[SOURCED]` — البدایہ ج۷ ص۵۴۸; الکامل ج۲ ص۷۴۳. **البدایہ reports two versions of who
    spoke the sentence** — ج۷ ص۵۴۸.
32. **The deputy's name differs between the books: البدایہ and الکامل call him «خارجة بن أبي حبيبة … من
    بني عامر بن لؤي», صاحب شرطة to عمرو ؓ; سیر calls him «خارجة بن حذافة بن غانم», says he had صحبة,
    fought at فتح مصر, and was على شرطة مصر — and says plainly that عمرو بن بكير الخارجي killed him
    بمصر** `[SOURCED]` — البدایہ ج۷ ص۵۴۸; الکامل ج۲ ص۷۴۳; سیر ص۲۸۶. See §12.4.
33. **الذهبي rules on ابن ملجم in one paragraph that also refuses the two extremes, and he groups him
    with the killers of عثمان ؓ, الزبير ؓ, طلحة ؓ, عمار ؓ, سعيد بن جبير رحمہ اللہ, خارجة and الحسين ؓ:
    «فَكُلُّ هَؤُلَاءِ نَبْرَأُ مِنْهُمْ وَنُبْغِضُهُمْ فِي اللهِ، وَنَكِلُ أُمُورَهُمْ إِلَى اللهِ عَزَّ وَجَلَّ»**
    `[SOURCED]` — سیر ص۲۸۷–۲۸۸. ⚠ The sentence immediately before it is printed in a form that does
    not read straightforwardly — see §8.5; do not put that clause on a slide without a page image.
34. **ابن كثير stops the narrative of 40 AH to doubt one of its most famous reports in his own voice:
    «وهذا الخبر مشهور عند أصحاب المغازي والسير، وفي صحته عندي نظر والله تعالى أعلم»** `[SOURCED]` —
    البدایہ ج۷ ص۵۳۸. This is the single most useful sentence in the strand for the platform, because
    it is the book itself declining to certify a report about a Companion's commander.
35. **البدایہ's own framing of why 40 AH looked the way it did is stated before any of it is narrated,
    and it praises علي ؓ in the same breath as it describes the collapse of obedience around him**
    `[SOURCED]` — ج۷ ص۵۳۹–۵۴۰.
36. **His last Friday sermon is reported, and it ends in a دعاء to be relieved of the people and them
    of him; the narrator says «فما صلى الجمعة الأخرى حتى قتل»** `[SOURCED]` — البدایہ ج۷ ص۵۴۳. **The
    same دعاء is reported independently in سیر from محمد ابن الحنفية via ابن سعد** — سیر ص۲۸۴.
37. **The books gather a set of reports that this death was foretold — «لتخضبن هذه من هذه» and «من
    أشقى الآخرين؟» — and they do not treat them all alike: البخاري is quoted grading one narrator
    «في حديثه هذا نظر», البيهقي is quoted saying «إن كان محفوظًا» and «فإن صح», and the printed
    edition's footnotes grade the chains one by one** `[SOURCED]` — البدایہ ج۷ ص۵۴۰–۵۴۳.
38. **الکامل gives the night before: the Ramadan habit of three mouthfuls, the geese at the door, and
    الحسن ؓ's account of his father's dream — «اللَّهُمَّ أَبْدِلْنِي بِهِمْ مَنْ هُوَ خَيْرٌ مِنْهُمْ، وَأَبْدِلْهُمْ
    بِي مَنْ هُوَ شَرٌّ مِنِّي»** `[SOURCED]` — ج۲ ص۷۳۷–۷۳۸.
39. **الکامل reports a line علي ؓ used to say whenever he saw ابن ملجم: «أُرِيدُ حَيَاتَهُ وَيُرِيدُ قَتْلِي
    … عَذِيرَكَ مِنْ خَلِيلِكَ مِنْ مُرَادِ»** `[SOURCED]` — ج۲ ص۷۳۸.
40. **الحسن ؓ's address the morning after is in both books, with the detail of the estate: «وَاللَّهِ مَا
    تَرَكَ صَفْرَاءَ وَلَا بَيْضَاءَ إِلَّا ثَمَانَمِائَةٍ أَوْ سَبْعَمِائَةٍ»** `[SOURCED]` — الکامل ج۲ ص۷۵۰; البدایہ
    ج۷ ص۵۵۳. ⚠ **البدایہ grades its own version of this report: «وهذا غريب جدًّا وفيه نكارة»** — ج۷
    ص۵۵۳; a second chain from أحمد, without the estate figure, is graded حسن in the editor's footnote.
    The two books also differ on what the money was set aside for — البدایہ «لخادم», الکامل «لجارية».
41. **الکامل sets out, at his death, the زهد material: the pearl from بيت المال, the loaf divided into
    seven and drawn by lot, the worn قطيفة at الخورنق, the two skins of honey and ghee taken back from
    أم كلثوم and priced at three dirhams, the lawsuit before شريح against a Christian which علي ؓ lost

## 1. Where the year stands before the night — the book's own framing

ابن كثير does not begin the killing with the killing. He begins by telling the reader what kind of
year 40 AH was, and he does it in a single long sentence that both describes the collapse of
obedience and praises علي ؓ without qualification (البدایہ ج۷ ص۵۳۹–۵۴۰):

> كان أمير المؤمنين ﵁ قد انتقضت (٥) عليه الأمور، واضطرب عليه جيشه، وخالفه أهل
> — البدایہ والنہایہ ج۷ ص۵۳۹ · https://shamela.ws/book/30097/3685
> *English:* "The Commander of the Faithful ؓ — matters had come apart upon him, his army had grown
> unsteady under him, and there opposed him the people of …"

and the sentence continues on the next page:

> العراق [وغيرهم]، ونكلوا عن القيام معه، واستفحل أمر أهل الشام … هذا وأميرهم علي بن أبي طالب خير أهل الأرض في ذلك الزمان، أعبدهم وأزهدهم، وأعلمهم وأخشاهم لله ﷿، ومع هذا كله خذلوه وتخلوا عنه
> — البدایہ والنہایہ ج۷ ص۵۴۰ · https://shamela.ws/book/30097/3686
> *English:* "…ʿIrāq [and others]; they held back from standing with him, and the affair of the
> people of الشام grew strong … and this though their commander was علي بن أبي طالب, the best of the
> people of the earth in that age, the most devoted in worship, the most abstinent, the most
> knowledgeable and the most fearing of God ﷿ — and with all of this they failed him and drew away
> from him."

and it ends with what that did to him:

> حتى كره الحياة وتمنى الموت، وذلك لكثرة الفتن وظهور المحن [والظلم والفساد] فكان يكثر أن يقول: ما يحبس أشقاها
> — البدایہ والنہایہ ج۷ ص۵۴۰ · https://shamela.ws/book/30097/3686
> *English:* "…until he came to dislike life and to wish for death, and that was from the multitude
> of trials and the appearing of afflictions [and injustice and corruption]; so he used often to say:
> what is keeping back the most wretched of them?"

The square brackets are the printed edition's, marking manuscript variation. **This paragraph is the
frame the whole strand should be told inside**, because it belongs to ابن كثير, it praises علي ؓ in
the strongest terms the book has, and it explains the collapse without assigning blame to any named
Companion.

## 1.1 The last Friday sermon

البدایہ closes its chapter of foretellings with a sermon and a narrator's line about it (ج۷ ص۵۴۳):

> اللهم سئمتهم وسئموني، وكرهتهم وكرهوني، اللهم فأرحهم مني وأرحني منهم. قال: فما صلى الجمعة الأخرى حتى قتل ﵁ وأرضاه.
> — البدایہ والنہایہ ج۷ ص۵۴۳ · https://shamela.ws/book/30097/3689
> *English:* "O God, I have wearied of them and they of me, and I have come to dislike them and they
> me; O God, so relieve them of me and relieve me of them. He said: and he did not pray the next
> Friday before he was killed ؓ."

The same دعاء comes down independently in the سیر, from محمد ابن الحنفية through ابن سعد, and there
it is attached to his repeated attempts to march to الشام and the people's repeated refusal
(سیر ص۲۸۴):

> اللهم إني قد مللتهم وملوني، وأبغضتهم وأبغضوني، فأبدلني بهم خيرا منهم، وأبدلهم بي شرا مني.
> — سیر أعلام النبلاء جراشدون ص۲۸۴ · https://shamela.ws/book/10906/1424
> *English:* "O God, I have wearied of them and they of me, and I have come to dislike them and they
> me; so give me in exchange for them someone better than them, and give them in exchange for me
> someone worse than me."

**Note the difference, and do not smooth it.** البدایہ's wording asks for relief; سیر's wording asks
for an exchange, and the second half of it — «وأبدلهم بي شرا مني» — is the harder sentence. الکامل
carries the exchange-wording too, inside الحسن ؓ's account of the dream (§6.2).

## 1.2 The historian stops and refuses to certify a report

Before the killing, البدایہ narrates the campaign of بسر بن أبي أرطاة into the حجاز and اليمن in 40
AH, on ابن جرير's authority and through his chain, with detail that includes the killing of children.
Then ابن كثير, in his own voice, does this (ج۷ ص۵۳۸):

> ويقال إن بسرًا قتل خلقًا من شيعة علي في ميسره هذا، وهذا الخبر مشهور عند أصحاب المغازي والسير، وفي صحته عندي نظر والله تعالى أعلم.
> — البدایہ والنہایہ ج۷ ص۵۳۸ · https://shamela.ws/book/30097/3684
> *English:* "And it is said that بسر killed a great number of the partisans of علي on this march of
> his. This report is well known among the people of the مغازي and the سير, but in my view there is
> something to look into concerning its authenticity, and God most high knows best."

**This is the most valuable sentence in the strand for the platform.** It is the book itself saying,
of a famous report that charges a Companion's commander with an atrocity, that fame is not
authenticity. The atrocity detail on that page is listed under *Not for the hall*; the refusal is
not.

## 1.3 The truce of 40 AH, as the book reports it

On the same page-run البدایہ reports, again from ابن جرير, that a truce was concluded in 40 AH
(ج۷ ص۵۳۹):

> أما بعد فإن الأمة قد قتل بعضها بعضًا فلك العراق ولي الشام فأقر بذلك علي ﵁.
> — البدایہ والنہایہ ج۷ ص۵۳۹ · https://shamela.ws/book/30097/3685
> *English:* "To proceed: the ummah has killed some of itself, so العراق is yours and الشام is mine —
> and علي ؓ affirmed that."

The page frames it as a مهادنة «بعد مكاتبات يطول ذكرها» — after letters too long to set down — with
the stated terms that neither would enter the other's territory with an army, a raid or a campaign.
**Report it as the book reports it — as what ابن جرير relates and ابن كثير transmits — and go no
further.**

## 2. The reports that this death was foretold, and how the book handles them

البدایہ devotes ج۷ ص۵۴۰–۵۴۳ to this, chain by chain, under headings the editor supplies («طريق
أخرى», «حديث آخر في ذلك»). What matters for us is not only the content — it is that **the book
visibly sorts them**.

### 2.1 «لتخضبن هذه من هذه»

The core wording, in علي ؓ's own mouth, in the البيهقي chain (ج۷ ص۵۴۰):

> والذي فلق الحبة وبرأ النسمة لتخضبن هذه من هذه للحيته من رأسه فما يحبس أشقاها"؟
> — البدایہ والنہایہ ج۷ ص۵۴۰ · https://shamela.ws/book/30097/3686
> *English:* "By Him who split the grain and created the soul, this shall surely be dyed from this —
> his beard, from his head — so what is keeping back the most wretched of them?"

الکامل states the same content without a chain and with the plainest possible gloss (ج۲ ص۷۳۷):

> وَقِيلَ مِنْ غَيْرِ وَجْهٍ: إِنَّ عَلِيَّا كَانَ يَقُولُ: مَا يَمْنَعُ أَشْقَاكُمْ أَنْ يُخَضِّبَ هَذِهِ مِنْ هَذِهِ؟ يَعْنِي لِحْيَتَهُ مِنْ دَمِ رَأْسِهِ.
> — الکامل فی التاریخ ج۲ ص۷۳۷ · https://shamela.ws/book/21712/1426
> *English:* "And it is said, by more than one route, that علي used to say: what prevents the most
> wretched of you from dyeing this from this? — meaning his beard, from the blood of his head."

### 2.2 The exchange with the خوارج, from الطيالسي

البدایہ, ج۷ ص۵۴۰, through شريك عن عثمان بن المغيرة عن زيد بن وهب:

> جاءت الخوارج إلى علي فقالوا: اتق الله أ يا علي، فإنك ميت. قال: لا والذي فلق الحبة وبرأ النسمة، ولكن مقتول من ضربة على هذه تخضب هذه - وأشار بيده إلى لحيته - عهد معهود وقَضاء مقضي، وقد خاب من افترى.
> — البدایہ والنہایہ ج۷ ص۵۴۰ · https://shamela.ws/book/30097/3686
> *English:* "The خوارج came to علي and said: fear God, O علي, for you are going to die. He said: no —
> by Him who split the grain and created the soul, but rather killed, by a blow upon this, which
> shall dye this — and he pointed with his hand to his beard — a covenant covenanted and a decree
> decreed; and he who invents a lie has failed."

⚠ **The Shamela text of this line carries a stray letter — «اتق الله أ يا علي» — which is not a word.
Do not quote this sentence from the cache; read it off a page image first.** The printed edition's
own footnote on the same page notes the report is موقوف على عليٍّ ﵁ in الطيالسي's مسند.

### 2.3 «من أشقى الأولين؟»

Two chains give the same shape, one through صهيب ؓ (أبو يعلى, ج۷ ص۵۴۱) and one through جابر بن سمرة ؓ
(الخطيب البغدادي, ج۷ ص۵۴۳). The shorter is the cleaner (ج۷ ص۵۴۳):

> قال رسول الله ﷺ لعلي: "من أشقى الأولين؟ " قال: عاقر الناقة، قال: "فمن أشقى الآخرين؟ " قال الله ورسوله أعلم، قال: "قاتلك".
> — البدایہ والنہایہ ج۷ ص۵۴۳ · https://shamela.ws/book/30097/3689
> *English:* "The Messenger of God ﷺ said to علي: who is the most wretched of the former peoples? He
> said: the hamstringer of the she-camel. He said: and who is the most wretched of the later ones? He
> said: God and His Messenger know best. He said: the one who kills you."

The printed edition's footnote on the أبو يعلى version (ج۷ ص۵۴۱ n.1) reads «وإسناده ضعيف». **That is
the editor's grading.** The الخطيب version at ص۵۴۳ carries no such note on the page we read.

### 2.4 The report where he refuses to name a successor

البدایہ ج۷ ص۵۴۰, and again through أحمد at ص۵۴۱:

> فقالوا: يا أمير المؤمنين ألا تستخلف؟ فقال: لا ولكن أترككم كما ترككم رسول الله ﷺ. قالوا: فما تقول لربك إذا لقيته وقد تركتنا هملًا؟ قال: أقول: اللهم استخلفتني فيهم ما بدا لك، ثم قبضتني، وتركتك فيهم، فإن شئت أصلحتهم، وإن شئت أفسدتهم [فيه ضعف في بعض ألفاظه].
> — البدایہ والنہایہ ج۷ ص۵۴۰ · https://shamela.ws/book/30097/3686
> *English:* "They said: Commander of the Faithful, will you not appoint a successor? He said: no,
> but I leave you as the Messenger of God ﷺ left you. They said: and what will you say to your Lord
> when you meet Him, having left us untended? He said: I will say — O God, You made me successor over
> them as long as You willed, then You took me, and I left You among them; if You will, You set them
> right, and if You will, You let them go to ruin. [There is weakness in some of its wordings.]"

⚠ **The clause in square brackets at the end — «فيه ضعف في بعض ألفاظه» — is printed inside the
editor's brackets on this page**, i.e. it is not part of the reported speech. Reproduce the brackets
if this is quoted at length; better, quote only up to «أفسدتهم».

### 2.5 The one he would not have others killed for

In two of these chains the people offer to wipe out the killer's clan in advance, and he forbids it
(ج۷ ص۵۴۰, and again ص۵۴۱):

> فقال أنشدكم بالله أن يُقتل بي غير قاتلي.
> — البدایہ والنہایہ ج۷ ص۵۴۰ · https://shamela.ws/book/30097/3686
> *English:* "He said: I adjure you by God that none be killed for me but my killer."

This is the same instruction he will give again when he is actually struck (§8.1) — **the books have
him saying it before and after, which is the strongest thing that can be said about it from the
platform.**

### 2.6 The report the book itself will not vouch for

البدایہ ج۷ ص۵۴۳ reports «إن الأمة ستغدر بك بعدي», then immediately quotes two authorities against
resting on it:

> قال البخاري (٢): ثعلبة بن يزيد الحمَّاني في حديثه هذا نظر.
> — البدایہ والنہایہ ج۷ ص۵۴۳ · https://shamela.ws/book/30097/3689
> *English:* "البخاري said: as for ثعلبة بن يزيد الحمّاني — in this hadith of his there is something
> to look into."

> قال البيهقي (٣): وقد رويناه بإسناد آخر عن علي إن كان محفوظًا.
> — البدایہ والنہایہ ج۷ ص۵۴۳ · https://shamela.ws/book/30097/3689
> *English:* "البيهقي said: we have narrated it by another chain from علي — if it is preserved."

and then البيهقي's conditional reading of its meaning, which is itself a model of not overreaching:

> قال البيهقي: فإن صح فيحتمل أن يكون المراد به والله أعلم في خروج من خرج عليه ثم في قتله.
> — البدایہ والنہایہ ج۷ ص۵۴۳ · https://shamela.ws/book/30097/3689
> *English:* "البيهقي said: if it is sound, it may be that what is meant by it — and God knows best —
> is the going-out of those who went out against him, and then his killing."

### 2.7 الکامل's separate report, through أنس ؓ

الکامل does not run the chains البدایہ runs. It gives one report, through أنس بن مالك ؓ, that has the
Prophet ﷺ speaking over علي ؓ in an illness (ج۲ ص۷۳۷):

> " لَنْ يَمُوتَ هَذَا الْآنَ، وَلَنْ يَمُوتَ حَتَّى يُمْلَأَ غَيْظًا، وَلَنْ يَمُوتَ إِلَّا مَقْتُولًا»
> — الکامل فی التاریخ ج۲ ص۷۳۷ · https://shamela.ws/book/21712/1426
> *English:* "This one will not die now; he will not die until he is filled with anger; and he will
> not die except slain."

**The two books are not telling the same reports here.** البدایہ's material is a bank of chains all
turning on «لتخضبن»; الکامل's is one report through أنس ؓ plus the undifferentiated «قيل من غير وجه».
Say so if both are used.

## EVENT CARDS

### E-QA1 · The year the obedience came apart
**Tier:** CORE · **When:** 40 AH `[SOURCED]` · **Map:** الكوفة and الشام both lit; the line between them dark.

**What happened:** ابن كثير does not begin this year with the killing. He begins by telling the
reader what kind of year it was: matters had come apart, the army had grown unsteady, the people of
العراق held back from standing with their commander, and the affair of the people of الشام grew
strong. In the same breath he says who their commander was, in the strongest words the book has for
any living man. Then he says what all of it did to him — he came to dislike life and to wish for
death, and used often to ask what was keeping back the most wretched of them.

**The statement:**
> العراق [وغيرهم]، ونكلوا عن القيام معه، واستفحل أمر أهل الشام … هذا وأميرهم علي بن أبي طالب خير أهل الأرض في ذلك الزمان، أعبدهم وأزهدهم، وأعلمهم وأخشاهم لله ﷿، ومع هذا كله خذلوه وتخلوا عنه
> — البدایہ والنہایہ ج۷ ص۵۴۰ · https://shamela.ws/book/30097/3686
> *English:* "…ʿIrāq [and others]; they held back from standing with him, and the affair of the people of الشام grew strong … and this though their commander was علي بن أبي طالب, the best of the people of the earth in that age, the most devoted in worship, the most abstinent, the most knowledgeable and the most fearing of God ﷿ — and with all of this they failed him and drew away from him."

**عبرت:** A people can have the best man of its age at its head and still lose him, by refusing to stand with him.

### E-QA3 · The historian who would not certify a famous report
**Tier:** CORE · **When:** 40 AH `[SOURCED]` · **Map:** No map.

**What happened:** Before he reaches the killing, ابن كثير narrates a campaign into the حجاز and
اليمن with a heavy charge attached to it, and he narrates it through the chain by which he received
it. Then he stops, in his own voice. He says that although the report is well known among the people
of the مغازي and the سير, in his own view there is something to look into concerning whether it is
sound. This is a historian declining to certify a story that everyone of his own time already knew.

**The statement:**
> وهذا الخبر مشهور عند أصحاب المغازي والسير، وفي صحته عندي نظر والله تعالى أعلم.
> — البدایہ والنہایہ ج۷ ص۵۳۸ · https://shamela.ws/book/30097/3684
> *English:* "This report is well known among the people of the مغازي and the سير, but in my view there is something to look into concerning its authenticity, and God most high knows best."

**عبرت:** A report can be famous for six hundred years and still not be established.

**Hands-up?** Who here has heard a story about the past so often that they never once asked where it came from?

### E-QA5 · The same saying, stripped of its chain
**Tier:** GOOD · **When:** before رمضان 40 AH `[SOURCED]` · **Map:** No map.

**What happened:** الکامل carries the same saying but does not run the chains البدایہ runs. It gives
the content by itself, says only that it comes down by more than one route, and then adds a plain
gloss so that no reader can mistake the two pronouns. The two books are therefore not transmitting
identical material here, and if both are used that should be said aloud. This is what a compiler
looks like when he is summarising rather than examining.

### E-QA78 · Fourteen sons and seventeen daughters
**Tier:** GOOD · **When:** 40 AH `[SOURCED]` · **Map:** No map.

**What happened:** Both books count his children the same way, which after so many disagreements is
worth saying aloud: fourteen sons and seventeen daughters. Both also agree on where the line
continued — through five of them only: الحسن ؓ, الحسين ؓ, محمد ابن الحنفية, العباس, and عمر. البدایہ
attributes its count to ابن جرير and the line of descent to الواقدي.

**The statement:**
> فَجَمِيعُ وَلَدِهِ أَرْبَعَةُ عَشَرَ ذَكَرًا، وَسَبْعَ عَشْرَةَ امْرَأَةً، وَكَانَ النَّسْلُ مِنْهُمْ لِلْحَسَنِ، وَالْحُسَيْنِ، وَمُحَمَّدِ ابْنِ الْحَنَفِيَّةِ، وَالْعَبَّاسِ بْنِ الْكِلَابِيَّةِ، وَعُمَرَ بْنِ التَّغْلِبِيَّةِ.
> — الکامل فی التاریخ ج۲ ص۷۴۸ · https://shamela.ws/book/21712/1437
> *English:* "So the whole of his children were fourteen males and seventeen females; and the line among them continued through الحسن, الحسين, محمد ابن الحنفية, العباس son of the كلابية, and عمر son of the تغلبية."

**عبرت:** Where two books agree exactly on a number, that is worth saying, because they so often do not.

**Hands-up?** no

### E-QA79 · The بيعة of الحسن ؓ, in the same year
**Tier:** CORE · **When:** 40 AH `[SOURCED]` · **Map:** الكوفة.

**What happened:** In the same year, after his father's killing, الحسن ؓ was given the بيعة. The
first man to give it was قيس بن سعد الأنصاري ؓ, who said: stretch out your hand, I give you the
pledge on the Book of God and the Sunnah of His Prophet, and on fighting those who make lawful what
is forbidden. الحسن ؓ took the first two and left the third — on the Book of God and the Sunnah of
His Messenger, for those two cover every condition. The people then gave the pledge.

**The statement:**
> وَأَوَّلُ مَنْ بَايَعَهُ قَيْسُ بْنُ سَعْدٍ الْأَنْصَارِيُّ، وَقَالَ لَهُ: ابْسُطْ يَدَكَ أُبَايِعْكَ عَلَى كِتَابِ اللَّهِ وَسُنَّةِ نَبِيِّهِ، وَقِتَالِ الْمُحِلِّينَ. فَقَالَ الْحَسَنُ: عَلَى كِتَابِ اللَّهِ وَسُنَّةِ رَسُولِهِ فَإِنَّهُمَا يَأْتِيَانِ عَلَى كُلِّ شَرْطٍ.
> — الکامل فی التاریخ ج۲ ص۷۵۱ · https://shamela.ws/book/21712/1440
> *English:* "And the first to give him the pledge was قيس بن سعد الأنصاري, who said to him: stretch out your hand, I pledge you on the Book of God and the Sunnah of His Prophet, and on fighting those who make lawful what God forbade. الحسن said: on the Book of God and the Sunnah of His Messenger — for those two come over every condition."

**عبرت:** The first sentence of the new caliphate declined to write war into the pledge.

**Hands-up?** no

### E-QA80 · Forty nights later
**Tier:** CUT · **When:** شوال 40 AH `[SOURCED]` · **Map:** No map.

**What happened:** الکامل closes the year with its obituaries, and the first of them is الأشعث بن قيس
الكندي, who died forty nights after the killing, and over whom الحسن ؓ prayed. The سیر, elsewhere and
for its own reasons, records that the killer had spent his last night in conversation with الأشعث in
his mosque. Neither book joins those two facts to each other. Neither may we: the card exists so
that the material is on the record and so that no one re-researches it, not so that an inference is
drawn from it.

**The statement:**
> وَفِيهَا مَاتَ الْأَشْعَثُ بْنُ قَيْسٍ الْكِنْدِيُّ بَعْدَ قَتْلِ عَلِيٍّ بِأَرْبَعِينَ لَيْلَةً، وَصَلَّى عَلَيْهِ
> — الکامل فی التاریخ ج۲ ص۷۵۱ · https://shamela.ws/book/21712/1440
> *English:* "And in it died الأشعث بن قيس الكندي, forty nights after the killing of علي; and there prayed over him…"

**عبرت:** Two facts standing next to each other on the calendar are not, by themselves, one fact.

**Hands-up?** no
