# People of the fitna — Profiles I: the circle of عثمان ؓ and the men and women of الجمل

**Question this note answers.** Session 3 («پہلا امتحان», 23–40 AH) has to introduce eight
Companions inside the story, each in one breath, without a تعارف block and without adjudicating
anything. This note is the تراجم register for the first half of that session: طلحة بن عبيد الله ؓ,
الزبير بن العوام ؓ, أم المؤمنين عائشة ؓ, عبد الرحمن بن عوف ؓ, سعد بن أبي وقاص ؓ, عمار بن ياسر ؓ,
أبو ذر الغفاري ؓ and عبد الله بن مسعود ؓ. For each: the لقب the tradition already gave him, his
clan and his kin among the others, the three or four sentences of pre-23 AH background the room
needs, what he did between 23 and 41 AH, one واقعہ that can be told as a scene, his own words in
verbatim Arabic where the page carries them, and the date and manner of his death with the
disagreement where the book records one. It also collects, deliberately, every place where
الذهبي رحمہ اللہ himself **grades, refuses or suspends judgement** on a report — because that is
what lets the speaker decline to adjudicate from the platform.

## 1. طلحة بن عبيد الله ؓ

### The تراجم notice, in one breath

- **Name + لقب** — طلحة بن عبيد الله بن عثمان التيمي القرشي, أبو محمد. The Prophet ﷺ is reported to
  have named him **طلحة الخير**, **طلحة الفياض** and **طلحة الجود** on three separate days; الذهبي
  records the report and then grades its chain **إسناده لين** — weak-ish. `[SOURCED]` سیر ج۱ ص۳۰.
- **Dates** — died **جمادى الآخرة ۳۶ھ**, *or* it is said رجب, aged **about sixty-two**; so born
  about ۲۶ before the hijra. `[SOURCED]` سیر ج۱ ص۴۰. On the Line: the year of الجمل, five years
  after عمر ؓ, twenty-four years after the Prophet ﷺ.
- **What he did** — one of the ten given the good news of الجنة and one of the six عمر ؓ left to
  choose his successor; he shielded the Prophet ﷺ with his own hand at أحد until the hand was
  crippled, and he was killed at الجمل in ۳۶ھ by an arrow from within his own side.

### Clan and kin

**بنو تيم بن مرة** of قريش — the same بطن as أبو بكر الصديق ؓ. The سیر gives the full line: طلحة بن
عبيد الله بن عثمان بن عمرو بن كعب بن سعد بن **تيم بن مرة** بن كعب بن لؤي (ج۱ ص۲۳). `[SOURCED]`
That is why عمر ؓ can tease him about **إمارة ابن عمك** — meaning أبو بكر ؓ (ج۱ ص۳۸). `[SOURCED]`
His wife was **أم كلثوم بنت الصديق**, أبو بكر's daughter, named in the سیر by name (ج۱ ص۳۱);
another wife named on the page is **سعدى بنت عوف المرية** (ج۱ ص۳۲). `[SOURCED]` So طلحة is
brother-in-law to عائشة ؓ and clansman to أبو بكر ؓ — the تيم tie is the single fact that makes his
presence beside عائشة ؓ at الجمل legible to a room.

### Before 23 AH, in three or four sentences

الذهبي states plainly that he was **among those who came early to Islam**, was persecuted for it,
and emigrated (ج۱ ص۲۵). `[SOURCED]` He missed بدر because he was away on trade in الشام, and grieved
at missing it; the Prophet ﷺ assigned him his share and his reward all the same (ج۱ ص۲۵).
`[SOURCED]` At أحد he took **twenty-four wounds** in one day, his finger was crippled, and he
carried the Prophet ﷺ backwards out of the press until he had him up in the شعب (ج۱ ص۳۲).
`[SOURCED]` البخاري records قيس saying he saw with his own eyes the hand of طلحة, the one he
shielded the Prophet ﷺ with at أحد, withered (ج۱ ص۲۶). `[SOURCED]` And ابن عساكر records that he was
with عمر ؓ when عمر came to الجابية, and that عمر put him over the مهاجرين (ج۱ ص۲۵). `[SOURCED]`

### In this window (23–41 AH)

- He was one of the **six** of the شورى, and one of the two Zuhrī-and-Taymī pair praised in the
  verses الرياشي recites on ج۱ ص۳۴ alongside علي وعثمان والزبير. `[SOURCED]`
- He was **enormously rich** in this window and gave it away in whole estates: 700,000 from
  حضرموت distributed in a morning (ج۱ ص۳۱); 400,000 handed to his clan in one sitting (ج۱ ص۳۲);
  an estate عثمان ؓ had offered him 300,000 for, given away to a stranger who asked by kinship
  (ج۱ ص۳۱). `[SOURCED]` ابن سعد's figures for his income: 400,000 a year from العراق and about
  10,000 dinars from السراة, and he sent عائشة ؓ ten thousand every year when the crop came in
  (ج۱ ص۳۳). `[SOURCED]`
- **In the affair of عثمان ؓ**: الذهبي records طلحة's own words — that something came from him in
  the matter of عثمان whose expiation he could see only in his own blood being spilled (ج۱ ص۳۴);
  and that they had *temporised* (داهنّا) in the matter of عثمان (ج۱ ص۳۵). `[SOURCED]` الذهبي then
  gives his own reading, which is the single most useful sentence in the ترجمة for our purposes —
  see below.
- **He was the first to give بیعہ to علي ؓ.** الذهبي says so directly, and adds that the killers of
  عثمان pressed him hard and brought him until he gave it (ج۱ ص۳۵). `[SOURCED]`
- He went out with الزبير ؓ and عائشة ؓ **seeking the blood of عثمان ؓ** — الذهبي's own phrase is
  للطلب بدم عثمان — and on the way they turned aside at ذات عرق and sent back عروة بن الزبير and
  أبا بكر بن عبد الرحمن as too young (ج۱ ص۳۴). `[SOURCED]`
- He was killed at **الجمل** (ج۱ ص۳۶); the editor's note fixes الجمل in **سنة ۳۶** at البصرة
  (editor's footnote, ج۱ ص۳۶). `[SOURCED, editor's note — flagged as such]`

### One واقعہ worth telling — the night he could not sleep

Seven hundred thousand came to him from حضرموت, and he spent the night turning over in bed. His
wife asked what was wrong. He said he had been thinking all night: *what is a man's opinion of his
Lord, if he passes the night with this money in his house?* She told him to send for bowls in the
morning and divide it. He said, God have mercy on you, you are rightly-guided, daughter of a
rightly-guided man — and she was أم كلثوم, daughter of الصديق ؓ. In the morning he called for the
bowls and divided the money between the مهاجرين and the أنصار, and sent a bowl of it to علي ؓ. Then
his wife said: أبا محمد, was there no share in this money for us? He said: and where were you all
day? Take what is left. What was left was a purse with about a thousand dirhams in it.

### The date and manner of death, and the disagreement

- **جمادى الآخرة ۳۶ھ**, or it is said **رجب**; aged sixty-two **or thereabouts**; buried outside
  البصرة (ج۱ ص۴۰). `[SOURCED]` الطبراني has a report that he was sixty-four and buried at البصرة in
  the quarter of ثقيف, but the editor notes الواقدي in its chain and that he is متروك
  (editor's note, ج۱ ص۴۰). `[SOURCED — recorded as the book records it]`
- **Who killed him.** يحيى بن بكير, خليفة بن خياط and أبو نصر الكلاباذي all say the man who killed
  طلحة was **مروان بن الحكم** (ج۱ ص۴۰). `[SOURCED]` قيس reports seeing مروان shoot him in the knee
  on that day, and it bled until he died — a chain the editor calls **إسناده صحيح** (ج۱ ص۳۵–۳۶).
  `[SOURCED]`
- الذهبي's own line on the killer is short and is the line to have ready:

> {{Q:1462:8}}
> — سير أعلام النبلاء ج۱ ص۳۶ · https://shamela.ws/book/10906/1462
> *English:* "I say: the killer of طلحة stands, in the burden of sin, in the same place as the
> killer of علي."

- After the battle **علي ؓ came upon him where he lay**, got down from his mount, sat him up and
  wiped the dust from his face and beard (ج۱ ص۳۶–۳۷); by another report he said he wished he had
  died twenty years before that day — الذهبي marks it **مرسل** (ج۱ ص۳۷). `[SOURCED]`
- His son **محمد السجاد**, born in the Prophet's ﷺ lifetime, was killed at الجمل on the same day;
  علي ؓ grieved for him and said **صرعه بره بأبيه** — his devotion to his father laid him low
  (ج۱ ص۴۰). `[SOURCED]`

### ⚠ What not to say about him from the platform

- **Do not adjudicate his part in the agitation against عثمان ؓ, and do not repeat the word طلحة
  himself used as if it were a finding of ours.** Attribute it to الذهبي, by name, and stop there.
  الذهبي's own sentence is the safe form and it is on the page:

> {{Q:1461:6}}
> — سير أعلام النبلاء ج۱ ص۳۵ · https://shamela.ws/book/10906/1461
> *English:* "I say: what came from him in the matter of عثمان was inadvertence and agitating —
> he did it by اجتهاد; then he changed when he witnessed the killing of عثمان, and regretted having
> failed to come to his aid, رضي الله عنهما. And طلحة was the first to give the pledge to علي; the
> killers of عثمان pressed him and brought him until he pledged."

  The words **فعله باجتهاد** and **رضي الله عنهما** are الذهبي's own, on his own page, and they are
  precisely how a Sunni authority narrates this without judging between Companions. Quote him;
  do not improve on him.
- **Do not turn مروان بن الحكم's arrow into a thesis about بنو أمية.** Report the three names who
  say he shot طلحة, report الذهبي's sentence about the killer, and move on.
- **Do not use the ثلاث مائة حمل من الذهب figure.** الذهبي himself flags ابن الجوزي's claim as the
  most astonishing thing he came across — أعجب ما مر بي — which is a refusal, not a report
  (ج۱ ص۴۰). `[SOURCED]`

## 2. الزبير بن العوام ؓ

### The تراجم notice, in one breath

- **Name + لقب** — الزبير بن العوام بن خويلد بن أسد بن عبد العزى بن قصي, أبو عبد الله. The لقب the
  tradition gave him is on the page in the Prophet's ﷺ own reported words: **حواريّ رسول الله ﷺ** —
  "every prophet has a حواري, and my حواري is الزبير." الذهبي heads the ترجمة with exactly that
  title, and adds **أول من سلّ سيفه في سبيل الله**. `[SOURCED]` سیر ج۱ ص۴۱, ج۱ ص۴۸.
- **Dates** — killed **رجب ۳۶ھ** (so البخاري and others); aged **sixty-four** per الواقدي and
  ابن نمير, but الذهبي records the alternative **بضع وخمسون** — fifty-odd — and says of it
  **وهو أشبه**, "that is the more likely". `[SOURCED]` سیر ج۱ ص۶۴. On the Line: the same year and
  battle as طلحة ؓ.
- **What he did** — the Prophet's ﷺ own cousin and حواري, one of the ten, one of the six of the
  شورى; one of only two horsemen at بدر; he withdrew from الجمل before the fighting ended, after
  علي ؓ reminded him of a saying, and was killed as he went, at وادي السباع.

### Clan and kin

**بنو أسد بن عبد العزى** of قريش. الذهبي's opening line makes the kinship the point: he is
**ابن عمة رسول الله ﷺ**, son of **صفية بنت عبد المطلب**, the Prophet's ﷺ paternal aunt (ج۱ ص۴۱).
`[SOURCED]` His wife was **أسماء بنت أبي بكر ؓ** — so عائشة ؓ was his sister-in-law and عبد الله
بن الزبير ؓ her nephew; القحذمي names his four wives: أسماء بنت أبي بكر, عاتكة sister of سعيد بن
زيد, أم خالد بنت خالد بن سعيد and أم مصعب الكلبية (ج۱ ص۶۴). `[SOURCED]` عائشة ؓ calls عروة
**يا ابن أختي** — nephew — and speaks of **أبواك** meaning الزبير and أبو بكر (ج۱ ص۴۷). `[SOURCED]`
ابن إسحاق reports that الزبير, عثمان, طلحة, عبد الرحمن and سعد all entered Islam **at the hand of
أبي بكر ؓ** (ج۱ ص۴۵). `[SOURCED]` Four of the five men in this note's first half are therefore tied
to أبي بكر ؓ at the moment of their entry into Islam — one sentence that organises the whole board.

### Before 23 AH, in three or four sentences

He accepted Islam very young — الذهبي's own heading says at sixteen, while عروة's report says he
was eight, and that at twelve he ran through مكة with a drawn sword on a rumour that the Prophet ﷺ
had been seized (ج۱ ص۴۱–۴۲). `[SOURCED]` He emigrated to الحبشة and did not stay long (ج۱ ص۴۷).
`[SOURCED]` At بدر he was one of **two horsemen**, on the right wing, with المقداد بن الأسود on the
left (ج۱ ص۴۶). `[SOURCED]` At الخندق the Prophet ﷺ asked three times who would bring news of
بني قريظة, and three times الزبير said *I will* — and it was then that the حواري saying was said of
him (ج۱ ص۴۷–۴۸, البخاري and مسلم). `[SOURCED]` عروة his son said there were **three sword-cuts** in
his body, one in the shoulder deep enough for a child to put his fingers into: two taken at بدر and
one at **اليرموك** (ج۱ ص۵۲). `[SOURCED]`

### In this window (23–41 AH)

- **He struck his own name off the register.** When عمر ؓ was killed, الزبير erased himself from
  the ديوان; and when عثمان ؓ was killed, his son عبد الله did the same (ج۱ ص۵۷). `[SOURCED]`
- He was one of the **six**, and عثمان ؓ — when pressed in the year of the nosebleed to name a
  successor and told the people were saying *الزبير* — answered with an oath that he was the best
  of them as far as he knew and the most beloved of them to the Messenger of Allah ﷺ; البخاري
  carries it and the editor calls the chain **إسناده صحيح** (ج۱ ص۵۴). `[SOURCED]`
- **Seven Companions made him the executor of their estates** — among them عثمان ؓ, ابن مسعود ؓ and
  عبد الرحمن بن عوف ؓ — and he spent on the heirs from his own money and guarded theirs (ج۱ ص۵۵).
  `[SOURCED]` This is the single most useful fact about him for a room: he was the man the others
  trusted with their children's money.
- الأوزاعي's chain reports he had **a thousand slaves paying him tribute, and he brought none of it
  into his house** — سعيد بن عبد العزيز adds that he gave all of it away (ج۱ ص۵۶). `[SOURCED]`
- He went out to البصرة with طلحة ؓ and عائشة ؓ. At الجمل علي ؓ called him out between the lines and
  reminded him of something he had heard from the Prophet ﷺ; الزبير **withdrew from the battle**
  (ج۱ ص۵۸–۵۹). `[SOURCED — with the gradings recorded below]`
- He was killed on the way out, at **وادي السباع**, seven فراسخ from البصرة, by **عمير بن جرموز**
  and two others (ج۱ ص۶۰–۶۱, ج۱ ص۶۴). `[SOURCED]`

### One واقعہ worth telling — the man who would not be blamed for leaving

When الزبير turned away from علي ؓ on the day of الجمل, his own son عبد الله met him and threw the
word at him: cowardice, cowardice. الزبير answered that the people knew he was no coward, but that
علي had reminded him of something he had heard from the Messenger of Allah ﷺ, and he had sworn he
would not fight him. Then he recited a line of verse — that leaving the affairs whose consequences
he feared, for the sake of God, was better in this world and in religion. He had barely gone before
ابن جرموز killed him.

### The date and manner of death, and the disagreement

- **رجب ۳۶ھ** — البخاري وغيره (ج۱ ص۶۴). `[SOURCED]` Buried at **وادي السباع**, seven فراسخ from
  البصرة, and علي ؓ **sat and wept over him, he and his companions** (ج۱ ص۶۱, ج۱ ص۶۴). `[SOURCED]`
- **Age: sixty-four** per الواقدي and ابن نمير; **fifty-odd** per others — and الذهبي prefers the
  second, **وهو أشبه** (ج۱ ص۶۴). `[SOURCED]` A real disagreement, recorded as one.
- **His estate.** عبد الله ؓ found his father had left **no dinar and no dirham** — only lands at
  الغابة and houses at المدينة, البصرة, الكوفة and مصر — because الزبير refused to take money as a
  deposit and took it as a loan instead, fearing it would be lost; his debt came to **2,200,000**,
  and عبد الله ؓ proclaimed at the موسم for four years running that any creditor should come, before
  he would divide the inheritance (ج۱ ص۶۶–۶۷, البخاري). `[SOURCED]` The whole estate came to
  **50,200,000** (ج۱ ص۶۷). `[SOURCED]` (A separate report at ج۱ ص۶۵ gives 50,000,000 in goods plus
  50,000,000 in cash; الذهبي's own comment on it is only **كذا هذه الرواية** — "thus this report" —
  which is a flag, not an endorsement, and ابن عيينة's figure at the same place is 40,000,000.)
  `[SOURCED]`
- **On his killer.** علي ؓ said, when the head was brought to him — and in another report when
  ابن جرموز asked leave to enter — that the killer of the son of صفية should take his seat in the
  Fire (ج۱ ص۴۹, ج۱ ص۶۱). `[SOURCED]` Later عبد الله بن الزبير ؓ refused to have him killed in
  retaliation — *am I to kill a bedouin for الزبير? not for the thong of his sandal* (ج۱ ص۶۴).
  `[SOURCED]` الذهبي then adds his own line, which distinguishes this killer from the others:

> {{Q:1490:15}}
> — سير أعلام النبلاء ج۱ ص۶۴ · https://shamela.ws/book/10906/1490
> *English:* "I say: the wretch ate his own hands in remorse for killing him, and he asked
> forgiveness — not like the killer of طلحة, and the killer of عثمان, and the killer of علي."

### ⚠ What not to say about him from the platform

- **The single most important passage in this whole note is here, and it is الذهبي's own refusal.**
  A report is transmitted from الحسن that علي ؓ, after الجمل, said he knew a leader of fitna who
  entered الجنة while his followers went to the Fire, and that he named الزبير. الذهبي does not
  narrate it and pass on; he **rejects both its chain and its wording**, and then states the Sunni
  position on all three armies in one sentence:

> {{Q:1489:17}}
> — سير أعلام النبلاء ج۱ ص۶۳ · https://shamela.ws/book/10906/1489
> *English:* "In its chain there is إرسال, and in its wording there is نكارة. God forbid that we
> should testify against the followers of الزبير, or the army of معاوية, or of علي, that they are in
> the Fire. Rather we consign their affair to God, and ask forgiveness for them. Yes — the خوارج are
> the dogs of the Fire and the worst slain under the vault of heaven, because they passed clean out
> of Islam; then we do not know what their end is, and we do not rule that they abide in the Fire
> for ever, but we stop."

  **Read this out as الذهبي's, by name.** It is the whole مشاجرات guardrail in one Sunni authority's
  own words: *نفوض أمرهم إلى الله، ونستغفر لهم*. A page earlier he had already written:

> {{Q:1488:9}}
> — سير أعلام النبلاء ج۱ ص۶۲ · https://shamela.ws/book/10906/1488
> *English:* "I say: because they are of the ten given the good news of الجنة, and of the men of
> بدر, and of the people of بيعة الرضوان, and of the first forerunners of whom God Most High
> announced that He was pleased with them and they with Him; and because the four of them were
> killed and granted martyrdom. So we love them, and we hate the four who killed the four."

- **Do not use the report «حاربني خمسة» attributed to علي ؓ** (ج۱ ص۵۹), which lists عائشة ؓ,
  الزبير ؓ, طلحة ؓ and two others with a characterising word each. The printed edition's note on it
  says flatly **خبر لا يصح**, that its transmitter is unknown and the man below him متروك
  (editor's note, ج۱ ص۵۹). `[SOURCED — recorded here only so that it is never re-researched]`
- **The «ظالم» exchange between علي ؓ and الزبير ؓ must be handled exactly as the book handles it.**
  It is carried at ج۱ ص۵۸ and again at ج۱ ص۵۹, and the printed edition grades the first chain's key
  link مجهول and the second's transmitters ضعيفان, while noting that الحاكم graded a third route
  صحيح and الذهبي agreed (editor's notes, ج۱ ص۵۸–۵۹). `[SOURCED]` The safe form is the one الذهبي's
  own main text supports and the son's rebuke at ج۱ ص۶۰ independently confirms: *the books record
  that علي ؓ reminded him of a saying, and that الزبير ؓ then withdrew from the fighting.* Do not
  build an argument on the wording of the reminder itself.

## 3. أم المؤمنين عائشة بنت أبي بكر الصديق ؓ

### The تراجم notice, in one breath

- **Name + لقب** — عائشة بنت الصديق أبي بكر التيمية, **أم المؤمنين**; and the تراجم tradition also
  calls her **الحميراء** and **الصديقة**. الذهبي's own heading line gives her the description that
  should carry her onto a slide: **أفقه نساء الأمة على الإطلاق** — the most learned in fiqh of all
  the women of the ummah, without qualification (ج۲ ص۱۳۵). `[SOURCED]`
- **Dates** — born in Islam (الذهبي: **ممن وُلد في الإسلام**, eight years younger than فاطمة ؓ),
  died **۵۷ھ** per هشام بن عروة, أحمد بن حنبل and خليفة بن خياط, or **۵۸ھ** per أبو عبيدة معمر بن
  المثنى and الواقدي; aged **sixty-three years and some months** (ج۲ ص۱۳۹, ج۲ ص۱۹۲, ج۲ ص۱۹۳).
  `[SOURCED]` On the Line: seventeen years after الجمل, in معاوية's ؓ caliphate.
- **What she did** — the Prophet's ﷺ wife, whose innocence in the حديث الإفك was sent down from
  heaven; the largest transmitter of ḥadīth among the women of the ummah with **2,210** reports in
  her مسند; and the one who rode to البصرة in ۳۶ھ calling for the blood of عثمان ؓ and for the
  matter to be returned to a شورى — and who repented of it completely for the rest of her life.

### Clan and kin

**بنو تيم بن مرة** of قريش — daughter of أبي بكر الصديق ؓ; الذهبي gives the whole line back to
تيم بن مرة بن كعب بن لؤي (ج۲ ص۱۳۵). `[SOURCED]` Her mother was **أم رومان بنت عامر الكنانية**
(ج۲ ص۱۳۵). `[SOURCED]` So she is of **the same بطن as طلحة ؓ**, and طلحة's wife أم كلثوم was her
own sister; ابن عباس ؓ's remark at الجمل names طلحة as **قرابتها** — her kinsman — in so many words
(ج۲ ص۱۷۸). `[SOURCED]` Her sister **أسماء ؓ** was the wife of **الزبير ؓ**, so الزبير was her
brother-in-law and **عبد الله بن الزبير ؓ** her nephew — and الذهبي carries the report in which
ابن عمر ؓ tells her he had seen a man dominating her counsel, *meaning ابن الزبير* (ج۲ ص۱۹۳).
`[SOURCED]` **This kinship map is the whole explanation a room needs for who stood where at الجمل,
and it needs no interpretation added to it.**

### Before 23 AH, in three or four sentences

The Prophet ﷺ married her after خديجة ؓ died, and consummated the marriage in **شوال of year 2**,
on his return from بدر (ج۲ ص۱۳۵). `[SOURCED]` She is the only virgin he ﷺ married, and the only one
of his wives in whose garment revelation came to him — he said so himself, to أم سلمة ؓ, when the
other wives sent her to ask for the gifts to be spread evenly: **لا تؤذيني في عائشة** (ج۲ ص۱۴۲–۱۴۴,
البخاري and مسلم). `[SOURCED]` عمرو بن العاص ؓ asked him ﷺ who was most beloved to him of people, and
he said **عائشة**; and of the men, **أبوها** — البخاري, مسلم, and الذهبي says of it **هذا حديث صحيح**
(ج۲ ص۱۴۲, ج۲ ص۱۴۷–۱۴۸). `[SOURCED]` جبريل عليه السلام sent her his salām and she answered it
(ج۲ ص۱۴۶, البخاري and مسلم). `[SOURCED]` Her innocence in the حديث الإفك came down from heaven; her
own mother أم رومان described the day she collapsed with fever on hearing of it, and her words when
the verses came: **بحمد الله، لا بحمد أحد، ولا بحمدك** (ج۲ ص۲۰۰–۲۰۱, البخاري). `[SOURCED]`

### In this window (23–41 AH)

- **عمر ؓ set the stipends of the mothers of the believers at ten thousand each, and added two
  thousand for عائشة ؓ**, saying *she is the beloved of the Messenger of Allah ﷺ* (ج۲ ص۱۹۷).
  `[SOURCED]`
- After the killing of عثمان ؓ she took a public position, in these terms: **عثمان was killed
  wronged; I call you to seek his blood, and to return the matter to a شورى** (ج۲ ص۱۷۸, from
  صالح بن كيسان وغيره). `[SOURCED]` That is the whole of her programme as the book states it, and
  the speaker should state it in exactly those two clauses and not one word more.
- **The water of الحوأب.** On the road, at night, at the waters of بني عامر, dogs barked. She asked
  what water this was, was told **ماء الحوأب**, and said she thought she should turn back. Someone
  with her pressed her on. الذهبي carries it from أحمد's مسند and says of it **هذا حديث صحيح
  الإسناد، ولم يخرجوه** (ج۲ ص۱۷۷–۱۷۸). `[SOURCED]`
- **الجمل, ۳۶ھ.** الذهبي does not narrate the battle here — he says explicitly that he has given it
  in summary in the مناقب of علي ؓ — and gives only the meeting afterwards: علي ؓ stood at her tent
  and reproached her for the journey; she answered **ملكت فأسجح** — *you have won, so be gentle* —
  and he equipped her for المدينة and gave her twelve thousand, **فرضي الله عنه وعنها** (ج۲ ص۱۷۸).
  `[SOURCED]`
- **She repented of it for the rest of her life**, and الذهبي states this in his own voice, twice.
  She wept at the verse **{وقرن في بيوتكن}** until her khimār was wet (ج۲ ص۱۷۷); and she asked to be
  buried not in her own house but in البقيع with the other wives, because — as she put it — she had
  brought about a حدث after the Messenger of Allah ﷺ (ج۲ ص۱۹۳). `[SOURCED]`

### One واقعہ worth telling — «ادفنوني مع أزواجه»

She used to tell herself she would be buried in her own room, beside the Prophet ﷺ and her father.
Then she said: *I brought about something new after the Messenger of Allah ﷺ — bury me with his
wives.* And she was buried in البقيع. الذهبي, who has just quoted her, then tells the reader in his
own voice what she meant by that word, and refuses to let it be read as blame:

> {{Q:2173:9}}
> — سير أعلام النبلاء ج۲ ص۱۹۳ · https://shamela.ws/book/10906/2173
> *English:* "I brought about a حدث — an innovation of my own — after the Messenger of Allah ﷺ.
> Bury me with his wives."

> {{Q:2173:11}}
> — سير أعلام النبلاء ج۲ ص۱۹۳ · https://shamela.ws/book/10906/2173
> *English:* "I say: she means by الحدث her going out on the day of الجمل, for she regretted it with
> a complete regret and repented of it — though she did not do it except as one interpreting, aiming
> at the good, just as طلحة بن عبيد الله and الزبير بن العوام and a company of the great ones
> exercised اجتهاد. رضي الله عن الجميع."

**This is the single most useful passage in the entire note for session 3.** الذهبي gives, in one
sentence, both halves of the Sunni position: *she repented*, and *she did it as an interpreter
aiming at good*. Neither half may be dropped.

### The date and manner of death, and the disagreement

- **۵۷ھ** per هشام بن عروة, أحمد بن حنبل and شباب (خليفة بن خياط); **۵۸ھ** per أبو عبيدة معمر بن
  المثنى, الواقدي and others (ج۲ ص۱۹۲). `[SOURCED]` A real disagreement of one year, recorded.
- الواقدي's chain gives the night of **17 رمضان**, after the وتر, and says she ordered burial that
  same night; the أنصار gathered and no night was seen with more people in it; she was buried at
  **البقيع** (ج۲ ص۱۹۲–۱۹۳). `[SOURCED — but the editor's note at ج۲ ص۱۹۲ says الواقدي and his shaykh
  are not to be relied on, so this is a report, not a fixed date.]`
- **أبو هريرة ؓ prayed the funeral prayer over her** at البقيع, as مروان's deputy over المدينة

  palm-branch torches with rags and oil were carried, and the women were at البقيع as if it were an
  عید (ج۲ ص۱۹۲). `[SOURCED]`
- **Age: sixty-three years and some months** (ج۲ ص۱۹۳). `[SOURCED]`
- **She is buried at البقيع, and nowhere else.** الذهبي disposes of a claim that she lies west of the
  جامع of دمشق in one line: **وهذا غلط فاحش** — a gross error — she never came to دمشق at all
  (ج۲ ص۱۹۳). `[SOURCED]` A clean, quotable correction of a popular mistake.

### ⚠ What not to say about her from the platform

- **Never narrate الجمل as a contest between علي ؓ and عائشة ؓ.** الذهبي himself declines to narrate
  the battle in her ترجمة at all. Narrate what he narrates: her stated demand in two clauses, the
  warning at الحوأب, the meeting at the tent afterwards, and her lifelong regret — and stop.
- **Do not quote the sentence «وهذا خبر ثابت على رغم أنوف الروافض»** (ج۲ ص۱۴۲). It is on the page and
  الذهبي wrote it, but it is a polemical aside, it names a sect, and a Tanzeem-e-Islami hall is
  precisely the room in which it would be heard as an invitation to a different argument. The
  ḥadīth it defends — «أحب الناس إليك؟ عائشة» — is in البخاري and مسلم and needs no polemic.
- **Do not use the report attributed to ابن عباس ؓ at الجمل — «هذه عائشة تملّك الملك لقرابتها طلحة»**
  (ج۲ ص۱۷۸). It is a Companion's charge against a Companion, stated in the heat of the day, and
  repeating it from a platform is exactly the مشاجرات the guardrail exists to prevent.
- **Handle the ḥadīth of الجمل الأدبب with care** (ج۲ ص۱۹۸). ابن عبد البر calls it **من أعلام
  النبوة** and the transmitter عصام is ثقة, and its second half is **وتنجو بعد ما كادت** — *and she
  escapes after being close to it.* If it is used at all, the second half must be said with the
  first; half of it alone reads as an accusation, which is not what the report says.
- **Do not adjudicate الذهبي's own aside comparing خديجة ؓ and عائشة ؓ** (ج۲ ص۱۴۰). He first says he
  suspends judgement, then says he settled on خديجة's superiority for reasons not given there. If it
  comes up, report both sentences of his and stop.

## 4. عبد الرحمن بن عوف ؓ

### The تراجم notice, in one breath

- **Name + لقب** — عبد الرحمن بن عوف بن عبد عوف الزهري القرشي, أبو محمد. He has no popular لقب, so
  use the plain factual descriptors الذهبي himself puts in the heading: **أحد العشرة · أحد الستة
  أهل الشورى · أحد السابقين البدريين**, and **أحد الثمانية الذين بادروا إلى الإسلام** — one of the
  eight who hastened to Islam (ج۱ ص۶۸). `[SOURCED]` His name in الجاهلية was **عبد عمرو**, or it is
  said **عبد الكعبة**, and the Prophet ﷺ named him **عبد الرحمن** (ج۱ ص۶۹, ج۱ ص۷۴). `[SOURCED]`
- **Dates** — born **ten years after عام الفيل** per المدائني (ج۱ ص۷۴); died **۳۲ھ** per المدائني,
  الهيثم بن عدي and a group, buried at **البقيع**, having lived **seventy-five years** per يعقوب بن
  المغيرة (ج۱ ص۹۲). `[SOURCED]` On the Line: mid-way through عثمان's ؓ caliphate, four years before
  الجمل — **he did not live to see the fitna.**
- **What he did** — he was the man who conducted the شورى of ۲۳ھ: he took himself out of the running
  and then canvassed the community and gave the بیعہ to عثمان ؓ; and he was the richest of the
  Companions and gave it away by the caravan-load.

### Clan and kin

**بنو زهرة بن كلاب** of قريش — the clan of the Prophet's ﷺ mother آمنة بنت وهب, and therefore the
same بطن as **سعد بن أبي وقاص ؓ**. الذهبي makes the closeness explicit at the decisive moment: had
عبد الرحمن been showing favour in the شورى, *he would have taken it for himself, or given it to his
cousin and the closest of the group to him, سعد بن أبي وقاص* (ج۱ ص۸۶). `[SOURCED]` **That one
sentence is the whole reason the شورى did not become a clan affair, and it is الذهبي's own.**
He married **أم كلثوم بنت عقبة** (ج۱ ص۸۴). `[SOURCED]` المسور بن مخرمة calls him **خالك** — my
maternal uncle — in the report at ج۱ ص۸۸. `[SOURCED]` ابن إسحاق counts him among the five who
entered Islam **at the hand of أبي بكر ؓ** (ج۱ ص۴۵, in الزبير's ترجمة). `[SOURCED]`

### Before 23 AH, in three or four sentences

He came to المدينة with nothing. The Prophet ﷺ made a brotherhood between him and **سعد بن الربيع**
of the أنصار, one of the نقباء, who offered to split his property with him and to divorce the better
of his two wives for him. عبد الرحمن answered: *God bless you in your family and your property — but
show me the market.* He went, bought and sold, and profited (ج۱ ص۹۱, البخاري). `[SOURCED]` At أحد he
was struck: two front teeth gone, lame in one leg, and **twenty wounds** (ج۱ ص۷۵). `[SOURCED]` He
gave half his property — four thousand — in the Prophet's ﷺ own lifetime, and some of the منافقون
said *عبد الرحمن is a great show-off*; الزهري's fuller list adds forty thousand dinars, five hundred
horses and five hundred riding-camels given in the way of God, and notes that the bulk of his wealth
came from **trade** (ج۱ ص۸۰, ج۱ ص۸۱). `[SOURCED]` And the Prophet ﷺ prayed **behind him** — المغيرة
بن شعبة ؓ saw it and prayed with him, one rakʿah of الصبح (ج۱ ص۷۹). `[SOURCED]`

### In this window (23–41 AH)

- **عمر ؓ accepted his single testimony as decisive.** When no one present knew what the Prophet ﷺ
  had instructed about سهو in the prayer, عبد الرحمن came and said he had heard it. عمر's answer is
  the sentence to keep: **فأنت عندنا عدل** — and in ابن عساكر's wording, **العدل الرضا**
  (ج۱ ص۷۲). `[SOURCED]` It was on his testimony alone that عمر ؓ **took the جزية from المجوس**,
  because he testified the Prophet ﷺ had taken it from مجوس هجر (ج۱ ص۷۰, البخاري). `[SOURCED]`
  الذهبي draws the general rule from it himself (see his own words below).
- He came to **الجابية** with عمر ؓ, on the right wing, and at **سرغ** was on the left (ج۱ ص۶۹).
  `[SOURCED]` He **led the حج of the Muslims in year 13** — the year عمر ؓ became caliph (ج۱ ص۸۷,
  from مجالد عن الشعبي — a weak-ish route, so attribute it as the book does). `[SOURCED]`
- **The شورى of ۲۳ھ is his act.** He put the proposal to the six himself: *shall I choose for you,
  and take myself out of it?* (ج۱ ص۸۷) `[SOURCED]` When سعد بن أبي وقاص ؓ sent a man to him while he
  was standing on the pulpit, telling him to raise his own head to the matter of the people — that
  is, to call the people to himself — his answer was blunt, and its chain is **رجاله ثقات**
  (ج۱ ص۸۷). `[SOURCED]` When عمرو بن العاص ؓ needled المسور with the argument that a man who gives
  the office to someone he knows to be lesser than himself has a poor opinion of God, عبد الرحمن's
  reply was that he would rather have a knife put to his throat and pushed through to the other side
  (ج۱ ص۸۸). `[SOURCED]`
- الذهبي states the verdict in his own voice: **ومن أفضل أعمال عبد الرحمن عزله نفسه من الأمر وقت
  الشورى** — the finest of his deeds was removing himself from the office at the time of the شورى
  (ج۱ ص۸۶). `[SOURCED]`
- **He refused the succession a second time.** In the year of عثمان's ؓ nosebleed, عثمان had the
  covenant written for him. When حمران brought him the news as good news, he went and stood between
  the grave and the pulpit and prayed that if this appointment was real, God take him first. He died
  within six months (ج۱ ص۸۸). `[SOURCED — but the chain here is weak; two of its men are unknown or
  barely known, per the editor's note at ج۱ ص۸۸. Attribute it as a report.]`
- **المدينة lived off him**: a third of the town he lent his money to, a third whose debts he paid,
  and a third he gave to (ج۱ ص۸۸). `[SOURCED — from a chain with an unnamed man in it.]`

### One واقعہ worth telling — «شهر, then he was gone»

He fainted in his final illness, so deeply that the household thought his soul had gone out, and
they had covered him over and left him. Then he came round saying **الله أكبر**, and the household
said it after him. He asked them: did I faint just now? They said yes. He said: you are right. Two
men took me away in my swoon — I found in them harshness and roughness — and they said, come, we
will bring you to judgement before the Mighty, the Faithful. They took me until they met a man, and
he said: where are you taking this one? They said: to judgement before the Mighty, the Faithful.
And he said: go back — he is one of those for whom God wrote happiness and forgiveness while they
were in their mothers' wombs, and his sons will be given the use of him for as long as God wills.
He lived a month after that.

The editor's note on this report reads **إسناده صحيح** (ج۱ ص۸۹).

### ⚠ What not to say about him from the platform

- **Do not use the report that the Prophet ﷺ said عبد الرحمن would enter الجنة only crawling**
  (ج۱ ص۷۶) as a bare line. أحمد رحمہ اللہ himself said of that wording **حديث منكر**, and الذهبي
  gives the alternative wording, notes the transmitter's weakness, and then settles it in his own
  voice: even if he were delayed for the reckoning and entered crawling, that would be a metaphor,
  and *his rank in الجنة is not below the rank of علي and الزبير* (ج۱ ص۷۷–۷۸). `[SOURCED]` The
  version at ج۱ ص۸۱, in which the Prophet ﷺ tells him to lend to God and his feet will be freed, has
  خالد بن يزيد in it, whom **الذهبي himself weakens** in his note on المستدرك (editor's note,
  ج۱ ص۸۱). If the story is told at all, tell it as الذهبي resolves it, and give his resolution.
- **Do not read the dispute between him and خالد بن الوليد ؓ as a quarrel to be judged.** The book
  carries it only for the sake of what the Prophet ﷺ said in it — **دعوا لي أصحابي** on one side
  (ج۱ ص۸۲), and **لا تؤذوا خالداً فإنه سيف من سيوف الله** on the other (ج۱ ص۸۳). `[SOURCED]` Both
  sentences are said, and either one alone misrepresents the passage.
- **الذهبي's aside at ج۱ ص۹۲ comparing the grateful rich man with the patient poor one names other
  Companions and the Shamela text of the middle name is unclear.** Do not reproduce that line until

## 5. سعد بن أبي وقاص ؓ

### The تراجم notice, in one breath

- **Name + لقب** — سعد بن أبي وقاص مالك بن أهيب الزهري القرشي, أبو إسحاق. الذهبي heads him
  **الأمير** and then lists: **أحد العشرة · أحد السابقين الأولين · أحد من شهد بدراً والحديبية ·
  أحد الستة أهل الشورى** (ج۱ ص۹۲–۹۳). `[SOURCED]` The tradition's own phrase for him, on the page,
  is the Prophet's ﷺ: **هذا خالي** — *this is my maternal uncle* (editor's note at ج۱ ص۹۹, from
  الحاكم and الترمذي, صححه الحاكم ووافقه الذهبي). `[SOURCED]`
- **Dates** — accepted Islam at **seventeen** per ابن مندة (ج۱ ص۹۶); died **۵۵ھ**, at his own
  residence at **العقيق**, seven miles from المدينة, and was carried into the city (ج۱ ص۹۷).
  `[SOURCED]` On the Line: nineteen years after الجمل, in معاوية's ؓ caliphate — **he outlived the
  whole fitna.**
- **What he did** — the first man in Islam to shoot an arrow in the way of God, the one for whom the
  Prophet ﷺ pledged both his parents at أحد, one of the six of the شورى — and the one who, when the
  fitna came, went out to his sheep and stayed there.

### Clan and kin

**بنو زهرة بن كلاب** — the clan of the Prophet's ﷺ mother, which is why the Prophet ﷺ called him
**خالي**. So he is **the cousin of عبد الرحمن بن عوف ؓ**, which is exactly the point الذهبي makes
about the شورى: had عبد الرحمن been favouring anyone he would have given it to سعد (ج۱ ص۸۶).
`[SOURCED]` His mother was **حمنة بنت سفيان بن أمية بن عبد شمس** per ابن سعد (ج۱ ص۹۶) `[SOURCED]` —
which puts an عبد شمس grandmother on the man who will not take a side. His brother **عمير** was
turned back from بدر as too young, wept, was allowed, and سعد tied on his sword-belt himself
(ج۱ ص۹۷). `[SOURCED]` ابن إسحاق counts him among the five who entered Islam at the hand of
أبي بكر ؓ (ج۱ ص۴۵). `[SOURCED]`

### Before 23 AH, in three or four sentences

He said of himself: *no one accepted Islam on the day I did, and I waited seven nights, and I was a
third of Islam* — البخاري (ج۱ ص۹۸). `[SOURCED]` At أحد the Prophet ﷺ emptied his own quiver out for
him and said **ارم، فداك أبي وأمي** — *shoot, may my father and mother be your ransom* — and علي ؓ
himself said he never heard the Prophet ﷺ pledge both his parents for anyone but سعد (ج۱ ص۱۰۰,
ج۱ ص۱۰۱, البخاري and مسلم). `[SOURCED]` He was the Prophet's ﷺ night-guard: on a night the Prophet ﷺ
could not sleep and said he wished a righteous man of his Companions would watch over him, they
heard the sound of weapons, and it was سعد (ج۱ ص۱۰۲, البخاري and مسلم). `[SOURCED]` And at الخندق
his arrow found the forehead of a man behind a shield, and the Prophet ﷺ laughed until his back
teeth showed (ج۱ ص۱۰۲–۱۰۳). `[SOURCED]`

### In this window (23–41 AH)

- **The people complained of him — even about his prayer — and عمر ؓ put it to him.** He answered
  that he lengthened the first two rakʿahs and shortened the last two, and did not fall short of the
  prayer of the Messenger of Allah ﷺ as he had followed it; and عمر said **ذاك الظن بك** — *that is
  what I thought of you* (ج۱ ص۹۳–۹۴, البخاري and مسلم). `[SOURCED]` **This is the one clean,
  authentic fact that places him in authority over people who complained of him,** and it needs no
  embellishment.
- He was one of the **six of the شورى** (ج۱ ص۹۳), and it was سعد who sent word to عبد الرحمن بن
  عوف ؓ telling him to put himself forward — and got the answer recorded in §4 (ج۱ ص۸۷). `[SOURCED]`
- He was at **سرغ** with المسور, during the plague year, for fifty nights; رمضان came in and he
  **broke the fast while the other two fasted**. Asked how he — a Companion, a man of بدر — could do
  that, he said: **أنا أفقه منهما** (ج۱ ص۹۵). `[SOURCED — editor: إسناده حسن.]`
- ابن عيينة, from عمرو, records in one line: **شهد سعد وابن عمر الحكمين** — سعد and ابن عمر ؓ were
  present at the two arbitrators (ج۱ ص۹۶). `[SOURCED — one line, no grading given by the book;
  report it as the book reports it and no further.]`
- He went to **معاوية ؓ** and stayed with him a month shortening the prayer, and رمضان came and he
  broke the fast; الذهبي's own one-word comment on the chain is **منقطع** (ج۱ ص۹۶). `[SOURCED]`
- **He withdrew from the fighting entirely, and the book gives his reason in his own words.** See
  the واقعہ below.

### One واقعہ worth telling — the old man among his sheep

سعد was out with his flock. His son عمر rode up, and when سعد saw him coming he said: *I seek refuge
with God from the evil of this rider.* When the young man reached him he said: father, are you
content to be a bedouin among your sheep while the people are contending over the kingship at
المدينة? سعد struck his son on the chest and said: be quiet — I heard the Messenger of Allah ﷺ say:
**God, Mighty and Majestic, loves the servant who is God-fearing, self-sufficient, and hidden.**
مسلم carries the ḥadīth (ج۱ ص۱۰۲).

### A second واقعہ — the greeting that was not returned

سعد passed عثمان ؓ in the mosque and greeted him. عثمان looked full at him and did not return the
greeting. سعد went to عمر ؓ and asked whether something new had happened in Islam. عمر sent for
عثمان, who said he had not done it; they went as far as swearing at each other. Then عثمان
remembered, and said: yes — I ask God's forgiveness and turn to Him. You passed me just now while I
was turning over in my mind a word I had heard from the Messenger of Allah ﷺ — by God, I have never
recalled it without a veil coming over my sight and my heart. And سعد said: then I will tell you
what it is. And he told him: the prayer of ذي النون — **لا إله إلا أنت سبحانك إني كنت من الظالمين**
— which no Muslim has ever prayed to his Lord in any matter without being answered (ج۱ ص۹۴–۹۵).

الذهبي notes الترمذي carries it; the editor adds that الحاكم graded it صحيح and الذهبي agreed
(ج۱ ص۹۵). `[SOURCED]` **A scene with عمر ؓ, عثمان ؓ and سعد ؓ all in it, in which the misunderstanding
is cleared up in one sitting — exactly the tone the first half of this session needs before the
second half is told.**

### ⚠ What not to say about him from the platform

- **Do not say he "refused to give بیعہ" or "stood against علي ؓ".** Nothing on these pages says
  that. What the pages say is that he went out to his flock while the people contended, and why. Say
  that, and no more.
- **Do not build anything on «شهد سعد وابن عمر الحكمين»** (ج۱ ص۹۶). It is a single unannotated line
  and it touches صفين, which is outside this strand and inside the مشاجرات. Record it; do not
  narrate it.
- The reports at ج۱ ص۱۰۳–۱۰۶ in which سعيد بن زيد ؓ rises in the mosque of الكوفة because علي ؓ was
  being reviled, and recites the ten given the good news of الجنة, are strong — the editor calls one
  chain **إسناده صحيح** (ج۱ ص۱۰۳) — and the **list of the ten itself** is entirely usable and is
  precisely the frame this session needs. **But the occasion — a Companion being reviled from a
  pulpit — is not narrated in this hall.** Use the ḥadīth; leave the setting out.
