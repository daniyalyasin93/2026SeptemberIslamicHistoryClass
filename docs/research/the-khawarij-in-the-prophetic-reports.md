# The ⁨خوارج⁩ in the Prophetic reports — ⁨البدایہ⁩'s chapter of ⁨الأحاديث الشريفة⁩

**Question this note answers:** What ⁨ابن كثير رحمہ اللہ⁩ collects in his long chapter «⁨ما ورد فيهم من⁩
⁨الأحاديث الشريفة⁩» — which reports about the ⁨خوارج⁩ he gathers, from which Companion each one comes,
and in what order he arranges them. What the principal wordings actually say: the men who recite the
Qurʾān and it does not pass their throats, the «⁨أحداث الأسنان، سفهاء الأحلام⁩» report, the two parties
and which of them kills them, the sign of ⁨ذو الثدية⁩, and the man at ⁨الجعرانة⁩ who told the Prophet ﷺ to
be just. Which of these ⁨ابن كثير⁩ says are in ⁨البخاري⁩ and ⁨مسلم⁩. And his own concluding judgement on the
whole chapter — including the reports inside it that he himself refuses.

## 0. The sentences this note licenses

1. **⁨البدایہ⁩ gives the reports about the ⁨خوارج⁩ their own chapter, «⁨ما ورد فيهم من الأحاديث الشريفة⁩»,
   which opens on ⁨ص۴۸۹⁩ and runs to ⁨ص۵۱۶⁩** `[SOURCED]` — ⁨ص۴۸۹⁩–⁨ص۵۱۶⁩.
2. **The chapter opens immediately after ⁨علي ؓ⁩ is asked what the men of ⁨النهروان⁩ were. He answers that
   they are not ⁨مشركون⁩ and not ⁨منافقون⁩, but «⁨إخواننا بغوا علينا⁩» — our brothers who transgressed
   against us** `[SOURCED]` — ⁨ص۴۸۹⁩. ⚠ **⁨البدایہ⁩ attributes this exchange to ⁨ابن جرير وغيره⁩ in its own
   next sentence** — «⁨هذا ما أورده ابن جرير وغيره في هذا المقام⁩» — so it is quoted as *what ⁨البدایہ⁩
   reports from ⁨ابن جرير⁩*, never as a page of ours from ⁨الطبری⁩.
3. **⁨ابن كثير⁩ counts twelve separate routes (⁨طرق⁩) from ⁨علي ؓ⁩ alone, names all twelve narrators, and
   says that this much reaches the degree of ⁨تواتر⁩** `[SOURCED]` — ⁨ص۴۸۹⁩.
4. ⚠ **The printed edition's editor disagrees with ⁨ابن كثير⁩ in a footnote on that very page**, calling
   the ⁨تواتر⁩ claim «⁨مبالغة ظاهرة⁩» because most of the routes are weak `[SOURCED]` — ⁨ص۴۸۹⁩ fn (٤).
   **He dissents a second time on ⁨ص۴۹۸⁩**, where ⁨ابن كثير⁩ makes the ⁨ابن مسعود ؓ⁩ report the strongest
   proof: the editor answers that «⁨يخرج قوم في آخر الزمان⁩» does not obviously mean ⁨علي ؓ⁩'s own time
   `[SOURCED]` — ⁨ص۴۹۸⁩ fn (٥). **Both are the edition's modern editor, not ⁨ابن كثير⁩, and must be
   labelled as such wherever they are used.**
5. **The first report is ⁨مسلم⁩'s, through ⁨زيد بن وهب⁩, who was in the army: "a people will come out of my
   ummah who recite the Qurʾān — your reciting is nothing beside theirs, your prayer nothing beside
   their prayer, your fasting nothing beside their fasting; they recite the Qurʾān supposing it is for
   them, and it is against them."** `[SOURCED]` — ⁨ص۴۸۹⁩, ⁨ابن كثير⁩ citing ⁨صحیح مسلم⁩.
6. **The best-known wording — «⁨يقرؤون القرآن لا يجاوز حناجرهم⁩ / ⁨تراقيهم، يمرقون من الدين كما يمرق السهم⁩
   ⁨من الرمية⁩» — occurs in this chapter from eight different Companions: ⁨علي ؓ⁩, ⁨ابن مسعود ؓ⁩, ⁨أنس ؓ⁩,
   ⁨جابر ؓ⁩, ⁨أبو سعيد ؓ⁩, ⁨سهل بن حنيف ؓ⁩, ⁨ابن عمر ؓ⁩, ⁨عبد الله بن عمرو ؓ⁩** `[SOURCED]` — «⁨حناجرهم⁩» at ⁨ص۴۹۰⁩,
   ⁨ص۵۰۰⁩, ⁨ص۵۰۴⁩, ⁨ص۵۰۵⁩, ⁨ص۵۰۶⁩, ⁨ص۵۰۸⁩, ⁨ص۵۰۹⁩; «⁨تراقيهم⁩» at ⁨ص۴۹۳⁩, ⁨ص۴۹۵⁩, ⁨ص۴۹۹⁩, ⁨ص۵۰۰⁩, ⁨ص۵۰۲⁩, ⁨ص۵۰۳⁩, ⁨ص۵۰۴⁩, ⁨ص۵۱۰⁩;
   «⁨لا يعدو تراقيهم⁩» at ⁨ص۴۹۸⁩ and ⁨ص۵۰۸⁩. ⚠ **The two nouns are not interchangeable — ⁨حنجرة⁩ is the
   throat and ⁨ترقوة⁩ the collarbone — and the book prints both.**
7. **The «young in years, foolish in mind» report is from ⁨علي ؓ⁩ through ⁨سويد بن غفلة⁩, and ⁨ابن كثير⁩ says
   both ⁨الشيخان⁩ recorded it: «⁨يخرج قوم في آخر الزمان أحداث الأسنان، سفهاء الأحلام⁩»** `[SOURCED]` —
   ⁨ص۴۹۰⁩; ⁨البدایہ⁩ names ⁨صحیح البخاري⁩ and ⁨صحیح مسلم⁩ on the same page.
8. **The same report from ⁨ابن مسعود ؓ⁩ is the strongest chronological point in the chapter, and ⁨ابن كثير⁩

## 1. Where the chapter begins, and the sentence it begins from

The chapter of Prophetic reports does not open cold. The page before it ends with ⁨علي ؓ⁩ being asked
what, exactly, the men he had just fought at ⁨النهروان⁩ were — and his answer is the frame for everything
that follows `[SOURCED]` — ⁨ص۴۸۹⁩.

> النهروان أمشركون هم؟ فقال: من الشرك فروا، قيل: أفمنافقون؟ قال: إن المنافقين لا يذكرون الله إِلَّا قليلًا. فقيل: فما هم يا أمير المؤمنين؟ قال: إخواننا بَغَوا علينا فقاتلناهم ببغيهم علينا.
> — ⁨البدایہ والنہایہ ج۷ ص۴۸۹⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/30097/3635
> *English:* "…of ⁨النهروان⁩ — were they idolaters? He said: from idolatry they fled. It was said: then were they hypocrites? He said: the hypocrites do not remember Allah except a little. It was said: then what are they, O commander of the believers? He said: our brothers who transgressed against us, so we fought them for their transgression against us."

⁨البدایہ⁩ then says in its own voice where that exchange comes from, and this matters for how we quote it:

> هذا ما أورده ابن جرير وغيره في هذا المقام.
> — ⁨البدایہ والنہایہ ج۷ ص۴۸۹⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/30097/3635
> *English:* "This is what ⁨ابن جرير⁩ and others have brought forward in this place."

So the sentence is used as **what ⁨البدایہ⁩ reports from ⁨ابن جرير⁩**, never as a page of ⁨الطبری⁩ read by us.
The chapter heading follows immediately `[SOURCED]` — ⁨ص۴۸۹⁩:

> ما ورد فيهم من الأحاديث الشريفة (١)
> — ⁨البدایہ والنہایہ ج۷ ص۴۸۹⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/30097/3635
> *English:* "What has come down about them of the noble hadiths."

The (١) is the printed edition's footnote marker; the footnote records that manuscript ⁨أ⁩ words the
heading differently.

## 2. Twelve routes from ⁨علي ؓ⁩, and a claim of ⁨تواتر⁩ the editor will not accept

⁨ابن كثير⁩ opens by counting. He names twelve men who narrate this from ⁨علي ؓ⁩, says he will give each with
its isnad and its wording, and states his conclusion before he gives the evidence `[SOURCED]` — ⁨ص۴۸۹⁩:

> الحديث الأول: عن علي ﵁، ورواه (٢) عنه: زيد بن وهب، وسويد بن غفلة، وطارق بن زياد، وعبد اللّه بن شداد، وعبيد اللّه بن أبي رافع، وعَبِيدة بن عَمْرو السلماني، وكليب أبو عاصم، وأبو كثير وأبو مريم، وأبو موسى، وأبو وائل، [وأبو] الوضيء (٣) فهذه اثنتا عشرة طريقًا إليه سرَاها بأسانيدها وألفاظها، ومثل هذا يبلغ حد التواتر (٤).
> — ⁨البدایہ والنہایہ ج۷ ص۴۸۹⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/30097/3635
> *English:* "The first hadith: from ⁨علي ؓ⁩. It is narrated from him by: ⁨زيد بن وهب⁩, ⁨سويد بن غفلة⁩, ⁨طارق بن زياد⁩, ⁨عبد الله بن شداد⁩, ⁨عبيد الله بن أبي رافع⁩, ⁨عبيدة بن عمرو السلماني⁩, ⁨كليب أبو عاصم⁩, ⁨أبو كثير⁩, ⁨أبو مريم⁩, ⁨أبو موسى⁩, ⁨أبو وائل⁩, and ⁨أبو الوضيء⁩ — these are twelve routes to him, which I shall relate with their isnads and their wordings; and the like of this reaches the degree of ⁨تواتر⁩."

⚠ **Shamela typo flag.** The word printed here as «⁨سرَاها⁩» is a mangling — the sense requires
"I shall relate them". Do not project this line from the text file; read it off a page image first.

⚠ **The edition's editor dissents, on the same page, in footnote (٤)** `[SOURCED]` — ⁨ص۴۸۹⁩ fn (٤):

> قال بشار: في قوله: ومثل هذا يبلغ حد التواتر، فيه مبالغة ظاهرة، ذلك أن أكثر هذه الطرق ضعيفة، كما سيأتي!
> — ⁨البدایہ والنہایہ ج۷ ص۴۸۹⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/30097/3635
> *English:* "⁨بشار⁩ says: in his words ‘and the like of this reaches the degree of ⁨تواتر⁩’ there is plain exaggeration, because most of these routes are weak, as will come."

That is the **modern editor of this printed edition**, not ⁨ابن كثير⁩ and not one of the safe-list
authorities. It is recorded because a listener who looks the chapter up will meet it, and because the
speaker should never be caught claiming more than the page claims. Where it is used it is attributed
to "the editor of this edition".

## 3. The first report — ⁨مسلم⁩, through a man who was in the army

The first route is the longest and it is ⁨مسلم⁩'s. ⁨زيد بن وهب الجهني⁩ was himself in the army that marched
against them, and he reports ⁨علي ؓ⁩ addressing the troops before the fighting `[SOURCED]` — ⁨ص۴۸۹⁩:

> "يخرج قوم من أمتي يقرؤون القرآن ليس قراءتكم إلى قراءتهم بشيء، ولا صلاتكم إلى صلاتهم بشيء، ولا صيامكم إلى صيامهم بشيء، يقرؤون القرآن يحسبون أنه لهم وهو عليهم".
> — ⁨البدایہ والنہایہ ج۷ ص۴۸۹⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/30097/3635
> *English:* "A people will come out of my ummah who recite the Qurʾān — your reciting is nothing beside their reciting, your prayer nothing beside their prayer, your fasting nothing beside their fasting. They recite the Qurʾān supposing that it is for them, and it is against them."

Then the sign, given in the same breath, before anyone had been killed `[SOURCED]` — ⁨ص۴۸۹⁩:

> وآية ذلك أن فيهم رجلًا له عَضُد ليس له ذراع، على رأس عضده [مثل] حلمة الثَّدي، عليه شعرات بيضى،
> — ⁨البدایہ والنہایہ ج۷ ص۴۸۹⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/30097/3635
> *English:* "And the sign of that is that among them is a man with an upper arm and no forearm, on the end of his upper arm something like the nipple of a breast, with white hairs on it,"

And the order to march, which in this wording is ⁨علي ؓ⁩ choosing these men over the Syrian campaign
`[SOURCED]` — ⁨ص۴۸۹⁩:

> فتذهبون إلى معاوية وأهل الشام، وتتركون هؤلاء يخلفونكم في ذراريِّكم وأموالكم، و [اللّه] إني لأرجو أن يكونوا هؤلاء القومَ، فإنَّهم قد سفكوا الدَّم الحرام وأغاروا في سرح الناس، فسيروا على اسم اللّه.
> — ⁨البدایہ والنہایہ ج۷ ص۴۸۹⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/30097/3635
> *English:* "You go off to ⁨معاوية⁩ and the people of ⁨الشام⁩ and leave these people behind you among your children and your property — and by Allah I hope that these are the people, for they have shed forbidden blood and raided the people’s grazing herds. So march, in the name of Allah."

**This is ⁨صحیح مسلم⁩'s wording as ⁨البدایہ⁩ prints it, and the clause naming ⁨الشام⁩ is part of it.** See
§Not for the hall: the sentence is quotable, the argument it invites is not.

## 4. The bridge, the two dead, and the search

The same route continues into the fighting. The order given to the ⁨خوارج⁩ by their own commander ⁨عبد⁩
⁨الله بن وهب الراسبي⁩ is preserved `[SOURCED]` — ⁨ص۴۹۰⁩:

> فقال لهم: ألقوا الرماح وسُلّوا سيوفكم واكسروا جفونَها (١) فإني أخاف أن يناشدوكم كما ناشدوكم يوم حروراء، فرجعوا فوحشوا برماحهم وسلّوا السيوف فشجرهم الناس برماحهم.
> — ⁨البدایہ والنہایہ ج۷ ص۴۹۰⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/30097/3636
> *English:* "He said to them: throw down the spears and draw your swords and break their scabbards, for I fear that they will appeal to you as they appealed to you on the day of ⁨حروراء⁩. So they went back and threw away their spears and drew the swords — and the people ran them through with their spears."

Then the count, and the search `[SOURCED]` — ⁨ص۴۹۰⁩:

> قال: وقتل بعضهم على بعض وما أصيب من الناس يومئذ إِلَّا رجلان، فقال (٢) علي: التمسوا فيهم المُخْدَج (٣)، فالتمسوه فلم يجدوه، فقام على بنفسه حتى أتى ناسًا [قد قتل] بعضهم على بعض، فقال: أخروهم فوجدوه مما يلي الأرض (٤) فكبر [علي] ثم قال: صدق الله وبلغ رسوله.
> — ⁨البدایہ والنہایہ ج۷ ص۴۹۰⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/30097/3636
> *English:* "He said: and they were killed one upon another, and of the [other] people only two men were lost that day. ⁨علي⁩ said: look among them for the maimed man. They looked for him and did not find him. So he rose himself until he came to some people fallen one upon another, and said: move them back — and they found him next to the ground. Then ⁨علي⁩ made takbīr and said: Allah has spoken truly and His Messenger has conveyed."

And then, still in ⁨مسلم⁩, the oath `[SOURCED]` — ⁨ص۴۹۰⁩:

> قال: فقام إليه عَبيدة السلماني فقال: يا أمير المؤمنين والله الذي لا إله (إلَّا هو لسمعت هذا من رسول اللّه ﷺ قال: إي واللّه الذي لا إله إِلَّا هو)، فاستحلفه ثلاثًا وهو يحلف له أنه سمعه من رسول الله ﷺ، هذا لفظ مسلم.
> — ⁨البدایہ والنہایہ ج۷ ص۴۹۰⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/30097/3636
> *English:* "⁨عبيدة السلماني⁩ rose to him and said: O commander of the believers, by Allah beside whom there is no god — did you hear this from the Messenger of Allah ﷺ? He said: Yes, by Allah beside whom there is no god. He made him swear three times, and each time he swore to him that he had heard it from the Messenger of Allah ﷺ. This is ⁨مسلم⁩’s wording."

⁨البدایہ⁩ adds that ⁨أبو داود⁩ narrated it too, from ⁨الحسن بن علي الخلال⁩ from ⁨عبد الرزاق⁩, to the like effect.

## 5. «⁨أحداث الأسنان، سفهاء الأحلام⁩» — and ⁨علي ؓ⁩'s rule for narrating

The second route, through ⁨سويد بن غفلة⁩, is the one the room will remember. It begins with ⁨علي ؓ⁩ drawing
a line between two kinds of speech — what he reports from the Prophet ﷺ, and what he says on his own
account in war `[SOURCED]` — ⁨ص۴۹۰⁩:

> إذا حدَّثتكم عن رسول الله ﷺ فلأن أخرَّ من السماء أحبُّ إليَّ من أن أكذب عليه، وإذا حدثتكم فيما بيني وبينكم فإنّ الحربَ خدعةٌ،
> — ⁨البدایہ والنہایہ ج۷ ص۴۹۰⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/30097/3636
> *English:* "When I narrate to you from the Messenger of Allah ﷺ, then for me to fall from the sky is dearer to me than that I should lie about him; and when I speak to you of what is between me and you — then war is deception."

Then the report itself `[SOURCED]` — ⁨ص۴۹۰⁩:

> "يخرجُ قومٌ في آخر الزمان أحداث الأسنان، سفهاء الأحلام، يقولون من قول خير البريَّة (يقرؤون القرآنَ لا يجاوز حناجرهم - قال عبد الرحمن لا يجاوز إيمانهم حناجرهم -) يمرقون من الدين كما يمرق السهم من الرَّميَّة (٩)، فإذا لقيتموهم فاقتلوهم فإن في قتلهم أجرًا لمن قتلهم عند اللّه يوم القيامة"
> — ⁨البدایہ والنہایہ ج۷ ص۴۹۰⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/30097/3636
> *English:* "There will come out a people at the end of time, young in years, foolish in mind. They speak with the speech of the best of creation — they recite the Qurʾān and it does not pass beyond their throats (⁨عبد الرحمن⁩ said: their faith does not pass beyond their throats). They pass clean out of the religion as the arrow passes out of the game. So when you meet them, kill them: for in killing them there is a reward with Allah on the Day of Rising for whoever kills them."

And ⁨البدایہ⁩ states where it is `[SOURCED]` — ⁨ص۴۹۰⁩:

> وأخرجاه في الصحيحين (١٠) من طرق عن الأعمش به.
> — ⁨البدایہ والنہایہ ج۷ ص۴۹۰⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/30097/3636
> *English:* "The two of them [⁨البخاري⁩ and ⁨مسلم⁩] brought it out in the two Ṣaḥīḥs by several routes from ⁨الأعمش⁩."

The printed footnote on that page gives the two numbers — ⁨صحيح البخاري⁩ in ⁨كتاب استتابة المرتدين⁩ and
⁨صحيح مسلم⁩ in ⁨كتاب الزكاة⁩. **Those numbers belong to the edition's apparatus; the note does not repeat
them as ours, and a slide should say "in ⁨البخاري⁩ and ⁨مسلم⁩, as ⁨ابن كثير⁩ states", nothing finer.**

## 6. "If he is among them… and if he is not" — the route of ⁨طارق بن زياد⁩

This route makes the sign a two-edged test, and the men who heard it wept `[SOURCED]` — ⁨ص۴۹۱⁩:

> "سيجيء قومٌ يتكلمون بكلمة الحق لا تجاوز حلوقهم، يمرقون من الإسلام كما يمرقُ السهمُ من الرمية، سيماهم أو فيهم رجلٌ أسود مخدج اليد في يده شعرات سودٌ، إن كان فيهم [فقد] قتلتم شر الناس، وإن لم يكن فيهم فقد قتلتم خير الناس"
> — ⁨البدایہ والنہایہ ج۷ ص۴۹۱⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/30097/3637
> *English:* "A people will come who speak the word of truth and it does not pass their throats. They pass clean out of Islam as the arrow passes out of the game. Their mark — or: among them — is a black man with a maimed hand, on his hand black hairs. If he is among them, you have killed the worst of people; and if he is not among them, you have killed the best of people."
> قال الوليد في روايته: فبكينا قال: [ثم] إنا وجدنا المُخْدَجَ فخررنا سجودًا وخرَّ عليٌّ (ساجدًا) معنا.
> — ⁨البدایہ والنہایہ ج۷ ص۴۹۱⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/30097/3637
> *English:* "⁨الوليد⁩ said in his narration: so we wept. He said: then we found the maimed man, and we fell down in prostration, and ⁨علي⁩ fell prostrate with us."

⁨ابن كثير⁩ notes that ⁨أحمد⁩ alone narrates it by this route — «⁨تفرَّد به أحمد من هذا الوجه⁩» — and the
edition's editor marks the isnad weak for the obscurity of ⁨طارق بن زياد⁩ while calling the hadith itself
⁨حسن⁩ `[SOURCED]` — ⁨ص۴۹۱⁩ and fn (١).

## 7. «⁨كلمة حق أريد بها باطل⁩» — ⁨مسلم⁩, through ⁨عبيد الله بن أبي رافع⁩

This is the exchange that gives the speaker his safest single line about slogans `[SOURCED]` — ⁨ص۴۹۱⁩:

> قالوا: لا حكمَ إِلَّا للّه، قال علي: كلمةُ حقّ أريدَ بها باطل، إن رسول الله ﷺ وصف ناسًا إني لأعرف صفتهم في هؤلاء "يقولون الحقَّ بألسنتهم لا يجاوز هذا منهم - وأشار إلى حلقه - من أبغض خلق اللّه، منهم أسود. إحدى يديه طُبْيُ (٦) شاة أو حلمة ثدي"
> — ⁨البدایہ والنہایہ ج۷ ص۴۹۱⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/30097/3637
> *English:* "They said: judgement belongs to none but Allah. ⁨علي⁩ said: a word of truth by which falsehood is intended. The Messenger of Allah ﷺ described a people — I recognise their description in these men: ‘They speak the truth with their tongues, and it does not pass beyond this of them’ — and he pointed to his throat — ‘of the most hateful of Allah’s creation to Him; among them is a black man, one of his two hands is like the teat of a ewe, or the nipple of a breast.’"

And when the body was not found at once `[SOURCED]` — ⁨ص۴۹۱⁩:

> فقال: ارجعوا فانظروا فواللّه ما كذَبْتُ ولا كُذبْتُ - مرتين أو ثلاثًا
> — ⁨البدایہ والنہایہ ج۷ ص۴۹۱⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/30097/3637
> *English:* "He said: go back and look — for by Allah I have not lied and I have not been lied to — twice or three times."

⁨البدایہ⁩ says of this route: «⁨تفرد به مسلم⁩» — ⁨مسلم⁩ alone `[SOURCED]` — ⁨ص۴۹۱⁩.

## 8. The oath by the Lord of the Kaʿba, and the chain of oaths

Four short routes through ⁨عبيدة السلماني⁩ follow, all from ⁨مسند أحمد⁩. The wording that repeats
`[SOURCED]` — ⁨ص۴۹۲⁩:

> ولولا أن تبطروا لحدَّثتُكم بما وعدَ اللهُ الذين يقتلونهم على لسان محمد ﷺ، قال: قلت: أنتَ سمعتَه من محمد ﷺ؟ قال: إي وربِّ الكعبة: إي وربِّ الكعبة، إي وربِّ الكعبة.
> — ⁨البدایہ والنہایہ ج۷ ص۴۹۲⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/30097/3638
> *English:* "And were it not that you would grow insolent, I would tell you what Allah has promised, on the tongue of ⁨محمد ﷺ⁩, to those who kill them. He said: I said, did you yourself hear it from ⁨محمد ﷺ⁩? He said: Yes, by the Lord of the Kaʿba; yes, by the Lord of the Kaʿba; yes, by the Lord of the Kaʿba."

⁨ابن كثير⁩ then draws the chain of oaths together — three men, each swearing to the next `[SOURCED]` —
ص۴۹۳:

> وقد ذكرناه من طرق متعددة تفيد القطع عند كثيرين عن محمد بن سيرين، وقد حلف (على) أنه سمعه من عبيدة، وحلف عبيدة أنه سمعه من علي [وحلف عليٌّ] أنه سمعه (٣) من رسول اللّه ﷺ، (وقد) قال (علي): لأنْ أخرَّ من السماء إلى الأرض أحبُّ إليَّ من أن أكذب على رسول اللّه ﷺ.
> — ⁨البدایہ والنہایہ ج۷ ص۴۹۳⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/30097/3639
> *English:* "We have given it by several routes which for many yield certainty, from ⁨محمد بن سيرين⁩; and he swore that he heard it from ⁨عبيدة⁩, and ⁨عبيدة⁩ swore that he heard it from ⁨علي⁩, and ⁨علي⁩ swore that he heard it from the Messenger of Allah ﷺ. And ⁨علي⁩ said: for me to fall from the sky to the earth is dearer to me than that I should lie about the Messenger of Allah ﷺ."

A further route, through ⁨كليب⁩ the father of ⁨عاصم⁩, places the telling in the Prophet's ﷺ own house, with
⁨عائشة ؓ⁩ present `[SOURCED]` — ⁨ص۴۹۳⁩:

> "قوم يخرجون من قبل المشرق، يقرؤون القرآن لا يجاوز تراقيهم يمرقون من الدِّين كما يمرقُ السهمُ من الرميَّةِ، فيهم رجل مُخْدجُ اليد كأن يديه ثدْيُ حَبَشيَّةٍ"
> — ⁨البدایہ والنہایہ ج۷ ص۴۹۳⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/30097/3639
> *English:* "A people who come out from the direction of the east; they recite the Qurʾān and it does not pass beyond their collarbones; they pass clean out of the religion as the arrow passes out of the game; among them is a man with a maimed hand, as though his hands were the breast of an Abyssinian woman."

⁨ابن كثير⁩ says of that one: «⁨وإسناده جيد⁩» — its isnad is good — and that the six books did not bring it
out `[SOURCED]` — ⁨ص۴۹۳⁩.

## 9. The hour of the search — five accounts of the same hour, and they differ

This is the most valuable methodological page in the strand, because ⁨البدایہ⁩ prints five accounts of a
single hour and does not smooth them into one. **They are not merged here either.**

**⁨أبو جحيفة⁩'s account — the name of the place** `[SOURCED]` — ⁨ص۴۹۴⁩:

> فقال: ويلكم ما اسم هذا المكان؟ قالوا: النهروان، قال: كذبتم إنه لفيهم فثورنا (٥) القتلى فلم نجده فعدنا إليه فقلنا: يا أمير المؤمنين ما نجد (٦)، قال: ما اسم هذا المكان؟ قلنا: النهروان، قال: صدق اللّه ورسوله وكذبتم، إنه لفيهم فالتمسوه،
> — ⁨البدایہ والنہایہ ج۷ ص۴۹۴⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/30097/3640
> *English:* "He said: woe to you — what is the name of this place? They said: ⁨النهروان⁩. He said: you are mistaken, he is certainly among them. So we turned over the slain and did not find him, and we came back to him and said: commander of the believers, we do not find him. He said: what is the name of this place? We said: ⁨النهروان⁩. He said: Allah has spoken truly, and His Messenger; and you are mistaken — he is certainly among them, so search for him."

The same route adds that the narrator never saw ⁨علي ؓ⁩ in greater distress than that day — «⁨فما رأيت⁩
⁨عليًّا جزع جزعًا أشد من جزعه يومئذ⁩» `[SOURCED]` — ⁨ص۴۹۴⁩. The edition's editor marks this isnad weak
(⁨عطاء بن السائب⁩ after his memory changed) while saying the matn itself is sound `[SOURCED]` — ⁨ص۴۹۴⁩
fn (٧).

**⁨أبو كثير مولى الأنصار⁩'s account — why the search mattered to the army** `[SOURCED]` — ⁨ص۴۹۴⁩:

> فكانَ الناسَ وجدوا في أنفسهم من قتلهم، فقال عليٌّ: يا أيّها الناسُ إنَّ رسولَ اللّه ﷺ، قد حَدَّثَنَا بأقوام يمرقون من الدِّين كما يمرق السهم من الرمية ثم لا يرجعون فيه أبدًا حتى يرجع السهم على فُوقه، وإن آيةَ ذلك أن فيهم رجلًا أسود مُخْدجَ اليدِ، إحدى يديه كَثَدْي المرأة، لها حلمة كحلمة ثدي المرأة، حوله سبع هلبات فالتمسوه فإني أُراه فيهم"
> — ⁨البدایہ والنہایہ ج۷ ص۴۹۴⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/30097/3640
> *English:* "The people had found something within themselves about having killed them. So ⁨علي⁩ said: O people, the Messenger of Allah ﷺ told us of a people who would pass clean out of the religion as the arrow passes out of the game, and then never return to it until the arrow returns to its notch; and the sign of that is that among them is a black man with a maimed hand, one of his two hands like a woman’s breast, having a nipple like the nipple of a woman’s breast, around it seven coarse hairs. So search for him, for I think he is among them."
> فالتمسوه فوجدوه إلى شفير النهر تحت القتلى فأخرجوه فكبَّر عليٌّ، فقال: الله أكبر! صدق الله ورسوله، وإنه لمُتقلِّدٌ قوسًا له عربية، فأخذها بيده فجعل يطعنُ بها في مُخْدَجته (٩) ويقول: صدق الله ورسوله. وكبّر الناسُ حين رأوه واستبشروا، وذهب عنهم ما كانوا يجدون"
> — ⁨البدایہ والنہایہ ج۷ ص۴۹۴⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/30097/3640
> *English:* "So they searched for him and found him at the lip of the river beneath the slain, and brought him out, and ⁨علي⁩ made takbīr and said: Allah is greatest! Allah has spoken truly, and His Messenger. He had an Arab bow slung on him; ⁨علي⁩ took it in his hand and began to prod the maimed arm with it, saying: Allah has spoken truly, and His Messenger. And the people made takbīr when they saw him and were glad, and what they had been finding within themselves left them."

**⁨أبو مريم⁩'s account, and the name of the man** — this is the most concrete thing in the chapter, and it
comes through ⁨أبو داود⁩ `[SOURCED]` — ⁨ص۴۹۵⁩:

> قال: إن كان ذاك المُخْدج لمعنا يومئذ في المسجد نجالسه بالليل (٥) والنهار، وكان فقيرًا، ورأيته مع المساكين يشهد طعامَ عليٍّ مع الناس، وقد كسوتُه برنسًا لي. قال أبو مريم: وكان المُخْدج يسمى نافعًا ذا الثُّدية، وكان في يده مثل ثدي المرأة، على رأسه حَلَمةُ مثل حَلَمة الثَّدي عليه شعرات مثل سبالة السِّنَّور.
> — ⁨البدایہ والنہایہ ج۷ ص۴۹۵⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/30097/3641
> *English:* "He said: that maimed man was with us that day in the mosque; we used to sit with him by night and by day, and he was poor. I saw him among the destitute, present at ⁨علي⁩’s food along with the people, and I had clothed him in a hooded cloak of mine. ⁨أبو مريم⁩ said: the maimed man was called ⁨نافع ذو الثدية⁩; in his hand was the like of a woman’s breast, on its end a nipple like the nipple of a breast, with hairs on it like a cat’s whiskers."

**⁨أبو مؤمن⁩'s account — the seven under the palm tree** `[SOURCED]` — ⁨ص۴۹۶⁩:

> فخرَّ عليٌّ ساجدًا وقال: أبشروا قتلاكم في الجنة وقتلاهم في النار. ثم قال البزار: لا نعلم روى أبو مؤمن (١) عن علي غير هذا الحديث.
> — ⁨البدایہ والنہایہ ج۷ ص۴۹۶⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/30097/3642
> *English:* "⁨علي⁩ fell down prostrate and said: rejoice — your slain are in the Garden and their slain are in the Fire."

⁨البزار⁩, from whom ⁨البدایہ⁩ takes it, adds that this narrator is not known to have narrated anything else
from ⁨علي ؓ⁩; the edition's editor marks the isnad weak for that reason `[SOURCED]` — ⁨ص۴۹۵⁩ fn (٨).

**⁨أبو وائل⁩'s account — the weeping, and the grey mule** `[SOURCED]` — ⁨ص۴۹۶⁩:

> فطلبناه فلم نجده، فبكى [علي] وقال: اطلبوه، فوالله ما كَذَبْتُ ولا كُذِبْتُ، قال: (فطلبناه فلم نجده فبكى وقال: اطلبوه فواللّه ما كَذَبْتُ ولا كُذِبْتُ، قال: فطلبناه فلم نجده قال: فركب بغلته الشهباء فطلبناه فوجدناه تحت بردي. فلما رآه سجد.
> — ⁨البدایہ والنہایہ ج۷ ص۴۹۶⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/30097/3642
> *English:* "We searched for him and did not find him, and he wept and said: search for him, for by Allah I have not lied and I have not been lied to. He said: we searched for him and did not find him, and he wept and said: search for him, for by Allah I have not lied and I have not been lied to. He said: we searched for him and did not find him. He said: then he rode his dapple-grey mule, and we searched and found him under a reed-bed; and when he saw him he prostrated."

⁨البدایہ⁩ immediately records ⁨البزار⁩'s own remark that this is the only thing ⁨حبيب⁩ narrates from ⁨شقيق⁩ from
⁨علي ؓ⁩, and the edition's editor adds that the sounder view is that it is ⁨مرسل⁩ `[SOURCED]` — ⁨ص۴۹۶⁩ and
fn (٣). **Two accounts of the same moment therefore differ on whether he wept, sweated, or made
takbīr — and ⁨البدایہ⁩ prints them side by side rather than choosing.**

**⁨أبو الوضيء⁩'s account — the description** `[SOURCED]` — ⁨ص۴۹۶⁩:

> فكأني أنظر إليه حبشي عليه ثَدْي قد طبق إحدى يديه مثل ثدي المرأة، عليها (٦) شعرات مثل شعرات تكون على ذنب اليربوع.
> — ⁨البدایہ والنہایہ ج۷ ص۴۹۶⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/30097/3642
> *English:* "It is as though I am looking at him — an Abyssinian, on him a breast that covered one of his two hands, like a woman’s breast, on it hairs like the hairs that are on the tail of a jerboa."

The edition's editor calls this isnad **⁨صحيح⁩** `[SOURCED]` — ⁨ص۴۹۶⁩ fn (٤).

## 10. The one report in this run that ⁨ابن كثير⁩ will not swallow

Immediately after those routes ⁨البدایہ⁩ prints a version in which ⁨علي ؓ⁩ is made to speak of three
brothers among the jinn `[SOURCED]` — ⁨ص۴۹۷⁩:

> أما أن خليلي أخبرني بثلاثة إخوة من الجن، هذا أكبرهم، والثاني له جمع كثير، والثالث فيه ضعف.
> — ⁨البدایہ والنہایہ ج۷ ص۴۹۷⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/30097/3643
> *English:* "…my dear friend ﷺ told me of three brothers among the jinn: this is the eldest of them, and the second has a great host, and in the third there is weakness."

And then refuses to build on it `[SOURCED]` — ⁨ص۴۹۷⁩:

> وهذا السياق فيه غرابة [شديدة] جدًّا. وقد يمكن أن يكون ذو الثدية من الجن؟ بل هو من الشياطين إما شياطين الإنس أو شياطين الجن، إن صح هذا السياق، واللّه تعالى أعلم.
> — ⁨البدایہ والنہایہ ج۷ ص۴۹۷⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/30097/3643
> *English:* "This account has a very severe strangeness in it. It may be that ⁨ذو الثدية⁩ was of the jinn — rather, he is of the devils, either the devils of mankind or the devils of the jinn — if this account is sound. And Allah most high knows best."

**That is the model for the whole strand: the collector prints the report and then tells you what he
thinks of it, in the same paragraph.** Record it that way; never quote the jinn report without the
sentence that follows it.

## 11. ⁨ابن كثير⁩'s verdict on the ⁨علي ؓ⁩ material

He closes the twelve routes with a summary that is worth having verbatim, because it says precisely how
far he is willing to go `[SOURCED]` — ⁨ص۴۹۷⁩:

> والمقصود أن هذه طرق متواترة عن علي؛ إذ قد روي من طرق متعددة عن جماعة متباينة لا يمكن تواطؤهم على الكذب، فأصل القصة محفوظ (٧) وإن كان بعض الألفاظ وقع فيها اختلاف بين الرواة ولكن معناها وأصلها الذي تواطأت الروايات عليه صحيح لا يُشك فيه عن علي أنه رواه عن رسول اللّه ﷺ أنه أخبر (٨) عن صفة الخوارج و [صفة] ذي الثدية الذي هو علامة عليهم.
> — ⁨البدایہ والنہایہ ج۷ ص۴۹۷⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/30097/3643
> *English:* "The point is that these are routes ⁨متواترة⁩ from ⁨علي⁩; for it has been narrated by numerous routes from a scattered body of men who cannot have colluded on a lie. So the substance of the story is preserved — even if some of the wordings vary between the narrators, its meaning and its substance, upon which the narrations agree, is sound, not to be doubted, from ⁨علي⁩, that he narrated it from the Messenger of Allah ﷺ, that he gave news of the description of the ⁨خوارج⁩ and of the description of ⁨ذو الثدية⁩ who is a sign upon them."

**Note what he concedes in the middle of it: «⁨وإن كان بعض الألفاظ وقع فيها اختلاف بين الرواة⁩».** The
wordings differ; the substance does not. That single clause is the honest answer to a listener who
notices that the report is quoted three different ways.

## 12. The roll of Companions, and why ⁨ابن مسعود ؓ⁩ is placed second

Having finished with ⁨علي ؓ⁩, ⁨ابن كثير⁩ lists the other Companions from whom the report comes `[SOURCED]` —
ص۴۹۸:

> منهم أنس بن مالك، وجابر بن عبد الله، ورافع بن عمرو الغفاري، وسعد بن أبي وقاص، (وأبو سعيد سعد بن مالك بن سنان الأنصاري، وسهل بن حُنيف، وعبد اللّه بن عباس، وعبد اللّه بن عمر، وعبد اللّه بن عَمرو)، وعبد اللّه بن مسعود، وأبو ذَرّ، وعائشة (أم المؤمنين ﵃ أجمعين) (١).
> — ⁨البدایہ والنہایہ ج۷ ص۴۹۸⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/30097/3644
> *English:* "Among them: ⁨أنس بن مالك⁩, ⁨جابر بن عبد الله⁩, ⁨رافع بن عمرو الغفاري⁩, ⁨سعد بن أبي وقاص⁩, (and ⁨أبو سعيد سعد بن مالك بن سنان الأنصاري⁩, ⁨سهل بن حنيف⁩, ⁨عبد الله بن عباس⁩, ⁨عبد الله بن عمر⁩, ⁨عبد الله بن عمرو⁩), ⁨عبد الله بن مسعود⁩, ⁨أبو ذر⁩, and ⁨عائشة⁩ (⁨أم المؤمنين ﵃⁩ all together)."

The edition's editor adds a footnote at that point: ⁨سلمان الفارسي ؓ⁩ is narrated later in the chapter and
is missing from this roll `[SOURCED]` — ⁨ص۴۹۸⁩ fn (١). Then ⁨ابن كثير⁩ explains his own ordering
`[SOURCED]` — ⁨ص۴۹۸⁩:

> وقد قدمنا حديث علي بطرقه لأنه أحد الخلفاء الأربعة وأحد العشرة وصاحب القصة. ولنذكر بعده حديث ابن مسعود لتقدم وفاته على وقعة الخوارج.
> — ⁨البدایہ والنہایہ ج۷ ص۴۹۸⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/30097/3644
> *English:* "We have put ⁨علي⁩’s hadith first with its routes because he is one of the four caliphs, one of the ten, and the man the affair happened to. And let us give after it the hadith of ⁨ابن مسعود⁩, because his death came before the affair of the ⁨خوارج⁩."

**That last clause is the whole argument of the chapter compressed into one line, and it is worth saying
from the platform exactly as he says it.** He repeats it as a statement on the same page `[SOURCED]` —
ص۴۹۸:

> ابن مسعود مات قبل ظهور الخوارج بنحو من خمس سنين، فخبره في ذلك من أقوى الأسانيد (٥).
> — ⁨البدایہ والنہایہ ج۷ ص۴۹۸⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/30097/3644
> *English:* "⁨ابن مسعود⁩ died about five years before the appearance of the ⁨خوارج⁩; so his report in this matter is among the strongest of supports."

The report itself `[SOURCED]` — ⁨ص۴۹۸⁩:

> "يخرج قوم في آخر الزمان سفهاء الأحلام، أحداث - أو قال حُدَثاء - الأسنان، يقولون من خيرِ قولِ الناس يقرؤون القرآن بألسنتهم لا يعدو تراقيهم، يمرقون من الإسلام كما يمرق السهم من الرمية، فمن أدركهم فليقتُلهم، فإن في قتلهم أجرًا عظيمًا عند الله لمن قتلهم".
> — ⁨البدایہ والنہایہ ج۷ ص۴۹۸⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/30097/3644
> *English:* "A people will come out at the end of time, foolish in mind, young — or he said: fresh — in years. They speak from the best of what people say; they recite the Qurʾān with their tongues and it does not go past their collarbones. They pass clean out of Islam as the arrow passes out of the game. So whoever reaches them, let him kill them, for in killing them there is a great reward with Allah for whoever kills them."

⁨البدایہ⁩ adds that ⁨الترمذي⁩ and ⁨ابن ماجة⁩ brought it out and that ⁨الترمذي⁩ called it ⁨حسن صحيح⁩ `[SOURCED]` —
ص۴۹۸.

⚠ **And the edition's editor objects, in a footnote on the same page** `[SOURCED]` — ⁨ص۴۹۸⁩ fn (٥): he
writes that this does not by itself establish that the Prophet ﷺ meant the men who went out against
⁨علي ؓ⁩, and points at the words «⁨يخرج قوم في آخر الزمان⁩» — was ⁨علي ؓ⁩'s age the end of time? He then adds
that the people of hadith do not doubt the soundness of the matn. **That is the editor of this printed
edition arguing with the author whose book it is.** It is recorded here so that nobody meets it for the
first time in a hostile question.

## 13. ⁨أنس ؓ⁩, and the honest sentence inside his report

The third hadith is ⁨أنس ؓ⁩'s, and what makes it worth the slide is the clause the narrator refuses to drop
`[SOURCED]` — ⁨ص۴۹۸⁩:

> قال: ذُكِرَ لي أن نبي الله ﷺ قال - ولم أسمعه منه -: "إنَّ فيكم قومًا (٧) يعبدون ويدأبوان (٨) حتى يعجبوا الناس وتعجبهم أنفسُهم، يمرقون من الدِّين كما يمرق السهم من الرمية".
> — ⁨البدایہ والنہایہ ج۷ ص۴۹۸⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/30097/3644
> *English:* "He said: it was mentioned to me that the Prophet of Allah ﷺ said — and I did not hear it from him — "Among you is a people who worship and toil until the people marvel at them and they marvel at themselves; they pass clean out of the religion as the arrow passes out of the game."

**«⁨ولم أسمعه منه⁩» — "and I did not hear it from him."** A Companion marking the limit of his own
knowledge inside the report itself. This is the single best "how do we know?" remark in the strand, and
it costs nothing to say (`docs/catalogue/HOWWEKNOW.md` is the bank it belongs in).

The second route from ⁨أنس ؓ⁩, shared with ⁨أبو سعيد ؓ⁩, is the fullest description of them in the chapter
`[SOURCED]` — ⁨ص۴۹۹⁩:

> "سيكون في أمتي اختلاف وفُرقة، قومٌ يحسنون القيل ويُسيئون الفعل، يقرؤون القرآن لا يجاوز تراقيهم، يحقر أحدكم صلاته مع صلاتهم وصيامه مع صيامهم، يمرقون من الدين كما يمرق السهم من الرَّمِيَّة، لا يرجعون حتى يرتد السهم على فُوقه (٢)، هم شر الخلق والخليقة طوبى لمن قتلهم وقتلوه (٣)، يدعون إلى كتاب الله وليسوا منه في شيء، مَنْ قاتلهم كان أولى بالله منهم" قالوا: يا رسول اللّه ما سيماهم؟ قال: "التحليق".
> — ⁨البدایہ والنہایہ ج۷ ص۴۹۹⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/30097/3645
> *English:* "There will be in my ummah disagreement and division: a people who speak well and act badly; they recite the Qurʾān and it does not pass their collarbones; one of you will think little of his prayer beside their prayer and of his fasting beside their fasting. They pass clean out of the religion as the arrow passes out of the game — they do not come back until the arrow returns to its notch. They are the worst of creation and of created things; blessed is he who kills them and whom they kill. They call to the Book of Allah and they are nothing of it. Whoever fights them is nearer to Allah than they are. They said: Messenger of Allah, what is their mark? He said: the shaven head."

## 14. ⁨جابر ؓ⁩ and the man at ⁨الجعرانة⁩ — the scene the whole chapter is built on

This is the origin scene, and ⁨البدایہ⁩ gives it three times over one page from ⁨جابر ؓ⁩ `[SOURCED]` — ⁨ص۵۰۰⁩:

> فقال رجل: يا رسول الله، اعْدِلْ، فقال: "وَيْلَكَ ومَنْ يَعْدِلُ إذا لم أَعْدل؟ لقد خِبت إن لم أكن أعدل"
> — ⁨البدایہ والنہایہ ج۷ ص۵۰۰⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/30097/3646
> *English:* "A man said: Messenger of Allah, be just. He said: "Woe to you — and who is just if I am not just? I have failed if I am not just."
> فقال عمر: يا رسول اللّه دعني أقتل هذا المنافق، فقال: "معاذ اللّه أن يتحدَّثَ الناس أني أقتل أصحابي، إن هذا وأصحابه يقرؤون القرآن لا يجاوز حناجرهم، أو تراقيهم، يمرقون من الدين مروق (٢) السهم من الرمية".
> — ⁨البدایہ والنہایہ ج۷ ص۵۰۰⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/30097/3646
> *English:* "⁨عمر⁩ said: Messenger of Allah, let me kill this hypocrite. He said: "Allah forbid that people should talk of it, that I kill my own companions. This man and his companions recite the Qurʾān and it does not pass their throats — or: their collarbones; they pass clean out of the religion as the arrow passes out of the game."

The third telling on the same page names the man's tribe — «⁨قام رجل من بني تميم⁩» — and ⁨البدایہ⁩ says ⁨مسلم⁩
and ⁨النسائي⁩ narrate it `[SOURCED]` — ⁨ص۵۰۰⁩.

## 15. سعد بن أبي وقاص ؓ — «شيطان الردهة»

A short, strange, memorable report, and ⁨البدایہ⁩ hedges it carefully `[SOURCED]` — ⁨ص۵۰۱⁩:

> "شيطان الردهة كراعي الجَبَل يحتدره (٥) رجل من بجيلة يقال له الأشهب أو ابن الأشهب علامة في قوم ظَلَمةٍ"
> — ⁨البدایہ والنہایہ ج۷ ص۵۰۱⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/30097/3647
> *English:* "The devil of the rock-pool, like the mountain herdsman, whom a man of ⁨بجيلة⁩ called ⁨الأشهب⁩ — or ⁨ابن الأشهب⁩ — will bring down: a sign among a wrongdoing people."
> سمعت سعد بن أبي وقاص يقول: "قتل عليٌّ شيطان الرَّدهة" قال (الحافظ أبو بكر) البيهقي (١٠): يريد واللّه أعلم قتله أصحاب علي بأمره.
> — ⁨البدایہ والنہایہ ج۷ ص۵۰۱⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/30097/3647
> *English:* "I heard ⁨سعد بن أبي وقاص⁩ say: "⁨علي⁩ killed the devil of the rock-pool." ⁨البيهقي⁩ said: he means — and Allah knows best — that ⁨علي⁩’s companions killed him by his order."

⁨البدایہ⁩ records ⁨البخاري⁩'s remark, from ⁨علي بن المديني⁩, that he never heard of the narrator ⁨بكر بن قرواش⁩
except in this hadith — and the edition's editor draws the conclusion: «⁨ولذلك فإسناده ضعيف⁩»
`[SOURCED]` — ⁨ص۵۰۱⁩ and fn (٩). A third version comes through ⁨الهيثم بن عدي⁩, of whom the same footnote
apparatus says plainly «⁨والهيثم بن عدي كذاب⁩» `[SOURCED]` — ⁨ص۵۰۱⁩ fn (١١). **The saying is usable; the
isnad discussion must travel with it.**

## 16. ⁨أبو سعيد الخدري ؓ⁩ — seven routes, and the man the verse came down about

⁨ابن كثير⁩ gives ⁨أبو سعيد ؓ⁩ more routes than anyone but ⁨علي ؓ⁩. The first of them is the report he himself
will not defend: ⁨أبو بكر ؓ⁩ and then ⁨عمر ؓ⁩ sent to kill a man found praying, and each declining
`[SOURCED]` — ⁨ص۵۰۲⁩. The edition's editor rejects it outright in the footnote — the isnad is weak and
«⁨في ألفاظ الحديث نكارة ظاهرة⁩»: how could ⁨أبو بكر⁩ and ⁨عمر⁩ have been unwilling to kill a man the Prophet ﷺ
had ordered killed `[SOURCED]` — ⁨ص۵۰۲⁩ fn (٦). **Recorded, not narrated.**

The third route carries the sentence that fixes the whole chapter in living memory `[SOURCED]` — ⁨ص۵۰۳⁩:

> قال أبو سعيد: فحدَّثني عشرون أو بضع (٥) وعشرون من أصحاب النَّبِيّ ﷺ أن عليًا ولي قتلهم. قال: فرأيت أبا سعيد بعدما كبر ويديه ترتعش ويقول: قتالهم عندي أحل من قتال عدتهم من الترك.
> — ⁨البدایہ والنہایہ ج۷ ص۵۰۳⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/30097/3649
> *English:* "⁨أبو سعيد⁩ said: twenty, or some twenty-odd, of the Companions of the Prophet ﷺ narrated to me that ⁨علي⁩ was the one who undertook the killing of them. He said: and I saw ⁨أبو سعيد⁩ after he had grown old, his hands trembling, saying: fighting them is more lawful in my eyes than fighting the like number of Turks."

The same route preserves the form of oath in which the Prophet ﷺ gave it `[SOURCED]` — ⁨ص۵۰۳⁩:

> "لا والذي نفس أبي القاسم بيده (٣) ليخرجن قوم من أمتي تحقرون أعمالكم عند أعمالهم يقرؤون القرآن لا يجاوز تراقيهم، يمرقون من الإسلام كما يمرق السهمُ من الرمية" قالوا: فهل من علامة يُعرفون بها؟ قال: "فيهم رجل ذو يُدَيَّة (٤) أو ثُدَيَّة مُحَلِّقي رُؤُوسِهم".
> — ⁨البدایہ والنہایہ ج۷ ص۵۰۳⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/30097/3649
> *English:* "No, by Him in whose hand is the soul of ⁨أبو القاسم⁩ — there will come out of my ummah a people beside whose deeds you will think little of your own deeds; they recite the Qurʾān and it does not pass their collarbones; they pass clean out of Islam as the arrow passes out of the game. They said: is there a mark by which they are known? He said: among them is a man with a little hand — or a little breast — and they shave their heads."

The fourth route is the scene at the division of the gold sent from ⁨اليمن⁩ `[SOURCED]` — ⁨ص۵۰۳⁩:

> بعث علي وهو باليمن إلى النبي (٨) ﷺ بذُهَيبةٍ في تربتها فقسمها رسول اللّه ﷺ بين الأقرع بن حابس الحنظلي ثم أحد بني مجاشع وبين عُيَينة بن بدر الفزاري وبين علقمة بن علاثة (٩) أحد بني كلاب، وبين زيد الخير (١٠) الطائي، ثم أحد بني نبهان. قال فغضبت قريش والأنصار. قالوا: يعطي صناديد أهل نجد ويدعنا؟ قال: "إنما أتألفهم".
> — ⁨البدایہ والنہایہ ج۷ ص۵۰۳⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/30097/3649
> *English:* "⁨علي⁩, while he was in ⁨اليمن⁩, sent to the Prophet ﷺ a piece of gold still in its earth, and the Messenger of Allah ﷺ divided it between ⁨الأقرع بن حابس الحنظلي⁩ of ⁨بنو مجاشع⁩, ⁨عيينة بن بدر الفزاري⁩, ⁨علقمة بن علاثة⁩ of ⁨بنو كلاب⁩, and ⁨زيد الخير الطائي⁩ of ⁨بنو نبهان⁩. He said: so ⁨قريش⁩ and the ⁨أنصار⁩ were angry. They said: he gives it to the chiefs of ⁨نجد⁩ and leaves us? He said: "I am only reconciling their hearts."
> قال: فأقبل رجل غائر العينين، ناتئ الجبين، كثُّ اللحية مشرف الوجنتين محلوق الرأس فقال: يا محمد اتق الله فقال: "من يطع اللّه إذا عصيته؟ يأمنني على أهل الأرض ولا تأمنوني"
> — ⁨البدایہ والنہایہ ج۷ ص۵۰۳⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/30097/3649
> *English:* "Then a man came forward — sunken-eyed, prominent in the forehead, thick-bearded, high in the cheekbones, his head shaven — and said: O ⁨محمد⁩, fear Allah. He said: "Who obeys Allah if I disobey Him? He entrusts me over the people of the earth and you do not trust me?"

When the man turned away `[SOURCED]` — ⁨ص۵۰۴⁩:

> "إن من ضئضئ (١) هذا قومًا يقرؤون القرآن لا يجاوز حناجرهم، يمرقون من الإسلام مروق السَّهم من الرميَّة يقتلون أهل الإسلام ويدعون أهل الأوثان، لئن أنا أدركتهم لأقتلنهم قتل عاد".
> — ⁨البدایہ والنہایہ ج۷ ص۵۰۴⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/30097/3650
> *English:* "Out of the stock of this man there will be a people who recite the Qurʾān and it does not pass their throats; they pass clean out of Islam as the arrow passes out of the game; they kill the people of Islam and leave the worshippers of idols. If I live to see them I will kill them as ⁨عاد⁩ were killed."

⁨البدایہ⁩ says ⁨البخاري⁩ narrated it; that the route naming ⁨خالد بن الوليد ؓ⁩ as the one who asked leave to
kill the man does not contradict the route naming ⁨عمر ؓ⁩; and then ⁨ابن كثير⁩ glosses «⁨ضئضئ⁩» in his own
voice — and the gloss is the most quotable thing on the page `[SOURCED]` — ⁨ص۵۰۴⁩:

> وليس المراد بهم أنه يخرج من] صلبه ونسله، لأن الخوارج الذين ذكرنا (٧) لم يكونوا من سلالة هذا، بل ولا أعلم أحدًا منهم من نسله، وإنما أراد من ضئضئ هذا، أي: من شكله وشبهه وصفته فعلًا وقولًا، فاللّه أعلم. [وهذا الشكل وهذه الصفة كثيرة في الناس جدًّا في كل زمان وكل مكان في قرّاء القرآن وغيرهم لمن تأملها، واللّه أعلم].
> — ⁨البدایہ والنہایہ ج۷ ص۵۰۴⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/30097/3650
> *English:* "What is meant by them is not that they come out of his loins and his descendants — for the ⁨خوارج⁩ we have mentioned were not of this man’s progeny; indeed I do not know one of them from his line. He meant only "out of the stock of this one", that is: of his shape and his likeness and his description, in deed and in word — and Allah knows best. [And this shape and this description is very common among people, in every time and every place, among the reciters of the Qurʾān and others, for whoever considers it; and Allah knows best.]"

The bracketed clause is the edition's supplement from the other manuscript, and it is printed as part of
the text. ⁨البدایہ⁩ then names the man `[SOURCED]` — ⁨ص۵۰۴⁩:

> وهذا الرجل [المذكور] هو ذو الخُويصرَة التميمي وسماه بعضهم حرقوصًا، فاللّه أعلم.
> — ⁨البدایہ والنہایہ ج۷ ص۵۰۴⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/30097/3650
> *English:* "And this man is ⁨ذو الخويصرة التميمي⁩; some of them named him ⁨حرقوص⁩ — and Allah knows best."

## 17. The mark, the arrow, and ⁨يزيد الفقير⁩'s question

**The mark** `[SOURCED]` — ⁨ص۵۰۴⁩:

> "يخرجُ أناسٌ من قِبَلِ المشرقِ يقرؤون القرآن لا يجاوز تَرَاقيهم يمرقون من الدين كما يمرقُ السهم من الرَّميَّة، ثم لا يعودون فيه حتى يعود السَّهم على فُوقه" قيل: ما سيماهم؟ قال: "سيماهم التحليق أو التسبيد" (٩)
> — ⁨البدایہ والنہایہ ج۷ ص۵۰۴⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/30097/3650
> *English:* "People will come out from the direction of the east; they recite the Qurʾān and it does not pass their collarbones; they pass clean out of the religion as the arrow passes out of the game, and then they do not return to it until the arrow returns upon its notch. It was said: what is their mark? He said: their mark is the shaven head, or the close-cropped head."

⁨البدایہ⁩ says ⁨البخاري⁩ narrated this one too `[SOURCED]` — ⁨ص۵۰۴⁩.

**The question that a modern room asks, asked in the first century** `[SOURCED]` — ⁨ص۵۰۵⁩:

> قلت لأبي سعيد: إنَّ منَّا رجالًا هم أقرؤنا للقرآن، وأكثرنا صلاة، وأوصلنا للرَّحم، وأكثرنا صومًا، خرجوا علينا بأسيافهم.
> — ⁨البدایہ والنہایہ ج۷ ص۵۰۵⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/30097/3651
> *English:* "I said to ⁨أبو سعيد⁩: among us are men who are the best of us at reciting the Qurʾān, and the most of us in prayer, and the best of us at keeping kinship, and the most of us in fasting — and they have come out against us with their swords."

⁨أبو سعيد ؓ⁩ answered with the hadith and nothing else `[SOURCED]` — ⁨ص۵۰۵⁩. **That is the shape of the
answer the speaker should give from the platform too.**

**The arrow, examined part by part** — the ⁨الزهري⁩ route through ⁨أبو سلمة⁩ `[SOURCED]` — ⁨ص۵۰۵⁩:

> "دعه فإن له أصحابًا يحقر أحدكم صلاته مع صلاتهم، وصيامه مع صيامهم يمرقون من الدين كما يمرق السهم من الرَّمِيَّة فينظر في قُذَذِهِ (٥) فلا يوجد فيه شيء، ثم ينظر في نضيَّتِهِ (٦) فلا يوجد فيه شيء"، ثم ينظر في رضافه (٧) فلا يوجد فيه شيء، ثم ينظر في نصله فلا يوجد فيه شيء، قد سبق الفرث والدم، آيتهم (٨) رجل أسود إحدى يديه [أو قال: إحدى ثدييه] (٩) مثل ثدي المرأة، أو مثل البَضْعَة تَدَرْدَرٌ (١٠)، يخرجون على حين فتر (١١) من الناس"
> — ⁨البدایہ والنہایہ ج۷ ص۵۰۵⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/30097/3651
> *English:* "Leave him. He has companions beside whose prayer one of you will think little of his own prayer and beside whose fasting of his own fasting. They pass clean out of the religion as the arrow passes out of the game: one looks at its flight-feathers and finds nothing on them, then looks at its shaft and finds nothing on it, then looks at its binding and finds nothing on it, then looks at its head and finds nothing on it — it has outstripped the dung and the blood. Their sign is a black man, one of his hands — or he said: one of his breasts — like a woman’s breast, or like a lump of flesh that quivers. They come out at a time of slackness among the people."

And on the same page ⁨البدایہ⁩ reports that the verse ⁨﴿وَمِنْهُمْ مَنْ يَلْمِزُكَ فِي الصَّدَقَاتِ﴾⁩ came
down about that man `[SOURCED]` — ⁨ص۵۰۵⁩. ⁨أبو سعيد ؓ⁩ then testifies to both halves at once `[SOURCED]` —
ص۵۰۶:

> وأشهد أن عليًا حين قتلهم وأنا معه جيء بالرجل على النعت الذي نعت رسول اللّه ﷺ.
> — ⁨البدایہ والنہایہ ج۷ ص۵۰۶⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/30097/3652
> *English:* "And I bear witness that when ⁨علي⁩ killed them, and I was with him, the man was brought in the very description the Messenger of Allah ﷺ had described."

⁨البدایہ⁩ says ⁨البخاري⁩ brought this out, and by a second route, and ⁨مسلم⁩ too `[SOURCED]` — ⁨ص۵۰۶⁩. One more
wording, through ⁨أبو سلمة⁩, is worth keeping for its verb `[SOURCED]` — ⁨ص۵۰۷⁩:

> سمعته يذكر قوما يتعمَّقون (٤) في الدين يحقر أحدكم صلاته عند صلاتهم، وصومه عند صومهم، يمرقون من الدين كما يمرق السهم من الرَّميَّة،
> — ⁨البدایہ والنہایہ ج۷ ص۵۰۷⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/30097/3653
> *English:* "I heard him mention a people who go to excessive depth in the religion; one of you will think little of his prayer beside their prayer and of his fasting beside their fasting; they pass clean out of the religion as the arrow passes out of the game,"

## 18. سلمان ؓ, سهل بن حنيف ؓ, ابن عباس ؓ, ابن عمر ؓ, عبد الله بن عمرو ؓ, أبو ذر ؓ

**⁨سلمان الفارسي ؓ⁩** — the one report here that comes only through ⁨الهيثم بن عدي⁩. In it a man asks ⁨سلمان ؓ⁩
to move his tent nearer so they may hear hadith from him, and ⁨سلمان ؓ⁩ answers with a warning
`[SOURCED]` — ⁨ص۵۰۸⁩:

> قال سَلْمان: قد بلغني عنك معروف. بلغني أنك تخفُّ في سبيل اللّه، وتقاتل العدو، وتخدم أصحاب رسول اللّه ﷺ، فإن أخطأتك واحدة أن تكون من هؤلاء القوم الذين ذكرهم لنا رسول اللّه ﷺ قالوا: فوجد ذلك الرجل قتيلًا في أصحاب النهروان (٢).
> — ⁨البدایہ والنہایہ ج۷ ص۵۰۸⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/30097/3654
> *English:* "⁨سلمان⁩ said: word of good has reached me about you. I hear that you go light in the way of Allah, and fight the enemy, and serve the Companions of the Messenger of Allah ﷺ — if only one thing does not overtake you: that you be one of those people the Messenger of Allah ﷺ described to us. They said: and that man was found slain among the men of ⁨النهروان⁩."

⚠ **The edition's footnote on that page is blunt: «⁨في سنده الهيثم بن عدي، وهو كذاب، ومتروك⁩»**
`[SOURCED]` — ⁨ص۵۰۸⁩ fn (٢). **Tiered CUT below. It is recorded so that nobody finds it again and puts it
on a slide.**

**⁨سهل بن حنيف ؓ⁩** — the shortest and the most disciplined answer in the chapter `[SOURCED]` — ⁨ص۵۰۸⁩:

> قال: أحدثك ما سمعت من النَّبِيِّ ﷺ لا أزيدك عليه شيئًا، سمعت رسول الله ﷺ "يذكر قومًا يخرجون من هاهنا - وأشار بيده نحو العراق - يقرؤون القرآن لا يجاوز حناجرهم، يمرقون من الدين كما يمرق السهم من الرَّميَّة" قال: قلت هل ذكر لهم علامة؟ قال: هذا ما سمعت لا أزيد عليه [شيئًا].
> — ⁨البدایہ والنہایہ ج۷ ص۵۰۸⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/30097/3654
> *English:* "He said: I will narrate to you what I heard from the Prophet ﷺ; I will not add to it a thing for you. I heard the Messenger of Allah ﷺ mention a people who come out from over there — and he pointed with his hand towards ⁨العراق⁩ — "they recite the Qurʾān and it does not pass their throats; they pass clean out of the religion as the arrow passes out of the game." I said: did he mention a mark for them? He said: this is what I heard; I add nothing to it."

⁨البدایہ⁩ states that both ⁨الشيخان⁩ brought it out; and it prints ⁨مسلم⁩'s own wording immediately after, in
which the pointing is «⁨نحو المشرق⁩» and not «⁨نحو العراق⁩» `[SOURCED]` — ⁨ص۵۰۸⁩. ⚠ **The two directions are
in the same book on the same page. Do not print one and suppress the other.**

**⁨ابن عباس ؓ⁩**, through ⁨البزار⁩ and ⁨ابن ماجة⁩, in one line `[SOURCED]` — ⁨ص۵۰۹⁩:

> "يقرأ (٢) القرآن أقوام من أمتي يمرقون من الدين كما يمرق السهم من الرمية".
> — ⁨البدایہ والنہایہ ج۷ ص۵۰۹⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/30097/3655
> *English:* "Peoples of my ummah will recite the Qurʾān and pass clean out of the religion as the arrow passes out of the game."

**⁨ابن عمر ؓ⁩**, with the sentence that reaches past ⁨النهروان⁩ `[SOURCED]` — ⁨ص۵۰۹⁩:

> "يخرج من أمتي قوم يسيؤون الأعمال يقرؤون القرآن لا يجاوز حناجرهم" قال يزيد: لا أعلمه إِلَّا قال: "يحقر أحدكم عمله مع عملهم يقتلون أهل الإسلام، فإذا خرجوا فاقتلوهم فطوبى لمن قتلهم، وطوبى لمن قتلوه، كلما طلع منهم قرن قطعه اللّه، كلما طلع منهم قرن قطعه اللّه"
> — ⁨البدایہ والنہایہ ج۷ ص۵۰۹⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/30097/3655
> *English:* "There will come out of my ummah a people who do evil deeds; they recite the Qurʾān and it does not pass their throats — [⁨يزيد⁩ said: I do not know it except that he said:] one of you will think little of his own deed beside their deed; they kill the people of Islam. So when they come out, kill them; blessed is he who kills them and blessed is he whom they kill. Every time a horn of them appears Allah cuts it off, every time a horn of them appears Allah cuts it off."

⁨البدایہ⁩ notes that ⁨أحمد⁩ alone has that route, and then that ⁨ابن عمر ؓ⁩'s other, firmly established report
is in ⁨البخاري⁩ and ⁨مسلم⁩: «⁨الفتنة من هاهنا من حيث يطلع قرن الشيطان⁩» — with his hand towards the east
`[SOURCED]` — ⁨ص۵۰۹⁩.

**⁨عبد الله بن عمرو ؓ⁩**, narrated in a scene dated by the narrator to the arrival of ⁨يزيد⁩'s ⁨بيعة⁩
`[SOURCED]` — ⁨ص۵۱۰⁩:

> "سيخرج ناس من أمتي [من] قبل المشرق يقرؤون القرآن لا يجاوز تراقيهم، كلَّما خرج منهم قرن قُطِع حتى عدها زيادة على عَشر مرَّات، كُلَّما خرج منهم قرن قُطع حتى يخرج الدجال في بقيَّتهم]
> — ⁨البدایہ والنہایہ ج۷ ص۵۱۰⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/30097/3656
> *English:* "People of my ummah will come out from the direction of the east; they recite the Qurʾān and it does not pass their collarbones; every time a horn of them comes out it is cut off — until he counted it more than ten times — every time a horn of them comes out it is cut off, until the ⁨دجال⁩ comes out among the remnant of them."

⚠ The edition's footnote marks that isnad weak, with witnesses for part of it `[SOURCED]` — ⁨ص۵۱۰⁩ fn (١).

**⁨أبو ذر ؓ⁩** — ⁨مسلم⁩'s wording, with a second Companion volunteering corroboration on the spot
`[SOURCED]` — ⁨ص۵۱۰⁩:

> "إن بعدي من أمتي - أو سيكون بعدي من أمتي - قوم يقرؤون القرآن لا يجاوز حلاقيمهم يخرجون من الدين كما يخرج السهم من الرَّميَّة لا يعودون فيه [هم] شر الخلق والخليقة".
> — ⁨البدایہ والنہایہ ج۷ ص۵۱۰⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/30097/3656
> *English:* "After me there will be of my ummah — or: there will come after me of my ummah — a people who recite the Qurʾān and it does not pass their gullets; they go out of the religion as the arrow goes out of the game and do not return to it. They are the worst of creation and of created things."
> قال ابن الصامت: فلقيت رافع بن عمرو الغفاري أخا الحَكم (٧) الغفاري. قلت: ما حديث سمعته من أبي ذرٍّ كذا وكذا؟ فقال: وأنا سمعته من رسول الله ﷺ.
> — ⁨البدایہ والنہایہ ج۷ ص۵۱۰⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/30097/3656
> *English:* "⁨ابن الصامت⁩ said: I met ⁨رافع بن عمرو الغفاري⁩, the brother of ⁨الحكم الغفاري⁩, and said: what is this hadith I heard from ⁨أبو ذر⁩, thus and thus? He said: I too heard it from the Messenger of Allah ﷺ."

⁨البدایہ⁩ adds «⁨لم يروه البخاري⁩» — ⁨البخاري⁩ did not narrate this one `[SOURCED]` — ⁨ص۵۱۰⁩. **That single
clause is worth keeping: the book is as careful to say where a report is *not* as where it is.**

## 19. ⁨أم المؤمنين عائشة ؓ⁩ — and the ten witnesses from every ⁨سُبع⁩

Her report first `[SOURCED]` — ⁨ص۵۱۱⁩:

> ذكر رسول الله ﷺ الخوارج فقال: "شِرارُ أمتي يقتلهم خيار أمتي" (٤).
> — ⁨البدایہ والنہایہ ج۷ ص۵۱۱⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/30097/3657
> *English:* "The Messenger of Allah ﷺ mentioned the ⁨خوارج⁩ and said: "the worst of my ummah, whom the best of my ummah will kill."

⚠ **Shamela typo flag: the word before that sentence is printed «⁨فالت⁩» where the sense is «⁨قالت⁩».** Do
not project the run-in line; quote from «⁨ذكر رسول الله ﷺ⁩» onward, and check the page image first.

⁨ابن كثير⁩ records ⁨البزار⁩'s remark that the second isnad is a lone one and that ⁨سليمان بن قرم⁩ was spoken
of, and then rules `[SOURCED]` — ⁨ص۵۱۱⁩:

> وسليمان بن قرم قد تكلَّموا فيه لكن الإسناد الأول يشهد لهذا كما أن هذا يشهد للأول (٧) فهما متعاضدان، وهو غريب من حديث أم المؤمنين
> — ⁨البدایہ والنہایہ ج۷ ص۵۱۱⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/30097/3657
> *English:* "⁨سليمان بن قرم⁩ has been spoken of, but the first isnad bears witness for this one just as this one bears witness for the first — so the two support one another; and it is ⁨غريب⁩ as a hadith of ⁨أم المؤمنين⁩."

Then the scene that is the most useful thing in the entire strand. ⁨عائشة ؓ⁩ does not take the matter of
⁨ذو الثدية⁩ on hearsay — she sends ⁨مسروق⁩ back to ⁨الكوفة⁩ to collect signed testimony `[SOURCED]` — ⁨ص۵۱۲⁩:

> قالت عائشة: عندك علم عن ذي الثدية الذي أصابه علي في الحرورية؟ قلت: لا. قالت: فاكتب لي بشهادة من شهدهم، فرجعت إلى الكوفة وبها يومئذ أسباع فكتبت شهادة عشرة من كل سُبع، ثم أتيتها بشهادتهم فقرأتها عليها، قالت: أكل هؤلاء عاينوه؛ قلت: لقد سألتهم فأخبروني بأن كلهم قد عاينه فقالت: لعن الله فلانًا فإنه كتب إلي أنه أصابهم بنيل (٣) مصر ثم أرخت (٤) عينيها فبكت فلما سكنت عبرتها قالت: رحم اللّه عليًّا لقد كان على حق، وما كان بيني وبينه إِلَّا كما يكون بين المرأة وأحمائها.
> — ⁨البدایہ والنہایہ ج۷ ص۵۱۲⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/30097/3658
> *English:* "⁨عائشة⁩ said: have you knowledge of ⁨ذو الثدية⁩, whom ⁨علي⁩ struck down among the ⁨حرورية⁩? I said: no. She said: then write down for me the testimony of those who witnessed them. So I returned to ⁨الكوفة⁩ — and in those days it was in seven divisions — and I wrote the testimony of ten men from each division, and then I brought her their testimony and read it to her. She said: did all these see him with their own eyes? I said: I asked them, and they told me that every one of them had seen him. She said: may Allah curse so-and-so, for he wrote to me that he struck them down at the Nile of ⁨مصر⁩. Then she lowered her eyes and wept; and when her tears had stilled she said: may Allah have mercy on ⁨علي⁩ — he was upon the truth; and there was nothing between me and him but what is between a woman and her husband’s kin."

**Nothing in this strand should be cut before that paragraph is.** It is ⁨أم المؤمنين ؓ⁩, in her own words,
doing three things the course wants done: refusing a report until it is verified, verifying it by
counted eyewitnesses, and closing the matter between herself and ⁨علي ؓ⁩. The speaker adjudicates nothing;
he reads her sentence and stops.

⁨ابن كثير⁩ states his purpose for the chapter on the same page `[SOURCED]` — ⁨ص۵۱۲⁩:

> أوردنا هذه الطرق كلّها ليعلم الواقف عليها أن ذلك حق وصدق وهو من أكبر دلالات النبوة، كما ذكره غير واحد من الأئمة فيها، واللّه تعالى أعلم.
> — ⁨البدایہ والنہایہ ج۷ ص۵۱۲⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/30097/3658
> *English:* "…we brought all these routes so that whoever comes upon them may know that this is truth and veracity, and that it is among the greatest of the proofs of prophethood, as more than one of the imams has said about it; and Allah most high knows best."

⚠ **Shamela typo flag: the two characters immediately before «⁨أنما أوردنا⁩» are printed «⁨د ا⁩» where the
text requires «⁨وإنما⁩».** The quotation above deliberately starts after the damage.

The same page carries، from the same isnad، the remark that ⁨عائشة ؓ⁩ had **found the ⁨ذو الثدية⁩ report
strange** when she first heard it, and afterwards «⁨فتيقنته من طرق متعددة⁩» — she became certain of it by
several routes `[SOURCED]` — ⁨ص۵۱۲⁩.

## 20. In praise of ⁨علي ؓ⁩ — ⁨خاصف النعل⁩ — and the report ⁨ابن كثير⁩ refuses

The chapter's last movement is a short section headed «⁨حديث آخر في مدح علي ﵁ على قتاله الخوارج⁩». The
sound one first `[SOURCED]` — ⁨ص۵۱۳⁩:

> فقال: "إن منكم من يقاتل على تأويل القرآن كما قاتلت على تنزيله" فاستشرفنا لها وفينا (٥) أبو بكر، وعمر فقال: "لا، ولكنه خاصفُ النعل"
> — ⁨البدایہ والنہایہ ج۷ ص۵۱۳⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/30097/3659
> *English:* "He said: "Among you is one who will fight over the interpretation of the Qurʾān as I fought over its sending down." So we craned for it — and ⁨أبو بكر⁩ and ⁨عمر⁩ were among us — and he said: "No; but it is the mender of the sandal."

⁨ابن كثير⁩ gives it from ⁨مسند أحمد⁩ by two routes; the edition's editor marks it ⁨صحيح⁩ `[SOURCED]` — ⁨ص۵۱۳⁩
and fn (٢). **This is a praise of ⁨علي ؓ⁩ for this fight, from the Prophet ﷺ, and it is safe.**

Then the famous «⁨الناكثين والقاسطين والمارقين⁩» report — and ⁨ابن كثير⁩ will not have it `[SOURCED]` —
ص۵۱۳:

> فإنه حديث غريبٌ ومنكرٌ، على أنه قد روي من طرقٍ عن علي وعن غيره، ولا تخلو واحدة منها عن ضعف.
> — ⁨البدایہ والنہایہ ج۷ ص۵۱۳⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/30097/3659
> *English:* "…for it is a hadith ⁨غريب⁩ and ⁨منكر⁩ — although it has been narrated by several routes from ⁨علي⁩ and from others, not one of them is free of weakness."

He gives the received gloss of the three words anyway `[SOURCED]` — ⁨ص۵۱۳⁩:

> والمراد بالناكثين: يعني: أهل الجمل، وبالقاسطين: أهل الشام [والقاسط هو الجائر الظالم] وأما المارقون: فالخوارج لأنهم مرقوا من الدين.
> — ⁨البدایہ والنہایہ ج۷ ص۵۱۳⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/30097/3659
> *English:* "What is meant by ⁨الناكثون⁩ is the people of ⁨الجمل⁩; by ⁨القاسطون⁩, the people of ⁨الشام⁩ — and ⁨القاسط⁩ is the one who deviates and wrongs; and as for ⁨المارقون⁩, they are the ⁨خوارج⁩, because they passed clean out of the religion."

⚠ **That gloss names Companions on two sides of the ⁨مشاجرات⁩. It is recorded here because it is in the
book, and it is listed under §Not for the hall below. The speaker does not narrate it.**

Three pages later a longer version of the same, with much more in it, is disposed of in one sentence
`[SOURCED]` — ⁨ص۵۱۶⁩:

> هذا السياق الظاهر أنه موضوع وآفته من جهة المعلى بن عبد الرحمن فإنه متروك الحديث، والله أعلم (٧).
> — ⁨البدایہ والنہایہ ج۷ ص۵۱۶⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/30097/3662
> *English:* "This account is, to all appearance, fabricated, and its affliction comes from ⁨المعلى بن عبد الرحمن⁩ — for he is ⁨متروك الحديث⁩; and Allah knows best."

**Four gradings in eleven pages: ⁨متواترة⁩ (⁨ص۴۹۷⁩) · ⁨غرابة شديدة⁩ (⁨ص۴۹۷⁩) · ⁨غريب ومنكر⁩ (⁨ص۵۱۳⁩) · ⁨موضوع⁩
(⁨ص۵۱۶⁩).** That range, in one author, on one subject, is the best possible answer to "so he just collected
everything."

## What could NOT be established

1. **Whether the ⁨خوارج⁩ are, in these pages, judged to be outside Islam.** They are not. The chapter opens
   with ⁨علي ؓ⁩'s answer that they are neither ⁨مشركون⁩ nor ⁨منافقون⁩ but «⁨إخواننا بغوا علينا⁩» (⁨ص۴۸۹⁩), and the
   reports call them «⁨شر الخلق والخليقة⁩» and «⁨يمرقون من الدين⁩» without ⁨البدایہ⁩ drawing a takfīr
   conclusion on these pages. **No sentence on these thirty-three pages settles the question either way,
   and the note does not supply one.**
2. **The number of the ⁨خوارج⁩ at ⁨النهروان⁩.** Not on these pages — the figures (8,000 / 12,000 / 16,000)
   are on ⁨ص۴۷۴⁩–⁨ص۴۷۶⁩, outside this strand's range. Take them from the strand that owns them.
3. **How many were killed at ⁨النهروان⁩.** The ⁨مسلم⁩ route says only two of ⁨علي ؓ⁩'s army were lost (⁨ص۴۹۰⁩);
   no total for the other side appears in this chapter.
4. **Whether ⁨ذو الثدية⁩ is the same man as ⁨ذو الخويصرة التميمي⁩.** ⁨البدایہ⁩ names ⁨ذو الخويصرة⁩ as the man at
   the division of the gold (⁨ص۵۰۴⁩) and ⁨نافع ذو الثدية⁩ as the maimed man found among the slain (⁨ص۴۹۵⁩),
   and **nowhere on these pages identifies the two with each other.** Do not merge them.
5. **The date of the ⁨الجعرانة⁩ incident.** The reports place it at the division of the ⁨هوازن⁩ spoils at
   ⁨الجعرانة⁩ (⁨ص۵۰۰⁩) and at a distribution of gold sent from ⁨اليمن⁩ (⁨ص۵۰۳⁩) — ⁨البدایہ⁩ does not date either
   here, and does not say whether they are one occasion or two.
6. **Whether ⁨حرقوص⁩ is the right name for ⁨ذو الخويصرة⁩.** ⁨البدایہ⁩ says only «⁨وسماه بعضهم حرقوصًا، فاللّه⁩
   ⁨أعلم⁩» (⁨ص۵۰۴⁩) — "some named him", and then leaves it. Anything firmer is `[STANDARD]` *(to verify)*.
7. **The exact ⁨عدد⁩ of Companions who narrate this.** ⁨ابن كثير⁩'s roll on ⁨ص۴۹۸⁩ names twelve; the chapter
   itself then adds ⁨سلمان ؓ⁩ (⁨ص۵۰۷⁩–⁨ص۵۰۸⁩) and two unnamed men of ⁨الحجاز⁩ (⁨ص۵۱۲⁩); the editor notes the
   discrepancy. **Give the roll, not a number.**
8. **What ⁨سعد بن أبي وقاص ؓ⁩ meant by «⁨شيطان الردهة⁩».** ⁨البدایہ⁩ reports ⁨البيهقي⁩'s gloss that ⁨سعد ؓ⁩ meant
   ⁨علي ؓ⁩'s men killed him by his order (⁨ص۵۰۱⁩), and gives no explanation of the phrase itself.
9. **Any Urdu rendering of any of this.** None is given anywhere in this note, by design
   (`DECISIONS.md` #19). Daniyal renders at the lectern.
10. **⁨الکامل⁩, ⁨سیر⁩ and ⁨الإعلان⁩ on this chapter.** Not opened in this pass — this strand was assigned the
    ⁨البدایہ⁩ chapter only. Whether ⁨ابن الاثیر⁩ or ⁨الذهبي⁩ arrange the same reports differently is
    **unexamined**, and any claim that "the sources agree" would be unfounded.

## Not for the hall

Real material on these pages that must not be narrated from the platform.

4. **The ⁨سلمان الفارسي ؓ⁩ report** (⁨ص۵۰۸⁩) and the **two men of ⁨الحجاز⁩** (⁨ص۵۱۲⁩) — both from ⁨الهيثم بن عدي⁩,

### E-KR1 · What ⁨علي ؓ⁩ answered when asked what they were
**Tier:** CORE · **When:** after ⁨النهروان⁩, 38 AH `[SOURCED]` · **Map:** ⁨النهروان⁩ lights, east of the Tigris

**What happened:** As soon as the fighting was over men came to ⁨علي ؓ⁩ with the question the whole room
will be carrying. Were these people idolaters? He said no: it was idolatry they had fled from. Were they
hypocrites, then? He said no: the hypocrites hardly remember Allah at all, and these men did nothing but
remember Him. Then what were they? He gave them a single sentence and stopped there. ⁨البدایہ⁩ places this
exchange at the head of the chapter and says openly that it takes it from ⁨ابن جرير⁩ and others.

**The statement:**
> قيل: فما هم يا أمير المؤمنين؟ قال: إخواننا بَغَوا علينا فقاتلناهم ببغيهم علينا.
> — ⁨البدایہ والنہایہ ج۷ ص۴۸۹⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/30097/3635
> *English:* "It was said: then what are they, O commander of the believers? He said: our brothers who transgressed against us, so we fought them for their transgression against us."

**⁨عبرت⁩:** The man who had just fought them refused both of the easy names for them, and the sentence he
chose cost him more than either.

### E-KR2 · A chapter of reports, and a count of twelve
**Tier:** CORE · **When:** ⁨البدایہ⁩'s own arrangement `[SOURCED]` · **Map:** No map.

**What happened:** ⁨ابن كثير⁩ stops the narrative at this point and opens a separate chapter for the
Prophetic reports about these people. He begins with ⁨علي ؓ⁩, and before he gives a single report he counts
his evidence: twelve different men narrate this from ⁨علي ؓ⁩, and he names all twelve. He then says what
that number means to him, and he says it before he has proved anything, which is his way of telling the
reader where the chapter is going.

**The statement:**
> فهذه اثنتا عشرة طريقًا إليه سرَاها بأسانيدها وألفاظها، ومثل هذا يبلغ حد التواتر (٤).
> — ⁨البدایہ والنہایہ ج۷ ص۴۸۹⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/30097/3635
> *English:* "These are twelve routes to him, which I shall relate with their isnads and their wordings; and the like of this reaches the degree of ⁨تواتر⁩."

**⁨عبرت⁩:** Counting the routes before telling the story is not a scholar's fussiness; it is what makes the
story usable a thousand years later.

### E-KR54 · ⁨عائشة ؓ⁩ will not take it on report — she sends for witnesses

**What happened:** ⁨عائشة ؓ⁩ asked ⁨مسروق⁩ whether he had any knowledge about ⁨ذو الثدية⁩, the man ⁨علي ؓ⁩ had
