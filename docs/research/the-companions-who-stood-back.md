# The Companions who would not fight — the ⁨اعتزال⁩ of 36–40 AH

**Question this note answers:** When the fighting began in 36 AH, a number of the Prophet's ﷺ
Companions refused to take a side. This note asks who they were, exactly what each is reported to
have said when he was asked to come out, which hadith he cited if a hadith is cited, what he did
instead of fighting, and what our four books say about the position of withholding itself. It also
asks the arithmetic question a listener always asks: **how many Companions actually fought on each
side at ⁨الجمل⁩ and ⁨صفين⁩?** — and records the two very different answers our pages give. Finally it
collects, verbatim, every place where **⁨الذهبي رحمہ اللہ⁩ or ⁨ابن کثیر رحمہ اللہ⁩ themselves refuse a
report, grade it, or call it a forgery**, because that is the material that lets a speaker narrate
this window without adjudicating anything.

## 1. The four names — ⁨وكيع⁩'s list

The shortest statement of the whole strand is a single line in the ⁨ترجمة⁩ of ⁨أسامة بن زيد ؓ⁩. It is
worth noting where it stands: it sits between a report from ⁨ابن سعد⁩ about the Prophet ﷺ waiting for
⁨أسامة ؓ⁩ at ⁨عرفة⁩ and ⁨الذهبي⁩'s own «⁨قلت⁩» about him, and it comes in as the plain historical summary
(سیر جـ۲ ص۵۰۰):

> قَالَ وَكِيْعٌ: سَلِمَ مِنَ الفِتْنَةِ مِنَ المَعْرُوْفِيْنَ: سَعْدٌ، وَابْنُ عُمَرَ، وَأُسَامَةُ بنُ زَيْدٍ، وَمُحَمَّدُ بنُ مَسْلَمَةَ.
> — ⁨سیر أعلام النبلاء جـ۲ ص۵۰۰⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/10906/2480
> *English:* "Wakīʿ said: those of the well-known men who came through the fitna safe were Saʿd, Ibn ʿUmar, Usāma ibn Zayd and Muḥammad ibn Maslama."

Four things to hold on to about this sentence `[SOURCED]`:

- The word is **⁨سَلِمَ⁩** — "came through safe" — not "was right". ⁨وكيع⁩ is not issuing a ruling on
  those who did fight; he is saying these four did not have to carry the consequence of it.
- It is expressly limited: **⁨مِنَ المَعْرُوْفِيْنَ⁩**, "of the well-known ones". It is a list of
  famous names, not a census. §2 gives the census, and the census is far larger.
- **⁨أبو موسى الأشعري ؓ⁩ and ⁨أبو بكرة ؓ⁩ are not in it.** That does not mean they fought; it means
  ⁨وكيع⁩ named four.
- Three of the four have their own ⁨ترجمة⁩ in ⁨سیر⁩ with the reason stated in their own words. Those
  are §§3, 6 and 7 below. The fourth — ⁨ابن عمر ؓ⁩ — gives the flattest sentence of all of them (§5).

## 2. How many Companions actually fought — the two answers, which differ

This is the question a room will ask, and our pages give two answers that are not the same size.
Both are on one page of ⁨البدایہ⁩, printed a paragraph apart. **Do not merge them.**

### 2.1 The census: not a hundred, "rather they did not reach thirty"

⁨ابن کثیر⁩ opens the section on who was killed at ⁨الجمل⁩ with this (⁨البدایہ جـ۷ ص۴۲۷⁩):

> وقال الإمام أحمد: حدَّثنا إسماعيل، حدَّثنا أيوب، عن محمد بن سيرين قال: هاجت الفتنة وأصحاب رسول الله ﷺ عشرات ألوف فلم يحضرها منهم مئة، بل لم يبلغوا ثلاثين.
> — ⁨البدایہ والنہایہ جـ۷ ص۴۲۷⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/30097/3573
> *English:* "Imām Aḥmad said: Ismāʿīl related to us, Ayyūb related to us, from Muḥammad ibn Sīrīn, who said: the fitna broke out while the Companions of the Messenger of Allāh ﷺ were tens of thousands, and not a hundred of them were present at it — rather they did not reach thirty."

`[SOURCED]` — ⁨البدایہ جـ۷ ص۴۲۷⁩, reported there **from ⁨الإمام أحمد⁩**, with ⁨أيوب⁩ and ⁨ابن سيرين⁩ in the
chain. Two lines earlier on the same page ⁨ابن کثیر⁩ has already said it in his own voice, of the two
armies at ⁨الجمل⁩:

> ولم يكن في الفريقين من الصحابة إلا القليل.
> — ⁨البدایہ والنہایہ جـ۷ ص۴۲۷⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/30097/3573
> *English:* "There were only a few of the Companions in either of the two parties."

### 2.2 The harder version — ⁨الشعبي⁩'s four, and ⁨ابن کثیر⁩ contradicting him in the next line

Immediately after, on the same page, comes a much sharper claim and then ⁨ابن کثیر⁩'s open refusal of
it. This is one of the most instructive passages in the whole window, because a reader watches a
historian correct his own source in public:

> وقال الإمام أحمد أيضاً: حدَّثنا إسماعيل -هو ابن عليّة- حدَّثنا منصور بن عبد الرحمن قال: قال الشعبي: لم يشهد الجمل من أصحاب النبي ﷺ غير علي وعمّار وطلحة والزبير؛ فإن جاؤوا بخامس فأنا كذاب. قلت: قد حضرتها عائشة وابن الزبير والحسن والحسين ومحمد بن أبي بكر وسهل بن حنيف وآخرون.
> — ⁨البدایہ والنہایہ جـ۷ ص۴۲۷⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/30097/3573
> *English:* "Imām Aḥmad also said: Ismāʿīl — that is Ibn ʿUlayya — related to us, Manṣūr ibn ʿAbd al-Raḥmān related to us, who said: al-Shaʿbī said: none of the Companions of the Prophet ﷺ was present at al-Jamal except ʿAlī, ʿAmmār, Ṭalḥa and al-Zubayr; and if they produce a fifth, then I am a liar. I say: ʿĀʾisha was present at it, and Ibn al-Zubayr, and al-Ḥasan and al-Ḥusayn, and Muḥammad ibn Abī Bakr, and Sahl ibn Ḥunayf, and others."

The «⁨قلت⁩» is ⁨ابن کثیر⁩'s own first person. He then offers a way out for ⁨الشعبي⁩, and marks it as a
guess rather than a fact:

> قلت: قد يكون الشعبي أراد أنه لم يحضرها من المهاجرين غير من ذكر والله أعلم.
> — ⁨البدایہ والنہایہ جـ۷ ص۴۲۷⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/30097/3573
> *English:* "I say: it may be that al-Shaʿbī meant that none of the Muhājirūn was present at it except those he named — and God knows best."

`[SOURCED]` — ⁨البدایہ جـ۷ ص۴۲۷⁩. **The finding is the disagreement itself.** A slide may say: one
authority said four; the historian who copied him named six more and said the man probably meant
something narrower. It may not say "four Companions fought at the Camel" as a fact.

### 2.3 The dead

⁨البدایہ⁩ gives the same figure twice in the same account (⁨جـ۷ ص۴۲۵⁩, then ⁨ص۴۲۷⁩):

> وكان مجموع من قتل يوم الجمل من الفريقين عشرة آلاف: خمسة من هؤلاء، وخمسة من هؤلاء، ﵏ ورضي عن الصحابة منهم (١).
> — ⁨البدایہ والنہایہ جـ۷ ص۴۲۵⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/30097/3571
> *English:* "The total of those killed on the day of al-Jamal from the two parties was ten thousand: five from these and five from those — may God have mercy on them, and be pleased with the Companions among them."

(The (١) is the printed edition's own footnote marker; its note records that one manuscript reads
⁨أجمعين⁩ in place of that word.) On ⁨ص۴۲۷⁩ he repeats it and adds that the wounded were beyond counting:

> وقد قدَّمنا أن عدَّة القَتْلى نحو من عشرة آلافٍ، وأمّا الجَرْحى فلا يُحْصون كثرةً
> — ⁨البدایہ والنہایہ جـ۷ ص۴۲۷⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/30097/3573
> *English:* "We have already given that the number of the slain was about ten thousand; and as for the wounded, they cannot be counted for their number."

`[SOURCED]` — ⁨البدایہ جـ۷ ص۴۲۵⁩, ⁨ص۴۲۷⁩.

⚠ **For ⁨صفين⁩ our pages give no figure at all, on either side.** See "What could NOT be established".

## 3. ⁨سعد بن أبي وقاص ؓ⁩ — "a sword with two eyes and a tongue"

⁨سعد ؓ⁩ is the heaviest name on the list: one of the ⁨عشرة⁩, one of the six of the ⁨شورى⁩, the commander
at ⁨القادسية⁩, and a man ⁨عمر ؓ⁩ went out of his way to say he had not dismissed for weakness or
treachery. ⁨سیر⁩ gives his refusal in two separate wordings, from two separate chains, on the last two
pages of his ⁨ترجمة⁩.

### 3.1 The first wording — ⁨ص۱۱۸⁩

> ابْنُ عُلَيَّةَ: حَدَّثَنَا أَيُّوْبُ، عَنْ مُحَمَّدٍ، قَالَ:
> نُبِّئْتُ أَنَّ سَعْداً قَالَ: مَا أَزْعُمُ أَنِّي بِقَمِيْصِي هَذَا أَحَقُّ مِنِّي بِالخِلاَفَةِ، جَاهَدْتُ وَأَنَا أَعْرَفُ بِالجِهَادِ، وَلاَ أَبْخَعُ نَفْسِي إِنْ كَانَ رَجُلاً خَيْراً مِنِّي، لاَ أُقَاتِلُ حَتَّى يَأْتُوْنِي بِسَيْفٍ لَهُ عَيْنَانِ وَلِسَانٌ، فَيَقُوْلَ: هَذَا مُؤْمِنٌ، وَهَذَا كَافِرٌ (٣) .
> — ⁨سیر أعلام النبلاء جـ۱ ص۱۱۸⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/10906/1544
> *English:* "Ibn ʿUlayya: Ayyūb related to us, from Muḥammad [ibn Sīrīn], who said: I was informed that Saʿd said: I do not claim that I have a better right to this shirt of mine than I have to the caliphate. I waged jihād, and I know jihād better. Nor would I destroy myself if there were a man better than I. I will not fight until they bring me a sword that has two eyes and a tongue, so that it says: this is a believer, and this is an unbeliever."

`[SOURCED]` — ⁨سیر جـ۱ ص۱۱۸⁩. The (٣) is the printed edition's footnote marker; that footnote says
**⁨رجاله ثقات⁩** and traces the report to ⁨ابن سعد⁩, ⁨أبو نعيم⁩'s ⁨الحلية⁩ and ⁨الطبراني⁩'s ⁨الكبير⁩. **This is
the best-attested wording and the one to put on a slide.**

### 3.2 The second wording — to his son ⁨عامر⁩, with the hadith attached — ⁨ص۱۱۹⁩

The very next page carries the same refusal with a different image and, this time, with ⁨سعد ؓ⁩'s
authority named:

> أَنَّهُ جَاءهُ ابْنُهُ عَامِرٌ، فَقَالَ: أَيْ بُنَيَّ! أَفِي الفِتْنَةِ تَأْمُرُنِي أَنْ أَكُوْنَ رَأْساً؟ لاَ وَاللهِ، حَتَّى أُعْطَى سَيْفاً، إِنْ ضَرَبْتُ بِهِ مُسْلِماً نَبَا عَنْهُ، وَإِنْ ضَرَبْتُ كَافِراً قَتَلَهُ.
> — ⁨سیر أعلام النبلاء جـ۱ ص۱۱۹⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/10906/1545
> *English:* "His son ʿĀmir came to him, and he said: My son! Is it in the fitna that you order me to be a head? No, by God — not until I am given a sword which, if I strike a Muslim with it, turns aside from him, and if I strike an unbeliever, kills him."

And in the same breath, the hadith:

> سَمِعْتُ رَسُوْلَ اللهِ -صَلَّى اللَّهُ عَلَيْهِ وَسَلَّمَ- يَقُوْلُ: (إِنَّ اللهَ يُحِبُّ الغَنِيَّ، الخَفِيَّ، التَّقِيَّ (١)) .
> — ⁨سیر أعلام النبلاء جـ۱ ص۱۱۹⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/10906/1545
> *English:* "I heard the Messenger of Allāh ﷺ say: God loves the self-sufficient, the obscure, the God-fearing."

`[SOURCED]` — ⁨سیر جـ۱ ص۱۱۹⁩; the edition's footnote (١) calls the chain ⁨حسن⁩ and places it in ⁨المسند⁩
and ⁨الحلية⁩. **The round brackets around the Prophet's ﷺ words are the printed edition's own
quotation marks. Reproduce them or drop them, but do not turn them into anything else.**

⚠ **The same hadith appears earlier in the same ⁨ترجمة⁩ with the three words in a different order.**
At ⁨جـ۱ ص۱۰۲⁩ it reads, with ⁨العبد⁩ added:

> العَبْدَ التَّقِيَّ، الغَّنِيَّ، الخَفِيَّ
> — ⁨سیر أعلام النبلاء جـ۱ ص۱۰۲⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/10906/1528
> *English:* "the servant who is God-fearing, self-sufficient, obscure."

and at ⁨جـ۱ ص۱۱۹⁩ it reads:

> الغَنِيَّ، الخَفِيَّ، التَّقِيَّ
> — ⁨سیر أعلام النبلاء جـ۱ ص۱۱۹⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/10906/1545
> *English:* "the self-sufficient, the obscure, the God-fearing."

Both are in the same book, seventeen pages apart.
**Quote one page or the other; never a blend.** (The ⁨ص۱۰۲⁩ printing carries a ⁨شدّة⁩ on the second of
those three words which is a printing artefact, not a word; that page in particular must be read off
a page image before it is projected.)

### 3.3 The scene that sets it — ⁨سعد ؓ⁩ among his sheep, ⁨جـ۱ ص۱۰۲⁩

This is the tellable version, and the one that puts the hadith in a setting:

> أَنَّ أَبَاهُ سَعْداً كَانَ فِي غَنَمٍ لَهُ، فَجَاءَ ابْنُهُ عُمَرُ، فَلَمَّا رَآهُ قَالَ: أَعُوْذُ بِاللهِ مِنْ شَرِّ هَذَا الرَّاكِبِ.
> — ⁨سیر أعلام النبلاء جـ۱ ص۱۰۲⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/10906/1528
> *English:* "His father Saʿd was among some sheep of his when his son ʿUmar came. When he saw him he said: I take refuge with God from the evil of this rider."

> فَلَمَّا انْتَهَى إِلَيْهِ، قَالَ: يَا أَبَةِ أَرَضِيْتَ أَنْ تَكُوْنَ أَعْرَابِياً فِي غَنَمِكَ، وَالنَّاسُ يَتَنَازَعُوْنَ فِي الملْكِ بِالمَدِيْنَةِ.
> — ⁨سیر أعلام النبلاء جـ۱ ص۱۰۲⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/10906/1528
> *English:* "When he reached him he said: Father, have you been content to be a bedouin among your sheep, while the people are contending over rule at Medina?"

> فَضَرَبَ صَدْرَ عُمَرَ وَقَالَ: اسْكُتْ، فَإِنِّي سَمِعْتُ رَسُوْلَ اللهِ -صَلَّى اللَّهُ عَلَيْهِ وَسَلَّمَ- يَقُوْلُ (٢) : (إِنَّ اللهَ -عَزَّ وَجَلَّ- يُحِبُّ العَبْدَ التَّقِيَّ، الغَّنِيَّ، الخَفِيَّ (٣)) .
> — ⁨سیر أعلام النبلاء جـ۱ ص۱۰۲⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/10906/1528
> *English:* "He struck ʿUmar's chest and said: Be quiet — for I heard the Messenger of Allāh ﷺ say: God, mighty and majestic, loves the servant who is God-fearing, self-sufficient, obscure."

`[SOURCED]` — ⁨سیر جـ۱ ص۱۰۲⁩; the edition's footnote traces the hadith to ⁨المسند⁩ and to **⁨مسلم⁩** in
⁨كتاب الزهد⁩, and to ⁨أبو نعيم⁩. The footnote on ⁨ص۱۱۹⁩ also glosses the two hard words: **⁨الغنى⁩** here is
⁨غنى النفس⁩, self-sufficiency of soul, and **⁨الخفي⁩** is the obscure man withdrawn to worship, who does
not seek fame and does not put himself before people for it. `[SOURCED]` — ⁨سیر جـ۱ ص۱۱۹⁩, ⁨الهامش⁩.

## 4. ⁨سعد ؓ⁩ at ⁨أذرح⁩, at the arbitration, and defending the men who were fighting

Three small items from the same ⁨ترجمة⁩ which together stop a room from thinking that withholding
meant absence, or indifference.

### 4.1 He was there

> ابْنُ عُيَيْنَةَ: عَنْ عَمْرٍو قَالَ: شَهِدَ سَعْدٌ وَابْنُ عُمَرَ الحَكَمَيْنِ.
> — ⁨سیر أعلام النبلاء جـ۱ ص۹۶⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/10906/1522
> *English:* "Ibn ʿUyayna, from ʿAmr, who said: Saʿd and Ibn ʿUmar were present at the two arbitrators."

`[SOURCED]` — ⁨سیر جـ۱ ص۹۶⁩. **The page says nothing about what they did there.** Two of ⁨وكيع⁩'s four
attended the arbitration; that is the whole of what this line licenses.

The same ⁨ترجمة⁩ independently places ⁨سعد ؓ⁩ on that journey. A report about breaking the fast when
travelling opens:

> خَرَجْتُ مَعَ أَبِي، وَسَعْدٍ، وَعَبْدِ الرَّحْمَنِ بنِ الأَسْوَدِ بنِ عَبْدِ يَغُوْثَ عَامَ أَذْرح، فَوَقَعَ الوَجَعُ بِالشَّامِ، فَأَقَمْنَا بِسَرْغٍ خَمْسِيْنَ لَيْلَةً
> — ⁨سیر أعلام النبلاء جـ۱ ص۹۵⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/10906/1521
> *English:* "I went out with my father, and Saʿd, and ʿAbd al-Raḥmān ibn al-Aswad ibn ʿAbd Yaghūth, in the year of Adhruḥ; and the sickness fell on Syria, so we stayed at Sargh fifty nights."

`[SOURCED]` — ⁨سیر جـ۱ ص۹۵⁩; the edition's footnote on that report reads ⁨إسناده حسن⁩. **That ⁨أذرح⁩ is
the place of the arbitration is the standard identification and is not stated on this page** —
`[STANDARD]` *(to verify)*.

### 4.2 "Do not fall upon my brothers"

The strongest single sentence for the register of this whole evening. A man was reviling ⁨علي ؓ⁩,
⁨طلحة ؓ⁩ and ⁨الزبير ؓ⁩; ⁨سعد ؓ⁩ — who had fought alongside none of them — kept forbidding him:

> أَنَّ رَجُلاً كَانَ يَقَعُ فِي عَلِيٍّ، وَطَلْحَةَ، وَالزُّبَيْرِ، فَجَعَلَ سَعْدٌ يَنْهَاهُ، وَيَقُوْلُ:
> لاَ تَقَعْ فِي إِخْوَانِي.
> — ⁨سیر أعلام النبلاء جـ۱ ص۱۱۶⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/10906/1542
> *English:* "A man used to fall upon ʿAlī, Ṭalḥa and al-Zubayr, and Saʿd kept forbidding him, saying: Do not fall upon my brothers."

`[SOURCED]` — ⁨سیر جـ۱ ص۱۱۶⁩. ⁨ابن کثیر⁩ carries the same incident inside his own account of ⁨الجمل⁩:

> أن رجلاً كان يقع في طلحة والزبير وعثمان وعلي ﵃، فجعل سعدٌ ينهاه ويقول: لا تقع في إخواني
> — ⁨البدایہ والنہایہ جـ۷ ص۴۳۰⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/30097/3576
> *English:* "A man used to fall upon Ṭalḥa and al-Zubayr and ʿUthmān and ʿAlī — may God be pleased with them — and Saʿd kept forbidding him and saying: Do not fall upon my brothers."

**The two books agree on the sentence and differ on the list of names reviled** — ⁨سیر⁩ gives three,
⁨البدایہ⁩ gives four, adding ⁨عثمان ؓ⁩. `[SOURCED, the books differ]`

The report continues in both books with a Khurasani camel crushing the man after ⁨سعد ؓ⁩ prayed two
rakʿas. ⁨البدایہ⁩ gives ⁨سعد ؓ⁩'s prayer in full:

> اللهم إن كان [هذا] سخطاً لك فيما يقول، فأرني فيه اليوم آية واجعله للناس عبرةً
> — ⁨البدایہ والنہایہ جـ۷ ص۴۳۰⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/30097/3576
> *English:* "O God, if what he says is a thing that angers You, then show me a sign in him today, and make him a lesson for the people."

The square brackets are the editor's supplement.
⚠ **The edition flags the chain of the ⁨البدایہ⁩ version: ⁨علي بن زيد بن جدعان⁩ is weak.** See
"Not for the hall" before narrating the ending.

### 4.3 ⁨عمر ؓ⁩'s testimonial, quoted on the same page as the sword

> وَقَالَ: مَنِ اسْتَخْلَفُوْهُ فَهُوَ الخَلِيْفَةُ بَعْدِي، وَإِنْ أَصَابَتْ سَعْداً، وَإِلاَّ فَلْيَسْتَعِنْ بِهِ الخَلِيْفَةُ بَعْدِي، فَإِنَّنِي لَمْ أَنْزَعْهُ -يَعْنِي: عَنِ الكُوْفَةِ- مِنْ ضَعْفٍ وَلاَ خِيَانَةٍ (٢) .
> — ⁨سیر أعلام النبلاء جـ۱ ص۱۱۸⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/10906/1544
> *English:* "And he said: whomever they appoint, he is the caliph after me. If it falls to Saʿd, [then so]; and if not, let the caliph after me seek his help — for I did not remove him, meaning from Kūfa, for weakness or for treachery."

`[SOURCED]` — ⁨سیر جـ۱ ص۱۱۸⁩. It matters to the strand because it establishes, from ⁨عمر ؓ⁩'s own mouth
and before any of this began, that ⁨سعد ؓ⁩'s later stillness was not the stillness of a man who had
been found wanting.

## 5. ⁨عبد الله بن عمر ؓ⁩ — "I held my hand back, and I did not regret it"

### 5.1 The sentence

In the middle of a page of ascetic notices, with no framing at all, ⁨الذهبي⁩ prints this:

> أَبُو المَلِيْحِ الرَّقِّيُّ: عَنْ مَيْمُوْنٍ:
> قَالَ ابْنُ عُمَرَ: كَفَفْتُ يَدِي، فَلَمْ أَندَمْ، وَالمُقَاتِلُ عَلَى الحَقِّ أَفْضَلُ.
> — ⁨سیر أعلام النبلاء جـ۳ ص۲۱۳⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/10906/2822
> *English:* "Abū al-Malīḥ al-Raqqī, from Maymūn: Ibn ʿUmar said: I held my hand back, and I did not regret it — and the one who fights for the truth is better."

`[SOURCED]` — ⁨سیر جـ۳ ص۲۱۳⁩. **This is the load-bearing sentence of the note, and it has two halves.**
The first half is a man refusing to fight. The second half — "and the one who fights for the truth
is better" — is that same man refusing to turn his own choice into a rebuke of anybody else. A slide
carrying only the first half changes what he said.

### 5.2 What the same page says about how he lived

The line sits immediately beside a note on his poverty, from the same transmitter, which is why it
reads as biography rather than as a position paper:

> قَالَ: وَلَقَدْ دَخَلْتُ عَلَى ابْنِ عُمَرَ، فَقَوَّمْتُ كُلَّ شَيْءٍ فِي بَيْتِهِ مِنْ أَثَاثٍ مَا يَسْوَى مائَةَ دِرْهَمٍ (٢) .
> — ⁨سیر أعلام النبلاء جـ۳ ص۲۱۳⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/10906/2822
> *English:* "He said: and I entered upon Ibn ʿUmar and priced every piece of furniture in his house: it was not worth a hundred dirhams."

`[SOURCED]` — ⁨سیر جـ۳ ص۲۱۳⁩.

### 5.3 The one thing on these pages he is reported to regret

Not the withholding. On ⁨جـ۳ ص۲۱۱⁩:

> قَالَتْ عَائِشَةُ لابْنِ عُمَرَ: مَا مَنَعَكَ أَنْ تَنْهَانِي عَنْ مَسِيْرِي؟
> قَالَ: رَأَيْتُ رَجُلاً قَدِ اسْتَوْلَى عَلَيْكِ، وَظَنَنْتُ أَنَّكِ لَنْ تُخَالِفِيْهِ - يَعْنِي: ابْنَ الزُّبَيْرِ -.
> — ⁨سیر أعلام النبلاء جـ۳ ص۲۱۱⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/10906/2820
> *English:* "ʿĀʾisha said to Ibn ʿUmar: What kept you from forbidding me my journey? He said: I saw a man who had prevailed with you, and I supposed you would not go against him — meaning Ibn al-Zubayr."

`[SOURCED]` — ⁨سیر جـ۳ ص۲۱۱⁩, from ⁨أبو سفيان بن العلاء المازني عن ابن أبي عتيق⁩. **Narrate it and
stop.** It is a question asked by ⁨أم المؤمنين ؓ⁩ and an answer given by ⁨ابن عمر ؓ⁩; nothing on the
page resolves it, and we do not resolve it either.

### 5.4 The same position, held twenty-eight years later — and the page dates it itself

The refusal did not soften. After the death of ⁨يزيد⁩, when the caliphate was there for the taking
(سیر جـ۳ ص۲۱۶):

> أَنَّ مَرْوَانَ قَالَ لابْنِ عُمَرَ -يَعْنِي: بَعْدَ مَوْتِ يَزِيْدَ -: هَلُمَّ يَدَكَ نُبايِعْكَ، فَإِنَّك سَيِّدُ العَربِ، وَابْنُ سَيِّدِهَا.
> قَالَ: كَيْفَ أَصْنَعُ بِأَهْلِ المَشرقِ؟
> قَالَ: نَضرِبُهُم حَتَّى يُبَايِعُوا.
> قَالَ: وَاللهِ مَا أُحِبُّ أَنَّهَا دَانَتْ لِي سبعِيْنَ سَنَةً، وَأَنَّهُ قُتلَ فِي سَيْفِي رَجُلٌ وَاحِدٌ.
> — ⁨سیر أعلام النبلاء جـ۳ ص۲۱۶⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/10906/2825
> *English:* "Marwān said to Ibn ʿUmar — that is, after the death of Yazīd: Give me your hand, let us give you the bayʿa, for you are the lord of the Arabs and the son of their lord. He said: What am I to do about the people of the East? He said: We strike them until they give the bayʿa. He said: By God, I would not wish it to submit to me for seventy years, at the cost of one single man being killed by my sword."

`[SOURCED]` — ⁨سیر جـ۳ ص۲۱۶⁩; the edition's footnote calls the chain ⁨حسن⁩ and places the report in
⁨طبقات ابن سعد⁩. ⚠ **The page puts no year on it. It dates it only by an event, in the words the
narrator uses to introduce it — ⁨يعني⁩: ⁨بعد موت يزيد⁩, "that is, after the death of Yazīd" (the Arabic
is in the blockquote above). That death is conventionally 64 AH** `[STANDARD]` *(to verify — the
year is not on this page)* **— long after 36–40 AH.** Use it as what the man said later about the
same position, and say so aloud. It must never be moved back into the window.

### 5.5 One line about ⁨ابن عمر ؓ⁩ that must not be misread

On ⁨جـ۳ ص۲۰۸⁩:

> أَنَّ ابْنَ عُمَرَ بَارَزَ رَجُلاً فِي قِتَالِ أَهْلِ العِرَاقِ، فَقَتَلَهُ، وَأَخَذَ سَلَبَهُ (١) .
> — ⁨سیر أعلام النبلاء جـ۳ ص۲۰۸⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/10906/2817
> *English:* "that Ibn ʿUmar met a man in single combat in the fighting of the people of Iraq, killed him and took his spoils."

`[SOURCED]` — ⁨سیر جـ۳ ص۲۰۸⁩. **On this page ⁨قتال أهل العراق⁩ reads most naturally as the conquest
fighting in Iraq against the Persians, not as fighting between Muslims** — the same page lists his
campaigning:

> قَدِمَ الشَّامَ، وَالعِرَاقَ، وَالبَصْرَةَ، وَفَارِسَ غَازِياً.
> — ⁨سیر أعلام النبلاء جـ۳ ص۲۰۸⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/10906/2817
> *English:* "He came to Syria, and Iraq, and Basra, and Persia as a warrior."

— and the edition's footnote traces the report to ⁨ابن سعد⁩ with an added clause about his father
confirming the spoils to him, which places it in ⁨عمر ؓ⁩'s lifetime. The sentence is recorded here
only so that nobody later finds it and mistakes it for ⁨ابن عمر ؓ⁩ fighting in the ⁨فتنة⁩.

## 6. ⁨أسامة بن زيد ؓ⁩ — and the one day that decided it

⁨أسامة ؓ⁩ is the only one of the four for whom ⁨الذهبي⁩ supplies the reason **in his own voice**, and he
supplies it as a cause, not as a coincidence. The passage runs across a page break; quote it as
جـ۲ ص۵۰۰–۵۰۱.

The end of ⁨ص۵۰۰⁩:

## 7. ⁨محمد بن مسلمة ؓ⁩ — the sword of wood

The richest of the four, and the only one where the withdrawal has a physical object attached to it
that a room will remember for a year.

### 7.1 The summary line — ⁨سیر جـ۲ ص۳۶۹⁩

⁨الذهبي⁩ puts it in the opening paragraph of the ⁨ترجمة⁩, before any report at all:

> وَكَانَ -رَضِيَ اللهُ عَنْهُ- مِمَّنِ اعْتَزَلَ الفِتْنَةَ، وَلاَ حَضَرَ الجَمَلَ، وَلاَ صِفِّيْنَ؛ بَلِ اتَّخَذَ سَيْفاً مِنْ خَشَبٍ، وَتَحَوَّلَ إِلَى الرَّبَذَةِ، فَأَقَامَ بِهَا مُدَيْدَةً (٢) .
> — ⁨سیر أعلام النبلاء جـ۲ ص۳۶۹⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/10906/2349
> *English:* "And he — may God be pleased with him — was among those who withdrew from the fitna. He was not present at al-Jamal, nor at Ṣiffīn; rather he took to himself a sword of wood, and moved out to al-Rabadha, and stayed there a short while."

`[SOURCED]` — ⁨سیر جـ۲ ص۳۶۹⁩. This is the one line in either book that states the position and names

⁨الربذة⁩, for the map: the settlement on the Iraq road east of ⁨المدينة⁩ where ⁨أبو ذر ؓ⁩ had died.

### 7.2 The instruction he says he was obeying — and it comes in two wordings

**Do not run these together. They are two different reports on two different pages, with two
different chains and two different sets of instructions.**

**Wording one** — to ⁨أبو بردة⁩, at ⁨الربذة⁩ itself (⁨سیر جـ۲ ص۳۷۱⁩):

> فَقَالَ: قَالَ لِي النَّبِيُّ - صَلَّى اللَّهُ عَلَيْهِ وَسَلَّمَ -: (يَا مُحَمَّدُ، سَتُكُوْنُ فُرْقَةٌ، وَفِتْنَةٌ، وَاخْتِلاَفٌ، فَاكْسِرْ سَيْفَكَ، وَاقْطَعْ وَتَرَكَ، وَاجْلِسْ فِي بَيْتِكَ) .

> — ⁨سیر أعلام النبلاء جـ۲ ص۳۷۱⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/10906/2351
> *English:* "We passed by al-Rabadha, and there was the tent of Muḥammad ibn Maslama. I said: If only you would go out to the people and command and forbid? He said: The Prophet ﷺ said to me: O Muḥammad, there will be division, and fitna, and disagreement — so break your sword, and cut your bowstring, and sit in your house. And I did what he ordered me."

⚠ `[SOURCED, chain weak in the edition]` — ⁨سیر جـ۲ ص۳۷۱⁩. The edition's footnote (٢) says: **⁨ابن⁩
⁨جدعان⁩: ⁨هو علي بن زيد وهو ضعيف، وباقي رجاله ثقات⁩** — Ibn Judʿān is weak, the rest of the chain are

**Wording two** — a ⁨مرسل⁩ of ⁨الحسن البصري⁩, two pages later (⁨سیر جـ۲ ص۳۷۳⁩):

> — ⁨سیر أعلام النبلاء جـ۲ ص۳۷۳⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/10906/2353

⚠ `[SOURCED, ⁨مرسل⁩]` — ⁨سیر جـ۲ ص۳۷۳⁩. The edition's footnote (٣): **⁨رجاله ثقات، إلا أن الحسن لم يسمع⁩
⁨من محمد بن مسلمة، فهو منقطع⁩** — sound men, but ⁨الحسن⁩ did not hear from ⁨محمد بن مسلمة⁩, so the chain

**The differences are the finding.** Wording one: break your sword, cut your bowstring, sit in your
house. Wording two: strike Uḥud with the sword until it breaks, sit in your house, and wait for
either an erring hand or a decreed death. **The second contains a prediction about the manner of his
death** — and §7.4 shows what the same ⁨ترجمة⁩ says happened to him. That is precisely the kind of
detail which makes a report attractive and which makes a careful reader check the chain, which is
what the edition has done for us.

## 8. ⁨أبو بكرة ؓ⁩ — the man who turned another man back, and the hadith he used

He said to me: O Aḥnaf, go back, for I heard the Messenger of Allāh ﷺ say: 'When two Muslims face one another with their swords, the killer and the killed are both in the Fire.' `[SOURCED — from the printed edition's own footnote, which traces it to ⁨البخاري⁩ (⁨كتاب الإيمان⁩, and

**How to use this, exactly.** This is the answer to "which hadith did they cite" — it is the only
place in everything fetched for this note where a man who refused to fight names a hadith of the
Prophet ﷺ as his reason **and the edition calls that version the sound one**. Three rules:

1. **Say that it is a hadith in ⁨البخاري⁩ and ⁨مسلم⁩** — the footnote gives the references — and say
   that the *scene* of ⁨أبو بكرة ؓ⁩ turning ⁨الأحنف⁩ back comes with it in those collections.
2. **Do not say "⁨الذهبي⁩ says"**. The Arabic block above is the modern editor's footnote, not
   ⁨الذهبي⁩'s text. ⁨الذهبي⁩'s own text on that page is the weaker wording.
3. **Do not use the weaker wording — "they are fighting one another over this world"** — on a slide,
   in a script, or aloud. It is a sentence about Companions, it is graded weak in the book it comes
   from, and the sound alternative is sitting right beside it. Recording it here is what stops it
   from being "discovered" later and used.

editor's supplements and the (٦) and (٧) are the edition's footnote markers; the footnotes record
that another manuscript reads ⁨المريض⁩, "the sick man", for ⁨المتربص⁩, "the one who held back", and that
⁨البدایہ⁩'s wording here agrees with ⁨تاريخ الطبري ٤⁩/٥٤٣ — **⁨البدایہ⁩'s own attribution, recorded as
such, not a citation of ⁨الطبری⁩ by us.**

This is the most useful scene in the strand, because it shows the position and the answer to it in
one exchange: a caliph names a man as the one who hung back, then walks to his sickbed, accepts his
excuse, and offers him a province. `[SOURCED]`

### 8.3 What ⁨أبو بكرة ؓ⁩ said he feared

Two pages earlier, in his own ⁨ترجمة⁩, a scene from his sickbed after a fall at a burial. His children
cried out over him, and he told them not to (⁨سیر جـ۳ ص۷⁩):

> فَأَفَاقَ، فَقَالَ: لاَ تَصْرُخُوا، فَوَاللهِ مَا مِنْ نَفْسٍ تَخْرُجُ أَحَبُّ إِلَيَّ مِنْ نَفْسِي.
> فَفَزِعَ القَوْمُ، وَقَالُوا: لِمَ يَا أَبَانَا؟!
> قَالَ: إِنِّيْ أَخْشَى أَنْ أُدْرِكَ زَمَاناً لاَ أَسْتَطِيْعُ أَنْ آمُرَ بِمَعْرُوْفٍ، وَلاَ أَنْهَى عَنْ مُنْكَرٍ، وَمَا خَيْرٌ يَوْمَئِذٍ (٣) .
> — ⁨سیر أعلام النبلاء جـ۳ ص۷⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/10906/2616
> *English:* "He came round and said: Do not cry out. By God, there is no soul whose going out is dearer to me than my own. The people were alarmed and said: Why, our father? He said: I fear to live to see a time in which I can neither command what is right nor forbid what is wrong — and there is no good on that day."

`[SOURCED]` — ⁨سیر جـ۳ ص۷⁩; ⁨الذهبي⁩ says the report is from ⁨معجم الطبراني⁩. The same page records that
when ⁨زياد⁩ seized a man's timber to build the colonnade of the mosque of ⁨البصرة⁩, **⁨أبو بكرة ؓ⁩ never
prayed in it until it was pulled down**:

> قَالَ: فَلَمْ يُصَلِّ أَبُو بَكْرَةَ فِيْهَا حَتَّى قُلِعَتْ (٤) .
> — ⁨سیر أعلام النبلاء جـ۳ ص۷⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/10906/2616
> *English:* "He said: and Abū Bakra never prayed in it until it was pulled down."

`[SOURCED]` — ⁨سیر جـ۳ ص۷⁩. That is the shape of his objection: not a sword, and not silence either.

## 9. ⁨الأحنف بن قيس⁩ — where the two books do not agree, and why he cannot be used

**⁨الأحنف⁩ is on every popular list of the men who would not fight. Our pages do not support putting
him there without a large qualification, and the qualification is itself the finding.**

**⁨سیر⁩ says he commanded for ⁨علي ؓ⁩ at ⁨صفين⁩.** In the opening paragraph of his ⁨ترجمة⁩, with no chain
and no hesitation:

> كَانَ مِنْ قُوَّادِ جَيْشِ عَلِيٍّ يَوْمَ صِفِّيْنَ.
> — ⁨سیر أعلام النبلاء جـ۴ ص۸۷⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/10906/3240
> *English:* "He was one of the commanders of ʿAlī's army on the day of Ṣiffīn."

`[SOURCED]` — ⁨سیر جـ۴ ص۸۷⁩.

**⁨البدایہ⁩ says his people had withdrawn at ⁨الجمل⁩.** Days after the battle, the notables came to
علي ؓ (البدایہ جـ۷ ص۴۲۵):

> فكان ممن جاءه الأحنف بن قيس في بني سعد -وكانوا قد اعتزلوا القتال- فقال له علي: تربصت (٣) - (يعني) بنا- فقال: ما كنتُ أراني إلا قد (٤) أحسنت، وبأمرك كان ما كان يا أمير المؤمنين، فارفق فإن طريقك الذي سلكت بعيد، وأنت إليَّ غداً أحوج منك أمس، فاعرف إحساني، واستبق مودتي لغد، ولا تقل (لي) مثل هذا فإني لم أزل لك ناصحاً.
> — ⁨البدایہ والنہایہ جـ۷ ص۴۲۵⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/30097/3571
> *English:* "Among those who came to him was al-Aḥnaf ibn Qays, with Banū Saʿd — and they had withdrawn from the fighting. ʿAlī said to him: You held back — [that is,] from us. He said: I did not see myself as having done anything but well; and what happened was by your order, O Commander of the Faithful. So be gentle, for the road you have taken is long, and you will need me tomorrow more than you needed me yesterday. So acknowledge my good service, and keep my affection in reserve for tomorrow, and do not say such a thing to me, for I have never ceased to be a sincere adviser to you."

`[SOURCED]` — ⁨البدایہ جـ۷ ص۴۲۵⁩. (The (٣) and (٤) are the edition's footnote markers; footnote (٣)
records that one printing has ⁨تربعت⁩, which the editor calls a corruption.)

**⁨البدایہ⁩ repeats the withdrawal six pages later**, in the account of ⁨الزبير ؓ⁩ turning back from the
battle:

> فمر بقوم الأحنف بن قيس -وكانوا قد اعتزلوا (٥) عن الفريقين-
> — ⁨البدایہ والنہایہ جـ۷ ص۴۳۱⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/30097/3577
> *English:* "and he passed by the people of al-Aḥnaf ibn Qays — and they had withdrawn from the two parties."

`[SOURCED]` — ⁨البدایہ جـ۷ ص۴۳۱⁩.

**And at ⁨صفين⁩, ⁨سیر⁩ shows him inside ⁨علي ؓ⁩'s council, arguing about who should arbitrate.** In
⁨أبو موسى ؓ⁩'s ⁨ترجمة⁩:

> فَقَالَ الأَحْنَفُ لِعَلِيٍّ: حَكِّمِ ابْنَ عَبَّاسٍ، فَإِنَّهُ رَجُلٌ مُجَرَّبٌ.
> — ⁨سیر أعلام النبلاء جـ۲ ص۳۹۵⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/10906/2375
> *English:* "al-Aḥnaf said to ʿAlī: appoint Ibn ʿAbbās as arbitrator, for he is a tried man."

⚠ **The edition grades that chain: ⁨الواقدي متروك⁩.** `[SOURCED, chain rejected in the edition]`

**The resolution, stated plainly and left there.** Nothing on our pages contradicts the possibility
that ⁨الأحنف⁩ withheld at ⁨الجمل⁩ with his clan and fought at ⁨صفين⁩ with ⁨علي ؓ⁩; ⁨سیر⁩'s one line and
⁨البدایہ⁩'s two are about different years. **But we do not assert a reconciliation we have not read.**
What may be said: ⁨البدایہ⁩ reports that ⁨بنو سعد⁩, ⁨الأحنف⁩'s people, stayed out of ⁨الجمل⁩, and that ⁨علي ؓ⁩
said so to his face; ⁨سیر⁩ states that ⁨الأحنف⁩ was one of ⁨علي ؓ⁩'s commanders at ⁨صفين⁩. **Both, side by
side, and no synthesis.** `[SOURCED, the books differ]`

`[SOURCED]` The rest of his ⁨ترجمة⁩ has nothing on the ⁨فتنة⁩ at all: it is ⁨عمر ؓ⁩ detaining him a year
to test him (⁨سیر جـ۴ ص۸۸⁩–۸۹), his speech about ⁨البصرة⁩'s salt ground (⁨ص۸۹⁩–۹۰), ⁨خراسان⁩ (⁨ص۹۰⁩–۹۱), and
his ⁨حلم⁩ (⁨ص۹۱⁩–۹۲). Two lines from it are worth having anyway, because they describe the temperament
that the withdrawal is usually explained by. On being asked why he would not speak when others were
speaking about ⁨معاوية ؓ⁩:

> قَالَ: أَخْشَى اللهَ إِنْ كَذَبْتُ، وَأَخْشَاكُم إِنْ صَدَقْتُ.
> — ⁨سیر أعلام النبلاء جـ۴ ص۹۲⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/10906/3245
> *English:* "He said: I fear God if I lie, and I fear you if I tell the truth."

And:

> ثَلاَثٌ فِيَّ مَا أَذْكُرُهُنَّ إِلاَّ لِمُعْتَبِرٍ: مَا أَتَيْتُ بَابَ سُلطَانٍ إِلاَّ أَنْ أُدْعَى، وَلاَ دَخَلْتُ بَيْنَ اثْنَيْنِ حَتَّى يُدْخِلاَنِي بَيْنَهُمَا، وَمَا أَذْكُرُ أَحَداً بَعْدَ أَنْ يَقُوْمَ مِنْ عِنْدِي إِلاَّ بِخَيْرٍ (٣) .
> — ⁨سیر أعلام النبلاء جـ۴ ص۹۲⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/10906/3245
> *English:* "Three things are in me which I mention only for one who will take a lesson: I never came to a ruler's door unless I was summoned; I never came in between two men until they brought me in between them; and I never mention anyone, after he has risen from my company, except well."

`[SOURCED]` — ⁨سیر جـ۴ ص۹۲⁩.

## 10. ⁨أبو موسى الأشعري ؓ⁩ — the man who did not fight, and then had to judge

### 10.1 ⁨الذهبي⁩ states the fact, and states it inside an explanation

The plainest statement in ⁨سیر⁩ that a named Companion did not fight comes in a passage where ⁨الذهبي⁩
is explaining a dislike, not making a charge (⁨سیر جـ۲ ص۳۹۴⁩):

> وَلاَ رَيْبَ أَنَّ غُلاَةَ الشِّيْعَةِ يُبْغِضُوْنَ أَبَا مُوْسَى -رَضِيَ اللهُ عَنْهُ- لِكَوْنِهِ مَا قَاتَلَ مَعَ عَلِيٍّ، ثُمَّ لَمَّا حَكَّمَهُ عَلِيٌّ عَلَى نَفْسِهِ عَزَلَهُ، وَعَزَلَ مُعَاوِيَةَ، وَأَشَارَ بِابْنِ عُمَرَ؛ فَمَا انْتَظَمَ مِنْ ذَلِكَ حَالٌ.
> — ⁨سیر أعلام النبلاء جـ۲ ص۳۹۴⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/10906/2374
> *English:* "There is no doubt that the extremists of the Shīʿa hate Abū Mūsā — may God be pleased with him — because he did not fight alongside ʿAlī; and then, when ʿAlī made him arbitrator over himself, he deposed him and deposed Muʿāwiya, and pointed to Ibn ʿUmar — and no state of affairs came right out of that."

`[SOURCED]` — ⁨سیر جـ۲ ص۳۹۴⁩. Read what this one sentence carries:

- **He did not fight alongside ⁨علي ؓ⁩.** ⁨الذهبي⁩ states it as a fact, in his own voice, and keeps the
  honorific ؓ in the same breath.
- **The whole outcome of the arbitration**, in ⁨الذهبي⁩'s compression: he removed both men and pointed
  to a third. **And the third man he pointed to was ⁨ابن عمر ؓ⁩ — one of ⁨وكيع⁩'s four, the man who said
  "I held my hand back".** The strand closes on itself here: the arbitrator who would not fight
  named, as the way out, the man who would not fight.
- **⁨الذهبي⁩'s verdict on the result**, in the closing clause of the same sentence (the Arabic is in
  the blockquote above): nothing came right of it. `[SOURCED]`

### 10.2 The appointment was argued against beforehand — and the objection is graded weak

Two reports on ⁨جـ۲ ص۳۹۵⁩ show ⁨ابن عباس ؓ⁩ trying to keep ⁨أبو موسى ؓ⁩ out of the arbitration:

> قُلْتُ لِعَلِيٍّ يَوْمَ
> — ⁨سیر أعلام النبلاء جـ۲ ص۳۹۴⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/10906/2374
> الحَكَمَيْنِ: لاَ تُحَكِّمِ الأَشْعَرِيَّ؛ فَإِنَّ مَعَهُ رَجُلاً حَذِراً مَرِساً قَارِحاً (١) ، فَلُزَّنِي (٢) إِلَى جَنْبِهِ، فَلاَ يَحُلُّ عُقْدَةً إِلاَّ عَقَدْتُهَا، وَلاَ يَعْقِدُ عُقْدَةً إِلاَّ حَلَلْتُهَا.
> — ⁨سیر أعلام النبلاء جـ۲ ص۳۹۵⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/10906/2375
> *English:* "I said to ʿAlī on the day of the two arbitrators: Do not appoint al-Ashʿarī as arbitrator, for with him is a wary man, practised and seasoned. Set me beside him, so that he unties no knot but I tie it, and ties no knot but I untie it."

And ⁨علي ؓ⁩'s answer, on the same page:

> قَالَ: يَا ابْنَ عَبَّاسٍ، مَا أَصْنَعُ؟
> إِنَّمَا أُوْتَى مِنْ أَصْحَابِي، قَدْ ضَعُفَتْ نِيَّتُهُمْ، وَكَلُّوا، هَذَا الأَشْعَثُ يَقُوْلُ: لاَ يَكُوْنُ فِيْهَا مُضَرِيَّانِ أَبَداً، حَتَّى يَكُوْنَ أَحَدُهُمَا يَمَانٍ.
> قَالَ ابْنُ عَبَّاسٍ: فَعَذَرْتُهُ، وَعَرَفْتُ أَنَّهُ مُضْطَهَدٌ (٣) .
> — ⁨سیر أعلام النبلاء جـ۲ ص۳۹۵⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/10906/2375
> *English:* "He said: Son of ʿAbbās, what am I to do? I am only undone from the side of my own companions. Their resolve has weakened and they are worn out. Here is al-Ashʿath saying: there shall never be two men of Muḍar in it — one of the two must be a Yemeni. Ibn ʿAbbās said: So I excused him, and I knew that he was hard pressed."

⚠ `[SOURCED, chain rejected in the edition]` — ⁨سیر جـ۲ ص۳۹۵⁩. Footnote (٣) on that page reads:
**⁨إسناده ضعيف لضعف محمد بن عمر هو الواقدي⁩** — the chain is weak because of ⁨محمد بن عمر⁩, who is
⁨الواقدي⁩ — and the second, similar report on the same page (the one in which ⁨الأحنف⁩ urges ⁨ابن عباس ؓ⁩
for the role) is graded **⁨والواقدي متروك⁩**. **Both of these are therefore recorded here and are not
for a slide.** What they are good for is the "Not for the hall" list and for answering a questioner
who has met them elsewhere.

Also on ⁨جـ۲ ص۳۹۵⁩–۳۹۶, in a single line, is ⁨علي ؓ⁩'s charge to him, from ⁨أبو صالح السمان⁩ — the
sentence runs across the page break:

> قَالَ أَبُو صَالِحٍ السَّمَّانُ: قَالَ عَلِيٌّ: يَا أَبَا مُوْسَى، احْكُمْ، وَلو عَلَى حَزِّ
> — ⁨سیر أعلام النبلاء جـ۲ ص۳۹۵⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/10906/2375
> عُنُقِي (١) .
> — ⁨سیر أعلام النبلاء جـ۲ ص۳۹۶⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/10906/2376
> *English:* "Abū Ṣāliḥ al-Sammān said: ʿAlī said: O Abū Mūsā, judge — even if it be to the cutting of my neck."

`[SOURCED]` — ⁨سیر جـ۲ ص۳۹۵⁩–۳۹۶. The edition traces it to ⁨ابن عساكر⁩ through ⁨يحيى بن معين⁩ and
الأعمش.

### 10.3 The offer he refused — and this one the edition calls sound

> أَنَّ مُعَاوِيَةَ كَتَبَ إِلَيْهِ:
> أَمَّا بَعْدُ، فَإِنَّ عَمْرَو بنَ العَاصِ قَدْ بَايَعَنِي عَلَى مَا أُرِيْدُ، وَأُقْسِمُ بِاللهِ، لَئِنْ بَايَعْتَنِي عَلَى الَّذِي بَايَعَنِي، لأَسْتَعْمِلَنَّ أَحَدَ ابْنَيْكَ عَلَى الكُوْفَةِ، وَالآخَرَ عَلَى البَصْرَةِ؛ وَلاَ يُغْلَقُ دُوْنَكَ بَابٌ، وَلاَ تُقْضَى دُوْنَكَ حَاجَةٌ، وَقَدْ كَتَبْتُ إِلَيْكَ بِخَطِّي، فَاكْتُبْ إِلَيَّ بِخَطِّ يَدِكَ.
> — ⁨سیر أعلام النبلاء جـ۲ ص۳۹۶⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/10906/2376
> *English:* "Muʿāwiya wrote to him: To proceed — ʿAmr ibn al-ʿĀṣ has given me his hand on what I want. I swear by God, if you give me your hand on what he gave me his on, I will appoint one of your two sons over Kūfa and the other over Basra; no door will be shut before you, and no need settled without you. I have written to you in my own hand — so write to me in the hand of your own."

> فَكَتَبَ إِلَيْهِ: أَمَّا بَعْدُ، فَإِنَّكَ كَتَبْتَ إِلَيَّ فِي جَسِيْمِ أَمْرِ الأُمَّةِ، فَمَاذَا أَقُوْلُ لِرَبِّي إِذَا قَدِمْتُ عَلَيْهِ، لَيْسَ لِي فِيْمَا عَرَضْتَ مِنْ حَاجَةٍ، وَالسَّلاَمُ عَلَيْكَ.
> — ⁨سیر أعلام النبلاء جـ۲ ص۳۹۶⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/10906/2376
> *English:* "So he wrote back to him: To proceed — you have written to me concerning a weighty matter of this umma. What shall I say to my Lord when I come before Him? I have no need of what you have offered. And peace be upon you."

`[SOURCED — the edition calls this chain ⁨صحيح⁩]` — ⁨سیر جـ۲ ص۳۹۶⁩. Footnote (٢) on that page traces it
through ⁨ابن عساكر⁩ and through ⁨ابن سعد⁩ from three transmitters of ⁨سليمان بن المغيرة⁩, **⁨وهذا سند⁩
⁨صحيح⁩**. The report ends with ⁨أبو بردة⁩, the son, saying that when ⁨معاوية ؓ⁩ did come to rule, "he shut
no door before me, and I had no need but it was met." `[SOURCED]`

**This is the best single card in the ⁨أبو موسى ؓ⁩ material**, because it is soundly transmitted, it
is a letter rather than a battle, and it answers by itself the charge that a man who would not fight
was playing for advantage.

### 10.4 ⁨الذهبي⁩'s own settled verdict on him

> قُلْتُ: قَدْ كَانَ أَبُو مُوْسَى صَوَّاماً، قَوَّاماً، رَبَّانِيّاً، زَاهِداً، عَابِداً، مِمَّنْ جَمَعَ العِلْمَ وَالعَمَلَ وَالجِهَادَ وَسَلاَمَةَ الصَّدْرِ، لَمْ تُغَيِّرْهُ الإِمَارَةُ، وَلاَ اغْتَرَّ بِالدُّنْيَا.
> — ⁨سیر أعلام النبلاء جـ۲ ص۳۹۶⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/10906/2376
> *English:* "I say: Abū Mūsā was much-fasting, much-praying, a man of the Lord, ascetic, devoted in worship — one of those who gathered knowledge, action, jihād, and soundness of breast. Command did not change him, nor was he deluded by this world."

`[SOURCED]` — ⁨سیر جـ۲ ص۳۹۶⁩. Note where ⁨الذهبي⁩ places it: **immediately after the letter**, as the
summing up of the whole ⁨ترجمة⁩. That is the order in which to tell it.

### 10.5 The assessment ⁨علي ؓ⁩ is reported to have given of him — record it, do not use it

On ⁨جـ۲ ص۳۸۸⁩, among a set of one-line assessments of the Companions attributed to ⁨علي ؓ⁩:

> قُلْنَا: أَبُو مُوْسَى؟ قَالَ: صُبِغَ فِي العِلْمِ صِبْغَةً، ثُمَّ خَرَجَ مِنْهُ.
> — ⁨سیر أعلام النبلاء جـ۲ ص۳۸۸⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/10906/2368
> *English:* "We said: and Abū Mūsā? He said: He was dipped in knowledge a dipping, then came out of it."

`[SOURCED]` — ⁨سیر جـ۲ ص۳۸۸⁩. The edition says **⁨رجاله ثقات⁩** and traces it to
⁨الفسوي⁩'s ⁨تاريخ⁩. **It is recorded here so that nobody later meets it and thinks it was hidden. It is
a judgement by one Companion about another, it is capable of more than one reading, and it belongs
in the "Not for the hall" list.**

## 11. It was not only individuals — whole communities stood out

### 11.1 ⁨زياد⁩ at ⁨البصرة⁩

In one clause, as an aside, while ⁨ابن عباس ؓ⁩ is being appointed over ⁨البصرة⁩ (⁨البدایہ جـ۷ ص۴۲۶⁩):

> وجعل معه زياد بن أبيه على الخراج وبيت المال، وأمر ابن عباس أن يسمع من زياد -وكان زياد معتزلًا-
> — ⁨البدایہ والنہایہ جـ۷ ص۴۲۶⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/30097/3572
> *English:* "And he set Ziyād ibn Abīhi with him over the land tax and the treasury, and ordered Ibn ʿAbbās to listen to Ziyād — and Ziyād had been one who stood aside."

`[SOURCED]` — ⁨البدایہ جـ۷ ص۴۲۶⁩. The point worth making from it: **standing aside was not a
disqualification from office.** The man who had withheld was put over the treasury by the caliph's
own order, exactly as ⁨أبو بكرة ؓ⁩ — his brother by their mother, per ⁨سیر جـ۳ ص۶⁩ — was offered a
province in §8.2.

### 11.2 ⁨خِرْبتا⁩ in Egypt — some ten thousand, and a truce instead of a battle

> واستقامت له طاعة بلاد مصر سوى قرية منها يقال لها خِرْبتا (٤)، فيها ناس قد أعظموا قتل عثمان - وكانوا سادة الناس ووجوههم وكانوا في نحو من عشرة آلاف [منهم بشر بن أبي أرطأة ومسلمة بم مخلَّد ومعاوية بن خديج وجماعة من الأكابر] وعليهم رجل يقال له يزيد بن الحارث المُدْلجي - وبعثوا إلى قيس بن سعد فوادعهم، وكذلك مسلمة بن مُدْلج الأنصاري تأخَّر عن البيعة فتركه قيس (بن سعد) ووادعه.
> — ⁨البدایہ والنہایہ جـ۷ ص۴۳۵⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/30097/3581
> *English:* "The obedience of the land of Egypt was settled for him, except for one village of it called Khirbitā, in which were people who held the killing of ʿUthmān to be a grave thing — and they were the leaders of the people and their notables, and they were about ten thousand [among them Bishr ibn Abī Arṭāt, Maslama ibn Mukhallad, Muʿāwiya ibn Khadīj and a group of the great men], and over them was a man called Yazīd ibn al-Ḥārith al-Mudlijī. They sent to Qays ibn Saʿd, and he made a truce with them. Likewise Maslama ibn Mudlij al-Anṣārī held back from the bayʿa, and Qays ibn Saʿd left him alone and made a truce with him."

`[SOURCED]` — ⁨البدایہ جـ۷ ص۴۳۵⁩. The square brackets are the editor's supplement; the (٤) is the
edition's footnote marker, and that footnote locates ⁨خِرْبتا⁩ near ⁨الإسكندرية⁩ on the authority of
⁨معجم البلدان⁩. ⚠ **The printed text as fetched reads «⁨مسلمة بم مخلَّد⁩» — "⁨بم⁩" for "⁨بن⁩" — which is a
typesetting slip in the source. Do not reproduce it on an artifact and do not silently correct it
without reading a page image.**

Two things to take from this `[SOURCED]`:
- A body of ten thousand — described as the notables, not as rebels — stayed out of the ⁨بيعة⁩
  altogether. **The withholding was not an eccentricity of four famous men.**
- The response to them was **⁨وادعهم⁩**, a truce. ⁨علي ؓ⁩'s own governor chose not to compel them. Two
  paragraphs later on ⁨ص۴۳۶⁩ that same governor is ordered to attack them, replies that they are many
  and are the notables of the people, and asks to be replaced rather than do it — and is replaced,
  and later comes to ⁨علي ؓ⁩, is excused, and fights at ⁨صفين⁩. `[SOURCED]` — ⁨البدایہ جـ۷ ص۴۳۶⁩
  (https://shamela.ws/book/30097/3582).

### 11.3 The men who hid at ⁨الكوفة⁩

In the tail of the ⁨ترجمة⁩ of ⁨أبو مسعود الأنصاري ؓ⁩ — whom ⁨علي ؓ⁩ had left in charge of ⁨الكوفة⁩ when he
marched — ⁨سیر⁩ records the plain fact of desertion (⁨سیر جـ۲ ص۴۹۶⁩):

> لَمَّا خَرَجَ عَلِيٌّ، اسْتَخْلَفَ أَبَا مَسْعُوْدٍ عَلَى الكُوْفَةِ، وَتَخَبَّأَ رِجَالٌ لَمْ يَخْرُجُوا مَعَ عَلِيٍّ.
> فَقَالَ أَبُو مَسْعُوْدٍ عَلَى المِنْبَرِ: أَيُّهَا النَّاسُ، مَنْ كَانَ تَخَبَّأَ، فَلْيَظْهَرْ؛
> — ⁨سیر أعلام النبلاء جـ۲ ص۴۹۶⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/10906/2476
> *English:* "When ʿAlī went out he left Abū Masʿūd in charge of Kūfa, and men hid themselves and did not go out with ʿAlī. Abū Masʿūd said from the pulpit: O people, whoever has hidden himself, let him show himself."

`[SOURCED]` — ⁨سیر جـ۲ ص۴۹۶⁩. ⚠ **The rest of that sermon, on the same page, is difficult to
construe as printed and is NOT to be quoted until a page image has been read.** It is recorded here
verbatim so that the next pass does not have to fetch it again:

> وَمَا نَعُدُّهُ قُبْحاً أَنْ يَلْتَقِيَ هَذَانِ الجَبَلاَنِ غَداً مِنَ المُسْلِمِيْنَ، فَيَقْتُلَ هَؤُلاَءِ هَؤُلاَءِ؛ وَهَؤُلاَءِ هَؤُلاَءِ. حَتَّى إِذَا لَمْ يَبْقَ إِلاَّ رِجْرِجَةٌ (٢) مِنْ هَؤُلاَءِ وَهَؤُلاَءِ؛ ظَهَرَتْ إِحْدَى الطَّائِفَتَيْنِ. وَلَكِنْ نَعُدُّ قُبْحاً أَن يَأْتِيَ اللهُ بِأَمْرٍ مِنْ عِنْدِهِ، يَحْقِنُ بِهِ دِمَاءهُم، وَيُصْلِحُ بِهِ ذَاتَ بَيْنِهِم.
> — ⁨سیر أعلام النبلاء جـ۲ ص۴۹۶⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/10906/2476
> *English:* **not rendered — the sense is unresolved; see the paragraph below.**

The (٢) is the edition's own footnote marker, which had been dropped from an earlier copy of this
note. The edition glosses ⁨رِجْرِجَة⁩ as the dregs and rabble of the people. A literal rendering of the last
clause gives a sense opposite to what the passage appears to be arguing, so either an interrogative
or a negation is being carried by the delivery rather than by the printing.
`[SOURCED but sense unresolved]` — ⁨سیر جـ۲ ص۴۹۶⁩.

## 12. Where the historians themselves refuse a report

**This is the most valuable material in the note.** It is what lets a speaker say from the platform:
"the scholars of this ummah sifted this material themselves, and here is one of them throwing a
report away" — and then decline to adjudicate anything further.

### 12.1 ⁨ابن کثیر⁩, on the whole body of partisan reports about this window

At the close of his ⁨الجمل⁩ narrative, having just said that what he gave is a summary of what ⁨ابن⁩
⁨جرير⁩ reported from the imāms of the field (⁨البدایہ جـ۷ ص۴۲۷⁩):

> هذا ملخص ما ذكره (أبو جعفر) بن جرير ﵀ عن أئمة هذا الشأن، وليس فيما ذكر (٢) أهل الأهواء من الشيعة وغيرهم من الأحاديث المختلقة على الصحابة والأخبار الموضوعة التي ينقلونها بما فيها، واذا دُعوا [إلى الله وإلى] الحق الواضح أعرضوا عنه وقالوا: لنا أخبارنا ولكم أخباركم، (فنحن حينئذ) نقول لهم: سلام عليكم لا نبتغي الجاهلين.
> — ⁨البدایہ والنہایہ جـ۷ ص۴۲۷⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/30097/3573
> *English:* "This is a summary of what Abū Jaʿfar ibn Jarīr — may God have mercy on him — reported from the imāms of this field. It contains none of what the people of caprice among the Shīʿa and others have mentioned of ḥadīths fabricated against the Companions and forged reports which they circulate with what is in them. And when they are called [to God and to] the plain truth they turn away from it and say: we have our reports and you have yours. We then say to them: peace be upon you; we do not seek out the ignorant."

`[SOURCED]` — ⁨البدایہ جـ۷ ص۴۲۷⁩. The closing phrase is a Qurʾānic allusion. **Note also what this
sentence licenses about method: ⁨ابن کثیر⁩ is here telling the reader, in his own words, that his
narrative of ⁨الجمل⁩ came through ⁨ابن جرير⁩ — ⁨البدایہ⁩'s own attribution, and the reason we may use
⁨البدایہ⁩ for this window without opening ⁨الطبری⁩ ourselves.**

### 12.2 ⁨ابن کثیر⁩ declaring one famous report forged, and naming the man

At ⁨ص۵۱۶⁩ he prints, in full, the report from ⁨الخطيب البغدادي⁩ in which ⁨علقمة⁩ and ⁨الأسود⁩ come to
⁨أبو أيوب الأنصاري ؓ⁩ on his return from ⁨صفين⁩ and he tells them the Prophet ﷺ commanded the fighting
of three named parties alongside ⁨علي ؓ⁩, followed by a long passage about ⁨عمار ؓ⁩. Then, without a
paragraph break:

> هذا السياق الظاهر أنه موضوع وآفته من جهة المعلى بن عبد الرحمن فإنه متروك الحديث، والله أعلم (٧).
> — ⁨البدایہ والنہایہ جـ۷ ص۵۱۶⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/30097/3662
> *English:* "This account is apparently a forgery, and its defect comes from the side of al-Muʿallā ibn ʿAbd al-Raḥmān, for he is abandoned in ḥadīth — and God knows best."

`[SOURCED]` — ⁨البدایہ جـ۷ ص۵۱۶⁩. The edition's footnote (٧) goes further than ⁨ابن کثیر⁩: **⁨بل هو كذاب⁩
⁨يسرق الحديث، وقد أخرجه ابن الجوزي في الموضوعات⁩** — rather he is a liar who steals ḥadīth, and ⁨ابن⁩
⁨الجوزي⁩ placed it in his ⁨الموضوعات⁩. `[SOURCED — the edition's footnote]`

⚠ **Handle with care and precision.** What ⁨ابن کثیر⁩ rejects here is **this ⁨سياق⁩, this chain,
through this man**. He is not making a general statement about every hadith touching ⁨عمار ؓ⁩, and we
must not make one either. The usable sentence for the platform is narrow and true: *a report of this
kind, in this chain, was examined by ⁨ابن کثیر⁩ and thrown out by name.* Anything wider than that is
ours, not his, and does not go on a slide.

Two lines further down the same page, ⁨ابن کثیر⁩ cites ⁨الهيثم بن عدي⁩'s book on the ⁨خوارج⁩ and praises
it:

> قال الهيثم بن عدي في كتابه الذي جمعه في الخوارج وهو من أحسن ما صنف في ذلك
> — ⁨البدایہ والنہایہ جـ۷ ص۵۱۶⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/30097/3662
> *English:* "al-Haytham ibn ʿAdī said, in the book he compiled on the Khawārij — and it is among the best that has been written on that —"

The edition's footnote (٩) contradicts him flatly: **⁨لكنه كذاب متروك⁩!** —
"but he is a liar, abandoned!" `[SOURCED]` — ⁨البدایہ جـ۷ ص۵۱۶⁩. **The editor disagreeing with ⁨ابن⁩
⁨کثیر⁩ on the page is itself worth knowing, and is another reason not to build anything on the
⁨الهيثم بن عدي⁩ material at ⁨ص۵۱۷⁩–۵۲۰.**

### 12.3 ⁨الذهبي⁩ saying he does not know what a report means

A report in ⁨أبو موسى ؓ⁩'s ⁨ترجمة⁩ has ⁨حذيفة ؓ⁩ saying, as ⁨عبد الله بن مسعود ؓ⁩ and ⁨أبو موسى ؓ⁩ enter the
mosque:

> وَأَبُو مُوْسَى المَسْجِدَ، فَقَالَ: أَحَدُهُمَا مُنَافِقٌ.
> — ⁨سیر أعلام النبلاء جـ۲ ص۳۹۴⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/10906/2374
> *English:* "…and Abū Mūsā, the mosque; and he said: one of the two is a hypocrite."

⁨الذهبي⁩ prints it — and then:

> قُلْتُ: مَا أَدْرِي مَا وَجْهُ هَذَا القَوْلِ، سَمِعَهُ عَبْدُ اللهِ بنُ نُمَيْرٍ مِنْهُ، ثُمَّ يَقُوْلُ الأَعْمَشُ: حَدَّثْنَاهُمْ بِغَضَبِ أَصْحَابِ مُحَمَّدٍ -صَلَّى اللَّهُ عَلَيْهِ وَسَلَّمَ- فَاتَّخَذُوْهُ دِيْناً (٢) .
> — ⁨سیر أعلام النبلاء جـ۲ ص۳۹۴⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/10906/2374
> *English:* "I say: I do not know what the sense of this statement is. ʿAbd Allāh ibn Numayr heard it from him; and then al-Aʿmash says: we narrated to them the anger of the Companions of Muhammad ﷺ, and they took it as a religion."

`[SOURCED]` — ⁨سیر جـ۲ ص۳۹۴⁩. **The ⁨الأعمش⁩ clause in that blockquote — "we narrated to them the anger
of the Companions of Muhammad ﷺ, and they took it as a religion" — is the single most useful
sentence in this note for the platform.** It is a second-century scholar saying that words spoken in
anger between the Companions were turned by later people into a creed — which is exactly the
guardrail the course installs in session 1, said by an authority the room cannot dismiss.

**The printed edition's own footnote on this page refuses the report outright**, and the refusal had
been dropped from an earlier copy of this note:

> فإن صح هذا عن حذيفة ولا إخاله يصح، فإنه قد أخطأ في حق هذا الصحابي الجليل الذي استعمله النبي صلى الله عليه وسلم هو ومعاذا على اليمن، وولي للخليفتين عمر وعثمان، وشهد له فضلاء الصحابة بوفور عقله، واستقامة سيرته، وورعه وفضله
> — ⁨سیر أعلام النبلاء جـ۲ ص۳۹۴⁩, ⁨الهامش⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/10906/2374
> *English:* "And if this is sound from Ḥudhayfa — and I do not think it is sound — then he erred with respect to this eminent Companion, whom the Prophet ﷺ employed, he and Muʿādh, over the Yemen, and who held office for the two caliphs ʿUmar and ʿUthmān, and to whose abundant sense, upright conduct, scrupulousness and merit the excellent among the Companions bore witness."

The same footnote then argues that if the report is sound at all it must be a thing said in anger:

> على أن قول الأعمش الذي سيورده المصنف يفهم منه أن حذيفة إنما قال ذلك في حالة الغضب التي يقول فيها الإنسان كلاما لا يعتقد أحقيته إذا روجع، حين يسكت عنه الغضب، ولا يتعلق بما يقال في مثل هذه الحالة إلا الذين في قلوبهم مرض.
> — ⁨سیر أعلام النبلاء جـ۲ ص۳۹۴⁩, ⁨الهامش⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/10906/2374
> *English:* "Moreover al-Aʿmash's statement, which the author will bring next, gives to understand that Ḥudhayfa said that only in the state of anger in which a man says words whose rightness he does not hold to once he is spoken to again, when the anger has gone quiet in him; and only those in whose hearts is a sickness attach themselves to what is said in such a state."

`[SOURCED — the edition's footnote, ⁨سیر جـ۲ ص۳۹۴⁩]` **Neither the report nor its refutation goes in
front of the room. The word "hypocrite" is not to be spoken of any Companion, in any frame. The
principle ⁨الأعمش⁩ states may be.**

### 12.4 A smaller one, and useful for the same reason

At ⁨ص۵۲۰⁩, ⁨ابن کثیر⁩ quotes ⁨الهيثم⁩ relaying a version of the ⁨بنو ناجية⁩ affair in which a clan of the

> قال الهيثم: وهذا قول الشيعة ولم يسمع بحيٍّ من العرب ارتدوا [عن الإسلام] بعد الردة التى كانت في أيام الصديق.
> — ⁨البدایہ والنہایہ جـ۷ ص۵۲۰⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/30097/3666
> *English:* "al-Haytham said: and this is the saying of the Shīʿa; no clan of the Arabs is known to have apostatised [from Islam] after the ridda that was in the days of al-Ṣiddīq."

`[SOURCED]` — ⁨البدایہ جـ۷ ص۵۲۰⁩. ⚠ Given the
edition's verdict on ⁨الهيثم بن عدي⁩ in §12.2, cite this as *what ⁨البدایہ⁩ prints in the mouth of
⁨الهيثم⁩*, and nothing more.

## 13. What our books say about the position itself

There is no chapter in either book arguing for or against withholding. What there is instead is a
set of gestures, and the gestures are consistent.

1. **⁨وكيع⁩ calls the four "safe" — the word on the page is ⁨سَلِمَ⁩.** Not "right". §1. `[SOURCED]` —
   سیر جـ۲ ص۵۰۰.
2. **⁨الذهبي⁩ closes the ⁨أسامة ؓ⁩ passage with one word of his own — "and he did well".** That is the
   only explicit approval of the act in anything fetched here, and it is in ⁨الذهبي⁩'s own voice. §6.
   `[SOURCED]` — ⁨سیر جـ۲ ص۵۰۰⁩–۵۰۱.
3. **⁨ابن عمر ؓ⁩ refuses to make his choice into anyone else's rebuke:** "and the one who fights for
   the truth is better". §5.1. `[SOURCED]` — ⁨سیر جـ۳ ص۲۱۳⁩.
4. **⁨سعد ؓ⁩, who would not fight, forbids anyone to revile the men who did:** "Do not fall upon my
   brothers". §4.2. `[SOURCED]` — ⁨سیر جـ۱ ص۱۱۶⁩; ⁨البدایہ جـ۷ ص۴۳۰⁩.
5. **⁨علي ؓ⁩ names a man as ⁨المتربص⁩ and then goes to his sickbed, excuses him, and offers him a
   province.** §8.2. `[SOURCED]` — ⁨البدایہ جـ۷ ص۴۲۵⁩–۴۲۶.
6. **⁨علي ؓ⁩'s governor is told to attack ten thousand who would not give the ⁨بيعة⁩, refuses, and asks
   to be replaced — and ⁨علي ؓ⁩ replaces him and later excuses him.** §11.2. `[SOURCED]` —
   البدایہ جـ۷ ص۴۳۵–۴۳۶.
7. **⁨أم المؤمنين عائشة ؓ⁩ leaves ⁨البصرة⁩ with a sentence that forbids the recrimination itself**
   (البدایہ جـ۷ ص۴۲۶):

> وقالت: يا بنيَّ لا يعتب بعضنا على بعض (٥)، إنه والله ما كان بيني وبين علي في القدم إلا ما يكون بين المرأة وأحمائها، وإنه على معتبتي [له] لمن الأخيار. فقال علي: صدقتِ والله ما كان بيني وبينها إلا ذاك، وإنها لزوجةُ نبيكم ﷺ في الدنيا والآخرة.
> — ⁨البدایہ والنہایہ جـ۷ ص۴۲۶⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/30097/3572
> *English:* "And she said: My sons, let none of us reproach another. By God, there was never anything between me and ʿAlī, from of old, but what is between a woman and her husband's kin; and despite my reproach of him he is among the best. ʿAlī said: She has spoken the truth. By God, there was nothing between me and her but that — and she is the wife of your Prophet ﷺ in this world and the next."

`[SOURCED]` — ⁨البدایہ جـ۷ ص۴۲۶⁩. The (٥) is the edition's footnote marker, recording a variant
wording in one manuscript; the square brackets are the editor's supplement. **This passage and
⁨سعد ؓ⁩'s "Do not fall upon my brothers" are the two that should close any treatment of this strand.**

The same page also records, in one line, ⁨علي ؓ⁩'s order when two men at the door were reviling
⁨أم المؤمنين ؓ⁩ — a hundred lashes each:

> فقال له رجل: يا أمير المؤمنين إن على الباب رجلين ينالان من عائشة، فأمر عليٌّ القعقاعَ بن عمرو أن يجلدَ كلَّ واحدٍ منهما مئة وأن يخرجَهما من ثيابهما.
> — ⁨البدایہ والنہایہ جـ۷ ص۴۲۶⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/30097/3572
> *English:* "A man said to him: O Commander of the Faithful, there are two men at the door speaking against ʿĀʾisha. So ʿAlī ordered al-Qaʿqāʿ ibn ʿAmr to give each of them a hundred lashes and to strip them of their clothes."

And that when she asked after the dead of both armies she prayed for each one named:

> فجعلت كلما ذكر لها واحدٌ منهم تَرَحَّمتْ عليه ودَعَتْ له.
> — ⁨البدایہ والنہایہ جـ۷ ص۴۲۶⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/30097/3572
> *English:* "and every time one of them was named to her she asked God's mercy on him and prayed for him."

`[SOURCED]` — ⁨البدایہ جـ۷ ص۴۲۶⁩.

   passage (⁨جـ۷ ص۴۲۷⁩) is about ⁨الجمل⁩. The ⁨صفين⁩ chapter opens at ⁨جـ۷ ص۴۳۶⁩, the last page fetched in
   that block, and gives none there. **Anyone wanting a ⁨صفين⁩ number must fetch ⁨جـ۷ ص۴۳۷⁩ onward.**
2. **No total for the dead at ⁨صفين⁩.** Same reason.
3. **No narrative anywhere in these pages of ⁨علي ؓ⁩ sending to ⁨سعد ؓ⁩, or to ⁨ابن عمر ؓ⁩, or to
   ⁨أسامة ؓ⁩, and being refused.** The famous exchanges of that shape are not on any page fetched
   here. What we have is what each man said about the position, not a summons and a refusal. **Do
   not narrate a summons.**
4. **⁨أسامة بن زيد ؓ⁩'s own words are not on these pages.** §6 gives ⁨الذهبي⁩'s statement about him;
   ⁨أسامة ؓ⁩ himself is not quoted refusing anything. The one hadith attached to him — "What will you
   do about lā ilāha illā Allāh, Usāma?", whose Arabic is blockquoted in §6 — is quoted by ⁨الذهبي⁩ as
   the cause, from the incident before the Prophet ﷺ, not as something ⁨أسامة ؓ⁩ said during the ⁨فتنة⁩.
5. **Whether ⁨سعد ؓ⁩ or ⁨ابن عمر ؓ⁩ spoke or acted at the arbitration.** ⁨سیر جـ۱ ص۹۶⁩ says only that they
   were present.
6. **Whether ⁨الأحنف⁩ fought at ⁨صفين⁩ or withheld there.** §9. The two books point in different
   directions for different years and neither reconciles them.
7. **The date of ⁨محمد بن مسلمة ؓ⁩'s death is not agreed with the report of his killing.** ⁨سیر⁩ gives
   ⁨صفر⁩ 43 AH from four named authorities and a group, and separately reports him killed in his house by a man
   from ⁨الأردن⁩ when ⁨معاوية ؓ⁩ came with the people of ⁨الشام⁩. The pages do not say the two are the
   same event, and we do not say it either.
8. **Nothing was found on these pages about ⁨أبو أيوب الأنصاري ؓ⁩, ⁨حسان بن ثابت ؓ⁩, ⁨أبو هريرة ؓ⁩,
   ⁨عمران بن حصين ؓ⁩, ⁨زيد بن ثابت ؓ⁩ or ⁨كعب بن مالك ؓ⁩ in relation to withholding**, although several of
   those names appear on popular lists. ⁨البدایہ جـ۷ ص۵۱۶⁩ mentions ⁨أبو أيوب ؓ⁩ only inside the report
   it declares forged. **An additional pass would need the ⁨تراجم⁩ of each of those men in ⁨سیر⁩.**
9. **⁨الکامل⁩ (21712) and ⁨الإعلان⁩ (34) were not opened for this strand**, so no cross-check of any of
   the above against ⁨ابن الاثیر⁩ exists yet. **That is the cheapest next pass:** the ⁨الجمل⁩ and ⁨صفين⁩
   years of ⁨الکامل⁩ would either corroborate or complicate §2, §8 and §9.
10. **The arithmetic of ⁨ابن سيرين⁩'s "tens of thousands"** — how many Companions were alive in 36 AH — is not given by
    any of these pages as a number. `[CONVENTIONAL-ESTIMATE]` figures exist in general scholarship
    and **must be labelled as such on the slide itself if used at all**.

## Not for the hall

Real material, page-cited, which must not be narrated from the platform. Each entry gives the
reason.

1. **The weaker wording of ⁨أبو بكرة ؓ⁩'s words to ⁨الأحنف⁩ — "they are fighting one another over this
   world, and they only took it without consultation", blockquoted in §8.1** (⁨سیر جـ۳ ص۸⁩). It is a
   statement about Companions fighting over this world; the
   edition that prints it grades its chain weak and supplies a sound alternative on the same page.
   **Use the sound alternative (§8.1) and leave this one here.**
2. **⁨حذيفة ؓ⁩'s one-word verdict on one of two men entering the mosque, blockquoted in §12.3**
   (⁨سیر جـ۲ ص۳۹۴⁩). ⁨الذهبي⁩ himself says he does not know what it means; the edition says outright that
   it does not think the report sound, and argues that if it is sound it can only be words spoken in
   anger. **The word is not to be spoken in the hall about any Companion, in any frame, including in
   order to refute it.**
3. **⁨علي ؓ⁩'s "he was dipped in knowledge a dipping, then came out of it"** about ⁨أبو موسى ؓ⁩,
   blockquoted in §10.5 (⁨سیر جـ۲ ص۳۸۸⁩). A judgement of one Companion about another, capable of more
   than one reading, and certain to be heard in the room as a slight.
4. **⁨ابن عباس ؓ⁩'s objection to the appointment and ⁨علي ؓ⁩'s reply about his own companions' weakened
   resolve** (⁨سیر جـ۲ ص۳۹۵⁩). The edition rejects the chain (⁨الواقدي⁩). Keep it for answering a
   questioner who brings it; never narrate it.
5. **⁨أبو بكرة ؓ⁩'s answer to those who said he objected for worldly reasons:**

> إِنِّيْ إِنَّمَا عَتَبْتُ عَلَيْهِم لأَنَّهُم كَفَرُوا.
> — ⁨سیر أعلام النبلاء جـ۳ ص۹⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/10906/2618
> *English:* "I found fault with them only because they disbelieved."

   In context it is his answer to the charge that he objected
   to ⁨زياد⁩'s regime for worldly reasons, and it is about the appointment of his own sons. **It
   carries ⁨تكفير⁩ language, it belongs to the Umayyad period rather than to 36–40 AH, and there is no
   safe way to put it in front of a mixed room.** Recorded so that it is never "found" fresh.
6. **The ending of the ⁨سعد ؓ⁩ / camel report** (⁨سیر جـ۱ ص۱۱۶⁩; ⁨البدایہ جـ۷ ص۴۳۰⁩). The story of a man
   being crushed after a Companion's prayer against him is sound enough to record and wrong for this
   room: it converts a lesson about restraint into a spectacle about punishment. **The usable half is
   "Do not fall upon my brothers" and it stops there.** (The ⁨البدایہ⁩ version's chain is flagged weak
   in any case.)
7. **The killing of ⁨الزبير ؓ⁩ and the part played by the men of ⁨الأحنف⁩'s clan** (⁨البدایہ جـ۷ ص۴۳۱⁩).
   ⁨ابن کثیر⁩ himself hedges the identification:

> فقال قائل [منهم] يقال له الأحنف
> — ⁨البدایہ والنہایہ جـ۷ ص۴۳۱⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/30097/3577
> *English:* "and a speaker [among them] said to be al-Aḥnaf said —"

   The square brackets there are the editor's. **The hedge is
   part of the report and must never be dropped; and this material sits inside the ⁨مشاجرات⁩, where
   the standing instruction is to narrate the book and adjudicate nothing.** Session 3's guardrail
   governs it, not this note.
8. **⁨الهيثم بن عدي⁩'s material at ⁨البدایہ جـ۷ ص۵۱۷⁩–۵۲۰**, including the ⁨الخِرِّيت بن راشد⁩ affair and
   the ⁨عدي بن حاتم⁩ exchange with ⁨علي ؓ⁩. The edition calls ⁨الهيثم⁩ **⁨كذاب متروك⁩** on ⁨ص۵۱۶⁩. Nothing
   here rests on it, and nothing should.
9. **The whole apparatus of §12** — the gradings, the forgery verdicts, the editor's footnotes —
   **is production apparatus, not slide content** (`DECISIONS.md` #30). What reaches the room from
   §12 is one plain sentence in the speaker's own words: *the scholars sifted these reports
   themselves, and threw some of them away.* The names of the rejected transmitters stay in the
   speaker notes.

### E-SB1 · Ten thousand dead and almost no Companions present
**Tier:** CORE · **When:** 36 AH `[SOURCED]` — the month of the battle is not on the fetched
pages `[STANDARD]` *(to verify)* · **Map:** ⁨البصرة⁩ lights; nothing moves.

**What happened:** When the fighting at the Camel was over, the count of the dead was about ten
thousand men, five thousand from each of the two armies. The wounded were past counting. But when
the historians ask how many of the Prophet's ﷺ own Companions were in either army, the answer is
startling. They were tens of thousands of men alive in the world that year, and of all of them not a
hundred were on that field. One of the earliest reporters says they did not even reach thirty.

**The statement:**
> وقال الإمام أحمد: حدَّثنا إسماعيل، حدَّثنا أيوب، عن محمد بن سيرين قال: هاجت الفتنة وأصحاب رسول الله ﷺ عشرات ألوف فلم يحضرها منهم مئة، بل لم يبلغوا ثلاثين.
> — ⁨البدایہ والنہایہ جـ۷ ص۴۲۷⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/30097/3573
> *English:* "Imām Aḥmad said: Ismāʿīl related to us, Ayyūb related to us, from Muḥammad ibn Sīrīn, who said: the fitna broke out while the Companions of the Messenger of Allāh ﷺ were tens of thousands, and not a hundred of them were present at it — rather they did not reach thirty."

**⁨عبرت⁩:** The best-instructed generation in the history of this ummah, faced with a fight between
its own, overwhelmingly chose not to be in it.

**Hands-up?** How many of the Prophet's ﷺ Companions do you think fought at the Camel — hands for
more than a thousand, hands for fewer than a hundred?

### E-SB2 · The four who came through the fitna safe
**Tier:** CORE · **When:** 36–40 AH `[SOURCED]` · **Map:** No map.

**What happened:** Later scholars kept a short list of the famous men who came out of these years
untouched by what happened in them. Wakīʿ names four. Every one of them is a man the room already
knows from the conquests and from the Prophet's ﷺ own lifetime. Not one of them is obscure, and not

**The statement:**
> قَالَ وَكِيْعٌ: سَلِمَ مِنَ الفِتْنَةِ مِنَ المَعْرُوْفِيْنَ: سَعْدٌ، وَابْنُ عُمَرَ، وَأُسَامَةُ بنُ زَيْدٍ، وَمُحَمَّدُ بنُ مَسْلَمَةَ.

### E-SB3 · Saʿd ؓ among his sheep, and his son's rebuke
**Tier:** CORE · **When:** 36–40 AH `[SOURCED]` · **Map:** ⁨المدينة⁩ lights; a pin outside it for the
pasture.

**What happened:** The conqueror of Iraq, the commander at Qādisiyya, one of the ten and one of the
six of the shūrā, had taken himself out to his sheep. His son ʿUmar came riding out to him. Saʿd ؓ
saw him coming and said aloud that he took refuge with God from the evil of this rider. The son
reached him and asked how his father could be content to live like a bedouin among his animals while
the people were contending over rule at Medina. Saʿd ؓ struck him on the chest and told him to be
quiet, and then told him why.

**The statement:**
> فَضَرَبَ صَدْرَ عُمَرَ وَقَالَ: اسْكُتْ، فَإِنِّي سَمِعْتُ رَسُوْلَ اللهِ -صَلَّى اللَّهُ عَلَيْهِ وَسَلَّمَ- يَقُوْلُ (٢) : (إِنَّ اللهَ -عَزَّ وَجَلَّ- يُحِبُّ العَبْدَ التَّقِيَّ، الغَّنِيَّ، الخَفِيَّ (٣)) .
> — ⁨سیر أعلام النبلاء جـ۱ ص۱۰۲⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/10906/1528
> *English:* "He struck ʿUmar's chest and said: Be quiet — for I heard the Messenger of Allāh ﷺ say: God, mighty and majestic, loves the servant who is God-fearing, self-sufficient, obscure."

**⁨عبرت⁩:** A man can be told by his own household that his stillness is cowardice and still know what
he is standing on.

**Hands-up?** no

### E-SB4 · A sword with two eyes and a tongue
**Tier:** CORE · **When:** 36–40 AH `[SOURCED]` · **Map:** No map.

**What happened:** Saʿd ؓ was asked, again and again, to come out and take a side. His answer went
past the question. He said he made no claim to the caliphate at all, that he had done his fighting
and knew what fighting was, and that he would not destroy himself over a better man than himself.
Then he named his condition. He would fight when someone brought him a sword that could see and
speak — a sword that would say, of the man in front of him, whether he was a believer or not.

**The statement:**
> لاَ أُقَاتِلُ حَتَّى يَأْتُوْنِي بِسَيْفٍ لَهُ عَيْنَانِ وَلِسَانٌ، فَيَقُوْلَ: هَذَا مُؤْمِنٌ، وَهَذَا كَافِرٌ (٣) .
> — ⁨سیر أعلام النبلاء جـ۱ ص۱۱۸⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/10906/1544
> *English:* "I will not fight until they bring me a sword that has two eyes and a tongue, so that it says: this is a believer, and this is an unbeliever."

**⁨عبرت⁩:** He did not say that both sides were wrong. He said that a sword cannot tell them apart,
and that he would not be the one holding it.

**Hands-up?** no

### E-SB5 · The sword that turns aside from a Muslim
**Tier:** GOOD · **When:** 36–40 AH `[SOURCED]` · **Map:** No map.

**What happened:** A second son of Saʿd ؓ, ʿĀmir, came to him with the same request, and this time
it was more than a request. He wanted his father to be at the head of men. Saʿd ؓ answered him with
a question of his own, and then with a second image for the same refusal. He would take a sword when
he was given one that could not cut a Muslim.

**The statement:**
> أَيْ بُنَيَّ! أَفِي الفِتْنَةِ تَأْمُرُنِي أَنْ أَكُوْنَ رَأْساً؟ لاَ وَاللهِ، حَتَّى أُعْطَى سَيْفاً، إِنْ ضَرَبْتُ بِهِ مُسْلِماً نَبَا عَنْهُ، وَإِنْ ضَرَبْتُ كَافِراً قَتَلَهُ.
> — ⁨سیر أعلام النبلاء جـ۱ ص۱۱۹⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/10906/1545
> *English:* "My son! Is it in the fitna that you order me to be a head? No, by God — not until I am given a sword which, if I strike a Muslim with it, turns aside from him, and if I strike an unbeliever, kills him."

**⁨عبرت⁩:** The pressure to take a side came from inside the house before it came from anywhere else.

**Hands-up?** no

### E-SB6 · ʿUmar ؓ's last word about Saʿd ؓ
**Tier:** GOOD · **When:** 23 AH `[STANDARD]` *(to verify — the page does not date it)* ·
**Map:** ⁨المدينة⁩ · ⁨الكوفة⁩.

**What happened:** When ʿUmar ؓ was dying he set the matter in the hands of six men and gave an
instruction about one of them by name. If the choice fell on Saʿd ؓ, well and good. If it did not,
then whoever became caliph was to make use of him. And then ʿUmar ؓ said the thing he wanted on the
record, because he had removed Saʿd ؓ from Kūfa and everybody knew it. He had not removed him for
weakness and he had not removed him for treachery.

**The statement:**
> وَقَالَ: مَنِ اسْتَخْلَفُوْهُ فَهُوَ الخَلِيْفَةُ بَعْدِي، وَإِنْ أَصَابَتْ سَعْداً، وَإِلاَّ فَلْيَسْتَعِنْ بِهِ الخَلِيْفَةُ بَعْدِي، فَإِنَّنِي لَمْ أَنْزَعْهُ -يَعْنِي: عَنِ الكُوْفَةِ- مِنْ ضَعْفٍ وَلاَ خِيَانَةٍ (٢) .
> — ⁨سیر أعلام النبلاء جـ۱ ص۱۱۸⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/10906/1544
> *English:* "And he said: whomever they appoint, he is the caliph after me. If it falls to Saʿd, then so; and if not, let the caliph after me seek his help — for I did not remove him, meaning from Kūfa, for weakness or for treachery."

**⁨عبرت⁩:** A reputation cleared in advance is worth more than a defence made afterwards.

**Hands-up?** no

### E-SB7 · Two of the four were at the arbitration

### E-SB9 · I held my hand back and did not regret
**Tier:** CORE · **When:** 36–40 AH `[SOURCED]` · **Map:** ⁨المدينة⁩.

years. His answer is one sentence long and it has two halves, and the second half is the part people

> قَالَ ابْنُ عُمَرَ: كَفَفْتُ يَدِي، فَلَمْ أَندَمْ، وَالمُقَاتِلُ عَلَى الحَقِّ أَفْضَلُ.

**⁨عبرت⁩:** He would not take back his own choice and he would not turn it into a charge against
anybody else.

### E-SB14 · Muḥammad ibn Maslama ؓ and his wooden sword
**Tier:** CORE · **When:** 36–40 AH `[SOURCED]` · **Map:** ⁨المدينة⁩, then ⁨الربذة⁩ east of it.

**What happened:** Muḥammad ibn Maslama ؓ was a man of Badr and of every battle after it, and the
man ʿUmar ؓ used to send out whenever a governor was complained of. He was not present at the Camel

**The statement:**
> وَكَانَ -رَضِيَ اللهُ عَنْهُ- مِمَّنِ اعْتَزَلَ الفِتْنَةَ، وَلاَ حَضَرَ الجَمَلَ، وَلاَ صِفِّيْنَ؛ بَلِ اتَّخَذَ سَيْفاً مِنْ خَشَبٍ، وَتَحَوَّلَ إِلَى الرَّبَذَةِ، فَأَقَامَ بِهَا مُدَيْدَةً (٢) .

### E-SB25 · Two books, two pictures of the same man
