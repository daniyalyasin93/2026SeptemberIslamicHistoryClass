# The death of أبو بكر الصدیق ؓ (13 AH) and the succession of عمر ؓ

**Question this note answers:** How did the first caliphate end and the second begin? Specifically:
when the final illness began and how long it ran; the exact date of death, his age, where he was
buried; **whom** he consulted before naming عمر ؓ and **what each man said**, verbatim; the
objections raised about عمر ؓ's severity; the written عهد dictated to عثمان ؓ and the incident of
عثمان ؓ writing the name while أبو بكر ؓ lay unconscious; his last words; and the accounting he
ordered returned to the بيت المال. Plus the precise length of the caliphate — the number the
evening opens on.

## 1. Where the material actually is — and where it is not

**ابن کثیر رحمہ اللہ does not narrate this death at length in البدایہ.** He says so himself, on the
very page, and this is worth knowing before anyone hunts for it again:

> وقد ذكرنا ترجمة الصدّيق ﵁، وسيرته (٣) وأيامه وما روى من الأحاديث، وما روي عنه من الأحكام في مجلد ولله الحمد والمنة.
> — البدایہ والنہایہ ج۷ ص۱۰۲ · https://shamela.ws/book/30097/3248
> *"We have already given the Ṣiddīq's ؓ biographical notice, his conduct, his days, the ḥadīth he
> narrated and the rulings reported from him, in a separate volume — praise and thanks be to God."*

So البدایہ gives the **dates and the frame** in a single compact paragraph at ج۷ ص۱۰۲, and nothing
more. **The narrative detail of this strand comes from سیر أعلام النبلاء (الراشدون ص۱۴–۲۰) and
الکامل (ج۲ ص۲۶۲–۲۶۸).** Both are on the safe list. Anyone re-opening this strand should start at
those two places, not in البدایہ.

## 2. The frame — البدایہ's one paragraph

This is the single most economical page in the strand. Everything an opening slide needs is here.

> كانت وفاة الصدِّيق ﵁ في يوم الإثنين عشيةً، وقيل بعدَ المغرب ودفن من ليلته، وذلك لثمانٍ بقينَ من جمادى الآخرة سنة ثلاثَ عشرةَ بعد مرض خمسة عشر يومًا، وكان عمر بن الخطاب يصلي عنه فيها بالمسلمين، وفي أثناء هذا المرض عهد بالأمر من بعده إلى عمر بن الخطاب، وكان الذي كتب العهد عثمان بن عفان، وقرئ على المسلمين فأقروا به وسمعوا له وأطاعوا.
> — البدایہ والنہایہ ج۷ ص۱۰۲ · https://shamela.ws/book/30097/3248
> *"The Ṣiddīq ؓ died on Monday in the evening — some say after maghrib — and was buried that same
> night, with eight nights remaining of Jumādā al-Ākhira of the year thirteen, after an illness of
> fifteen days, during which ʿUmar ibn al-Khaṭṭāb led the Muslims in prayer in his place. In the
> course of that illness he committed the affair after him to ʿUmar ibn al-Khaṭṭāb; the one who
> wrote the covenant was ʿUthmān ibn ʿAffān; and it was read out to the Muslims, who acknowledged
> it, heard and obeyed."*

And the tally, on the same page:

> فكانت خلافة الصدِّيق سنتين وثلاثة أشهر، وكان عمره يوم توفي ثلاثًا وستين سنة، للسن الذي توفي فيه رسول الله ﷺ
> — البدایہ والنہایہ ج۷ ص۱۰۲ · https://shamela.ws/book/30097/3248
> *"The Ṣiddīq's caliphate was two years and three months. He was sixty-three the day he died —
> the same age at which the Messenger of God ﷺ died."*

> ⚠ **Typographical caveat on this page.** The Shamela text continues «وقد جمع الله بينهما في
> **التوبة**، كما جمع بينهما في الحياة». The word is almost certainly **التُّربة** (the earth, i.e.
> the grave) — "God joined them in the earth as He joined them in life." **Do not quote that clause
> from this text.** Either drop it or read it off a printed copy first.

The accession date of عمر ؓ, from the year-13 summary eighteen pages on:

> وفيها ولي عمر بن الخطاب ﵁ يوم الثلاثاء لثمان بقين من جمادى الآخرة منها.
> — البدایہ والنہایہ ج۷ ص۱۲۰ · https://shamela.ws/book/30097/3266
> *"In it ʿUmar ibn al-Khaṭṭāb ؓ took office, on Tuesday, with eight nights remaining of Jumādā
> al-Ākhira of that year."*

Monday evening for the death, Tuesday for the accession, both dated to the same night — consistent,
because the Islamic day begins at sunset. Say that once and the room stops worrying at it.

## 3. The final illness

### 3.1 عائشة ؓ's report — the cold bath

This is the report الذہبی رحمہ اللہ leads with, and it is the one to use.

> وعن عائشة قالت: أول ما بدئ مرض أبي بكر أنه اغتسل، وكان يوما باردًا فحم خمسة عشر يوما لا يخرج إلى صلاة، وكان يأمر عمر بالصلاة، وكانوا يعودونه، وكان عثمان ألزمهم له في مرضه. وتوفي مساء ليلة الثلاثاء لثمان بقين من جمادى الآخرة، وكانت خلافته سنتين ومائة يوم.
> — سیر أعلام النبلاء، ج الراشدون ص۱۶ · https://shamela.ws/book/10906/1164
> *"ʿĀʾisha ؓ said: the beginning of Abū Bakr's illness was that he bathed on a cold day and was
> feverish for fifteen days, not going out to the prayer. He would order ʿUmar to lead the prayer.
> People visited him, and ʿUthmān was the most constant of them at his side through the illness. He
> died on the evening of the night of Tuesday, with eight nights remaining of Jumādā al-Ākhira. His
> caliphate was two years and one hundred days."*

Two things worth noticing and saying aloud: **the fever kept him from the mosque, and the man he
sent to the miḥrāb is the man he then put in his place**; and **عثمان ؓ was the most constant
visitor** — which is exactly why عثمان ؓ is the one holding the pen a few days later.

### 3.2 The doctor

ابن الاثیر رحمہ اللہ, on the same page as the death notice:

> وَلَمَّا مَرِضَ قَالَ لَهُ النَّاسُ: أَلَا نَدْعُوَ الطَّبِيبَ؟ قَالَ: قَدْ أَتَانِي وَقَالَ لِي: أَنَا فَاعِلٌ مَا أُرِيدُ. فَعَلِمُوا مُرَادَهُ وَسَكَتُوا عَنْهُ، ثُمَّ مَاتَ.
> — الکامل فی التاریخ ج۲ ص۲۶۲ · https://shamela.ws/book/21712/953
> *"When he fell ill the people said to him: shall we not call the physician? He said: he has
> already come to me, and he said to me — I do what I will. They understood what he meant, fell
> silent, and then he died."*

Four lines, no commentary needed, and it lands. **This is the best short quotation in the strand.**

### 3.3 The poison account — recorded, not adopted

Our sources also carry a second account of the cause. It must be recorded, because a listener who
has read anything will raise it; and it must be handled carefully, because it is transmitted as
«يقال» and does not sit easily beside عائشة ؓ's report.

الذہبی رحمہ اللہ, through عقيل from الزهري:

> وقال عقيل: عن الزهري أن أبا بكر والحارث بن كلدة كانا يأكلان خزيرة أهديت لأبي بكر، فقال الحارث: ارفع يدك يا خليفة رسول الله، والله إن فيها لسم سنة، وأنا وأنت نموت في يوم واحد، قال: فلم يزالا عليلين حتى ماتا في يوم واحد عند انقضاء السنة.
> — سیر أعلام النبلاء، ج الراشدون ص۱۵–۱۶ · https://shamela.ws/book/10906/1163 و https://shamela.ws/book/10906/1164
> *"Abū Bakr and al-Ḥārith ibn Kalada were eating a khazīra that had been presented to Abū Bakr.
> al-Ḥārith said: lift your hand, Caliph of the Messenger of God — by God, there is a year's poison
> in this, and you and I will die on one day. They remained ailing until they died on one day, at
> the end of the year."*
> ⚠ **The report spans a printed page break**: it runs to «قال: فلم» on ص۱۵ and resumes at
> «يزالا عليلين» on ص۱۶.

And, bare, at the close of the same notice:

> ويقال: إن اليهود سمته في أرزة فمات بعد سنة، وله ثلاث وستون سنة رضي الله عنه وأرضاه.
> — سیر أعلام النبلاء، ج الراشدون ص۲۰ · https://shamela.ws/book/10906/1168
> *"It is said that the Jews poisoned him in rice, and he died a year later, aged sixty-three, may
> God be pleased with him."*

ابن الاثیر gives both together and does not choose:

> وَكَانَ قَدْ سَمَّهُ الْيَهُودُ فِي أَرُزٍّ، وَقِيلَ فِي حَرِيرَةٍ، وَهِيَ الْحَسْوُ، فَأَكَلَ هُوَ وَالْحَارِثُ بْنُ كَلَدَةَ، فَكَفَّ الْحَارِثُ وَقَالَ لِأَبِي بَكْرٍ: أَكَلْنَا طَعَامًا مَسْمُومًا سُمَّ سَنَةً. فَمَاتَا بَعْدَ سَنَةٍ. وَقِيلَ: إِنَّهُ اغْتَسَلَ وَكَانَ يَوْمًا بَارِدًا، فَحُمَّ خَمْسَةَ عَشَرَ يَوْمًا لَا يَخْرُجُ إِلَى صَلَاةٍ، فَأَمَرَ عُمَرَ أَنْ يُصَلِّيَ بِالنَّاسِ.
> — الکامل فی التاریخ ج۲ ص۲۶۲ · https://shamela.ws/book/21712/953
> *"...and it is said he bathed on a cold day and was feverish fifteen days, not going out to the
> prayer, and ordered ʿUmar to lead the people."*

> ⚠ **Recommended handling.** Narrate عائشة ؓ's account (§3.1); it is the one الذہبی leads with and
> the one البدایہ's fifteen-day figure matches. If the poison account is raised from the floor, say
> that both are in the books, that سیر gives the second as «يقال», and that the sources do not
> settle it. **Do not build a slide on it, and do not name a community as the agent of a death from
> a «يقال» report.** Log the question in `QA_BANK.md`.

## 4. The consultation — who was asked, and what each man said

### 4.1 الذہبی's composite report — the fullest list of names

الواقدی's three chains, merged by الذہبی («دخل حديث بعضهم في بعض»):

> أن أبا بكر لما ثقل دعا عبد الرحمن بن عوف، فقال: أخبرني عن عمر، فقال: ما تسألني عن أمر إلا وأنت أعلم به مني، قال: وإن، فقال: هو والله أفضل من رأيك فيه. ثم دعا عثمان فسأله عن عمر، فقال: علمي فيه أن سريرته خير من علانيته وأنه ليس فينا مثله فقال: يرحمك الله، والله لو تركته ما عدوتك، وشاور معهما سعيد بن زيد، وأسيد بن الحضير وغيرهما، فقال قائل: ما تقول لربك إذا سألك عن استخلافك عمر وقد ترى غلظته؟ فقال: أجلسوني، أبالله تخوفوني! أقول: استخلفت عليهم خير أهلك.
> — سیر أعلام النبلاء، ج الراشدون ص۱۶ · https://shamela.ws/book/10906/1164
> *"When Abū Bakr grew heavy with illness he called ʿAbd al-Raḥmān ibn ʿAwf and said: tell me about
> ʿUmar. He said: you ask me about nothing that you do not know better than I. He said: even so. He
> said: by God, he is better than your opinion of him. Then he called ʿUthmān and asked him about
> ʿUmar. He said: what I know of him is that his inward is better than his outward, and that there
> is none among us like him. He said: God have mercy on you — by God, had I left him, I would not
> have passed you over. And he consulted along with those two Saʿīd ibn Zayd and Usayd ibn
> [al-]Ḥuḍayr and others. Someone said: what will you say to your Lord when He asks you about
> appointing ʿUmar, when you see his harshness? He said: sit me up. Do you frighten me with God? I
> shall say: I appointed over them the best of Your people."*

> ⚠ **Typography.** The Shamela text prints **«أسيد بن الحضير»** with the article; the standard form
> of the name is **أسيد بن حُضَير ؓ**. Print the standard form, and note that the source page carries
> the variant.

**Names established by this page:** عبد الرحمن بن عوف ؓ · عثمان بن عفان ؓ · سعيد بن زيد ؓ ·
أسيد بن حضير ؓ · "and others". That is the answer to "whom did he consult", and it is one citation.

### 4.2 ابن الاثیر's version — where عمر ؓ's severity is answered

الکامل carries the same consultation with two things سیر does not have: **عبد الرحمن بن عوف ؓ names
the severity outright**, and **أبو بكر ؓ explains it**.

> لَمَّا نَزَلَ بِأَبِي بَكْرٍ - رَضِيَ اللَّهُ عَنْهُ - الْمَوْتُ دَعَا عَبْدَ الرَّحْمَنِ بْنَ عَوْفٍ فَقَالَ: أَخْبِرْنِي عَنْ عُمَرَ. فَقَالَ: إِنَّهُ أَفْضَلُ مِنْ رَأْيِكَ إِلَّا أَنَّهُ فِيهِ غِلْظَةٌ. فَقَالَ أَبُو بَكْرٍ: ذَلِكَ لِأَنَّهُ يَرَانِي رَقِيقًا، وَلَوْ أَفْضَى الْأَمْرُ إِلَيْهِ لَتَرَكَ كَثِيرًا مِمَّا هُوَ عَلَيْهِ، وَقَدْ رَمَقْتُهُ فَكُنْتُ إِذَا غَضِبْتُ عَلَى رَجُلٍ أَرَانِي الرِّضَاءَ عَنْهُ، وَإِذَا لِنْتُ لَهُ أَرَانِي الشِّدَّةَ عَلَيْهِ.
> — الکامل فی التاریخ ج۲ ص۲۶۶ · https://shamela.ws/book/21712/957
> *"When death came down upon Abū Bakr ؓ he called ʿAbd al-Raḥmān ibn ʿAwf and said: tell me about
> ʿUmar. He said: he is better than your opinion of him — except that there is harshness in him.
> Abū Bakr said: that is because he sees me being gentle. If the affair passed to him he would leave
> much of what he is now. I have watched him closely: when I was angry with a man he would show me
> mildness toward him, and when I was soft with a man he would show me severity toward him."*

**That is the single best passage in the strand for a room that will hear about عمر ؓ's severity all
evening.** أبو بكر ؓ is not denying the severity. He is saying that a deputy calibrates against his
chief, and that the calibration will change when the chief does.

Then عثمان ؓ, and a sentence about عثمان ؓ himself that is worth its own beat:

> فَقَالَ: سَرِيرَتُهُ خَيْرٌ مِنْ عَلَانِيَتِهِ، وَلَيْسَ فِينَا مِثْلُهُ. فَقَالَ أَبُو بَكْرٍ لَهُمَا: لَا تَذْكُرَا مِمَّا قُلْتُ لَكُمَا شَيْئًا، وَلَوْ تَرَكْتُهُ مَا عَدَوْتُ عُثْمَانَ، وَالْخِيرَةُ لَهُ أَنْ لَا يَلِيَ مِنْ أُمُورِكُمْ شَيْئًا، وَلَوَدِدْتُ أَنِّي كُنْتُ مِنْ أُمُورِكُمْ خِلْوًا، وَكُنْتُ فِيمَنْ مَضَى مِنْ سَلَفِكُمْ.
> — الکامل فی التاریخ ج۲ ص۲۶۷ · https://shamela.ws/book/21712/958
> *"He said: his inward is better than his outward, and there is none among us like him. Abū Bakr
> said to the two of them: mention nothing of what I have said to you. Had I left him, I would not
> have passed over ʿUthmān — and the better thing for him would be that he hold no office of yours
> at all. And I could wish that I were free of your affairs, and were among those of your
> predecessors who have gone."*

⚠ The clause «والخيرة له أن لا يلي من أموركم شيئا» reads, after 23 AH, like a foreshadowing. **Do not
present it that way.** It is a sentence about the burden of office, spoken by a man who has just
called the office a thing he wishes he were free of. Read it as that and stop.

### 4.3 طلحة بن عبيد الله ؓ raises it to his face

سیر gives this exchange with an unnamed «قائل» (§4.1). **الکامل names him.**

> وَدَخَلَ طَلْحَةُ بْنُ عُبَيْدِ اللَّهِ عَلَى أَبِي بَكْرٍ فَقَالَ: اسْتَخْلَفْتَ عَلَى النَّاسِ عُمَرَ وَقَدْ رَأَيْتَ مَا يَلْقَى النَّاسُ مِنْهُ وَأَنْتَ مَعَهُ، وَكَيْفَ بِهِ إِذَا خَلَا بِهِمْ وَأَنْتَ لَاقٍ رَبَّكَ فَسَائِلُكَ عَنْ رَعِيَّتِكَ! فَقَالَ أَبُو بَكْرٍ: أَجْلِسُونِي، فَأَجْلَسُوهُ، فَقَالَ: أَبِاللَّهِ تُخَوِّفُنِي! إِذَا لَقِيتُ رَبِّي فَسَأَلَنِي قُلْتُ: اسْتَخْلَفْتُ عَلَى أَهْلِكَ خَيْرَ أَهْلِكَ.
> — الکامل فی التاریخ ج۲ ص۲۶۷ · https://shamela.ws/book/21712/958
> *"Ṭalḥa ibn ʿUbayd Allāh came in to Abū Bakr and said: you have appointed ʿUmar over the people,
> and you have seen what the people meet from him while you are still with him — how will it be when
> he is alone with them? And you will meet your Lord, and He will question you about your flock. Abū
> Bakr said: sit me up. They sat him up. He said: do you frighten me with God? When I meet my Lord
> and He asks me, I shall say: I appointed over Your people the best of Your people."*

> **Disagreement between the two sources, recorded, not resolved:** سیر (الراشدون ص۱۶) gives the
> objector as an unnamed «قائل» inside the consultation; الکامل (ج۲ ص۲۶۷) gives it as a separate
> visit by **طلحة بن عبيد الله ؓ**. The wording of the reply is near-identical in both.
> **Safest delivery: "one of them objected" — and if pressed, say that الکامل names طلحة ؓ.**
> This is a Companion criticising a Companion's judgement, so it stays factual and un-adjudicated,
> exactly per `CLAUDE.md` §1.6.

## 5. The written عهد

### 5.1 The text, as سیر carries it — the fullest wording in the safe list

> ثم دعا عثمان، فقال: اكتب: بسم الله الرحمن الرحيم، هذا ما عهد أبو بكر بن أبي قحافة في آخر عهده بالدنيا خارجا منها، وعند أول عهده بالآخرة داخلا فيها، حيث يؤمن الكافر، ويوقن الفاجر، ويصدق الكاذب إني استخلفت عليكم بعدي عمر بن الخطاب فاسمعوا له وأطيعوا، وإني لم آل الله ورسوله ودينه ونفسي وإياكم خيرا، فإن عدل فذلك ظني به وعلمي فه، وإن بدل فلكل امرئ ما اكتسب، والخير أردت ولا أعلم الغيب {وَسَيَعْلَمُ الَّذِينَ ظَلَمُوا أَيَّ مُنْقَلَبٍ يَنْقَلِبُون} [الشعراء: ٢٢٧]
> — سیر أعلام النبلاء، ج الراشدون ص۱۶–۱۷ · https://shamela.ws/book/10906/1164 و https://shamela.ws/book/10906/1165
> *"In the name of God, the Merciful, the Compassionate. This is what Abū Bakr ibn Abī Quḥāfa
> covenants at the last of his time with this world, going out of it, and at the first of his time
> with the next, entering it — where the disbeliever believes, the corrupt man attains certainty,
> and the liar tells the truth. I have appointed over you after me ʿUmar ibn al-Khaṭṭāb, so hear him
> and obey. I have not fallen short toward God, His Messenger, His religion, myself or you in
> seeking good. If he deals justly, that is my opinion of him and what I know of him; and if he
> changes, then every man has what he has earned. Good is what I intended, and I do not know the
> unseen — 'and those who did wrong shall know by what a turning they will be turned'."*

> ⚠ **The quotation spans a printed page break.** Everything up to «وعند أول» is on ص۱۶; the text
> from «عهده بالآخرة» to the end is on ص۱۷. Cite both pages whenever the whole passage is used.

> ⚠ **Two typographic defects on these pages, both flagged rather than silently corrected:**
> **(a)** «وعلمي **فه**» is plainly a slip for «وعلمي **فيه**».
> **(b)** the sentence runs «ويصدق الكاذب إني استخلفت…» with no punctuation between the clauses.
> **Read this text off a printed سیر before any part of it reaches a slide.** The opening clause
> — *"where the disbeliever believes, the corrupt man attains certainty, and the liar tells the
> truth"* — is the most quotable line in the whole document and must be exactly right.

### 5.2 The عهد as الکامل carries it — shorter, and it shows the interruption

> ثُمَّ إِنَّ أَبَا بَكْرٍ أَحْضَرَ عُثْمَانَ بْنَ عَفَّانَ خَالِيًا لِيَكْتُبَ عَهْدَ عُمَرَ، فَقَالَ لَهُ: اكْتُبْ: بِسْمِ اللَّهِ الرَّحْمَنِ الرَّحِيمِ، هَذَا مَا عَهِدَ أَبُو بَكْرِ بْنُ أَبِي قُحَافَةَ إِلَى الْمُسْلِمِينَ، أَمَّا بَعْدُ ٠٠٠ - ثُمَّ أُغْمِيَ عَلَيْهِ - فَكَتَبَ عُثْمَانُ: أَمَّا بَعْدُ، فَإِنِّي قَدِ اسْتَخْلَفْتُ عَلَيْكُمْ عُمَرَ بْنَ الْخَطَّابِ، وَلَمْ آلُكُمْ خَيْرًا. ثُمَّ أَفَاقَ أَبُو بَكْرٍ فَقَالَ: اقْرَأْ عَلَيَّ. فَقَرَأَ عَلَيْهِ، فَكَبَّرَ أَبُو بَكْرٍ وَقَالَ: أَرَاكَ خِفْتَ أَنْ يَخْتَلِفَ النَّاسُ إِنْ مُتُّ فِي غَشْيَتِي. قَالَ: نَعَمْ. قَالَ: جَزَاكَ اللَّهُ خَيْرًا عَنِ الْإِسْلَامِ وَأَهْلِهِ.
> — الکامل فی التاریخ ج۲ ص۲۶۷ · https://shamela.ws/book/21712/958
> *"Then Abū Bakr brought ʿUthmān ibn ʿAffān in privately to write ʿUmar's covenant, and said to
> him: write — In the name of God, the Merciful, the Compassionate. This is what Abū Bakr ibn Abī
> Quḥāfa covenants to the Muslims. To proceed… — and then he lost consciousness. So ʿUthmān wrote:
> To proceed: I have appointed over you ʿUmar ibn al-Khaṭṭāb, and I have not fallen short toward
> you in good. Then Abū Bakr came round and said: read it to me. He read it to him, and Abū Bakr
> said takbīr and said: I see you feared the people would fall into disagreement if I died in my
> swoon. He said: yes. He said: may God reward you well on behalf of Islam and its people."*

⚠ The «٠٠٠» in the Shamela text is the **editor's** ellipsis marking where the dictation broke off,
not a lacuna in the manuscript reading. Do not reproduce the zeros on a slide.

### 5.3 The same incident, as سیر has it

> وقال بعضهم في الحديث: لما أن كتب عثمان الكتاب أغمي على أبي بكر، فكتب عثمان من عنده اسم عمر، فلما أفاق أبو بكر قال: اقرأ ما كتبت، فقرأ، فلما ذكر "عمر" كبر أبو بكر وقال: أراك خفت إن افتلتت نفسي الاختلاف، فجزاك الله عن الإسلام خيرا، والله إن كنت لها أهلا.
> — سیر أعلام النبلاء، ج الراشدون ص۱۷ · https://shamela.ws/book/10906/1165
> *"Some of them said in the report: when ʿUthmān was writing the document Abū Bakr fainted, so
> ʿUthmān wrote ʿUmar's name on his own initiative. When Abū Bakr came round he said: read me what
> you wrote. He read it, and when he mentioned 'ʿUmar', Abū Bakr said takbīr and said: I see you
> feared disagreement if my soul were taken suddenly. May God reward you well on behalf of Islam —
> and by God, you were fit for it."*

**Both books carry the incident, independently worded.** That matters for the lecture: this is not
one stray report. And the closing clause in سیر — «والله إن كنت لها أهلا» — is a Companion's
judgement on a Companion, quoted from the source, which is exactly the register `CLAUDE.md` asks for.

### 5.4 The ratification — the people are asked

> فَلَمَّا كُتِبَ الْعَهْدُ أَمَرَ بِهِ أَنْ يُقْرَأَ عَلَى النَّاسِ، فَجَمَعَهُمْ وَأَرْسَلَ الْكِتَابَ مَعَ مَوْلًى لَهُ وَمَعَهُ عُمَرُ، فَكَانَ عُمَرُ يَقُولُ لِلنَّاسِ: أَنْصِتُوا وَاسْمَعُوا لِخَلِيفَةِ رَسُولِ اللَّهِ - صَلَّى اللَّهُ عَلَيْهِ وَسَلَّمَ - فَإِنَّهُ لَمْ يَأْلُكُمْ نُصْحًا. فَسَكَنَ النَّاسُ، فَلَمَّا قُرِئَ عَلَيْهِمُ الْكِتَابُ سَمِعُوا وَأَطَاعُوا، وَكَانَ أَبُو بَكْرٍ أَشْرَفَ عَلَى النَّاسِ وَقَالَ: أَتَرْضُونَ بِمَنِ اسْتَخْلَفْتُ عَلَيْكُمْ؟ فَإِنِّي مَا اسْتَخْلَفْتُ عَلَيْكُمْ ذَا قَرَابَةٍ، وَإِنِّي قَدِ اسْتَخْلَفْتُ عَلَيْكُمْ عُمَرَ فَاسْمَعُوا لَهُ وَأَطِيعُوا، فَإِنِّي وَاللَّهِ مَا أَلَوْتُ مِنْ جُهْدِ الرَّأْيِ. فَقَالُوا: سَمِعْنَا وَأَطَعْنَا.
> — الکامل فی التاریخ ج۲ ص۲۶۷ · https://shamela.ws/book/21712/958
> *"When the covenant was written he ordered it read to the people. He gathered them and sent the
> document with a freedman of his, ʿUmar with him, and ʿUmar was saying to the people: be silent and
> listen to the Caliph of the Messenger of God ﷺ, for he has not fallen short in counselling you.
> The people settled, and when the document was read to them they heard and obeyed. And Abū Bakr
> looked out over the people and said: are you content with the one I have appointed over you? For I
> have not appointed over you a relative of mine. I have appointed ʿUmar over you, so hear him and
> obey — for by God, I have not fallen short of the utmost of my judgement. They said: we hear and
> we obey."*

**«فَإِنِّي مَا اسْتَخْلَفْتُ عَلَيْكُمْ ذَا قَرَابَةٍ» is the load-bearing clause of the whole strand.**
It is the one sentence that makes the session's later material — خلافت becoming ملوکیت — narratable
without the speaker having to draw a single inference himself. He reads the sentence; the room does
the rest.

### 5.5 The private وصية to عمر ؓ

> يَا عُمَرُ، إِنَّ لِلَّهِ حَقًّا بِاللَّيْلِ لَا يَقْبَلُهُ فِي النَّهَارِ، وَحَقًّا فِي النَّهَارِ لَا يَقْبَلُهُ بِاللَّيْلِ، وَإِنَّهُ لَا يَقْبَلُ نَافِلَةً حَتَّى تُؤَدَّى الْفَرِيضَةُ
> — الکامل فی التاریخ ج۲ ص۲۶۷ · https://shamela.ws/book/21712/958
> *"ʿUmar — God has a right by night that He does not accept by day, and a right by day that He
> does not accept by night; and He accepts no voluntary act until the obligation has been
> discharged."*

It runs on through the scales of the Day of Judgement and closes, on the following page:

> فَإِنْ حَفِظْتَ وَصِيَّتِي فَلَا يَكُونَنَّ غَائِبٌ أَحَبَّ إِلَيْكَ مِنْ حَاضِرٍ مِنَ الْمَوْتِ، وَلَسْتَ بِمُعْجِزِهِ.
> — الکامل فی التاریخ ج۲ ص۲۶۸ · https://shamela.ws/book/21712/959
> *"If you keep my counsel, then let no absent thing be dearer to you than the one present thing —
> death; and you will not escape it."*

⚠ The وصية **spans a page break**: it begins at ج۲ ص۲۶۷ and finishes at ج۲ ص۲۶۸. Cite both pages if
the middle (the passage on the scales) is used.

## 6. The accounting — what he ordered returned

This is the strongest عبرت in the whole session, and all of it is page-cited.

### 6.1 The instruction to عائشة ؓ

> قال: أما إنا منذ ولينا أمر المسلمين لم أنأكل لهم دينارا ولا درهما ولكنا أكلنا من جريش طعامهم في بطوننا، ولبسنا من خشن ثيابهم على ظهورنا، وليس عندنا من فيء المسلمين شيء إلا هذا العبد الحبشي وهذا البعير الناضح وجرد هذه القطيفة، فإذا مت فابعثي بهن إلى عمر، ففعلت.
> — سیر أعلام النبلاء، ج الراشدون ص۱۹ · https://shamela.ws/book/10906/1167
> *"He said: since we took charge of the Muslims' affair we have not eaten of theirs a dinar or a
> dirham; but we have eaten of the coarsest of their food in our bellies and worn the roughest of
> their clothing on our backs. We have nothing of the Muslims' fayʾ except this Abyssinian servant,
> this water-carrying camel, and the worn nap of this blanket. When I die, send them to ʿUmar. And
> she did."*

> ⚠ «لم **أنأكل**» is a typing slip in the Shamela text for «لم **نأكل**». Flag it; do not reproduce it.

A second version of the same instruction, through القاسم from عائشة ؓ, on the same page, names a milch
camel and the servant who used to burnish the Muslims' swords:

> وقال القاسم عن عائشة، أن أبا بكر حين حضره الموت قال: إني لا أعلم عند آل أبي بكر غير هذه اللقحة وغير هذا الغلام الصيقل، كان يعمل سيوف المسلمين ويخدمنا، فإذا مت فادفعيه إل عمر، فلما دفعته إلى عمر قال: رحم الله أبا بكر لقد أتعب من بعده.
> — سیر أعلام النبلاء، ج الراشدون ص۱۹ · https://shamela.ws/book/10906/1167
> *"Abū Bakr, when death came to him, said: I know of nothing with the family of Abū Bakr but this
> milch camel and this burnisher-boy, who used to work the Muslims' swords and serve us. When I die,
> hand him over to ʿUmar. When she handed him over to ʿUmar he said: God have mercy on Abū Bakr —
> he has exhausted whoever comes after him."*
>
> ⚠ «فادفعيه **إل** عمر» is a typing slip in the Shamela text for «فادفعيه **إلى** عمر». Flag it;
> do not reproduce it.

### 6.2 عمر ؓ receiving it, and refusing to soften it

> فَلَمَّا رَآهُ بَكَى حَتَّى سَالَتْ دُمُوعُهُ إِلَى الْأَرْضِ وَجَعَلَ يَقُولُ: رَحِمَ اللَّهُ أَبَا بَكْرٍ! لَقَدْ أَتْعَبَ مَنْ بَعْدَهُ. وَيُكَرِّرُ ذَلِكَ. وَأَمَرَ بِرَفْعِهِ. فَقَالَ عَبْدُ الرَّحْمَنِ بْنُ عَوْفٍ: سُبْحَانَ اللَّهِ! تَسْلُبُ عِيَالَ أَبِي بَكْرٍ عَبْدًا وَنَاضِحًا وَسَحَقَ قَطِيفَةٍ ثَمَنُهَا خَمْسَةُ دَرَاهِمَ، فَلَوْ أَمَرْتَ بِرَدِّهَا عَلَيْهِمْ. فَقَالَ: لَا وَالَّذِي بَعَثَ مُحَمَّدًا - صَلَّى اللَّهُ عَلَيْهِ وَسَلَّمَ - لَا يَكُونُ هَذَا فِي وِلَايَتِي، وَلَا خَرَجَ أَبُو بَكْرٍ مِنْهُ وَأَتَقَلَّدُهُ أَنَا.
> — الکامل فی التاریخ ج۲ ص۲۶۵ · https://shamela.ws/book/21712/956
> *"When he saw it he wept until his tears ran to the ground, saying over and over: God have mercy
> on Abū Bakr — he has exhausted whoever comes after him. And he ordered it taken in. ʿAbd al-Raḥmān
> ibn ʿAwf said: glory be to God — will you strip Abū Bakr's family of a servant, a water-camel and
> a threadbare blanket worth five dirhams? Order it returned to them. He said: no, by the One who
> sent Muḥammad ﷺ — this shall not happen under my authority. Abū Bakr did not get clear of it for
> me to hang it round my own neck."*

### 6.3 The salary repaid out of his own land

> وَأَمَرَ أَبُو بَكْرٍ أَنْ يُرَدَّ جَمِيعُ مَا أَخَذَ مِنْ بَيْتِ الْمَالِ لِنَفَقَتِهِ بَعْدَ وَفَاتِهِ.
> — الکامل فی التاریخ ج۲ ص۲۶۵ · https://shamela.ws/book/21712/956
> *"And Abū Bakr ordered that everything he had taken from the treasury for his maintenance be

> — الکامل فی التاریخ ج۲ ص۲۶۶ · https://shamela.ws/book/21712/957
> *"When death came to him he directed that land of his be sold and its price paid over in

### 6.4 The treasury, opened

> وَلَمَّا تُوُفِّيَ أَبُو بَكْرٍ جَمَعَ عُمَرُ الْأُمَنَاءِ وَفَتَحَ بَيْتَ الْمَالِ، فَلَمْ يَجِدُوا فِيهِ شَيْئًا غَيْرَ دِينَارٍ سَقَطَ مِنْ غِرَارَةٍ، فَتَرَحَّمُوا عَلَيْهِ.
> — الکامل فی التاریخ ج۲ ص۲۶۵ · https://shamela.ws/book/21712/956
> *"When Abū Bakr died, ʿUmar gathered the trustees and opened the treasury. They found nothing in
> it but a single dinar that had fallen out of a sack. And they asked God's mercy on him."*

### 6.5 The salary itself, and why the figures do not agree

He is the first ruler whose people voted him a maintenance:

> وَكَانَ أَوَّلَ وَالٍ فَرَضَ لَهُ رَعِيَّتُهُ نَفَقَتَهُ، وَأَوَّلَ خَلِيفَةٍ وَلِيَ وَأَبُوهُ حَيٌّ، وَأَوَّلَ مَنْ سَمَّى مُصْحَفَ الْقُرْآنِ مُصْحَفًا، وَأَوَّلَ مَنْ سُمِّيَ خَلِيفَةً.
> — الکامل فی التاریخ ج۲ ص۲۶۶ · https://shamela.ws/book/21712/957
> *"He was the first ruler for whom his subjects fixed a maintenance, the first caliph to hold
> office while his father was alive, the first to call the codex of the Qurʾān a muṣḥaf, and the
> first to be called khalīfa."*

**The amount is not fixed by our sources.** See §7.

## 7. Numbers, and why they are unreliable

Early figures of this kind are **transmitted, not audited.** Give them as the source gives them,
name the source, and do not average them.

### 7.1 The length of the caliphate — four figures in three approved books

| **سنتين وأربعة أشهر إلا أربع ليال** — 2 years 4 months less 4 nights (أبو معشر) | سیر أعلام النبلاء | الراشدون ص۱۶ |
| **سنتين وثلاثة أشهر وعشر ليال** — 2 years 3 months 10 nights | الکامل | ج۲ ص۲۶۲ |
| **سنتين وأربعة أشهر إلا أربع ليال** — the same as أبو معشر's, given as the alternative | الکامل | ج۲ ص۲۶۲ |

> وَكَانَتْ خِلَافَتُهُ سَنَتَيْنِ وَثَلَاثَةَ أَشْهُرٍ وَعَشْرَ لَيَالٍ، وَقِيلَ: كَانَتْ سَنَتَيْنِ وَأَرْبَعَةَ أَشْهُرٍ إِلَّا أَرْبَعَ لَيَالٍ، وَكَانَ مَوْلِدُهُ بَعْدَ الْفِيلِ بِثَلَاثِ سِنِينَ.
> — الکامل فی التاریخ ج۲ ص۲۶۲ · https://shamela.ws/book/21712/953
> *"His caliphate was two years, three months and ten nights; and it is said two years and four
> months less four nights. His birth was three years after the Year of the Elephant."*

> **Recommended for the slide:** the headline number is **"about two years and three months"**
> `[SOURCED]` (البدایہ ج۷ ص۱۰۲), with a footnote that the books give figures between 2 years 100 days
> and 2 years 4 months. **Never print a single figure to the day.** The honest version is more
> impressive than the false precision: *the entire first caliphate ran a little over two years — and
> in it the peninsula was held and Syria and Iraq were entered.*

### 7.2 The salary

| Figure | Source | Page |
|---|---|---|
| **ستة آلاف درهم** a year | الکامل | ج۲ ص۲۶۶ |
| **شطر شاة** a day — half a sheep | سیر (عطاء بن السائب) | الراشدون ص۱۴ |
| **ألفين وخمس مائة** | سیر (ميمون بن مهران) | الراشدون ص۱۴ |
| «فرضوا له ما يكفيه» — "they fixed him what sufficed him" | الکامل | ج۲ ص۲۶۶ |

> ⚠ **Correction to an earlier reading of this page.** The عطاء بن السائب report continues
> «وماكسوه في الرأس والبطن» — the verb is **ماكس** (to haggle), *not* كسا (to clothe).
> It means they haggled with him over the head and the belly, i.e. over which parts of the sheep the
> allowance covered. **It is not a clothing allowance.** Do not repeat that gloss.

Three incompatible figures. **The usable fact is not the amount; it is that there was an amount, and
that he ordered every unit of it repaid out of his own land.** Say the second, not the first.

**because ابن الاثیر tells it**, and it should be framed that way: *this is what the book says the
trustees found.*

## 8. The death itself, the burial, and the handover

### 8.1 عائشة ؓ at the bedside

> وقال محمد بن عمرو بن علقمة بن وقاص، عن أبيه، عن جده، أن عائشة قالت: حضرت أبي وهو يموت فأخذته غشية فتمثلت:
> من لا يزال دمعه مقنعا ... فإنه لا بد مرة مدفوق
> فرفع راسه وقال: يا بنية ليس كذاك، ولكن كما قال الله تعالى: {وَجَاءَتْ سَكْرَةُ الْمَوْتِ بِالْحَقِّ ذَلِكَ مَا كُنْتَ مِنْهُ تَحِيدُ} [ق: ١٩]
> — سیر أعلام النبلاء، ج الراشدون ص۱۸ · https://shamela.ws/book/10906/1166
> *"ʿĀʾisha ؓ said: I was present with my father as he was dying, and a faintness took him, so I
> recited a line of verse — 'whoever's tears are held back, they must one day be poured out.' He
> lifted his head and said: little daughter, it is not so; rather it is as God said — 'and the
> agony of death comes with the truth: that is what you were shying away from.'"*

⚠ «راسه» is a slip for «رأسه» in the Shamela text.

الذہبی gives a second wording of the same moment, and ابن الاثیر gives the same second wording:

> لعمرك ما يغني الثراء عن الفتى ... إذا حشرجت يوما وضاق بها الصدر
> — سیر أعلام النبلاء، ج الراشدون ص۱۸ · https://shamela.ws/book/10906/1166 · and الکامل ج۲ ص۲۶۵ · https://shamela.ws/book/21712/956
> *"By your life, wealth avails a man nothing when the death-rattle comes one day and the breast is
> straitened by it."*

**The teaching point is exact and needs no ornament: the daughter reached for poetry; the father
corrected her to the Qurʾān.** That is a Companion's instinct under the last pressure there is.

### 8.2 The last words

> وَكَانَ آخِرُ مَا تَكَلَّمَ بِهِ: تَوَفَّنِي مُسْلِمًا وَأَلْحِقْنِي بِالصَّالِحِينَ.
> — الکامل فی التاریخ ج۲ ص۲۶۳ · https://shamela.ws/book/21712/954
> *"The last thing he spoke was: take me as a Muslim, and join me to the righteous."*

> ⚠ **This is ابن الاثیر's alone among the pages fetched in this pass.** It was **not** found in
> سیر الراشدون ص۱۴–۲۰ nor anywhere in البدایہ's cached pages. الکامل is on the safe list, so it may
> be used — **but it must be attributed to الکامل ج۲ ص۲۶۳, not to "the books" in general.**

### 8.3 The shroud

> وَأَوْصَى أَنْ تُغَسِّلَهُ زَوْجَتُهُ أَسْمَاءُ بِنْتُ عُمَيْسٍ وَابْنُهُ عَبْدُ الرَّحْمَنِ، وَأَنْ يُكَفَّنَ فِي ثَوْبَيْهِ وَيُشْتَرَى مَعَهُمَا ثَوْبٌ ثَالِثٌ، وَقَالَ: الْحَيُّ أَحْوَجُ إِلَى الْجَدِيدِ مِنَ الْمَيِّتِ، إِنَّمَا هُوَ لِلْمُهْلَةِ وَالصَّدِيدِ.
> — الکامل فی التاریخ ج۲ ص۲۶۲ · https://shamela.ws/book/21712/953
> *"He directed that his wife Asmāʾ bint ʿUmays and his son ʿAbd al-Raḥmān should wash him, and that
> he be shrouded in his own two garments with a third bought to go with them, and said: the living
> have more need of the new than the dead — this is only for the decay."*

الذہبی corroborates the washing:

> وقال الزهري: أوصى أبو بكر أن تغسله امرأته أسماء بنت عميس، فإن لم تستطع استعانت بابنه عبد الرحمن.
> — سیر أعلام النبلاء، ج الراشدون ص۱۹ · https://shamela.ws/book/10906/1167
> *"al-Zuhrī said: Abū Bakr directed that his wife Asmāʾ bint ʿUmays should wash him, and if she
> could not manage it she should be helped by his son ʿAbd al-Raḥmān."*

### 8.4 The burial

> وَدُفِنَ لَيْلًا وَصَلَّى عَلَيْهِ عُمَرُ بْنُ الْخَطَّابِ فِي مَسْجِدِ رَسُولِ اللَّهِ - صَلَّى اللَّهُ عَلَيْهِ وَسَلَّمَ - وَكَبَّرَ عَلَيْهِ أَرْبَعًا، وَحُمِلَ عَلَى السَّرِيرِ الَّذِي حُمِلَ عَلَيْهِ رَسُولُ اللَّهِ - صَلَّى اللَّهُ عَلَيْهِ وَسَلَّمَ - وَدَخَلَ قَبْرَهُ ابْنُهُ عَبْدُ الرَّحْمَنِ وَعُمَرُ وَعُثْمَانُ وَطَلْحَةُ، وَجُعِلَ رَأْسُهُ عِنْدَ كَتِفَيِّ النَّبِيِّ - صَلَّى اللَّهُ عَلَيْهِ وَسَلَّمَ - وَأَلْصَقُوا لَحْدَهُ بِلَحْدِ النَّبِيِّ - صَلَّى اللَّهُ عَلَيْهِ وَسَلَّمَ - وَجُعِلَ قَبْرُهُ مِثْلَ قَبْرِ النَّبِيِّ - صَلَّى اللَّهُ عَلَيْهِ وَسَلَّمَ - مُسَطَّحًا.
> — الکامل فی التاریخ ج۲ ص۲۶۲ · https://shamela.ws/book/21712/953
> *"He was buried at night. ʿUmar ibn al-Khaṭṭāb prayed over him in the Mosque of the Messenger of
> God ﷺ and said four takbīrs. He was carried on the same bier on which the Messenger of God ﷺ had
> been carried. Into his grave went down his son ʿAbd al-Raḥmān, ʿUmar, ʿUthmān and Ṭalḥa. His head
> was placed at the Prophet's ﷺ shoulders, and they joined his laḥd to the Prophet's ﷺ laḥd, and his
> grave was made flat, like the Prophet's ﷺ grave."*

سیر, on the same, twice:

> وعن القاسم، قال: أوصى أبو بكر أن يدفن إلى جنب رسول الله صلى الله عليه وسلم فحفر له، وجعل رأسه عند كتفي رسول الله صلى الله عليه وسلم.
> — سیر أعلام النبلاء، ج الراشدون ص۱۹ · https://shamela.ws/book/10906/1167
> *"al-Qāsim said: Abū Bakr directed that he be buried beside the Messenger of God ﷺ. It was dug for
> him, and his head was placed at the Messenger of God's ﷺ shoulders."*

> وقالت عائشة: مات ليلة الثلاثاء، ودفن قبل أن يصبح.
> — سیر أعلام النبلاء، ج الراشدون ص۱۹ · https://shamela.ws/book/10906/1167
> *"ʿĀʾisha ؓ said: he died on the night of Tuesday, and was buried before morning came."*

And the sentence that fixes all three graves in one line — usable directly on a diagram:

> وعن عامر بن عبد الله بن الزبير قال: رأس أبي بكر عند كتفي رسول الله صلى الله عليه وسلم، ورأس عمر عند حقوي أبي بكر.
> — سیر أعلام النبلاء، ج الراشدون ص۱۹ · https://shamela.ws/book/10906/1167
> *"ʿĀmir ibn ʿAbd Allāh ibn al-Zubayr said: Abū Bakr's head is at the shoulders of the Messenger of
> God ﷺ, and ʿUmar's head is at Abū Bakr's waist."*

### 8.5 علي ؓ over the shrouded body

> وقال عبد الواحد بن أيمن وغيرهم، عن أبي جعفر الباقر، قال: دخل علي على أبي بكر بعد ما سجي، فقال: ما أحد ألقى الله بصحيفته أحب إلي من هذا المسجي.
> — سیر أعلام النبلاء، ج الراشدون ص۱۹ · https://shamela.ws/book/10906/1167
> *"ʿAlī came in to Abū Bakr after he had been laid out, and said: there is no one whose record I
> would rather meet God with than this man lying here."*

This is transmitted **through أبو جعفر الباقر رحمہ اللہ** — which is worth naming aloud, once,
because it makes the report harder to dismiss and it does the guardrail's work without any argument.

### 8.6 عمر ؓ's first words as caliph

> وَتُوُفِّيَ أَبُو بَكْرٍ، فَلَمَّا دُفِنَ صَعِدَ عُمَرُ بْنُ الْخَطَّابِ فَخَطَبَ النَّاسَ ثُمَّ قَالَ: إِنَّمَا مَثَلُ الْعَرَبِ مَثَلُ جَمَلٍ أَنِفٍ اتَّبَعَ قَائِدَهُ، فَلْيَنْظُرْ قَائِدُهُ حَيْثُ يَقُودُهُ، وَأَمَّا أَنَا فَوَرَبِّ الْكَعْبَةِ لَأَحْمِلَنَّكُمْ عَلَى الطَّرِيقِ!
> — الکامل فی التاریخ ج۲ ص۲۶۸ · https://shamela.ws/book/21712/959
> *"Abū Bakr died, and when he had been buried ʿUmar ibn al-Khaṭṭāb went up and addressed the people
> and said: the Arabs are only like a nose-ringed camel that follows its driver — so let its driver
> look where he is leading it. As for me, by the Lord of the Kaʿba, I shall carry you on the road."*

### 8.7 The title, and the first act

> وهو أوَّلُ من سُمِّي بامير المؤمنين، وكان أول من حيّاه بها المغيرةُ بن شعبة، وقيل غيره
> — البدایہ والنہایہ ج۷ ص۱۰۲ · https://shamela.ws/book/30097/3248
> *"He was the first to be called Commander of the Believers, and the first to greet him with it was
> al-Mughīra ibn Shuʿba — though others are named."*

⚠ «بامير» is a slip for «بأمير» in the Shamela text.

The first executive act — the recall of خالد ؓ from command in Syria — is at البدایہ ج۷ ص۱۰۳
(https://shamela.ws/book/30097/3249) and الکامل ج۲ ص۲۶۸ (https://shamela.ws/book/21712/959).
**That belongs to the عمر ؓ strand, not this one**; it is flagged here only so the next pass does not
go looking for it.

### 8.8 The news reaching the front

> وقد كتب بوفاة الصدِّيق إلى أمراء الشام مع شدَّاد بن أوس، ومَحْميَة بن جَزْء (٥)، فوصلا والناس مصافون جيوش الروم يوم اليرموك كما قدمنا.
> — البدایہ والنہایہ ج۷ ص۱۰۲ · https://shamela.ws/book/30097/3248
> *"Word of the Ṣiddīq's death was written to the commanders in Syria with Shaddād ibn Aws and
> Maḥmiya ibn Jazʾ. The two arrived while the people were drawn up facing the armies of the Romans
> at Yarmūk, as we have said."*

And what happened on that field the next morning, five pages earlier:

> وما فرحوا بما وجدوا بقدر حزنهم على الصدِّيق حين أعلمهم خالد بذلك، ولكن عوضهم الله بالفاروق ﵁.
> — البدایہ والنہایہ ج۷ ص۹۷ · https://shamela.ws/book/30097/3243
> *"Their joy at what they found was not equal to their grief for the Ṣiddīq when Khālid told them
> of it — but God gave them the Fārūq ؓ in exchange."*

**One sentence carries the whole handover.** It is also the natural join between this strand and the
اليرموك strand: the same page holds both.

### 9.3 The blind woman — and a real trap

الکامل ج۲ ص۲۶۵ (https://shamela.ws/book/21712/956), from أبو صالح الغفاري, tells that عمر ؓ used to

> **These are two distinct reports about two different men, in two different books.** They are close
> enough in shape that a speaker under pressure will fuse them. **If either is told, name the book
> and name the man, and tell only one of them in an evening.**

# EVENT CARDS

Strand letter: **B** (أبو بكر ؓ — the death and the handover).

### E-B1 · The bath on a cold day
**Tier:** CORE · **When:** جمادى الآخرة 13 AH `[SOURCED]` · **Map:** nothing moves. The pin stays on
المدینہ while the two arrows — الشام and العراق — keep running off the edge of the frame. That
contrast is the point: the man is in a room; the state is at two horizons.
**What happened:** He washed on a cold day. The fever came, and it did not leave. For fifteen days he
did not walk out to the mosque. He sent عمر ؓ to lead the prayer in his place. People came in to sit
with him, and عثمان ؓ came more than anyone. On the fifteenth day the state that had held Arabia
together was being run from a bed.
**The statement:**
> وعن عائشة قالت: أول ما بدئ مرض أبي بكر أنه اغتسل، وكان يوما باردًا فحم خمسة عشر يوما لا يخرج إلى صلاة، وكان يأمر عمر بالصلاة، وكانوا يعودونه، وكان عثمان ألزمهم له في مرضه.
> — سیر أعلام النبلاء، ج الراشدون ص۱۶ · https://shamela.ws/book/10906/1164
> *English:* "ʿĀʾisha ؓ said: the beginning of Abū Bakr's illness was that he bathed on a cold day
> and was feverish for fifteen days, not going out to the prayer. He would order ʿUmar to lead the
> prayer. People visited him, and ʿUthmān was the most constant of them at his side."
**عبرت:** A man may be carrying a whole community and still be, that week, only a sick man in a room.
**Hands-up?** no

### E-B2 · "The doctor has already been"
**Tier:** CORE · **When:** during the fifteen days `[SOURCED]` · **Map:** nothing moves.
**What happened:** They asked whether they should call a physician for him. He told them the
physician had already come. They asked what he had said. He said: *I do what I will.* They understood
who he meant. Nobody said anything more about doctors.
**The statement:**
> وَلَمَّا مَرِضَ قَالَ لَهُ النَّاسُ: أَلَا نَدْعُوَ الطَّبِيبَ؟ قَالَ: قَدْ أَتَانِي وَقَالَ لِي: أَنَا فَاعِلٌ مَا أُرِيدُ. فَعَلِمُوا مُرَادَهُ وَسَكَتُوا عَنْهُ، ثُمَّ مَاتَ.
> — الکامل فی التاریخ ج۲ ص۲۶۲ · https://shamela.ws/book/21712/953
> *English:* "When he fell ill the people said to him: shall we not call the physician? He said: he
> has already come to me, and he said to me — I do what I will. They understood what he meant, fell
> silent, and then he died."
**عبرت:** A man who has spent his life in submission does not change his vocabulary when he is dying.
**Hands-up?** no

### E-B3 · He asks عبد الرحمن بن عوف ؓ about عمر ؓ
**Tier:** CORE · **When:** the last days of the illness `[SOURCED]` · **Map:** nothing moves.
**What happened:** He called عبد الرحمن بن عوف ؓ in and asked him to tell him about عمر ؓ. عبد الرحمن
ؓ said what everyone in المدینہ already thought: he is better than you think — except there is
harshness in him. أبو بكر ؓ did not argue with the description. He explained it. *He is hard because
he sees me being soft. When I was angry with a man, عمر showed me leniency; when I was gentle with a
man, عمر showed me severity. Put the office in his hands and much of that will go.*
**The statement:**
> فَقَالَ: إِنَّهُ أَفْضَلُ مِنْ رَأْيِكَ إِلَّا أَنَّهُ فِيهِ غِلْظَةٌ. فَقَالَ أَبُو بَكْرٍ: ذَلِكَ لِأَنَّهُ يَرَانِي رَقِيقًا، وَلَوْ أَفْضَى الْأَمْرُ إِلَيْهِ لَتَرَكَ كَثِيرًا مِمَّا هُوَ عَلَيْهِ
> — الکامل فی التاریخ ج۲ ص۲۶۶ · https://shamela.ws/book/21712/957
> *English:* "He said: he is better than your opinion of him — except that there is harshness in
> him. Abū Bakr said: that is because he sees me being gentle. If the affair passed to him he would
> leave much of what he is now."
**عبرت:** A man's edge is often the shape of the gap he thinks he is filling.
**Hands-up?** Before telling it: *"Hands up — who here has worked with someone whose hardness was a
response to somebody else's softness?"*

**What happened:** Somebody came in and said it to his face. *You have put عمر over the people. You

### E-B6 · The pen keeps writing while he is unconscious
**Tier:** CORE · **When:** the last days `[SOURCED]` · **Map:** nothing moves.
**What happened:** He brought عثمان ؓ in privately and began dictating. *In the name of God, the
Merciful, the Compassionate. This is what Abū Bakr ibn Abī Quḥāfa covenants to the Muslims. To
proceed —* and there he lost consciousness. عثمان ؓ did not put the pen down and did not wait. He
wrote عمر ؓ's name himself. When أبو بكر ؓ came round he asked for it to be read back. When he heard
the name he said "Allāhu akbar", and then: *I see you were afraid the people would split if I died in
that faint.* عثمان ؓ said yes. He said: may God reward you well on behalf of Islam.
**The statement:**
> ثُمَّ أَفَاقَ أَبُو بَكْرٍ فَقَالَ: اقْرَأْ عَلَيَّ. فَقَرَأَ عَلَيْهِ، فَكَبَّرَ أَبُو بَكْرٍ وَقَالَ: أَرَاكَ خِفْتَ أَنْ يَخْتَلِفَ النَّاسُ إِنْ مُتُّ فِي غَشْيَتِي. قَالَ: نَعَمْ. قَالَ: جَزَاكَ اللَّهُ خَيْرًا عَنِ الْإِسْلَامِ وَأَهْلِهِ.
> — الکامل فی التاریخ ج۲ ص۲۶۷ · https://shamela.ws/book/21712/958
> *English:* "Then Abū Bakr came round and said: read it to me. He read it to him, and Abū Bakr said
> takbīr and said: I see you feared the people would fall into disagreement if I died in my swoon. He
> said: yes. He said: may God reward you well on behalf of Islam and its people."
**عبرت:** He was not angry that a man had acted without instruction — he thanked him, because the man
had been thinking about the community and not about himself.
**Hands-up?** Before telling it: *"Hands up — if your chief fell unconscious mid-sentence, would you
finish the sentence for him?"*
> Corroboration in the second source, same incident, different wording:
> سیر الراشدون ص۱۷ (https://shamela.ws/book/10906/1165) — «فكتب عثمان من عنده اسم عمر … والله إن كنت
> لها أهلا».

### E-B7 · "I have not appointed a relative over you"
**Tier:** CORE · **When:** the last days `[SOURCED]` · **Map:** nothing moves; المدینہ's mosque fills.
**What happened:** The document was finished and he ordered it read out. The people were gathered.

listen. It was read. They said: we hear and we obey. Then أبو بكر ؓ looked out over them himself and

### E-B8 · The covenant itself
**Tier:** GOOD · **When:** the last days `[SOURCED]` · **Map:** nothing moves. Full-bleed slide: the
Arabic, large, and nothing else.
**What happened:** The wording he dictated is preserved. It opens by placing itself at a specific
moment — the last hour a man has of this world and the first hour he has of the next, the hour when
the disbeliever believes and the liar tells the truth. Then, in that frame, one sentence of business:
I have appointed ʿUmar; hear him and obey. And then a clause that most rulers would have left out —
*if he changes, every man has what he has earned. Good is what I intended. I do not know the unseen.*
**The statement:**
> هذا ما عهد أبو بكر بن أبي قحافة في آخر عهده بالدنيا خارجا منها، وعند أول عهده بالآخرة داخلا فيها، حيث يؤمن الكافر، ويوقن الفاجر، ويصدق الكاذب
> — سیر أعلام النبلاء، ج الراشدون ص۱۶–۱۷ · https://shamela.ws/book/10906/1164 و https://shamela.ws/book/10906/1165

> going out of it, and at the first of his time with the next, entering it — where the disbeliever
> believes, the corrupt man attains certainty, and the liar tells the truth."
**عبرت:** He wrote his own limits into the appointment: I chose, I meant well, and I do not know what

### E-B9 · The list of what he owned
**Tier:** CORE · **When:** the last day `[SOURCED]` · **Map:** nothing moves.
**What happened:** He called عائشة ؓ. He told her that since the day he took charge of the Muslims'
affair he had not eaten a dinar or a dirham of theirs — only the coarsest of their food, only the
roughest of their clothing. Then he listed what was in the house that belonged to the Muslims and not

> وليس عندنا من فيء المسلمين شيء إلا هذا العبد الحبشي وهذا البعير الناضح وجرد هذه القطيفة، فإذا مت فابعثي بهن إلى عمر، ففعلت.

> camel, and the worn nap of this blanket. When I die, send them to ʿUmar. And she did."
**عبرت:** He kept an account of what was not his, and he closed it before he died.

### E-B10 · عمر ؓ weeps, and refuses to give it back
**Tier:** CORE · **When:** after his death `[SOURCED]` · **Map:** nothing moves.

### E-B15 · Monday night, and a grave already dug beside another
**Tier:** CORE · **When:** Monday evening, 8 nights remaining of جمادى الآخرة 13 AH `[SOURCED]` ·
**Map:** the pin on المدینہ. Then, on the diagram of the three graves: three positions, one behind
the other.
**What happened:** He died on the Monday evening. He had asked to be buried beside the
Messenger of God ﷺ, so they dug there. عمر ؓ prayed over him in the Prophet's ﷺ mosque, four takbīrs.
They carried him on the same bier the Prophet ﷺ had been carried on. Four men went down into the
grave: his son عبد الرحمن ؓ, عمر ؓ, عثمان ؓ, and طلحة ؓ. They laid his head at the Prophet's ﷺ
shoulders and put his niche against the Prophet's ﷺ niche. He was buried before morning came. He was
sixty-three — the age at which the Prophet ﷺ had died.
**The statement:**
> وقالت عائشة: مات ليلة الثلاثاء، ودفن قبل أن يصبح.

### E-B19 · عمر ؓ takes the minbar
**Tier:** GOOD · **When:** the day of the burial `[SOURCED]` · **Map:** المدینہ.
**What happened:** Once أبو بكر ؓ was in the ground, عمر ؓ went up and addressed the people. He did

### E-B20 · The وصية to عمر ؓ — the two rights and the scales
**Tier:** GOOD · **When:** after the covenant was read `[SOURCED]` · **Map:** nothing moves.
**What happened:** He had عمر ؓ brought in and told him he had appointed him over the Companions of
the Messenger of God ﷺ. Then he gave him a piece of advice that had nothing to do with governing.

the one present thing — death — and you will not escape it.

> — الکامل فی التاریخ ج۲ ص۲۶۷ · https://shamela.ws/book/21712/958
> *English:* "ʿUmar — God has a right by night that He does not accept by day, and a right by day

**عبرت:** The handover instruction to the most powerful man alive was about the five prayers.
**Hands-up?** no

### E-B21 · His father outlives him

### E-B22 · He kept milking their goats
**Tier:** GOOD · **When:** the first six months of the caliphate `[SOURCED]` · **Map:** السُّنْح, the
outlying quarter — then the move into المدینہ proper.
**What happened:** For the first six months after the بیعت he went on living at السُّنْح with his wife
and walking into المدینہ on foot to lead the prayer, and he went on going to the market to buy and
sell. He also used to milk the neighbourhood's sheep for them. When the بیعت happened a girl of the
quarter said out loud: well, he won't be milking our sheep for us now. He heard her, and answered her
directly. Six months later he gave up trade — *a people's affairs are not put right alongside a
business* — and moved into the city.
**The statement:**
> فَسَمِعَهَا فَقَالَ: بَلَى لَعَمْرِي لَأَحْلِبَنَّهَا لَكُمْ، وَإِنِّي لَأَرْجُو أَنْ لَا يُغَيِّرَ بِي مَا دَخَلْتُ فِيهِ. فَكَانَ يَحْلِبُ لَهُمْ.
> — الکامل فی التاریخ ج۲ ص۲۶۶ · https://shamela.ws/book/21712/957
> *English:* "He heard her and said: on the contrary — by my life, I shall milk them for you; and I
> hope that what I have entered into will not change me. And he went on milking for them."
**عبرت:** He said out loud, on the first week, the thing office usually takes from a man — and then he
proved it for two years.

the same person."*
