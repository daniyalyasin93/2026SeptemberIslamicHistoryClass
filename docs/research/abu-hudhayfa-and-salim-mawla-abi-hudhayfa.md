# ⁨أبو حذيفة بن عتبة ؓ⁩ and ⁨سالم مولى أبي حذيفة ؓ⁩ — one household, from ⁨بدر⁩ to ⁨اليمامة⁩

**Question this note answers:** Daniyal's observation is that the same families recur across the
evenings, and that the ⁨عبرت⁩ lives there. This note establishes, from pages, the one household that
carries the point most cleanly: **a Qurashī nobleman whose father and brother were killed at ⁨بدر⁩
fighting against the Muslims, and the freed Persian slave of his own house who led the Muhājirūn —
⁨عمر ؓ⁩ among them — in prayer because he carried the most Qurʾān.** They died on the same field at
⁨اليمامة⁩ in ⁨۱۲ھ⁩, and the last thing the freedman asked was to be laid down beside his master.

Specifically: who was whose; what the sources say about ⁨أبو حذيفة ؓ⁩ at ⁨بدر⁩ (the ⁨القليب⁩, and the
sentence he never forgave himself); how ⁨سالم ؓ⁩'s ⁨ولاء⁩ came about and whose it was; the imāmate at
⁨قُباء⁩; whether ⁨سالم ؓ⁩ is named in the "take the Qurʾān from four" ḥadīth and on which page; the
marriage and ⁨عائشة ؓ⁩'s transmission about ⁨رضاعة الكبير⁩ (recorded, **not** adjudicated); what each
man did and said at ⁨اليمامة⁩; and the report that ⁨عمر ؓ⁩ said he would have entrusted the office to
سالم ؓ.

## 2. ⁨أبو حذيفة بن عتبة ؓ⁩ — who he was

### 2.1 The heading ⁨الذہبی⁩ gives him

> <div dir="rtl">⁨السَّيِّدُ الكَبِيْرُ، الشَّهِيْدُ، أَبُو حُذَيْفَةَ ابْنُ شَيْخِ الجَاهِلِيَّةِ⁩: ⁨عُتْبَةَ بنِ رَبِيْعَةَ بنِ عَبْدِ شَمْسٍ بنِ عَبْدِ مَنَافٍ بنِ قُصَيِّ بنِ كِلاَبٍ القُرَشِيُّ، العَبْشَمِيُّ، البَدْرِيُّ⁩.</div>
>
> — ⁨سیر أعلام النبلاء ج۱ ص۱۶۴⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/10906/1590
> *"The great chief, the martyr, Abū Ḥudhayfa — son of the elder of the Jāhiliyya, ʿUtba b. Rabīʿa
> b. ʿAbd Shams b. ʿAbd Manāf b. Quṣayy b. Kilāb — al-Qurashī, al-ʿAbshamī, the man of Badr."*

**This one line is the whole segment.** In a single breath ⁨الذہبی⁩ names him a martyr, names his
father the elder of the Jāhiliyya, and names him a ⁨بدری⁩. Nothing needs to be added to it.

### 2.2 Precedence, Abyssinia, and the family

> <div dir="rtl">⁨أَحَدُ السَّابِقِيْنَ، وَاسْمُهُ⁩: ⁨مِهْشَمٌ⁩ - ⁨فِيْمَا قِيْلَ⁩ -. ⁨أَسْلَمَ قَبْلَ دُخُوْلِهِم دَارَ الأَرْقَمِ⁩.<br>⁨وَهَاجَرَ إِلَى الحَبَشَةِ مَرَّتَيْنِ، وَوُلِدَ لَهُ بِهَا مُحَمَّدُ بنُ أَبِي حُذَيْفَةَ⁩ … ⁨وَلَدَتْهُ لَهُ⁩: ⁨سَهْلَةُ بِنْتُ سُهَيْلِ بنِ عَمْرٍو، وَهِيَ المُسْتَحَاضَةُ⁩.<br>⁨وَقَدْ تَزَوَّجَ بِهَا عَبْدُ الرَّحْمَنِ بنُ عَوْفٍ، وَهِيَ الَّتِي أَرْضَعَتْ سَالِماً وَهُوَ كَبِيْرٌ، لِتَظْهَرَ عَلَيْهِ، وَخُصَّا بِذَاكَ الحُكْمِ عِنْدَ جُمْهُوْرِ العُلَمَاءِ⁩.</div>
>
> — ⁨سیر أعلام النبلاء ج۱ ص۱۶۵⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/10906/1591

**«⁨أَسْلَمَ قَبْلَ دُخُوْلِهِم دَارَ الأَرْقَمِ⁩»** — he was Muslim before the Companions began meeting
in ⁨دار الأرقم⁩ at all. That is as early as precedence goes. ⁨البدایہ ج۷ ص۵۵⁩ says the same:
«أسلم قديمًا قبل دار الأرقم».

The last clause — **«⁨وَخُصَّا بِذَاكَ الحُكْمِ عِنْدَ جُمْهُوْرِ العُلَمَاءِ⁩»**, *the ruling was
specific to the two of them in the view of the majority of the scholars* — is ⁨الذہبی⁩'s own, and it is
the sentence that keeps §6 safe.

### 2.3 What he looked like, and the lines his sister said about him

⁨الذہبی⁩ records, from ⁨أبو الزناد⁩, that **⁨أبو حذيفة ؓ⁩ challenged his own father to single combat at
⁨بدر⁩**, and that his sister ⁨هند بنت عتبة⁩ (mother of ⁨معاوية⁩) answered with two lines of verse
attacking him (⁨سیر ج۱ ص۱۶۵⁩–۱۶۶, from ⁨ابن سعد⁩ per the editor). Then:

> <div dir="rtl">⁨قَالَ⁩: ⁨وَكَانَ أَبُو حُذَيْفَةَ طَوِيْلاً، حَسَنَ الوَجْهِ، مُرَادفَ الأَسْنَانِ، وَهُوَ الأَثْعَلُ⁩.<br>⁨اسْتُشْهِدَ أَبُو حُذَيْفَةَ⁩ -⁨رَضِيَ اللهُ عَنْهُ⁩- ⁨يَوْمَ اليَمَامَةِ، سَنَةَ اثْنَتَيْ عَشْرَةَ، هُوَ وَمَوْلاَهُ سَالِمٌ⁩.</div>
>
> — ⁨سیر أعلام النبلاء ج۱ ص۱۶۶⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/10906/1592
> *"He was tall and good-looking, with a doubled tooth — and that is what al-athʿal means. Abū
> Ḥudhayfa ؓ was martyred on the day of al-Yamāma, in the year twelve — he and his freedman Sālim."*

⁨البدایہ ج۷ ص۵۵⁩ has the same physical description and glosses ⁨الأثعل⁩ the same way: «⁨وكان طويلًا حسنَ⁩
⁨الوجه، أثْعل⁩ - ⁨وهو الذي له سِنٌّ زائدة⁩». **Two independent books agreeing on a physical detail is
rare and worth one sentence from the podium.**

⚠ **The challenge-to-the-father report needs care.** It is one line in ⁨سیر ج۱ ص۱۶۵⁩, from **⁨أبو الزناد⁩
without a chain**, and the sources do **not** say he fought his father or killed him — see §10.1.
The verses ⁨هند بنت عتبة⁩ is said to have spoken are an attack on a Companion and **are not to be
read out**; they are noted here only so that nobody has to look for them again.

### 2.4 The obituary notice — ⁨البدایہ ج۷ ص۵۵⁩

> <div dir="rtl">⁨أبو حذيفه بن عُتْبة بن رَبيعة بن عبد شمس القُرشي العَبْشمي⁩: ⁨أسلم قديمًا قبل دار الأرقم، وهاجر إلى الحبشة وإلى المدينة وشهد بدرًا وما بعدها، وآخى رسول الله ﷺ بينه وبين عَبَّاد بن بِشْر وقد قُتلا شَهيدين يومَ اليمامة⁩. ⁨وكان عُمْرُ أبي حذيفة يومئذ ثلاثًا أو أربعًا وخمسين سنة، وكان طويلًا حسنَ الوجه، أثْعل،⁩ - ⁨وهو الذي له سِنٌّ زائدة⁩ - ⁨وقيل اسمه هُشَيْم، وقيل هاشم⁩.</div>
>
> — ⁨البدایہ والنہایہ ج۷ ص۵۵⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/30097/3201

**His name.** «⁨مِهْشَم⁩» (⁨سیر ج۱ ص۱۶۵⁩ and ⁨البدایہ ج۴ ص۱۳۶⁩) · «⁨هُشَيْم⁩» or «⁨هاشم⁩» (⁨البدایہ ج۷ ص۵۵⁩).
The ⁨سیر⁩ editor's footnote adds ⁨السهيلي⁩'s objection that ⁨مهشم⁩ is a confusion with a different man and
that «⁨أبو حذيفة بن عتبة⁩» was named **⁨قيس⁩**. **Four candidate names. Say only «⁨أبو حذيفة ؓ⁩» on any
slide.**

**The ⁨مؤاخاة⁩ pairing:** with **⁨عباد بن بشر ؓ⁩**, and both were killed at ⁨اليمامة⁩ (⁨البدایہ ج۷ ص۵۵⁩).
⚠ A **different** pairing — with **⁨أبو عبيدة بن الجراح ؓ⁩** — is recorded for **⁨سالم ؓ⁩** at ⁨سیر ج۱ ص۱۶۹⁩,
which ⁨الذہبی⁩ immediately marks **«⁨هَذَا مُنْقَطِعٌ⁩»**. Do not merge the two.

## 3. ⁨بدر⁩ — a father and a son on opposite sides

**Deliver this soberly and exactly. It needs no dramatising whatever, and any attempt to dramatise
it will make it worse.** What the pages give is a plain sequence: the father fought and was killed;
the son was on the other side; the Prophet ﷺ noticed his face and asked him a question; he answered
honestly; the Prophet ﷺ prayed for him.

### 3.1 The father and the brother

The single combat that opened ⁨بدر⁩, from ⁨ابن اسحاق⁩:

> <div dir="rtl">⁨فبارز عبيدة، وكان أسنّ القوم، عتبة، وبارز حمزة شيبة، وبارز عليٌّ الوليد بن عتبة، فأمّا حمزة، فلم يمهل شيبة أن قتله، وأمّا عليٌّ، فلم يمهل الوليد أن قتله، واختلف عبيدة وعتبة بينهما ضربتين، كلاهما أثبت صاحبه، وكرّ حمزة وعليٌّ بأسيافهما على عتبة، فذفّفا عليه، واحتملا صاحبهما فحازاه إلى أصحابه⁩.</div>
>
> — ⁨البدایہ والنہایہ ج۴ ص۶۰⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/30097/1549

So on the same page: **⁨عتبة⁩ (the father) and ⁨الوليد⁩ (the brother) both killed**, along with ⁨شيبة⁩
(the uncle). ⁨البدایہ⁩ notes on the same page that ⁨البخاري⁩ records ⁨أبو ذر ؓ⁩ swearing that
⁨﴿هَذَانِ خَصْمَانِ اخْتَصَمُوا فِي رَبِّهِمْ﴾⁩ [⁨الحج⁩: ١٩] was revealed about these six men.

⚠ **A divergence on who killed ⁨عتبة⁩.** ⁨البدایہ ج۴ ص۶۰⁩ (⁨ابن اسحاق⁩) has ⁨عبيدة ؓ⁩ paired with ⁨عتبة⁩, and
⁨حمزة ؓ⁩ and ⁨علي ؓ⁩ finishing him. **⁨سیر ج۱ ص۱۷۲⁩** carries a different report — from ⁨أبو إسحاق⁩ ← ⁨حارثة⁩
⁨بن مضرب⁩ ← ⁨علي ؓ⁩ — in which **⁨حمزة ؓ⁩ duelled ⁨عتبة⁩ and killed him**: «⁨فَبَارَزَ يَوْمَئِذٍ حَمْزَةُ⁩
⁨عُتْبَةَ، فَقَتَلَهُ⁩». **Record both; do not put a name to the killer of ⁨أبو حذيفة ؓ⁩'s father from the
platform.** Nothing in this segment depends on it.

⁨سیر ج۱ ص۱۷۱⁩ lists the Quraysh dead at ⁨بدر⁩ and heads the list: «⁨عُتْبَةُ بنُ رَبِيْعَةَ بنِ عَبْدِ⁩
شَمْسٍ بنِ عَبْدِ مَنَافٍ، وَأَخُوْهُ شَيْبَةُ».

### 3.2 The sentence he was afraid of for the rest of his life

Before the fighting, the Prophet ﷺ named men who had been brought out against their will and were
not to be killed, ⁨العباس ؓ⁩ among them. Three approved books carry what ⁨أبو حذيفة ؓ⁩ then said, and all
three carry what he said about it afterwards.

> <div dir="rtl">⁨فقال أبو حُذيفة بنُ عُتبة بن ربيعة⁩: ⁨أنقتلُ آباءنا وأبناءنا وإخواننا ونترُكُ العباس، واللَّه لئن لقيتُه لألحمنّه بالسيف⁩. ⁨فبلغت رسول اللَّه ﷺ فقال لعُمر⁩: "⁨يا أبا حفصٍ⁩ … ⁨أيُضربُ وجهُ عمّ رسول اللَّه بالسيف؟⁩"</div>
>
> — ⁨البدایہ والنہایہ ج۴ ص۷۷⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/30097/1566
> *"Abū Ḥudhayfa b. ʿUtba b. Rabīʿa said: Are we to kill our fathers and our sons and our brothers,
> and leave al-ʿAbbās? By God, if I meet him I will put the sword through him. Word of it reached
> the Messenger of Allah ﷺ, and he said to ʿUmar: 'Abū Ḥafṣ … is the face of the Messenger of
> Allah's uncle to be struck with a sword?'"*

And immediately after, on the same page:

> <div dir="rtl">⁨فقال أبو حُذيفة⁩: ⁨ما أنا بآمنٍ من تلك الكلمة التي قُلتُ يومئذٍ، ولا أزالُ منها خائفًا إلّا أن تُكفّرها عنّي الشهادةُ، فقُتل يوم اليمامة شهيدًا، ﵁⁩.</div>
>
> — ⁨البدایہ والنہایہ ج۴ ص۷۷⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/30097/1566
> *"Abū Ḥudhayfa said: I am not safe from that word I said that day. I shall not stop fearing it
> unless martyrdom wipes it away from me. And he was killed a martyr on the day of al-Yamāma ؓ."*

**⁨الکامل ج۲ ص۲۳⁩** gives it in its own words, and **ends the sentence in the same place**:

> <div dir="rtl">⁨فَقَالَ أَبُو حُذَيْفَةَ⁩: ⁨لَا أَزَالُ خَائِفًا مِنْ تِلْكَ الْكَلِمَةِ، وَلَا يُكَفِّرُهَا عَنِّي إِلَّا الشَّهَادَةُ⁩. ⁨فَقُتِلَ يَوْمَ الْيَمَامَةِ شَهِيدًا⁩.</div>
>
> — ⁨الکامل فی التاریخ ج۲ ص۲۳⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/21712/714

**⁨سیر⁩ (⁨مغازي موسى بن عقبة⁩) ⁨ص۳۵۷⁩** — ⁨الذہبی⁩ reproduces the same report inside the chapter he
introduces as «⁨ذكر غزوة بدر من مغازي موسى بن عقبة⁩ **⁨فإنها من أصح المغازي⁩**» — and the wording there
is: «⁨فكان أبو حذيفة بعد يقول⁩: ⁨والله ما آمن من تلك الكلمة التي قلت، ولا أزال منها خائفا، إلا أن⁩
⁨يكفرها الله عني بشهادة⁩. ⁨فاستشهد يوم اليمامة⁩» (https://shamela.ws/book/10906/494).

**Three books, ten years apart in the narrative, closing the same circle.** This is the single
strongest structural fact in the whole strand: **the sentence is said at ⁨بدر⁩ in ⁨۲ھ⁩ and it is
answered at ⁨اليمامة⁩ in ⁨۱۲ھ⁩, and every one of the three books says so itself.** The speaker does not
have to draw the line; the books draw it.

⚠ **Two cautions on this passage, and they matter.**

1. **The chain.** ⁨ابن اسحاق⁩'s route is «⁨العباس بن عبد الله بن معبد،⁩ **⁨عن بعض أهله⁩**⁨، عن ابن عباس⁩»
   (⁨البدایہ ج۴ ص۷۷⁩; ⁨سیر ص۳۵۷⁩) — an unnamed link. This is ⁨مغازی⁩ reporting, not ⁨صحیح⁩ material. If
   asked, say so plainly, exactly as the course does with ⁨سہیل ؓ⁩.
2. **⁨عمر ؓ⁩'s reply is on the page and should NOT be read out.** ⁨البدایہ ج۴ ص۷۷⁩ and ⁨سیر ص۳۵۷⁩ both
   record ⁨عمر ؓ⁩ asking permission to strike ⁨أبو حذيفة ؓ⁩'s neck, with the words «⁨فوالله لقد نافق⁩».
   **It is recorded here so the speaker is not ambushed by it; it does not go on a slide, into a
   script, or into an answer volunteered from the floor.** The reason is not squeamishness: reading
   one Companion's heated word about another to this room, out of its context and without ⁨اصول⁩,
   is exactly what `CLAUDE.md` §1.6 exists to prevent. If a written slip asks about it, the answer
   is that the same page records ⁨أبو حذيفة ؓ⁩'s own fear of what he had said and his death as a
   martyr, and that the ummah has settled its view of both men.

### 3.3 ⁨القليب⁩ — the question the Prophet ﷺ asked him

> <div dir="rtl">⁨قال ابن إسحاق⁩: ⁨ولمّا أمر رسول اللَّه ﷺ أن يلقوا في القليب، أُخذ عتبة بن ربيعة فسحب في القليب، فنظر رسول اللَّه ﷺ، فيما بلغني، في وجه أبي حذيفة بن عتبة، فإذا هو كئيبٌ قد تغيّر لونه، فقال⁩: "⁨يا أبا حذيفة، لعلك قد دخلك من شأن أبيك شيءٌ⁩". ⁨أو كما قال رسول اللَّه ﷺ، فقال⁩: ⁨لا واللَّه يا رسول اللَّه، ما شككت في أبي ولا في مصرعه، ولكني كنت أعرف من أبي رأيًا وحلمًا وفضلًا،⁩ [⁨ص٩٢⁩] ⁨فكنت أرجو أن يهديَه ذلك للإسلام، فلمّا رأيتُ ما أصابه، وذكرت ما مات عليه من الكفر بعد الذي كنت أرجو له، أحزنني ذلك⁩. ⁨فدعا له رسول اللَّه ﷺ بخيرٍ، وقال له خيرًا⁩.</div>
>
> — ⁨البدایہ والنہایہ ج۴ ص۹۱⁩–۹۲ · https://shamela.ws/book/30097/1580 ⁨و⁩ https://shamela.ws/book/30097/1581
> *"When the Messenger of Allah ﷺ ordered them thrown into the well, ʿUtba b. Rabīʿa was taken and
> dragged into it. The Messenger of Allah ﷺ looked — so I have been told — into the face of Abū
> Ḥudhayfa b. ʿUtba, and there he was, downcast, his colour changed. So he said: 'Abū Ḥudhayfa —
> perhaps something has entered you about the matter of your father.' Or words to that effect. He
> said: No, by Allah, Messenger of Allah. I have not doubted my father, nor where he fell. But I
> used to know in my father a judgement, a forbearance and a merit, and I used to hope that would
> guide him to Islam. When I saw what had befallen him, and remembered what he had died upon of
> unbelief after what I had hoped for him — that grieved me. So the Messenger of Allah ﷺ prayed for
> good for him, and spoke kindly to him."*

**⁨سیر⁩ (⁨السیرة النبویة⁩) ⁨ص۳۱۲⁩** carries the same incident **from ⁨أنس ؓ⁩** — a named Companion rather
than ⁨ابن اسحاق⁩'s «⁨فيما بلغني⁩» — and its wording is slightly shorter:

> <div dir="rtl">⁨وعن أنس⁩: ⁨لما سحب عتبة بن ربيعة إلى القليب نظر رسول الله ﷺ في وجه أبي حذيفة ابنه، فإذا هو كئيب متغير⁩. ⁨فقال⁩: "⁨لعلك قد دخلك من شأن أبيك شيء⁩"؟. ⁨قال⁩: ⁨لا والله ما شككت في أبي ولا في مصرعه، ولكني كنت أعرف منه رأيا وحلما، فكنت أرجو أن يسلم، فلما رأيت ما أصابه وما مات عليه أحزنني ذلك⁩. ⁨فدعا له النبي ﷺ وقال له خيرا⁩.</div>
>
> — ⁨سیر أعلام النبلاء⁩ (⁨السیرة النبویة⁩) ⁨ص۳۱۲⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/10906/449

**Use the ⁨سیر⁩ (⁨أنس ؓ⁩) wording on the slide** — it is shorter, it is from a named Companion, and it
says the same thing. **Caption it «⁨سیر أعلام النبلاء⁩ — ⁨قسم السیرة النبویة، ص۳۱۲⁩» and not to
ابن کثیر.**

**What the passage actually shows, and what to say about it:** it is not a story about a son's
loyalty being tested. It is a son saying he never doubted the judgement, and grieving for a
particular thing — that a man he thought was near to accepting Islam died without it. And the
Prophet ﷺ's response was not a rebuke.

### 3.4 Abyssinia — the ship for half a dīnār

> <div dir="rtl">⁨وروى الواقدي أنَّ خروجهم إليها في رجب سنة خمسٍ من البعْثَة، وأن أولَ من هاجر منهم أحد عشر رجلًا وأربع نسوة، وأنهم انتهوا إلى البحر، ما بين ماشٍ وراكب، فاستأجروا سفينةً بنصف دينار إلى الحبشة، وهم عثمان بن عفان، وامرأته رُقَيَّة بنت رسول الله ﷺ، وأبو حُذيفة بن عتبة، وامرأته سهلة بنت سُهيل، والزُّبَير بن العوَّام، ومُصْعَب بن عُمير، وعبد الرحمن بن عوف، وأبو سلمة بن عبد الأسد، وامرأته أم سلمة بنت أبي أمية، وعثمان بن مَظْعون، وعامر بن ربيعة العَنْزي، وامرأته ليلى بنت أبي حَثْمة، وأبو سَبْرَة بن أبي رُهْم، وحاطب بن عمرو، وسُهيل بن بَيْضاء، وعبد الله بن مسعود ﵃⁩.</div>
>
> — ⁨البدایہ والنہایہ ج۳ ص۲۸۱⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/30097/1255

**Attribute this as ⁨ابن کثیر⁩ reports it: «⁨روى الواقدي⁩…».** ⁨الواقدی⁩ is ⁨مترو ك⁩ and the course handles
him with care (`suhayl-ibn-amr.md` §4). What survives regardless is that ⁨البدایہ⁩ places
**⁨أبو حذيفة ؓ⁩ and ⁨سهلة ؓ⁩ on the first ship**, alongside ⁨عثمان ؓ⁩ and ⁨رقية ؓ⁩. ⁨سیر ج۱ ص۱۶۵⁩ independently
says he went to Abyssinia **twice**, and that ⁨محمد⁩ was born to him there.

**The detail worth one sentence:** the ship was hired for **half a dīnār** — the first organised
departure in the ummah's history, and it was a shared fare.

**The reason, on the same page**, in ⁨ابن اسحاق⁩'s words rather than ⁨الواقدی⁩'s: the Prophet ﷺ told them
«⁨لو خرَجْتُمْ إلى أرض الحبشة، فإنَّ بها مَلِكًا لا يُظلم عنده أحد، وهي أرضُ صِدْق⁩» — *if you
went out to the land of Abyssinia, there is a king there under whom no one is wronged, and it is a
land of truth* (⁨البدایہ ج۳ ص۲۸۱⁩, https://shamela.ws/book/30097/1255). **This is the only sentence
about Abyssinia's king that this note licenses.**

## 4. ⁨سالم ؓ⁩ — origin, the ⁨ولاء⁩, and the names

### 4.1 ⁨الذہبی⁩'s heading, and what ⁨موسى بن عقبة⁩ said

> <div dir="rtl">١٤ - ⁨سَالِمٌ مَوْلَى أَبِي حُذَيْفَةَ⁩<br>⁨مِنَ السَّابِقِيْنَ الأَوَّلِيْنَ، البَدْرِيِّيْنَ، المُقَرَّبِيْنَ، العَالِمِيْنَ⁩.<br>⁨قَالَ مُوْسَى بنُ عُقْبَةَ⁩: ⁨هُوَ سَالِمُ بنُ مَعْقِلٍ، أَصْلُهُ مِنْ إِصْطَخْرَ، وَالَى أَبَا حُذَيْفَةَ، وَإِنَّمَا الَّذِي أَعْتَقَهُ هِيَ ثُبَيْتَةُ بِنْتُ يعَار الأَنْصَارِيَّةُ، زَوْجَةُ أَبِي حُذَيْفَةَ بنِ عُتْبَةَ، وَتَبَنَّاهُ أَبُو حُذَيْفَةَ، كَذَا قَالَ⁩.</div>
>
> — ⁨سیر أعلام النبلاء ج۱ ص۱۶۷⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/10906/1593
> *"Sālim, the freedman of Abū Ḥudhayfa. Of the first forerunners, of the men of Badr, of those
> brought near, of the learned. Mūsā b. ʿUqba said: he is Sālim b. Maʿqil, his origin is from
> Iṣṭakhr. He took Abū Ḥudhayfa as his patron; the one who actually freed him was Thubayta bt.
> Yaʿār al-Anṣāriyya, the wife of Abū Ḥudhayfa b. ʿUtba; and Abū Ḥudhayfa adopted him. So he said."*

**Four words in a row — ⁨السابقين الأولين⁩ · ⁨البدريين⁩ · ⁨المقربين⁩ · ⁨العالمين⁩ — applied to a freed
slave.** That is ⁨الذہبی⁩'s own register, not ours, and it is the whole point of the segment.

**⁨إصطخر⁩** is in ⁨فارس⁩. `[SOURCED]` that the source says his origin was from there; the source does not
say more, and **the speaker should not add a nationality claim beyond the word on the page.**

### 4.2 The names, and where they differ

| | سیر ج۱ ص۱۶۷ | البدایہ ج۷ ص۵۰ |
|---|---|---|
| His name | **⁨سالم بن معقل⁩** (⁨موسى بن عقبة⁩) | «**⁨سالم بن عبيد⁩**⁨، ويقال⁩: **⁨ابن معقل⁩**» |
| Who freed him | **⁨ثبيتة بنت يعار الأنصارية⁩** | «**⁨ثُبَيْتة بنت يَعَار⁩**» — then, later on the same page, «**⁨بثينة⁩**» |
| ⁨أبو حذيفة ؓ⁩'s wife in the ⁨رضاعة⁩ report | **⁨سهلة بنت سهيل⁩** | «**⁨سَهْلة بنت سَهْل بن عمرو⁩**» |

⚠ **⁨البدایہ ج۷ ص۵۰⁩ names the freedwoman twice on one page and spells her differently each time** —
⁨ثبيتة⁩, then ⁨بثينة⁩. The editor's footnote on that page says the dispute is between those two forms
and a third, **⁨عمرة⁩**, citing ⁨الاستيعاب⁩. **Do not print her name on a slide.** Say «the woman of the
household who had freed him» and move on.

⚠ **«⁨سهل بن عمرو⁩» on ⁨البدایہ ج۷ ص۵۰⁩ is against ⁨سیر ج۱ ص۱۶۵⁩ and ⁨ج۱ ص۱۶۷⁩ and against ⁨البدایہ⁩'s own
⁨ج۳ ص۲۸۱⁩, all of which say ⁨سُهيل بن عمرو⁩.** Treat the ⁨ج۷ ص۵۰⁩ form as a slip of the printed text;
**do not build the ⁨سہیل ؓ⁩ connection on that page — build it on ⁨سیر ج۱ ص۱۶۵⁩.**

## 5. What he was known for — the Qurʾān

### 5.1 The imāmate at ⁨قُباء⁩, before the Prophet ﷺ arrived

> <div dir="rtl">⁨وَعَنِ ابْنِ عُمَرَ، قَالَ⁩: ⁨كَانَ سَالِمٌ مَوْلَى أَبِي حُذَيْفَةَ يَؤُمُّ المُهَاجِرِيْنَ الَّذِيْنَ قَدِمُوا مِنْ مَكَّةَ حِيْنَ قَدِمَ المَدِيْنَةَ، لأَنَّهُ كَانَ أَقْرَأَهُم⁩.</div>
>

> *"From Ibn ʿUmar: Sālim the freedman of Abū Ḥudhayfa used to lead in prayer the Muhājirūn who had

The same page, and the top of the next, give a second transmission of the same fact through ⁨نافع⁩ ←

> <div dir="rtl">⁨أَنَّ المُهَاجِرِيْنَ نَزَلُوا بِالعُصْبَةِ إِلَى جَنْبِ قُبَاءَ، فَأَمَّهُم سَالِمٌ مَوْلَى أَبِي حُذَيْفَةَ، لأَنَّهُ كَانَ⁩ [⁨ص١٦٩⁩] ⁨أَكْثَرَهُم قُرْآناً، فِيْهِم عُمَرُ، وَأَبُو سَلَمَةَ بنُ عَبْدِ الأَسَدِ⁩.</div>
>

> *"The Muhājirūn camped at al-ʿUṣba, beside Qubāʾ, and Sālim the freedman of Abū Ḥudhayfa led them
> in prayer, because he had the most Qurʾān of them — among them ʿUmar and Abū Salama b. ʿAbd
> al-Asad."*

**The editor's footnote on ⁨ج۱ ص۱۶۹⁩ gives the takhrīj: ⁨البخاري⁩ (٦٩٢), «⁨كتاب الأذان⁩: ⁨باب إمامة العبد⁩
⁨والمولى⁩», and ⁨البخاري⁩ (٧١٧٥), «⁨كتاب الأحكام⁩: ⁨باب استقضاء الموالي واستعمالهم⁩».** ⚠ **Those two

edition traces it to ⁨البخاري ٦٩٢ و ٧١٧٥⁩» if they are used at all.** The same footnote notes that in
the second Bukhārī narration **⁨أبو بكر ؓ⁩** is also named among those present, and that scholars found

**That is a «⁨کیسے پتا چلا؟⁩» moment in miniature — a footnote noticing that a name cannot fit the
chronology — and it is the same kind of catch as ⁨الذہبی⁩'s «⁨فَهَذَا لاَ يَسْتَقِيْمُ⁩» in
`suhayl-ibn-amr.md`.**

⁨البدایہ ج۷ ص۵۰⁩ carries the fact in Ibn Kathīr's own words and adds the reason plainly:

> <div dir="rtl">⁨أسلمَ قديمًا وهاجرَ إلى المدينة قبل رسول الله ﷺ فكان يُصلي بمن بها من المُهاجرين، وفيهم عمر بن الخطاب لكثرةِ حفظِهِ القرآن، وشهد بدرًا وما بعدها⁩.</div>
>
> — ⁨البدایہ والنہایہ ج۷ ص۵۰⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/30097/3196

### 5.2 The night the Prophet ﷺ went out to listen

> <div dir="rtl">⁨حَنْظَلَةُ بنُ أَبِي سُفْيَانَ⁩: ⁨عَنْ عَبْدِ الرَّحْمَنِ بنِ سَابِطٍ، عَنْ عَائِشَةَ، قَالَتْ⁩: ⁨اسْتَبْطَأنِي رَسُوْلُ اللهِ ذَاتَ لَيْلَةٍ، فَقَالَ⁩: (⁨مَا حَبَسَكِ؟⁩) . ⁨قُلْتُ⁩: ⁨إِنَّ فِي المَسْجِدِ لأَحْسَنَ مَنْ سَمِعْتُ صَوْتاً بِالقُرْآنِ⁩. ⁨فَأَخَذَ رِدَاءهُ، وَخَرَجَ يَسْمَعُهُ، فَإِذَا هُوَ سَالِمٌ مَوْلَى أَبِي حُذَيْفَةَ⁩. ⁨فَقَالَ⁩: (⁨الحَمْدُ لِلِّهِ الَّذِي جَعَلَ فِي أُمَّتِي مِثْلَكَ⁩) .<br>⁨إِسْنَادُهُ جَيِّدٌ⁩.</div>
>

### 5.3 «⁨استقرئوا القرآن من أربعة⁩» — exactly what the page carries, and no more

> <div dir="rtl">⁨وهو أحد الأربعة الذين قال فيهم رسول الله ﷺ⁩: "⁨استقرئوا القرآنَ من أربعةٍ⁩" ⁨فذكر منهم سالمًا مولى أبي حذيفة⁩.</div>
>
> — ⁨البدایہ والنہایہ ج۷ ص۵۰⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/30097/3196
> *"And he is one of the four of whom the Messenger of Allah ﷺ said: 'Take the Qurʾān from four' —
> and he mentioned among them Sālim, the freedman of Abū Ḥudhayfa."*

**⚠ Read this carefully before it goes anywhere near a slide.** ⁨ابن کثیر⁩'s own text quotes **only the
opening clause** — «⁨استقرئوا القرآنَ من أربعةٍ⁩» — and then says «**⁨فذكر منهم سالمًا⁩**», *and he
mentioned Sālim among them*. **He does not list the four in his own text.** The list and the ḥadīth
number are in the editor's footnote (٥) on that page:

> <div dir="rtl">⁨تمام الحديث⁩: "⁨استقرؤوا القرآن من أربعة⁩: ⁨من ابن مسعود، وسالم مولى أبي حذيفة، وأبيّ بن كعب، ومعاذ بن جبل⁩" ⁨أخرجه مسلم في صحيحه⁩ (٢٤٦٤) (١١٧) ⁨في فضائل الصحابة⁩.</div>
>
> — editor's footnote, ⁨البدایہ والنہایہ ج۷ ص۵۰⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/30097/3196

**How to use it honestly.** Two acceptable options, and no third:

- **Safest:** put only ⁨ابن کثیر⁩'s own sentence on the slide — «⁨استقرئوا القرآن من أربعة⁩» — caption it
  **⁨البدایہ والنہایہ ج۷ ص۵۰⁩**, and say aloud that Ibn Kathīr names ⁨سالم ؓ⁩ as one of the four.
- **If the four names are wanted on screen:** print them and caption them
  **«⁨صحیح مسلم ٢٤٦٤⁩ — as given by the editor of ⁨البدایہ⁩, ⁨ج۷ ص۵۰⁩»**. That is what the page actually
  supports. **Do not caption the four names to ⁨ابن کثیر⁩, and do not supply the number from memory.**

## 6. The adoption undone, the marriage, and ⁨رضاعة الكبير⁩

**This section is recorded for completeness and for the speaker's safety. It is a ⁨فقہی⁩ crux. The
course does not enter it.** `CLAUDE.md` §1.6: narrate what the book narrates and stop.

### 6.1 What ⁨البدایہ⁩ puts in one sentence

> <div dir="rtl">⁨وقد تَبنّاهُ أبو حُذَيْفة وزوَّجَهُ بابنةِ أخيه فاطمة بنت الوليد بن عُتْبة، فلما أنزل الله ﴿ادْعُوهُمْ لِآبَائِهِمْ﴾⁩ [⁨الأحزاب⁩: ٥] ⁨جاءت امرأة أبي حُذَيْفة سَهْلة بنت سَهْل بن عمرو فقالت⁩: ⁨يا رسولَ الله إن سالمًا يدخل عليَّ وأنا غفلٌ، فأمرها أن تُرْضِعهُ فأرْضَعَتْهُ فكانَ يدخلُ عليها بتلك الرضاعة، وكان من سادات المسلمين⁩.</div>
>
> — ⁨البدایہ والنہایہ ج۷ ص۵۰⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/30097/3196
> *"Abū Ḥudhayfa adopted him and married him to his brother's daughter, Fāṭima bt. al-Walīd b.
> ʿUtba. When Allah sent down ﴾Call them by their fathers﴿ [al-Aḥzāb 5], Abū Ḥudhayfa's wife Sahla
> bt. Sahl b. ʿAmr came and said: Messenger of Allah, Sālim comes in upon me when I am unprepared.
> So he instructed her to nurse him; she nursed him, and he used to come in upon her by that
> nursing. And he was one of the chiefs of the Muslims."*

**The marriage is the sentence Daniyal's instinct is about.** ⁨أبو حذيفة ؓ⁩ did not merely free and
adopt him; **he married him to his own niece — ⁨فاطمة⁩, the daughter of ⁨الوليد بن عتبة⁩, the brother who
had been killed at ⁨بدر⁩ fighting on the other side.** That is one household absorbing a freed slave
completely, in the same generation in which it lost two men to ⁨بدر⁩.

⚠ **Only ⁨البدایہ ج۷ ص۵۰⁩ carries the marriage within the approved list** — a phrase search of the
three narrative works for «⁨فاطمة بنت الوليد بن عتبة⁩» returned that page alone. Cite it to ⁨ابن کثیر⁩
and to nobody else.

### 6.2 ⁨عائشة ؓ⁩'s transmission, as ⁨سیر⁩ gives it

> <div dir="rtl">⁨ابْنُ أَبِي مُلَيْكَةَ⁩: ⁨عَنِ القَاسِمِ بنِ مُحَمَّدٍ⁩: ⁨أَنَّ سَهْلَةَ بِنْتَ سُهَيْلٍ أَتَتْ رَسُوْلَ اللهِ ﷺ وَهِيَ امْرَأَةُ أَبِي حُذَيْفَةَ، فَقَالَتْ⁩: ⁨يَا رَسُوْلَ اللهِ⁩! ⁨إِنَّ سَالِماً مَعِي، وَقَدْ أَدْرَكَ مَا يُدْرِكُ الرِّجَالُ⁩.<br>⁨فَقَالَ⁩: (⁨أَرْضِعِيْهِ، فَإِذَا أَرْضَعْتِهِ فَقَدْ حَرُمَ عَلَيْكِ مَا يَحْرُمُ مِنْ ذِي المَحْرَمِ⁩).<br>⁨قَالَتْ أُمُّ سَلَمَةَ⁩: ⁨أَبَى أَزْوَاجُ رَسُوْلِ اللهِ ﷺ أَنْ يَدْخُلَ أَحَدٌ عَلَيْهِنَّ بِهَذَا الرَّضَاعِ، وَقُلْنَ⁩: ⁨إِنَّمَا هِيَ رُخْصَةٌ لِسَالِمٍ خَاصَّةً⁩.</div>
>
> — ⁨سیر أعلام النبلاء ج۱ ص۱۶۷⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/10906/1593

The editor's footnote on the same page records that the report as transmitted by ⁨ابن سعد⁩ is ⁨مرسل⁩
with reliable men, that it is connected through ⁨أحمد⁩, **⁨مسلم⁩ (١٤٥٣)(٢٨) «⁨كتاب الرضاع⁩: ⁨باب رضاعة⁩
⁨الكبير⁩»**, ⁨النسائي⁩, ⁨ابن ماجه⁩, ⁨أبو داود⁩, ⁨عبد الرزاق⁩ and ⁨مالك⁩ by several routes, and it refers the
reader to **«⁨زاد المعاد⁩» ٥/٥٧٨–٥٩٣** for the scholars' positions.

### 6.3 The two sentences that close the matter and let the speaker stop

> <div dir="rtl">⁨وَخُصَّا بِذَاكَ الحُكْمِ عِنْدَ جُمْهُوْرِ العُلَمَاءِ⁩.</div>
>
> — ⁨سیر أعلام النبلاء ج۱ ص۱۶۵⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/10906/1591 — **⁨الذہبی⁩'s own words**
> *"The two of them were singled out by that ruling, in the view of the majority of the scholars."*

> <div dir="rtl">⁨إِنَّمَا هِيَ رُخْصَةٌ لِسَالِمٍ خَاصَّةً⁩.</div>
>
> — ⁨سیر أعلام النبلاء ج۱ ص۱۶۷⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/10906/1593 — **the words of the Prophet's ﷺ
> wives, as ⁨أم سلمة ؓ⁩ reports them**
> *"It is only a dispensation for Sālim in particular."*

**⚠ SPEAKER'S DISCRETION — and the recommendation is to leave it out.** The evening does not need it,
the room does not expect it, and it is a live ⁨مسئلہ⁩ among the fuqahāʾ. **If a written slip asks:**
say that the report is in ⁨صحیح مسلم⁩, that the Prophet's ﷺ wives themselves held it a dispensation
specific to ⁨سالم ؓ⁩, that ⁨الذہبی رحمہ اللہ⁩ records the majority of the scholars taking it that way,
and that the ruling itself is a matter for the ⁨فقہاء⁩ and not for this lecture. **Then stop.** Do not
adjudicate, do not name schools, do not go further.

**What may safely be told from the platform is the un-contested half:** that the verse ⁨﴾ادْعُوهُمْ⁩
⁨لِآبَائِهِمْ﴿⁩ ended adoption as a legal fact, that ⁨سالم ؓ⁩ therefore could no longer be «⁨ابن أبي⁩
⁨حذيفة⁩» — and that **the household kept him anyway**, by marriage and by every other means available
to it. That is the ⁨عبرت⁩, and it needs none of the ⁨فقہ⁩.

## 7. ⁨اليمامة⁩, ⁨۱۲ھ⁩ — what each of them did

For the shape of the battle — ⁨عَقْرَباء⁩, the enemy at 40,000, the rout, «⁨امتازوا⁩», ⁨حديقة الموت⁩, the
death of ⁨مسيلمة⁩ — see `ridda-campaign-the-conduct-of-the-wars.md` §6. **This section adds only what
those two men did and said.**

### 7.1 Where they stood

| Source | ⁨أبو حذيفة ؓ⁩ | ⁨سالم ؓ⁩ |
|---|---|---|
| ⁨البدایہ ج۷ ص۳۳⁩ | on a wing, with ⁨زيد بن الخطاب ؓ⁩ — «⁨وعلى المَجْنبتين زيدًا وأبا حذيفة⁩» | **the Muhājirūn's banner** |
| ⁨الکامل⁩ **⁨ج۲ ص۲۱۵⁩** | over the Muhājirūn, with ⁨زيد ؓ⁩ — «⁨وَعَلَى الْمُهَاجِرِينَ أَبُو حُذَيْفَةَ وَزَيْدُ بْنُ الْخَطَّابِ⁩» (index 906) | — |

> *"The Muslims' banner was with him on the day of al-Yamāma, and he kept pressing forward with it
> into the enemy's throat, and then fought until he was killed. The banner fell — and Sālim, the
> freedman of Abū Ḥudhayfa, took it up."*

### 7.2 What ⁨سالم ؓ⁩ was asked, and what he answered

**البدایہ ج۷ ص۳۴:**

> <div dir="rtl">⁨وقال المهاجرون لسالم مولى أبي حذيفة⁩: ⁨أتخشى أن نؤتى من قبلك؟ فقال⁩: ⁨بئس حامل القرآن أنا إذًا⁩.</div>
>
> — ⁨البدایہ والنہایہ ج۷ ص۳۴⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/30097/3180
> *"The Muhājirūn said to Sālim, the freedman of Abū Ḥudhayfa: Do you fear we shall be broken from
> your side? He said: A wretched bearer of the Qurʾān I should be, then."*

**⁨الکامل ج۲ ص۲۱۶⁩ puts the question differently** — and the difference is worth knowing, because the
⁨الکامل⁩ version is a question about **him**, not about the line:

> <div dir="rtl">⁨فَقَالُوا⁩: ⁨تَخْشَى عَلَيْنَا مِنْ نَفْسِكَ شَيْئًا فَقَالَ⁩: ⁨بِئْسَ حَامِلُ الْقُرْآنِ أَنَا إِذًا⁩!</div>
>
> — ⁨الکامل فی التاریخ ج۲ ص۲۱۶⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/21712/907
> *"They said: Do you fear anything for us from yourself? He said: A wretched bearer of the Qurʾān I
> should be, then!"*

**The answer is identical in both books, word for word.** That is the line for the slide.
**Attribute the question to whichever book is captioned; do not blend the two questions.**

### 7.3 What ⁨أبو حذيفة ؓ⁩ was calling out

> <div dir="rtl">⁨وقال أبو حذيفة⁩: ⁨يا أهلَ القرآنِ زيِّنوا القرآنَ بالفِعال، وحملَ فيهم حتى أبعدَهم وأصيب ﵁⁩.</div>
>
> — ⁨البدایہ والنہایہ ج۷ ص۳۴⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/30097/3180
> *"Abū Ḥudhayfa said: People of the Qurʾān — adorn the Qurʾān with deeds. And he charged into them
> until he drove them back, and he was struck down ؓ."*

⁨الکامل ج۲ ص۲۱۷⁩ has the call in the same words: «⁨وَقَالَ أَبُو حُذَيْفَةَ⁩: ⁨يَا أَهْلَ الْقُرْآنِ،⁩
⁨زَيِّنُوا الْقُرْآنَ بِالْفِعَالِ⁩» (https://shamela.ws/book/21712/908).

**Two books, identical wording, no chain given by either.** It is ⁨مغازی⁩ reporting. Say so if asked.

**The pairing that makes it land:** on the same field, in the same hour, the master calls to the
carriers of the Qurʾān to adorn it with deeds, and the freedman — the best carrier of the Qurʾān in
the army — refuses to be the point where the line breaks, in the name of the same thing.

### 7.4 The pit, and the hand

Two separate reports, and they belong to different levels of reliability.

**(a) The pit — ⁨البدایہ ج۷ ص۴۸⁩, and it is about ⁨سالم ؓ⁩ *and* ⁨ثابت بن قيس ؓ⁩:**

>
> — ⁨البدایہ والنہایہ ج۷ ص۴۸⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/30097/3194
> *"Thābit and Sālim, the freedman of Abū Ḥudhayfa, said: This is not how we used to fight alongside
> the Messenger of Allah ﷺ. And the two of them made a pit for themselves and went down into it and
> fought until they were killed."*

⁨سیر ج۱ ص۱۶۹⁩ carries the same act attributed to **⁨سالم ؓ⁩ alone**, with the banner in his hand — but
**through ⁨الواقدي⁩, whom the ⁨سیر⁩ editor marks ⁨متروك⁩ on that very page:**

> <div dir="rtl">⁨وَجَاءَ مِنْ رِوَايَةِ الوَاقِدِيِّ⁩: ⁨أَنَّ مُحَمَّدَ بنَ ثَابِتِ بنِ قَيْسٍ، قَالَ⁩: ⁨لَمَّا انْكَشَفَ المُسْلِمُوْنَ يَوْمَ اليَمَامَةِ، قَالَ سَالِمٌ مَوْلَى أَبِي حُذَيْفَةَ⁩: ⁨مَا هَكَذَا كُنَّا نَفْعَلُ مَعَ رَسُوْلِ اللهِ ﷺ⁩. ⁨فَحَفَرَ لِنَفْسِهِ حُفْرَةً، فَقَامَ فِيْهَا، وَمَعَهُ رَايَةُ المُهَاجِرِيْنَ يَوْمَئِذٍ، ثُمَّ قَاتَلَ حَتَّى قُتِلَ⁩.</div>
>
> — ⁨سیر أعلام النبلاء ج۱ ص۱۶۹⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/10906/1595

⚠ **The same words — «⁨ما هكذا كنا نفعل⁩/⁨نقاتل مع رسول الله ﷺ⁩» — are recorded elsewhere for ⁨ثابت بن⁩
⁨قيس ؓ⁩ alone**, from ⁨أنس ؓ⁩, with the ⁨سیر⁩ editor citing **⁨صحیح البخاري ٢٨٤٥⁩** (⁨سیر ج۱ ص۳۱۱⁩; see
`ridda-campaign-the-conduct-of-the-wars.md` §6.5). **Given that, the safe attribution on a slide is
⁨ثابت بن قيس ؓ⁩, and the safe statement about ⁨سالم ؓ⁩ is ⁨البدایہ ج۷ ص۴۸⁩'s: the two of them said it and
the two of them dug in.** Do not give ⁨سالم ؓ⁩ the Bukhārī-anchored words on his own.

**(b) The hand — ⁨البدایہ ج۷ ص۵۰⁩. This is Ibn Kathīr's own text, in the obituary.**

> <div dir="rtl">⁨انقطعت يدُه اليمنى فأخذها بيساره، فقُطعت فاحتضنها وهو يقول⁩: ⁨﴿وَمَا مُحَمَّدٌ إِلَّا رَسُولٌ قَدْ خَلَتْ مِنْ قَبْلِهِ الرُّسُلُ﴾⁩ [⁨آل عمران⁩: ١٤٤] ⁨﴿وَكَأَيِّنْ مِنْ نَبِيٍّ قَاتَلَ مَعَهُ رِبِّيُّونَ كَثِيرٌ﴾⁩ [⁨آل عمران⁩: ١٤٦]</div>
>
> — ⁨البدایہ والنہایہ ج۷ ص۵۰⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/30097/3196
> *"His right hand was severed, so he took it [the banner] in his left; that was cut off, so he
> clasped it to his chest, saying: ﴾Muḥammad is no more than a Messenger; messengers have passed
> away before him﴿ [Āl ʿImrān 144] ﴾And how many a prophet fought, and with him many devoted
> men﴿ [Āl ʿImrān 146]."*

**The pronoun.** «⁨فأخذها⁩» and «⁨فاحتضنها⁩» refer to **⁨الراية⁩** — the banner named in the preceding
clause of the same sentence («⁨ولما أخذ الرايةَ يومَ اليمامةِ⁩…»). Render it as *the banner*, as above.

**⚠ And here is the fact worth the whole evening.** ⁨﴾وَمَا مُحَمَّدٌ إِلَّا رَسُولٌ﴿⁩ **is the verse
⁨سیدنا ابوبکر ؓ⁩ recited in his ⁨خطبہ⁩ to the people of ⁨المدینہ⁩ on the day the Prophet ﷺ died** —
page-cited at `docs/research/wafat-and-medina-11h.md` (⁨البدایہ ج۵ ص۳۴۰⁩–۳۴۲, three consecutive pages),
where the setting is «⁨فَتَشَهَّد أبو بكر، فأقبل الناسُ إليه⁩». **The same āya, in ⁨المدینہ⁩ in ⁨۱۱ھ⁩ and on
the field at ⁨اليمامة⁩ in ⁨۱۲ھ⁩.** Both ends are sourced; the speaker states the two facts and says
nothing more. The room will do the rest.

⚠ **Do not put an interval in months on this.** No page held gives one. The only month-anchored
figures in the corpus are **⁨ربيع الأول ۱۱ھ⁩** for the Prophet's ﷺ death (`wafat-and-medina-11h.md`) and
**«⁨اسْتُشْهِدَ فِي رَبِيْعٍ الأَوَّلِ، سَنَةَ اثْنَتَيْ عَشْرَةَ⁩»** for ⁨زيد بن الخطاب ؓ⁩ at ⁨اليمامة⁩ (⁨سیر ج۱ ص۲۹۸⁩)
— which is **about a year**, not ten months. Say «the year before», or say nothing.

### 7.5 «⁨فأضجعوني بينهما⁩» — and how they were found

> <div dir="rtl">⁨فلما صرع قال لأصحابه⁩: ⁨ما فعل أبو حُذَيْفة؟ قالوا⁩: ⁨قُتل، قال⁩: ⁨فما فعلَ فلان؟ قالوا⁩: ⁨قُتل، قال⁩: ⁨فأضجعوني بينهما⁩.</div>
>
> — ⁨البدایہ والنہایہ ج۷ ص۵۰⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/30097/3196
> *"When he was struck down he said to his companions: What has become of Abū Ḥudhayfa? They said:
> He has been killed. He said: And what has become of so-and-so? They said: He has been killed. He
> said: Then lay me down between the two of them."*

**⚠ «⁨فلان⁩» — the second man is not named in the source.** The report preserves that ⁨سالم ؓ⁩ asked
about a second man and that the man was dead; it does not preserve who he was. **Say «and one other
man» and do not supply a name.** This precision is what makes the sentence trustworthy.

And, separately, ⁨الذہبی⁩ on how they were found:

> <div dir="rtl">⁨وَقِيْلَ⁩: ⁨إِنَّ سَالِماً وُجِدَ هُوَ وَمَوْلاَهُ أَبُو حُذَيْفَةَ، رَأْسُ أَحَدِهِمَا عِنْدَ رِجْلَي الآخَرِ صَرِيْعَيْنِ⁩ -⁨رَضِيَ اللهُ عَنْهُمَا⁩-.</div>
>
> — ⁨سیر أعلام النبلاء ج۱ ص۱۶۹⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/10906/1595
> *"And it is said: Sālim was found, he and his patron Abū Ḥudhayfa, the head of one of them at the
> feet of the other, both struck down ؓ."*

**⚠ «⁨وَقِيْلَ⁩» is ⁨الذہبی⁩'s own hedge and must be carried across into the delivery** — *it is said
that…* The editor points to ⁨مستدرك الحاكم ٣⁩/٢٢٥. **Nothing in any approved source says they were
buried in one grave** — see §10.3.

And ⁨سیر⁩'s roll of the day's martyrs opens with them, in that order:

> <div dir="rtl">⁨وَاسْتُشْهِدَ يَوْمَئِذٍ مِنْ أَصْحَابِ رَسُوْلِ اللهِ ﷺ وَغَيْرِهِم نَحْوٌ مِنْ سِتِّ مَائَةٍ، مِنْهُم⁩: ⁨أَبُو حُذَيْفَةَ بنُ عُتْبَةَ العَبْشَمِيُّ، وَمَوْلاَهُ سَالِمٌ أَحَدُ القُرَّاءِ⁩ …</div>
>
> — ⁨سیر أعلام النبلاء ج۱ ص۲۹۸⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/10906/1724
> *"About six hundred of the Companions of the Messenger of Allah ﷺ and others were martyred that
> day; among them: Abū Ḥudhayfa b. ʿUtba al-ʿAbshamī, and his freedman Sālim, one of the reciters…"*

### 7.6 The estate — two different accounts

| | البدایہ ج۷ ص۵۰ | سیر ج۱ ص۱۶۹ |
|---|---|---|
| What ⁨عمر ؓ⁩ did | sent the inheritance **to the woman who had freed him** | **sold** the inheritance, realising **200 dirhams** |
| What happened to it | she refused it — «⁨إنّما أعْتَقَتْهُ سائبةٌ⁩» — so **⁨عمر ؓ⁩ put it in ⁨بيت المال⁩** | he **gave it to «⁨أُمَّه⁩»** and said «⁨كليها⁩» |
| Route | ⁨ابن کثیر⁩'s own text | ⁨عبيد بن أبي الجعد⁩ ← ⁨عبد الله بن الهاد⁩ |

> <div dir="rtl">⁨وقد بعثَ عمرُ بميراثه إلى مولاته التي أعْتَقَتْه⁩ "⁨بثينة⁩" ⁨فردَّتْه وقالتْ⁩: ⁨إنّما أعْتَقَتْهُ سائبةٌ، فجعله عمر في بيت المال⁩.</div>
>
> — ⁨البدایہ والنہایہ ج۷ ص۵۰⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/30097/3196
> *"ʿUmar sent his inheritance to the woman who had freed him, Buthayna; and she sent it back and
> said: I freed him only as sāʾiba. So ʿUmar put it into the treasury."*

**⚠ Two irreconcilable accounts of one estate. Do not merge them and do not tell both.** The ⁨البدایہ⁩
account is the stronger telling and the more useful: **the woman who had freed him refused to profit
by him, on the ground that she had freed him outright and had never expected a return.** If the card
is used, caption it to ⁨البدایہ ج۷ ص۵۰⁩ alone and say nothing about 200 dirhams.

## 8. ⁨عمر ؓ⁩ on ⁨سالم ؓ⁩ — two reports, both to be handled carefully

### 8.1 البدایہ ج۷ ص۵۰

> <div dir="rtl">⁨وروي عن عمر أنه قال لما احتُضِر⁩: ⁨لو كانَ سالمٌ حيًّا لما جعلتها شُورى، قال أبو عمر بن عبد البر⁩: ⁨معناه أنّه كان يصدرُ عن رأيه فيمن يُولّيه الخلافةَ⁩.</div>
>
> — ⁨البدایہ والنہایہ ج۷ ص۵۰⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/30097/3196
> *"And it is related of ʿUmar that he said, when death was upon him: Had Sālim been alive I would
> not have made it a shūrā. Abū ʿUmar b. ʿAbd al-Barr said: the meaning of it is that he would have
> proceeded on his opinion as to whom he would appoint to the caliphate."*

⚠ **«⁨وروي عن عمر⁩» — Ibn Kathīr's own passive, meaning the report is transmitted without his
endorsing it.** Carry that across: *"it is related that…"*. And the gloss is **⁨ابن عبد البر⁩'s**, not
Ibn Kathīr's — attribute it if it is used.

### 8.2 ⁨سیر ج۱ ص۱۷۰⁩, and ⁨الذہبی⁩ weakening it in front of the reader

⁨الذہبی⁩ places this under the heading **«⁨وَمِنْ مَنَاقِبِ سَالِمٍ⁩»** — *among Sālim's merits* — and
gives it with his full chain (⁨أحمد⁩ ← ⁨عفان⁩ ← ⁨حماد⁩ ← ⁨علي بن زيد⁩ ← ⁨أبي رافع⁩). ⚠ **The heading itself is
the last line of ⁨ج۱ ص۱۶۹⁩** (index 1595); the report below runs on ⁨ج۱ ص۱۷۰⁩ (index 1596):

> <div dir="rtl">⁨ثُمَّ قَالَ⁩: ⁨لَوْ أَدْرَكَنِي أَحَدُ رَجُلَيْنِ، ثُمَّ جَعَلْتُ إِلَيْهِ الأَمْرَ، لَوَثِقْتُ بِهِ⁩: ⁨سَالِمٌ مَوْلَى أَبِي حُذَيْفَةَ، وَأَبُو عُبَيْدَةَ بنُ الجَرَّاحِ⁩.<br>⁨عَلِيُّ بنُ زَيْدٍ⁩: ⁨لَيِّنٌ، فَإِنْ صَحَّ هَذَا، فَهُوَ دَالٌّ عَلَى جَلاَلَةِ هَذَيْنِ فِي نَفْسِ عُمَرَ، وَذَلِكَ عَلَى أَنَّهُ يُجَوِّزُ الإِمَامَةَ فِي غَيْرِ القُرَشِيِّ، وَاللهُ أَعْلَمُ⁩.</div>
>
> — ⁨سیر أعلام النبلاء ج۱ ص۱۷۰⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/10906/1596
> *"Then he said: If either of two men had lived to see me, and I had committed the affair to him, I
> would have trusted him: Sālim the freedman of Abū Ḥudhayfa, and Abū ʿUbayda b. al-Jarrāḥ. —
> ʿAlī b. Zayd is weak. **If this is sound**, then it indicates the standing of these two in ʿUmar's
> own estimation; and that on the basis that he permits the imāmate in a non-Qurashī. And Allah
> knows best."*

**This is the best «⁨کیسے پتا چلا؟⁩» item in the whole strand and it should be used as one.** In three
lines on one page a historian: names the report's weak link; refuses to build on it («⁨فإن صح هذا⁩»);
says what it *would* show if sound; and then names the theological consequence that would follow —
that ⁨عمر ؓ⁩ would have been permitting the imāmate outside ⁨قريش⁩. **He does all of that, and then
writes «⁨والله أعلم⁩» and stops.** That is the method the course is trying to show the room, performed
on a page about a freed slave.

**⚠ Do not put the non-Qurashī point on a slide, and do not develop it aloud.** Report it only if a
written slip raises it, and report it exactly as ⁨الذہبی⁩ frames it: conditional, unverified, and left
to Allah's knowledge.

**How the two reports relate.** They are not the same sentence. ⁨البدایہ⁩'s is about the ⁨شورى⁩;
⁨سیر⁩'s is about two named men and it names **⁨أبو عبيدة ؓ⁩ alongside ⁨سالم ؓ⁩**. Do not merge.
See also `docs/research/nahawand-the-shura-and-the-death-of-umar.md` §E-U18, which handles the same
moment from ⁨عمر ؓ⁩'s side.

## 9. Numbers, and why they are unreliable

Early figures of this kind are **transmitted, not audited**. Give them as the source gives them, and
say so.

| Figure | As the source gives it | Where |
|---|---|---|
| ⁨أبو حذيفة ؓ⁩'s age at death | «**⁨ثلاثًا وخمسين سنة⁩**» — introduced with «⁨قِيْلَ⁩» | ⁨سیر ج۱ ص۱۶۷⁩ |
| — same | «**⁨ثلاثًا أو أربعًا وخمسين سنة⁩**» | ⁨البدایہ ج۷ ص۵۵⁩ |
| The first party to Abyssinia | «**⁨أحد عشر رجلًا وأربع نسوة⁩**», ship hired for «**⁨نصف دينار⁩**» — ⁨ابن کثیر⁩ reporting **⁨الواقدي⁩** | ⁨البدایہ ج۳ ص۲۸۱⁩ |
| Muslim dead at ⁨اليمامة⁩ | «**⁨نَحْوٌ مِنْ سِتِّ مَائَةٍ⁩**» | ⁨سیر ج۱ ص۲۹۸⁩ |
| — same | «**⁨أربعمئة وخمسون⁩** ⁨من حَملة القرآن ومن الصَّحابة وغيرهم⁩» | ⁨البدایہ ج۷ ص۵۵⁩ |

approved books is 450 to 1,080 (ridda note §Y). Put no casualty figure on a slide without the
range and the label.**

`ridda-campaign-the-conduct-of-the-wars.md` §6.10.

⚠ **The two books are NOT consistent here, and an earlier draft of this note had it backwards.**
- **⁨سیر ج۱ ص۱۶۶⁩ says ⁨۱۲ھ⁩ in words:** «⁨اسْتُشْهِدَ أَبُو حُذَيْفَةَ⁩ … ⁨يَوْمَ اليَمَامَةِ، سَنَةَ اثْنَتَيْ عَشْرَةَ،⁩
  ⁨هُوَ وَمَوْلاَهُ سَالِمٌ⁩». `[SOURCED]`
- **⁨البدایہ⁩ gives no year in either obituary, and files both under ⁨۱۱ھ⁩.** ⁨ابن کثیر⁩'s «⁨ذكر من توفي في⁩
  ⁨هذه السنة⁩» opens at **⁨ج۷ ص۴۳⁩** (index 3189) inside **«⁨سنة إحدى عشرة من الهجرة⁩»** (⁨ج۷ ص۵⁩, index
  3151); «⁨سنة اثنتي عشرة من الهجرة النبوية⁩» does not open until **⁨ج۷ ص۶۱⁩** (index 3207). Both ⁨سالم ؓ⁩
  (⁨ص۵۰⁩) and ⁨أبو حذيفة ؓ⁩ (⁨ص۵۵⁩) sit inside the ⁨۱۱ھ⁩ chapter. `[SOURCED]`
- That is not a contradiction ⁨ابن کثیر⁩ was unaware of: on ⁨ج۷ ص۳۵⁩–۳۶ he reconciles the two datings
  himself — «⁨والجمع⁩ [⁨ص٣٦⁩] ⁨بينها أن ابتداءها في سنة إحدى عشرة، والفراغ منها في سنة ثنتي عشرة⁩»
  (⁨البدایہ ج۷ ص۳۵⁩–۳۶ — the sentence straddles the break: «⁨والجمع⁩» is the last word of ⁨ص۳۵⁩, index
  3181, and the rest opens ⁨ص۳۶⁩, index 3182; ridda note §6.10).

**What the speaker may say:** *the sources put it at the turn of the year — ⁨۱۱ھ⁩ into ⁨۱۲ھ⁩; ⁨الذہبی⁩
writes ⁨۱۲ھ⁩.* **Do not say "both books say ⁨۱۲ھ⁩".**

## 10. What could NOT be established

**This section is as valuable as the findings.** Each item names a story, says where it was looked
for, and says what was found instead.

1. **That ⁨أبو حذيفة ؓ⁩ fought his father at ⁨بدر⁩, or killed him.** This is the version most often
   retold, and **no approved source says it.** What exists is one line at **⁨سیر ج۱ ص۱۶۵⁩**, from
   **⁨أبو الزناد⁩ without a chain**: «⁨أَنَّ أَبَا حُذَيْفَةَ بنَ عُتْبَةَ⁩ **⁨دَعَا⁩** ⁨يَوْمَ بَدْرٍ أَبَاهُ⁩
   ⁨إِلَى البرَازِ⁩» — that he *called* his father to single combat. The books then say the father was
   in fact met by **⁨عبيدة بن الحارث ؓ⁩**, and finished by **⁨حمزة ؓ⁩ and ⁨علي ؓ⁩** (⁨البدایہ ج۴ ص۶۰⁩), or
   killed by **⁨حمزة ؓ⁩** (⁨سیر ج۱ ص۱۷۲⁩). Phrase searches of all three narrative works for «⁨فقتل أباه⁩»
   and for «⁨أبو حذيفة⁩» with «⁨قتل أباه⁩» returned **nothing**. **The speaker must not say that
   ⁨أبو حذيفة ؓ⁩ fought or killed his father.** The sourced sentence is: *his father and his brother
   were killed at Badr, and he was on the other side.*
2. **A ṣaḥīḥ, chain-verified text for the ⁨القليب⁩ exchange.** ⁨البدایہ ج۴ ص۹۱⁩ gives it through
   ⁨ابن اسحاق⁩ with «**⁨فيما بلغني⁩**» — a ⁨بلاغ⁩. ⁨سیر⁩ (⁨السیرة⁩) ⁨ص۳۱۲⁩ gives it «**⁨وعن أنس⁩**», which is
   better but still without a chain printed on the page. **No ⁨صحیح بخاری⁩/⁨مسلم⁩ text for this incident
   was found within the approved list.** Say so if asked — it is the same candour the course applies
   to ⁨سہیل ؓ⁩.
3. **That the two were buried together, or in one grave.** **Nothing in the approved list says
   this.** What the sources say is two different things: **⁨سالم ؓ⁩ asked to be laid down between
   ⁨أبو حذيفة ؓ⁩ and one other man** (⁨البدایہ ج۷ ص۵۰⁩), and **«⁨وقيل⁩» they were found fallen head-to-feet**
   (⁨سیر ج۱ ص۱۶۹⁩). A phrase search of the three narrative works for «⁨قبر واحد⁩» with «⁨اليمامة⁩»
   returned nothing. **Do not say "they were buried in one grave."**
4. **The name of the second man ⁨سالم ؓ⁩ asked about.** The source has **«⁨فلان⁩»** (⁨البدایہ ج۷ ص۵۰⁩).
   Popular retellings supply a name. **Do not.**
5. **The name «⁨سلمى⁩» for the woman who freed ⁨سالم ؓ⁩.** The approved pages give **⁨ثبيتة بنت يعار⁩**
   (⁨سیر ج۱ ص۱۶۷⁩; ⁨البدایہ ج۷ ص۵۰⁩), **⁨بثينة⁩** (⁨البدایہ ج۷ ص۵۰⁩, later in the same entry), and — in the

### E-AS01 · The ship they hired for half a dinar
**Tier:** GOOD · **When:** ۵ years after the beginning of revelation `[SOURCED]` (⁨البدایہ ج۳ ص۲۸۱⁩,

**What happened:** The persecution in Mecca got past bearing. A small party left for Abyssinia,

### E-AS02 · The son of the elder of the Jāhiliyya
**Tier:** CORE · **When:** before ⁨دار الأرقم⁩ `[SOURCED]` · **Map:** n/a. One line of text, large.

**What happened:** Abū Ḥudhayfa ؓ was the son of ʿUtba b. Rabīʿa — one of the great men of Quraysh

### E-AS03 · Badr — the father and the brother
**Tier:** CORE · **When:** ⁨۲ھ⁩ `[SOURCED]` · **Map:** **⁨بدر⁩**, the first battle marker on the line.

**What happened:** Badr opened with single combat. Quraysh called for their equals to come out. The
Prophet ﷺ sent ʿUbayda b. al-Ḥārith ؓ, Ḥamza ؓ and ʿAlī ؓ. Against them came ʿUtba b. Rabīʿa, his

of Abū Ḥudhayfa ؓ, and al-Walīd was his brother — and Abū Ḥudhayfa ؓ was standing with the Muslims.

**The statement:**

### E-AS04 · The face the Prophet ﷺ noticed at the well
**Tier:** CORE · **When:** ⁨۲ھ⁩, after ⁨بدر⁩ `[SOURCED]` · **Map:** n/a — the same ⁨بدر⁩ marker.

**What happened:** After the battle the dead of Quraysh were put into the well. ʿUtba b. Rabīʿa was
taken and dragged to it. The Prophet ﷺ looked at the face of his son, and saw it had gone pale. So
he asked him whether something had entered him about his father. The answer was not a denial of
grief. He said he had never doubted his father's fate, but that he had known a judgement and a
forbearance in the man and had hoped it would bring him to Islam — and it had not. The Prophet ﷺ
prayed for him, and spoke kindly to him.

council at all. Ibn ʿAbd al-Barr ⁨رحمہ اللہ⁩ explains what that means: he would have gone by Sālim's ؓ
judgement about whom to appoint.

**The statement:**
> وروي عن عمر أنه قال لما احتُضِر: لو كانَ سالمٌ حيًّا لما جعلتها شُورى
> — ⁨البدایہ والنہایہ ج۷ ص۵۰⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/30097/3196
> *English:* "And it is related of ʿUmar that he said, when death was upon him: Had Sālim been alive
> I would not have made it a shūrā."

**⁨عبرت⁩:** Eleven years later, the man the Commander of the Faithful wished were still in the room
was a freed slave who had died holding a banner.

**Hands-up?** no

⚠ Say **"it is related that…"** — Ibn Kathīr's own passive. A second, differently worded report in
⁨سیر ج۱ ص۱۷۰⁩ names **both ⁨سالم ؓ⁩ and ⁨أبو عبيدة ؓ⁩**, and **⁨الذہبی⁩ weakens its chain on the page**.
Do not merge the two.

### E-AS19 · The inheritance that was sent back
**Tier:** GOOD · **When:** after ⁨۱۲ھ⁩ `[SOURCED]` · **Map:** n/a.

**What happened:** Sālim ؓ died leaving property and no heir of his own. Under the law of walāʾ it
went to the woman who had freed him, so ʿUmar ؓ sent it to her. She sent it back. Her reason was
that she had freed him outright — sāʾiba — and had never freed him expecting a return. ʿUmar ؓ put
the estate into the treasury instead.

**The statement:**
> وقد بعثَ عمرُ بميراثه إلى مولاته التي أعْتَقَتْه "بثينة" فردَّتْه وقالتْ: إنّما أعْتَقَتْهُ سائبةٌ، فجعله عمر في بيت المال
> — ⁨البدایہ والنہایہ ج۷ ص۵۰⁩ · https://shamela.ws/book/30097/3196
> *English:* "ʿUmar sent his inheritance to the woman who had freed him, Buthayna; and she sent it
> back and said: I freed him only as sāʾiba. So ʿUmar put it into the treasury."
>
> *(⚠ The name «⁨بثينة⁩» appears in the printed text here and «⁨ثُبَيْتة⁩» a few lines above it on the
> same page. **Do not read the name aloud** — say "the woman who had freed him". See §4.2.)*

**⁨عبرت⁩:** She had a legal claim on what he left, and she said she had not freed him in order to
have one.

**Hands-up?** no

⚠ **⁨سیر ج۱ ص۱۶۹⁩ tells the same estate differently** (sold for 200 dirhams and given to «⁨أمه⁩»).
**Tell one version only, captioned to ⁨البدایہ ج۷ ص۵۰⁩.** See §7.6.
