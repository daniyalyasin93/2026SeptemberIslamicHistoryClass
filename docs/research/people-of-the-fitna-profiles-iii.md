# People of the fitna — Profiles III: الشام, the governors, and the house of the Prophet ﷺ

**Question this note answers.** Session 3 («پہلا امتحان», ۲۳–۴۰ھ) cannot be told unless the room can
put a face and a clan to every name that appears in it. This note supplies the تراجم register for the
twelve remaining principals of that window: the governor of الشام who becomes the counter-pole to
الکوفہ; the grandson who ends the war; the cousin who becomes the ummah's exegete; the secretary
whose hand is said to be on the fatal letter; the governors of الکوفہ and البصرہ whose removal is what
the complainants demanded; the Yemeni convert whose sayings are quoted about عثمان ؓ; the tribal chief
of طیّئ who crosses from one caliphate to the next; the daughter married into the house of عمر ؓ; the
reciter whose مصحف stands behind the جمع; and the Kufan jurist through whom much of علي's ؓ practice is
transmitted. For each: clan, kin, what he did before ۲۳ھ, what he did in this window, one scene worth
telling, his own words where the page carries them, and how he died.

It answers a second question as well, and this is the one that matters most for session 3: **where does
الذهبي رحمہ اللہ himself refuse a report?** Those refusals — he calls some reports ظاهرة الوضع, he says of
another that الواقدي لا يعي ما يقول, he says of a third لا يصح — are recorded here verbatim with their
pages, because they are what lets the speaker decline to adjudicate from the platform without appearing
to duck the question.

1. معاویۃ ؓ is معاویۃ بن أبي سفيان صخر بن حرب بن أمیۃ بن عبد شمس بن عبد مناف بن قصي — the أموي line of
   قریش; his mother is هند بنت عتبۃ بن ربیعۃ, also of عبد شمس. `[SOURCED]` سیر ج۳ ص۱۱۹، ج۳ ص۱۲۰.
2. الذهبي رحمہ اللہ heads his notice **أمير المؤمنين، ملك الإسلام، أبو عبد الرحمن القرشي الأموي المكي**.
   `[SOURCED]` سیر ج۳ ص۱۲۰.
3. It is said he became Muslim before his father, at عمرۃ القضاء, and kept it hidden; his Islam became
   public only on the day of the فتح. `[SOURCED]` سیر ج۳ ص۱۲۰، ج۳ ص۱۲۲.
4. He wrote for the Prophet ﷺ. `[SOURCED]` سیر ج۳ ص۱۲۳.
5. عمر ؓ appointed him over الشام after the death of his brother يزيد بن أبي سفيان; **عثمان ؓ confirmed him

6. He governed الشام as deputy for twenty years and held the caliphate twenty years. `[SOURCED]`
   سیر ج۳ ص۱۳۳.
7. قیساریۃ was taken in ۱۹ھ with معاویۃ ؓ as its commander; he raided قبرص in ۲۵ھ. `[SOURCED]`

   سیر ج۳ ص۱۳۱. This is the single most useful sentence in the strand.

## 1. معاوية بن أبي سفيان ؓ

### The تراجم notice, in one breath

- **Name + لقب:** مُعَاوِيَةُ بنُ أَبِي سُفْيَانَ صَخْرِ بنِ حَرْبٍ الأُمَوِيُّ ؓ, أبو عبد الرحمن — الذهبي رحمہ اللہ
  heads the notice **«أَمِيْرُ المُؤْمِنِيْنَ، مَلِكُ الإِسْلاَمِ، أَبُو عَبْدِ الرَّحْمَنِ القُرَشِيُّ، الأُمَوِيُّ، المَكِّيُّ»**.
  `[SOURCED]` سیر ج۳ ص۱۱۹–۱۲۰. *He has no لقب from the tradition beyond this; do not invent one.*
- **Dates:** Islam at عمرۃ القضاء (۷ھ), made public at the فتح (۸ھ) `[SOURCED]` سیر ج۳ ص۱۲۰، ج۳ ص۱۲۲ ·
  governor of الشام under عمر ؓ and عثمان ؓ, twenty years as deputy `[SOURCED]` سیر ج۳ ص۱۳۳ · caliph twenty
  years `[SOURCED]` سیر ج۳ ص۱۳۳ · died ۶۰ھ `[STANDARD]` *(to verify — the death notice is not on the pages
  read for this note).* **On the Line:** he occupies the whole right-hand end of session 3 and the
  opening of session 4.
- **What he did, one sentence:** he held الشام for عمر ؓ and عثمان ؓ as a frontier province facing الروم,
  and after the killing of عثمان ؓ he became the pole around which الشام stood apart from الکوفہ.

### Clan and kin

بنو أمية بن عبد شمس of قریش — the same بطن as عثمان ؓ, مروان بن الحكم, الوليد بن عقبة ؓ, سعيد بن العاص ؓ and
عبد الله بن عامر ؓ. His father is أبو سفيان صخر بن حرب; his mother **هند بنت عتبة بن ربيعة**, herself of
عبد شمس `[SOURCED]` سیر ج۳ ص۱۲۰. His sister is **أم حبيبة ؓ**, a mother of the believers — so he is a
brother-in-law of the Prophet ﷺ, and this is why he narrates from her `[SOURCED]` سیر ج۳ ص۱۲۰. His
brother **يزيد بن أبي سفيان ؓ** held الشام before him `[SOURCED]` سیر ج۳ ص۱۳۲. عمرو بن العاص ؓ is addressed
on these pages as his **ابن عم** `[SOURCED]` سیر ج۳ ص۱۲۵. The room should be able to see that عثمان ؓ,
معاویۃ ؓ, مروان, الوليد ؓ, سعيد ؓ and ابن عامر ؓ are all one house: that is the whole complaint about the
governors in one picture.

### Before ۲۳ھ, in three or four sentences

He is one of the كُتّاب of the Prophet ﷺ. الذهبي رحمہ اللہ transmits from عبد الله بن عمرو ؓ simply
«كَانَ مُعَاوِيَةُ يَكْتُبُ لِرَسُوْلِ اللهِ ﷺ» `[SOURCED]` سیر ج۳ ص۱۲۳, and from المفضّل الغلابي the division
of labour: زيد بن ثابت ؓ wrote the revelation, and معاویۃ ؓ wrote what passed between the Prophet ﷺ and
the Arabs `[SOURCED]` سیر ج۳ ص۱۲۲–۱۲۳. He fought at حنین `[SOURCED]` سیر ج۳ ص۱۲۲. عمر ؓ appointed him over
الشام on the death of his brother يزيد ؓ, and خليفة رحمہ اللہ records that عمر ؓ then gathered all of
الشام for him **and عثمان ؓ confirmed him** `[SOURCED]` سیر ج۳ ص۱۳۲. قیساریۃ was taken in ۱۹ھ with معاویۃ ؓ
as its commander, according to أحمد بن حنبل رحمہ اللہ `[SOURCED]` سیر ج۳ ص۱۳۵.

### In this window (۲۳–۴۱ھ)

- **عثمان ؓ removed عمير بن سعد ؓ from حمص and put معاویۃ ؓ in his place, joining the whole of الشام to
  him.** This is stated twice on the pages read — once in a narrative of how the people of حمص took the
  news hard, and once from الزهري رحمہ اللہ. `[SOURCED]` سیر ج۳ ص۱۲۵–۱۲۶، ج۳ ص۱۳۵.
- الزهري رحمہ اللہ is explicit that **معاویۃ ؓ did not hold الشام alone until عثمان ؓ was made caliph**;
  الذهبي رحمہ اللہ notes a competing report that عمر ؓ singled him out with الشام at eighty dinars a month
  and says **«وَالمَحْفُوْظُ: أَنَّ الَّذِي أَفْرَدَ مُعَاوِيَةَ بِالشَّامِ عُثْمَانُ»** — the better-preserved report is
  that it was عثمان ؓ. `[SOURCED]` سیر ج۳ ص۱۳۳، ج۳ ص۱۳۵. *This matters: the concentration of الشام in one
  hand is dated to عثمان's ؓ caliphate by الذهبي رحمہ اللہ himself.*
- **He raided قبرص in ۲۵ھ**, on the report of يزيد بن عبيدة. `[SOURCED]` سیر ج۳ ص۱۳۵. This is the first
  sea expedition named in this window and belongs on the map.
- الذهبي رحمہ اللہ's summary of the reign that follows: twenty years as deputy, twenty as caliph; no one
  rose against him in his state; his rule ran over الحرمین، مصر، الشام، العراق، خراسان، فارس، الجزیرة،
  الیمن and المغرب. `[SOURCED]` سیر ج۳ ص۱۳۳.

### One واقعہ worth telling — عمر ؓ and the great retinue at the Syrian frontier

عمر ؓ came to الشام and معاویۃ ؓ met him in a great procession, in state. عمر ؓ asked him: are you the man
of the great procession? He said yes. And what reaches me about people with needs standing long at your
door? He said yes. Why do you do it? He answered: we are in a land where the enemy's spies are many, so
we must show enough of the authority's dignity to frighten them — and if you forbid me, I stop. عمر ؓ
said: I ask you about nothing but you leave me as though between the sockets of a tooth. If what you say
is true, it is the judgement of a shrewd man; and if it is false, it is the trick of a clever one. He
said: then command me. عمر ؓ said: **I neither command you nor forbid you.**

> قَالَ: يَا مُعَاوِيَةُ! مَا أَسْأَلُكَ عَنْ شَيْءٍ إِلاَّ تَرَكْتَنِي فِي مِثْلِ رَوَاجِبِ الضَّرِسِ، لَئِنْ كَانَ مَا قُلْتَ حَقّاً، إِنَّهُ لَرَأْيُ أَرِيْبٍ، وَإِنْ كَانَ بَاطِلاً، فَإِنَّهُ لَخُدْعَةُ أَدِيْبٍ.
> — سیر أعلام النبلاء ج۳ ص۱۳۳ · https://shamela.ws/book/10906/2742
> *English:* "He said: Muʿāwiyah! I ask you about nothing but you leave me as though between the sockets
> of a tooth. If what you have said is true, then it is the judgement of a shrewd man; and if it is
> false, then it is the trick of a clever one."

The editor's footnote records that ابن أبي الدنيا رحمہ اللہ transmitted it and that ابن كثير رحمہ اللہ
carried it at البدایۃ ۸/۱۲۴ `[SOURCED]` سیر ج۳ ص۱۳۳ (printed footnote).

### A second واقعہ — the green suit and the درّة

معاویۃ ؓ came in to عمر ؓ wearing a green suit and the Companions looked at it. عمر ؓ sprang at him with
the درّة, معاویۃ ؓ saying "God, God, Commander of the Believers! For what? For what?" — and عمر ؓ did not
speak to him until he had gone back. They said: why did you strike him, when there is none like him among
your people? He said: I have seen nothing of him and heard nothing of him but good — but I saw him (and
he gestured with his hand), and **I wanted to bring him down a little**.

> قَالَ: مَا رَأَيْتُ وَمَا بَلَغَنِي إِلاَّ خَيْراً، وَلَكِنَّهُ رَأَيْتُهُ - وَأَشَارَ بِيَدِهِ - فَأَحْبَبْتُ أَنْ أَضَعَ مِنْهُ (١) .
> — سیر أعلام النبلاء ج۳ ص۱۳۵ · https://shamela.ws/book/10906/2744
> *English:* "He said: I have seen nothing and heard nothing of him but good — but I saw him (and he
> gestured with his hand), and I wanted to bring him down a little."

The `(١)` is the printed edition's own footnote marker; the footnote records that ابن كثير رحمہ اللہ
carries the report at ۸/۱۲۵ and adds at its end «ما شمخ» `[SOURCED]` سیر ج۳ ص۱۳۵ (printed footnote).

### A third واقعہ — the perfumed suit at ذو طویٰ

معاویۃ ؓ went out to الحج with عمر ؓ. عمر ؓ would look at him and marvel, and put his finger on his back
and lift it off a strap's thickness of flesh, saying: excellent, excellent — we are the best of people
then, if the good of this world and the next is gathered for us. معاویۃ ؓ said: Commander of the
Believers, we are in a land of bath-houses and rich country. عمر ؓ said: I will tell you — it is nothing
but your indulging yourself with the best food, and sleeping into the morning until the sun strikes your
back, while those with needs are behind the door. Then at ذو طویٰ معاویۃ ؓ brought out a suit and put it
on, and عمر ؓ caught its scent and objected that a man goes out on الحج unperfumed, and then at the place
God has made most inviolable brings out two garments as if they had been kept in perfume, and puts them
on. معاویۃ ؓ said he had put them on only to come before his own clan — and then he took the two garments
off and put on his two إحرام garments. `[SOURCED]` سیر ج۳ ص۱۳۴.

### الذهبي's رحمہ اللہ own verdict, given whole

This is the passage the speaker should have in front of him, because it is a classical authority doing
exactly what the platform must do: weighing, conceding, and declining.

> وَإِنْ كَانَ غَيْرُهُ مِنْ أَصْحَابِ رَسُوْلِ اللهِ -صَلَّى اللَّهُ عَلَيْهِ وَسَلَّمَ - خَيْراً مِنْهُ بِكَثِيْرٍ، وَأَفْضَلَ، وَأَصْلَحَ، فَهَذَا الرَّجُلُ سَادَ وَسَاسَ العَالَمَ بِكَمَالِ عَقْلِهِ، وَفَرْطِ حِلْمِهِ، وَسَعَةِ نَفْسِهِ، وَقُوَّةِ دَهَائِهِ وَرَأْيِهِ. وَلَهُ هَنَاتٌ وَأُمُوْرٌ، وَاللهُ المَوْعِدُ.
> — سیر أعلام النبلاء ج۳ ص۱۳۳ · https://shamela.ws/book/10906/2742
> *English:* "And although others among the Companions of the Messenger of God ﷺ were far better than he,
> and more excellent, and more upright — this man led and governed the world by the completeness of his
> intellect, the abundance of his forbearance, the largeness of his soul, and the strength of his
> shrewdness and judgement. He has faults and matters against him, and God is the appointed reckoning."

And the non-adjudication passage, which is the guardrail itself in الذهبي's رحمہ اللہ voice:

> وَتَبَصَّرْنَا، فَعَذَرْنَا، وَاسْتَغْفَرْنَا، وَأَحْبَبْنَا بِاقْتِصَادٍ، وَتَرَحَّمْنَا عَلَى البُغَاةِ بِتَأْوِيْلٍ سَائِغٍ فِي الجُمْلَةِ، أَوْ بِخَطَأٍ - إِنْ شَاءَ اللهُ - مَغْفُوْرٍ، وَقُلْنَا كَمَا عَلَّمَنَا اللهُ: {رَبَّنَا اغْفِرْ لَنَا وَلإِخْوَانِنَا الَّذِيْنَ سَبَقُوْنَا بِالإِيْمَانِ، وَلاَ تَجْعَلْ فِي قُلُوْبِنَا غِلاًّ لِلَّذِيْنَ آمَنُوا} [الحَشْرُ: ١٠] .
> — سیر أعلام النبلاء ج۳ ص۱۲۸ · https://shamela.ws/book/10906/2737
> *English:* "And we looked closely, and excused, and sought forgiveness, and loved with moderation, and
> asked mercy upon those who transgressed — on an interpretation broadly defensible, or upon an error,
> God willing, forgiven — and we said as God taught us: 'Our Lord, forgive us and our brothers who
> preceded us in faith, and put not in our hearts rancour toward those who believed' [al-Ḥashr: 10]."

On the same page he names those who withdrew from both sides and says he is pleased with them too:

> وَتَرَضَّيْنَا أَيْضاً عَمَّنِ اعْتَزَلَ الفَرِيْقَيْنِ، كَسَعْدِ بنِ أَبِي وَقَّاصٍ، وَابْنِ عُمَرَ، وَمُحَمَّدِ بنِ مَسْلَمَةَ، وَسَعِيْدِ بنِ زَيْدٍ، وَخَلْقٍ.
> — سیر أعلام النبلاء ج۳ ص۱۲۸ · https://shamela.ws/book/10906/2737
> *English:* "And we are pleased also with those who withdrew from the two parties — such as Saʿd b. Abī
> Waqqāṣ, Ibn ʿUmar, Muḥammad b. Maslamah, Saʿīd b. Zayd, and a great many."

### The forgeries الذهبي رحمہ اللہ throws out

He first says that ابن عساكر رحمہ اللہ filled the notice with feeble and baseless reports:

> وَقَدْ سَاقَ ابْنُ عَسَاكِرَ فِي التَّرْجَمَةِ أَحَادِيْثَ وَاهِيَةً وَبَاطِلَةً، طَوَّلَ بِهَا جِدّاً (٦) .
> — سیر أعلام النبلاء ج۳ ص۱۲۷ · https://shamela.ws/book/10906/2736
> *English:* "Ibn ʿAsākir brought into the notice reports that are feeble and baseless, and lengthened it
> greatly with them."

He then lists them — among them that معاویۃ ؓ nearly was sent as a prophet for his forbearance; that
جبريل ؑ brought down a golden pen for him; that "the trustworthy ones are three: I, Jibrīl, and
Muʿāwiyah"; that love of معاویۃ ؓ is an obligation upon God's servants — and concludes:

> فَهَذِهِ الأَحَادِيْثُ ظَاهِرَةُ الوَضْعِ - وَاللهُ أَعْلَمُ - (١) .
> — سیر أعلام النبلاء ج۳ ص۱۳۱ · https://shamela.ws/book/10906/2740
> *English:* "These reports are plainly forged — and God knows best."

The printed footnote adds that most of them are collected by الشوكاني رحمہ اللہ in *الفوائد المجموعة في
الأحاديث الموضوعة* pp. ۴۰۳–۴۰۷, and that ابن كثير رحمہ اللہ, having quoted one of them at البدایۃ ۸/۱۲۰,
expressed astonishment that ابن عساكر رحمہ اللہ, with all his memory and range, did not warn against them
`[SOURCED]` سیر ج۳ ص۱۳۱ (printed footnote). On one of these — the quinces given to معاویۃ ؓ, reported from
جعفر ؓ — الذهبي رحمہ اللہ disposes of it by chronology in one line:

> قُلْتُ: وَجَعْفَرٌ قَدِ اسْتُشْهِدَ قَبْلَ قُدُوْمِ مُعَاوِيَةَ مُسْلِماً.
> — سیر أعلام النبلاء ج۳ ص۱۳۰ · https://shamela.ws/book/10906/2739
> *English:* "I say: Jaʿfar was martyred before Muʿāwiyah came as a Muslim."

That single line is the best available illustration, anywhere in this window, of **dating used as a test
of a report** — the exact principle the مقدمہ attributes to سفیان ثوری رحمہ اللہ. On the next page
الذهبي رحمہ اللہ quotes إسحاق ابن راهويه رحمہ اللہ:

> لاَ يَصِحُّ عَنِ النَّبِيِّ -صَلَّى اللَّهُ عَلَيْهِ وَسَلَّمَ - فِي فَضْلِ مُعَاوِيَةَ شَيْءٌ (١) .
> — سیر أعلام النبلاء ج۳ ص۱۳۲ · https://shamela.ws/book/10906/2741
> *English:* "Nothing is authentically established from the Prophet ﷺ concerning the merits of
> Muʿāwiyah."

**And he throws out the report on the other side too**, which is what makes him usable from a platform.
A report from أبو برزة ؓ has the Prophet ﷺ invoking against معاویۃ ؓ and عمرو ؓ; الذهبي رحمہ اللہ writes
after it `هَذَا مِمَّا أُنْكِرَ عَلَى يَزِيْدَ` — this is among what was held against يزيد بن أبي زياد
`[SOURCED]` سیر ج۳ ص۱۳۲. The printed footnote adds that ابن الجوزي رحمہ اللہ placed it in his
*الموضوعات* and said لا يصح `[SOURCED]` سیر ج۳ ص۱۳۲ (printed footnote).

Between the two extremes الذهبي رحمہ اللہ does keep a small number of reports he considers bearable —
«أحاديث مقاربة» — including the supplication اللَّهُمَّ عَلِّمْهُ الكِتَابَ وَالحِسَابَ وَقِهِ العَذَابَ and
اللَّهُمَّ اجْعَلْهُ هَادِياً مَهْدِيّاً وَاهْدِ بِهِ, the latter graded حسن by الترمذي رحمہ اللہ
`[SOURCED]` سیر ج۳ ص۱۲۴–۱۲۷.

### Death

**Not on the pages read for this note.** ۶۰ھ is the conventional date `[STANDARD]` *(to verify)*. The
death notice falls outside indices 2728–2744 and should be fetched before it is put on a slide.

### ⚠ What not to say about him from the platform

- Do not quote the praise-reports listed at سیر ج۳ ص۱۲۹–۱۳۱ **at all**. الذهبي رحمہ اللہ says they are
  plainly forged, and quoting a forgery from a platform in this room would be noticed.
- Do not quote the curse-report at سیر ج۳ ص۱۳۲ either. الذهبي رحمہ اللہ rejects it.
- Do not quote `لاَ أَشْبَعَ اللهُ بَطْنَهُ` on its own. It is authentic (مسلم), but الذهبي رحمہ اللہ pairs it
  immediately with the Prophet's ﷺ supplication that whoever of the ummah he reproached, let it be mercy
  for him. Either both or neither.
- Do not adjudicate الشام against العراق. The ج۳ ص۱۲۸ passage is the model: excuse, seek forgiveness,
  love with moderation, and stop.

## 2. الحسن بن علي بن أبي طالب ؓ

### The تراجم notice, in one breath

- **Name + لقب:** الحَسَنُ بنُ عَلِيِّ بنِ أَبِي طَالِبٍ بنِ عَبْدِ المُطَّلِبِ بنِ هَاشِمِ بنِ عَبْدِ مَنَافٍ ؓ, أبو محمد —
  الذهبي رحمہ اللہ heads him **«الإِمَامُ السَّيِّدُ، رَيْحَانَةُ رَسُوْلِ اللهِ ﷺ وَسِبْطُهُ، وَسَيِّدُ شَبَابِ أَهْلِ الجَنَّةِ»**.
  `[SOURCED]` سیر ج۳ ص۲۴۵–۲۴۶. Every one of those titles is from the tradition; none is ours.
- **Dates:** born **شعبان ۳ھ** in المدینہ — and it is also said the middle of رمضان of that year, but
  الذهبي رحمہ اللہ says **«وَفِي شَعْبَانَ أَصَحُّ»** `[SOURCED]` سیر ج۳ ص۲۴۶، ج۳ ص۲۴۸ · caliph on his
  father's death and **he remained in the caliphate seven months** `[SOURCED]` سیر ج۳ ص۲۶۰ · died ۴۹ھ or
  ۵۰ھ `[STANDARD]` *(to verify — the death notice is outside the pages read).* **On the Line:** ۴۰–۴۱ھ,
  the last peg of session 3.
- **What he did, one sentence:** he took the بیعہ after his father and then handed the authority over
  rather than let the fighting continue — the single act the Prophet ﷺ is authentically reported to have
  foretold of him.

### Clan and kin

بنو هاشم بن عبد مناف. Son of **علي بن أبي طالب ؓ** and **فاطمة ؓ**, and therefore the Prophet's ﷺ own
grandson — سبطه. Brother of **الحسين ؓ** and of **أم كلثوم بنت علي ؓ** (the three whose hair فاطمہ ؓ weighed
and gave its weight in silver) `[SOURCED]` سیر ج۳ ص۲۴۹. Through عبد مناف he and معاویۃ ؓ share an ancestor
four generations up: هاشم and عبد شمس were brothers. **That single fact — one house of عبد مناف, split
into هاشم and عبد شمس — is the whole geometry of the window,** and a slide can carry it as a two-branch
tree with nothing else on it.

### Before ۲۳ھ, in three or four sentences

He is the grandchild the Prophet ﷺ carried. علي ؓ wanted to name him حرب and the Prophet ﷺ named him
الحسن, and did the same again with الحسين — «إِنَّنِي سَمَّيْتُ ابْنَيَّ هَذَيْنِ بِاسْمِ ابْنَيْ هَرُوْنَ شَبَّرَ
وَشَبِيْرَ» `[SOURCED]` سیر ج۳ ص۲۴۷. أنس ؓ said he was the one most resembling the Prophet ﷺ, and the chain
for it is sound `[SOURCED]` سیر ج۳ ص۲۴۹ (printed footnote: `وهذا سند صحيح`). أبو بكر الصديق ؓ once
carried him on his shoulder after عصر reciting **«بأَبِي شَبِيْهٌ النَّبِيّ … لَيْسَ شَبِيْهٌ بِعَلِيّ»** while
علي ؓ smiled — البخاري carries it `[SOURCED]` سیر ج۳ ص۲۴۹. When عمر ؓ established the دیوان he attached
الحسن ؓ and الحسين ؓ to their father's stipend for their nearness to the Prophet ﷺ and set **five thousand
dirhams each** `[SOURCED]` سیر ج۳ ص۲۵۹ (the book's own attribution: الواقدي — so use the number only with
that attribution stated).

### In this window (۲۳–۴۱ھ)

- **He was quick to go to the help of عثمان ؓ and much given to defending him.** The sentence is short
  and it is exactly what a session-3 audience needs to hear from the family itself. `[SOURCED]`
  سیر ج۳ ص۲۶۰.
- **He held the caliphate seven months after his father.** `[SOURCED]` سیر ج۳ ص۲۶۰.
- علي ؓ once stood to address the people and said that الحسن ؓ had gathered wealth and meant to divide it
  among them `[SOURCED]` سیر ج۳ ص۲۶۰ (the passage breaks at the page end).
- On his generosity and حج in this period: ابن عباس ؓ said he regretted nothing of his youth except that
  he never made حج on foot, and that الحسن ؓ made **twenty-five حج on foot** with his riding-camels led
  along beside him, and divided his property with God three times — «حَتَّى إِنَّهُ يُعطِي الخُفَّ وَيُمْسِكُ
  النَّعْلَ». الذهبي رحمہ اللہ prints beside it a second report, from محمد بن سعد, giving **fifteen**
  instead of twenty-five. `[SOURCED]` سیر ج۳ ص۲۶۰، and the fifteen also at ج۳ ص۲۵۳. **Give both numbers or
  neither.** The printed footnote weakens the chain of the twenty-five (عبيد الله بن الوليد الوصافي
  ضعيف) while noting البخاري رحمہ اللہ mentions in تعليق that he made حج on foot with the mounts led
  before him `[SOURCED]` سیر ج۳ ص۲۶۰ (printed footnote).

### One واقعہ worth telling — the Prophet ﷺ on the منبر, with the boy beside him

أبو بكرة ؓ said: I saw the Messenger of God ﷺ on the pulpit with الحسن beside him, and he was saying —

> (إِنَّ ابْنِي هَذَا سَيِّدٌ، وَلَعَلَّ اللهَ أَنْ يُصْلِحَ بِهِ بَيْنَ فِئَتَيْنِ مِنَ المُسْلِمِيْنَ (١)) .
> — سیر أعلام النبلاء ج۳ ص۲۵۱ · https://shamela.ws/book/10906/2859
> *English:* "This son of mine is a chief, and it may be that God will make peace through him between two
> parties of the Muslims."

The printed footnote traces it to البخاري in four places — in فضائل أصحاب النبي ﷺ, in الصلح under a
chapter heading made out of this very hadith, in الأنبياء under علامات النبوة في الإسلام, and in العتق —
and to الترمذي، النسائي، أبو داود، الطبراني and أحمد `[SOURCED]` سیر ج۳ ص۲۵۱ (printed footnote). A second
chain, from جابر ؓ, is given at ج۳ ص۲۵۹ with the editor's note `إسناده قوي`.

**This is the load-bearing card of the whole strand.** It is authentic, it is in البخاري, it is spoken
about a child in the ۳۰s of the Prophet's ﷺ life about an event in ۴۱ھ, and it means the speaker never has
to characterise the صلح himself — the Prophet ﷺ already called it إصلاح between two parties **of the
Muslims**, which settles the standing of both parties without the platform saying a word.

### A second واقعہ — the long سجدہ

شداد ؓ said: the Messenger of God ﷺ came out to us carrying الحسن — or الحسين — and set him down and
began the prayer, and made one prostration so long that I raised my head, and there was the child on his
back; so I went back into my prostration. When he finished they said: you made it long! He said —

> (إِنَّ ابْنِي ارْتَحَلَنِي، فَكَرِهْتُ أَنْ أُعْجِلَهُ حَتَّى يَقْضِيَ حَاجَتَهُ (١)) .
> — سیر أعلام النبلاء ج۳ ص۲۵۷ · https://shamela.ws/book/10906/2865
> *English:* "My son made a mount of me, and I disliked to hurry him until he had finished what he
> wanted."

The printed footnote reads `إسناده صحيح` and traces it to المسند ۳/۴۹۳–۴۹۴ and النسائي `[SOURCED]`
سیر ج۳ ص۲۵۷ (printed footnote). الذهبي رحمہ اللہ's own comment beneath it is one line: **«قُلْتُ: أَيْنَ
الفَقِيْهُ المُتَنَطِّعُ عَنْ هَذَا الفِعْلِ؟»** — where is the over-rigorous jurist beside this act?
`[SOURCED]` سیر ج۳ ص۲۵۷.

### A third واقعہ — the date of صدقہ

أبو الحوراء asked him: what do you remember of the Messenger of God ﷺ? He said: I remember that I took a
date from the dates of the صدقہ and put it in my mouth, and the Messenger of God ﷺ took it out of my
mouth with its moisture on it and put it back among the dates. Someone said: Messenger of God, what harm
would this one date have done to this child? He said:

> (إِنَّا - آلَ مُحَمَّدٍ - لاَ تَحِلُّ لَنَا الصَّدَقَةُ) .
> — سیر أعلام النبلاء ج۳ ص۲۴۶ · https://shamela.ws/book/10906/2854
> *English:* "We — the family of Muḥammad — are not permitted charity."

### His own words, and the words said of him

The قنوت he was taught by the Prophet ﷺ, which he then taught on:

> عَلَّمَنِي رَسُوْلُ اللهِ -صَلَّى اللَّهُ عَلَيْهِ وَسَلَّمَ - كَلمَاتٍ أَقُوْلُهُنَّ فِي القُنُوتِ: (اللَّهُمَّ اهْدِنِي فِيْمَنْ هَدَيْتَ (١)) .
> — سیر أعلام النبلاء ج۳ ص۲۴۷ · https://shamela.ws/book/10906/2855
> *English:* "The Messenger of God ﷺ taught me words that I say in the qunūt: 'O God, guide me among
> those whom You have guided…'"

The counsel he transmitted from the Prophet ﷺ, which suits this window exactly:

> (دَعْ مَا يَرِيْبُكَ إِلَى مَا لاَ يَرِيْبُكَ، فَإِنَّ الصِّدْقَ طُمَأْنِيْنَةٌ، وَالكَذِبَ رِيْبَةٌ) .
> — سیر أعلام النبلاء ج۳ ص۲۴۶ · https://shamela.ws/book/10906/2854
> *English:* "Leave what makes you doubt for what does not make you doubt: truthfulness is tranquillity,
> and falsehood is doubt."

الذهبي's رحمہ اللہ own description of the man:

> وَقَدْ كَانَ هَذَا الإِمَامُ سَيِّداً، وَسِيماً، جَمِيْلاً، عَاقِلاً، رَزِيناً، جَوَاداً، مُمَدَّحاً، خَيِّراً، دَيِّناً، وَرِعاً، مُحتشِماً، كَبِيرَ الشَّأْنِ.
> — سیر أعلام النبلاء ج۳ ص۲۵۳ · https://shamela.ws/book/10906/2861
> *English:* "This imām was a chief, handsome, comely, intelligent, grave, generous, much praised, good,
> religious, scrupulous, dignified, of great standing."

### Death

**Not on the pages read for this note.** الذهبي رحمہ اللہ calls him **الشهيد** in the heading
`[SOURCED]` سیر ج۳ ص۲۴۶, which points forward to the manner of his death, but the death notice itself and
the year fall outside indices 2853–2868. ۴۹ھ/۵۰ھ is the conventional range `[STANDARD]` *(to verify)*.

### ⚠ What not to say about him from the platform

- Do not use the report at سیر ج۳ ص۲۵۴ («من أحب هذين وأباهما وأمهما كان معي في درجتي يوم القيامة»).
  الذهبي رحمہ اللہ writes under it `إِسْنَادُهُ ضَعِيْفٌ، وَالمَتْنُ مُنْكَرٌ` — the chain is weak and the text is
  objectionable. `[SOURCED]` سیر ج۳ ص۲۵۴.
- Do not use the أسامہ ؓ report at ج۳ ص۲۵۱–۲۵۲ («هذان ابناي وابنا بنتي…») as though it were established.
  الذهبي رحمہ اللہ says its حسن grade by الترمذي رحمہ اللہ is open to criticism — `فَهَذَا مِمَّا يُنتقَدُ
  تَحسينُهُ عَلَى التِّرْمِذِيِّ` — and the printed footnote reproduces his fuller judgement from تاریخ الإسلام
  that three of its narrators are unknown `[SOURCED]` سیر ج۳ ص۲۵۲. **There are authentic alternatives on
  the very next lines; use those.**
- The pages carry material about his marriages and divorces (سیر ج۳ ص۲۵۳). It is in the book, it is not
  in the story of ۲۳–۴۱ھ, and it is precisely the kind of detail a room remembers instead of the point.
  Leave it out.
- Never present the صلح as one party being right and the other wrong. The Prophet's ﷺ own wording —
  **فئتين من المسلمين** — is the whole framing, and it is enough.

## 3. عبد الله بن عباس ؓ

### The تراجم notice, in one breath

- **Name + لقب:** عَبْدُ اللهِ بنُ عَبَّاسٍ ؓ, أبو العباس — الذهبي رحمہ اللہ heads the notice
  **«عَبْدُ اللهِ بنُ عَبَّاسٍ البَحْرُ أَبُو العَبَّاسِ الهَاشِمِيُّ … حَبْرُ الأُمَّةِ، وَفَقِيْهُ العَصْرِ، وَإِمَامُ التَّفْسِيْرِ»**.
  `[SOURCED]` سیر ج۳ ص۳۳۱–۳۳۲. **البحر · حبر الأمة · ترجمان القرآن** — three ألقاب the tradition gave
  him, all on the page; none is ours.
- **Dates:** born in **شعب بني هاشم three years before the هجرہ**, while بنو هاشم were under the boycott
  `[SOURCED]` سیر ج۳ ص۳۳۲، ج۳ ص۳۳۵ · he kept the Prophet's ﷺ company about **thirty months**
  `[SOURCED]` سیر ج۳ ص۳۳۲ · died ۶۸ھ at الطائف `[STANDARD]` *(to verify — outside these pages).*
  **On the Line:** present from ۱۱ھ to the end of the window and well past it.
- **What he did, one sentence:** he made himself the ummah's student — going to the Anṣār's doors and
  waiting in the dust for men to wake — and became the reference for the Qurʾān's meaning, consulted by
  عمر ؓ ahead of men twice his age.

### Clan and kin

بنو هاشم. Son of **العباس بن عبد المطلب ؓ**, the Prophet's ﷺ paternal uncle — so he is the Prophet's ﷺ
**ابن عم** and a first cousin of علي ؓ, and the two families of هاشم stand together in this window. His
mother is **أم الفضل لبابة بنت الحارث الهلالية ؓ**, sister of **ميمونة ؓ**, a mother of the believers — which
is why he stays the night in ميمونة's ؓ house as a boy `[SOURCED]` سیر ج۳ ص۳۳۳، ج۳ ص۳۳۶، ج۳ ص۳۳۷.
الذهبي رحمہ اللہ adds one more link the room will enjoy: **«وَهُوَ ابْنُ خَالَةِ خَالِدِ بنِ الوَلِيْدِ
المَخْزُوْمِيِّ»** — he is the maternal cousin of خالد بن الوليد ؓ `[SOURCED]` سیر ج۳ ص۳۳۶. His son **علي**
is called on the page **أبو الخلفاء** — the ancestor of the عباسی caliphs, which is where session 5 starts
`[SOURCED]` سیر ج۳ ص۳۳۳.

### Before ۲۳ھ, in three or four sentences

He is a child at the Prophet's ﷺ death — the reports on the page put him at ten, at thirteen and at
fifteen, and الذهبي رحمہ اللہ prefers the birth in the شعب three years before the هجرہ, which makes him
thirteen `[SOURCED]` سیر ج۳ ص۳۳۵، ج۳ ص۳۳۶. أحمد بن حنبل رحمہ اللہ said the ten-year report is واهٍ in his
view `[SOURCED]` سیر ج۳ ص۳۳۶. The Prophet ﷺ prayed for him more than once — for **الحكمة**, for
**تأویل القرآن**, and for **فقہ في الدين**, and the last of these is in البخاري and مسلم `[SOURCED]`
سیر ج۳ ص۳۳۴، ج۳ ص۳۳۷، ج۳ ص۳۳۹ and printed footnotes. Under عمر ؓ he was brought into the council of the
men of بدر over the objections of the مهاجرون, and عمر ؓ used that to display his learning `[SOURCED]`
سیر ج۳ ص۳۴۳.

### In this window (۲۳–۴۱ھ)

- **He fought in إفريقيہ with عبد الله بن أبي سرح ؓ** — أبو سعيد بن يونس رحمہ اللہ says so, and adds that
  fifteen Egyptians narrate from him. `[SOURCED]` سیر ج۳ ص۳۳۶. This is the one clean fact placing him on
  the ۲۷ھ North African campaign.
- **عثمان ؓ sent him to lead the موسم — the حج — in ۳۵ھ, while عثمان ؓ was under siege.** The printed
  footnote records this from أبو نعيم رحمہ اللہ: «وزاد أنه كان على الموسم يعني سنة خمس وثلاثين، كان عثمان
  رضي الله عنه أرسله لما حصر». `[SOURCED]` سیر ج۳ ص۳۳۷ (printed footnote). **This is a load-bearing fact
  for session 3** — it dates him away from المدینہ at the moment of the killing, and it is a fact in the
  book, not an inference.
- The exchange with معاویۃ ؓ (below) belongs to the end of this window or just after it `[SOURCED]`
  سیر ج۳ ص۳۴۲.
- The exchange with علي ؓ about burning apostates (below) belongs to علي's ؓ caliphate `[SOURCED]`
  سیر ج۳ ص۳۴۶.

### One واقعہ worth telling — waiting in the dust at the Anṣār's doors

He said: when the Messenger of God ﷺ died, I said to a man of the Anṣār, come, let us ask the Companions
of the Messenger of God ﷺ — they are many today. He said: I am astonished at you, Ibn ʿAbbās. Do you
think people need you, when among them are the Companions you see? So he left it, and I went to the
asking. If a hadith reached me from a man, I would come to him while he was resting at midday and lay my
cloak as a pillow at his door, and the wind would blow the dust over me; and he would come out and see me
and say: cousin of the Messenger of God — why did you not send for me and I would have come to you? And I
would say: I have more right to come to you and ask you. He said: and that man lived until he saw me with
the people gathered around me, and he said: **this young man was wiser than I was.**

> فَقَالَ: هَذَا الفَتَى أَعقَلُ مِنِّي (٢) .
> — سیر أعلام النبلاء ج۳ ص۳۴۳ · https://shamela.ws/book/10906/2951
> *English:* "He said: this young man was wiser than I."

The printed footnote reads `إسناده صحيح` `[SOURCED]` سیر ج۳ ص۳۴۳ (printed footnote). A parallel report,
graded حسن by the editor, adds his own reason for not waking the sleeping man: **لأَسْتطِيبَ بِذَلِكَ
قَلْبَهُ** — to make his heart glad by it `[SOURCED]` سیر ج۳ ص۳۴۴. He also said he would ask thirty of
the Prophet's ﷺ Companions about a single matter, and the editor grades the chain صحيح `[SOURCED]`
سیر ج۳ ص۳۴۴.

### A second واقعہ — عمر ؓ, the elders of بدر, and سورۃ النصر

Some of the مهاجرون resented عمر's ؓ bringing ابن عباس ؓ near while keeping them back. عمر ؓ said: today I
will show you of him what will make you know his merit. He asked them about **{إِذَا جَاءَ نَصْرُ اللهِ}**.
Some said: God commanded His Prophet ﷺ, when he saw people entering God's religion in crowds, to praise
Him and seek His forgiveness. Then عمر ؓ said: Ibn ʿAbbās, speak. He said: God informed him when he would
die — **this is your sign of death, so glorify your Lord with praise and seek His forgiveness.**
`[SOURCED]` سیر ج۳ ص۳۴۳. The printed footnote traces the fuller البخاري version in four places and
records عمر's ؓ closing words: **ما أعلم منها إلا ما تقول** — I know nothing of it but what you say
`[SOURCED]` سیر ج۳ ص۳۴۳–۳۴۴ (printed footnote). عمر's ؓ own description of him, given twice on these pages:

> ذَلِكَ فَتَى الكُهُولِ، لَهُ لِسَانٌ سَؤُولٌ، وَقَلْبٌ عَقُوْلٌ (١) .
> — سیر أعلام النبلاء ج۳ ص۳۴۵ · https://shamela.ws/book/10906/2953
> *English:* "That is the young man of the elders: he has a questioning tongue and an understanding
> heart."

And عمر ؓ would say, when a matter weighed on him and he consulted him: **غُصْ غَوَّاصُ** — dive, diver
`[SOURCED]` سیر ج۳ ص۳۴۶.

### A third واقعہ — the answer to معاویۃ ؓ, which is the strand's guardrail in one line

> ابْنُ طَاوُوْسٍ: عَنْ أَبِيْهِ، عَنِ ابْنِ عَبَّاسٍ: قَالَ لِي مُعَاوِيَةُ: أَنْتَ عَلَى مِلَّةِ عَلِيٍّ؟ قُلْتُ: وَلاَ عَلَى مِلَّةِ عُثْمَانَ، أَنَا عَلَى مِلَّةِ رَسُوْلِ اللهِ (٤) -صَلَّى اللَّهُ عَلَيْهِ وَسَلَّمَ -.
> — سیر أعلام النبلاء ج۳ ص۳۴۲ · https://shamela.ws/book/10906/2950
> *English:* "Ibn Ṭāwūs, from his father, from Ibn ʿAbbās: Muʿāwiyah said to me: are you upon the way of
> ʿAlī? I said: nor upon the way of ʿUthmān. I am upon the way of the Messenger of God ﷺ."

**This may be the single most useful card in the whole session.** It is a Companion, a Hāshimī, علي's ؓ own
cousin, refusing in one sentence to be sorted into a party — and refusing *both* labels, not one. It puts
the platform's own position into a Companion's mouth, which is exactly where it should be.

### A fourth واقعہ — the apostates and the fire

عكرمة said that علي ؓ burned some people who had apostatised from Islam. That reached ابن عباس ؓ and he
said: I would not have burned them with fire, for the Messenger of God ﷺ said **«لاَ تُعَذِّبُوا بِعَذَابِ
اللهِ»** — do not punish with God's punishment; and I would have killed them, for his ﷺ saying **«مَنْ بَدَّلَ
دِيْنَهُ، فَاقْتُلُوْهُ»**. That reached علي ؓ, and he said:

> وَيْحَ ابْنِ أُمِّ الفَضْلِ، إِنَّهُ لَغَوَّاصٌ عَلَى الهَنَاتِ (٢) .
> — سیر أعلام النبلاء ج۳ ص۳۴۶ · https://shamela.ws/book/10906/2954
> *English:* "Ibn Umm al-Faḍl! He is indeed a diver after difficult matters."

The printed footnote grades the chain صحيح, traces it to البخاري in two places and to النسائي, and quotes
الخطابي رحمہ اللہ that the wording **ويح** here is praise and admiration, not reproach; it also gives
الترمذي's رحمہ اللہ wording of علي's ؓ reply — **صدق ابن عباس** — and البلاذري's — **لله در ابن عباس**
`[SOURCED]` سیر ج۳ ص۳۴۶ (printed footnote).

**This is the best teaching example in the strand of how Companions disagreed** — openly, on a point of
law, in public, with the senior man answering with praise rather than anger. It is a disagreement the
room is safe to hear in full, and it makes the guardrail intelligible rather than merely asserted.

### His own words

> ابْنُ جُرَيْجٍ: عَنِ ابْنِ أَبِي مُلَيْكَةَ: قَالَ ابْنُ عَبَّاسٍ: ذَهَبَ النَّاسُ وَبَقِيَ النَّسْنَاسُ. قِيْلَ: مَا النَّسْنَاسُ؟ قَالَ: الَّذِيْنَ يُشْبِهُوْنَ النَّاسَ وَلَيْسُوا بِالنَّاسِ (٣) .
> — سیر أعلام النبلاء ج۳ ص۳۴۲ · https://shamela.ws/book/10906/2950
> *English:* "Ibn Jurayj, from Ibn Abī Mulaykah: Ibn ʿAbbās said: the people have gone and the nasnās
> remain. It was said: what is the nasnās? He said: those who resemble people but are not the people."

> إِسْرَائِيْلُ: أَخْبَرَنَا سِمَاكٌ، عَنْ عِكْرِمَةَ، عَنِ ابْنِ عَبَّاسٍ، قَالَ: كُلُّ القُرْآنِ أَعْلَمُهُ إِلاَّ ثَلاَثاً؛ (الرَّقِيْمَ) ، و (غِسْلِيْنَ) ، و (حَنَاناً (٢)) .
> — سیر أعلام النبلاء ج۳ ص۳۴۵ · https://shamela.ws/book/10906/2953
> *English:* "Isrāʾīl: Simāk informed us, from ʿIkrimah, from Ibn ʿAbbās, who said: the whole of the
> Qurʾān I know, except three: al-raqīm, ghislīn, and ḥanān."

*(The editor notes that Simāk's transmission specifically from ʿIkrimah is unsettled, so quote this one
with that caveat or not at all `[SOURCED]` سیر ج۳ ص۳۴۵, printed footnote.)*

And the counsel his father gave him when عمر ؓ began bringing him near — three lines, and they are as
good a piece of advice as anything in the window:

> قَالَ ابْنُ عَبَّاسٍ: قَالَ لِي أَبِي: يَا بُنَيَّ! إِنَّ عُمَرَ يُدنِيكَ، فَاحفَظْ عَنِّي ثَلاَثاً: لاَ تُفْشِيَنَّ لَهُ سِرّاً، وَلاَ تَغْتَابَنَّ عِنْدَهُ أَحَداً، وَلاَ يُجَرِّبَنَّ عَلَيْكَ كَذِباً (١) .
> — سیر أعلام النبلاء ج۳ ص۳۴۶ · https://shamela.ws/book/10906/2954
> *English:* "Ibn ʿAbbās said: my father said to me: my son, ʿUmar brings you near — so keep three things
> from me: never disclose a secret of his, never backbite anyone in his presence, and never let him catch
> you in a lie."

### Death

**Not on the pages read for this note.** ۶۸ھ at الطائف is the conventional report `[STANDARD]`
*(to verify)*. The pages read do carry the reports that he would lose his sight — one graded لين by
الذهبي رحمہ اللہ `[SOURCED]` سیر ج۳ ص۳۴۰.

### ⚠ What not to say about him from the platform

- Do not use «إن حبر هذه الأمة ابن عباس» as a prophetic hadith. الذهبي رحمہ اللہ marks its narrator
  **واهٍ** `[SOURCED]` سیر ج۳ ص۳۳۹. The title حبر الأمة is well established as the ummah's own usage —
  use it as that, not as a quoted hadith.
- Do not use the جبريل ؑ report «إنه كائن هذا حبر الأمة فاستوص به خيرا». الذهبي رحمہ اللہ writes beneath
  it **حَدِيثٌ مُنْكَرٌ** `[SOURCED]` سیر ج۳ ص۳۳۹.
- Do not use the دحية الكلبي ؓ report about his sight. الذهبي رحمہ اللہ writes **إِسْنَادُهُ لَيِّنٌ**
  `[SOURCED]` سیر ج۳ ص۳۴۰.
- Do not use «هذا شيخ قريش». الذهبي رحمہ اللہ writes **إِسْنَادُهُ ضَعِيْفٌ** `[SOURCED]` سیر ج۳ ص۳۴۱.
- Do not turn his answer to معاویۃ ؓ into a rebuke of معاویۃ ؓ. It is a refusal of party labels, and the
  refusal covers عثمان's ؓ name as well as علي's ؓ. Read it whole or not at all.

## 4. مروان بن الحكم

*(No ؓ. الذهبي رحمہ اللہ places him at the head of **كبار التابعين**, and says only «وَقِيْلَ: لَهُ رُؤْيَةٌ،
وَذَلِكَ مُحْتَمَلٌ» — it is said he saw the Prophet ﷺ, and that is possible. `[SOURCED]` سیر ج۳ ص۴۷۶. So he
is not treated as a Companion here, and he must not be given a Companion's honorific on a slide.)*

### The تراجم notice, in one breath

- **Name + لقب:** مَرْوَانُ بنُ الحَكَمِ بنِ أَبِي العَاصِ الأُمَوِيُّ, أبو عبد الملك — الذهبي رحمہ اللہ heads him
  **المَلِكُ**, and records the nickname the people gave him: **«يُلَقَّبُ: خَيْطَ بَاطِلٍ»**. `[SOURCED]`
  سیر ج۳ ص۴۷۶، ج۳ ص۴۷۷. That is a لقب from the tradition, not from us; it is unkind, and it belongs in the
  briefing rather than on a slide.
- **Dates:** born at مکہ, **four months younger than عبد الله بن الزبير ؓ** — i.e. in ۲ھ or ۳ھ
  `[SOURCED]` سیر ج۳ ص۴۷۶ · secretary of عثمان ؓ during his caliphate `[SOURCED]` سیر ج۳ ص۴۷۷ · at الجمل
  in ۳۶ھ `[SOURCED]` سیر ج۳ ص۴۷۷، ج۳ ص۴۷۹ · governor of المدینہ for معاویۃ ؓ more than once, after this
  window `[SOURCED]` سیر ج۳ ص۴۷۷ · held الشام and مصر nine months and **died in the first of رمضان ۶۵ھ**
  `[SOURCED]` سیر ج۳ ص۴۷۸–۴۷۹. **On the Line:** he sits under both ۳۵ھ and ۳۶ھ.
- **What he did, one sentence:** he was عثمان's ؓ secretary and held his seal, and — in الذهبي's رحمہ اللہ
  own words on the page — betrayed him, and it was on his account that the people were roused against
  عثمان ؓ.

### Clan and kin

بنو أمية بن عبد شمس. Son of **الحكم بن أبي العاص**, who is عثمان's ؓ paternal uncle — so مروان is عثمان's ؓ
**ابن عم**, which is how الذهبي رحمہ اللہ describes him on the page `[SOURCED]` سیر ج۳ ص۴۷۷. Father of the
caliph **عبد الملك**, who narrates from him, and of **عبد العزيز** `[SOURCED]` سیر ج۳ ص۴۷۷، ج۳ ص۴۷۹ —
so the whole Marwānid line of session 4 runs from him. Among those who narrate from him are **سهل بن
سعد ؓ** (older than he was), **سعيد بن المسيب رحمہ اللہ**, **علي بن الحسين رحمہ اللہ** and **عروة رحمہ اللہ**
`[SOURCED]` سیر ج۳ ص۴۷۶–۴۷۷.

### Before ۲۳ھ, in three or four sentences

His father الحكم had been driven out by the Prophet ﷺ to الطائف, **and عثمان ؓ brought him back to
المدینہ because he was his uncle** `[SOURCED]` سیر ج۳ ص۴۷۷. That single sentence is one of the grievances
of ۳۵ھ stated by our own source without commentary, and it is the reason مروان is in المدینہ at all. He
himself narrates from عمر ؓ, عثمان ؓ, علي ؓ and زيد ؓ `[SOURCED]` سیر ج۳ ص۴۷۶.

### In this window (۲۳–۴۱ھ)

الذهبي رحمہ اللہ gives the whole of it in one sentence, and the honest thing is to quote him rather than
paraphrase:

> وَكَانَ كَاتِبَ ابْنِ عَمِّهِ عُثْمَانَ، وَإِلَيه الخَاتِمُ، فَخَانَهُ، وَأَجْلَبُوا بِسَبِبِهِ عَلَى عُثْمَانَ، ثُمَّ نَجَا هُوَ، وَسَارَ مَعَ طَلْحَةَ وَالزُّبَيْرِ لِلطَّلَبِ بِدَمِ عُثْمَانَ، فَقَتَلَ طَلْحَةَ يَوْمَ الجَمَلِ، وَنَجَا - لاَ نُجِّيَ - ثُمَّ وَلِي المَدِيْنَةَ غَيْرَ مَرَّةٍ لِمُعَاوِيَةَ.
> — سیر أعلام النبلاء ج۳ ص۴۷۷ · https://shamela.ws/book/10906/3085
> *English:* "He was the secretary of his cousin ʿUthmān, and the seal was in his keeping, and he
> betrayed him; and it was on his account that they were roused against ʿUthmān. Then he himself escaped,
> and went out with Ṭalḥah and al-Zubayr demanding the blood of ʿUthmān, and killed Ṭalḥah on the day of
> al-Jamal, and escaped — may he not be saved. Then he governed Madīnah more than once for Muʿāwiyah."

Note carefully what this licenses and what it does not. **The betrayal is الذهبي's رحمہ اللہ statement**,
and it is said of a تابعي, not of a Companion. **The killing of طلحۃ ؓ is stated twice**, here and again
from ابن سعد رحمہ اللہ at ج۳ ص۴۷۹, with the detail that مروان fought hardest at الجمل and, when he saw the
rout, shot طلحۃ ؓ with an arrow and killed him `[SOURCED]` سیر ج۳ ص۴۷۹. ابن سعد رحمہ اللہ adds that
مروان was wounded that day, was carried to a woman's house and treated, and went into hiding — and that
**علي ؓ gave him security, and he gave him the بیعہ, and was sent back to المدینہ** `[SOURCED]`
سیر ج۳ ص۴۷۹. That last clause matters: the man who killed طلحۃ ؓ was given امان by علي ؓ, and the book
records it without comment.

ابن سعد رحمہ اللہ also states the grievance in the plainest possible words:

> قَالَ ابْنُ سَعْدٍ: كَانُوا يَنْقِمُوْنَ عَلَى عُثْمَانَ تَقْرِيبَ مَرْوَانَ، وَتَصَرُّفَهُ.
> — سیر أعلام النبلاء ج۳ ص۴۷۹ · https://shamela.ws/book/10906/3087
> *English:* "Ibn Saʿd said: they held it against ʿUthmān that he brought Marwān near, and his conduct."

**This is the sentence session 3 needs**, because it states what the complaint actually was — about a
secretary, not about the caliph's religion — and it states it in a classical voice, so the platform never
has to formulate the grievance itself.

### One واقعہ worth telling — علي ؓ asking after him at الجمل

الشافعي رحمہ اللہ reports that when they were routed on the day of الجمل, علي ؓ asked after مروان and
said:

> يَعْطِفُنِي عَلَيْهِ رَحِمٌ مَاسَّةٌ، وَهُوَ مَعَ ذَلِكَ سَيِّدٌ مِنْ شَبَابِ قُرَيْشٍ (٤) .
> — سیر أعلام النبلاء ج۳ ص۴۷۷ · https://shamela.ws/book/10906/3085
> *English:* "A close kinship inclines me toward him; and with that, he is a chief among the young men of
> Quraysh."

Told beside the previous card it is almost unbearable, and it is the truest thing in the strand: the man
had just killed طلحۃ ؓ, and علي ؓ, asking after him, spoke of kinship. **This is the شکل of the fitna in
one exchange** — everyone in it is a cousin of everyone else.

### A second واقعہ — الحسن ؓ and الحسين ؓ praying behind him

جعفر الصادق رحمہ اللہ, from his father: **الحسن ؓ and الحسين ؓ used to pray behind مروان and did not
repeat the prayer** `[SOURCED]` سیر ج۳ ص۴۷۸. ابن كثير رحمہ اللہ's version, cited in the printed footnote
from الشافعي رحمہ اللہ, adds «ويقعدان بها» `[SOURCED]` سیر ج۳ ص۴۷۸ (printed footnote).

This card does a great deal of work in a room that is inclined to sort everyone into camps: the two
grandsons of the Prophet ﷺ prayed behind the man, and the point of law survives the personal history
intact.

### A third واقعہ — what he said at the end

> قَالَ مَالِكٌ: تَذَكَّرَ مَرْوَانُ، فَقَالَ: قَرَأْتُ كِتَابَ اللهِ مِنْ أَرْبَعِيْنَ سَنَةً، ثُمَّ أَصْبَحْتُ فِيمَا أَنَا فِيْهِ مِنْ هرقِ الدِّمَاءِ وَهَذَا الشَّأْنِ (١) ؟!
> — سیر أعلام النبلاء ج۳ ص۴۷۹ · https://shamela.ws/book/10906/3087
> *English:* "Mālik said: Marwān reflected and said: I have read the Book of God for forty years, and
> then I have come to be in what I am in — the shedding of blood, and this business?"

### Death

**أول رمضان ۶۵ھ**, after holding الشام and مصر nine months — الذهبي رحمہ اللہ says **مَاتَ خَنْقاً**, he
died by suffocation `[SOURCED]` سیر ج۳ ص۴۷۸–۴۷۹. ابن سعد رحمہ اللہ gives the circumstance in full
(a quarrel over the succession and خالد بن يزيد, after which خالد's mother, married to مروان, and her
women smothered him with a cushion) `[SOURCED]` سیر ج۳ ص۴۷۹. **وَقِيْلَ: مَاتَ بِالطَّاعُوْنِ** — and it is
said he died of the plague `[SOURCED]` سیر ج۳ ص۴۷۹. **Give both; the book gives both.** This is outside
session 3's window; it belongs to session 4.

### ⚠ What not to say about him from the platform

- Do not give him ؓ, and do not call him a Companion. الذهبي رحمہ اللہ puts him in كبار التابعين and says
  the sighting is only *possible* `[SOURCED]` سیر ج۳ ص۴۷۶.
- **Do not use the «إذا بلغ بنو العاص ثلاثين رجلا…» report as a hadith of the Prophet ﷺ.** الذهبي
  رحمہ اللہ prints it as a saying of أبو هريرة ؓ and then writes **«جَاءَ هَذَا مَرْفُوْعاً، لَكِنْ فِيْهِ عَطِيَّةُ
  العَوْفِيُّ»** — it has come raised to the Prophet ﷺ, but عطية العوفي is in it; and the printed footnote
  runs through four chains and says of the other routes, on ابن كثير's رحمہ اللہ authority, **وكلها لا
  تصح** `[SOURCED]` سیر ج۳ ص۴۷۸ and its footnote. In this room, quoting that as a hadith would be a real
  fault.
- Do not narrate the exchange at ج۳ ص۴۷۸ in which مروان calls the Prophet's ﷺ household cursed and
  الحسن ؓ answers him. الذهبي رحمہ اللہ discredits the chain in one line: **«وَأَبُو يَحْيَى هَذَا: نَخَعِيٌّ،
  لاَ أَعْرِفُهُ»** — I do not know him `[SOURCED]` سیر ج۳ ص۴۷۸.
- Do not extend الذهبي's رحمہ اللہ «فخانه» beyond مروان. It is said of مروان, a تابعي; it is not said of
  any Companion, and it must not be allowed to slide into a statement about بنو أمية as such.
- The material about the Ḥarrah and مسرف بن عقبة (ج۳ ص۴۷۹) is real and is ۶۳ھ. It is session 4's, not
  session 3's, and it is inflammatory out of sequence.

## 5. الوليد بن عقبة ؓ

### The تراجم notice, in one breath

- **Name + لقب:** الوَلِيْدُ بنُ عُقْبَةَ بنِ أَبِي مُعَيْطٍ بنِ أَبِي عَمْرٍو الأُمَوِيُّ ؓ, أبو وهب — الذهبي رحمہ اللہ
  heads him simply **الأَمِيْرُ** and says **«لَهُ: صُحْبَةٌ قَلِيْلَةٌ، وَرِوَايَةٌ يَسِيْرَةٌ»** — his companionship
  was slight and his transmission small. `[SOURCED]` سیر ج۳ ص۴۱۲–۴۱۳. **He has no لقب; do not give him
  one.**
- **Dates:** among **مسلمة الفتح**, those who accepted Islam at the conquest of مکہ (۸ھ) `[SOURCED]`
  سیر ج۳ ص۴۱۳ · sent by the Prophet ﷺ over the صدقات of بنو المصطلق `[SOURCED]` سیر ج۳ ص۴۱۳ · sent by
  عمر ؓ over the صدقات of بنو تغلب `[SOURCED]` سیر ج۳ ص۴۱۴ · **governor of الکوفہ for عثمان ؓ**
  `[SOURCED]` سیر ج۳ ص۴۱۴ · withdrew to الجزیرہ after his brother's killing and **fought with neither
  party** `[SOURCED]` سیر ج۳ ص۴۱۴ · date of death not given, **and ابن عساكر رحمہ اللہ does not give it
  either** `[SOURCED]` سیر ج۳ ص۴۱۵. His grave is near الرقّہ `[SOURCED]` سیر ج۳ ص۴۱۴. **On the Line:** he
  is the name under ۳۰ھ.
- **What he did, one sentence:** he governed الکوفہ for عثمان ؓ in place of سعد بن أبي وقاص ؓ, was removed
  and flogged by عثمان ؓ himself when the ḥadd was established against him, and thereafter took no side in
  the fighting.

### Clan and kin

بنو أمية بن عبد شمس. **He is عثمان's ؓ half-brother on his mother's side** — «وَهُوَ أَخُو أَمِيْرِ
المُؤْمِنِيْنَ عُثْمَانَ لأُمِّهِ» `[SOURCED]` سیر ج۳ ص۴۱۳ — which is exactly the relation that makes his
appointment a grievance. His father **عقبة بن أبي معيط** was among those the Prophet ﷺ ordered put to
death after بدر `[SOURCED]` سیر ج۳ ص۴۱۳–۴۱۴. So the room is looking at a man whose father was executed at
بدر, who himself became Muslim at the فتح, and who ends as governor of الکوفہ — and that whole arc is on a
single page of our own source.

### Before ۲۳ھ, in three or four sentences

The Prophet ﷺ sent him over the صدقات of بنو المصطلق. The printed footnote sets out the well-known report
behind that mission at length — that he turned back partway, that the Ḥārith ؓ and his people came to
المدینہ and denied what had been reported of them, and that سورۃ الحجرات's verse **{إِنْ جَاءَكُمْ فَاسِقٌ
بِنَبَإٍ فَتَبَيَّنُوا}** came down over it — and it quotes ابن عبد البر رحمہ اللہ that there is no
disagreement among the people of Qurʾānic interpretation, so far as he knows, that this verse came down
concerning الوليد بن عقبة `[SOURCED]` سیر ج۳ ص۴۱۳–۴۱۴ (printed footnote). ⚠ **The footnote also says the
chain of that narrative is not strong** (دينار the father of عيسى is not documented by anyone but
ابن حبان رحمہ اللہ) — so narrate the verse's occasion as **ابن عبد البر's رحمہ اللہ report of the
exegetes' consensus**, which is what the page actually supports, and not as a scene. عمر ؓ later sent him
over the صدقات of بنو تغلب `[SOURCED]` سیر ج۳ ص۴۱۴.

### In this window (۲۳–۴۱ھ)

- **عثمان ؓ removed سعد بن أبي وقاص ؓ from الکوفہ and appointed الوليد ؓ.** الذهبي رحمہ اللہ states plainly
  that this was among what they held against عثمان ؓ. `[SOURCED]` سیر ج۳ ص۴۱۵.
- **الوليد ؓ led the فجر prayer as four rakʿahs and then turned and said: shall I give you more?** It
  reached عثمان ؓ, who summoned him and applied the ḥadd. `[SOURCED]` سیر ج۳ ص۴۱۴ — and the full account,
  from **صحیح مسلم**, is printed in the footnote beneath (below).
- الذهبي رحمہ اللہ's balance on him, in his own words: **«وَكَانَ مَعَ فِسْقِهِ - وَاللهُ يُسَامِحُهُ - شُجَاعاً،
  قَائِماً بِأَمْرِ الجِهَادِ»** `[SOURCED]` سیر ج۳ ص۴۱۵. He also calls him سخي, much praised, a poet
  `[SOURCED]` سیر ج۳ ص۴۱۴.
- **After عثمان's ؓ killing he withdrew to الجزیرہ and fought with neither party** — «ثُمَّ اعْتَزَلَ
  بِالجَزِيْرَةِ بَعْدَ قَتْلِ أَخِيْهِ عُثْمَانَ، وَلَمْ يُحَارِبْ مَعَ أَحَدٍ مِنَ الفَرِيْقَيْنِ» `[SOURCED]`
  سیر ج۳ ص۴۱۴. He joins the list of those who withdrew.

### One واقعہ worth telling — the ḥadd applied in المدینہ

This is the scene, and it belongs to the evening whether or not the appointment does, because it is the
cleanest available answer to the charge that عثمان ؓ shielded his relatives. The printed footnote gives
the **مسلم** narrative in full: عثمان ؓ was brought الوليد, two men testified — one of them حمران — that
he had drunk wine, and another that he had seen him vomit; عثمان ؓ said, he did not vomit it until he had
drunk it. Then:

> فَقَالَ: يا علي قم فاجلده، فقال علي: قم يا حسن فاجلده، فقال الحسن: ول حارها من تولى قارها - فكأنه وجد عليه - فقال: يا عبد الله بن جعفر، قم فاجلده، فجلده - وعلي يعد - حتى بلغ أربعين، فقال: أمسك، ثم قال: جلد النبي صلى الله عليه وسلم أربعين، وجلد أبو بكر أربعين، وعمر ثمانين، وكل سنة، وهذا أحب إلي.
> — سیر أعلام النبلاء ج۳ ص۴۱۴ (the printed edition's footnote, quoting مسلم ۱۷۰۷) ·
> https://shamela.ws/book/10906/3022
> *English:* "He said: ʿAlī, stand and flog him. ʿAlī said: Ḥasan, stand and flog him. Ḥasan said: give
> its hot part to whoever took its cool part — as though he was displeased at it. So he said: ʿAbd Allāh
> b. Jaʿfar, stand and flog him. And he flogged him — ʿAlī counting — until he reached forty. Then he
> said: hold. Then he said: the Prophet ﷺ flogged forty, and Abū Bakr flogged forty, and ʿUmar eighty;
> and all of it is سنة, and this is dearer to me."

Notice what the room gets from this in one minute, without the platform arguing anything: **the caliph's
own half-brother and governor is flogged in المدینہ; علي ؓ counts the strokes; the punishment is carried
out.** The quoted text is the printed edition's footnote, quoting مسلم — say so, and cite مسلم ۱۷۰۷ when
it is used, not سیر.

*(⚠ The line of verse beginning **لأَشْرَبَنَّ وَإِنْ كَانَتْ مُحَرَّمَةً** at سیر ج۳ ص۴۱۴ is on the page and
is attributed to him. Do not put it on a slide; it adds nothing the ḥadd scene does not already carry, and
it invites the room into ridicule of a Companion.)*

### A second واقعہ — الوليد ؓ and علي ؓ

ابن أبي ليلى, from الحكم, from سعيد بن جبير, from ابن عباس ؓ: الوليد بن عقبة said to علي ؓ: I am sharper
of spear than you, and more fluent of tongue, and better able to fill a squadron. علي ؓ said: be quiet —
you are only a فاسق. And then **{أَفَمَنْ كَانَ مُؤْمِناً كَمَنْ كَانَ فَاسِقاً}** came down `[SOURCED]`
سیر ج۳ ص۴۱۵.

**الذهبي رحمہ اللہ does not let this stand.** He writes immediately beneath it:

> قُلْتُ: إِسْنَادُهُ قَوِيٌّ، لَكِنَّ سِيَاقَ الآيَةِ يَدُلُّ عَلَى أَنَّهَا فِي أَهْلِ النَّارِ.
> — سیر أعلام النبلاء ج۳ ص۴۱۵ · https://shamela.ws/book/10906/3023
> *English:* "I say: its chain is strong, but the context of the verse indicates that it is about the
> people of the Fire."

And he adds that it is also said the exchange of insults was between علي ؓ and **عقبة himself** — the
father — on the authority of ابن لهيعة, from عمرو بن دينار, from ابن عباس ؓ `[SOURCED]` سیر ج۳ ص۴۱۵.

**This is a model of the method the course is teaching**, and it is worth a card on its own: a strong
chain is not the end of the inquiry; the Qurʾānic context can still overturn what the chain reports about
a verse's occasion, and a classical scholar says so about a report that would have been convenient to
keep.

### A third واقعہ — the message to ابن مسعود ؓ

جرير بن حازم, from عيسى بن عاصم: الوليد sent to ابن مسعود ؓ telling him to be silent about these words —
**«أَحْسَنُ الهَدْيِ هَدْيُ مُحَمَّدٍ ﷺ وَشَرُّ الأُمُوْرِ مُحْدَثَاتُهَا»** `[SOURCED]` سیر ج۳ ص۴۱۶. The governor
of الکوفہ asking ابن مسعود ؓ to stop saying that particular sentence is, in one line, the whole texture of
what الکوفہ was complaining about — and it is narrated without any comment from the platform being needed.

### Death

**Not established.** الذهبي رحمہ اللہ notes that he has long notices in تاریخ دمشق **«وَلَمْ يَذْكُرْ
وَفَاتَهُ»** — and ابن عساكر رحمہ اللہ did not give his date of death `[SOURCED]` سیر ج۳ ص۴۱۵. All that is
given is that **his grave is near الرقّہ** `[SOURCED]` سیر ج۳ ص۴۱۴. **Do not supply a year.**

### ⚠ What not to say about him from the platform

- Do not narrate the بنو المصطلق affair as a scene. The printed footnote itself weakens the chain; what
  the page supports is ابن عبد البر's رحمہ اللہ statement of the exegetes' position on the verse.
- Do not say the verse **{أَفَمَنْ كَانَ مُؤْمِناً كَمَنْ كَانَ فَاسِقاً}** came down about him. الذهبي رحمہ اللہ
  rejects that reading on the page `[SOURCED]` سیر ج۳ ص۴۱۵.
- Do not quote the drinking verse. See above.
- Keep the ḥadd scene as narrative and let it end there. Do not use it to argue that the complainants
  were right or that they were wrong — the point that carries is that the ḥadd was carried out.
- He is a Companion, however slight the صحبۃ, and carries ؓ. الذهبي رحمہ اللہ says فسقه in the same
  breath as **واللہ يسامحه**; if the first half is quoted, the second half goes with it.
