## The honest picture
The 40-min deck solidly delivers ~14 of these and lightly touches ~9. That's a real editorial choice forced by
the 40-min budget — the muqaddima is genuinely a 75–90 min body of material.

## My recommendation — 3 pieces worth restoring even in a tight deck (they're cheap and high-value)
- **⭐⭐ #34 — "Tārīkh al-Islām = Tārīkh al-Muslimīn."** This single idea (we study the history of the *Muslims* —
  with highs and lows — not of the *religion*) is the cleanest possible frame for the *mushājarāt* lectures.
  One slide. Strongly recommend.
- **⭐ #2 — the real definition** (time-ordering is what makes it tārīkh). Currently only implied; one slide fixes it.
- **⭐ #9 — the four stages** (the "building a house" picture). Memorable, one slide.

To fit them in ~40 min, the cheapest trades are dropping one of the two detective stories (keep the forged
document, drop the wine cup) and keeping the giants/books to one slide each (already done).

## If you'd rather not lose anything
Then the muqaddima honestly wants **two Tuesdays** (Part A: how-do-we-know + timeline + map + rules · Part B:
who-saved-it + books + the benefits/dangers). Same content, just unhurried. Your call — this map lets you decide
with the full picture in front of you.
