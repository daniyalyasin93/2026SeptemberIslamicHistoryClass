## The light slides — what to SAY out loud (detail is in the script)
- **What is history:** same event in poem / hadith / tārīkh — only tārīkh is time-ordered. Subject = *al-insān wa'l-zamān*.
- **Nasī':** they shuffled months to keep Hajj in trade season; added a 13th month; Prophet ﷺ ended it at the Farewell Hajj («زمانہ گھوم کر واپس آ گیا»). Why dates in seerah differ. (Tawba 9:37)
- **History of the MUSLIMS, not the religion:** the dīn is perfect, the people are human — highs & lows. → your shield for Lessons 5 & 7.
- **4 stages:** tāsīs → tadwīn → tanqīḥ → takmīl (building a house). Refined 7th c. (Dhahabī, Ibn Kathīr), peak 8th (Ibn Khaldūn).
- **Giants:** Ibn Isḥāq→Ibn Hishām · Wāqidī vs Ibn Saʿd · Balādhurī · ⚠ Yaʿqūbī/Masʿūdī were Shīʿa.
- **5 books:** Ṭabarī · al-Kāmil · Tārīkh al-Islām · al-Bidāya · Ibn Khaldūn. Fun: **two Ṭabarīs**, ours is Sunnī.
