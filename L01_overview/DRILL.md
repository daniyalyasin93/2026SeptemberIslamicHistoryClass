# DRILL — نشست ۱

Cover the right-hand column. Answer **out loud**, standing. Confidence comes from being tested,
not from re-reading. Do this twice: Sunday, and again an hour before you leave.

**You have passed when you can do all fifteen without looking, and when items 1, 6 and 13 come out
in your own words rather than recited.**

## The three you are most likely to get wrong under pressure

- **۱۹۲۴ → session 7. ۱۹۴۷ → session 9.** Easy to swap when nervous, and swapping it looks careless.
- **Two volunteers maximum** after آج کی بات, and you say «شکریہ» and **nothing else** — no improving,
  no building on what they said. The conclusion is theirs, not yours.
- **The narrator paragraph goes in آغاز, not at the end.** If you leave it to the close, you have
  spent the whole evening without your shield up.

## If you can only rehearse one thing

The **opening scene**, standing, out loud, three times. If the first five minutes land, the room
gives you the other forty.
