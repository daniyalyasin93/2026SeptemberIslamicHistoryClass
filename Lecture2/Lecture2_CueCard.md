# 🆘 CUE CARD — Lecture 2 · "The Treasure House"

## Opening move
Recap L1 in 30 sec (rules: isnād + asma al-rijāl) → "Today we meet the PEOPLE who saved it, and the BOOKS we trust."

## The 2 pillars
**Seerah-nigārī** (the Prophet's ﷺ life in order) + **Fann al-Rijāl** (biographies of every narrator). Both from hadith.

## The 4 stages (a house)
1 **Tāsīs** foundation · 2 **Tadwīn** walls (gather) · 3 **Tanqīḥ** plaster (sift) · 4 **Takmīl** paint (perfect). → Islamic history: founded pre-Islam → collected 2nd–4th c. → refined 7th → perfected 8th (Ibn Khaldūn).

## The giants (you don't need every date — cards carry them)
- Seerah pioneers: **Zuhrī** (d.124, first maghāzī) → **Ibn Isḥāq** (d.151) → **Ibn Hishām** (d.213, the famous Sīrah).
- **Wāqidī** (d.207, vivid but loose) vs his student **Ibn Saʿd** (d.230, the careful Ṭabaqāt).
- **Khalīfa b. Khayyāṭ** (d.240, first true tārīkh) · **Balādhurī** (d.279, Futūḥ al-Buldān).
- ⚠ Lens: **Yaʿqūbī** & **Masʿūdī** (d.346) were Shīʿa — read aware.

## The 5 mother-books (the shelf)
**Ṭabarī** d.310 (raw well, every chain) → **al-Kāmil**/Ibn al-Athīr d.630 (year-by-year) → **Tārīkh al-Islām**/Dhahabī d.748 (most careful) → **al-Bidāya wa'l-Nihāya**/Ibn Kathīr d.774 (reason+chains, friendly) → **Ibn Khaldūn** d.808 (Muqaddima = social science).
Hidden gems: **al-Muntaẓam** (Ibn al-Jawzī d.597) · **Mir'āt al-Zamān** (Sibṭ Ibn al-Jawzī d.654).

## The 2 fun stories
1. **Two Ṭabarīs**: same name (Abū Jaʿfar Muḥammad b. Jarīr) — ours (grandson of Yazīd) = Sunnī imam; the other (Ibn Rustam) = Shīʿa. Pre-empts the "Ṭabarī was Shīʿa" objection.
2. **The wine cup**: fake story that Shāfiʿī drank wine before Maʾmūn → Ibn Ḥajar (Lisān al-Mīzān): they never met (chain fails) + Shāfiʿī avoided even cold water dulling his mind (reason). Absurd.
